**Stochology** (from Greek **στόχος, *stókhos***, aim or conjecture, and **λόγος, *lógos***, reasoned account) is an integrative framework and transversal research program for the theory and ethics of aim in judgment and decision-making under structural uncertainty when action must precede the outcome.

In classical Greek thought, a sharp epistemic boundary separated the exact sciences of invariant measure (*metrētikē*, governed by mechanical *akríbeia*) from the **conjectural arts** (**στοχαστικαὶ τέχναι, *stokhastikaì tékhnai***)—medicine, navigation, agriculture, strategy, and statecraft—which operate in open, shifting, and irreversible worlds. In the *Philebus* (55e–56c), Plato contrasts the carpenter's rule with the navigator's and musician's practiced conjecture (*ou métrōi allà melétēs stokhasmōi*); yet whereas Plato ranked the conjectural arts *below* the measuring arts in scientific purity (*akríbeia*), Stochology consciously inverts that hierarchy for open historical and biological systems: wherever contingency, non-ergodic time, and strategic reactivity render mechanical gauges incomplete, forcing false *akríbeia* onto a turbulent medium produces dogmatic blindness, making self-critical *stokhasmós* the higher form of practical rationality.

Whether such conjectural practices deserved the status of a rational art (*tékhnē*) accountable to criticism (*lógos*) rather than mere blind knack or routine (*empeiría*, as Plato pressed in the *Gorgias* 463a–465a) was one of the defining controversies of ancient epistemology. In the Hippocratic treatises *On Ancient Medicine* (§9), *Aphorisms* (I.1, on the fleeting **καιρός, *kairós***), and *Prognostic* (on diagnostic signs, **σημεῖα, *sēmeîa***), the physician has no external mechanical weight beyond the living body's response; in the *Nicomachean Ethics* (II.6, 1106b28; VI.5, 1140a25–b30), Aristotle defines moral virtue and practical wisdom (**φρόνησις, *phrónēsis***) as the reasoned capacity to aim (*stokhastikos tou mesou*) at the intermediate good amid contingent particulars; in the Aristotelian commentator tradition (Alexander of Aphrodisias) and Galenic medicine (*De sectis*, *Ars medica*), this crystallized as **στοχαστικὴ τέχνη (*stokhastikē tékhnē*)**, a rational art whose method is fully accountable to criticism even though external realization remains exposed to fortune; and in the Stoic doctrine of Antipater of Tarsus—preserved in Stobaeus (*Eclogae* II.7.6a, II.76.3–15 Wachsmuth) and Plutarch (*De communibus notionibus* 1070F–1071E, LS 64C; cf. Cicero, *De Finibus* III.22 on the *ultimum bonorum*) in response to Carneades—the archer's internal ethical duty (**τέλος, *télos***, doing everything within one's power to aim well) is distinguished from the external result (**σκοπός, *skopós***, hitting the target, which remains exposed to crosswinds after the arrow leaves the bow).

Two millennia later, in Part IV of *Ars Conjectandi* (1713), Jakob Bernoulli coined *ars conjectandi sive stochastice*—the art of measuring the probabilities of things as accurately as possible so that in our judgments and actions we may always choose what is healthier, safer, or better advised. Connecting ancient *stokhastikē tékhnē* and Antipater's archer to Bernoulli's *stochastice* is an explicit structural analogy across distinct epistemic epochs rather than a Whiggish claim of unbroken doctrinal filiation. Yet over the twentieth century, the adjective *stochastic* was reduced to a technical synonym for a random process indexed by time, severed from the philosophical, institutional, and ethical problem of how human agents and institutions actually observe, conjecture, and intervene.

### Epistemic Demarcation: Is Stochology a "Science"?

Why formulate Stochology as an **integrative framework and transversal research program**—the critical *lógos* of conjectural aim (*stokhastikē tékhnē*)—rather than claiming the title of a new autonomous "positive science"?

1. **Refusal of False *Akríbeia* and Disciplinary Inflation:** A positive science typically claims either an exclusive empirical domain or an invariant mechanical gauge (*metrētikē*). Claiming that Stochology is a standalone "science" that annexes statistics, physics, or ethics would repeat the very positivist hubris it critiques: mistaking formal models for a closed mechanical mirror of the real. Conversely, abandoning formal rigor would reduce judgment under uncertainty to arbitrary knack (*empeiría*).
2. **An Indivisible Unit of Analysis Across Three Planes:** What justifies Stochology as a distinct transversal program is not a monopoly over isolated technical tools, but the delimitation of a unit of analysis that specialized disciplines habitually sever into non-communicating fragments. Whereas statistics examines the mapping from world to archive, decision theory analyzes choice under given distributions, and law or sociology studies institutional norms, Stochology investigates the **complete mediation circuit across world, archive, and institution**—and the structural collapses that occur whenever any one of its three safeguards is omitted.
3. **Instrument vs. Empirical Discovery (Scope and Falsifiability):** Epistemic honesty requires separating what this codebase **evaluates analytically** (`[Assumed]`), what it **measures empirically** on sealed historical archives (`[Measured]`), and what remains an **open empirical hypothesis** requiring prospective institutional testing (`[Assumed]`):
   - **Analytical Volume-Preservation Property of Non-Substitutable Aggregators (`[Assumed]`):** The nested Leontief minimum $M_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S_{\mathrm{epi}}, S_{\mathrm{vital}}, S_{\mathrm{gov}})$ is an axiomatic normative commitment to non-substitutability across safeguards whenever an absorbing ruin barrier or severe defect is active ($\sigma \to 0^+$; whereas in interior mild-shock regimes with doubled physical reserve stock, partial compensation is possible, as explicitly tested and refuted in Chapter 50's Falsifier `F4` with $d_{\mathrm{obs}} = 0.077 \le d_{\mathrm{crit}} = 0.1104, \mathrm{SEV} = 0.996, \text{refuted} = \text{true}$ `[Simulated]`), backed by the geometric volume-preservation audit in [`LeontiefVsMultiplicativeAudit`](crates/stochology/src/synthesis.rs#L455-L492) ($V_{\min} = (1-\tau)^3 = 0.1250$ vs. $V_{\mathrm{prod}} = 0.0333$, eliminating $73.35\%$ viable-volume collapse, $79.01\%$ volume-matched corner false collapses at $\tau_{\mathrm{prod}} = 0.2951$, and $100.0\%$ single-axis false-safe masking under arithmetic means).
   - **Mechanism-Design Verification vs. External Empirical Validation (`[Simulated]`):** The $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial ablation in [`CommunityReserveEnv`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs#L416-L423) is a deterministic mechanism-design stress test verifying that the computational instrument implements the three operators and their failure modes across $3^5 = 243$ threshold and structural DGP perturbations (`100.0%` local invariance, `66.7%` wide-band dominance). It verifies that the **instrument** is internally consistent; testing whether real-world institutions obey the triad externally requires prospective field evaluation under the four pre-registered Mayo severe falsifiers (`F1`–`F4` in Chapter 50).

This book—and the zero-dependency Rust codebase that accompanies it—develops **Stochology** across that full mediation circuit.

## 1. Three Critical Lineages and the Nuclear Triad

Why do sophisticated statistical models, machine-learning pipelines, and bureaucratic metrics so often produce catastrophic failures when deployed in the real world? Across the history of science and social thought, three traditions diagnosed three distinct dimensions of this failure, yet rarely spoke to one another:

1. **The Epistemological and Statistical Lineage** (Cromwell, Peirce, Keynes, Neyman, Wald, Popper, Lakatos, Mayo, Manski, Meng, Rubin, Pearl) showed that data never speak for themselves: samples suffer from selection defects and cluster correlation, multiple hypotheses can fit the same finite archive, and point estimates routinely masquerade as certainties.
2. **The Physical, Biological, and Cybernetic Lineage** (Canguilhem, Wiener, Ashby, Shannon, Itô, Kelly, Kingman, Scarf, Georgescu-Roegen, Samuelson, Holling, Beer, Perdomo) showed that action takes place in irreversible entropic time: variance drains multiplicative growth, queues explode nonlinearly near full utilization, performative models alter the very world they predict, and living or institutional systems without physical slack collapse at absorbing ruin barriers.
3. **The Critical, Sociological, and Ethical Lineage** (Spinoza, Kant, Adorno, Horkheimer, Simon, Arendt, Weil, Bateson, Habermas, Girard, Desrosières, Porter, Hacking, Harding, Williams, Nagel, Sosa, Fricker, Holmström, List, Pettit, Chouldechova) showed that archives are institutional artifacts: categories exclude the vulnerable, high-powered proxy targets and double-bind traps corrupt unmeasured care, moral luck and scapegoating distort retrospective blame, and frontline warnings are silenced by testimonial injustice.

Moreover, this mediation circuit operates from the **micro-hermeneutic scale of intimate dialogue** to macro-institutional governance: long before bureaucratic metrics are reified under Goodhart's Law, the impulse to annex another person's reception (**skopós**) through mirror-surveillance rather than caring for the integrity of aim (**télos**) suppresses the singular *Non-Identical* (Adorno) and erodes the dialogical "margin of tolerance for the inconsistencies of the environment" (Canguilhem). Stochology therefore articulates a three-fold ethical and phenomenological refusal—renouncing narcissistic control of intimate reception, exposing the social architecture of betrayal and scapegoating, and preserving the communicative transparency of decision premises—into a single **Nuclear Triad** of non-substitutable safeguards:

$$\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{Stochology}} = \bigl(\text{Epistemic Margin},\; \text{Vital Margin},\; \text{Institutional Governance}\bigr)$$

1. **Epistemic Margin (`EpistemicMarginState` in `epistemic.rs` · Chapters 43–45):** Never collapse underdetermination, selection bias, or cluster correlation into a dogmatic point estimate. Report partial-identification intervals $[L_t, U_t]$, unified effective sample size $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$, and Keynesian evidential weight $V(n_{\mathrm{eff}}) = \frac{n_{\mathrm{eff}}}{n_{\mathrm{eff}} + n_0}$.
2. **Vital Margin (`VitalMarginState` in `vital.rs` · Chapters 46–47):** Never confuse the evaluation at the mean $\phi(\mathbb{E}[X])$ with the expected evaluation $\mathbb{E}[\phi(X)]$, nor ensemble averages with single-trajectory survival over irreversible time. Maintain physical slack ($\rho < 1$, $\mathrm{TTS}_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}_{\mathrm{recovery}}$) and shrink leverage against estimation error.
3. **Institutional Governance (`GovernanceState` in `governance.rs` · Chapters 48–49):** Never evaluate an agent by retrospective outcome luck (*skopós*) alone, nor govern a complex social system through high-powered single proxies or silenced frontline testimony. Separate ex-ante competence (*télos*) from ex-post fortune, protect the algedonic emergency channel ($S3^*$, $\delta_{\mathrm{cred}} = 0$), and align liability with structural decision power.

## 3. How to Read This Book: The Contiguous Nuclear Triad and Sequential Loop

Inspired by the pedagogy of Reinforcement Learning (Sutton & Barto), this book avoids dry encyclopedic catalogs. Instead, its five parts form an integrated pedagogical and computational ascent from schoolroom intuition to the full multilayer architecture of human judgment and action:

### 3.1 The Five Parts of the Treatise

1. **Part I — Thinking Under Uncertainty (Chapters 01–08):** Ordinary-language foundations requiring no advanced mathematics. Establishes the three foundational refusals, the umbrella threshold $p^* = C / (\eta L)$, the flaw of point averages, heavy-tailed surprises, the moral luck of outcome bias, base-rate neglect, selective archive doors, Goodhart gaming, and the necessity of the Leontief minimum operator.
2. **Part II — The Bases (Chapters 09–26):** The rigorous tripartite foundation presupposed by subsequent modeling:
   - *Mathematics (Chapters 09–15):* Measure-theoretic probability, heavy tails, non-ergodic time dynamics, convexity and Jensen gaps, partial identification bounds, high-dimensional concentration, and game-theoretic incentives.
   - *Philosophy and Epistemology (Chapters 16–20):* Fallibilism and inductive skepticism, social production of ignorance (agnotology), epistemic injustice and testimony discounting, performativity, and the ethics of risk imposition.
   - *Natural Science (Chapters 21–26):* Classical mechanics and deterministic chaos, thermodynamic entropy, non-equilibrium dissipative structures, chemical buffering kinetics, evolutionary variation, and physiological allostasis.
3. **Part III — Foundations of Judgment (Chapters 27–41):** The formal architecture of judgment under structural uncertainty.
