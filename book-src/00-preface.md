**Stochology** (from Greek **στόχος, *stókhos***, aim or conjecture, and **λόγος, *lógos***, reasoned account) is an integrative framework, and a transversal research program, for the theory and ethics of aim in judgment and decision-making under structural uncertainty, for the case where action must precede the outcome.

Picture an archer on a gusty ridge, or a municipal water-reserve operator on the eve of an uncertain monsoon. At instant $t$ the bowstring must be released or the sluice gate set. The crosswind that deflects the shaft, or the storm surge that tests the levee, shows itself only at $t + 1$, after the shot can no longer be recalled.

In classical Greek thought a sharp epistemic boundary separated the exact sciences of invariant
measure (*metrētikē*, governed by mechanical *akríbeia*) from the **conjectural arts**
(**στοχαστικαὶ τέχναι, *stokhastikaì tékhnai***): music, medicine, navigation, agriculture, strategy. Those arts work in open, shifting,
irreversible worlds. In the *Philebus* (55d–56c) Plato
sets the carpenter's rule against the navigator's practiced conjecture (*ou métrōi allà melétēs
stokhasmōi*) (Plato c. 360 BCE) and ranks the conjectural arts *below* the measuring arts in purity
(*akríbeia*). For open historical and biological systems Stochology inverts that hierarchy. Wherever
contingency, non-ergodic time and strategic reactivity leave mechanical gauges incomplete, forcing
false *akríbeia* onto a turbulent medium produces dogmatic blindness, and self-critical *stokhasmós*
is the higher form of practical rationality.

Whether such practices deserved the status of a rational art (*tékhnē*) accountable to criticism
(*lógos*), rather than blind knack (*empeiría*, as Plato pressed in the *Gorgias* 463a–465a), was a
defining controversy of ancient epistemology.

The Hippocratic treatises *On Ancient Medicine* (§9), *Aphorisms* (I.1, on the fleeting **καιρός, *kairós***), and *Prognostic* (on diagnostic signs, **σημεῖα, *sēmeîa***) give the physician no mechanical weight beyond the living body's response. In the *Nicomachean Ethics*
(II.6; VI.5, 1140a25–b30) Aristotle predicates the capacity to aim (*stokhastikos tou mesou*) of the
stable disposition that hits the intermediate good amid contingent particulars, and defines practical
wisdom (**φρόνησις, *phrónēsis***) as the reasoned capacity to deliberate well about them. Among the
commentators this crystallized as **στοχαστικὴ τέχνη (*stokhastikē tékhnē*)**: a rational art,
accountable to criticism in its method even where realization stays exposed to fortune, with Alexander
of Aphrodisias supplying the theory and the medical introductory tradition the classification of the
conjectural arts, in a text transmitted under Galen's name whose authenticity is disputed. The Stoic doctrine of Antipater of Tarsus (preserved in Stobaeus, *Eclogae* II.76.3–15 Wachsmuth, and Plutarch (*De communibus notionibus* 1070F–1071E, LS 64C; cf. Cicero, *De Finibus* III.22 on the *ultimum bonorum*) in response to Carneades) separates the archer's internal ethical duty (**τέλος, *télos***, doing everything within one's power to aim well) from the external result (**σκοπός, *skopós***, hitting the target, which stays exposed to crosswinds after the arrow has left the bow).

Two millennia later, in Part IV of *Ars Conjectandi* (1713), Jakob Bernoulli coined *ars conjectandi
sive stochastice*: the art of measuring the probabilities of things as accurately as possible so that
in our judgments and actions we may always choose what is healthier, safer, or better advised
(Bernoulli 1713). The link from Antipater's archer to Bernoulli's *stochastice* is a structural analogy
across distinct epistemic epochs, not a claim of unbroken doctrinal filiation. Over the twentieth
century the adjective *stochastic* narrowed to a technical synonym for a random process indexed by
time, and lost the philosophical, institutional and ethical problem of how agents and institutions
observe, conjecture and intervene.

### Epistemic Demarcation: Is Stochology a "Science"?

Why call Stochology an **integrative framework and transversal research program** (the critical *lógos* of conjectural aim (*stokhastikē tékhnē*)) instead of claiming the title of a new autonomous "positive science"?

1. **Refusal of False *Akríbeia* and Disciplinary Inflation:** A positive science claims either an exclusive empirical domain or an invariant mechanical gauge (*metrētikē*). A standalone "science" annexing statistics, physics or ethics would repeat the positivist hubris it criticizes, taking formal models for a closed mirror of the real. Abandoning formal rigor would leave judgment to arbitrary knack (*empeiría*).
2. **A Narrower Claim: An Executable Circuit, Not a New Unit of Analysis:** The mediation circuit across world, archive, and institution is older than this book, and Stochology's distinctness cannot rest on having delimited it. Geoffrey Vickers had already described institutional regulation as the work of an appreciative system that judges a situation before it decides (Vickers 1965), and Beer, Simon, and the institutional economists argued the same indivisibility from other directions. What is claimed here is narrower and checkable. The circuit is formalized as a construction whose relations can be re-derived and checked against a fixed specification rather than asserted in prose: the three canonical operators, the seven guard sites and the thirty-five orders are defined as objects, and the definitions are held to their own stated consequences. And the program is argued rather than decorated: the three collapse propositions are formulated so that each one can fail, and the chapter that carries a claim also names the literature the claim stands on. Statistics examines the mapping from world to archive, decision theory analyses choice under given distributions, law and sociology study institutional norms. The claim to distinctness is a single object that holds the three planes together, not the observation that they belong together.
3. **Instrument vs. Empirical Discovery (Scope and Falsifiability):** Epistemic honesty requires separating what this book **argues analytically**, what it **reports from published empirical work**, and what remains an **open empirical hypothesis** requiring prospective institutional testing:
   - **Ordinal Box Invariance and Chart-Conditional Volume of Non-Substitutable Aggregators:** The nested Leontief minimum $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}})$ is a commitment to non-substitutability across the three planes ($\sigma \to 0^+$), evaluated strictly as a trajectory-level diagnostic rather than a per-step control law. Whether doubled physical reserve stock could partially compensate a weak axis in mild interior regimes without an active absorbing barrier is treated not as an assumed exception but as the explicit empirical target of pre-registered falsifier $F\_4$ in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), where a refutation under $F\_4$ would force a narrowing of the claim. Geometrically, the Leontief feasible region $\prod\_a [\tau\_a, 1]$ enjoys Stevens ordinal box invariance under any strictly increasing per-axis recoding $m\_j \leftrightarrow S\_\ast$, whereas Lebesgue-volume comparisons such as $V\_{\min}(\tau) = (1-\tau)^3$ versus $V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)$ hold conditionally within one declared chart and transform by the Jacobian $\det J\_\varphi$ under nonlinear recoding. Within that declared chart, the minimum differs from the arithmetic mean of the same three coordinates in the asymmetric corners, where the mean reports a pass while the minimum reports the bottleneck.
   - **Mechanism-Design Verification vs. External Empirical Validation:** A factorial stress test of present and absent axes is an argument about the instrument: it shows that the three operators and their failure modes are distinguished by the construction itself. It verifies internal coherence. Whether real institutions obey the triad requires prospective field evaluation under the four pre-registered severe falsifiers of Chapter 50. This book claims the first and invites the second.

The book develops **Stochology** across that circuit, one plane and one margin at a time.

## 1. Three Critical Lineages and the Nuclear Triad

Why do sophisticated statistical models, machine-learning pipelines, and bureaucratic metrics so often fail catastrophically once they are deployed in the real world? Take an everyday institutional scene. A hospital board or a municipal utility commission ends a quiet quarter by promoting a manager who trimmed physical reserves to the last per cent of utilization and booked an immediate cost saving, and by dismissing a colleague whose buffered reserve absorbed an unrecorded regional surge. Both evaluations inspect only the realized outcome (**skopós**). Neither inspects the mediation circuit that produced it. At the moment of decision three pieces of information are systematically missing across the planes the circuit traverses: in the **Archive**, the unrecorded households or the cluster
correlation hidden inside a point average; in the **World**, the arrival time of the next heavy-tailed
shock, or the thermodynamic clock that does not reverse once recovery time exceeds survival time; in
the **Institution**, whether a calm quarter was earned by competent aim (**télos**) or borrowed from
fortune by silencing frontline alarms, the $2 \times 2$ distinction between competence and luck
formalized by Ernest Sosa (Sosa 2007).

Three traditions diagnosed three distinct dimensions of this failure, and rarely spoke to one another:

1. **The Epistemological and Statistical Lineage** (Cromwell, Peirce, Keynes, Neyman, Wald, Popper, Lakatos, Mayo, Manski, Meng, Rubin, Pearl) showed that data never speak for themselves: samples suffer selection defects and cluster correlation, multiple hypotheses can fit the same finite archive, and point estimates routinely pass for certainties.
2. **The Physical, Biological, and Cybernetic Lineage** (Canguilhem, Wiener, Ashby, Shannon, Itô, Kelly, Kingman, Scarf, Georgescu-Roegen, Samuelson, Holling, Beer, Perdomo) showed that action takes place in irreversible entropic time: variance drains multiplicative growth, queues explode nonlinearly near full utilization, performative models alter the very world they predict, and living or institutional systems without physical slack collapse at absorbing ruin barriers.
3. **The Critical, Sociological, and Ethical Lineage** (Spinoza, Kant, Adorno, Horkheimer, Simon, Arendt, Weil, Bateson, Habermas, Girard, Desrosières, Porter, Hacking, Harding, Williams, Nagel, Sosa, Fricker, Holmström, List, Pettit, Chouldechova) showed that archives are institutional artifacts: categories exclude the vulnerable, high-powered proxy targets and double-bind traps corrupt unmeasured care, moral luck and scapegoating distort retrospective blame, and frontline warnings are silenced by testimonial injustice.

Read as three separate corrections, the lineages stay separate, which is how they were presented above. They also share a form, and the form is dialectical. Heraclitus stated it first: the attunement that holds a thing together is a tension of opposed pulls, back-turning, as of bow and lyre (Heraclitus c. 500 BCE, DK B51), and the same thing is present in the living and the dead, the waking and the sleeping, the young and the old (Heraclitus c. 500 BCE, DK B88). For Hegel the negative is not the absence of a determination but a determination of its own: what a shape of thought loses when it is negated is replaced by the specific distortion the loss produces (Hegel 1807). The collapse propositions below name a pathology, not a deficit. Marx turned that form on the institution, where a relation between persons comes to confront them as a thing with laws of its own (Marx 1867), and Lukács gave the inversion its name: reification (Lukács 1923). The three margins are internally related in Bertell Ollman's sense, each what it is only through the others, and the Leontief minimum is the arithmetic of that relation rather than a convention about aggregation (Ollman 1993). Dialectic is the motor of the framework in exactly this sense: a margin that succeeds at its own plane produces the conditions that corrupt another, and the triad records that movement instead of scoring it. The order of this book repeats, without claiming the title, what Ilienkov reconstructed in Marx: thought ascends from the abstract to the concrete, and the concrete is a unity of determinations rather than a heap of instances (Ilienkov 1960). The mathematics comes first here for the same reason it comes first there: it is the abstract moment that has to be secured before the circuit can be read as one thing.

The circuit also runs below the institutional scale. Long before bureaucratic metrics are reified
under Goodhart's Law, the impulse to annex another person's reception (**skopós**) through
mirror-surveillance rather than caring for the integrity of aim (**télos**) suppresses the singular
*Non-Identical* (Adorno) and erodes the dialogical "margin of tolerance for the inconsistencies of the
environment" (Canguilhem 1943/1966). Across the three interpersonal refusals $R\_1, R\_2, R\_3$ of Chapter 27, the Nuclear Triad and the three collapse propositions of Chapters 29 and 41, the trajectory syntax over seven guard sites of [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md), the termination of the uncertainty regress at the agent's own *télos*, declared as a stopping decision of the instrument and priced in width, the three regimes of action of Chapter 41, the $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ orders of Part V, and the political ontology of *potentia*, *multitudo* and immanent institutions in Chapter 20, Stochology folds a three-fold ethical refusal (no narcissistic control of intimate reception, no concealed architecture of betrayal and scapegoating, no opacity in the premises of a decision) into one **Nuclear Triad** of non-substitutable safeguards:

$$\mathcal{T}\_{\mathrm{Stochology}} = \bigl(\text{Epistemic Margin},\mkern5mu \text{Vital Margin},\mkern5mu \text{Institutional Governance}\bigr)$$

1. **Epistemic Margin (Chapters 43–45):** Never collapse underdetermination, selection bias, or cluster correlation into a dogmatic point estimate. Report partial-identification intervals $[L\_t, U\_t]$, the unified effective sample size $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ together with the defect bound it presumes, and Keynesian evidential weight $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}) = \frac{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}} + n\_0}$.
2. **Vital Margin (Chapters 46–47):** Never confuse the evaluation at the mean $\phi(\mathbb{E}[X])$ with the expected evaluation $\mathbb{E}[\phi(X)]$, nor ensemble averages with single-trajectory survival over irreversible time. Hold physical slack ($\rho < 1$, $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$) and shrink leverage against estimation error.
3. **Institutional Governance (Chapters 48–49):** Never judge an agent by retrospective outcome luck (*skopós*), and never govern a complex social system through high-powered single proxies or silenced frontline testimony. Separate ex-ante competence (*télos*) from ex-post fortune, protect the algedonic emergency channel ($S3^\ast$, zero status or credential discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ paired with corroborated likelihood-ratio sequential filtering), and align liability with structural decision power.

## 2. The Three Collapse Propositions

The three axes are complements in the strict sense of Leontief, so the aggregate is the minimum displayed in
[The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md).

Why fold the three chart-mapped safeguard coordinates $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}} \in [0, 1]$ (each a fixed monotone chart $S\_a = \varphi\_a(s\_a)$ of a native-unit axis margin, read diagnostically at the end of a trajectory) with a minimum rather than a weighted sum, or with the Constant Elasticity of Substitution (CES) mean $M\_{\mathrm{CES}}(\sigma) = \bigl(\frac{1}{3}(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}^\rho + S\_{\mathrm{vital}}^\rho + S\_{\mathrm{gov}}^\rho)\bigr)^{1/\rho}$ with $\rho = (\sigma - 1)/\sigma$? Each coordinate records survival capacity on a distinct, incommensurable plane of the circuit—so the Leontief product-box ordering is invariant under strictly increasing per-axis recodings $m\_j \leftrightarrow S\_\ast$, whereas compensatory means and chart volumes shift with the chart's Jacobian—and two shapes of the limit state the non-substitutability:

1. **A single safeguard at zero under an active barrier ($\sigma \to 0^+$ vs. $\sigma \to \infty$ as $S\_i \to 0^+$):** Let any one safeguard approach zero at an active ruin barrier while the other two stay high. The strict Leontief limit ($\sigma \to 0^+ \implies \rho \to -\infty$) yields $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} \to 0$ and flags structural collapse at once, while the linear arithmetic mean ($\sigma \to \infty \implies \rho = 1$) stays in the neighbourhood of two thirds of the surviving scores and misclassifies every single-axis ruin boundary state as safe. That is not a rounding difference: one reading reports a functioning institution at the exact moment the institution has lost the plane it must survive on.
2. **The same budget over a longer horizon:** Hold the budget and lengthen the horizon. The intact triad compounds its slack, because a buffered reserve is replenished faster than the shock that would exhaust it; the cell without the vital axis does not degrade gradually but crosses the absorbing barrier and stops, and a longer horizon does not improve its record, it truncates it. Improvement against termination: a sum cannot express that asymmetry and a minimum can.

Removing any single axis triggers a specific structural pathology (**Collapse Propositions 1–3**):

| **Proposition 1 (Degradation & Erosion)** | $\neg\text{Epistemic Margin}$ ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}} \to 0$) | **Blind Undersizing & Net Reserve Erosion:** Well-intentioned institutions size physical buffers against a false point certainty ($\bar{Y}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$), ignoring selection defects ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$) and cluster echoes ($\mathrm{DEFF}$) until surprises erode the reserve below its initial level, leaving coverage short on exactly the shifts the buffer was bought for. |
| **Proposition 2 (Absorbing Ruin)** | $\neg\text{Vital Margin}$ ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}} \to 0$) | **Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin:** The analyst computes the exact width of uncertainty and sees the crisis coming, yet operates at the last per cent of utilization or excess leverage with $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$, and is absorbed at the barrier. |
| **Proposition 3 (Absorbing Ruin)** | $\neg\text{Institutional Governance}$ ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}} \to 0$) | **Cynical Capture and Pillaging:** Epistemic intervals become an agnotological alibi while common vital reserves are pillaged under limited-liability moral hazard, proxy gaming and silenced frontline warnings, ending in ruin at the barrier. |

In the political layer of the argument ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)) this third pathology is traced to alienated *potestas* and sovereign command: capture the common vital reserves in centralized authority, and epistemic uncertainty becomes an agnotological pretext for inaction. Against transcendent sovereignty and naive assembleism alike, Stochology establishes an architecture of immanent institutions rooted in Spinoza's *potentia* and collective *conatus*, sustaining polycentric commons through horizontal pre-commitments, non-discounted frontline algedonic channels, and biophysical basin ceilings.

## 3. How to Read This Book: The Contiguous Nuclear Triad and Sequential Loop

The pedagogy here owes something to Reinforcement Learning (Sutton & Barto 2018), and the book avoids
encyclopedic catalogues. Its five parts climb from schoolroom intuition to the full
multilayer architecture of human judgment and action:

### 3.1 The Five Parts of the Treatise

1. **Part I — Thinking Under Uncertainty (Chapters 01–08):** Ordinary-language foundations, no advanced mathematics required: the three refusals, the umbrella threshold $p^\ast = C / (\eta L)$, the flaw of point averages, heavy-tailed surprises, outcome bias and moral luck, base-rate neglect, selective archive doors, Goodhart gaming, and why the minimum operator is not optional.
2. **Part II — The Bases (Chapters 09–26):** The rigorous tripartite foundation the modeling presupposes:
   - *Mathematics (Chapters 09–15):* Measure-theoretic probability, heavy tails, non-ergodic dynamics, convexity and Jensen gaps, partial identification, high-dimensional concentration, game-theoretic incentives.
   - *Philosophy and Epistemology (Chapters 16–20):* Fallibilism and inductive skepticism, agnotology, epistemic injustice and testimony discounting, performativity, the ethics of risk imposition.
   - *Natural Science (Chapters 21–26):* Deterministic chaos, thermodynamic entropy, dissipative structures, chemical buffering, evolutionary variation, physiological allostasis.
3. **Part III — Foundations of Judgment (Chapters 27–41):** The formal architecture of judgment under structural uncertainty, from the three planes of aim to the triadic minimum operator.
4. **Part IV — The Framework (Chapters 42–50):** The theory applied in sequence to one running case, *The Community and the Reserve*. An agent provisions a shared buffer for a community whose demand it observes only through an incomplete record, the world delivers shocks no record anticipates, and an institution audits the result afterwards. Each chapter develops one element of that loop (the Sequential Agent–World–Institution Loop, the Three Mediating Filters, the Three Canonical Operators, the Leontief synthesis operator) and argues it against the literature that established the mechanism: Cromwell's rule and the multiverse against framing, Manski and Lee's partial identification against underdetermination, the LaLonde question against selection, the harmonic law of effective sample size against the illusion of volume, Hill's index and Jensen's gap against the benign mean, Itô's drain and Kingman's queue against full utilization, Kelly and the survival clocks against leverage, Sosa's aptness, Holmström and Innes on liability against career luck, and Chouldechova's trilemma against the neutral score.
5. **Part V — The Multilayer Architecture of Knowledge and Action (Chapters 51–85):** The comprehensive 35-order synthesis, formalizing how knowledge and aim are stratified across the physical, biological, cognitive, social, and planetary domains.

### 3.2 The 35-Layer Multilayer Knowledge Graph ($\mathcal{G}\_1 \leftrightarrow \mathcal{G}\_2$)

The central theoretical contribution is a formal equivalence between the combinatorial

### 3.3 The Sequential Framework Arc (Chapters 42–50)

Part IV applies the theory to the running case. **Chapter 42** introduces the loop: an agent
provisions a shared reserve, the world delivers a shock it did not announce, the institution reads
the result afterwards. The naive optimizer that waits for point certainty is ruined before the shock
resolves; the agent that buffers before the outcome survives. **Chapters 43–45** develop
**Mediating Filter I · Axis 1 ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$: Epistemic Margin)** and **Canonical Operator I**:
first framing, Cromwell's rule, the multiverse and the severity of a claim; then partial identification
in the Manski–Lee tradition and Ville's *e*-processes over the LaLonde question, where a randomized
benchmark and an observational comparison answer differently and the difference is the point; then the
harmonic law of effective sample size, uniting Meng's Big Data Paradox, the Campbell–Moulton design
effect, Kish's weighting, Chao's lower bound on the unseen, and bitemporal vintages. **Chapters 46 and
47** develop **Mediating Filter II · Axis 2 ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$: Vital Margin)** through **Canonical
Operator II**: scale invariance, Jensen's convexity gap $\Delta\_J$ and Hill's heavy-tail index; then
Itô's variance drain, Baker–McHale epistemic Kelly shrinkage, Kingman's heavy-traffic queues,
Canguilhem–Holling resilience basins, the survival inequality $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge
\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$ and the Wald–Sniedovich safeguards. **Chapters 48 and 49** develop
**Mediating Filter III · Axis 3 ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$: Institutional Governance)** through **Canonical
Operator III**: Gate 1 ex-ante calibration with Murphy's decomposition and Invariant Causal Prediction,
the Kennedy–O'Hagan discrepancy, performative prediction and Goodhart's Law; then Gate 2, the real-time
algedonic bypass combining zero status discount ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) with corroborated
likelihood-ratio sequential filtering under the human throughput ceiling near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025) and the List–Pettit conjunctive veto; then Gate 3,
the ex-post audit: Sosa's aptness grade, Holmström–Innes liability against Simon–Girard scapegoating,
and Chouldechova's fairness trilemma. **Chapter 50** closes the **Synthesis
($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$)**: the Five Methodological Families, process tracing, the Four Internal
Goodhart Brakes, and the factorial evaluation of the Three Collapse Propositions.

Two boundary conditions keep the framework from becoming a slogan. First, an intact triad is necessary
against absorbing barriers but does not guarantee dominance everywhere: in benign or ultra-buffered
corners a simpler policy holds a cheaper reserve, and the book says so rather than letting the minimum
carry a claim it cannot support. Second, when an institution cannot observe the three axes separately,
the Leontief minimum is itself partially identified, and a fold that is only partially identified can be reported but cannot be acted on: the diagnosis of such a trajectory names a set of binding axes rather than one. The action is not restricted by the fold. It is restricted by the identification region of the epistemic axis, whose bounds may straddle the intervention cost; where they do, the per-step operator licenses only a reversible fractional probe, and the fold, computed afterwards, reports that the reading was **Boundary**.

### What the three axes cost when one is removed

Hold the budget fixed and remove one axis at a time from an intact triad. The three removals are not
of one kind. Losing the epistemic margin raises the harm of the same trajectory: the reserve is sized
against a point average and is eaten from below. Losing the vital margin does not raise the harm, it
ends the run: the trajectory stops at the barrier and every later shift is missing from the record.
Losing governance ends it earlier still, because the reserve is not merely unmeasured or unprovisioned
but deliberately consumed by a proxy that pays the agent and charges someone else.

That asymmetry is why the aggregate is a minimum and not an average. An average over the three
normalised scores reports the barrier event as a loss of a few tenths; the minimum reports it as what
it is. It is the distinction a production function makes between inputs that can be swapped and inputs
that cannot (Leontief 1941), and Parts IV and V rest on the second reading: on the same cell, a minimum
and an average do not differ by a margin, they disagree about whether the institution is still there.

Every claim above is answerable to a named source or a stated derivation, and every chapter that
follows names the argument it inherits rather than pointing at a layer below the page. What the book
does not do is promise prediction under structural uncertainty, treat a point estimate as a decision,
or accept a single axis as a proxy for the other two. The three collapse propositions name what
happens when one is dropped, and each is a failure that has occurred, not a hypothetical.
