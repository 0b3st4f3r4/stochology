## 1. Falsificationism and the Demarcation Criterion

Two advisory teams present economic risk frameworks to a central bank board. The first team offers an elastic narrative that calls a boom "liquidity confidence" and the next morning's crash an "endogenous sentiment reversal." No market number can ever prove it wrong. The second team registers a crisp prediction before the quarter opens: if interbank rate dispersion stays above a stated threshold for a stated run of days without a funding freeze, its contagion hypothesis fails.

Every scientific observation arrives already wrapped in expectations, instruments, and definitions—what philosophers call the **theory-ladenness of observation**. A thermometer reading presupposes thermal expansion; a bank stress score presupposes accounting categories. Because observation never speaks in a vacuum, Karl Popper (1934/1959) asked what separates genuine empirical science from unfalsifiable storytelling.

Popper drew his demarcation line on an ancient cross-cultural refusal to accept unexamined authority (Popper 1959). Long before modern European philosophy, the Pāli *Kālāma Sutta* rejected reliance on hearsay, lineage, or scripture in favor of what can be tested directly in practice—and checked against the wise—to avoid harm (Bodhi 2012). In eleventh-century Cairo, Ibn al-Haytham pushed this skeptical method further in *Al-Shukūk ʿalā Baṭlamyūs*, refusing blind deference to ancient masters and demanding logical proof plus demonstration (Sabra & Shehaby 1971). In *Kitāb al-Manāẓir* (*The Optics*), he forged controlled experimental variation (*iʿtibār*) into a public test anyone could repeat (Sabra 1989).

Popper noticed a sharp logical asymmetry: no finite stack of examples proves a universal law, yet one genuine counterexample deductively refutes it. Write $\Omega$ for the universe of possible observation statements. A hypothesis $H$ has empirical content if and only if it divides $\Omega$ into two non-empty parts:

1. The permitted set $\mathcal{C}(H) \subset \Omega$ of outcomes compatible with $H$;
2. The non-empty **forbidden set** $\mathcal{F}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(H) = \Omega \setminus \mathcal{C}(H) \ne \emptyset$ of potential falsifiers (subscripted to stay distinct from the measurable event algebra $\mathcal{F}$ of [Probability as a Measure](15-probability-as-a-measure.md)).

When $\mathcal{F}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(H)$ is empty, $H$ forbids nothing and predicts nothing; as the forbidden set grows, refutability and real content grow together (Popper 1959). Because probability statements cannot be disproved outright by a single lucky or unlucky draw, testing them demands a pre-committed **rejection rule** that forbids after-the-fact excuses—the machinery developed in §4.

## 2. The Duhem–Quine Thesis and Holistic Underdetermination

Picture a bank risk desk whose Value-at-Risk model breaches its loss limit three times in one month (observation $\neg O$). Management does not abandon its core Gaussian bell-curve assumption ($H$). Instead, it blames an "overnight data-feed lag" ($A\_1$), a "holiday calendar artifact" ($A\_2$), and a "temporary dealer shutdown" ($A\_3$). The core model stays running on the server because the web of supporting assumptions absorbed the shock.

Pierre Duhem (1906) and Willard Van Orman Quine (1951) showed why this loophole is built into empirical testing. A core hypothesis $H$ never meets the world alone; any observable prediction $O$ follows from $H$ joined with auxiliary assumptions $A\_1, \dots, A\_k$ about instruments, calibrations, data pipelines, and conditions:

$$H \land A\_1 \land A\_2 \land \cdots \land A\_k \implies O.$$

Read aloud, the core idea plus every background assumption together imply prediction $O$. Therefore, when prediction $O$ fails ($\neg O$), deductive logic tells us only that at least one piece in the bundle failed:

$$\neg (H \land A\_1 \land \cdots \land A\_k) \iff \neg H \lor \neg A\_1 \lor \cdots \lor \neg A\_k.$$

Read aloud, the failure of the prediction means either the core hypothesis $H$ is false, or auxiliary assumption $A\_1$ failed, or another helper assumption failed. Duhem (1906) argued that physics tests an experimental whole (*tout un ensemble*) whose real culprit is localized only by disciplined scientific judgment (*bon sens*). Quine (1951) widened holism across our entire web of belief, showing that radical revisions to background assumptions can rescue almost any favored claim.

Thomas Kuhn's account of scientific **paradigms** adds a deeper layer to this holism. Inside an established paradigm, scientists share a language, instrument standards, and a set of accepted puzzles; rival paradigms do not merely disagree on parameters, they can reshape the observation vocabulary $\Omega$ itself. Institutions routinely exploit both leeway points—blaming pipeline quirks or shifting definitions—to shield failing policies from refutation.

Two empirical disciplines check that evasion without denying holism. First, **Invariant Causal Prediction (ICP)** (Peters, Bühlmann & Meinshausen 2016) screens candidate cause sets $S$ across multiple distinct environments $e \in \mathcal{E}$ (here $e$ indexes environments, distinct from the electron charge below). Absent hidden confounding or direct interference with target $Y$, the conditional relationship $Y^{(e)} \mid X\_{\mathbf{PA}(Y)}^{(e)}$ keeps both its slope and its residual noise variance invariant across settings. Intersecting all surviving subsets $\hat{S}^\ast = \bigcap\_{S \in \mathcal{A}} S$ screens out fragile spurious correlates. Yet ICP still leans on auxiliary premises: violated assumptions can reject every subset ($\mathcal{A} = \emptyset$) without naming which assumption broke, while low statistical power can pass the empty set ($\emptyset \in \mathcal{A}$) and collapse $\hat{S}^\ast = \emptyset$ unless flagged ([Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md)).

Second, precision metrology isolates suspect auxiliaries through independent measurement. In his oil-drop experiment, Robert Millikan (1913) coupled six ingredients: density difference, electric field strength, air viscosity $\eta$, terminal speeds $v\_1, v\_2$, droplet radius $a$, and the Stokes slip constant $A$. He showed $\eta$ enters at the $3/2$ power, revised $\eta\_{23}$ from $0.0001825$ to $0.0001824$, and reported elementary charge $e = (4.774 \pm 0.009) \times 10^{-10}\text{ electrostatic units}$ (Millikan 1913). Under the CGS-Gaussian electrostatic-unit convention $1\text{ C} = (c\_{\mathrm{CGS}}/10)\text{ esu} = 2.99792458 \times 10^9\text{ esu}$ combined with exact SI constants $c = 299{,}792{,}458\text{ m/s}$ and $e = 1.602176634 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$ (BIPM 2019; negative for the electron), the elementary charge is approximately $4.8032 \times 10^{-10}\text{ esu}$. Later independent measurements of air viscosity—not endless philosophical standoff—closed that gap while confirming charge quantization.

## 3. Lakatosian Research Programmes, Ad-Hoc Contraction, and Counter-Induction

Picture a forecasting unit that greets every quarterly miss by bolting on a new after-the-fact excuse: a "weather shock" in Q1, "port congestion" in Q2, and a "sentiment regime" in Q3. Each patch restores a spotless historical fit while predicting nothing new about next quarter. Imre Lakatos (1970) modeled this tension around **research programmes**: a central **hard core** shielded from immediate rejection, surrounded by an adjustable **protective belt** of auxiliary assumptions.

Lakatos divided scientific change into progressive and degenerating tracks (Lakatos 1970). A programme is **theoretically progressive** when each modification predicts novel, unexpected facts, and **empirically progressive** only when some of those novel predictions are confirmed. Otherwise it is **degenerating**—patchwork chasing yesterday's errors. Still, Lakatos warned against killing young programmes instantly before a stronger rival exists. Paul Feyerabend (1970) pressed the weak spot: without a clock, unlimited patience (*"how long are we supposed to wait?"*) excuses anything, yet a rigid rule kills **counter-induction**—bold new ideas that clash with old standards before their instruments mature.

To keep revision honest without pretending Lakatos wrote an equation, Stochology disciplines belt adjustments with a **pre-registered ceiling** $K\_{\max} \in \mathbb{N}\_0$ across a fixed family of $K\_{\max} + 1$ pre-specified variants—or strictly across future out-of-sample increments after an attested shift—applied to terminal sequential *e*-value evidence $E\_T \ge 0$ ($\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[E\_T] \le 1$; [Probability as a Measure](15-probability-as-a-measure.md)):

$$R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}(E\_T, K\_{\max}) = \frac{E\_T}{K\_{\max} + 1}, \qquad \Delta W\_{\mathrm{dB}}(K\_{\max}) = -10\log\_{10}(K\_{\max} + 1) \quad (E\_T > 0).$$

Read aloud, $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}$ divides the test *e*-value uniformly across $K\_{\max} + 1$ pre-specified variants—baseline plus allowed adjustments—while $\Delta W\_{\mathrm{dB}}$ converts that penalty to decibels for $E\_T > 0$. Fixing $K\_{\max} + 1$ upfront preserves Ville's bound, whereas picking adjustments *post hoc* inside $[0, T]$ or dividing on the fly by $k + 1$ yields harmonic sums $\sum\_{k=0}^{K\_{\max}} (k + 1)^{-1} > 1$ for $K\_{\max} \ge 1$ (diverging as $K\_{\max} \to \infty$) that break the union-bound Type-I guarantee ([Chapter 17](17-justification-and-induction.md); [Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md)). This fixed cap stops *ad-hoc* rescuing inside an old frame without blocking genuine replacement below.

Writing $\mathcal{F}\_t$ for the event algebra at step $t$ (distinct from §1's $\mathcal{F}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(H)$), $K\_{\max}$ blocks **opportunistic contraction** ($\mathcal{F}\_{t+1} \subset \mathcal{F}\_t$). When environmental drift (*infidélité du milieu*; Canguilhem 1943/1966) or counter-induction (Feyerabend 1970) breaks a frame outright, Part III–IV protocols trigger **attested reopening** ($\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$; [The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)) under [Chapter 17](17-justification-and-induction.md)'s append-only log, testing new claims strictly out of sample ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md); [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)).

## 4. Deborah Mayo's Error-Statistical Severe Testing

Compare two audits of a coastal storm-surge model. The first tunes dozens of knobs on past tide-gauge records until historical error drops below tolerance, then declares the model "validated." The second locks every parameter before hurricane season opens and tests predictions against a pre-set failure threshold where error cannot hide.

Deborah Mayo built this contrast into **error-statistical severe testing** (Mayo 1996; Mayo 2018). Data $\mathbf{x}\_0$ strongly corroborate hypothesis $H$ only if $\mathbf{x}\_0$ fits $H$ *and*, with high probability, the procedure would have yielded an outcome **more discordant** with $H$ had $H$ been false (Mayo 2018). Passing an easy test earns no credit. Post-data severity also requires verified **statistical adequacy** via misspecification checks (Spanos 2021; [Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md)), because violated independence or distributional assumptions invalidate nominal error rates (Spanos 2021; [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)).

Under verified adequacy and unscreened reporting, severity asks whether a test separates danger from safety above minimum relevant effect $\delta\_{\min} > 0$, feeding [Inference and Evidence](35-inference-and-evidence.md) and [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md).

## 5. Epistemic Injustice, Testimonial Triage, and Inductive Risk

Science and oversight operate inside institutions whose incentives often reward flashy, underpowered novelty (Smaldino & McElreath 2016) and reflect entrenched social hierarchy (Bourdieu 2004). Before the *Challenger* space shuttle disaster of 28 January 1986, sociologist Diane Vaughan (1996) traced how repeated O-ring erosion warnings became routine through the **normalization of deviance**. On the eve-of-launch teleconference of 27 January 1986, Morton Thiokol manager Jerry Mason instructed engineering vice-president Bob Lund to *"take off his engineering hat and put on his management hat."* That reversal forced engineers to prove launch was unsafe *"beyond a shadow of a doubt"* (Presidential Commission on the Space Shuttle Challenger Accident 1986; Vaughan 1996; [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)).

Miranda Fricker (2007) named two systemic wrongs behind such silencing, later tied to institutional **inferential inertia** (Fricker 2020):

- **Testimonial injustice**: discounting a speaker's warning because of rank, role, or identity prejudice.
- **Hermeneutical injustice**: lacking shared concepts or archival categories—leaving harms unaskable or unrecorded in $\mathcal{F}\_t$—so affected people cannot even state their injury in official language.

Scientific objectivity relies on **transformative criticism** across open forums, public standards, responsiveness, and tempered intellectual equality (Longino 1990; [Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md)). Discounting frontline testimony blinds an organization's earliest sensors and stretches detection past safe survival time—problems countered in [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) and [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) by evaluating warnings regardless of rank through corroborated triage inside human attention limits.

### When no test can be severe

When an archive lacks effective sample size or fails adequacy checks, the honest epistemic verdict is `"not testable"` (*non-informative*), barring both corroboration and refutation until better data are gathered ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)). Yet epistemic restraint never licenses reckless inaction when danger is asymmetric or irreversible. Under **inductive risk** (Rudner 1953; Douglas 2000; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), the gravity of possible error sets how much evidence we demand before certifying safety, without distorting evidence itself. That prevents doubt-manufacturing excuses from shifting irreversible harm onto external reception perimeters ([Chapter 17](17-justification-and-induction.md)), while keeping live action thresholds distinct from retrospective diagnostics ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)).
