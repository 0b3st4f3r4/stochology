## 1. Métron and stókhos

Consider a municipal water and emergency-reserve committee opening an operating season with an initial physical stock $R\_0 > 0$. Before each shift's demand and storm surge arrive, the committee must lock in its water release. Release too much, and a sudden spike pushes the tank to empty, an irreversible ruin barrier ($R\_t \le 0$). Hoard too tightly, and communities suffer immediate shortage. Improving the flow meter never turns that choice into a simple measurement problem, because the decision must be sealed before the shift's demand occurs.

Classical Greek marks this divide with two words where English uses one. *Métron* is measurement: a rod's length, a weight on a scale, or a census count. Its defining virtue is *akríbeia*, exactness. Crafts built on *métron*, such as land-surveying, weighing, and arithmetic, check their claims against public instruments.

*Stókhos*, by contrast, is the archer's target and the act of aiming at it. An archer does not measure a shifting crosswind with a gauge. She reads and estimates it through embodied judgment inseparable from the shot. Heraclitus made that bow the emblem of dynamic tension: *palíntropos harmoníē* (recorded in Plutarch and Porphyry as *palíntonos*, "back-stretched"), the back-turning attunement of bow and lyre (Heraclitus c. 500 BCE, DK B51). The string works only because opposing ends pull against each other, and the arrow flies out of that tension rather than its removal.

Plato sorted crafts by their share of counting, weighing, and measuring in the *Philebus* (55d–56d; Plato c. 360 BCE). Music ranks lowest on precision (56a). Medicine, agriculture, navigation, and strategy rely on conjecture (*stokhastikē*) and practiced experience (*empeiría*). Above them stands carpentry (*tektonikē*, 56b–c), whose straightedge, compass, and plumb line give greater exactness (*akríbeia*), while pure arithmetic holds the top rank (56d).

Hippocratic physicians rejected that dismissal and built clinical rigor around three conjectural terms: *sēmeîa* (diagnostic signs), *kairós* (the fleeting, unrepeatable moment when delay is fatal), and *mētis* (adaptive practical intelligence; Detienne & Vernant 1974; Hippocrates c. 400 BCE). *On Ancient Medicine* (ch. 1 and 9) argues that clinical conjecture needs bodily discernment beyond external scales. *Prognostic* (ch. 1) binds past, present, and future signs together, while *Aphorisms* (I.1) warns that *kairós* slips away quickly and judgment is hard.

Later thinkers turned that practice into a formal class of conjectural arts (*stokhastikaí*). A medical treatise preserved under Galen's name defines them as arts that aim at a mark and succeed for the most part, not always (Galen c. 200 CE). Alexander of Aphrodisias explains why: because chance intervenes, fixed mechanical rules fail, so the practitioner's duty is to fulfill every requirement of the craft rather than guarantee the hit (Alexander of Aphrodisias c. 200 CE). Galen's clinical works place reasoned technical conjecture (*stochasmós technikós*) cleanly between exact certainty and mere ignorance.

As James Allen shows, the Medical Empiricists broke "the stranglehold of the for-the-most-part" by grading evidential links across explicit frequency tiers, so a single unlucky failure never discredits sound clinical craft (Allen 2001). When Jacob Bernoulli named probability estimation *sive Stochastice* in Part IV, Chapter 2 of *Ars Conjectandi* (Bernoulli 1713), he claimed this exact lineage: aiming rigorously when certainty is unavailable.

Stochology breaks with Plato's hierarchy. Conjectural arts are not broken measuring tools waiting for sharper sensors. They answer a different question: not *what is this number*, but *what should an accountable agent do now, before the outcome arrives, when someone must answer for the result*. A predictive algorithm runs *métron* over stored records without timing pressure, ethical aim, or personal liability; institutional deception begins when organizations confuse that compressed calculation with judgment.

### A planetary invariant under a Greek name

Calling this discipline *stochology* establishes a compact vocabulary without claiming a Greek monopoly on its insight. Across independent traditions, practitioners facing irreversible action converged on the same three safeguards.

On the **epistemic safeguard**, Ptahhotep's Old Kingdom *Instruction* (preserved in the Prisse Papyrus) refused perfection to any closed craft and noted that wise speech may sit among maids at the grindstones (Lichtheim 1973). The Pāli *Kālāma Sutta* barred trusting claims merely by oral lineage, rumor, or textual prestige (Bodhi 2012). In *Al-Shukūk ʿalā Baṭlamyūs*, Ibn al-Haytham urged truth-seekers to question inherited authority and demand demonstration (Sabra & Shehaby 1971), pairing that critique with controlled empirical testing (*iʿtibār*) in *The Optics* (*Kitāb al-Manāẓir*; Sabra 1989). Alongside them, classical Jaina logicians indexed claims to explicit standpoints via *syādvāda* (Matilal 1981), and Brazilian paraconsistent logic proved formally that localized premise conflict neither authorizes concluding arbitrary absurdities nor silences inquiry (da Costa 1974; da Costa 1980). Long earlier, Mesopotamian collections of just verdicts (*dīnāt mīšarim*) recorded concrete casuistic judgments without hypostatizing an autonomous deductive code above lived practice (Bottéro 1992).

On the **vital and governance safeguards**, Egyptian surgical triage sorted wounds into three distinct regimes: treatable, to be contended with under uncertainty, or withheld from futile intervention (Breasted 1930). Sunzi separated self-secured non-defeat (*bùkěshèng*) from outcomes left to an adversary's mercy (Griffith 1963). Nahuatl *Huehuetlahtolli* compared moral action to keeping balance on slippery earth, *tlalticpac* (Anderson & Dibble 1969; Burkhart 1989). Meanwhile, the *Bhagavad Gītā* separated disciplined duty from claim over its external fruits (Radhakrishnan 1948). Akan *Sankofa* protected non-punitive recovery of overlooked truth, while Andean *Qhipnayra* and Māori *Whakataukī* steered forward decisions by inspecting the visible past rather than guessing blindly (Núñez & Sweetser 2006; Rivera Cusicanqui 2012; Rameka 2016).

Music, Plato's very first example in *Philebus* 56a (Plato c. 360 BCE), shows concretely why rigid formulas crack without judgment slack. In Pythagorean tuning, stacking twelve pure musical fifths (each multiplying pitch by $3/2$) never lands back on seven octaves (powers of $2$). The two stacks miss each other by a small physical gap called the **Pythagorean comma**:

$$\frac{(3/2)^{12}}{2^7} = \frac{531441}{524288} \neq 1,$$

which amounts to $1200\log\_2(531441/524288)$ cents. In plain words, forcing exact integer ratios everywhere traps that leftover discrepancy in one unbearable, howling "wolf fifth," shattering harmony whenever a piece changes key.

In Ming China, Zhu Zaiyu solved this mismatch independently in *Lüxue Xinshuo* by computing the equal-temperament ratio $2^{1/12}$, over a century before Andreas Werckmeister's European keyboard temperaments (Cho 2003; Werckmeister 1691). Tempered instruments survive modulation by distributing small, survivable slack across every interval. Immanuel Kant contrasted *determinative judgment* (applying an existing rule mechanically) with *reflective judgment*, which finds an apt rule for an unprecedented case (Kant 1790). Theodor Adorno warned against forcing singular reality into rigid categories that amputate the *Non-Identical* (*das Nichtidentische*; Adorno 1966). A naive optimizer pretends the comma is zero and shatters at the first regime shift; an agent holding protective margins endures.

## 2. Three sources of structural uncertainty

Uncertainty is often treated like temporary ignorance that shrinks automatically with larger datasets and faster chips. Three structural barriers defeat that hope.

Frank Knight drew the enduring boundary between risk (outcomes sortable into stable classes and priced) and structural uncertainty, which resists classification and explains why genuine enterprise faces uninsurable hazards (Knight 1921). No pile of observations creates a missing category. The epistemic margin measures that remaining structural width after data have spoken.

**The world is opaque.** Real social and physical systems are not stationary draws from a fixed urn. They shift while observed, generate unprecedented crises, and depend on variables missing from our schemas. Past patterns bind tomorrow only under a stability that major disruptions break. Honest estimation therefore yields an interval, often wide enough to show that the data leave the choice open.

**The archive is silent.** Recorded tables never equal lived reality. Institutions build archives under tight budgets, rigid categories, and reporting thresholds below which events vanish. The patient who never reached a clinic is not a random gap to impute, and an excluded category cannot be inferred from existing columns. Adding more rows under a biased intake filter only hardens the wrong answer.

**The institution is reactive.** Once a number controls rewards or penalties, people adapt around it. Agents optimize the score until the score breaks its link to the real goal. This happens without bad faith: turning an indicator into a target alters the system it tracks, and static historical records cannot neutralize that loop.

Each barrier is a structural property of the world–archive–institution circuit, not an analyst's laziness. Standard Gaussian error bars work decently in calm, stationary settings and fail disastrously anywhere else.

Each source also links directly to a quantitative tool that sizes uncertainty before physical capacity is committed:

1. **Opaque world $\mapsto$ Partial-identification bounds:** When confounding or selective dropout blocks a single answer, worst-case Fréchet–Manski bounds $[L\_{\mathrm{ATE}}, U\_{\mathrm{ATE}}]$ replace the point estimate with an explicit width $\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}} = U\_{\mathrm{ATE}} - L\_{\mathrm{ATE}} \ge 0$ (Manski 2003; Imbens & Manski 2004). As [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md) demonstrates on a classic job-training benchmark, naive observational comparisons can even flip the sign of an experimental effect; honest bounds price remaining uncertainty without fragile statistical overlap assumptions.
2. **Silent archive $\mapsto$ Selection and cluster deflation:** Nominal table size $n$ shrinks sharply under three corrections formalized later in [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md): inclusion correlation between whether a unit gets recorded ($R \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$, denoting Meng's recording indicator rather than §1's physical reserve $R\_t$) and the outcome ($\rho\_{R,Y} \in [-1, 1]$; Meng 2018), cluster grouping ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}}$; Moulton 1986), and unequal survey weights ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$; Kish 1965). When unit-level weights vary independently of clusters in the large-cluster limit, **the exact composition is additive** ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - 1$) rather than multiplicative. Combining $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}$ with a selection bound $|\rho\_{R,Y}| \le \bar{\rho}$ compresses nominal rows into effective count $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$, feeding John Maynard Keynes's evidential weight $V\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}), n\_0\bigr)$ against a declared reference scale $n\_0 > 0$ (formalized in [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md)) so weight tracks genuine design coverage rather than raw file size (Keynes 1921).
3. **Reactive institution $\mapsto$ Goodhart and performativity checks:** When a recorded indicator becomes a high-powered target, performative drift separates into four mechanisms (regressional, extremal, causal, and adversarial), requiring explicit institutional audits alongside statistical modeling (Goodhart 1975; Perdomo et al. 2020).

## 3. Acting before the outcome

Consequential judgment carries a strict time asymmetry: the action locks in before the result that tests it can arrive. The arrow leaves the bow; only during flight does the crosswind hit.

Antipater of Tarsus split internal craft (*télos*, doing everything within one's art continuously) from the external hit (*skopós*), a division Cicero echoed with his archer simile and Carneades mocked as indifferent to hitting the mark (Stobaeus 1884, *Ecl.* II.76–77, contrasted with the Peripatetic hitting-the-mark foil at II.47.7–11; Cicero 45 BCE, *De Finibus* III.22; Plutarch c. 100 CE, *Comm. not.* 1070f–1071e). Stochology answers Carneades by anchoring *télos* in transparent, pre-registered craft open to challenge from affected communities, a split formalized alongside virtue grading in [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md).

Hindsight therefore misreads quality. Sound aim can miss under a sudden shock, while reckless bets sometimes land by luck. Any system rewarding only hits (*skopós*) selects operators who hide fragility and dump losses onto others.

Athenian tragedy dramatized the three distinct ways human aim collapses long before probability existed (Aeschylus 458 BCE; Sophocles c. 441/429, 409 BCE):

1. *Blind Undersizing* (missing epistemic margin): Oedipus in *Oedipus Tyrannus* (c. 429 BCE) pursues an investigation with total procedural rigor while remaining blind to his own place inside the frame.
2. *Impotent Lucidity* (missing vital margin): Creon in Sophocles's *Antigone* reaches clear moral insight only after his order leaves less time to save a life than recovery requires.
3. *Cynical Capture* (missing institutional governance): Cassandra in Aeschylus's *Agamemnon* (ll. 1202–1213, 1240–1255) speaks exact truth under prophetic discredit, while Creon dismisses warnings from the Sentry and Tiresias as bribed lies. Meanwhile, Neoptolemus in *Philoctetes* faces an indivisible binary bind ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$) where dropping his bow refuses coercion yet passive withdrawal abandons a comrade. Reading Aristotle's *anagnórisis* as a structural turn from ignorance to recognition (*Poetics* 1452a29–33; Aristotle c. 335 BCE), Stochology shows where private refusal must become public contestation of the rules ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)).

Because one missing pillar ruins the entire act, these three safeguards combine through a non-compensatory minimum rather than a forgiving average. That bottleneck is formalized across the mediation circuit in [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) and synthesized in [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), and is never used as an online optimization target.

## 4. What this programme refuses

Three methodological refusals define this framework, and none can be traded away.

The first is **a single scalar good**. Any published composite index tied to rewards gets gamed until it breaks. The triadic minimum therefore acts strictly as an after-the-fact diagnostic, never a control rule or bonus formula.

The second is **a point forecast posing as knowledge**. A bare estimate stripped of identification bounds, sampling context, and model discrepancy is an institutional fiction. Whenever the record supports only an interval, that interval is the honest finding.

The third is **a verdict built on hidden premises**. An operation whose scope, reserves, and evidence rules were never declared upfront becomes unreadable after the fact. Withholding post-hoc credit blocks false praise without paralyzing physical emergency stops. Nor does pre-registration freeze inquiry blindly: drawing on Georges Canguilhem's vital normativity (Canguilhem 1943/1966) and reflective judgment (Kant 1790; Adorno 1966), genuine anomalies reopen framing explicitly (*anagnórisis*) while forbidding silent hindsight rewrites.

These three methodological refusals rest on the three ethical and interpersonal refusals of the [Prologue](00-preface.md): renouncing coercive control over another's reception, exposing pact betrayal and scapegoating, and refusing to turn organizational influence into covert manipulation. [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) maps them across the mediation circuit, while [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) and [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) bound them by the independent capacity of affected communities to absorb harm. To separate sound craft (*télos*) from hindsight (§3), accountability requires pre-declared criteria and blind scoring before metrics tie to rewards. Tying pay to first-measured targets, exemplified in the 1990s British Research Assessment Exercise by slicing work into minimum publishable units under the audit-gaming dynamics Marilyn Strathern (1997) and Goodhart (1975) diagnosed, degrades the measure itself, while Michael Power (1997) showed ritual audits arrive too late to alter the act.

Rejecting a single scalar target does not excuse opaque complexity. In small, noisy, or shifting samples, simple rules often beat elaborate models out of sample. Decomposing prediction error into bias, variance, and noise, Henry Brighton and Gerd Gigerenzer show that adding parameters lowers bias only by inflating variance when samples are thin (Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012). Across 20 empirical inference studies, lexicographic take-the-best checked 2.4 cues on average against 7.7 for tallying and multiple regression while beating regression out of sample. Similarly, equal $1/N$ allocation ranked first on certainty-equivalent returns out of 15 rules across 7 investment problems over 10-year rolling windows (Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012), demonstrating ecological rationality beyond idealized small worlds (Neth & Gigerenzer 2015).

Andrew Haldane and Vasileios Madouros documented the same reversal in banking (Haldane & Madouros 2012; detailed in [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §3), where simple leverage rules outperformed complex risk weights for large global banks. Under tight timing pressure (*kairós*), frugal stopping rules protect bounded human attention from both overfitting and cognitive overload.

Still, simple heuristics work only within clear boundaries rather than universally. Unchecked intuition breeds base-rate neglect: subjects give nearly identical guesses across 70-to-30 and 30-to-70 priors even though Bayes demands a $(0.7/0.3)^2$, or 5.44, posterior-odds ratio (Tversky & Kahneman 1974). It also suffers elicitation distortion, where events assigned extreme probability guesses of $0.10$ actually occurred 24 percent of the time, whereas events assigned odds-based guesses averaging $0.34$ occurred 26 percent of the time (Tversky & Kahneman 1974). Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein restrict valid intuition to high-validity environments backed by prolonged practice and rapid, unmistakable feedback (Kahneman & Klein 2009); otherwise, simple cues decay toward guessing (Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012).

Where base rates and outcome feedback hold, structured calibration excels. In IARPA forecasting tournaments, roughly 45 minutes of debiasing training and tracking the top 2 percent (60 superforecasters in 5 teams of 12) resisted regression toward the mean (Mellers et al. 2014). Across Years 2–3, they achieved standardized Brier scores of $-0.34$ (calibration $0.01$, AUC $96\%$) versus $-0.14$ for top-team individuals and $0.04$ across 1,498 peers (Mellers et al. 2015). Frugal heuristics win when small samples starve variance estimation, then yield to calibrated updating ([Chapter 30](30-probability-as-language.md)) once frequencies stabilize, holding only while frames avoid base-rate omissions, survival buffers bound risk, and incentives do not arbitrage the metric ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); Haldane & Madouros 2012).

### When margin is not owed

Run the same optimizer inside a small, reversible game and it wins with its reserve intact. Irreversibility is the premise of this discipline, not its conclusion.

Imagine testing all eight combinations of present or missing safeguards on the binary corner cube $\lbrace 0, 1\rbrace^3$. An arithmetic average and a weakest-link minimum agree only when all three safeguards sit at $(0, 0, 0)$ or $(1, 1, 1)$. At all six mixed corners, removing one safeguard drops the minimum to zero while the average still reports partial safety. In trajectory terms, once reserves hit zero, active shifts stop, meaning no cell missing survival headroom can stay viable longer than one keeping it. That contrast exposes single-axis failure; it does not estimate an empirical effect size.

### When the horizon is short and repeated

Precautionary reserves are owed whenever actions are irreversible or approach an absorbing ruin boundary. When operations remain genuinely reversible on a single plane (with comfortable survival headroom, zero incentive capture, and verified protection against spilling harm onto unconsenting outsiders, as formalized across [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) and [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)), expected-value criteria and reversible small probes replace retained stock, without ever turning the retrospective minimum into a weighted sum or live control rule.
