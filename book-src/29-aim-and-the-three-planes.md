## 1. Three planes, one circuit

Picture a rural health district during an outbreak week. In one valley clinic, a nurse records a sharp cluster of acute febrile patients. Down in the villages, viral transmission and patient dehydration advance minute by minute in continuous, irreversible physical time. Yet only some cases ever reach the clinic log: washed-out roads, limited opening hours, household clustering, and diagnostic test stockouts keep others out of sight. Meanwhile, at the district office, a procurement board holds a fixed emergency reserve $R\_0$ and must decide before the last supply truck leaves on Friday how many fluid kits to dispatch.

Watch how the decision breaks if any single link fails. If the clinic log misses an unrecorded household cluster, the district under-sizes its dispatch. If a slick forecast summarizes delayed records as a crisp point number, the board treats guesswork as certainty. If the committee dismisses the nurse's urgent radio warning because of her junior rank, trucks roll out too late. Real-world biology, partial records, and institutional authority do not sit in separate silos; they form one coupled loop.

To analyze that loop clearly, Stochology unfolds every act of consequential judgment across **three distinct planes**:

1. **The world plane ($P\_1$):** Physical and social reality—raw storm loads, material wear, viral dehydration, and market liquidity shocks. It runs in irreversible continuous time and never reads our spreadsheets.
2. **The archive plane ($P\_2$):** The structured ledger of what was sensed, entered, cleaned, and modeled—clinic charts, telemetry tables, fitted models, and uncertainty bounds. It is always thinner than the world, keeping only what budgets, sensors, and schemas allow inside.
3. **The institution plane ($P\_3$):** Deliberation, decision authority, and accountability—who signs off on dispatch, with what reserve stock, at whose physical risk, and under whose liability when outcomes unfold.

These three planes form **one coupled mediation circuit**, never a frictionless pipe. Translation across different clocks, vocabularies, and incentives always carries friction. That indivisibility is inherited rather than newly invented. Centuries before modern systems engineering, Mohist philosophy tested every public policy against its Three Standards (*Sānbiǎo*): anchor in historical archive precedent (*běn*), check against observed fact in the world (*yuán*), and verify practical benefit to ordinary people (*yòng*; Johnston 2010). Geoffrey Vickers framed institutional regulation as appreciating an entire situation before deciding any part (Vickers 1965), while Stafford Beer (1972) and Herbert Simon (1997 [1947]) argued the same indivisibility from viable systems and bounded rationality. Stochology turns that qualitative insight into checkable guard sites.

Cybernetics gives that closed loop an exact baseline. Under W. Ross Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety ([Chapter 25, *Evolution, Variation, and Selection*](25-evolution-variation-selection.md)), when disturbance options face fixed regulator moves without outcome repetition, output variety satisfies $\mathcal{V}\_O \ge \mathcal{V}\_D - \mathcal{V}\_R$ (distinct from phenotypic variance $V\_P$ and evidential weight $V$): only regulator variety absorbs external disturbance (Ashby 1956). Extending that bound, Roger Conant and Ashby prove that the simplest optimal regulator $R$ maps system states via $h : S \to R$ (Conant & Ashby 1970). Without an accurate internal model, regulation wastes variety; under drifting conditions $p(S)$, the map $h$ must adapt across locally stationary windows, while pure error-correction cannot drive outcome entropy $H(Z)$ to zero without anticipatory buffering. Because individual transducer bandwidth is limited, complex systems require organized teams rather than lone omniscient planners (Ashby 1958). Yet Conant and Ashby assume a benign regulator aimed at system stability—whereas real institutions on $P\_3$ often shift losses onto $P\_1$—so Stochology binds requisite variety to the ethical refusals $R\_1$–$R\_3$ unveiled in §4.

Safety engineering exposes the exact same cross-plane failures. Nancy Leveson states four requirements for safe control—goal, action, process model, and observability—summarized in one stark ceiling: "no control system will perform better than its measuring channel" (Leveson 2011). Accidents occur even when zero hardware parts snap, whenever the controller's internal model diverges from reality through bad design assumptions, missing feedback, flawed updates, or time lags. Complementing that anatomy, Erik Hollnagel, Robert Wears, and Jeffrey Braithwaite show why standard Safety-I audits miss daily survival (Hollnagel, Wears & Braithwaite 2015). By chasing rare failures (such as a 1 in 10,000 collapse, or adverse rates up to 10 percent among hospitalized patients), Safety-I ignores the 9,999 out of 10,000 everyday adjustments frontline staff make—leaving prescribed *Work-As-Imagined* detached from actual *Work-As-Done*.

Across three distinct planes, there are six ordered crossings between distinct planes plus one overall closure of the entire loop. That fixes $3 \cdot 2 + 1 = 7$ boundary crossings where the circuit can tear or mislead. Before reading the circuit diagram, walk through all **seven guard sites** in our rural health district:

**Three direct loop transits** move information and action forward around the circuit:

- **Observe (`T3`: $P\_1 \to P\_2$, *Observation and Transduction*):** Physical sickness in the valley becomes clinic records under screening rules, test availability, and measurement noise. If stockouts turn sick villagers away unlogged, the archive undercounts the surge.
- **Formalize (`T4`: $P\_2 \to P\_3$, *Scale, Curvature, and Tail Representation*):** Raw archive rows become summary tables for the committee without violating ordinal scales, hiding curved tail losses behind calm averages, or misreading fat-tailed outbreaks as mild Gaussian noise.
- **Perform (`T6`: $P\_3 \to P\_1$, *Intervention, Performativity, and Risk Imposition*):** The committee's dispatch order hits the valley, consuming physical stock, shifting how clinics triage future patients, and exposing communities to real risk if incentives reward metric compliance over health.

**Three reflexive locks** run backward along those exact same edges to audit the silent premises each forward transit relies on:

- **Frame (`T1`: $P\_3 \to P\_2$, *Pre-Draw Framing*):** Before querying data, the institution fixes what counts as a case, sets the decision loss threshold, enforces open priors under Cromwell's rule, and keeps inquiry open to overlooked harms.
- **Test for severity (`T2`: $P\_2 \to P\_1$, *Severe Testing and Identification*):** Estimates and intervals leaving the archive face severe probes against world outcomes, blocking cherry-picked models or p-hacked correlations from posing as evidence.
- **Temporal (`T5`: $P\_1 \to P\_3$, *Physical Vital Lock*):** Before congestion locks the system, the circuit verifies that physical survival reserves outlast recovery latency across the affected vital buffer and external reception perimeter.

**One integral closure** binds all three planes together before any outcome arrives:

- **Liability (`T7`: *Integral Three-Plane Closure*):** Predictions and thresholds are time-stamped and sealed before results land, safety overrides require joint safeguard clearance, frontline nurse alarms receive full standing regardless of rank, and craft (*télos*) is graded separately from outcome luck (*skopós*).

Here is how those three planes, direct transits, and reflexive locks sit together on one circuit:

                                   / \
                                  /   \
                  Decision & Act /     \ Formalization & Tail

While `T7` enforces step-by-step accountability on each move, [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) later synthesizes the finished trajectory into a strictly retrospective co-diagnostic pair—combining the non-compensatory weakest-link minimum with the external spillover margin so local balance never hides harm exported outside the district.

## 2. Cutting the circuit and missing data mechanisms

Sever the circuit at any single plane, and a predictable pathology follows immediately:

**World-only amnesia ($P\_1$ isolated).** Action occurs without institutional memory or data reflection. Every crisis hits as if nothing like it ever happened before, forcing teams to pay repeatedly for identical mistakes.

**Archive-only irrelevance ($P\_2$ isolated).** Models fit cleanly, intervals tighten, and reports accumulate, yet decisions ignore them. This is *irrelevance with high production values*: it looks like rigorous science while physical harm proceeds unchecked.

**Institution-only autocracy ($P\_3$ isolated).** Power decides by decree. Whenever records clash with convenience, archives get doctored or sidelined, and warnings are silenced until preventable ruin arrives.

Isolating any single plane severs the remaining two together—previewing the three single-axis Collapse Propositions (uncalibrated data, depleted vital slack, or captured governance) isolated in [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) and [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md).

At the world-to-archive crossing (`T3`), Donald Rubin's missing-data taxonomy classifies how records drop out (Rubin 1976):

- **Missing Completely at Random (MCAR):** Recording probability $\mathbb{P}(R = 1) > 0$ is independent of both observed and unobserved facts. Complete-case averages stay unbiased; conditional means also survive under covariate-dependent selection ($R \perp Y \mid X$ with positivity $\mathbb{P}(R = 1 \mid X) > 0$; Rubin 1976), and marginal means hold unconditionally whenever recording indicator $R \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$ has zero correlation with outcome $Y$ ($\rho\_{R,Y} = 0$), although variance, quantiles, and conditional relations still distort under nonlinear outcome dependence.
- **Missing at Random (MAR):** Dropout depends solely on recorded features. Unbiased likelihood or Bayesian adjustment requires realized/everywhere MAR and parameter distinctness (ignorability; Rubin 1976; Seaman et al. 2013), while propensity reweighting also requires **positivity** ($\mathbb{P}(R = 1 \mid X\_{\mathrm{obs}}) > 0$; [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)); MAR itself can never be proved against unrestricted MNAR from observed rows alone (Mohan, Pearl, & Tian 2013).
- **Missing Not at Random (MNAR):** Dropout depends on the missing outcome itself—depleted clinic reserves, severe illnesses too weak to travel, or concealed debts. Without auxiliary shadow variables or recoverable missingness graphs (Mohan, Pearl, & Tian 2013; Miao et al. 2019), MNAR blocks single-value recovery and forces worst-case bounds over declared limits $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ (Manski 2003) or monotonicity bounds (Lee 2009).

To prevent hindsight rewrites, sound archives enforce **bitemporal data architecture** (Snodgrass 2000)—logging both *valid time* and *transaction time*—so real-time vintages expose revisions (Croushore & Stark 2001) without leaking future corrections back into past decisions. Multi-list capture–recapture bounds the unseen "dark archive" via singletons versus doubletons (Chao 1984; Chao & Colwell 2017; [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md) §4), though groups excluded from every list remain invisible.

## 3. Good aim, bad outcome

Suppose an archer takes optimal stance and executes a flawless release, yet a sudden wind gust drives the arrow into the sand (Cicero 45 BCE). Did the archer fail at her craft?

In *Apt Belief* and *Judgment and Agency*, Ernest Sosa answers through his **AAA framework** of virtue epistemology (Sosa 2007; Sosa 2015), splitting judgment into three distinct questions:

1. **Accuracy** (*skopós*): Did the arrow hit the bullseye?
2. **Adroitness** (*télos*): Did the shot execute sound technique under the craft's standards? This matches Antipater's *télos*, Sunzi's priority of securing one's own posture before seeking external victory (Griffith 1963), and the *Bhagavad Gītā*'s custody over action rather than its fruits (Radhakrishnan 1948).
3. **Aptness**: Was the hit accurate *because* it was adroit?

Only when success flows non-deviantly from competence does an act earn **aptness**. To separate sound process from hindsight outcome bias (Baron & Hershey 1988), Stochology grades ex-ante adroitness against ex-post accuracy on a four-cell ledger ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)):

- $(\text{true}, \text{true}) \mapsto$ **first-order apt** when competence caused the hit—not when Gettier double luck (two opposing gusts returning a bad shot to the mark; [Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md)) fakes success;
- $(\text{true}, \text{false}) \mapsto$ **adroit, unlucky** (Antipater's master archer deflected by an unforecast squall);
- $(\text{false}, \text{true}) \mapsto$ **lucky gamble** (reckless action saved by fortune, wrongly rewarded by outcome-blind audits);
- $(\text{false}, \text{false}) \mapsto$ **inapt and inaccurate**.

Above first-order skill stands Sosa's (2015) **full aptness**: knowing the limits of your competence **and when to lower the bow**. Refusing to fire into impenetrable fog is not cowardice; it is sovereign epistemic restraint. Where institutional rules force an impossible binary trap ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$, like *Philoctetes* in [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md)), silent abstention harms an uncompensated good just as firing does—compelling public contestation and explicit rule revision. And even when physical telemetry ($P\_1$), fitted models ($P\_2$), and formal review boards ($P\_3$) are all present, the circuit still snaps if decision-makers silence frontline warnings through **testimonial injustice** (Fricker 2007).

## 4. The circuit as the unit of judgment

Because judgment belongs to the full circuit, claims confined to one isolated plane stay structurally defective:

- *"Our model predicts a low failure probability."* A bare archive ($P\_2$) claim: it says nothing about whether the physical grid ($P\_1$) survives a surprise hit, nor who holds authority ($P\_3$) to shed load.
- *"Our warehouse holds a fixed emergency stock."* A bare world ($P\_1$) claim: it ignores whether procurement rules ($P\_3$) can replenish stock faster than depletion.
- *"The safety board unanimously approved the waiver."* A bare institution ($P\_3$) claim: it hides whether the briefing rested on uncorrupted archive evidence ($P\_2$).

Complete judgment unites an honest bounded estimate from the archive, verified physical survival margin in the world, and accountable, pre-registered authority in the institution.

This brings us to the canonical mapping of the three foundational **ethical and interpersonal refusals** introduced in the [Prologue](00-preface.md) and [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md) ($R\_1, R\_2, R\_3$). Rather than mapping one-to-one onto separate axes, each ethical refusal anchors sites across the entire mediation circuit:

- **Refusal $R\_1$ (Renouncing control over reception):** Protects framing openness against rigid reduction (Adorno's *Non-Identical*, Adorno 1966), preserves physiological tolerance margins over time (Canguilhem 1943/1966), and bans coercive scorecards that turn attention into surveillance (Strathern 1997).
- **Refusal $R\_2$ (Exposing medium adulteration and pact fraud):** Rejects corrupted archive inputs, exposes contradictory institutional double binds (Bateson 1972), and holds promises accountable (Arendt 1958) instead of sacrificing frontline scapegoats (Girard 1986).
- **Refusal $R\_3$ (Refusing covert cognitive manipulation):** Guards framing against premise-rigging (Simon 1997), separates transparent persuasion from concealed strategic manipulation (Habermas 1984), and protects unfiltered frontline distress signals (Spinoza 2007; Beer 1972).

Even a two-person dialogue carries this three-plane circuit at small scale. Open exchange holds while both speakers remain uncoerced, and collapses when unilateral metric pressure saturates the listener's finite capacity to absorb strain—a non-export boundary formalized at institutional scale in [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) and [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7.

Healthy institutions organize collective capacity (*potentia*) rather than alienated domination (*potestas*; Spinoza 2007; Negri 1981). Because unchecked hierarchy decays into unilateral rule while self-revocable rules fail to prevent reserve pillaging, [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) and [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) pair horizontal contestability with constitutional rules above competing units, independent clocks, and non-self-extending waiver sunsets.

### Cutting the circuit

Sever the circuit at any plane, and the pathology that follows is the one that plane existed to stop. Judgment integrity is therefore a non-compensatory minimum, never a forgiving average.

Within each decision step, the seven guard sites partition `3 + 2 + 2 = 7` across the three safeguards:

- **Epistemic sites:** $\mathcal{G}\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \lbrace{}T\_1, T\_2, T\_3\rbrace$ (framing, severe testing, observation).
- **Vital sites:** $\mathcal{G}\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \lbrace{}T\_4, T\_5\rbrace$ (scale/tail formalization, temporal survival lock).
- **Governance sites:** $\mathcal{G}\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \lbrace{}T\_6, T\_7\rbrace$ (performative action, integral liability closure).

The three direct transits (`T3`, `T4`, `T6`), three reflexive locks (`T1`, `T2`, `T5`), and one integral closure (`T7`) total $3 + 3 + 1 = 7$, whose $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ three-site combinations organize Part V and [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md). Dialectical classification divides a subject at its real joints (Plato c. 360 BCE, *Sophist* 253d; *Phaedrus* 265d–266c). Fixed at three planes, the seven-site claim carries an explicit failure test: it fails if a guarded boundary crossing emerges that is neither an ordered crossing between distinct planes nor integral closure. Internal breakdowns inside one plane—such as broken table joins inside $P\_2$ or delegation drift inside $P\_3$—register on outgoing transits and closure (`T3`, `T4`, `T7`), requiring no eighth site.

### When the full circuit is an overcost

Full multi-site auditing is mandatory under irreversible risk and institutional stakes. It becomes an unnecessary overcost only for genuinely reversible, single-plane moves where physical survival headroom comfortably holds and independent verification confirms zero spillover onto external reception perimeters.

Even when irreversibility activates all seven sites $T\_1$–$T\_7$, forcing operators to run every check live during an acute crisis causes compliance overload against bounded human attention ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)). Routine guard audits therefore run off-shift under bitemporal records, while live operators receive only corroborated multi-stream anomaly alerts with full frontline standing, automatic physical pre-emption when survival buffers shrink, and an open path to attested reopening of framing premises ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)).
