## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Distinguish statistical target collapse, social process corruption, and audit inversion, and name the four Goodhart variants.
2. Trace a metric and a liability contract from the person who is scored to the person who chose the score and the distribution of downside risk.
3. State what an audit and algedonic channel require (observability, authority, consequence, and waiver-reopening sunset separation) and what fails when any one is missing.

## 1. The measure and the target: four Goodhart variants

Metrics begin as diagnostics inside an appreciative judgment of a whole situation before parts are scored (Vickers 1965). Tying those comparisons to promotions, budgets, or penalties turns the measure into a control target. Four distinct literatures diagnose the same collapse: monetary regularities used for control break down (Goodhart 1975); shifting policy rules alters the behavioral parameters equations treat as fixed (Lucas 1976); high-stakes social indicators corrupt the processes they track (Campbell 1979); and once a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure (Strathern 1997).

This distortion requires no bad faith. It is simply how competent people adapt to scoring rules. Four Goodhart mechanisms classify the breakdown (Manheim & Garrabrant 2018):

- **Regressional:** picking top scores on a noisy proxy $M = G + \varepsilon$ selects positive luck, so expected true quality $\mathbb{E}[G \mid M]$ sits below $M$ and future scores fall back toward the average.
- **Extremal:** pushing a system toward a boundary breaks the calm-day link between proxy and purpose, as when running at peak utilization strips physical survival slack.
- **Causal:** intervening on a correlated symptom alters the indicator without moving the underlying cause.
- **Adversarial:** actors with competing incentives game, alter, or filter records—such as downgrading crime codes or teaching strictly to exam formats (Campbell 1979; Manheim & Garrabrant 2018).

The first three arise mechanically from statistics or causality without dishonesty, and several variants often strike together (Manheim & Garrabrant 2018).

## 2. Mechanism design, asymmetric liability, and ethical refusals

Metrics separate an institutional author—who sets formulas, denominators, and exclusions—from frontline staff working under those constraints. As [Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md) and [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) showed, imposing asymmetric risk without Scanlonian contestability breaks legitimacy. **Limited corporate liability** sharpens that split: when owners keep private upside while public or unpaid buffers absorb collapse, convex payoffs reward gambling with tail risk (Jensen & Meckling 1976). Under hidden effort, discontinuous *live-or-die* rewards give way under monotonicity ($0 \le R'(y) \le 1$ when output can be destroyed) to debt plus a levered option (Innes 1990; Holmström 2016), tempered only by clawback escrows, deferral, or leverage caps—none of which rescues an entity whose survival time drops below recovery time.

Similarly, in **multitask agency** where measurable output $a\_1$ and unmeasured safety care $a\_2$ compete for time (Holmström & Milgrom 1991; Holmström 2016; Kerr 1975), steep piece rates on $a\_1$ starve $a\_2$ toward zero. Flat pay or direct operational rules beat high-powered incentives as quality noise grows—save under the contrary conditions of [The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) (complementary duties, muted pay paired with intrinsic care, or residual ownership).

Why do reckless firms outcompete cautious stewards during calm markets? In the evolutionary replicator model of [Evolution, Variation, and Selection](25-evolution-variation-selection.md), an organization holding physical reserves pays continuous maintenance cost $c > 0$ ($\pi\_R = -c$). A rival that strips reserves skips $c$ and pays only its amortized regulatory charge rate $q \ge 0$ ($\pi\_T = -q$). Comparing their payoffs yields the competitive **selection differential** $\pi\_R - \pi\_T = q - c = -\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}}$ ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} := c - q$).

Read aloud, limited liability sets $q = 0$ ($\pi\_T = 0$), leaving $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} = c > 0$: across long calm periods before shocks hit ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} > 1/T$ for mean shock gap $T > 0$), cautious firms get crowded out before the first crisis strikes (whereas multi-shock survival under $0 \le q < c$ remains open). This is what Collapse Proposition 3 names cynical capture, and why the governance coordinate is **not one reading among three**: it fixes the sign of the differential on which the other two are priced ([The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Across Elinor Ostrom's (1990) operational, collective-choice, and constitutional tiers, only an irrevocable floor $q \ge c$ set above competing units flips that sign—and even a constitutional rule fails if tail audits are captured, emergency powers override it, or harm spills onto unconsenting external reception perimeters (holding normalized margin $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} \in [0, 1]$, formalized in §5 and [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)).

Deficit variance decomposes into three dimensionless shares ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)): *structural variance* ($v\_{\mathrm{struct}}$, staffing ratios and capacity set at the top), *operational variance* ($v\_{\mathrm{op}}$, frontline execution noise), and *exogenous shock variance* ($v\_{\mathrm{exog}}$; paired alongside operational and exogenous loss magnitudes $L\_{\mathrm{oper}}$ and $L\_{\mathrm{exog}}$ in [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)). By Bengt Holmström's (1979) Informativeness Principle, attribution pairs top-level capacity signals with the blame-inversion check $b\_{\mathrm{fl}}/v\_{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ before blaming staff; treating top-level dominance as an empirical hypothesis (§6) exposes downward scapegoating ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

At institutional scale, these incentives enforce the three ethical refusals of the [Prologue](00-preface.md) and [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md) ($R\_1$–$R\_3$):

- **$R\_1$ (No metric surveillance over reception):** banning scorecards that erase alterity (Adorno 1966; Strathern 1997) or exhaust recipients' finite capacity $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ (§5).
- **$R\_2$ (No pact betrayal, scapegoating, or double binds):** banning sacrificial blame shifting (Girard 1986), silenced *double binds* (Bateson 1972/2000), and retaliation against dependent whistleblowers or workers refusing unsafe tasks (Freyd 1997; Smith & Freyd 2014), which cultivate institutional blindness (Near & Miceli 1996).
- **$R\_3$ (No covert cognitive manipulation):** separating transparent premise alignment from concealed framing rigs (Simon 1997 [1947]; Habermas 1984).

## 3. Audit requirements, anti-closure safeguard, and the algedonic channel

Four institutional properties keep oversight real—three internal pillars plus one anti-closure safeguard:

1. **Observability:** unblocked access to raw operational records before managers sanitize them.
2. **Authority:** independent jurisdiction to pause, halt, or roll back operations ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)).
3. **Consequence:** binding remediation and symmetric downside liability.
4. **Waiver-Reopening & Sunset Separation:** standing waivers cannot silence new emergency clocks, halt assessors sit outside override chains, and emergency exemptions carry automatic non-self-extending sunsets into fiduciary review (Elster 1979; Vaughan 1996).

Without the first three, auditing is theater; without the fourth, normalized deviance seals exceptions forever.

In Stafford Beer's (1972) Viable System Model, middle management filters out bad news heading upward. Beer paired sporadic direct audits ($S3^\ast$) with the **algedonic channel**: a fast out-of-band pain loop carrying urgent survival threats straight past managerial filters to top oversight. An algedonic line works only under three rules. First, **independent standing**: the channel stays pre-wired, needs zero boss approval, and protects identities. Second, **zero status discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) with corroborated triage**: warnings carry weight by evidence rather than sender rank—blocking rank delays that push recovery past survival limits ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md))—while sequential likelihood ratios stop unverified alert floods from saturating bounded live attention ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)). Third, **binding constitutional halt**: alerts reach an independent halt authority bound by strict sunsets.

Counting on individual heroism under asymmetric firing power selects against early alarms (Near & Miceli 1996; Alford 2001). Retaliation risk tracks **employment contract tiers**. Tenured officers resist sudden firing yet face social isolation (Alford 2001). Standard staff lack tenure and need a non-punitive **right to refuse imminent unsafe work**. Precarious workers—temps, subcontractors, platform gig staff, interns—sit closest to real hazards and spillover ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$), yet get silenced quietly via **contract non-renewal** where small-team anonymity fails. Internal-only reporting breaks first on the most exposed tier, requiring pre-registered **external and collective escalation channels** (unions, safety inspectorates, professional boards, ombudsmen) routed off-shift away from live queue overload.

## 4. Process tracing and Bayesian diagnostic tests

Testing institutional failures requires **process tracing** across four clue types—*straw-in-the-wind*, *hoop*, *smoking-gun*, and *doubly decisive* tests (Van Evera 1997; Bennett and Checkel 2015)—paired with Turing and Good's log-likelihood **deciban ($\mathrm{dB}$)** weights under Cromwell's interior priors ([Chapter 30](30-probability-as-language.md); Good 1950, 1979). Because Cromwell's bounds forbid infinite negative updates inside Bayesian sums, failing a necessary hoop test eliminates a claim solely through a pre-registered **Popper–Mayo lexical veto**, and combining clues requires checking evidential weight $V$ against common causes ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.2).

## 5. Deliberation paradoxes, polycentric commons, and the Temporal Vault

Christian List and Philip Pettit (2002, 2011; Pettit 2001) generalized the legal doctrinal paradox into the **discursive dilemma**. Suppose three judges evaluate $R = P \land Q$ under **disjoint dissent**, where two distinct $1/3$ minorities reject one premise each. Premise votes pass both $P$ ($2/3$) and $Q$ ($2/3$), affirming $R$, while direct conclusion voting rejects $R$ ($1/3$ vs. $2/3$). Across three non-substitutable safeguards ($C = P\_1 \land P\_2 \land P\_3$), separate $2/3$ premise majorities can coincide with $0/3$ endorsement of $C$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), requiring a **constitutional conjunctive veto** backed by corroborated evidence ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)).

Elinor Ostrom's (1990, 2010) eight principles for polycentric commons—clear boundaries, proportional fairness, monitoring, graduated sanctions, fast conflict resolution—prevent collapse without centralized coercion, which crowds out cooperation (Ostrom 2010). James C. Scott's (1998) *Seeing Like a State* documents the opposite failure: Prussian monoculture forestry (*Normalbaum*) boosted first-rotation timber output before triggering ecological collapse (*Waldsterben*). Global South scholarship underscores why governance never trades against aggregate abundance. Amartya Sen proved major famines struck when institutional entitlements failed (Sen 1981) alongside silenced public accountability (Sen 1999) while food averages held steady. Milton Santos (2021 [1996]) showed external technical mandates erode local territorial stability whenever external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) lack protective governance.

To block hindsight rewriting, models, reserve bounds, and **pre-termination warnings and unsafe-work refusals** seal before outcomes or dismissals using append-only cryptographic commitments such as RFC 2104 HMAC-SHA256 (Krawczyk et al. 1997). That **Temporal Vault** protects notice priority for precarious workers and external collectives (§3) from employer deletion. Still, timestamps prove integrity only *from sealing onward*—never pre-seal omissions, rigged frames, or non-discriminating protocols ($\mathbb{P}(E \mid H) = \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)$)—and fail enforcement if monopoly power crushes external sanctions. Preserving history against silent edits still keeps `T1` open to **attested counter-inductive reopening** (Canguilhem 1943/1966; [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Subverting this covenant breeds three forms of bad faith: **direct lying** (fabricating or deleting records; Arendt 1958; Weil 1952), **strategic self-deception** (rigging inquiry premises; McGoey 2012; Simon 1997 [1947]; Habermas 1984), and **bureaucratic insouciance** (box-ticking indifferent to truth; Frankfurt 2005).

Under polycentric and CARE-governed custody at Guard Site $T\_7$ (Ostrom 1990; Ostrom 2010; Carroll et al. 2020), institutional rules enforce two final boundaries: separating provable **equivocation** (fraud) from silent **network partition** (an *A-mark* earning revocable reintegration rather than permanent exile), and protecting **non-confiscatory peaceful lineage branching** under CARE and free, prior, and informed consent (`FPIC`) charters (Carroll et al. 2020) so exit stays credible ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)).

### A channel that exists is not a channel that works

Even with corroborated alert compression (§3), an internal halt button fails if job insecurity punishes pressing it or review queues saturate. Under Kingman's (1961) queueing formula with utilization $\rho \in [0, 1)$, the delay factor $\rho/(1 - \rho)$ stays below $1$ only when $\rho < 1/2$ and blows up as $(1 - \rho)^{-1}$ near capacity, turning compliance bloat into **compliance denial-of-service**. Likewise, when false alerts outnumber true alarms ($\mathrm{FP} > \mathrm{TP}$ with $\mathrm{TP} + \mathrm{FP} > 0$), precision $\mathrm{TP}/(\mathrm{TP} + \mathrm{FP})$ drops below one-half toward zero.

### When there is no third party

External audits are unnecessary only when every consequence stays inside the actor's own horizon with zero uncompensated drain on affected external reception perimeters' normalized margin $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} \in [0, 1]$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7)—a claim the actor may never self-certify. Where no outsider is exposed, a dated record suffices; whenever unpaid spillover shrinks $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ across neighbors or care buffers, full external auditing holds.

## 6. Two limits of the metric, an audit that had everything, and a share that is only an estimate

### The two ends, worked out

How much apparent "decline" after selecting top performers is pure random regression toward the mean? Look at a two-period signal-plus-noise score $Y\_t = \theta + \varepsilon\_t$ ($t \in \lbrace 1, 2\rbrace$), where true skill $\theta$ stays constant while zero-mean noises $\varepsilon\_1, \varepsilon\_2$ are uncorrelated with each other and with $\theta$, carry period-1 error variance $\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon\_1) = \sigma^2\_\varepsilon$ ($\sigma^2\_\theta + \sigma^2\_\varepsilon > 0$), satisfy $\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon\_2 \mid Y\_1] = 0$, and admit linear conditional expectation $\mathbb{E}[\theta \mid Y\_1]$ (as under Gaussian assumptions). Selecting the top decile on period $1$ retains only the **reliability fraction** $\rho\_{\mathrm{rel}} = \sigma^2\_\theta / (\sigma^2\_\theta + \sigma^2\_\varepsilon)$ (distinct from queue utilization $\rho$) of its initial lead in period $2$—while skewed or heavy-tailed errors deviate from $\rho\_{\mathrm{rel}}$.

Read both extremes: as $\rho\_{\mathrm{rel}} \to 0^+$, the metric measures pure noise and "deterioration" is purely statistical selection; as $\rho\_{\mathrm{rel}} \to 1^-$, changes reflect genuine shifts. Reliability must therefore be measured rather than guessed. Separately, for §4's diagnostic clues, reciprocal likelihood ratios carry equal magnitude in decibans, so turning a failed hoop test into hard rejection requires a separate lexical veto ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.2.6).

### The audit that had all three requirements

Having observability, authority, and consequence without an anti-closure lock still fails once waivers normalize danger. Prior to the Space Shuttle *Challenger* disaster of 28 January 1986, O-ring erosion histories were logged in pre-launch reviews and formal Flight Readiness Review sign-offs existed at *Level III* (Vaughan 1996). Yet six consecutive *Level III* launch-constraint waivers never reached *Level I* or *Level II*, and *Level III* managers declined to escalate objections on 27 January 1986 (Presidential Commission on the Space Shuttle Challenger Accident 1986). Morton Thiokol engineers lacked independent halt power once management reversed the burden of proof during the eve-of-launch call (Vaughan 1996). Formal transparency and after-the-fact inquiries never replace §3's independent anti-closure safeguard (Elster 1979; Vaughan 1996).

### The share of variance is a measurement, not a theorem

§2's claim that top-down structure drives most deficit variance is an **empirical hypothesis**, not an axiom. Where measurements show frontline execution noise ($v\_{\mathrm{op}}$) dominates, accountability sits on execution; where exogenous shocks ($v\_{\mathrm{exog}}$; tracked as loss $L\_{\mathrm{exog}}$ in [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)) dominate, outcome luck stays distinct from competence; where top capacity choices ($v\_{\mathrm{struct}}$) dominate, leadership answers ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)). Isolating those shares honestly requires **blameless post-mortem inquiry** (Allspaw 2012) bounded by explicit liability. Suspending individual punishment during incident reviews lets crews report near-misses and thin reserves candidly, protecting observability (Near & Miceli 1996). Yet blameless learning never grants impunity: formal liability remains binding for limited-liability stripping ($q \ge c$), waiver abuse (§3), record tampering (§5), and verified execution fault. Any review asserting this split without measuring its own variance shares—or collapsing into scapegoating—remains incomplete.
