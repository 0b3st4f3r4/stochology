## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Explain how a measure becomes a target, and name the four ways it then stops measuring.
2. Trace a metric from the person who is scored to the person who chose the score.
3. Say what an audit channel must have to work (access, authority, and a consequence) and what
   happens when one of the three is missing.

## 1. The measure and the target: four Goodhart variants

A metric is originally introduced to render a complex operational state visible to decision-makers. Once visible,
the number is used to compare units; once compared, it is tied to institutional incentives, promotions, and budgets.
At that point the measure becomes a target, and behaviour reorganises around the proxy instead of the mission.

This degeneration does not require dishonesty. It is what intelligent, competent agents do once an evaluation rule reaches them. Metric collapse then takes four forms.
In **regressional** collapse, the metric is subject to random noise: selecting or rewarding extreme performances
selects heavily for positive luck, and performance in subsequent cycles regresses to the population mean,
generating a spurious cycle of perceived decline. In **extremal** collapse, the target is achieved by driving the
operational system to an extreme margin where the historical relationship between proxy and purpose ruptures, such as
reducing patient wait times by discharging patients before stabilization. In **causal** collapse, the measured index
was historically correlated with the goal without causing it, so direct effort manipulates the correlate while the
goal remains untouched. In **adversarial** collapse, strategic actors game, falsify, or gatekeep the intake of cases
to flatter the metrics, as in crime-classification downgrading or teaching to the test (Manheim & Garrabrant 2018).

The first three are structural, occur among professionals doing their jobs with integrity, and account for most institutional failures on record.

## 2. The asymmetry of the metric and blame transfer

Every metric has an author and a subject, and in hierarchical systems they occupy unequal
positions of power:

- Those who author the metric establish the formulas, choose the denominators, dictate the exclusions, and set
  the performance thresholds.
- Those who are scored control only the frontline execution of the work under the constraints handed down to them.

Into this structural asymmetry, the legal architecture inserts **limited corporate liability**. When an executive
or algorithm designer captures the private upside of risk-taking while corporate reserves absorb the downside,
the private payoff of recklessness is positive even when the net social value is deeply negative. The first asset
cannibalized by this moral hazard is the organization's unmeasured resilience buffer.

Written as a payoff, limited liability is the term $q = 0$: the unit that divests bears the reserve's cost $c$ and none
of the tail cost the reserve was held against, so the private differential of
[Evolution, Variation, and Selection](25-evolution-variation-selection.md) selects divestment even where the social
differential does not. This is the mechanism Collapse Proposition 3 names cynical capture, and it is why the
governance coordinate is not one reading among three: it fixes the sign of the differential on which the other two
are priced ([The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). A rule restores $q \ge c$ only where it is
chosen above the unit it binds; below that level it is a move in the same game and is repealed by the same
differential, which is the sense in which the floor is constitutional rather than operational.

When systemic collapse eventually occurs, the institutional assignment of blame is systematically inverted.
Total deficit variance decomposes into two components: *structural variance* (governed by capacity sizing, staffing
ratios, and parameter budgets set at the top) and *operational variance* (stochastic noise handled by frontline
shifts). In the decomposition used throughout this book, structural variance dominates the total, and the
operational share is the only part that frontline discretion can actually move. Yet institutions
assign the whole of the blame to the frontline operators on duty when the barrier ruptured. This blame transfer
destroys organizational epistemology: a frontline that expects to be scapegoated for structural deficits will
cease reporting early warning anomalies (Near & Miceli 1996), blinding the institution to its own impending ruin.

The silence has a second source: the party who reports the failure loses the institution it depends on, so the
betrayal is not named in order to keep the relation that makes the harm bearable (Freyd 1997). The transfer
compounds: when the institution meant to protect the reporter mishandles the disclosure, the harm is doubled and
the next warning is withheld, which is institutional betrayal as distinct from the original failure (Smith & Freyd
2014).

## 3. The three audit requirements and the algedonic channel

Three properties make a review channel effective, and all three are operational.

1. **Observability**: Unfiltered, direct access to the operational phenomenon without advance sanitization.
2. **Authority**: Binding jurisdiction to command an operational halt, pause, or rollback.
3. **Consequence**: The investigation must produce enforceable structural modifications and genuine liability.

If any one of the three is absent, the audit is a hollow ritual.

In cybernetics, Stafford Beer established that hierarchical reporting structures inevitably filter out bad news.
Because bad news constitutes an implicit indictment of the managerial levels it traverses, each tier has a powerful
incentive to dilute, delay, or suppress the signal. Beer designed the **algedonic channel**, a dedicated,
out-of-band communication loop carrying raw distress signals directly past the administrative hierarchy to sovereign
veto holders.

An algedonic channel earns the name only if three rules hold.
First, **independent standing**: the route exists before emergencies and requires no permission to use, cannot be
conditioned on exhausting standard reporting lines, and shields the identities of those who activate it from the
criticized hierarchy. Second, **zero status discount at reception ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) with corroborated
sequential triage**: the distress warning is evaluated strictly on its evidential content without status, rank, or
credential discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$), because setting $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} > 0$ stretches
detection latency beyond the system's survival buffer, while signal escalation is ordered by corroborated likelihood
ratio so that finite human throughput — bounded near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025) — is not overwhelmed by
uncorroborated alarms. Third, **the halt holds**: the channel terminates at an authority with binding legal power to
halt operations immediately, and the halt stands until the physical safety condition is verified; a reporting channel
that can only file a memo is an organizational vent.

The protection a rulebook can write is a prohibition on reprisal, and the cost that suppresses the alarm is not
reprisal but the isolation that follows disclosure, which no prohibition reaches (Alford 2001).

## 4. Process tracing and Bayesian diagnostic tests

When auditing institutional failures or verifying causal mechanisms, investigators use **process tracing**
(Bennett and Checkel 2015). A causal hypothesis $H$ is evaluated against historical clues with four diagnostic tests, set on two axes.

```
                          IS EVIDENCE SUFFICIENT TO CONFIRM?
                                NO                    YES
                     +---------------------+---------------------+
                  NO | STRAW-IN-THE-WIND   | SMOKING GUN         |
IS EVIDENCE          | Low diagnostic power| Passing confirms H; |
NECESSARY            | Updates prior weakly| Failing leaves open |
TO SURVIVE?          +---------------------+---------------------+
                 YES | HOOP TEST           | DOUBLY DECISIVE     |
                     | Passing does not    | Single-handedly     |
                     | confirm; FAIL KILLS | confirms H & kills  |
                     +---------------------+---------------------+
```

A **straw-in-the-wind** test is neither necessary nor sufficient; it provides minor probability updating without
affirming or eliminating $H$. A **hoop test** is necessary without being sufficient: the hypothesis must jump
through the hoop to survive, so passing does not confirm $H$, while failing refutes and eliminates it. A **smoking
gun** is sufficient without being necessary: finding the gun in the suspect's hand confirms $H$, while its absence
does not exonerate. A **doubly decisive** test is both necessary and sufficient, confirming $H$ and refuting rival
explanations at once.

Following Alan Turing and I. J. Good, the diagnostic weight of an evidentiary clue is measured in **decibans (db)**:

$$W = 10 \log\_{10}\left(\frac{\mathbb{P}(E \mid H)}{\mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)}\right) \text{ db}.$$

A failed hoop test delivers infinite negative decibans, instantly terminating the hypothesis.

## 5. Deliberation paradoxes, commons governance, and the Temporal Vault

In collective institutional governance, Christian List and Philip Pettit discovered the **discursive
dilemma** (or doctrinal paradox; Pettit 2001; List & Pettit 2011). Consider a multi-member tribunal evaluating three propositions:
premise $P$, premise $Q$, and conclusion $R = P \land Q$. If a majority supports $P$ ($2/3$) and a
separate majority supports $Q$ ($2/3$), a premise-based voting procedure affirms $R$, whereas a direct majority vote on
the conclusion $R$ rejects $R$ ($2/3$). In symmetric doctrinal adjudication where errors on $R$ and $\neg R$ carry comparable weight, premise-based aggregation preserves deductive closure across published reasons; under asymmetric survival barriers ($C = P\_1 \land P\_2 \land P\_3$ across non-substitutable safeguards), however, premise-majority voting ($2/3$ per axis) can ratify an unsafe launch that every specialist vetoed ($0/3$ on $C$), which is why [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) replaces bare majority rule on safety premises with a **constitutional conjunctive veto** ($S3^\ast$ sporadic audit) whenever any safeguard premise is refuted.

Elinor Ostrom's principles for common-pool resources (*Governing the Commons*, Ostrom 1990) require auditable field monitoring, graduated sanctions and fast conflict resolution. James C. Scott's *Seeing Like a State* describes the opposite regime: when
Prussian foresters replaced diverse ecologies with a single metric of commercial timber (*Normalbaum*), the
first generation produced record volume while the second collapsed in catastrophic forest death (*Waldsterben*).
These divergent institutional regimes are directly contrasted across agent policies: a myopic optimizer that
predates on shared vital reserves until they are exhausted, a sovereign capture in which epistemic uncertainty is
weaponized as an alibi to privatize buffers under limited liability, and a precautionary policy that embodies
immanent self-governance of the commons, coordinating community reserves through horizontal pre-commitments,
non-discounted frontline channels, and biophysical basin ceilings.

The three regimes are not three proposals among which an institution should choose; they are three payoff structures, and which one is stable is fixed by the level at which the rules are made. Ostrom's principles do not add a preference for the commons to anyone's utility: they make defection observable and sanctionable, so that the unit which would otherwise divest bears a cost it cannot shift. That cost binds only where the rule carrying it is chosen above the unit it binds. A monitoring rule at the operational level is a move in the same game as the divestment, and the replicator differential derived in [Evolution, Variation, and Selection](25-evolution-variation-selection.md) repeals it before it collects its evidence; a rule at the constitutional level is a constraint on the game, and it is the constraint, not the admonition to hold reserves, that the arithmetic identifies. Where the constraint is absent, the equilibrium is the divestment: the reserve is spent in the calm and the shock meets the population that spent it. The governance axis is load-bearing exactly in that condition, and the condition is structural — it states what has to be true of the rule, not what a government ought to enact.

To inoculate an institution against hindsight bias and post-hoc narrative laundering, all hypotheses, parameter
bounds, and reserve allocations must be cryptographically committed before the wind reports: a sealed, append-only
record of what the institution held before the outcome, not a document the institution may later re-edit. The question *what did we know, and what did we
commit to, before the outcome?* must have an immutable answer. When institutional actors subvert this communicative pact ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)), the record distinguishes three structural pathologies of bad faith: a **direct lie**, the intentional fabrication of evidence or post-hoc tampering with sealed records (Arendt 1958; Weil 1952), where the actor knows the truth and suppresses it; **strategic self-deception**, engineering blind spots and rigging inquiry premises so leadership can claim plausible ignorance while instrumentalizing subordinates (McGoey 2012; Simon 1947; Habermas 1984); and **bureaucratic insouciance**, indifference to whether metrics correspond to reality so long as procedural box-ticking shields the official from liability and silences frontline algedonic alerts (Frankfurt 2005).

### A channel that exists is not a channel that works

With a fixed response budget the full panel needs more seconds than the budget allows and returns a small fraction of a bit per second, while the middle panel returns bits at the operator's own rate. The brake that fails is the expectation that pressing the button is safe, rather than the information on the panel.

The queue behind the button has the same shape as any other queue: at low load the dimensionless utilization multiplier $\rho/(1-\rho)$ stays well below one (adding only a fraction of the dispersion-scaled service time), at intermediate load ($\rho = 1/2$) it reaches one, and near saturation it explodes without bound as $(1-\rho)^{-1}$, so the channel that is fine at low load is useless at high load and the threshold between them is narrow. An institution that budgets the channel at average load has budgeted it for the load at which nobody needs it.

The precision of the shared signal is the last quantity: when false alerts outnumber true ones, the precision among survivors falls toward one-half or below, which is the number a duty officer experiences and the number the design report never prints.

### When there is no third party

The audit is dispensable when no third party is affected and the consequence falls on the decider. The procedure is to substitute a declared record for the audit and to say the substitution was made: the point of the audit was protection of someone who did not choose, and with no such person the record is enough for the decider, not for anyone who might later claim to have been affected.

## 6. Two limits of the metric, an audit that had everything, and a share that is only an estimate

### The two ends, worked out

The regressional variant of §1 has an exact arithmetic once the metric is read as signal plus noise. Let a day's
recorded performance be $\theta + \varepsilon$ with signal variance $\sigma^2\_\theta$ and noise variance
$\sigma^2\_\varepsilon$, and let the unit be scored on its first-period draw. Selecting the top decile
carries $\sigma^2\_\theta/(\sigma^2\_\theta + \sigma^2\_\varepsilon)$ of the first-period advantage into the second, that fraction is the reliability $\rho$, and the remainder is regression, not decline. A cohort selected on a noisy metric therefore keeps only the part of its advantage the metric actually carried, and the rest of the apparent slide is the selection being undone; an archive with a low reliability shows a collapse that no one performed.

The two limits follow. As $\rho \to 0$ the metric is pure noise, the selected cohort
retains nothing, and every apparent decline is an artefact of having selected on the noise, the regressional
collapse is total and the reward scheme is a machine for manufacturing the illusion of decay. As $\rho \to 1$ the
metric is the signal, nothing regresses, and a decline in the selected cohort is a real change in the world. No
archive sits at either end, which is why the variant has to be measured rather than assumed, and the reliability of
the metric is the quantity a reward rule in any archive is actually working with.

The second limit belongs to the diagnostic weight of §4. A passing smoking gun whose likelihood ratio is a factor of
ten carries exactly $+10$ decibans, and a failed hoop test whose likelihood ratio is a factor of ten against carries
$-10$ decibans. One factor of ten either way, and the pair is
exactly symmetric in weight while being utterly asymmetric in consequence: the same prior is lifted to a high
posterior by the smoking gun and driven to a near-zero one by the failed hoop, because the two move in opposite
directions from the same starting point. At the limit, a hoop test whose false-positive rate is zero delivers an infinitely negative
weight on failure, which is the arithmetic behind §4's claim that failing it eliminates the hypothesis rather than
discounting it.

### The audit that had all three requirements

§3 states a conjunction: observability, authority and consequence, and an audit missing any one of the three is a
hollow ritual. The contrapositive is what institutions actually rely on, and it is false. On 27 January 1986 the
three properties were present in the Space Shuttle programme in their formal sense. Observability: the solid-rocket
joint anomaly history was recorded, circulated and reproduced in the pre-launch reviews, and the engineers who
objected on the night had the telemetry in front of them. Authority: the waiver system required a Level III
signature at the centre that owned the hardware, and the launch-readiness review was the instrument that carried
it. Consequence: after 28 January the accountability machinery ran in full, to a presidential commission and a
redesign. The launch proceeded anyway, and the reason was not a missing pillar but a normalised one: each earlier
erosion had been classified as acceptable within a flight rationale, so the channel carried the signal and the
institution had already priced it (Vaughan 1996).

What the case establishes is that the three requirements are necessary conditions of an audit that can reach a
decision-maker, not sufficient conditions of one that can stop a launch. The property that was missing is a fourth
one the chapter does not name: the channel must carry a signal that has not already been absorbed by a standing
waiver, which is to say the algedonic route must be able to reopen a decision the hierarchy has already closed.
A channel audit can test the three named requirements; the reopening condition is the one
an institution has to add for itself, and it is the one that would have mattered.

### The share of variance is a measurement, not a theorem

The blame-inversion argument of §2 rests on an empirical premise: that structural variance dominates the total and
operational variance is the small part that frontline discretion can move. The chapter tags that premise as assumed
because it is assumed, and when the measured shares come out the other way the diagnosis inverts with them: if the
operational share dominates, the frontline really did cause most of the variance, the allocation of blame is
correct rather than transferred, and the remedy belongs at the point of execution instead of at the point of
sizing. The condition is therefore a testable switch rather than a caveat, and the procedure is to measure it before
arguing from it on the institution's own deficit series, to publish the two shares
alongside the allocation, and to treat a governance report that asserts the decomposition without the measurement
as an unfinished audit rather than a finding. The same discipline applies upward: an institution that has measured
its shares and finds them structural has established a claim about capacity, not an exoneration of the people who
set the capacity.
