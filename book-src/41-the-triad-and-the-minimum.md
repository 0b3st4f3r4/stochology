## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Name the three safeguards and say, for each, which failure it prevents and which it cannot.
2. Explain why the three combine by minimum and not by average, in words and with the operator's geometry, not one arithmetic contrast.
3. Recognise the three collapse patterns in a case description, and say which safeguard is missing.

## 1. Three non-substitutable safeguards

Enduring judgment under uncertainty requires three safeguards held at once. None of them can stand in for another, extending the three-axis complementarity previewed in [Chapter 08](08-three-things-you-cant-trade.md) and organized on the mediation circuit of [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md).

The **Epistemic Margin** ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$) measures what the empirical archive honestly permits an agent to claim. It gathers partial-identification intervals, declared selection filters, recognized schema silences, and verified Keynesian evidential weight. It is the unique defense against **Blind Undersizing**, acting upon a false point certainty. Yet knowing the bounds cannot ensure that the physical system holds enough reserves to survive the unknown range.

The **Vital Margin** ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$) measures what the physical, operational, or financial infrastructure can absorb without crossing an absorbing ruin barrier. It tracks reserve inventory, uncommitted capacity, queue headroom below the Kingman hyperbola, and whether emergency survival time outlasts recovery latency. It is the unique defense against **Impotent Lucidity**, seeing disaster clearly while lacking the buffer to endure it. In *ex-post* trajectory diagnosis over $t = 1, \dots, T$, $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ folds strictly over **pre-absorption** steps ($\lbrace 1 \le t \le T : \neg\mathrm{absorbed}\_t\rbrace$), whereas crossing the absorbing ruin barrier itself is recorded by a separate structural ruin indicator (absorption flag, ruin step, and terminal reserve), because post-absorption steps hold zero remaining capacity and carry no vital reading to compare. Still, physical slack alone cannot detect that statistical premises were rigged.

The **Institutional Governance Margin** ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$) tracks who decides, who audits, and who bears the loss. It pairs status-blind algedonic channels ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) with symmetric downside liability on **risk-imposing principals** (blocking limited-liability extraction under Jensen & Meckling 1976 and Innes 1990) and externally custodied cryptographic pre-outcome commitment (Krawczyk, Bellare, & Canetti 1997). Those algedonic channels split into pre-authorized physical hazard sentinels and off-shift discretionary alerts under corroborated likelihood-ratio sequential filtering ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), backed by pre-termination notice custody and external collective channels shielding tenured, standard, and precarious non-renewal staff ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md); Near & Miceli 1996; Alford 2001). Because cryptographic custody certifies only post-seal integrity after availability time $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$, preventing pre-seal premise rigging ($\mathrm{PRI}$) also requires external multi-list audits and $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ witness standing prior to $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Chapter 93](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md)). It is the unique defense against **Cynical Capture**, the weaponization of manufactured doubt to plunder common reserves under limited liability. Governance alone, however, cannot conjure physical stock or empirical evidence out of thin air.

Each safeguard blocks a lethal failure mode that the other two cannot reach. An institution holding two axes while neglecting the third is not two-thirds safe. It is structurally exposed, and ruin enters through the neglected axis.

That no single axis reduces to another is a long-standing institutional finding. Commons argued that an organization is not a mere veil over bilateral contracts, but a rule-making participant whose going concern outlives any single transaction inside it (Commons 1934). North located that irreducibility in the shared rules and enforcement mechanisms that make exchange possible in the first place (North 1990). Williamson showed further that a firm's boundary tracks the cost of contracting across that boundary rather than the production technology inside it (Williamson 1985). What the governance safeguard supplies, and what no bilateral contract reproduces, is the standing rule that outlives the parties who wrote it. Crucially, because entities can arbitrage contracting boundaries by offloading hazards into thinly capitalized subsidiaries, special-purpose shells, or precarious subcontractors, constitutional floors and principal liability must reach across corporate and contractual perimeters rather than stopping at nominal legal shells.

## 2. Why the Leontief minimum: ordinal box invariance and chart-conditional volume

Within each axis $a \in \lbrace\mathrm{epi}, \mathrm{vital}, \mathrm{gov}\rbrace$, individual site margins $m\_j$ first sit on their own ordinal scales around site thresholds $\tau\_j$ ([Appendix A.4](appendix-a-mathematics.md#a4-leontief-structural-complementarity-of-the-nuclear-triad)); either per-site normalization maps each site reading onto a shared axis scale anchored at physical or archival failure ($0$) and target clearance ($1$) before folding $s\_a = \min\_{j \in a} \varphi\_j(m\_j)$, or comparable shifted margins within axis $a$ fold to $s\_a = \min\_{j \in a} \tilde{m}\_j$. An **externally custodied**, strictly increasing chart $\varphi\_a$ anchored to physical ruin and archival defect at zero ($\varphi\_a(0) = 0$), never unilaterally chosen by the audited entity, then maps $s\_a$ onto the unit score $S\_a = \varphi\_a(s\_a) \in [0, 1]$, carrying the axis threshold to $\tau\_a = \varphi\_a(\tau\_{a,\mathrm{native}})$. Why combine those three scores with their minimum rather than an average or a product?

Any compensatory aggregator that averages the three coordinates allows surplus on one axis to mask total bankruptcy on another:

$$\text{Arithmetic Mean: } \frac{S\_{\mathrm{epi}} + S\_{\mathrm{vital}} + S\_{\mathrm{gov}}}{3}.$$

In plain words, an arithmetic score can portray an institution as healthy while one coordinate sits at annihilation. Because the mean lies between its smallest and largest inputs, high epistemic rigor and strong governance hide a vital axis sitting on the ruin floor. That same compensatory trap afflicts executive scoring matrices like Thomas Saaty's Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP; Saaty 1980). Even when pairwise comparisons are perfectly transitive, principal eigenvector weighting still awards a passing composite score when survival slack reaches zero. Multiplicative executive heuristics such as ICE and RICE repeat the error by letting reach and confidence mask negligible vital margin.

Pure multiplicative aggregation ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}} S\_{\mathrm{vital}} S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$) fails from the opposite direction: it distorts the geometry of safe configurations. Picture the unit cube $[0, 1]^3$ and apply the **OLC threshold-alignment condition** $\tau\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \tau\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \tau\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \tau \in (0, 1)$ (without common alignment across axes, $\min\_a S\_a \ge \min\_a \tau\_a$ departs from $\prod\_a [\tau\_a, 1]$). Survival requires every axis to clear $\tau$ simultaneously, which carves out a corner product box $[\tau, 1]^3$ rather than a curved corner-cut. Under the **Leontief minimum aggregator**,

$$M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{epi}},\mkern3mu S\_{\mathrm{vital}},\mkern3mu S\_{\mathrm{gov}}\bigr),$$

the viable region where all three coordinates satisfy $S\_a \ge \tau$ is simply that orthant box, with volume:

$$V\_{\min}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3.$$

Read carefully, that volume is a Lebesgue measure inside the single declared chart, not a chart-free invariant. Under Stevens's (1946) ordinal scale discipline, suppose we apply a separate, top-anchored $C^1$ strictly increasing cube recoding $\psi\_a : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($\psi\_a(1) = 1$) to each normalized coordinate $S\_a \mapsto \psi\_a(S\_a)$, rather than uncoordinated site margins $m\_j$. That transformation preserves coordinate-wise ordering and carries a general box $\prod\_a [\tau\_a, 1]$ cleanly to $\prod\_a [\psi\_a(\tau\_a), 1]$, rescaling Lebesgue volume to $\int\_{\prod\_a [\tau\_a, 1]} \lvert \det J\_\psi(S) \rvert \mkern3mu dS$ with Jacobian determinant $\det J\_\psi(S) = \prod\_a \psi\_a'(S\_a) > 0$ ([Appendix A.4](appendix-a-mathematics.md#a4-leontief-structural-complementarity-of-the-nuclear-triad)). Yet distinct per-axis maps $\psi\_a$ neither preserve symmetric cube thresholds $[\tau', 1]^3$, nor preserve the zero-collapse anchor when $\psi\_a(0) > 0$, nor keep weakest-axis identity invariant; §6 supplies the common, bottom-and-top-anchored ordinal level comparability (OLC) rule required for valid cross-axis comparison, so the volume comparison below reads both aggregators inside one fixed calibrated chart.

Social-choice theory clarifies why thresholds are stated in structural headroom rather than subjective utility. Arrow (1950) proved that excluding cardinal interpersonal comparability forces wide-domain aggregation toward either imposition or dictatorship. Sen (1985) formalized how **informational invariance** separates **open incompleteness** from assertive incompleteness and showed that both utilitarianism and utility-maximin rely on welfarist comparisons. Even Vickrey–Harsanyi expected-utility values, built from choices over equiprobable lotteries, conflate risk attitudes across units unless bounded by non-utility primary criteria (Sen 1985). In $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$, each coordinate $S\_a \in [0, 1]$ tracks structural headroom relative to its survival threshold $\tau\_a$, preserving ordinal box invariance without inventing cardinal exchange rates across planes.

Now place multiplicative aggregation inside that same fixed chart. Suppose every axis genuinely clears $\tau \in (0, 1)$, yet the product rule demands $S\_{\mathrm{epi}} S\_{\mathrm{vital}} S\_{\mathrm{gov}} \ge \tau$, forcing every pair of high scores to subsidize the third well above $\tau$. Writing integration variables $x := S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$, $y := S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$, and $z := S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ (with $x \ge \tau > 0$ and $xy \ge \tau > 0$), the product-viable set $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) := \lbrace{}S \in [0, 1]^3 : S\_{\mathrm{epi}} S\_{\mathrm{vital}} S\_{\mathrm{gov}} \ge \tau\rbrace$ sits strictly inside $[\tau, 1]^3$ for all $\tau \in (0, 1)$, whereas $V\_{\min}(\tau) = \int\_\tau^1 \int\_\tau^1 \int\_\tau^1 dz\mkern2mu dy\mkern2mu dx = (1 - \tau)^3$. Integrating the product region gives:

$$V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = \int\_\tau^1 dx \int\_{\tau/x}^1 dy \int\_{\tau/(xy)}^1 dz = \int\_\tau^1 \left(1 - \frac{\tau}{x} + \frac{\tau\ln\tau}{x} - \frac{\tau\ln x}{x}\right) dx = 1 - \tau + \tau\ln\tau - \frac{1}{2}\tau(\ln\tau)^2 = 1 - \tau \sum\_{k=0}^2 \frac{(-\ln \tau)^k}{k!}.$$

Under uniform Lebesgue measure on $(0, 1]^3$, $-\ln(S\_{\mathrm{epi}} S\_{\mathrm{vital}} S\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \sim \mathrm{Erlang}(3, 1)$ gives this tail formula directly, whereas inclusion–exclusion over the three marginal caps $\lbrace{}S\_a < \tau\rbrace$ yields $1 - 3\tau + 3\tau^2 - \tau^3 = (1 - \tau)^3 = V\_{\min}(\tau)$, matching the limit $\lim\_{\tau \to 0^+} V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = 1 = \lim\_{\tau \to 0^+} V\_{\min}(\tau)$. Taken in plain English, the product rule carves away the interior wedge $[\tau, 1]^3 \setminus \Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)$. For every $\tau \in (0, 1)$, it imposes a **viable-volume contraction** $V\_{\min}(\tau) - V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) > 0$, wrongly failing balanced institutions whose three margins each clear the bar. Within any single chart, only the Leontief minimum isolates the coordinate-wise box of non-substitutable survival.

Two common misreadings must be walled off immediately. First, $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ is never an unconditional action rule. Harsanyi (1975) used his New York–Chicago flight example and zero-savings critique to show that maximin used as an **unconditional choice rule** paralyzes ordinary life and forbids productive accumulation whenever tiny worst-case risks exist. Stochology fully accepts Harsanyi's refutation of maximin-as-action. Here $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ serves strictly as a retrospective **trajectory diagnostic** folded after execution (folding $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ and $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ across $1 \le t \le T$ and $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ across pre-absorption steps $\lbrace 1 \le t \le T : \neg\mathrm{absorbed}\_t\rbrace$, while never reporting a post-absorption vital score inside the binding-axis attribution), while live action runs through Regimes I–III in §5. Harsanyi (1975) noted himself that maximin remains useful where cardinal utilities cannot be compared and basic rules forbid absurd outcomes. That is the exact boundary held here: the minimum is not a weighted sum, nor an action command.

Second, $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ resembles **Maxmin Expected Utility (MEU)** (Gilboa & Schmeidler 1986) only by formal analogy, not mathematical identity. MEU represents uncertainty aversion under C-Independence as $\min\_{P \in C} \int u \circ f \mkern3mu dP$ over a closed convex set $C$ of priors for one utility $u$ and act $f$ (here $C$ and $f$ denote priors and an act, distinct from §5's intervention cost $C$ and §6's distortion map $f$). By contrast, $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ binds three structurally distinct planes (epistemic margin, vital buffer, and institutional governance) at their weakest link. Klibanoff, Marinacci, and Mukerji (2005) further showed that smooth ambiguity preferences $\mathbb{E}\_\mu \phi(\mathbb{E}\_\pi u \circ f)$ trade off across priors under finite ambiguity aversion and reach MEU only in the infinite-aversion limit across utility domain $U$ ($\lim\_n (\inf\_{x \in U} \alpha\_n(x)) = +\infty$, where $\alpha\_n$ denotes ambiguity aversion rather than §6's affine slopes $\alpha\_a$). Whether mild interior regimes away from absorbing ruin barriers allow partial physical slack substitution is therefore left open to pre-registered empirical falsifier $F\_4$ in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), never closed by axiomatic decree.

Materials science gives this weakest-link geometry its physical anchor. Waloddi Weibull derived his extreme-value distribution from serial chain mechanics (Weibull 1939). A chain under tension never snaps at the mean strength of its links, nor at a smooth product once a microscopic Griffith crack propagates; it snaps at its single weakest link, $\min\_i R\_i$ (writing $R\_i$ for link strength, distinct from §3's selection indicator $R$ and §5's reflexive vector $\mathcal{R}\_t$). That chain also refutes the picture of the world drawn by logical atomism, where elementary facts stand independently and non-substitutability reduces to an arbitrary convention of addition (Russell 1918–1919; Wittgenstein 1921). A viable orthant whose volume collapses to zero whenever any single coordinate exhausts its headroom ($1 - \tau\_a = 0$, giving $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = 0$) is an indivisible whole, not a heap of independent atoms.

## 3. The three collapse propositions

Set any single coordinate to zero and a distinct failure mode follows, because each safeguard blocks its own mechanism of ruin.

**Collapse Proposition 1 ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{epi}} \implies \text{Blind Undersizing}$)**: Where an organization
holds extensive physical buffers and flawless governance but lacks the epistemic margin, so that it estimates
demand using naive sample means that ignore unmeasured selection bias ($\rho\_{R,Y} \neq 0$), cluster design
effects ($\mathrm{DEFF}$) and the width of the partial-identification bounds $\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}}$, the
reserve buffer is sized against a false point certainty and fails on the first excursion beyond the support
that certainty assumes. It fails if a trajectory carrying all three omissions is observed to size its buffer
above the tail it later meets.

That point-certainty trap fixes Collapse Proposition 1's **empirical boundary**. Neth and Gigerenzer (2015) distinguish measurable risk from radical uncertainty and apply the bias–variance decomposition to explain why simple estimation-free heuristics (such as $1/N$ equal weighting) outperform complex optimization whenever parameter estimation variance overwhelms fine tuning.

Haldane and Madouros (2012) document both sides of this boundary in banking data. Across roughly 100 large global banks holding assets above $\unicode{x24}100\text{ billion}$ at end-2006 (37 of which failed), pre-crisis unweighted leverage averaged 1.2 percentage points lower in failing than in surviving banks (significant at the 1% level), while Basel Tier 1 risk-weighted capital was insignificant (Haldane & Madouros 2012). Preventing failure across those large banks would have required a leverage floor above 7%, versus an error-minimizing floor near 4% (Haldane & Madouros 2012). Yet across 8,500 simpler US FDIC-insured regional banks (nearly all below $\unicode{x24}100\text{ billion}$, with 442 failures since 2007), that ranking reversed: risk-based capital was significant at 1% while simple leverage was insignificant (Haldane & Madouros 2012). In matching GARCH simulations, a "true" parametric model needed over 100,000 days (400 years) and materially around 250,000 days (1,000 years) to beat a simple structural rule (Haldane & Madouros 2012). Proposition 1 thus indicts false point precision built by ignoring $\rho\_{R,Y} \neq 0$, $\mathrm{DEFF}$, and $\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}}$, not robust structural floors that renounce fragile estimation.

**Collapse Proposition 2 ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{vital}} \implies \text{Impotent Lucidity}$)**: Where impeccable empirical
bounds, auditable governance and near-full utilization or congested deliberation meet at
$\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}} = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$, the trajectory is absorbed: the Kingman queuing
hyperbola diverges, the Itô variance drain consumes working capital, and the system reaches the ruin barrier
while fully understanding the equations of its own demise. This is a theorem of the ruin model under the stated
condition, not a claim about how often organizations satisfy it. Its empirical content is that the condition is
reachable; whether a particular organization reaches it is a question for a pre-registered design
([Five Methodological Families, Process Tracing, and Reflexive Self-Application](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.5), and
what the proposition predicts is absorption once the barrier is crossed, not that a lean organization fails inside
any particular window. It fails if a trajectory whose survival time sits below its recovery time is observed to take
the next step at full utilization and still be there once the recovery interval has elapsed.

Proposition 2 carries an equally sharp **boundary condition** around budget hardness. Absorption holds for a self-contained balance sheet under a hard budget constraint; an external guarantor can temporarily postpone exit by softening that constraint through subsidies, tax relief, soft credit rescheduling, or cost-plus pricing (Kornai 1986). When failing banks or firms rely on external bailouts whenever bills come due, their ledger survives not because $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$ proved harmless, but because deficits shifted onto an outside insurer absorbing moral hazard. As Kornai (1986) emphasizes, soft rescue dulls precaution and creates a lasting tension between financial discipline and solidarity, so empirical tests of Proposition 2 must isolate bailout transfers before counting unit survival as a refutation.

**Collapse Proposition 3 ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{gov}} \implies \text{Cynical Capture}$)**: Where an organization
has accurate data and generous physical reserves but lacks the governance coordinate, operating under
one-sided limited liability where decision-makers capture private upside while shifting downside risk onto the
public, legitimate scientific uncertainty is weaponized as an agnotological excuse to delay precautions,
frontline warning testimony is discounted by status ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$), and common vital
reserves are privatized or offloaded onto external basins. It fails if an institution with one-sided limited liability and silenced frontline
testimony ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$), with epistemic bounds ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$) intact and external bailouts (Kornai 1986) or liability-offloading shells (Williamson 1985; Innes 1990) excluded, is observed to preserve common vital reserves and external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) intact across calm-window competitive depletion ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} > 1/T$) and severe shocks.

Empirical and contract-theoretic research pins down both the capture mechanism and its contrary boundaries. Kerr (1975) showed that narrow quantitative reward targets work in highly predictable tasks yet drive goal displacement everywhere else, even when an operator's "fortunate bystander" habit masks the rot while uncodified norms hold. Formally, Holmström and Milgrom (1991) proved that linear piece-rate pay under perfect effort substitution drives effort on unmeasured duties such as safety straight to zero. Holmström (2016) documents that breakdown across three public audits. The Atlanta Public Schools investigation found cheating on the 2009 Competency Test in 44 out of 56 schools with 178 teachers and principals altering answers, alongside cross-selling abuses at Wells Fargo and aggressive drilling targets that weakened safety at BPX, leading Holmström (2016) to conclude that misaligned metrics demand low-powered incentives.

Proxy gaming often pairs with manufactured ignorance. Proctor and Schiebinger (2008) trace engineered ignorance as a deliberate "active construct," illustrated by a 1969 Brown & Williamson internal memo stating "Doubt is our product." In climate risk, Cook et al. (2019) contrast Exxon's 1977 internal projection, that doubling $\mathrm{CO}\_2$ could warm Earth by $1^\circ\text{C}$ to $3^\circ\text{C}$ by 2050 A.D. ($\mathrm{CO}\_2$ emissions causing $1\text{–}3^\circ\text{C}$ warming), with its 2000 public advertorial claiming incomplete knowledge equals total ignorance, an argument categorized under their five-part FLICC denial taxonomy.

On the **contrary boundary**, Holmström and Milgrom (1991) specify four conditions where unmeasured-effort collapse does not happen: tasks act as complements in private cost, intrinsic motivation remains active up to satiation under fixed pay, agents hold residual asset ownership, or measurable and unmeasurable duties are split across agents within inseparability limits. In commons governance, Ostrom (2010) finds core polycentric design principles supported across "two-thirds" of reviewed field studies, and laboratory experiments pairing face-to-face communication with self-selected sanctioning reached "90 percent of optimal" returns net of minor defection fines. Top-down external mandates, by contrast, crowd out voluntary cooperation, especially where harms detach spatially from causes and cross-scale monitoring breaks down (Ostrom 2010; Dietz, Ostrom, & Stern 2003).

Once governance fails (turning epistemic uncertainty into a shield and common reserves into private booty), preserving collective stocks under compliance cost $c > 0$, principal sanction/clawback $q \ge 0$, and audit probability $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} \in (0, 1]$ ([Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md)) requires a binding constitutional floor $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} q \ge c$ (specializing to $q \ge c$ when detection is certain, $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} = 1$) enforced across parent–subsidiary and subcontracting perimeters above competing firms (Williamson 1985). Only that shared floor flips the calm-window replicator differential $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} := c - p\_{\mathrm{audit}} q$ (reducing to $c - q$ at unit audit probability) so prudent operators are not crowded out across calm spells regardless of data quality ([Chapter 25](25-evolution-variation-selection.md); [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)). That is why the governance coordinate is **not one reading among three**: it fixes the sign of the differential on which the epistemic and vital margins are priced.

Read properly, the three propositions characterize design configurations, not statistical rates across existing institutions. Each states what a trajectory becomes once an axis disappears; how frequently real institutions drop an axis is an empirical question left to [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) (§9.6). When two safeguards vanish together, the lone surviving axis speeds disaster: sharper data makes capture more efficient, and deeper reserves hide blind undersizing until collapse hits all at once.

### The form of the collapse

As a triad, the three propositions express a determinate negation rather than a simple shopping list of hazards. Hegel's Preface to the *Phenomenology* defines the structure: the negation of a form is never blank nothingness, but carries the specific content of what it negates (Hegel 1807). Dropping one axis transforms the surviving two instead of leaving them intact. Removing the epistemic margin turns sound governance and physical buffers into protection sized for a mirage. Removing the vital margin turns clear knowledge into impotent lucidity before ruin. Removing governance turns honest doubt into an alibi and shared reserves into a prize. Because each pathology lives inside the remaining axes, the three margins are internally related in Bertell Ollman's sense (Ollman 1993): the Leontief minimum states the arithmetic of that interdependence. Ascending from Part II's isolated bases to this concrete mediation circuit mirrors what E. V. Ilienkov reconstructed in Marx, advancing from abstract determinations to a concrete unity rather than a heap of anecdotes (Ilienkov 1960).

Calling these patterns internal contradictions invites a classical logical objection. Aristotle's *Metaphysics* Γ bars any true contradiction, and Popper restated the formal worry: in classical logic a contradiction entails every statement, so a dialectic admitting contradictions collapses inference itself (Aristotle c. 335 BCE; Popper 1940). Elster pressed a social-scientific version, arguing that supposed "contradictions" reduce without loss to scoped causal mechanisms (Elster 1978). The Collapse Propositions dodge that explosion completely: they name material incompatibilities between institutional planes, not logical contradictions from which conclusions may be deduced. A collapse diagnosis describes the whole system's failure mode; it never serves as an inferential premise.

Popper's (1940) formal worry also presupposes a classical-monist premise, the **Principle of Explosion** ($\lbrace A, \neg A \rbrace \vdash B$), which collapses *inconsistency* into *triviality*. Non-explosive **paraconsistent logic** ($\lbrace A, \neg A \rbrace ⊬\_{\mathcal{L}} B$) breaks that equivalence, pioneered independently by Stanisław Jaśkowski's discussive calculus $D\_2$ (Jaśkowski 1969 [1948]) and Newton da Costa's hierarchy $C\_n$ (da Costa 1993 [1963]; da Costa 1974), and generalized in **Logics of Formal Inconsistency** (`LFIs`; Carnielli, Coniglio, & Marcos 2007; Carnielli & Coniglio 2016), where explosion applies solely under attested local consistency ($\lbrace A, \neg A, \circ A \rbrace \vdash B$, with $A^\circ := \neg(A \land \neg A)$ and $\circ A \equiv A^\circ$ in $C\_1$). Crucially, Stochology endorses da Costa's (1980) **pragmatic logical pluralism** (treating formal calculi as domain-indexed tools) rather than Graham Priest's metaphysical **dialetheism**, which posits genuinely true contradictions (Priest 1979; Priest 2006 [1987]). Stochology likewise avoids embedding $\vdash\_{C\_1}$ or the implicit defeasible retraction of inconsistency-adaptive logics (Batens 2000) inside its live actuation kernel. Instead, it confines inconsistency across four explicit locks: logical-status separation of the retrospective collapse diagnoses, Stage-One syntactic `Unparsable` quarantine, attested counter-inductive reopening ($\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$), and linear/affine resource accounting ([Layer 06](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md)), reserving LFI and Belnap–Dunn conflict marking strictly for pre-decisional archival quarantine ([From World to Archive](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)).

Standard social theory offers three gentler nouns for the same breakdown, each keeping the surface symptom while omitting its generative motor. Parsons and Merton speak of dysfunction: friction on a baseline social equilibrium (Parsons 1951; Merton 1949). General equilibrium speaks of disequilibrium: a temporary mismatch of independent plans that prices clear (Debreu 1959). Boudon speaks of perverse effects: unintended consequences emerging when individual rationalities collide (Boudon 1977). Each captures real phenomena, yet none expresses how an institution's catastrophe is manufactured by the very success of its remaining mechanisms.

## 4. The triad as a diagnostic, not an optimizable score

The greatest operational threat to this framework is that the Leontief minimum becomes an executive target. Any published score tied to bonuses invites metric gaming. If operators were rewarded for maximizing $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ directly, they would inflate whichever coordinate looked smallest on paper: fabricating pro forma pre-registrations, padding denominator capacities to disguise congestion, or silencing frontline incident reports. The score would climb while real physical safety collapsed.

Hence the constitutional boundary: **the Nuclear Triad is a diagnostic, never an optimization objective.** Independent auditors operating under conflict-free appointment and fee firewalls (insulated from management hiring, contract renewal, or compensation discretion; [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)) read the score strictly after the fact, while operational halting authority remains separate from any composite index.

That distinction carries a long philosophical lineage. Hegel observed that *aufheben* simultaneously means to preserve and to put an end to (Hegel 1812–1816). Here that double sense separates cleanly: the triad is preserved as a retrospective reading and terminated as a control rule. Similarly, Vickers described institutional regulation as the appreciation of a whole situation before scoring or acting on its parts (Vickers 1965). Any composite index has already performed an act of appreciation, so rewarding the index replaces systemic judgment with checklist gaming.

Where diagnostic authority sits matters operationally. After a trajectory finishes, an auditor computes the **co-diagnostic pair** $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr)$ ([Appendix A, *Mathematical Reference*](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7). Across the finished run $1 \le t \le T$, $S\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} S\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}$ and $S\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} S\_{\mathrm{gov}, t}$ fold over all recorded steps, whereas **$S\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \inf\_{\lbrace 1 \le t \le T : \neg\mathrm{absorbed}\_t\rbrace} S\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}$ folds strictly over pre-absorption steps**; crossing the absorbing ruin barrier itself is recorded by the separate **structural ruin indicator** (absorption flag, ruin step, and terminal reserve), and on an absorbed trajectory $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$'s binding-axis attribution evaluates only the non-absorbed coordinates ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$) so it never reports a vital value from post-absorption absorbed states. Paired with the reception-basin infimum $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}$, $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ tracks cross-perimeter spillover without adding a fourth coordinate or mixing local viability with externalized harm. Live shifts, by contrast, act solely from each step's sealed pre-actuation state, never from either retrospective summary.

Three record tests verify this separation on transcripts without needing new empirical data:

- **Prefix test.** Two admissible steps sharing identical **pre-action sealed state** (attested control tape $\mathbf{u}\_t$, declared bounds, capacity, parameters, and guard readings $\mathbf{m}\_t$, leaving ex-post contingency $\mathbf{w}\_t$ unread) must receive the same action regime regardless of unsealed prior trajectory history. Prior history may enter step $t$ only through causal physical or archival state pushforwards (such as current reserve balance, care-wear state, or updated sample bounds) sealed into $\mathbf{u}\_t$ and capacity before step $t$, **strictly barring any retrospective $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$, $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$, or $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ aggregate from entering $\mathbf{u}\_t$ or $\mathbf{m}\_t$**.
- **Contingency test.** Two transcripts sharing pre-action sealed control tapes, declared bounds, capacity, parameters, and guard readings while differing in ex-post contingency tapes ($\mathbf{w}\_t$) must return both the same syntactic acceptance verdict and the same live action regime, reflecting **contingency invariance** (distinct from stage non-commutation proved in [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)).
- **Seal test.** Once a closing verdict is sealed, no recorded step may be retroactively re-derived to make the fold steer past action. Because cryptographic sealing certifies post-release integrity after availability time $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ while leaving pre-seal premise rigging ($\mathrm{PRI}$) observationally equivalent inside the vault ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Chapter 93](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md)), anti-tampering seals must pair with external `T1`–`T3` registry audits and $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ witness standing prior to $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$. Unlike unattested rewriting, an attested counter-inductive reopening of $T\_1$ ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); Canguilhem 1943/1966) enters quarantine, chains the prior digest append-only under strict expansion ($\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$) and custodian-borne reopening escrow ($\tau\_{\mathrm{reopen}} > 0$), writes a new forward declaration, and seals subsequent steps cleanly.

Alongside transcript invariance, a **Reflexive Brake Audit** checks ex post whether the framework's own protections are being gamed (detailed as the four Goodhart brakes in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3). None of these four diagnostic flags triggers live shutdowns:

1. *Epistemic Brake*: Decouples point-forecast resolution from interval width (Murphy 1973; Dimitriadis, Gneiting, & Jordan 2021; [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.1): flags an ex-post breach either when constant base-rate point forecasts collapse `CORP` discriminative resolution ($\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} \to 0$) despite clean calibration, or when partial-identification intervals $[L\_t, U\_t]$ breach explicit width limits or evidential-weight sufficiency floors (so sharp point forecasts cannot conceal padded intervals).
2. *Vital Brake*: Flags an ex-post breach when reserve slack drops below safety floors (or utilization breaches safety ceilings), when $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, or when hoarding reserves breaches the Scarf–Samuelson opportunity-cost ceiling $R^\ast$ (Scarf 1960; Samuelson 1971) computed strictly under **exogenously pre-anchored and sealed $(\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}, c\_{\mathrm{urgent}})$ parameters** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.2 & §9.7.3).
3. *Governance Brake*: Flags an ex-post breach when testimonial discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} > 0$) or performative drift surfaces, or when bureaucratic lag ($\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$) stalls containment. Only in the middle window $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$ under external clock custody does Brake 3 license pre-escrowed defensive release governed by automatic non-self-extending sunsets ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.3; Elster 1979; [Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md)), whereas $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ (or dropping below $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}$) triggers live halting strictly via the per-step operator below.
4. *Composite Brake*: Reads $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr)$ ex post and forbids turning either coordinate into a bonus target or checklist. When either coordinate breaches tolerance, it identifies the failing axis or spillover boundary without tripping a live switch.

## 5. Reflexive termination of higher-order uncertainty and the three regimes of action

When the world's first-order state $\theta\_t$ is unknown, trying to cure ignorance by estimating second-order hyperpriors over *skopós* triggers an infinite regress. Asking a model to account for everything its description leaves out does not yield a sharper sub-description; it returns the vast, unusable complement of everything outside the lens. To decide before outcomes arrive, the instrument must stipulate a finite closure on its own mediation circuit (*télos*). That pivot is a **stopping decision of the instrument, not a theorem about infinite inquiry**: it trades endless second-order speculation for pre-outcome decidability, at a price paid in explicit bound width.

Philosophically, this stop adapts Fichte and Savage from opposite sides. In the *Wissenschaftslehre*, Fichte resolved an infinite regress of grounds by having the subject posit itself (Fichte 1794); Stochology drops the metaphysics and posits the instrument's finite closure instead. Savage (1954), conversely, treated the state space as pre-given before any act so that decision merely picks a function over a finished world. Stochology keeps Savage's decision map yet insists that the state space is actively closed by the agent's own cut, whose omissions must be priced. Across the $P = 3$ mediation planes, three self-referential trunk questions (*What does my lens exclude? How much error can my breath absorb? Who pays and who can halt me?*) unpack into $n = P(P - 1) + 1 = 3 \cdot 2 + 1 = 7$ guard sites ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)), where three classifications of the same seven sites agree:

1. **By Triad Axis (`3 + 2 + 2 = 7`):** $\mathcal{G}\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \lbrace{}T\_1, T\_2, T\_3\rbrace$, $\mathcal{G}\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \lbrace{}T\_4, T\_5\rbrace$, $\mathcal{G}\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \lbrace{}T\_6, T\_7\rbrace$;
2. **By Circuit Transit (`3 + 3 + 1 = 7`):** three direct transits ($T\_3, T\_4, T\_6$), three reflexive locks ($T\_1, T\_2, T\_5$), and one integral three-plane closure ($T\_7$);
3. **By Plane of Origin (`2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 7`):** two sites originating in the World ($T\_3, T\_5$), two in the Archive ($T\_2, T\_4$), two in the Institution ($T\_1, T\_6$), and one integral closure ($T\_7$).

Halting the regress at the circuit does not shrink epistemic error to zero. Instead, it converts unmeasurable ignorance about the outer world into bounded uncertainty over the agent's own archive defect and stopping horizon, just as any sequential stopping rule must price waiting time and horizon truncation rather than treating them as free ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)). Because any defect bound over the agent's self-description remains an assumption rather than a measurement, the regress ends **bounded, not grounded**: publishing a point self-description merely hides an invented precision, and a unilaterally drawn *télos* boundary can still omit *Not askable* populations ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$) and externalized harm behind a clean seal ($\mathrm{PRI} \to 1$; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Chapter 93](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md)). Subordinate to external `T1`–`T3` multi-list cross-checks (Chao 1984, 1989), $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ affected-party witness standing before $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$, and non-self-certifiability of $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)), empirical distributionally robust optimization marks the contrast clearly. Wasserstein or $\phi$-divergence ambiguity balls anchor worst-case expectations around empirical data rather than climbing hyperprior ladders (Mohajerin Esfahani & Kuhn 2018; Duchi & Namkoong 2021), yet the ball radius itself still enters as an explicit assumed boundary to be priced. Nor is the stop universal: it binds only this declared circuit, buying in return a concrete action regime before reality settles the bet.

Live action is issued exclusively by a per-step canonical operator inspecting the *ex-ante* **reflexive state vector** $\mathcal{R}\_t := (\mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}, \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}, \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{gov}, t})$, the declared pre-outcome readiness across the three planes carried by sealed step bounds, provisioned capacity, reserve buffers, and guard readings (matching [Appendix A.5](appendix-a-mathematics.md#a5-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate) and [Appendix E.2](appendix-e-lexicon.md), distinct from Weibull's $R\_i$ and Meng's $R$). Meanwhile, the co-diagnostic $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = (M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}})$ waits until the trajectory closes ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)). Throughout this section, $\tau$ with latency subscripts ($\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}, \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}, \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}, \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}, \tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}, \tau\_{\mathrm{reopen}}$) denotes positive duration scales rather than §2's unit-cube threshold $\tau \in (0, 1)$, and survival and recovery clocks must sit under **external telemetry custody** outside asset-holder discretion ([Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md)). That operator evaluates three questions in strict lexicographic order, because each later question depends on an earlier guarantee. In structure, this three-way triage parallels the clinical taxonomy preserved in the Edwin Smith Surgical Papyrus (separating ailments treatable directly, conditions contested under open risk, and hopeless cases forbidden from false-certainty intervention; Breasted 1930), while replacing ancient prognostics with hard survival clocks and partial-identification bounds:

1. *Does the vessel outlive its own correction?* First verify that physical and causal rate clocks $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t}$ and $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ on Guard Site `T5` are calibrated, non-negative, and finite under external custody. Whenever a physical/causal rate clock is **missing, uncalibrated, negative, or non-finite** (`T5` clock absence), Question 1 never lets $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ proceed unchecked and triggers **Regime III-A (Quarantine)** on `T5`, or escalates directly to **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** whenever clock tampering or an active governance alarm corruption coincides so omitting a clock never masks an active alarm. When calibrated, non-negative finite clocks are present, verify $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}, t} = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$; whenever $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** fires immediately **even if another instrument slot is missing or a $T\_1$ reopening is open** (blocking *compliance denial-of-service*) and co-reports any concurrent alarm corruption. Physically, this priority respects the human deliberative bottleneck near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025): under simultaneous strain across $\lbrace T\_1, \dots, T\_7\rbrace$, bureaucratic clearance pushes $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ beyond remaining survival time. Any **binary lethal-omission impasse** ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$ where halting external actuation guarantees ruin) is flagged alongside Step 1 or Step 2 stops for immediate standpoint governance escalation ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)).
2. *Is the instrument known, and are the channel and floors intact?* Given calibrated non-negative clocks satisfying $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, any unsealed guard slot or open $T\_1$ reopening quarantine (awaiting strict superset expansion $\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$, custodian-borne reopening escrow $\tau\_{\mathrm{reopen}} > 0$, and re-declared bounds; [Chapter 93](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md)) routes to **Regime III-A (Quarantine)** while logging any concurrent alarm fault on the $T\_7$ docket. Once all seven instrument slots are sealed and no reopening is open, any corrupted governance alarm triggers **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** ahead of reading reserve floors, and any breached vital or governance floor shifts action inward to restore buffers (with Brake 3's pre-escrowed defensive release under non-self-extending sunset applying strictly when $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$ and $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$).
3. *Is the identification region decisive?* Once clocks, seals, alarm channels, and floors clear, compare avoidable-loss bounds against cost threshold $C$. Lower bounds above cost ($L\_t > C$, or boundary $L\_t = C < U\_t$) authorize full commitment, upper bounds at or below cost ($U\_t \le C$) warrant safe abstention when non-lethal and free of basin spillover, and strictly straddling bounds ($L\_t < C < U\_t$) license either a divisible reversible fractional probe or an irreversible-probe halt distinguishing unilateral probe refusal from a binary lethal-omission impasse.

To read the avoidance inequality cleanly, let $[p\_{L,t}, p\_{U,t}]$ bound hazard probability, unsubscripted $L > 0$ mark unmitigated **affected-party loss ($L = L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$)** rather than a liability-capped private exposure $L\_{\mathrm{inst}}$ (distinct from step-$t$ lower bound $L\_t$), $\eta \in (0, 1]$ capture mitigation effectiveness, and $C > 0$ represent intervention cost in the same unit, subject to verified $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ non-export. Avoidable-loss bounds then read $[L\_t, U\_t] := [p\_{L,t}\mkern2mu\eta L,\mkern3mu p\_{U,t}\mkern2mu\eta L]$, with clamped cost–loss threshold $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} := \min\bigl(1,\mkern3mu C/(\eta L)\bigr)$ ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1; note $C/(\eta L) < 1$ automatically whenever $C < U\_t \le \eta L$).

**Regime I-A: Buffered Action ($L\_t > C$, or boundary $L\_t = C < U\_t$).** Even the conservative lower bound clears intervention cost ($p\_{L,t} > p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$, or $p\_{L,t} = p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} < p\_{U,t}$), survival clocks satisfy $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, and governance gates are sealed and intact. Deploy full-scale operational intervention ($a = 1$).

**Regime II: Reversible Fractional Probe ($L\_t < C < U\_t$).** Here the sealed loss interval strictly straddles cost $C$, survival headroom holds, and the action is **divisible, linear in cost and effectiveness ($C(a) = aC$, $\eta(a) = a\eta$), and externally verified as physically and institutionally reversible** ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md); [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)). Full inaction pretends the best case ($L\_t$) is certain, whereas full rollout pretends the worst case ($U\_t$) is certain. To minimize maximum regret across both extremes, the operator deploys the **Minimax Regret (MMR) probe fraction**:

$$a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}} = \frac{U\_t - C}{U\_t - L\_t} = \frac{p\_{U,t} - p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}}{p\_{U,t} - p\_{L,t}} \in (0, 1).$$

Read aloud, the wider the uncertainty gap above cost relative to the total interval, the larger the safe probe; under compounding wealth dynamics, Kelly shrinkage caps that fraction further. To prevent asymmetric downward padding ($p\_{L,t} \downarrow 0$ at fixed $p\_{U,t}$) from shrinking $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}}$ toward zero, lower bounds require external audit against evidential-weight floors, and probes carry a **finite probe-learning sunset** demanding measurable reduction in interval width $\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}}$ or gain in evidential weight rather than running indefinitely under $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$ or *Not askable* ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$) exclusions. Whenever the candidate act is physically or institutionally irreversible (such as crossing an ecological tipping point or nuclear criticality), fractional probing is banned and Regime III engages. If waiting ($a = 0$) does not guarantee ruin, **Regime III enforces unilateral probe refusal**, blocking external probing ($a = 0$ externally) while pivoting authority inward to monitor and restore buffers, whereas a strictly binary choice ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$) where inaction guarantees ruin triggers a distinct **binary lethal-omission impasse** ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), escalating immediately to standpoint governance.

**Regime I-B: Safe Abstention ($U\_t \le C$).** When clocks, channels, and floors clear, omission is non-lethal with $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ non-export verified under $L = L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$, and upper loss sits at or below cost ($U\_t < C$, boundary $L\_t < C = U\_t$ where $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}} = 0$, or $L\_t = U\_t = C$), benign inaction ($a = 0$) acts as the peaceful twin of buffered action without triggering an emergency.

**Regime III: Lowering the Bow.** External action stops whenever Step 1 encounters missing/uncalibrated `T5` clocks (**Regime III-A: Quarantine**) or finite $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ (**Regime III-B: Algedonic Halt**), Step 2 hits unsealed parameters or open $T\_1$ reopenings (**Regime III-A: Quarantine**), sealed alarm corruption (**Regime III-B: Algedonic Halt**), or breached floors (**inward buffer restoration**), or Step 3 meets an irreversible straddle admitting survivable waiting (**unilateral probe refusal**). Authority pivots inward to shed load, raise algedonic alerts, and rebuild physical reserves, while flagging any **binary lethal-omission impasse** ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$) across Steps 1–3 for immediate standpoint escalation ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)).

This lexicographic order never trades physical survival for telemetry paperwork: impending ruin pre-empts missing slots against compliance denial-of-service, while intact clocks clear seals, alarms, and physical floors before any cost–loss calculation runs.

Empirical failures ground all three regimes rather than abstract axioms:

- *Precision without representativeness:* Meng's (2018) analysis of the 2016 US election polls shows tiny non-random selection shrinking a huge survey to a small draw; pricing this archival defect prevents running Regime II on a bent throttle.
- *Intervals straddling cost:* Manski's (2003) partial identification, Lee's (2009) monotone attrition bounds, and Peterson's (1976) competing-risks bounds leave intervals $[L\_t, U\_t]$ spanning $C$, motivating Regime II's minimax-regret probe.
- *Ruin outpacing authority:* When disruption outruns recovery latency, no archive refinement saves the vessel; Sosa's (2015) full aptness demands withholding the shot, voiced through Beer's (1972) algedonic halt.

Drive one safeguard to zero while two hold at unity: the arithmetic mean still reads $2/3$, whereas $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = 0$. Since $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} \le S\_a$ everywhere, averages hide single-axis collapse unless all coordinates happen to match.

When all three coordinates sit high and the live decision is trivial, declare saturation, note whichever axis nears its next constraint, leave allocation to per-step state, and drop any composite headline score.

## 6. Two limits of the minimum, and the purchase it recommends wrongly

### The viable volume at both ends of the threshold

Compare both threshold extremes on $[0, 1]^3$. Writing deficits $x\_a := 1 - S\_a \ge 0$ ($a \in \lbrace{}1, 2, 3\rbrace{}$) as $\tau = 1 - \epsilon \to 1^-$ ($\epsilon \to 0^+$), the Leontief box keeps $V\_{\min}(1 - \epsilon) = \epsilon^3$, whereas first-order product truncation cuts down to the simplex $\lbrace{}x\_1 + x\_2 + x\_3 \le \epsilon\rbrace$ with $V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(1 - \epsilon) = \frac{1}{6}\epsilon^3 + O(\epsilon^4)$, rejecting five-sixths of valid balanced states. As $\tau \to 0$, $V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)/V\_{\min}(\tau) \to 1$, so relative distortion $1 - V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)/V\_{\min}(\tau)$ rises monotonically from $0$ to $5/6$ across $\tau \in (0, 1)$.

Changing $\tau\_2$ to $\tau\_1$ scales orthant volume by $V\_{\min}(\tau\_1)/V\_{\min}(\tau\_2) = \bigl((1-\tau\_1)/(1-\tau\_2)\bigr)^3$: doubling headroom $1 - \tau$ (from $\tau = 3/4$ to $1/2$) multiplies viable volume eightfold. Within the top-anchored **restricted affine monoid** $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ ($0 < \alpha\_a \le 1$, closed under composition with identity $\alpha\_a = 1$), constant Jacobian $\det J\_\psi = \prod\_a \alpha\_a \in (0, 1]$ cancels in $V\_{\min}(\tau\_1)/V\_{\min}(\tau\_2)$ and scales general product boxes $\prod\_a [\psi\_a(\tau), 1]$ inside $\psi([0, 1]^3)$ ([Appendix A.4](appendix-a-mathematics.md#a4-leontief-structural-complementarity-of-the-nuclear-triad)). Once $\alpha\_a < 1$, $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ ceases to stay a product level set, and top-anchored $\psi\_a(0) = 1 - \alpha\_a > 0$ lifts the ruin floor off zero, erasing the zero-collapse anchor ($S\_a = 0 \implies M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = 0$); moreover, whenever slopes $\alpha\_a$ vary across axes, even this linear family breaks both symmetric cube thresholds ($[\tau', 1]^3$) and weakest-axis identity ($\arg\min\_a S\_a$). Valid $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$-preserving OLC recodings therefore require a **common, bottom-and-top-anchored** strictly increasing map ($\psi(0) = 0, \psi(1) = 1$) shared identically across all three axes, while nonlinear per-axis recodings additionally break constant Jacobian volume-ratio invariance.

### A district where the smallest coordinate is not the binding one

Place the three coordinates on a recurring municipal procurement district holding finite physical stock to see how an uncalibrated minimum misleads action. Design A scores low on its epistemic summary yet high on vital stock and governance. Design B scores moderately on epistemology and lower on vital stock and governance, so a naive raw minimum ranks Design B as safer.

The ledger quickly exposes the mistake. Because a decade of records bounds weekly demand strictly, Design A sizes capacity to that known support ceiling, making deficit identically zero while carrying surplus grows reserves safely over time. Design B sizes below that bound: whenever several high-demand weeks hit back-to-back, expediting costs drain its working reserve straight past zero into ruin, even though its raw minimum score looked cleaner.

That reversal happened because the district compared uncalibrated scores as common currency and turned rankings into spending rules. Design A's epistemic uncertainty carried a hard finite ceiling ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}} > 0$), so physical slack hedged the tail without contradicting non-substitutability at zero. Whether physical slack can partially trade off against degraded epistemic margins far from absorbing barriers stays open to pre-registered falsifier $F\_4$ in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) (§9.5.3).

### Where the minimum stops being a diagnosis

The minimum diagnoses reliably only when its three axes share one **externally custodied and physically/archivally anchored** scale satisfying **ordinal level comparability (OLC)**, invariant under any common strictly increasing rescaling across all three axes, rather than requiring cardinal dollars. Without OLC, independent rescaling flips weakest-axis identity: applying a strictly concave monotone map $f : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($f(0) = 0, f(1) = 1, f'' < 0$, so $f(x) > x$ on $(0, 1)$) solely to the smallest axis $S\_1 \in (0, 1)$ flips any neighbor in $S\_1 < S\_2 < f(S\_1)$ with zero change in reality (distinct from MEU's act $f$ in §2).

This constraint adapts Sen's (1985) informational-invariance test cleanly. Pure ordinal scaling tests single-axis threshold breaches, whereas cross-axis ranking requires OLC calibration (§2's ex-post bound already avoids Harsanyi's live maximin critique). Even under OLC, finite-sample minimum estimators carry downward bias $b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) \le 0$ ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.6), demanding simultaneous confidence rectangles rather than bare argmin point scores. Whenever OLC calibration fails genuinely across externally anchored scales, drop cross-axis $\arg\min$ ranking without reverting to cliff-gameable boolean flags: report three **unaggregated, non-compensatory native-unit shortfall ratios** relative to each axis's physical or archival failure threshold.

Continuous constant-elasticity aggregators $\bigl(\frac{1}{3}\sum\_a S\_a^{(\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}} - 1)/\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}}}\bigr)^{\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}}/(\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}} - 1)}$ on strictly positive coordinates $S\_a > 0$ ($\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}} > 0, \rho\_{\mathrm{CES}} \neq 1$) recover $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ as $\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}} \to 0^+$ and the unweighted geometric mean as $\rho\_{\mathrm{CES}} \to 1$. Such interior indices serve solely as declared, case-by-case sensitivity diagnostics far from absorbing barriers, never replacing the non-compensatory diagnostic minimum (never a weighted sum) and never steering the per-step operator, leaving bounded interior compensation open to falsifier $F\_4$ ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)).
