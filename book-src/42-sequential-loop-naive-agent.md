# Chapter 42 — The Sequential Loop and the Naive Optimizer

> Laboratory 1 of the `stochology` CLI: `--chapter 1`.

In Reinforcement Learning (Sutton & Barto), an agent interacts with an environment across discrete time steps $t = 0, 1, 2, \dots, T-1$. At each step, the agent receives a state or observation, chooses an action, and observes a scalar reward and the next state.

Why does that clean textbook abstraction break down when we move from a board game to a hospital emergency network, a municipal water reserve, a public credit guarantee fund, or a civil-defense evacuation system? Because in human and physical institutions, **four structural realities** intervene between the world and the decision-maker:

1. **The Archive is Mutilated:** The decision-maker never sees the latent world state $S_t$ directly; the decision-maker sees an institutional archive $O_t$ that arrives in correlated clusters and systematically under-records the most vulnerable strata ($\rho_{R,Y} \ne 0$).
2. **Harm is Convex and Tails are Heavy:** Demand surges follow power-law tails ($\alpha < 2$), and when service utilization $\rho_t = S_t / C_t$ approaches $100\%$, queue waiting times and unmet human needs explode nonlinearly according to Kingman's heavy-traffic law $\frac{\rho_t}{1 - \rho_t}$.
3. **The World Reacts Performatively:** High-powered bureaucratic targets on proxy metrics ($\beta_{\mathrm{proxy}} \to 1$) induce strategic gaming and erode the underlying capacity of the institution over subsequent steps.
4. **Time is Irreversible and Ruin is Absorbing:** The community depends on a finite vital reserve $R_t \ge 0$. If over-leverage or unbuffered overload drains $R_{t+1} \le 0$ before the institution can recover ($\mathrm{TTS}_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}_{\mathrm{recovery}}$), the system hits an absorbing ruin barrier.

## 1.1 The Evolving Environment: "The Community and the Reserve" (`CommunityReserveEnv`)

In [`crates/stochology/src/engine.rs`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs), [`CommunityReserveEnv`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs#L416-L423) formalizes this sequential loop over $T = 24$ shifts under a pre-registered [`SplitMix64`](crates/stochology/src/kernel.rs#L21-L23) stream (`DEFAULT_SEED = 2026`):

- **Latent Community Demand ($S_t$):** At each shift $t$, the community consists of $C = 6$ neighborhood clusters of $10$ individuals each ($N = 60$). Clusters $c \in \lbrace 0, 1, 2, 3\rbrace$ are well-served neighborhoods; clusters $c \in \lbrace 4, 5\rbrace$ are high-vulnerability neighborhoods experiencing higher baseline need ($+12.0$ units) and stronger exposure to Pareto tail surges ($\alpha = 1.45$) on crisis shifts.
- **Selective Archive Recording ($O_t$):** While well-served neighborhoods have a $95\%$ recording propensity in the administrative database, vulnerable neighborhoods suffer $60\%$ non-recording (`MNAR` exclusion). Consequently, the raw archive mean $\bar{O}_t$ systematically underestimates true community need $S_t$, while intra-cluster shocks inflate the variance of $\bar{O}_t$.
- **Agent Action (`AgentDecision`):** Given [`ArchiveObservation`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs#L16-L29), any policy implementing [`AgentPolicy`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs#L170-L176) chooses an [`AgentDecision`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs#L33-L50):
  - an epistemic demand interval $[L_t, U_t]$ and recognized effective sample size $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$;
  - provisioned shift capacity $C_t$, vital reserve buffer $B_t$, and growth leverage $f_t$;
  - proxy incentive pressure $\beta_{\mathrm{proxy}}$, frontline testimonial discount $\delta_{\mathrm{cred}}$, and whether the constitutional emergency brake ($S3^*$) is active.

### 1.1.1 State-Space Dynamics of the Triadic Mediation Loop

Formally, the interaction unfolds as a non-Markovian dynamical system across World ($P_1$), Archive ($P_2$), and Institution ($P_3$):

1. **Latent World Transition:** The true community state evolves as:
   $$S_{t+1} \sim \mathcal{T}(S_t, A_t, \boldsymbol{\omega}_t)$$
   where $\boldsymbol{\omega}_t \sim \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{shock}}$ is an exogenous vector of cluster-level baseline vulnerabilities and heavy-tailed Pareto shocks, modulated by performative erosion induced by prior policy choices $A_t$.
2. **Selective Archive Filtering:** The administrative observation is generated by a censoring and clustering filter:
   $$O_t \sim \mathcal{M}(S_t, \mathbf{R}_t)$$
   where $\mathbf{R}_t \in \{0, 1\}^N$ is the recording indicator vector. Because vulnerable strata have non-random reporting propensities ($\mathbb{P}(R_{ci} = 1 \mid c \ge 4) = 0.40$ vs. $0.95$ for $c < 4$, so $\rho_{R,Y} \ne 0$), the raw sample mean $\bar{O}_t$ is systematically displaced downward from the true latent mean $\bar{S}_t$.
3. **Vital Reserve Evolution:** The community's physical buffer evolves by the balance equation:
   $$R_{t+1} = R_t + \Delta R_t(A_t, S_t)$$
   where net drift $\Delta R_t$ accounts for shift replenishment, baseline holding costs, spot-procurement expediting costs when demand exceeds provisioned capacity ($S_t > C_t$), and queue congestion penalties.

### 1.1.2 Symmetric Convex Loss vs. Dirac-Infinite Absorbing Ruin

Standard reinforcement learning and econometric loss functions typically assume symmetric or unconstrained convex penalties:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sym}}(C_t, S_t) = \frac{1}{2}(C_t - S_t)^2 \quad \text{or} \quad \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{lin}}(C_t, S_t) = |C_t - S_t|$$
Under such formulations, under-provisioning ($C_t < S_t$) is treated as commensurable with over-provisioning ($C_t > S_t$): a deficit of $5$ units today can supposedly be offset by a surplus of $5$ units tomorrow.

In physical and institutional reality, this symmetry is a catastrophic illusion. The vital reserve $R_t$ possesses an **absorbing ruin boundary** at zero:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{vital}}(R_{t+1}) = \begin{cases} \phi(S_t - C_t) & \text{if } R_{t+1} > 0 \\ +\infty & \text{if } R_{t+1} \le 0 \end{cases}$$
When $R_{t+1} \le 0$, the institution enters bankruptcy, stockout, or clinical shutdown: effective capacity drops to zero ($C_\tau = 0$) for all remaining time steps $\tau > t$. Once absorbed, no future positive expectation can resurrect the institution. Sizing capacity for "lean" $95\%$ utilization of an underestimated mean guarantees that an unbuffered tail shock drives $R_{t+1} \le 0$, converting a finite temporary deficit into an absorbing institutional fatality.

## 1.2 Why the `NaiveOptimizer` Collapses

Consider the standard point-optimizing benchmark, [`NaiveOptimizer`](crates/stochology/src/engine.rs#L184):

```rust
impl AgentPolicy for NaiveOptimizer {
    fn name(&self) -> &str {
        "NaivePointOptimizer"
    }

    fn act(&mut self, obs: &ArchiveObservation) -> AgentDecision {
        let all_obs: Vec<f64> = obs
            .observed_clusters
            .iter()
            .flat_map(|c| c.iter().copied())
            .collect();
        let raw_mean = mean(&all_obs);
        let raw_n = all_obs.len() as f64;
        let raw_se = (sample_variance(&all_obs) / raw_n.max(1.0)).sqrt();
        let vital = VitalMarginState::for_shift_sizing(
            &all_obs,
            raw_mean,
            obs.current_reserve,
            raw_n,
            0.02,
        );
        // Textbook i.i.d. Gaussian 95% CI, 95% utilization ("lean efficiency"), tiny 2% margin, and unshrunk plug-in Kelly
        AgentDecision {
            epistemic_demand_bounds: (raw_mean - 1.96 * raw_se, raw_mean + 1.96 * raw_se),
            recognized_n_eff: raw_n,
            provisioned_capacity: raw_mean / 0.95,
            vital_reserve_allocation: 0.02 * obs.current_reserve,
            growth_leverage_f: vital.plugin_kelly_fraction,
            proxy_incentive_pressure: 0.85,
            frontline_testimonial_discount: 0.60,
            constitutional_brake_active: false,
        }
    }
}
```

By treating the raw archive sample as i.i.d. and missing completely at random ($\bar{O}_t \pm 1.96 s/\sqrt{n}$, $n_{\mathrm{eff}} = n$), sizing capacity for "lean" $95\%$ utilization of that downward-biased mean, applying unshrunk plug-in Kelly leverage (`vital.plugin_kelly_fraction`), imposing high proxy pressure ($\beta_{\mathrm{proxy}} = 0.85$), and discounting frontline algedonic warnings by $\delta_{\mathrm{cred}} = 0.60$, `NaiveOptimizer` violates all three axes of the Nuclear Triad simultaneously.

- **The Naive Optimizer (`NaivePointOptimizer`, `[Simulated]`):** Its textbook i.i.d. Gaussian 95% confidence interval $\bar{O}_t \pm 1.96 s/\sqrt{n}$ covers true community demand on only **16.7% of shifts** (`coverage = 16.7%`, `[Simulated]`) because Meng selection bias ($\rho_{R,Y} \ne 0$) shifts the archive center downward while cluster correlation ($\mathrm{DEFF} > 1$) understates the true standard error. Because true demand exceeds its downward-biased "lean" capacity, Kingman queue congestion and spot-procurement drains exhaust the vital reserve at shift $t = 2$ (`ruin_step = Some(2)`, `terminal_reserve = 0.00`, `[Simulated]`), after which absorbing ruin shuts down operational capacity (`effective_capacity = 0.0`) and drives mean unserved social harm to `125.83` (`[Simulated]`).
- **Proposition 1 Degradation (`NoEpistemicMargin`, `[Simulated]`):** When an agent preserves Vital Margin and Institutional Governance but disables the Epistemic Margin (`use_epistemic_margin = false`), it sizes capacity against the raw archive mean $\bar{O}_t$ without upper-bound precautionary compensation. Its intervals cover true demand on only **25.0% of shifts** (`0.0%` on tail shocks, `[Simulated]`), social harm nearly triples (`4.49` vs. `1.59`, `[Simulated]`), and emergency expediting drains plus buffer-holding costs from unbuffered epistemic surprises erode the reserve below its initial endowment ($R_{24} = 27.21 < R_0 = 45.0$, with a $4.0\%$ absorbing ruin rate across $100$ Monte Carlo seeds, `[Simulated]`).
- **The Stochological Agent (`StochologicalAgent::full()`, `[Simulated]`):** By combining the **Epistemic Margin** (Manski bounds + Meng selection location correction + multiplicative Moulton/Kish sampling-variance adjustment $n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = \max(n / (\mathrm{DEFF}_{\mathrm{cluster}}\cdot\mathrm{DEFF}_{\mathrm{Kish}}),\, 4)$), the **Vital Margin** (Jensen convexity correction + Kingman slack $\rho \le 0.76$ + dynamic empirical Baker–McHale epistemic Kelly shrinkage using $n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ and $r = 0.02$ + Canguilhem–Holling attraction-basin protection), and **Institutional Governance** (low proxy distortion + undiscounted frontline algedonic transmission $1 - \delta_{\mathrm{cred}} = 1.0$), the agent covers true community demand on **95.8% of shifts** (`75.0%` on heavy-tailed shock shifts where shift $t = 22$ exhibits an out-of-support radical novelty surge beyond $Y_{\max} = 52.0$, `[Simulated]`), reduces mean social harm to `1.59` (`[Simulated]`), never hits ruin (`ruin_step = None`), and grows the community's vital reserve from $45.00$ to $58.17$ (`[Simulated]`).
