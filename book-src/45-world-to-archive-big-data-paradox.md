The second is [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)'s **bias-penalized effective count** $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$ and [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md)'s evidential weight $V\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}), n\_0\bigr) \in [0, 1)$. Both are evaluated at a pre-declared selection-bias bound $\bar{\rho} \ge |\rho\_{R,Y}|$ (with $\bar{\rho} \in [0, 1]$, sampling fraction $f = n/N \in (0, 1]$, and target threshold $n\_0 > 0$).

## 4.1 Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$ vs. Probability $p$

In Chapter VI of *A Treatise on Probability* (1921), John Maynard Keynes drew a distinction that textbook statistics almost forgot:

> *"As the relevant evidence at our disposal increases, the magnitude of the probability of the argument may either decrease or increase, according as the new knowledge strengthens the unfavourable or the favourable evidence; but **something** seems to have increased in either case, we have a more substantial basis upon which to rest our conclusion."*

In plain words, a deeply audited household cohort and two unverified rumors can yield the exact **same probability** $p$, yet rest on radically different evidential depth. Keynes (1921) compares evidential weight $V(a/h)$ ordinally rather than fixing a closed formula. Stochology turns that ordering into a bounded score on $[0, 1)$:
$$V\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}), n\_0\bigr) = \frac{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) + n\_0}.$$
Here $n\_0 := n\_{\mathrm{req}}(\alpha, \beta, p^\ast) > 0$ is [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md)'s required sample target. It is calibrated by error tolerances $\alpha, \beta \in (0, 1)$ ($\alpha + \beta < 1$) and relative resolution $\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}} > 0$ at interior cutoff $p^\ast = C/(\eta L) \in (0, 1)$ under $0 < C < \eta L$ and $p^\ast(1 + \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}}) \le 1$.

In plain words, $V$ hits $0$ under zero evidence, $1/2$ at $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) = n\_0$, and nears $1$ only well past target $n\_0$. Crucially, $V$ never averages with $p$; under **Reflexive Brake 1** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)), neither $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$ nor $V$ may be gamed by feigning $\bar{\rho} = 0$ or pushing $\bar{\rho} \to 1$ to manufacture inaction without reporting breakdown points.

Notice one vital limit: $V \to 1$ saturates *only inside the recording frame*. If peripheral households in *The Community and the Reserve* are *Not askable* ($\pi\_i = 0$ on every registry; [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md)), $V$ can near $1$ while uncounted harm registers only afterward in the strictly retrospective diagnostic $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr)$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)).

## 4.2 Xiao-Li Meng's (2018) Exact Big Data Paradox Identity: Algebraic Proof

Millions of rows can carry *less* truth about the mean than a few hundred random draws. In *Statistical paradises and paradoxes in big data (I)*, Xiao-Li Meng (2018) proved an exact identity separating true error from nominal archive size.

### 4.2.1 Proof of the Big Data Identity

Take a finite community of $N$ households with recorded fraction $f = n/N \in (0, 1)$. Xiao-Li Meng's (2018) non-asymptotic decomposition ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)) factors the gap between archive mean $\bar{Y}\_n$ and population mean $\bar{Y}\_N$ into three exact terms: **Data Defect** ($\rho\_{R,Y}$, correlation between recording and need), **Data Scarcity** ($\sqrt{(1 - f)/f}$), and **Problem Difficulty** ($\sigma\_Y$, population spread). Unlike superpopulation approximations, this finite-population identity vanishes identically at the full census boundary $f = 1$.

### 4.2.2 The Collapse of Selection-Adjusted Effective Sample Size ($n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$)

Under non-ignorable MNAR selection ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$; Rubin 1976—whereas MCAR/MAR designs remove expected conditional bias once weighted), Meng's (2018) bias-equivalent sample size is $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\rho\_{R,Y}) = \frac{f}{1 - f}\mkern3mu\rho\_{R,Y}^{-2}$ ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)). Notice how the two adjustments play different roles: $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = n / \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}$ inflates sampling variance and standard errors, whereas $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ prices squared bias inside total mean squared error (MSE).

At any fixed coverage fraction $f < 1$, $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ refuses to grow with raw sample size $n$. That extends P. C. Mahalanobis's (1946) classic warning from interpenetrating sub-samples: scaling up a biased survey only magnifies systematic distortion unless audited against parallel random sub-draws.

**The defect is a hypothesis, not a measurement.** Because $\rho\_{R,Y}$ compares recorded households against unseen $\bar{Y}\_N$, no internal archive contains it. Cauchy–Schwarz ($|\rho\_{R,Y}| \le 1$) leaves a worst-case bias of $\sigma\_Y\sqrt{(1 - f)/f}$ ([Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)) that never shrinks as $n \to \infty$ at fixed $f$. That sets a **breakdown correlation** $\rho^\star\_{R,Y} \in (0, 1]$ at action threshold $\bar{Y}\_{\mathrm{crit}}$ ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)), which itself requires an outside scale anchor or support bounds $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ since population spread $\sigma\_Y$ is likewise unobserved under selection.

Every effective-sample claim therefore rests on a pre-declared bound $\bar{\rho} \ge |\rho\_{R,Y}|$ (with weighted bounds entering via $f\_w$ in §4.4). Combining variance inflation $1/n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}/n$ (§4.3) with Meng's bias penalty places the effective count $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(|\rho\_{R,Y}|)$ across $|\rho\_{R,Y}| \le \bar{\rho}$ inside:

$$n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(|\rho\_{R,Y}|) \in \left[\Bigl(\frac{\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}}{n} + \frac{1 - f}{f}\bar{\rho}^{2}\Bigr)^{-1},\mkern5mu n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}\right],$$

In plain words, the top end $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ governs random standard errors, while the bottom end $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$ governs total error (MSE) and evidential weight $V$. Reporting $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ alone silently assumes zero selection bias ($\bar{\rho} = 0$).

**Sizing reads the same algebra backwards.** Suppose we need a target effective sample $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}} > 0$ inside community size $N \ge 1$, design effect $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 1$, and selection bound $\bar{\rho} \in [0, 1]$. Solving $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) \ge n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$ requires raw archive size $n$ to satisfy:

$$n \mkern5mu\ge\mkern5mu \frac{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}\bigl(\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} + N\bar{\rho}^{2}\bigr)}{1 + n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}\bar{\rho}^{2}}.$$

In plain words, as suspected selection bias $\bar{\rho}$ grows, the required raw sample size climbs steeply toward covering nearly the entire community $N$. At $\bar{\rho} = 0$, the floor simplifies to $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}\mkern3mu\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}$. For fixed $N$ and feasible variance targets ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}\mkern3mu\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} < N$), the floor climbs with $\bar{\rho}$ toward $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}(\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} + N)/(1 + n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}) < N$ as $\bar{\rho} \to 1$, reaching $N$ only as $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}\mkern3mu\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} \to N^-$ (or when $N, n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}} \to \infty$ with $\bar{\rho} > 0$). Sample-size targets stated without $\bar{\rho}$ therefore hide an unstated independence assumption.

## 4.3 Kish–Moulton Cluster Design Effects ($\mathrm{DEFF}$) and Unequal Cluster Sizes

Even when selection bias vanishes ($\rho\_{R,Y} = 0$), hospital wards and neighborhoods in *The Community and the Reserve* share local shocks and arrive in correlated waves. Throughout this book, *Kish–Moulton* denotes Kish's (1965) cluster design effect paired with Moulton's (1986) unequal-cluster-size adjustment.

Suppose $C \ge 1$ clusters have non-zero sizes $m\_1, \dots, m\_C$ with average cluster size $\bar{m} > 0$, relative dispersion $\mathrm{CV}(m) = \sigma\_m / \bar{m}$, and intra-cluster correlation $\rho\_I$. Brent R. Moulton (1986) proved that uneven cluster sizes inflate effective cluster size to $\tilde{m} = \bar{m}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr)$ and multiply variance by $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I > 0$ ($\ge 1$ under random-effects $\rho\_I \in [0, 1]$; [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)), so dropping $\mathrm{CV}^2(m)$ understates uncertainty. Under unit-level weights independent inside clusters and uncorrelated with cluster membership and cluster effects in the large-$C$ limit, **the exact composition is additive** ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - 1$); under cluster-constant or shock-correlated weights, multiplying both factors yields only a conservative upper bound.

## 4.4 Horvitz–Thompson / Kish Weighting and Chao's (1984) Lower Bound on the Uncounted

Two distinct instruments recover information about under-recorded groups, alongside a causal rule for merging disparate streams.

### Horvitz–Thompson, Hájek, and Kish Weighting

Unbiased Horvitz–Thompson (1952) estimation requires known inclusion probabilities $\pi\_i > 0$ across **every one of the $N$ population units** (plus known $N$ for the Horvitz–Thompson mean, normalized weights giving the **Hájek ratio estimator** when $N$ is unknown, and pairwise probabilities $\pi\_{ij} > 0$ for unbiased variance). Estimated propensity scores $\hat{\pi}\_i(X\_i)$ fail whenever missingness depends on unobserved need within covariates (Meng, 2018; [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)). Moreover, uneven weights inflate variance by Kish's (1965) design effect $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} = 1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(w) \ge 1$, shrinking Kish's effective count to $n\_{\mathrm{eff, Kish}} = n / \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$ ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)).

Under clustering ($\rho\_I \ge 0$), $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} \le n\_{\mathrm{eff, Kish}}$, so Kish's weighted count is only an upper bound, not a conservative floor. Better yet, look at the self-normalized **Hájek** ratio estimator $\tilde{Y}\_n := \sum\_{i=1}^N R\_i w\_i Y\_i / \sum\_{i=1}^N R\_i w\_i$, setting $\tilde{R}\_i = w\_i R\_i$. Meng (2018) proves an exact weighted bias identity (whereas the unnormalized Horvitz–Thompson mean diverges whenever $\sum\_{i=1}^N R\_i w\_i \ne N$):
$$\tilde{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = \rho\_{\tilde{R},Y}\sqrt{\frac{1 - f + \mathrm{CV}^2(w)}{f}}\mkern3mu\sigma\_Y = \rho\_{\tilde{R},Y}\sqrt{\frac{1 - f\_w}{f\_w}}\mkern3mu\sigma\_Y.$$

In plain words, noisy survey weights shrink the effective sampling fraction from $f$ down to $f\_w = f/\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} \le f$ (via $(1 - f\_w)/f\_w = (\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - f)/f$). For $f \in (0, 1)$, uneven weights also amplify any remaining weighted selection bias $|\rho\_{\tilde{R},Y}| \le \bar{\rho}\_w$ by the factor $a\_w = \sqrt{1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(w)/(1 - f)} \ge 1$—and even at a complete census ($f = 1$), $(1 - f\_w)/f\_w = \mathrm{CV}^2(w)$ leaves non-zero residual weighting error $\tilde{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = \rho\_{w,Y}\mathrm{CV}(w)\sigma\_Y$ whenever arbitrary weights correlate with need ($\rho\_{w,Y} \ne 0$), distinct from Kish's analytic variance inflation $1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(w)$.

### Capture-Recapture for *Not Observed* vs. *Not Askable* Units

When records come from incomplete lists rather than a probability sample, capture-recapture counts overlap across detections to lower-bound the uncounted (Chao 1984, 1987, 1989; Chao & Colwell 2017; Lum, Price, & Banks 2013). In single-stream **abundance** sampling with repeated detections ($\lambda\_i > 0$) and individual heterogeneity (**Model $M\_h$**), Chao1 gives $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao1}} = f\_1^2/(2 f\_2)$ whenever doubletons $f\_2 > 0$ (Chao 1984, 1987).

For deduplicated binary registries under symmetric binomial incidence (**Model $M\_h$**, Chao 1989; Chao & Colwell 2017), multi-list cross-referencing yields the $t = 2$ `Chao2` lower bound $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(2) = f\_1^2/(4 f\_2) \ge n\_{10} n\_{01}/n\_{11} = \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{LP}}$ ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)). In plain words, many single-list sightings ($f\_1$) paired with few cross-list matches ($f\_2$) imply a large unseen pool $\hat{n}\_{00}$.

Notice three boundaries where this estimator behaves differently:

- Under homogeneous list-varying coverage (**Model $M\_t$**, $p\_{ij} = p\_j$), $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}] = \mathbb{E}[n\_{10}]\mathbb{E}[n\_{01}]/\mathbb{E}[n\_{11}]$ matches Lincoln–Petersen, while AM–GM inequality can push `Chao2` *above* $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}]$ (even past $N$).
- Under mixed unit-and-list heterogeneity (**Model $M\_{th}$**, $p\_{i1} \ne p\_{i2}$) or negative list dependence, neither `Chao2` nor Lincoln–Petersen guarantees a safe lower bound (Chao 1989).
- Using the bias-corrected Chao–Colwell (2017) floor $\frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{f\_1(f\_1 - 1)}{2(f\_2 + 1)}$—matching $f\_1(f\_1 - 1)/\bigl(2(f\_2 + 1)\bigr)$ in Chao1 and $\frac{f\_1(f\_1 - 1)}{4(n\_{11} + 1)}$ at $t = 2$—preserves finiteness when zero doubletons appear ($f\_2 = n\_{11} = 0$).

Crucially, cross-list linkage sees only **Not observed** units eligible for at least one registry. Households in *The Community and the Reserve* that are **Not askable** ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$ outside every registry at Guard Site $T\_1$; [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md); [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)) never enter $f\_1$ or $f\_2$, leaving $\hat{n}\_{00}$ and evidential weight $V \to 1$ completely blind while real vulnerability spills across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

### Causal Data Fusion Across Heterogeneous Sources

Can we merge a biased selected dataset ($S = 1$) with an unbiased reference survey? Bareinboim and Pearl (2016) prove exact recovery $P(y \mid \mathrm{do}(x)) = \sum\_z P(y \mid x, z, S = 1)\mkern2mu P(z)$ when covariates split into nondescendants $Z^+$ and descendants $Z^-$ of treatment $X$ satisfying their selection-backdoor criterion inside the **selection-augmented causal graph $G\_s$** (keeping *selection diagrams* reserved for cross-domain transportability):

1. $Z^+$ blocks all backdoor paths from $X$ to $Y$.
2. $(X, Z^+)$ blocks paths between $Z^-$ and $Y$ ($Z^- \perp Y \mid X, Z^+$).
3. $(X, Z)$ separates selection indicator $S$ from $Y$.
4. $Z$ is recorded in the unbiased source alongside $(X, Y, Z)$ under $S = 1$.

Their data-fusion completeness theorem proves causal fusion fails at *any* sample size $n$ if admissibility breaks in $G\_s$. Conversely, when post-treatment mediators sit on causal paths to $Y$ ($Z \not\perp Y \mid X$), marginal $P(z)$ reweighting fails and requires interventional weights $P(z \mid \mathrm{do}(x))$. Their theorems also assume known selection structure and zero measurement error, pointing directly to §4.6.

## 4.5 Bitemporal Vintages: Event Time ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$) vs. Availability Time ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$)

### Event time, availability time, and vintage errors

Archives are moving targets: hospital logs and economic series get revised weeks or months after the shift ends. Building on Snodgrass's (2000) bitemporal schema above mutable storage engines and nullable joins ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)), every append-only record pairs **valid (event) time** $t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$ with **transaction (availability) time** $t\_{\mathrm{disp}} \ge t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$.

Any entry with $t\_{\mathrm{disp}} < t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$ indicates clock corruption or illicit back-dating and is rejected immediately. Conditioning a decision made at time $\tau$ on data posted afterward ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}} \le \tau < t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$) causes **look-ahead leakage**, whereas ignoring late-arriving events causes **right-truncation**. Real-time choices may read only records already available ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}} \le \tau$). Flattening vintages creates three classic errors:

- Real-time releases at $\tau$ can show growth ($\Delta Y > 0$) before later benchmark revisions flip them to contraction ($\Delta Y < 0$).
- Benchmark revisions can shift historical growth paths and alter sensitive empirical tests, forecasts, and policy-rule evaluations—even when broad stylized facts remain stable—as documented in the Philadelphia Fed Real-Time Data Set (Croushore & Stark 2001).
- Scoring past rules against final revised tables credits decision-makers with clairvoyance they could not possess, scrambling causal attribution.

### Urgency, edge disconnects, and CRDT custody

Cross-vintage reconciliation competes against limited human triage attention ([Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), and forcing exhaustive cross-registry checks during acute emergencies stalls response latency past survival capacity. Under time pressure, operators lock onto the **worst admissible prior sealed vintage** ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}} \le \tau$, not overturned by an audited fix) and postpone full reconciliation to off-shift review ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), keeping sealed inputs intact (**Seal test**, [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Locking caps deliberation delay only; it never licenses automated optimism, escalating instead to **Regime III** ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)) while preserving the bidirectional $T\_1 \leftrightarrow T\_3$ lock ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md) §2.3).

Remote sensors add another archive trap. When field batteries cut off at low-voltage disconnect (`LVD`), dropped packets correlate with local crises ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$) and breed survivorship bias unless pipelines log silent stations as explicit *A-marks* instead of zeros ([Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md)). Once partitioned units reconnect, merging append-only logs via conflict-free replicated data types (`CRDT`s; Shapiro, Preguiça, Baquero, & Zawirski 2011)—whose join operator is commutative, associative, and idempotent—guarantees consistent convergence without central locks, provided merges stay **causal-conservative** and preserve the widest admissible uncertainty bounds.

### Differential privacy and reliability-ratio attenuation

Confidentiality rules raise a subtle tension when clinical, welfare, or transitional-justice archives ([Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md)) must be audited without exposing individuals. Under **pure $\varepsilon$-differential privacy** (Dwork, McSherry, Nissim, & Smith 2006b), a randomized mechanism $\mathcal{K}$ with privacy budget $\varepsilon > 0$ protects adjacent datasets $x, x'$ differing by one record ($d\_H(x, x') \le 1$). For every measurable event $E$, it guarantees $\Pr[\mathcal{K}(x) \in E] \le e^\varepsilon \Pr[\mathcal{K}(x') \in E]$. Numerical queries $q$ achieve this using Laplace noise $\mathrm{Lap}(\Delta\_1 q / \varepsilon)$ scaled to $L\_1$ sensitivity $\Delta\_1 q = \max\_{d\_H(x, x') \le 1} \Vert{}q(x) - q(x')\Vert\_1$ (writing $\Delta\_1 q$ rather than $\Delta f$ to keep query sensitivity distinct from sampling fraction $f$).

**Approximate $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differential privacy** (Dwork, Kenthapadi, McSherry, Mironov, & Naor 2006a) relaxes that bound by an additive failure probability $\delta \in [0, 1)$:

$$\Pr[\mathcal{K}(x) \in E] \le e^\varepsilon \Pr[\mathcal{K}(x') \in E] + \delta.$$

Pure $\varepsilon$-DP sets $\delta = 0$; approximate $\delta > 0$ permits tail leakage up to $\delta$, requiring $\delta \ll 1/n$ because $\delta \sim 1/n$ exposes rare vulnerable individuals across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

Notice a critical non-commutation when auditing noisy mirrors. Perturbing individual records via $\mathcal{K}$ *before* computing a nonlinear statistic $\hat{\theta}$ does not match perturbing the statistic afterward:

$$\hat{\theta}\bigl(\mathcal{K}(X)\bigr) \neq \mathcal{K}\bigl(\hat{\theta}(X)\bigr).$$

In plain words, adding privacy noise before estimation warps nonlinear quantities—variances, MNAR quantiles, Meng bias ratios, and small-cell doubletons $f\_2$ in Chao's $\hat{n}\_{00}$ (§4.4). Even on mean diagnostics, represent record-level noise applied over observed rows ($R\_i = 1$) via a virtual population-length zero-mean perturbation $Z$ ($\mathbb{E}[Z] = 0$, distinct from §4.4's covariates) with variance $\sigma\_Z^2 \ge 0$. Assume finite-population orthogonality $\mathrm{Cov}\_N(R, Z) = \mathrm{Cov}\_N(Y, Z) = 0$ (which holds in expectation for independent draws, though finite realizations or data-dependent noise break scaling). For $\sigma\_Y > 0$ and $f \in (0, 1)$, total variance and Meng's correlation become:

$$\sigma\_{Y+Z}^2 = \sigma\_Y^2 + \sigma\_Z^2, \qquad \rho\_{R, Y+Z} = \frac{\mathrm{Cov}\_N(R, Y + Z)}{\sigma\_R \sqrt{\sigma\_Y^2 + \sigma\_Z^2}} = \rho\_{R,Y}\frac{\sigma\_Y}{\sqrt{\sigma\_Y^2 + \sigma\_Z^2}}.$$

In plain words, synthetic measurement noise masks real selection bias without curing it. Mirroring the classical reliability-ratio attenuation of [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md) (§1), whenever $\sigma\_Z > 0$ and $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$, $|\rho\_{R, Y+Z}| < |\rho\_{R,Y}|$ shrinks the apparent correlation. That inflates $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\rho\_{R, Y+Z}) = n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\rho\_{R,Y})(1 + \sigma\_Z^2/\sigma\_Y^2)$ **without reducing** true selection bias $\rho\_{R,Y}\sqrt{(1 - f)/f}\mkern3mu\sigma\_Y$—leaving *Blind Undersizing* and *Not askable* exclusions ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$) hidden behind milder-looking diagnostics.

These identities establish a **bitemporal two-layer archival rule** connecting [Chapter 06](06-who-is-missing-from-the-table.md), [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md), and [Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md). Public or exploratory audits may run on differentially private mirrors—provided each release states its calibration scale $\Delta\_1 q / \varepsilon$ (or added noise $\sigma\_Z^2$), $\delta$, and vintage time $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$. Legal custody chains, exact record linkage ($f\_2$ doubletons for $\hat{n}\_{00}$), and external checks of $\rho\_{R,Y}$, however, require unperturbed append-only originals. Holding those originals under strict off-shift audit controls ensures confidentiality never blocks retrospective $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ accountability.

## 4.6 Effective Sample Size in Three Real Archives

In *The Community and the Reserve* ([Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md)), **Collapse Proposition 1 (*Blind Undersizing*)** strikes through five linked channels:

- well-served clusters mask local distress ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}}$);
- survey weights amplify variance ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$) and shrink effective scarcity to $f\_w$;
- crisis dropout creates selection correlation ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$);
- multi-list linkage reaches only *Not observed* units ($\hat{n}\_{00}$), while *Not askable* households drain across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$);
- reporting lag right-truncates current shocks ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}} \le \tau < t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$).

Three classic archives illustrate those failures:

1. **Selection-Biased Surveys and Polls (Squire 1988; Meng 2018):** From the 1936 *Literary Digest* poll (Squire 1988) to massive non-probability panels (Meng 2018), even tiny $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$ collapses $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ orders of magnitude below raw count $n$, while weighting carries residual bias through $f\_w$. Re-reading vintages inside a biased stream never tests $\rho\_{R,Y} = 0$, because unrecorded people ($R\_i = 0$) appear in no vintage; auditing selection requires forced-response checks or external benchmarks (Meng 2018).
2. **Multi-Registry Human-Rights Documentation (Lum, Price, & Banks 2013; Chao 1989; Chao & Colwell 2017):** Combining incomplete civil and press records applies multiple-systems estimation (Lum, Price, & Banks 2013) alongside `Chao2` bounds on *Not observed* losses under $M\_h$, while staying blind to *Not askable* groups excluded from every list.
3. **Bitemporal Macroeconomic Vintages (Snodgrass 2000; Croushore & Stark 2001):** Bitemporal $(t\_{\mathrm{ev}}, t\_{\mathrm{disp}})$ append-only tables (Snodgrass 2000) preserve benchmark revisions (Croushore & Stark 2001); overwriting into flat tables destroys the revision denominator permanently.

### Beyond sampling defect: institutional label substitution at scale

Even when selection bias vanishes ($\rho\_{R,Y} = 0$), weighting is unnecessary, and clusters are independent, Guard Site $T\_3$ still breaks if the institution predicts a convenient proxy label instead of the physical need at stake. Auditing a widely deployed health-care risk-management algorithm, Obermeyer, Powers, Vogeli, and Mullainathan (2019) showed that training scores to predict annual medical spending rather than underlying illness achieves cost calibration across demographic groups while causing systematic clinical under-allocation. At equal chronic illness burden, unequal health-care access depresses recorded spending for disadvantaged patients, so ranking patients by predicted cost places healthier advantaged patients ahead of sicker disadvantaged ones (Obermeyer et al. 2019).

Human clinicians acting on predicted-cost tiers inherit that proxy distortion, and adding millions of insured records never cures an error baked into the target label itself. Fixing the bias requires replacing convenience spending labels with a label measuring clinical illness directly (Obermeyer et al. 2019). Just like definitional measurement limits in [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md), archive-to-institution transfer fails whenever convenience swaps spending for need.

## 4.7 The two ends of the sampling fraction, and the quantile the weight cannot see

### Two limits of the harmonic law

Take sampling fraction $f \in (0, 1)$ and non-zero selection correlation $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$ ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md)). As $f \to 1^-$, missing mass disappears and $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}} \to \infty$; as $f \to 0^+$ at fixed bias, $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}} \to 0$ completely. At any fixed coverage share $f$ as $n, N \to \infty$, $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ does not budge: multiplying archive rows a hundredfold leaves selection MSE untouched while shrinking naive standard errors tenfold. Even at fixed $N$ and small $f$, each added row contributes only at rate $1/(N\rho\_{R,Y}^2)$, whereas cutting selection bias scales inverse-quadratically.

Clustering obeys symmetric edge limits. As cluster-size dispersion vanishes ($\mathrm{CV}(m) \to 0$), $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} \to 1 + (\bar{m} - 1)\rho\_I$; as intra-cluster correlation vanishes ($\rho\_I \to 0$), cluster inflation disappears and $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$. Omitting either Moulton's $\mathrm{CV}^2(m)$ or Kish's weighting dispersion overstates precision in $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$.

### The quantile the effective sample cannot deliver

Reporting $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ beside raw $n$ still fails when an institution's promise governs a tail quantile rather than an average. A clinic staffed to the upper bound of a confidence interval for the *mean* daily load can fail its target on many days if its mandate protects a quantile, because a mean's design effect does not control a quantile estimand. Under clustering, the exceedance indicator $\mathbf{1}\lbrace{}Y\_i \le q\_p\rbrace$ has its own quantile correlation, and precision passes through local density $f\_Y(q\_p)$ rather than standard deviation $s$.

Even in independent finite-variance draws at quantile $p \in (0, 1)$ with positive continuous density $f\_Y(q\_p) > 0$, the two standard errors behave differently. Mean error $\mathrm{se}(\bar{y}) = s/\sqrt{n}$ ($n \ge 1$) and asymptotic quantile error $\mathrm{se}(\hat{q}\_p) = \sqrt{p(1-p)}/(\sqrt{n}\mkern3muf\_Y(q\_p))$ target distinct functionals—and flat tail density ($f\_Y(q\_p) \to 0^+$) collapses quantile precision at any $n$.

Now add missing-not-at-random selection ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$) with observed share $f \in (0, 1]$, observed distribution $F\_{\mathrm{obs}}(y) = \mathbb{P}(Y \le y \mid R = 1)$, and arbitrary unseen distribution $F\_{\mathrm{mis}}(y) \in [0, 1]$. Total probability traps $F\_Y(y) = f F\_{\mathrm{obs}}(y) + (1 - f) F\_{\mathrm{mis}}(y)$ inside $[f F\_{\mathrm{obs}}(y),\mkern3mu f F\_{\mathrm{obs}}(y) + 1 - f]$. On bounded physical support $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ (Manski 1990; Manski 2003), inverting that envelope produces sharp distribution-free **MNAR quantile bounds** $q\_L(p)$ and $q\_U(p)$:

$$q\_L(p) = \begin{cases} y\_{\min}, & p \le 1 - f, \\\\ F\_{\mathrm{obs}}^{-1}\\!\left(\frac{p - (1 - f)}{f}\right), & p > 1 - f, \end{cases} \qquad q\_U(p) = \begin{cases} F\_{\mathrm{obs}}^{-1}\\!\left(\frac{p}{f}\right), & p \le f, \\\\ y\_{\max}, & p > f, \end{cases}$$

In plain words, the unseen share $1 - f$ could lie anywhere inside $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$: $q\_L(p)$ stays pinned at $y\_{\min}$ for $p \le 1 - f$, while $q\_U(p)$ jumps to $y\_{\max}$ once $p > f$ regardless of sample size $n$—and both envelopes fail under unbounded heavy tails passed to Filter II.

### When the archive has no single denominator

A single harmonic effective count presupposes a fixed community $N$, design-known $\pi\_i > 0$ across all $N$ households, and one stable recording rule. Guard Site $T\_3$ hits its boundary in four situations:

1. Full local recording ($f = 1$) leaves $\rho\_{R,Y}$ undefined without certifying transportability or target labels (Obermeyer et al. 2019).
2. Households are *Not askable* ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$ on every registry).
3. $M\_t$/$M\_{th}$ heterogeneity or post-treatment mediators ($Z \not\perp Y \mid X$) break `Chao2` or causal fusion.
4. Revisions shift $N$ while leaving $t\_{\mathrm{ev}} \approx \tau$ right-truncated.

Paired with $\mathrm{PRI}$ at $T\_1$ (§4.5; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), archives must be read vintage by vintage without mixing denominators, auditing shifts in $N$ externally. Lock urgent choices onto the worst admissible sealed prior vintage (§4.5) while escalating to Regime III under vital strain ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)), keeping finished-run accounting strictly in the retrospective co-diagnostic pair $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr)$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)).

## Bridge

Filter I closes at Guard Site $T\_3$ with an honest archival denominator instead of a raw row count. Five bounded deliverables pass downstream to Filter II ([Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md) and [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)):

- $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ for standard errors and leverage shrinkage;
- the conditional interval $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(|\rho\_{R,Y}|)$ and evidential weight $V$ under declared $\bar{\rho}$ (or weighted $\bar{\rho}\_w$ at $f\_w$);
- the *Not observed* lower bound $\hat{n}\_{00}$ kept distinct from *Not askable* $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ harm;
- bounded-support MNAR quantile envelopes $[q\_L(p), q\_U(p)]$;
- the worst admissible sealed vintage ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}} \le \tau$, escalating to Regime III under deliberation strain), backed by unperturbed primary custody rather than noisy public mirrors ([Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md)).

Yet even a perfectly audited archive on bounded support still assumes interval scales, finite moments, and symmetric penalties. [The Non-Identical, Scale Invariance, Convexity, and Heavy Tails](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md) opens Filter II by confronting ordinal scale distortion, Jensen's convex harm gap ($\Delta\_J$), and heavy-tailed regimes ($\alpha \le 2$) where variance and bounded envelopes collapse.
