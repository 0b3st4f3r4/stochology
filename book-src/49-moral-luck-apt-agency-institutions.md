## 8.1 Sosa's Virtue Epistemology, Outcome Bias, and Bitemporal Telic Evaluation

In landmark accounts of **moral luck**, Thomas Nagel (1979) showed how events beyond an actor's control retroactively alter moral blame, while Bernard Williams (1981) showed how unlucky outcomes leave authentic non-punitive *agent-regret* even after careful execution. Jonathan Baron and John C. Hershey (1988) documented the experimental twin as **outcome bias**: observers downgrade the very same medical decision or monetary gamble whenever bad luck follows rather than good luck, a bias Stochology extends to high-stakes institutional and operational reviews. Ahead of acting under risk, institutional duties also constrain what an agent may reasonably be expected to know under high stakes (Fantl & McGrath 2009) and risk-imposition ethics ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md); Hansson 2013).

### 8.1.1 Antipater's Archer: Télos vs. Skopós and Sosa's AAA Taxonomy

Overcoming outcome bias must not excuse sloppy intentions. Following **Antipater of Tarsus**'s distinction between internal care (*télos*) and the external mark or hitting it (*skopós*; Stobaeus 1884, *Eclogae* II.76.11–15, II.77.16–27; Cicero 45 BCE, *De Finibus* III.22; Plutarch *c.* 100 CE, *Comm. not.* 1070f–1071e; [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md)) and **Ernest Sosa**'s (2007, 2015) **AAA** framework (*Accuracy, Adroitness, Aptness* free of Gettier-style luck; Gettier 1963; [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md)), Gate 3 sorts episodes into four verdicts:

- **Inadroit and inaccurate:** careless action that also failed.
- **Lucky accuracy:** a Gettier-style escape or unbuffered gamble that hit by chance, never praised as apt.
- **Adroit and unlucky:** sound care defeated by an exogenous tail shock ($L\_{\mathrm{exog}}$), never scapegoated.
- **Apt / reflectively fully apt:** accurate performance earned through skill (while under insufficient evidential weight $V$, meta-aptness demands **forbearance**, suspending the risky attempt rather than claiming an accurate hit).

### 8.1.2 Cryptographic Commitment to the Ex-Ante State

To lock that separation into institutional memory, Gate 3 seals the ex-ante competence record $C(\pi\_t \mid \mathcal{I}\_{t\_{\mathrm{disp}}})$ inside a single-opening SHA-256 and keyed HMAC-SHA256 commitment using RFC 2104's keyed-hash construction (Krawczyk, Bellare, & Canetti 1997) **before** outcome $Y\_{t+1}$ occurs. Because a symmetric HMAC key held solely by the audited organization still permits **key-holder equivocation** (drafting conflicting versions ahead of disclosure), valid commitment custody requires **external multi-party or asymmetric witness key custody paired with independent external timestamp anchoring**. That dual-custodied seal verifies chronological honesty without confusing an unlucky result under an intact key for post-event fraud. In **Hannah Arendt's (1958)** political philosophy, pre-registration formalizes the **human promise** that carves "islands of security" out of future uncertainty, while **Simone Weil (1952 [1949])** grounds unconditional obligation, truth, and restorative accountability (*le châtiment*) against impunity independently of contractual pacts.

Stochology draws a bright line between **natural crosswinds** and **poisoned air before the shot**. Non-punitive protection under moral luck (Nagel 1979), making space for Williams's (1981) *agent-regret* and repair without blame, applies **only** when genuine outside shocks ($L\_{\mathrm{exog}}$) derail an adroit decision. When leadership pre-corrupted the informational medium via top-down premise rigging (Simon 1997 [1947]) and downward scapegoating (Girard 1986 [1982]), the outcome is not misfortune. It is deliberate fraud against the institutional pact.

That line also exposes the **pre-registration limit** of any cryptographic vault: an externally anchored SHA-256 / HMAC-SHA256 seal proves integrity only **from write time ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$) onward**, so it cannot catch pre-seal premise rigging, selective pruning, conflicts of interest, or risk dumped onto community reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) before $\mathcal{I}\_{t\_{\mathrm{disp}}}$ was frozen. Cryptographic seals shield against retroactive rewriting rather than guaranteeing fairness alone: trustworthiness rests upstream on open framing, monitored via the Premise Rigging Index ($\mathrm{PRI}$, [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), and an undiscounted frontline alarm channel (§8.2.2) able to challenge omissions before locking.

## 8.2 List–Pettit Discursive Dilemma and Fricker's Testimonial Injustice on Beer's Algedonic Channel

Institutions do not reason with a single mind. They combine specialist judgments across committees and chains of command, and naive voting rules can endorse disasters that every individual voter rejected.

### 8.2.1 The List–Pettit Discursive Dilemma Matrix

Christian List and Philip Pettit (2002, 2011) extended the legal *doctrinal paradox* into the **Discursive Dilemma**. Suppose launching an autonomous policy requires clearing all three axes of the Nuclear Triad ($C \iff P\_1 \land P\_2 \land P\_3$), where $P\_1$ stands for Epistemic Margin, $P\_2$ for Vital Margin, and $P\_3$ for Institutional Governance. Three specialists cast votes:

| Voter / Specialist | Premise $P\_1$ (Epistemic) | Premise $P\_2$ (Vital) | Premise $P\_3$ (Governance) | Conclusion $C = P\_1 \land P\_2 \land P\_3$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Specialist 1 (Epistemologist)** | **True** | **True** | False | **False** (Veto: Gov broken) |
| **Specialist 2 (Physicist/Engineer)** | **True** | False | **True** | **False** (Veto: Vital broken) |
| **Specialist 3 (Governance/Ethicist)**| False | **True** | **True** | **False** (Veto: Epi broken) |
| **Premise-Majority Vote** | **True ($2/3$)** | **True ($2/3$)** | **True ($2/3$)** | **True ($2/3 \land 2/3 \land 2/3$)** |
| **Conclusion-Majority Vote** | — | — | — | **False ($0/3$ Approvals)** |

Every specialist finds a fatal flaw ($0/3$ overall approval). Yet majority voting premise by premise approves each axis ($2/3, 2/3, 2/3$) and launches the unsafe policy.

Gate 2 prevents that trap by pairing a **constitutional conjunctive veto** with Stafford Beer's (1972) **sporadic operational audit** (Beer's System $3^\ast$, written $S3^\ast$ and kept distinct from Triad axis scores $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$), placed **under independent external oversight outside executive line command** so shared leadership assumptions cannot blind the audit. That conjunctive veto halts deployment whenever any single axis fails. A $2/3$ committee majority may **never** dismiss a lone $1/3$ specialist veto as "uncorroborated"; resolving a contested veto requires a falsifiable specification of the claimed hazard or pre-seal gap adjudicated by an **independent external forum**, while mandatory binary escalation ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$) when inaction itself kills remains bound deterministically beneath $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ with pre-assigned tie-breaking failing safe inside `Regime III-B` ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)).

### 8.2.2 Miranda Fricker's Testimonial Injustice on Stafford Beer's Algedonic Channel

In Stafford Beer's (1972) Viable System Model, an ascending **algedonic signal** jumps past middle management to trip an emergency stop before survival headroom expires.

Miranda Fricker (2007) demonstrated how hierarchies commit **testimonial epistemic injustice** by discounting warnings from frontline staff, nurses, or marginalized residents, an injustice deepened by institutional **inferential inertia** (Fricker 2020). Suppose management applies a status discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$ (so $1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}} > 0$) to frontline alerts carrying a constant per-shift evidence factor $e\_s \equiv e\_t > 1$ (log-evidence $\ln e\_t > 0$, distinct from Euler's number $e$; [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)) against trip threshold $E\_{\mathrm{trip}} = 1/\alpha > 1$ ($\alpha \in (0, 1)$). Discounting reduces effective signal to $e\_{\mathrm{discounted}} = e\_t^{1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}}}$, stretching **detection latency** inside recovery time $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)) from undiscounted $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(0)$ to discounted $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}})$:

$$\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(0) = \left\lceil \frac{\ln E\_{\mathrm{trip}}}{\ln e\_t} \right\rceil, \qquad \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}) = \left\lceil \frac{\ln E\_{\mathrm{trip}}}{(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})\ln e\_t} \right\rceil$$

In plain words, discounting a speaker's credibility by $50\%$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0.5$) doubles the shifts required to reach the alarm threshold, because $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$ shrinks the positive denominator $(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})\ln e\_t > 0$. Detection latency scales like $1/(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})$ against remaining **detection headroom** $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}} := \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} - (\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}})$ (reducing to $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} - \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ under zero-deliberation sentinel bypass $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = 0$, matching $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ only in the idealized $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} = 0$ limit, and kept distinct from deliberation headroom $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ in [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)). Once delay exceeds $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$, the warning arrives only as a post-mortem artifact (§8.6.1).

Holding $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ without inviting false alarms preserves [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)'s two-track architecture. **Pre-authorized physical hazard sentinels** trip holds immediately on a validated sensor reading with zero committee deliberation ($\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = 0$) while still obeying strictly positive physical actuation latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > 0$ ([Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md); [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)); if sensor validation fails or stalls, alerts route to `Regime III-A` quarantine rather than silent drops. **Discretionary alerts** enter at zero discount ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) inside pre-registered **per-stream mixture sequential *e*-processes** ($E\_t^{(j)} = \sum\_{k=1}^t w\_k \prod\_{s=k}^t e\_s^{(j)}$ with pre-committed weights $w\_k > 0$, $\sum\_{k \ge 1} w\_k \le 1$, dominated by valid unit-expectation test supermartingales $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[e\_s^{(j)} \mid \mathcal{F}\_{s-1}] \le 1$, so Ville's inequality $\mathbb{P}\_{H\_0}(\exists t : E\_t^{(j)} \ge 1/\alpha) \le \alpha$ holds exactly at every stopping time without resetting paths or imposing a supermartingale-breaking wealth floor while each new shift enters with undiluted weight $w\_k$, or equivalently $\alpha$-spent discrete epoch resets $\sum\_{k \ge 1} \alpha\_k \le \alpha$ at $E\_{\mathrm{trip},k} = 1/\alpha\_k$; Ramdas et al. 2023; Ville 1939) with $e\_s^{(j)}$ grading pre-registered in independent external/union forums outside line command, so long calm runs cannot create negative wealth holes that delay genuine crises and supervisors cannot launder $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$ into depressed $e\_s^{(j)}$ weights ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.3). Across Guard Sites $T\_5$ and $T\_7$ ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)), Gate 2 backs that warning channel with a non-disciplinary **protected right of unsafe-work refusal**: grounded in validated hazard readings or corroborated imminent danger, refusal pauses dangerous work immediately without managerial veto or retaliation pending independent technical review.

Leaving $S3^\ast$ dependent on solo moral heroism fails reliably. In a comparative Dutch study ($N = 27$ whistleblowers versus matched and general controls), van der Velden, Pecoraro, Houwerzijl, and van der Meulen (2019) documented severe fallout. ## 8.3 Innes–Holmström–Milgrom Contracts and Chouldechova's Fairness Trilemma

How do contracts, incentives, and automated risk scores allocate risk and blame across real humans?

### 8.3.1 Multitask Distortion, Scapegoating, and the Bateson Double-Bind Trap

Four institutional mechanisms turn collective uncertainty into private capture or frontline blame:

1. **Limited Liability, Risk-Shifting, and Debt-Call Contracts:** Under limited liability, levered equity enjoys upside gains while capping downside losses, enabling asset-substitution risk-shifting that makes socially reckless gambles privately profitable (Jensen & Meckling 1976; Holmström 2016; alongside Innes 1990 on optimal debt contracts under hidden effort and monotonicity; [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)). Flattening that payoff convexity requires deferred pay, leverage caps, and **bankruptcy-remote, parent-consolidated clawback escrow** $\gamma\_{\mathrm{clawback}}$ locked across tail-realization horizons ([Chapter 80](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md)), paired with bans on corporate D&O liability hedging and statutory parent/dividend piercing so SPV shell isolation cannot strip recovery, recognizing that linear liability still fails when survival time runs out ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$). Note that $\gamma\_{\mathrm{clawback}}$ is this book's institutional device, not a theorem claimed in those papers.
2. **Holmström–Milgrom (1991) Multitask Care:** Separating absolute loss magnitudes across **structural capacity sizing, frontline operational error ($L\_{\mathrm{oper}}$), and exogenous shocks ($L\_{\mathrm{exog}}$)**, distinct from [Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)'s reversible solvency buffer $B\_{\mathrm{solvency}}$ and dimensionless variance shares $(v\_{\mathrm{struct}}, v\_{\mathrm{op}}, v\_{\mathrm{exog}})$ behind $b\_{\mathrm{fl}}/v\_{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) §2), Gate 3 reports all three via Bengt Holmström's (1979) **Informativeness Principle**.
3. **Simon–Girard Scapegoat Blame Transfer:** Suppose leadership rigs decision premises from above (Simon 1997 [1947]) and then activates René Girard's (1986 [1982]) scapegoat mechanism downward against operators. Measuring assigned frontline blame $b\_{\mathrm{frontline}} \ge 0$ and **externally adjudicated operational fault $L\_{\mathrm{oper}} \ge 0$ (strictly net of double-bind coercion and structural under-staffing $v\_{\mathrm{struct}}$)** in matching loss units ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3), excess scapegoating $\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}}$ is:
   $$\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}} = \max(0, b\_{\mathrm{frontline}} - L\_{\mathrm{oper}})$$
   In plain words, $\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}}$ measures how much blame landed on frontline staff beyond any harm their own uncoerced error caused. Gate 3 reports excess blame alongside the ratio $b\_{\mathrm{frontline}} / L\_{\mathrm{oper}}$ when $L\_{\mathrm{oper}} > 0$ (whereas $L\_{\mathrm{oper}} = 0$ with $b\_{\mathrm{frontline}} > 0$ marks pure scapegoating with an infinite ratio).
4. **Gregory Bateson's (1972/2000) Double-Bind Trap:** Across high-powered proxy pressure $\beta\_{\mathrm{proxy}, t} \in [0, 1]$, testimonial warning dismissal $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}, t} \in [0, 1]$ (permitting full silencing $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}, t} = 1$, distinct from §8.2.2's finite-latency domain $[0, 1)$), and active emergency-brake status $\mathbf{1}\lbrace\text{brake}\_t\rbrace \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$, the double-bind index $\mathrm{DB}\_t \in [0, 1]$ multiplies those three pressures:
   $$\mathrm{DB}\_t = \beta\_{\mathrm{proxy}, t} \cdot \delta\_{\mathrm{status}, t} \cdot \bigl(1 - \mathbf{1}\lbrace\text{brake}\_t\rbrace\bigr) \in [0, 1]$$
   In plain words, workers are punished if they miss proxy targets, silenced if they warn of danger, and barred from halting unsafe runs. Because a product vanishes whenever **any single** factor equals zero (letting paper-brake theater or two-of-three coercion pass $\mathrm{DB}\_t = 0$), Gate 2 never treats $\mathrm{DB}\_t = 0$ as a standalone clearance criterion, pairing it instead with a **non-compensatory conjunctive check** enforcing low-powered incentives ($\beta\_{\mathrm{proxy}, t} \le \beta\_{\max}$), zero testimonial discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}, t} = 0$), and verified non-retaliatory brake operability ($\mathbf{1}\lbrace\text{brake}\_t\rbrace = 1$ backed by §8.2.2 notice custody).

### 8.3.2 Proof of Alexandra Chouldechova's (2017) Algebraic Fairness Impossibility Theorem

A risk classifier deployed across communities with unequal base rates ($p\_A \ne p\_B$) is routinely asked to satisfy every standard fairness score at once. Alexandra Chouldechova (2017) proved that promise algebraically impossible ([Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md)); walking through the three-line proof keeps error allocation explicit.

**Theorem (Chouldechova, 2017, §2.3):** Let $Y \in \lbrace0, 1\rbrace$ be the true outcome, $\hat{Y} \in \lbrace0, 1\rbrace$ the binary prediction, and $p = \mathbb{P}(Y = 1)$ the group base rate. Define positive predictive value $\mathrm{PPV} = \mathbb{P}(Y = 1 \mid \hat{Y} = 1)$, false-negative rate $\mathrm{FNR} = \mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 0 \mid Y = 1)$, and false-positive rate $\mathrm{FPR} = \mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1 \mid Y = 0)$. For any classifier with $0 < p < 1$, $\mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1) > 0$, and $\mathrm{PPV} > 0$:

$$\mathrm{FPR} = \frac{p}{1 - p} \cdot \frac{1 - \mathrm{PPV}}{\mathrm{PPV}} \cdot \bigl(1 - \mathrm{FNR}\bigr)$$

*Proof:* By Bayes' rule and the definition of conditional probability:
$$\mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1, Y = 1) = \mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1) \cdot \mathrm{PPV} = p \cdot (1 - \mathrm{FNR})$$
$$\implies \mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1) = \frac{p(1 - \mathrm{FNR})}{\mathrm{PPV}}$$
By the law of total probability:
$$\mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1) = \mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1, Y = 1) + \mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1, Y = 0) = p(1 - \mathrm{FNR}) + (1 - p)\mkern3mu\mathrm{FPR}$$
Equating both expressions for $\mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1)$:
$$\frac{p(1 - \mathrm{FNR})}{\mathrm{PPV}} = p(1 - \mathrm{FNR}) + (1 - p)\mkern3mu\mathrm{FPR}$$
Subtracting $p(1 - \mathrm{FNR})$ from both sides:
$$(1 - p)\mkern3mu\mathrm{FPR} = p(1 - \mathrm{FNR})\left(\frac{1}{\mathrm{PPV}} - 1\right) = p(1 - \mathrm{FNR})\left(\frac{1 - \mathrm{PPV}}{\mathrm{PPV}}\right)$$
Dividing both sides by $(1 - p)$ completes the proof:
$$\mathrm{FPR} = \frac{p}{1 - p} \cdot \frac{1 - \mathrm{PPV}}{\mathrm{PPV}} \cdot (1 - \mathrm{FNR}) = \mathrm{odds}(p) \cdot \frac{1 - \mathrm{PPV}}{\mathrm{PPV}} \cdot (1 - \mathrm{FNR}) \qquad \square$$

**Corollary (Fairness Impossibility):** Take an imperfect classifier ($0 < \mathrm{PPV}\_g < 1$, $\mathrm{FNR}\_g < 1$) across two populations with different base rates $p\_A \ne p\_B$ in $(0, 1)$. If it matches predictive parity ($\mathrm{PPV}\_A = \mathrm{PPV}\_B$) and equal false-negative rates ($\mathrm{FNR}\_A = \mathrm{FNR}\_B$), then the false-positive rate ratio must match the base-rate odds ratio:

$$\frac{\mathrm{FPR}\_A}{\mathrm{FPR}\_B} = \frac{p\_A / (1 - p\_A)}{p\_B / (1 - p\_B)} = \frac{\mathrm{odds}(p\_A)}{\mathrm{odds}(p\_B)} \ne 1$$

In plain words, whenever two groups experience unequal underlying risk and a classifier is not perfect, forcing equal calibration ($\mathrm{PPV}$) and equal false negatives mathematically forces unequal false positives. No clever model architecture can escape that identity. An institution must justify openly **who bears false positives versus false negatives** under Thomas Scanlon's (1998) non-aggregative contractualist standard (§8.6.2).

## 8.4 Zheng–Meister's Throughput Bottleneck, Cognitive Queues, and Gigerenzer's Natural Frequencies

Institutional accountability breaks down whenever automated dashboards flood human monitors faster than people can think.

### 8.4.1 The Cognitive Queue Under Channel Limits

Jieyu Zheng and Markus Meister (2025) put human behavioral and sensory-motor conscious throughput at roughly **ten bits per second**. External validations of hospital sepsis alerts demonstrate how excessive false alarms trigger severe clinical alert fatigue under low prevalence ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md); Wong et al. 2021).

Gate 2 models the human reviewer as a finite-rate server whose conscious decision bandwidth $C\_{\mathrm{human}} > 0$ (distinct from raw scalar display counts) is bounded near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025). Reviewing an audit packet of $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ bits takes service time $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}} / C\_{\mathrm{human}}$. Under alert arrival rate $\lambda\_{\mathrm{cog}}$, cognitive utilization is $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} = \lambda\_{\mathrm{cog}}\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} \in [0, 1)$ (distinct from physical queue utilization $\rho$).

When overlapping regulators pile on unpruned disclosures (Black 2008), audit bloat creates **compliance denial-of-service**, eroding legitimacy and delaying real alarms ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) §4; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)). Under finite random arrival or service variability ($0 \le c\_a^2, c\_s^2 < \infty$, $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$, excluding strictly deterministic timing), Kingman's (1961) queue multiplier $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}})$ splits oversight into three regimes:

1. **A synchronous summary panel** sending pre-registered sufficient statistics **paired with distribution-free tail and bound-breach indicators** keeps $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}$ tiny and $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}$ far below saturation, holding recovery inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ without hiding non-parametric edge anomalies behind summary compression.
2. **A standard epistemic card** lengthens service time so waiting queues start eating into remaining survival headroom.
3. **A full uncompressed audit** drives $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \to 1^-$ so queue delay explodes. Even on a single isolated shift ($\lambda\_{\mathrm{cog}} \to 0^+$), since $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}$ constitutes the service core of deliberation time $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} + \mathbb{E}[W\_q] \to \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}$, survival fails whenever $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}) + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$, triggering Collapse Proposition 2 via an overloaded review queue ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md); [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)).

This discipline is not arbitrary censorship, which would invite pre-seal pruning under $\mathrm{PRI}$ ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), but budgeting live bit load against $C\_{\mathrm{human}}$ while leaving pre-registered sufficiency auditable upstream and keeping the algedonic bypass clear.

Crucially, attention saturation ($\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \to 1^-$) compounds across shifts in the sequential loop ([Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)). Sustained alert streams above roughly $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025) build up operator allostatic wear $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t}$, eroding usable capacity $C\_{\mathrm{human}}$ and turning oversight into rubber-stamping unless mandatory rotation with **cryptographic shift handoffs**, quiet-horizon alert throttling (**strictly exempting pre-authorized physical sentinels and verified *e*-process trips**), a **hard Gate 2 ceiling on $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t}$**, and rest floors stand above turn-level discretion.

### 8.4.2 Gerd Gigerenzer's Natural Frequency Trees Against Conditional Fallacies

Gate 2 translates alerts into Gerd Gigerenzer & Ulrich Hoffrage's (1995) integer natural frequency trees, because conditional probabilities invite base-rate neglect while natural counts make error trade-offs visible at a glance:

- In a shift cohort with low crisis prevalence $\pi \in (0, 1)$, the tree first splits whole counts into true crises versus calm shifts before branching by detector alarm.
- A sensitive detector with even a small false-positive rate fires on most crises *and* many calm shifts; because calm shifts vastly outnumber crises, positive predictive value collapses and false alarms dominate. That exact arithmetic explains Wong et al.'s (2021) measured false-alarm burden (§8.4.1): alert fatigue stems from **both** low prevalence $\pi$ and weak likelihood ratio $\mathrm{TPR}/\mathrm{FPR}$, not rarity alone.
- For $\mathrm{TPR}, \mathrm{FPR} \in (0, 1)$, the Turing–Good weight of evidence in decibans, $W = 10\log\_{10}(\mathrm{TPR}/\mathrm{FPR})$ (Good 1950, 1979), makes that ratio transparent: low $W$ warns that even a high-sensitivity detector emits noisy alarms.
- Since $\mathrm{PPV} = \pi\mathrm{TPR}/(\pi\mathrm{TPR} + (1-\pi)\mathrm{FPR})$, cutting $\mathrm{FPR}$ by factor $k > 1$ boosts $\mathrm{PPV}$ nearly $k$-fold only inside the false-alarm-dominated zone $(1-\pi)(\mathrm{FPR}/k) \gg \pi\mathrm{TPR}$ before flattening near $1$, at the price of missed crises that frequency trees keep visible.

## 8.6 Discounted Channels, Chosen Criteria, and Records That Do Not Discriminate

### 8.6.1 Two limits of the discounted channel

Section 8.2.2 priced status discounting as extra shift latency inside $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) on time and vital margin). Let $e\_s \equiv e\_t > 1$ (constant per-shift factor, distinct from Euler's $e$) be the evidence multiplier, $E\_{\mathrm{trip}} = 1/\alpha > 1$ the alarm cutoff, and $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$ the credibility discount. Write $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}} := \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} - (\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}) > 0$ for the positive **detection headroom**, which reduces to $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} - \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ under pre-authorized zero-deliberation sentinel bypass ($\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = 0$ with physical actuation lag $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > 0$), matching $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ only in the idealized $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} = 0$ limit (and kept distinct from [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)'s deliberation headroom $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$).

Write $q\_0 = \ln E\_{\mathrm{trip}}/\ln e\_t > 0$ for the undiscounted shift quotient, $q(\delta) = q\_0/(1-\delta)$ for $\delta \in [0, 1)$, and $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta) = \lceil q(\delta) \rceil$ for discrete detection time. Solving continuous $q(\delta) = T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$ gives $\delta^\star\_{\mathrm{cont}} = 1 - q\_0/T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$. Guaranteeing a discrete-shift trip $\lceil q(\delta)\rceil \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$ requires at least one full shift ($T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}} \ge 1$, so $\lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}\rfloor \ge 1$) and $q(\delta) \le \lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}\rfloor$. Note that $0 < T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}} < 1$ leaves $\lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}\rfloor = 0$ and prevents even one shift at $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$. Together these bounds fix the critical status discount $\delta^\star$:
$$\delta^\star = 1 - \frac{q\_0}{\lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}\rfloor} \qquad (T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}} \ge 1),$$

In plain words, $\delta^\star$ is the steepest credibility discount an alarm channel can tolerate before missing the rescue window. Whenever $q\_0 < \lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}\rfloor$, $\delta^\star$ sits in $(0, 1)$ (matching $1 - q\_0/T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$ on integer $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$); $\delta^\star = 0$ leaves zero discounting headroom ($q\_0 = \lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}\rfloor$, still passing at $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$), whereas $\delta^\star < 0$ marks a reserve already too thin even with zero discounting. Below $\delta^\star$, alarms arrive in time; past $\delta^\star$, warnings arrive only after ruin. At the singular edge $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$, $(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})\ln e\_t \to 0$ and detection time blows up past any finite reserve: discounting frontline voices is a physical survival bottleneck, not merely poor etiquette. Keeping $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ with mixture or $\alpha$-spent corroborated sequential screening keeps monitoring inside §8.4.1's human attention budget.

### 8.6.2 The criterion the algebra cannot choose

The forced trade-offs of §8.3.2, requiring institutions to justify who bears false positives versus false negatives across unequal base rates, cannot be settled by algebra alone, failing visibly in two ways.

First is the **sign of the prediction label**. Keep the exact same confusion numbers fixed with $p\_A > p\_B$, equal $\mathrm{PPV}$, and equal $\mathrm{FNR}$, so Chouldechova's theorem forces $\mathrm{FPR}\_A > \mathrm{FPR}\_B$. Suppose $\hat{Y} = 1$ triggers a **burden** (pretrial detention, account freeze, license revocation): false positives inflict unwarranted harm, so excess $\mathrm{FPR}\_A$ acts as a surcharge on higher-risk group $A$. Now suppose $\hat{Y} = 1$ awards a **benefit** (an ICU bed, tutoring slot, relief grant) at identical numbers. Excess false positives in $A$ become windfall grants to $A$, while group $B$ receives fewer windfall spots. Enforcing equal $\mathrm{FPR}$ alongside equal $\mathrm{PPV}$ flips the harm instead: false-negative denials concentrate in higher-risk group $A$ (§8.3.2). One equation, identical numbers, opposite human meaning: algebra cannot tell whether $\hat{Y} = 1$ harms or helps.

Second is the **choice of partition**. The identity applies to any grouping attribute, so splitting records by zip code, citizenship, or race produces different parity gaps from one unchanged rule, enabling **partition shopping** unless institutions commit upfront to **pre-registered intersectional partition disclosure**.

Because Corbett-Davies et al.'s (2017) uniform cutoff minimizes **aggregate expected social cost** across individuals whereas **Scanlon's (1998) contractualism** rejects shifting severe uncompensated false-positive burdens onto a higher-prevalence minority simply to yield smaller distributed gains elsewhere, Stochology resolves the tie lexicographically ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)): first **forbid calibration on uncorrected policing-skewed or selectively detained arrest labels** (applying [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)/[45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) selection intervals), enforce **Scanlonian non-aggregative side-constraints** (minimum evidential weight $V > 0$, non-export ceilings across external reception perimeters $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$, and anti-aggregation caps barring severe uncompensated burdens whenever non-carceral or upstream causal fixes exist, overriding cost minimization whenever individual complaints cannot be reasonably rejected), and **only inside that cleared zone** apply Corbett-Davies et al.'s (2017) social-cost inversion over **full affected-party error costs** ($L\_{\mathrm{inst}} \ll L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$, [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md); [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)) while pursuing upstream causal repair ([Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md)) and reporting parity disparities candidly across all pre-registered intersectional strata.

### 8.6.3 When the sealed record does not discriminate

Gate 3 rests on one evidential requirement: pre-sealing the ex-ante record distinguishes *adroit and unlucky* from *inadroit and lucky* only if different conduct produces different sealed traces. Whenever two strategies emit identical records, no vault can separate them regardless of good intentions.

Formally, write $H$ for adroit execution and $E$ for everything inside the sealed log. Distinguishing skill from negligence requires $\mathbb{P}(E \mid H) \ne \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)$. When those likelihoods match, Bayes's odds formula gives likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR} = 1$ and zero evidential update, leaving posterior odds pinned at the prior. Pre-registration is therefore necessary, never sufficient.

The negative-pressure test on the Macondo well in April 2010 illustrates the distinct failure of an underspecified protocol. The observed drill-pipe pressure of 1,400 psi versus 0 psi on the kill line was physically decisive. A passing test required 0 psi, and 1,400 psi on the drill pipe signaled a leak rather than any real "bladder effect" (National Commission 2011). Yet neither regulators nor operators had pre-registered crisp written failure criteria stating which gauge discrepancies invalidated well integrity, letting the crew rationalize the reading after the fact ([Chapter 84](84-layer-infrastructure-nuclear-hydraulic-safety-order.md)). Sealing an underspecified protocol creates the exact same accountability blind spot as $\mathrm{LR} = 1$: competing stories can fit the frozen numbers afterward.

Gate 3 therefore makes **ex-ante distinguishability ($\exists E : \mathbb{P}(E \mid H) \ne \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)$ with crisp observable failure cutoffs)** a mandatory **pre-launch block (`Unparsable` / `Regime III-A` quarantine)** rather than merely an ex-post classification rule, audited upstream against premise rigging ($\mathrm{PRI}$, [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)) and cross-perimeter spillover ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$, §§8.2.2, 8.3.1, 8.5). Wherever an unblocked or violated sign-off still slipped an indeterminate or ambiguous protocol past Gate 1/Gate 3 pre-launch checks, Gate 3 refuses frontline scapegoating ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}}$), marks individual execution undetermined, and pierces directly to **named executive sign-off clawbacks ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{clawback}}$)** alongside institutional redesign of the option menu, protocol, and clock, extended to high-hazard engineering in [Chapter 84](84-layer-infrastructure-nuclear-hydraulic-safety-order.md).
