## 9.2 Family 5 in Detail: Galenic *Sēmeîa*, Process Tracing, and Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$

Historical archival work, investigative journalism, legal fact-finding and bedside clinical diagnosis do not estimate a regression slope over a large sample. The investigator traces a causal mechanism in a **single unfolding case** by weighing qualitative clues (*sēmeîa* in Galen and Hippocrates, with *Prognostic* I setting the protocol for reading signs; *Process Tracing* in Stephen Van Evera, 1997, and Andrew Bennett & Jeffrey Checkel, 2015).

### 9.2.1 Epistemic Foundations: The Clinical and Archival Clue

Every qualitative clue $E$ is evaluated by two conditional probabilities:

1. **Sensitivity under Focal Hypothesis $H$:** $p\_H = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H) \in (0, 1)$
2. **False-Positive Rate under Rival Explanation $\neg H$:** $p\_{\neg H} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H) \in (0, 1)$

The pair is the whole object. A clue is not strong or weak in itself; it is strong or weak for a hypothesis. The second conditional is the one an inquiry is most likely to leave unexamined, because the rival hypothesis is the one nobody in the room is arguing for.

### 9.2.2 Bennett & Checkel Diagnostic Test Taxonomy

The two-dimensional space $(p\_H, p\_{\neg H})$ partitions into Van Evera's four canonical tests:

- **Straw in the wind:** modest necessity and modest sufficiency — a moderate likelihood ratio; the clue nudges the odds without settling anything.
- **Hoop test:** **high necessity.** If $H$ is true, the clue *must* be present.
  - Likelihood ratio if observed: $\mathrm{LR}^+ = \frac{p\_H}{p\_{\neg H}} > 1$ (modest confirmation).
  - Likelihood ratio if **absent**: $\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \ll 1$ (**severe elimination**).
  - *Epistemic principle:* Passing a Hoop test does not prove $H$, but **failing a Hoop test refutes $H$**!
- **Smoking gun:** **high sufficiency / pathognomonic sign.**
  - Likelihood ratio if observed: $\mathrm{LR}^+ = \frac{p\_H}{p\_{\neg H}} \gg 1$ (**massive confirmation**).
  - Likelihood ratio if absent: $\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \approx 1$ (does not refute $H$).
- **Doubly decisive:** **both necessary and sufficient.**
  - If observed: proves $H$ ($\mathrm{LR}^+ \gg 1$).
  - If absent: refutes $H$ ($\mathrm{LR}^- \ll 1$).

The taxonomy partitions an inquiry's ambitions, not its evidence. A case study built only from smoking guns can confirm and cannot eliminate; one built only from hoops can eliminate and can never confirm.

### 9.2.3 Turing–Good Deciban Calculus

Following Alan Turing and I. J. Good (1950, 1979) at Bletchley Park, evidence is accumulated on a logarithmic scale in **decibans** ($\mathrm{dB}$):
$$W(E; H : \neg H) = 10 \log\_{10} \frac{\mathbb{P}(E \mid H)}{\mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)} = 10 \log\_{10} \mathrm{LR}$$
Under conditional independence of clues:
$$W\_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum\_{j=1}^m W(E\_j; H : \neg H) = 10 \log\_{10} \prod\_{j=1}^m \mathrm{LR}\_j$$
Bayes' Rule in odds form:
$$\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}} = \mathrm{Odds}\_0 \times 10^{W\_{\mathrm{total}} / 10}, \qquad \mathbb{P}(H \mid E\_{1..m}) = \frac{\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}{1 + \mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}$$

### 9.2.4 John Maynard Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$

In *A Treatise on Probability* (1921), John Maynard Keynes distinguished probability balance $P$ from **evidential weight** $V$ (the absolute volume and depth of the evidentiary base):
$$V = \frac{K}{K + I}$$
where $K$ is relevant knowledge acquired and $I$ is recognized remaining ignorance. As new clues are audited with evidentiary depth $\Delta K\_j > 0$:
$$V\_{\mathrm{post}} = \frac{K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^m \Delta K\_j}{K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^m \Delta K\_j + I\_0}$$
A hypothesis can carry a high posterior probability on a flimsy weight, which is a dangerous guess, or the same posterior probability on a mature weight, which is a grounded finding. The two numbers are not rivals and neither substitutes for the other. Probability says what to believe; weight says how much the belief would survive being examined.

### 9.2.5 The Same Inference in Symbols

Let the prior probability be $p\_0$, the initial knowledge $K\_0$ and the initial ignorance $I\_0$. Initial odds and initial weight follow directly:
$$\mathrm{Odds}\_0 = \frac{p\_0}{1 - p\_0}, \qquad V\_0 = \frac{K\_0}{K\_0 + I\_0}$$

Now evaluate three diagnostic clues, one of each kind:

1. **A doubly decisive clue**, with high necessity and high sufficiency, observed. Its likelihood ratio is $\mathrm{LR}\_1 = p\_{H,1}/p\_{\neg H,1}$, worth $W\_1 = 10\log\_{10}\mathrm{LR}\_1$ decibans, and it adds $\Delta K\_1$ to the knowledge base.
2. **A smoking gun**, with modest necessity and high sufficiency, observed. $\mathrm{LR}\_2 = p\_{H,2}/p\_{\neg H,2}$, worth $W\_2$, adding $\Delta K\_2$.
3. **A hoop test**, with high necessity and modest sufficiency, observed. $\mathrm{LR}\_3 = p\_{H,3}/p\_{\neg H,3}$, worth $W\_3$, adding $\Delta K\_3$.

Total likelihood ratio, decibans, posterior odds and posterior probability:
$$\mathrm{LR}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \prod\_{j=1}^3 \mathrm{LR}\_j, \qquad W\_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum\_{j=1}^3 W\_j = 10\log\_{10}\mathrm{LR}\_{\mathrm{total}}$$
$$\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}} = \mathrm{Odds}\_0 \times \mathrm{LR}\_{\mathrm{total}} \implies p\_{\mathrm{post}} = \frac{\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}{1 + \mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}$$
$$K\_{\mathrm{post}} = K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^3 \Delta K\_j \implies V\_{\mathrm{post}} = \frac{K\_{\mathrm{post}}}{K\_{\mathrm{post}} + I\_0}$$
The three-step computation is the whole of the inference, and every step is a conditional probability the investigator must be able to defend. The ordering of the clues changes nothing: the product is commutative, so the posterior is the same whichever clue is evaluated first. A conclusion "reached gradually" is a statement about the investigator and not about the evidence.

### 9.2.6 Refutability and the Asymmetry of the Hoop Test

Qualitative inference is valid only if:

- probabilities respect Cromwell's interior bounds;
- each clue updates both Bayesian posterior odds and Keynes's evidential weight;
- **Popper–Mayo refutability:** the inquiry includes at least one hoop test or doubly decisive clue.

Suppose the hoop clue was absent. Its active likelihood ratio becomes
$$\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \ll 1,$$
so the total collapses by that factor, and the inference is marked refuted and no longer refutable-by-design: the hoop condition has been tested and failed. The remaining positive clues do not repair it. Popper's asymmetry, stated in arithmetic. A failed necessary condition eliminates the hypothesis while the surviving confirmations keep their weight, and no accumulation of straws in the wind outweighs the absence of something that had to be there.

## 9.3 Reflexive Self-Application: The Four Internal Goodhart Brakes

An institution that turns an evaluative metric into an administrative target destroys the metric (Goodhart, 1975; Strathern, 1997; Espeland & Stevens, 1998). Self-application is not complete until the analyst's own position is in the object: the field that supplies the problem, the career the verdict serves and the inherited instruments all enter the account (Bourdieu 2004). Stochology holds its own three safeguards against degeneration with four internal brakes, mathematical and institutional:

### 9.3.1 Brake 1 (Epistemic Margin): Skeptical Inflation vs. CORP Resolution

- *Perversion:* Analysts could achieve full coverage by inflating interval bounds to the trivial domain $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$, or predicting the constant sample mean everywhere. Coverage is monotone in width, so a coverage target rewards the widest interval.
- *Mathematical Brake:* Under the Murphy (1973) and Dimitriadis, Gneiting, and Jordan (2021) CORP decomposition:
  $$\mathrm{Brier}(p, y) = \bar{y}(1 - \bar{y}) + \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}$$
  A constant base-rate forecast achieves zero miscalibration and has **zero resolution**: it cannot separate a crisis shift from a calm one. The audit therefore requires the sharp forecast's resolution to exceed the lazy forecast's by a margin, and no widening of an interval buys that.

### 9.3.2 Brake 2 (Vital Margin): Regressive Hoarding vs. Scarf–Samuelson Welfare

- *Perversion:* Administrators could hoard excessive reserves ($R \to \infty$) under the pretext of risk aversion, starving urgent present human needs. A reserve target with no upper term rewards hoarding the way a coverage target rewards width.
- *Mathematical Brake:* Net social welfare balances concave precautionary benefit against linear marginal opportunity cost:
  $$W(R) = \kappa \ln(1 + R) - c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} R$$
  Differentiating with respect to $R$:
  $$\frac{dW}{dR} = \frac{\kappa}{1 + R} - c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} = 0 \implies R^\ast = \max\left(0,\mkern3mu \frac{\kappa}{c\_{\mathrm{urgent}}} - 1\right)$$
  The optimum is finite whenever the marginal cost of withholding is positive. Beyond it welfare falls linearly while the precautionary benefit grows only logarithmically. A hoarded buffer is therefore penalized as value-destructive, not rewarded as caution, and the penalty is a property of the functional form, not of a policy preference.

### 9.3.3 Brake 3 (Institutional Governance): Procedural Theater vs. Kairós Emergency Fast-Path

- *Perversion:* Compliance committees demand exhaustive reviews ($\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$) during an acute physical emergency whose time-to-survival is shorter than the review.
- *Mathematical Brake:* Whenever $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$, procedural delay guarantees catastrophic ruin. Sosa's (2015) meta-aptness activates the pre-committed emergency fast-path:
  $$\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$$
  The fast-path releases vital buffers immediately, records the wide partial-identification interval $[L\_t, U\_t]$ in the sealed record, and defers collegiate forensic review until physical survival is secured. Legitimacy does not buy latency. Procedural consensus and democratic mandate cannot substitute for buffer time.

### 9.3.4 Brake 4 (Composite Metric): Anti-Checklist Rule & Homothetic Degree-1 Nested Leontief Aggregation

- *Perversion:* The composite index $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}})$ is turned into an executive bonus metric or certification badge, inviting box-ticking, cosmetic denominator inflation, and gaming (Espeland & Stevens, 1998; Hacking, 2002).
- *Institutional & Mathematical Brake:*
  1. *Constitutional Rule:* $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ is prohibited from serving as a scalar reward target. It operates strictly as an ex post diagnostic: the fold is read off a trajectory that has already run, reported, and consumed by no decision of the loop.
  2. *Degree-1 Homotheticity:* Degree-1 nested Leontief aggregation $\min\_i g\_i$ avoids multiplicative volume collapse, which §9.4 proves.

## 9.4 Leontief Non-Substitutable Aggregation vs. Multiplicative and Arithmetic Collapse

### 9.4.1 Proof of Chart-Conditional 3D Lebesgue Feasible Volume Contraction

Fix a single strictly increasing chart mapping the three native-unit axis margins onto $(e, v, g) = \bigl(\varphi\_{\mathrm{epi}}(s\_{\mathrm{epi}}),\mkern3mu \varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}(s\_{\mathrm{vital}}),\mkern3mu \varphi\_{\mathrm{gov}}(s\_{\mathrm{gov}})\bigr) \in [0, 1]^3$, evaluated against safety threshold $\tau \in (0, 1)$ in that declared chart.

**1. Pure Nested Leontief ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(e, v, g) \ge \tau$):**
The feasible region is the cube $[\tau, 1]^3$. Its Lebesgue volume is:
$$V\_{\min}(\tau) = \int\_\tau^1 de \int\_\tau^1 dv \int\_\tau^1 dg = (1 - \tau)^3$$

**2. Continuous Multiplicative Product ($e \cdot v \cdot g \ge \tau$):**
The feasible region is $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}} = \{(e, v, g) \in [0, 1]^3 : e \ge \tau, \mkern5mu v \ge \tau / e, \mkern5mu g \ge \tau / (e v)\}$.
We integrate directly:
$$V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = \int\_\tau^1 de \int\_{\tau/e}^1 dv \int\_{\tau/(ev)}^1 dg = \int\_\tau^1 de \int\_{\tau/e}^1 \left(1 - \frac{\tau}{ev}\right) dv$$
Inner integral with respect to $v$:
$$\int\_{\tau/e}^1 \left(1 - \frac{\tau}{ev}\right) dv = \left[ v - \frac{\tau}{e}\ln v \right]\_{\tau/e}^1 = 1 - \left(\frac{\tau}{e} - \frac{\tau}{e}\ln\frac{\tau}{e}\right) = 1 - \frac{\tau}{e} + \frac{\tau}{e}(\ln\tau - \ln e)$$
Outer integral with respect to $e$:
$$V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = \int\_\tau^1 \left(1 - \frac{\tau}{e} + \frac{\tau\ln\tau}{e} - \frac{\tau\ln e}{e}\right) de$$
Computing each of the four terms from $\tau$ to $1$:

1. $\int\_\tau^1 1\mkern3mude = 1 - \tau$
2. $-\int\_\tau^1 \frac{\tau}{e}de = -\tau [\ln 1 - \ln\tau] = \tau\ln\tau$
3. $\int\_\tau^1 \frac{\tau\ln\tau}{e}de = \tau\ln\tau [-\ln\tau] = -\tau(\ln\tau)^2$
4. $-\int\_\tau^1 \frac{\tau\ln e}{e}de = -\tau \left[ \frac{(\ln e)^2}{2} \right]\_\tau^1 = \frac{\tau}{2}(\ln\tau)^2$

Summing the terms:
$$V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = 1 - \tau + \tau\ln\tau - \frac{\tau}{2}(\ln\tau)^2$$

**Volume Contraction Ratio and Chart Dependence:**
Both volumes are functions of $\tau$ alone only once the chart is fixed, and within that chart the ratio between them is not constant. As $\tau$ rises the multiplicative region shrinks much faster than the Leontief cube, so a gate written as a product destroys a large share of the operating space that a minimum preserves in that chart. The distinction between ordinal box invariance, curved level-set geometry, and chart-conditional volume is sharp. Under Stevens's ordinal scale discipline, any strictly increasing per-axis recoding $m\_j \leftrightarrow S\_\ast$ preserves the Leontief product-box structure—mapping $[\tau, 1]^3$ onto $\prod\_a [\varphi\_a(\tau), 1]$—whereas the curved boundary of $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ is not preserved as a product level set $\{S'\_e S'\_v S'\_g \ge \tau'\}$ under nonlinear recoding or even under distinct affine scales $\alpha\_a \ne 1$, and there is no chart-independent preservation of Lebesgue volume because both measures transform by integrating the chart's Jacobian determinant $\lvert \det J\_\varphi \rvert = \prod\_a \varphi\_a'(S\_a)$ displayed in Appendix A.4. Restricting the affine family $\varphi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ (anchored at $\varphi\_a(1) = 1$) to maps remaining inside $[0, 1]^3$ requires $0 < \alpha\_a \le 1$ (since $\varphi\_a(0) = 1 - \alpha\_a \ge 0$, with the identity $\alpha\_a = 1$ the unique affine map fixing both $0$ and $1$, and $\varphi\_a([\tau, 1]) = [1 - \alpha\_a(1 - \tau), 1] \subseteq [0, 1]$); on that restricted subgroup $\det J\_\varphi = \prod\_a \alpha\_a$ is constant across $[0, 1]^3$, so transporting both feasible sets by the chart itself ($\varphi([\tau, 1]^3)$ and $\varphi(\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}})$) rescales both Lebesgue volumes by $\prod\_a \alpha\_a$ and leaves their volume ratio invariant.

### 9.4.2 False Collapses on Healthy Grids vs. False-Safe Failures on Ruin Boundaries

Two grid constructions make the difference operational:

1. **A healthy factorial grid**, in which every coordinate is strictly above the threshold.
   - **Pure Leontief:** no false collapse on the grid, because a minimum above the threshold is above the threshold.
   - **Hybrid governance** (a minimum of some coordinates and a product of others): a large share of the grid is falsely collapsed, because the product term falls below the threshold even when every factor is healthy.
   - **Full product:** almost the entire grid is falsely collapsed; only the corner in which every coordinate sits near the top survives. Uncalibrated multiplicative damping condemns healthy states wholesale.
2. **A single-axis ruin grid**, in which one axis is in acute collapse while the other two are healthy.
   - **Compensatory arithmetic mean:** with one axis at zero and two healthy, the mean can still clear the threshold, so the arithmetic mean issues a **false-safe pass in every case**. That is the aggregation failure that matters: it is the one that lets an institution act.
   - **Pure Leontief:** no false-safe at all. Leontief records every catastrophic failure, which is the only sense in which it *halts*. The failure is diagnosed in each case, and no diagnosis of it is consulted while the shifts are still being decided. On any absorbing-barrier frontier where one safeguard fails, the Liebig–Leontief minimum strictly dominates compensatory arithmetic and CES aggregators; whether doubled physical endowments could partially compensate a weak coordinate in mild interior regimes without an active absorbing barrier is left open to pre-registered falsifier $F\_4$ (§9.5.3, §9.7.2) rather than conceded in advance.

## 9.5 Severe Testing and the Four Pre-Registered Falsifiers

### 9.5.1 Deborah Mayo's Severity Principle and Burden-of-Proof Inversion

A common methodological flaw sets the null hypothesis as "no difference" ($H\_0: \Delta = 0$) and calls a theory "corroborated" because a noisy test failed to reject $H\_0$. Stochology inverts the burden of proof (Mayo 2018):
$$H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} \ge \delta\_{\min} \quad \text{vs.} \quad H\_1^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} < \delta\_{\min}$$
To refute a Stochological safeguard, the challenger must actively show with high post-data severity that omitting the safeguard incurs less than the substantive harm threshold $\delta\_{\min}$.

### 9.5.2 Pre-Registered Design Formula

At one-sided significance level $\alpha \in (0, 0.5)$ (positive upper quantile $z\_{1-\alpha} = \Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha) > 0$) and target power $1 - \beta$ (quantile $z\_{1-\beta}$), the **pre-data planned** required effective sample size, planned standard error, and planned cutoffs—sealed before outcomes arrive—are:
$$n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}} = \left\lceil \left( \frac{(z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta})\sigma\_d}{\delta\_{\min}} \right)^2 \right\rceil, \qquad \mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}} = \frac{\sigma\_d}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}}},$$
$$d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}.$$
Once paired evaluation yields an observed mean harm reduction $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ at realized effective sample size $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, the **post-data realized** standard error and realized severity-equivalent cutoffs are:
$$\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \frac{\sigma\_d}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}.$$
Because $\Phi$ is strictly increasing with $\Phi(z\_{1-\alpha}) = 1 - \alpha$, Mayo's post-data severity for refuting $H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} \ge \delta\_{\min}$ and post-data severity for corroborating $\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} > \delta\_{\min}$ satisfy the exact equivalences:
$$\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{\delta\_{\min} - \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}{\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}\right) \ge 1 - \alpha \iff \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}},$$
$$\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - \delta\_{\min}}{\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}\right) \ge 1 - \alpha \iff \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \ge d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}.$$
When the realized effective sample hits the sealed target ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}} = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$), $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}$ and the realized cutoffs coincide with the planned ones.

### 9.5.3 The Four Canonical Falsifiers

Four falsifiers are pre-registered, one per component of the framework:

1. **$F\_1$ ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$, observational transport):** the claim that the epistemic margin's correction transports to a new population, with its own sealed threshold $\delta\_{\min}$ and paired standard deviation $\sigma\_d$. It fails if, on a population whose missingness and cluster structure are declared with the design, the corrected arm's coverage shortfall on the shifts the buffer was bought for is no smaller than the uncorrected arm's.
2. **$F\_2$ ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$, heavy tails and queues):** the claim that the vital margin's buffer prevents absorption under a heavy-tailed demand regime at the declared utilization $\rho$ and tail return period $T$. Because a window of length $n$ records zero shocks of return period $T$ with probability $(1 - 1/T)^n$ ([Chapter 03](03-the-biggest-number-in-the-room.md)), the design imposes a bilateral **shock-exposure gate**: the pre-registered observation horizon must satisfy $(1 - 1/T)^n \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ (or record at least one realized tail shock from the declared $T$-period regime). Any evaluation window that fails that gate with zero tail shocks is pre-classified as **non-informative** and may be recorded as neither a refutation nor a corroboration of $F\_2$, preventing a short, quiet window from generating a false refutation of $F\_2$ (or false corroboration of unbuffered operation). Once the exposure gate is met, $F\_2$ fails if a unit held at the declared utilization $\rho$ without the buffer traverses the shock period without reaching the absorbing barrier, showing the barrier the margin was bought against was not reachable from that utilization at that tail.
3. **$F\_3$ ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$, multitask proxy gaming):** the claim that governance prevents the proxy from cannibalizing the substantive goal. It fails if, with governance present and declared, the proxy rises while the substantive goal falls on the same unit over the same period.
4. **$F\_4$ ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$, non-compensability):** the claim that the minimum is not substitutable — the one component whose falsification the chapter has to take seriously. It fails if a compensated arm with doubled physical slack absorbs a mild shock, with no absorbing barrier active, at least as well as a strict-minimum arm whose weakest coordinate is the one the compensation is meant to replace.

Each falsifier's design is sealed before evaluation: the substantive threshold $\delta\_{\min}$, the paired standard deviation $\sigma\_d$, the significance level $\alpha$ and power $1 - \beta$, the required effective sample size $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$ and planned standard error $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}$ of §9.5.2, the planned pre-data cutoffs $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$ and $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, the severity bar $1 - \alpha$, and (for $F\_2$) the shock-exposure threshold $\alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ over $(T, n)$. Publishing those planned design quantities before the data arrive—and reporting the post-data realized cutoffs $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$, and §9.7.3 inversion band computed from $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ alongside the verdict—is what makes the verdict a test and not a description. The four are not interchangeable. A falsifier whose observable is not named before the run is not one of the four; it is a description of the chapter's own preference.

The pairing rule belongs to the design, not to the analysis. A paired difference is a difference only if the two arms differ in the safeguard and in nothing else. A pair is one unit observed with the safeguard and the same unit, or a unit matched on the declared blocking variables, observed without it, at a common reserve buffer and a common leverage. A pair whose arms differ in endowment is a comparison and does not enter $d\_{\mathrm{obs}}$. §9.6 runs the synthesis at that common endowment, and its two negative controls are what a pair looks like when the safeguard is the only thing removed. Which arm a unit occupies is recorded before the run, so the pairing cannot be chosen afterwards in favour of the arm that survived.

Pairing also decides which variance the design needs. A reader who keeps the cluster design effect as well as the paired standard deviation charges the same clustering twice. Write a unit's value as $\mu + u\_c + e$ with $u\_c$ the cluster shift and $e$ the unit term, so that the intra-cluster correlation is $\rho\_I = \sigma\_u^2/\sigma^2$ and the design effect of a clustered sample of mean size $\tilde m$ is $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (\tilde m - 1)\rho\_I$ ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.3; Kish 1965; Moulton 1986). For the two legs of a pair inside one cluster, $d\_c = (\mu + u\_c + e\_A) - (\mu + u\_c + e\_B) = e\_A - e\_B$, so $\mathrm{Var}(d\_c) = 2\sigma\_e^2 = 2\sigma^2(1 - \rho\_I)$: the cluster term cancels exactly, and the paired difference inherits the factor $(1 - \rho\_I)$ that the design effect would otherwise have supplied. The consequence is that $\rho\_I$ enters $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$ through $\sigma\_d$, as $\sigma\_d^2 = 2\sigma^2(1 - \rho\_I)$ within a cluster against $2\sigma^2$ between clusters, and does not enter through $\mathrm{DEFF}$ in the same design; applying both counts the cluster twice. A pair drawn between clusters is the other design: $\mathrm{Var}(d) = 2\sigma^2$ and $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1$ at the level of the pair, provided no cluster supplies more than one pair, and where one does, $\tilde m$ is read as the number of pairs it supplies, not the number of units. Kish weighting survives either way, because the weight acts unit by unit and the paired difference does not cancel it: $\mathrm{Var}(\bar d\_w) = \sigma\_d^2 \sum w\_i^2/(\sum w\_i)^2$; this is also why the severity edge of a clustered archive is priced in $n/\mathrm{DEFF}$ rather than in nominal rows ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md) §2.6, "The severity the archive can actually certify"). Selection survives for a different reason: both legs pass the same registration gate, so the defect term $((1-f)/f)\bar\rho^2$ of [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.2.2 is untouched by the pairing, which controls heterogeneity and not sampling. The rule the sealed design has to record is therefore narrow and it is the reason the pairing rule is sealed with the other three: a within-cluster pair spends its cluster correction inside $\sigma\_d$ and is not charged it again in the design effect, a between-cluster pair spends nothing there, and the weight and selection terms are charged in both designs.

### 9.5.4 How a Verdict Is Read

The reading rule is fixed before any falsifier runs. Let $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ be the observed paired mean difference at realized effective sample size $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, with realized standard error $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \sigma\_d/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}$ and realized cutoffs $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ and $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ from §9.5.2.

- **Exposure admissibility ($F\_2$ bilateral gate):** Before either branch of $F\_2$ is scored, the evaluation verifies the pre-registered shock-exposure gate $(1 - 1/T)^n \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ (or at least one realized $T$-period tail shock). If that gate fails without a realized tail shock, the run is logged as **non-informative** (*untested*) and **cannot be recorded as either a refutation or a corroboration** of $F\_2$.
- **Refutation:** If $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ (equivalently, $\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) \ge 1 - \alpha$) and any exposure gate is met, the falsifier fires: the component it targets is refuted for the pre-registered regime and design, and the chapter is obliged to narrow, restate, or withdraw the corresponding claim.
- **Corroboration:** If $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \ge d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ (equivalently, $\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) \ge 1 - \alpha$) and any exposure gate is met, the component is corroborated on that design and severity level, which is a statement about a test that could have failed, not about the world in general.
- **Inconclusive zone:** If $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \in (d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}, d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}})$, neither refutation nor corroboration reaches post-data severity $1 - \alpha$.

Either decisive verdict (refutation or corroboration) is published with its realized inversion band: the thresholds of $\delta\_{\min}$ at which the same observed data, realized $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$, and severity criterion $1 - \alpha$ would have returned the opposite reading. §9.7.3 derives those flip thresholds and says why they are published and not filed.

A falsifier exists because the reading must be pre-committed. A threshold chosen after the paired differences are known can produce either verdict from the same data, so a design that is not published in advance reports its author's preference in the units of a statistic. §9.7.3 carries that argument to its conclusion: the planned parameters have to be sealed, and the realized flip band published beside the verdict.

The fourth falsifier is worth stating in advance because its failure would be informative and not fatal. $F\_4$ tests non-substitutability, and a compensatory agent that doubles its physical reserve buffer could partially absorb mild demand shocks when no absorbing survival barrier is active. Were that to hold, non-substitutability would narrow to a **regime-conditional boundary law**: strict when absorbing ruin barriers and severe non-ergodic shocks are present, partially compensable under moderate shocks with doubled physical slack. §9.7.2 reads that narrowing against Lakatos's criterion, because a narrowing is what a conjunctive theory can always buy.

## 9.6 The Synthesis and the Pre-Registered Falsifiers

The synthesis chapter's instrument is the $2\times2\times2$ factorial design in which each of the three safeguards is either present or absent, read through the Leontief minimum. Its purpose is to make each collapse proposition a statement about a cell of the design and not a claim about institutions in general. The cell that keeps the vital margin and drops the epistemic one is where blind undersizing must appear, blind in the sense Collapse Proposition 1 fixes: the reserve is sized from naive sample means with $\rho\_{R,Y} \neq 0$ and $\mathrm{DEFF} > 1$ unmeasured and no partial-identification width counted. The cell that keeps the epistemic margin and drops the vital one is where impotent lucidity must appear, at a survival time below the recovery time ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$). The cell that drops only governance is where cynical capture must appear. A diagnosis then names the proposition and the mechanism; it is not a deficit score, and the design is informative because its cells are not a ranking.

The same toggles can be run at a common reserve buffer and a common leverage, so each axis's contribution is attributed against the same endowment and not against a favourable one. Two negative controls bound the attribution at both ends: a giant buffer without an epistemic margin, which must still fail wherever the barrier is reachable, and a benign world in which the naive rule survives, which shows where the triad is not needed at all. Without the second control the factorial would show only that margins are expensive.

The threshold and structural grids perturb the diagnostic thresholds and the generating parameters across their declared ranges and report invariance. Their value is the boundary they locate, not the invariance itself. With record probabilities, tail indices and overload coefficients all stressed at once, reserve growth dominance and intact survival have to hold in the interior and fail where the tail index is lowest and the overload cost highest at the same time. The claim is the location of that boundary, not the share of the interior that holds.

The closed-form coverage ladder belongs to the same chapter and to a different instrument. It links the finite-sample behaviour of an i.i.d. coverage claim, at a given cluster structure and selection correlation, to Meng's asymptotic identity across sampling fractions: coverage must fall away as the fraction and the defect dictate, and the support-based bound must stay where it was. The ladder is a proof obligation and not a measurement. It says what the naive coverage claim has to do as the archive grows, and it is why Chapter 45's identity is repeated here instead of assumed.

Van Evera's and Bennett and Checkel's process-tracing taxonomy, Mayo's severity criterion, the four brakes, the Leontief-versus-product comparison and the four falsifiers complete the instrument set. Each has its own section above. What the synthesis contributes is that they are read together, so that the epistemic margin's coverage claim, the vital margin's survival claim and the governance margin's alignment claim are three statements about one trajectory and not three literatures. Whether a particular trajectory satisfies them is a question for a pre-registered design: §9.5 fixes it, and §9.7.3 says it must be sealed before it runs.

### The decibans and the shape of a refutation

The deciban ledger records what the clue bought, and the evidential weight moves without the archive changing: the clue raises the probability and deepens the basis in one step, the distinction §9.2.4 exists to keep. The falsifier machinery is the same obligation in the currency of a test. What would count against the claim, stated in advance and in the units of the claim it is meant to discipline.

The method's reflexivity is why the book closes with it. A framework that tells institutions to pre-register their tests and publish their failure conditions has to do the same to itself, and the four brakes are that obligation written down. A framework publishing its falsifiers only after the results, or only as a verdict without a design, would have exempted itself from the rule it imposes on everyone else.

The five methodological families are the last piece of the map, and their value is negative as much as positive. Knowing which family a claim belongs to tells a reader what the claim cannot show. A process-tracing inference cannot estimate an average effect, and an average effect cannot say why one case went the way it did. The pair is a division of labour, not a hierarchy.

### When self-application is dispensable

Self-application is dispensable when the programme is used as a descriptive tool and prescribes no decision. Declare the descriptive use and switch off the four brakes explicitly; do not leave them in the text as decoration that no reader is expected to apply.

### Four brakes as four questions

The four brakes are better restated as questions than as commitments, because that is how they are used. Does the framework name the observation that would refute it? Does it say which of its own claims are informative and not definitional? Does it survive being applied to itself? And does it tell a reader what it cannot show? A programme that answers all four has earned the right to be used on other people's decisions; one that answers three has earned the right to be read. The questions cost a paragraph here and a rewrite wherever the answer is no.

The third and fourth brakes protect the framework against its own institutional capture. Reflexive Brake Three formalizes the operational principle that legitimacy does not buy latency: procedural consensus and democratic mandate cannot substitute for physical buffer time, so whenever recovery latency exceeds the survival window, emergency action takes precedence over prolonged assembleism. Reflexive Brake Four prohibits transforming the non-compensatory Leontief synthesis into an optimizable bureaucratic target, which keeps the triad an independent diagnostic and not an instrument of managerial gaming.

## 9.7. Weight, Severity, and the Parameters of the Test

### 9.7.1 Two limits of the deciban ledger

The calculus of §9.2.3 assumes conditional independence of the clues, and that assumption has a measurable limit. Two operational clues serve: the sustained wind at the current fix is at least one threshold, or at least a stricter one, each a diagnostic test for the next fix reaching hurricane force. The wider clue is the weaker test, the narrower clue the stronger one, and the nesting holds because the stricter event implies the wider one.

Summing the two weights overstates the evidence by the weight of the wider clue, because the conjunction of the two clues *is* the narrower clue. The error is not a small-sample bias that more history converges away. It is the flattering arithmetic of a nested pair: the analyst adds the weight of a condition and then adds the weight of a condition that entails it.

The second limit runs the other way and is closed form. A sequence of $m$ clues each worth a likelihood ratio $1+\eta$ accumulates $10m\log\_{10}(1+\eta)$ decibans; a long enough sequence drives the posterior odds arbitrarily high, and nothing in the calculus objects — and nothing in Keynes's weight objects either, because none of the clues adds to $K$: probability has been driven high on an unchanged evidentiary base, by counting the same evidence many times. That is why §9.2.6's refutability precondition is load-bearing, and why pairing $P$ with $V$ is not decorative. Weight distinguishes evidence that accumulated from the same evidence counted.

### 9.7.2 The narrowing a conjunctive theory can always buy

A refuted conjunct is never the end of a conjunctive theory, and the falsifier machinery has to be able to name that failure. Were $F\_4$'s design to return $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ with $\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) \ge 1 - \alpha$, the immediate rescue is available. Narrow the claim to the regime in which an absorbing barrier is active, and the falsifier's regime becomes the exception and not the refutation, while the framework keeps its thesis.

The rescue is not the defect. The defect is that it is indistinguishable from progress unless the narrowed version predicts something new. The criterion is Lakatos's: a modification is progressive if it predicts a new fact and degenerating if it only accommodates the old one (Lakatos 1970). A narrowing that predicts nothing the existing falsifiers do not already test is an accommodation, and by the criterion the chapter itself invokes, the machinery has been demonstrated while the thesis has not. To make the regime-conditional boundary law progressive rather than ad hoc, the narrowed claim must commit to an independently testable boundary prediction over the canonical survival inequality $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(B\_t) \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$—where $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ with queue utilization $\rho\_t$ entering the queue-drain actuation latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.3.2): compensatory physical slack ceases to rescue a collapsed axis strictly across the frontier $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(B\_t) < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ or when an absorbing barrier is active, whereas inside the interior $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(B\_t) \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ without an active absorbing barrier, doubled physical slack can partially buffer moderate shocks.

The recommendation follows and is cheap. A composite claim needs one pre-registered falsifier per conjunct, so that narrowing a conjunct costs a refutation instead of buying one. Read that way, $F\_4$ is worth pre-registering because its failure has a determinate consequence, the claim narrows to a regime-conditional boundary law anchored on $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(B\_t) \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, and because the cost of that narrowing is then a published refutation and not a silent accommodation.

### 9.7.3 The design parameters are not sealed

The four brakes make the programme self-auditable, on the assumption that someone who did not write it can verify the audit. One parameter breaks that assumption, and it is the one the falsifier machinery rests on: $\delta\_{\min}$ is chosen by the programme.

The arithmetic of that choice is three lines. At realized effective sample size $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, the realized standard error is $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \sigma\_d/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}$; with positive upper quantile $z\_{1-\alpha} > 0$, the realized cutoffs are $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ and $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$. Hence the post-data refutation threshold $\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}} \ge 1 - \alpha$ flips exactly when $\delta\_{\min}$ crosses $\delta\_{\min}^{\mathrm{fals,flip}} = \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$, and the post-data corroboration threshold $\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}} \ge 1 - \alpha$ flips exactly when $\delta\_{\min}$ crosses $\delta\_{\min}^{\mathrm{corrob,flip}} = \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$. That inversion band around $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ of half-width $z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is a property of the observed data and of the design, and the declared threshold sits above or below those flip boundaries by a margin that is itself a design choice. A shifted threshold would have produced the opposite verdict on the same data, the same design, and the same severity criterion.

That invalidates the chapter's final claim. Self-application is verifiable only so far as the test's parameters are fixed outside the programme's discretion, and the four brakes carry no brake on $\delta\_{\min}$. The procedure is the one [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.1.2 built for another purpose: seal the planned design—$\delta\_{\min}$, $\sigma\_d$, $\alpha$, $1-\beta$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, the $F\_2$ exposure threshold $\alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ over $(T, n)$, and the pairing rule—inside the commitment before the paired evaluation runs, and publish the realized flip band $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \pm z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ beside the verdict.

A falsifier reported with its band is worth more than one reported with its verdict, because the band tells a reader whether the verdict was a finding or a setting. The chapter's closing claim is that a programme stating the arithmetic that would refute it has earned the right to be applied to other people's decisions. The claim survives, weaker and defensible. It has earned that right when the stated arithmetic cannot be adjusted afterwards, which is a claim about sealing, and therefore one this book has already made.

## Bridge

The framework closes on a scalar, five methodological families, process tracing and four pre-registered
falsifiers. That scalar is per episode, and an episode is not a discipline.

Part V distributes it as a model. On paper each of the thirty-five orders inherits a margin not from a
neighbour it resembles but from every basal order in its own dependency cone, through the homomorphism
$\mathcal{H}(L\_i) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_j)$, so a collapse in a
physical margin would reach a legal one by the algebra of minima and not by analogy. What the corpus
executes is narrower: the cone is model data, and the fold is exercised at the level of the graph rather
than measured for any order, so the inheritance is a model reading and not a diagnostic. The first order,
[The Micro-Kinetic and Sensorimotor Order](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md), is a basal root
with no parents and therefore the chapter that shows what a margin looks like when nothing upstream can
excuse it. The debt carried into Part V is the aggregation over cones; the debt carried out of the book is
that each of the thirty-five is a place where the triad has to be re-earned.
