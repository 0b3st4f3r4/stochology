## 9.1 The Five Methodological Families of Stochology

Stochology replaces no discipline. It works as an integrative syntax across the three-plane mediation circuit linking world ($P\_1$), archive ($P\_2$), and institution ($P\_3$) ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)):

```
               INSTITUTION (P3: Decision, Court, Clinic)
              /  ^                                   /  ^
   T6 Perform/    \T5 Temporal            T1 Frame  /    \T4 Formalize
            v      \                               v      \
   WORLD (P1: Phenomenon)  <=== T3 Observe / T2 Test ===>  ARCHIVE (P2: Data & Model)
               \                                         /
                +--- Integral Closure: T7 (Liability) --+
```

Rather than carving up disconnected islands, the five methodological families represent **distinct inferential regimes** governed together by all three Canonical Operators—Epistemic Margin ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$), Vital Margin ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$), and Institutional Governance ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$)—with explicit limits on what each family can prove:

| Family | Domain of Inquiry | Primary Failure Mode of the Naive Optimiser | Canonical Stochological Safeguards | What the Family Cannot Establish |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 9.2 Family 5 in Detail: Galenic *Sēmeîa*, Process Tracing, and Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$

Historians, investigative reporters, judges, and bedside clinicians rarely estimate a slope over ten thousand spreadsheets. Instead, they reconstruct a causal chain inside a **single unfolding case** by weighing qualitative signs. That lineage runs from *sēmeîa* in Hippocrates's *Prognostic* I (c. 400 BCE, reading signs across past, present, and future) and Galen's *De locis affectis* I.1 (c. 200 CE) through modern *Process Tracing* (Van Evera 1997; Bennett & Checkel 2015).

This qualitative family stands on wide historical ground. Ibn al-Haytham's *iʿtibār* cross-tested optical and physical claims by varying isolated conditions against rival hypotheses (Sabra 1989). Ibn Khaldūn's *Muqaddimah* built structural historiography by testing chroniclers' narratives against demographic, ecological, and institutional realities (Rosenthal 1958). Galen (c. 200 CE) called diagnosis over hidden internal causes *tekhnikòs stokhasmós*—skilled conjecture between certainty and total ignorance—and warned that universal foolproof signs rarely exist for every disease, placing real diagnostic clues inside the interior probability interval $(0, 1)$.

### 9.2.1 Epistemic Foundations: The Clinical and Archival Clue

Every qualitative clue $E$ is scored by two conditional probabilities:

1. **Sensitivity under Focal Hypothesis $H$:** $p\_H = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H) \in (0, 1)$.
2. **False-Positive Rate under Rival Explanation $\neg H$:** $p\_{\neg H} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H) \in (0, 1)$.

The pair is the whole evaluation. A clue is never strong or weak in isolation; it is informative only relative to competing accounts. Under equifinality—when several rival mechanisms inside $\neg H$ could also produce clue $E$—$p\_{\neg H}$ must track the strongest plausible alternative rather than an easy strawman.

### 9.2.2 Bennett & Checkel Diagnostic Test Taxonomy

The square $(p\_H, p\_{\neg H})$ partitions into Van Evera's four classic qualitative tests:

- **Straw in the wind:** modest necessity and modest sufficiency. The likelihood ratio stays mild, nudging odds slightly without proving or refuting anything.
- **Hoop test:** **high necessity.** If $H$ happened, clue $E$ *must* appear.
  - Likelihood ratio if observed: $\mathrm{LR}^+ = \frac{p\_H}{p\_{\neg H}} > 1$ (gentle support).
  - Likelihood ratio if **absent**: $\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \ll 1$ (**severe elimination**).
  - *Epistemic principle:* Clearing a hoop does not prove $H$, whereas **failing a pre-registered hoop refutes $H$** under §9.2.6's lexical veto.
- **Smoking gun:** **high sufficiency** (approaching a pathognomonic signature where one exists, tempered by Galen's warning against assuming infallible signs).
  - Likelihood ratio if observed: $\mathrm{LR}^+ = \frac{p\_H}{p\_{\neg H}} \gg 1$ (**strong confirmation**).
  - Likelihood ratio if absent: $\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \approx 1$ when $p\_H, p\_{\neg H} \ll 1$ (absence does not clear $H$ out).
- **Doubly decisive:** **both necessary and sufficient.**
  - If observed: confirms $H$ strongly ($\mathrm{LR}^+ \gg 1$) within Cromwell's interior bounds.
  - If absent: refutes $H$ ($\mathrm{LR}^- \ll 1$, enforced by §9.2.6).

Notice how the four tests partition inquiry duties rather than clues themselves. A case built purely from smoking guns can confirm but never eliminate; a case built purely from hoops can eliminate but never confirm.

### 9.2.3 Turing–Good Deciban Calculus

Following Alan Turing's wartime Bletchley Park weights and I. J. Good (1950, 1979), evidence strength $W(E; H : \neg H)$ adds logarithmically in **decibans** ($\mathrm{dB}$) from likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H)/\mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)$:
$$W(E; H : \neg H) = 10 \log\_{10} \frac{\mathbb{P}(E \mid H)}{\mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)} = 10 \log\_{10} \mathrm{LR}$$

Given $m$ clues $E\_1, \dots, E\_m$ that are conditionally independent under both $H$ and $\neg H$, their individual likelihood ratios $\mathrm{LR}\_j$ multiply and total weight $W\_{\mathrm{total}}$ adds:
$$W\_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum\_{j=1}^m W(E\_j; H : \neg H) = 10 \log\_{10} \prod\_{j=1}^m \mathrm{LR}\_j$$

Starting from prior odds $\mathrm{Odds}\_0$, Bayes' Rule turns decibans into posterior odds $\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}$ and posterior probability $\mathbb{P}(H \mid E\_{1..m})$:
$$\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}} = \mathrm{Odds}\_0 \times 10^{W\_{\mathrm{total}} / 10}, \qquad \mathbb{P}(H \mid E\_{1..m}) = \frac{\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}{1 + \mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}$$

Adding $+10\text{ dB}$ multiplies the odds of $H$ tenfold, while $-10\text{ dB}$ shrinks them tenfold.

### 9.2.4 John Maynard Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$

In *A Treatise on Probability* (1921), John Maynard Keynes separated probability balance $P$ from **evidential weight** $V$—how much relevant evidence supports the verdict relative to remaining ignorance—without proposing one cardinal formula. Stochology tracks that depth alongside odds using acquired knowledge $K \ge 0$ and constant initial ignorance $I\_0 > 0$ (held fixed under a closed-frame idealization, which breaks whenever inquiry uncovers brand-new ignorance $\Delta I > 0$):
$$V = \frac{K}{K + I\_0}$$

Starting from initial knowledge $K\_0$, each genuinely non-redundant clue $j \in \lbrace 1, \dots, m \rbrace$ contributes knowledge increment $\Delta K\_j \ge 0$ to posterior weight $V\_{\mathrm{post}}$:
$$V\_{\mathrm{post}} = \frac{K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^m \Delta K\_j}{K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^m \Delta K\_j + I\_0}$$

Probability reports which way evidence leans; weight $V$ reports how deeply the record was checked, separating fragile high-odds hunches from well-tested conclusions.

### 9.2.5 The Same Inference in Symbols

Across three conditionally independent clues—doubly decisive, smoking gun, and hoop ($j = 1, 2, 3$)—each adds deciban score $W\_j = 10\log\_{10}\mathrm{LR}\_j$ and depth increment $\Delta K\_j \ge 0$ to prior odds $\mathrm{Odds}\_0 = p\_0 / (1 - p\_0)$ ($p\_0 \in (0, 1)$) and baseline weight $V\_0 = K\_0 / (K\_0 + I\_0)$ ($K\_0 \ge 0, I\_0 > 0$). Multiplying likelihood ratios sums $W\_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum\_j W\_j$ into $\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}} = \mathrm{Odds}\_0 \prod\_j \mathrm{LR}\_j$, while $K\_{\mathrm{post}} = K\_0 + \sum\_j \Delta K\_j$ yields $V\_{\mathrm{post}} = K\_{\mathrm{post}}/(K\_{\mathrm{post}} + I\_0)$: clues commute algebraically under independence, though chronological order still tests causal precedence.

### 9.2.6 Refutability and the Asymmetry of the Hoop Test

Qualitative case reconstruction is epistemically valid only under three rules:

- probabilities obey Cromwell's interior bounds ($p\_0, p\_H, p\_{\neg H} \in (0, 1)$);
- each non-redundant clue updates both Bayesian odds and evidential weight $V$;
- **Popper–Mayo refutability (lexical veto):** the protocol pre-registers at least one hoop test or doubly decisive clue whose failure imposes a **non-compensatory refutation veto** outside Bayes' rule.

Because Cromwell's bounds forbid $p\_H = 1$ or $p\_{\neg H} = 0$, a missing hoop clue leaves a finite likelihood factor $\mathrm{LR}^-$ ($W^- > -\infty$):
$$\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \in (0, 1) \qquad (W^- > -\infty),$$

so flattering clues could numerically swamp a missing necessity test. Pairing Bayesian odds with an external lexical veto closes that loophole: when a pre-registered hoop fails, $\mathrm{LR}^-$ lowers odds while the veto forces refutation regardless of positive decibans elsewhere.

## 9.3 Reflexive Self-Application: The Four Internal Goodhart Brakes

Any institution that converts a diagnostic indicator into an executive target destroys the indicator (Goodhart, 1975; Strathern, 1997; Espeland & Stevens, 1998). Reflexivity is incomplete until the analyst's own tools enter the frame: the discipline supplying the question, the career served by the verdict, and the measurement instruments themselves (Bourdieu 2004). Stochology protects its own safeguards from bureaucratic decay through four internal brakes.

### 9.3.1 Brake 1 (Epistemic Margin): Skeptical Inflation vs. CORP Resolution

- *Perversion:* Analysts could score $100\%$ coverage effortlessly by stretching bounds all the way to $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$, or predicting the flat sample mean everywhere. Coverage rises mechanically with width, so raw coverage targets reward uselessly wide intervals.
- *Mathematical Brake:* Recall Murphy (1973) and Dimitriadis, Gneiting, and Jordan's (2021) CORP Brier decomposition into $\bar{y}(1 - \bar{y}) + \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}$ ([Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md); [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md)). Predicting the constant base rate ($p\_t \equiv \bar{y}$) earns zero calibration error ($\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = 0$) yet **zero resolution** ($\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = 0$), because it cannot distinguish storms from calm days. Since CORP evaluates point probabilities rather than intervals, epistemic audits pair required resolution gains over base rates with explicit width limits and Regime II minimax-regret probes $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}}$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)), preventing lazy inflation to $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$.

### 9.3.2 Brake 2 (Vital Margin): Regressive Hoarding vs. Scarf–Samuelson Welfare

- *Perversion:* Managers could hoard idle reserves ($R \to \infty$) under the banner of extreme prudence, starving present community needs. A reserve floor with no upper diagnostic check rewards hoarding just as coverage rewards width.
- *Mathematical Brake:* With diminishing-benefit parameter $\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}} > 0$ and linear urgency cost $c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} > 0$ (Scarf 1960; Samuelson 1971), an ex-post welfare diagnostic $W(R)$ balances concave precautionary security against linear social opportunity cost:
  $$W(R) = \kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}} \ln(1 + R) - c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} R$$
  Setting the derivative to zero locates the finite ex-post hoarding ceiling $R^\ast$:
  $$\frac{dW}{dR} = \frac{\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}}{1 + R} - c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} = 0 \implies R^\ast = \max\left(0,\mkern3mu \frac{\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}}{c\_{\mathrm{urgent}}} - 1\right)$$
  In plain words, past ceiling $R^\ast$, every extra unit hoarded buys less logarithmic security than the linear social harm it inflicts by withholding vital supplies. Strictly, $W(R)$ and $R^\ast$ act solely as an **ex-post audit of regressive hoarding** ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md))—never as a live rationing controller—with $(\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}, c\_{\mathrm{urgent}})$ pre-anchored outside the unit (§9.7.3).

## 9.4 Leontief Non-Substitutable Aggregation vs. Multiplicative and Arithmetic Collapse

### 9.4.1 Proof of Chart-Conditional 3D Lebesgue Feasible Volume Contraction

Take a declared strictly increasing axis normalization $(e, v, g) = \bigl(\varphi\_{\mathrm{epi}}(s\_{\mathrm{epi}}), \varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}(s\_{\mathrm{vital}}), \varphi\_{\mathrm{gov}}(s\_{\mathrm{gov}})\bigr) \in [0, 1]^3$ and survival threshold $\tau \in (0, 1)$. [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4) compares the volume of the Leontief box $[\tau, 1]^3$, $V\_{\min}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3$, against the multiplicative product region $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}$, $V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = 1 - \tau + \tau\ln\tau - \frac{\tau}{2}(\ln\tau)^2$: as $\tau$ rises, $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ shrinks much faster than $[\tau, 1]^3$.

Crucially, ordinal box invariance differs from Euclidean volume invariance (Stevens 1946; Krantz, Luce, Suppes, & Tversky 1971; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Any strictly increasing $C^1$ per-axis recoding preserves the Leontief product box while distorting the curved boundary $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}$. Meanwhile, Lebesgue volume transforms by $\lvert\det J\_\psi\rvert$ and holds the ratio $V\_{\min}/V\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ uniform only across the **restricted affine monoid** $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ ($0 < \alpha\_a \le 1$; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4). Grounded in social-choice theory (Arrow 1950; Sen 1985), non-utility threshold boxes avoid arbitrary cross-axis exchange rates and confine $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ strictly to ex-post audits, avoiding Harsanyi's (1975) maximin-paralysis critique.

### 9.4.2 False Collapses on Healthy Grids vs. False-Safe Failures on Ruin Boundaries

Two grid experiments make the failure of alternative aggregators vivid:

1. **A healthy factorial grid**, where every single margin sits comfortably above threshold $\tau$:
   - **Pure Leontief ($\min$):** zero false collapses, because the minimum of numbers above $\tau$ stays above $\tau$.
   - **Hybrid governance** (combining a minimum with a product): falsely rejects a large fraction of healthy states whenever multiplying two moderate factors dips below $\tau$.
   - **Full product:** falsely rejects almost the entire healthy grid, admitting only the extreme corner where all three scores hug $1$.
2. **A single-axis ruin grid**, where one margin collapses to zero while the other two look healthy:
   - **Compensatory arithmetic mean:** averages one zero with two high scores and clears the cutoff—issuing a **false-safe pass in every single case**. That is the lethal failure mode because it authorizes ruinous action.
   - **Pure Leontief ($\min$):** zero false-safe admissions. Wherever a missing safeguard hits an absorbing cliff, the Sprengel–Liebig–Leontief minimum (Sprengel 1828; Liebig 1855; Leontief 1941) dominates averages and multiplicative blends.

Notice the strict role of analogy here. Gilboa and Schmeidler's (1986) Maxmin Expected Utility (MEU)—minimizing expected utility across priors—serves purely as a structural analogy across axes. Whether smooth ambiguity preferences (Klibanoff, Marinacci, & Mukerji 2005) allow doubled physical slack to partially buffer a weak axis during mild, barrier-free shocks stays open for pre-registered falsifier $F\_4$ (§9.5.3, §9.7.2).

## 9.5 Severe Testing and the Five Pre-Registered Falsifiers

### 9.5.1 Deborah Mayo's Severity Principle and Burden-of-Proof Inversion

A familiar methodological trick sets the null hypothesis to "zero difference" ($H\_0: \Delta = 0$) and boasts that a rule is "validated" whenever a noisy test fails to reject $H\_0$. Stochology reverses that burden of proof (Mayo 2018) by testing observed harm reduction $\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}}$ against substantive harm threshold $\delta\_{\min}$ under null $H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}$ and falsifying alternative $H\_1^{\mathrm{fals}}$:
$$H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} \ge \delta\_{\min} \quad \text{vs.} \quad H\_1^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} < \delta\_{\min}$$

Refuting a safeguard thus cannot rest on noisy silence: data must demonstrate with post-data severity that omitting it loses less than $\delta\_{\min}$.

### 9.5.2 Pre-Registered Design Formula

Model paired harm reductions under Spanos's (2021) adequacy checks as $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \sim \mathcal{N}(\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}}, \sigma\_d^2 / n\_{\mathrm{eff}})$ (switching to Student-$t$ when $\sigma\_d$ is estimated; Mayo 2018), where $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is defined in §9.5.3 and power $1 - \beta$ sits at $\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} = 0$.

Fix one-sided $\alpha \in (0, 0.5)$ ($z\_{1-\alpha} = \Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha) > 0$) and power $1 - \beta$ with $\beta \in (0, 1 - \alpha)$ ($z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta} > 0$). Given $\delta\_{\min} > 0$ and $\sigma\_d > 0$, four **pre-data planned** quantities seal beforehand:
$$n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}} = \left\lceil \left( \frac{(z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta})\sigma\_d}{\delta\_{\min}} \right)^2 \right\rceil, \qquad \mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}} = \frac{\sigma\_d}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}}},$$
$$d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}.$$

Given observed mean $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ at realized effective count $n\_{\mathrm{eff}} > 0$, realized cutoffs follow symmetrically:
$$\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \frac{\sigma\_d}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}.$$

Since $\Phi$ increases strictly with $\Phi(z\_{1-\alpha}) = 1 - \alpha$, Mayo's post-data severity satisfies:
$$\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) = \Phi\\!\left(\frac{\delta\_{\min} - \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}{\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}\right) \ge 1 - \alpha \iff \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}},$$
$$\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) = \Phi\\!\left(\frac{\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - \delta\_{\min}}{\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}\right) \ge 1 - \alpha \iff \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \ge d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}.$$

Observations must clear $z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ below $\delta\_{\min}$ to refute or above $\delta\_{\min}$ to corroborate, leaving the gap honestly inconclusive and matching planned cutoffs when $n\_{\mathrm{eff}} = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$.

Before trusting either severity score, the statistical model must pass **statistical adequacy** via rigorous misspecification checks (Spanos 2021). As Spanos proves, violated distributional, independence, or homogeneity assumptions distort nominal Type I error probabilities, turning both cutoffs and severity values into model artifacts rather than genuine evidence against $H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}$.

### 9.5.3 The Five Canonical Falsifiers

Five falsifiers are pre-registered before evaluation, each returning a paired harm-reduction contrast $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ judged under §9.5.2 and §9.5.4:

1. **$F\_1$ ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$, observational transport):** tests whether the epistemic margin cuts coverage shortfall when transporting across declared missingness and clustering. It fails if, on that declared transport design, paired shortfall reduction $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ over uncorrected arms satisfies $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.
2. **$F\_2$ ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$, heavy tails and queues):** tests whether the vital margin prevents barrier absorption under heavy-tailed demand at utilization $\rho$ and return period $T > 1$, once the bilateral shock-exposure gate passes. It fails if paired survival-shortfall reduction $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ over unbuffered arms at shared endowment obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ (even if unbuffered units survive the exposed window).
3. **$F\_3$ ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$, multitask proxy gaming):** tests whether governance curbs proxy cannibalization of the substantive goal. It fails if, with governance active over matched common-endowment control arms, proxy scores rise while paired substantive-goal gain $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.
4. **$F\_4$ ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$, non-compensability):** tests non-substitutability—our central structural claim—by contrasting a strict-minimum arm against an arm carrying doubled physical slack under a mild shock without an active absorbing barrier. It fails if the strict minimum's paired harm-reduction advantage obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.
5. **$F\_5$ ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$, cross-perimeter non-externalization):** tests whether the reception-perimeter fold catches cross-perimeter harm export invisible to local three-axis checks. It fails if, across coupled perimeters with declared spillover channel and tolerance $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}}$, paired deficit-detection gain $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ over perimeter-exporting arms obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.

Why does the second falsifier require an explicit shock-exposure gate? Over $n \ge 1$ effective independent steps, a quiet run misses a $T$-period tail shock with probability $(1 - 1/T)^n$ ([Chapter 03](03-the-biggest-number-in-the-room.md)). Requiring $(1 - 1/T)^n \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ (or a realized $T$-period shock) stops brief calm runs from logging fake outcomes.

All planned targets—$\delta\_{\min}$, $\sigma\_d$, $\alpha$, $1 - \beta$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $\alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ over $(T, n)$ for $F\_2$, and the pairing rule—seal cryptographically beforehand, published beside realized cutoffs and §9.7.3's inversion band.

For single-axis ablations ($F\_1$–$F\_3$, $F\_5$, and §9.6's factorial cube), matched pairs with and without that safeguard share common buffer and leverage; $F\_4$ uses its endowment-substitution contrast, and arm assignment seals beforehand so pairing cannot shift ex post.

Proper pairing also prevents double-counting cluster variance or confusing selection bias with variance. Write paired arm outcomes as $\mu\_A + u\_c + e\_A$ and $\mu\_B + u\_c + e\_B$, assuming zero cluster-by-arm interaction so cluster shock $u\_c$ cancels inside a cluster. Define total variance $\sigma^2 = \sigma\_u^2 + \sigma\_e^2$ and intra-cluster correlation $\rho\_I = \sigma\_u^2/\sigma^2$. Then the clustered sample design effect is $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I$ for Moulton's effective cluster size $\tilde{m} = \bar{m}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr)$ ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.3; Kish 1965; Moulton 1986). Within one cluster, difference $d\_c = (\mu\_A - \mu\_B) + (e\_A - e\_B)$ cancels $u\_c$, giving $\mathrm{Var}(d\_c) = 2\sigma\_e^2 = 2\sigma^2(1 - \rho\_I)$, so $\rho\_I$ enters $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$ once inside $\sigma\_d^2$ ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = 1$).

Across distinct clusters ($c\_1 \ne c\_2$), $u\_{c\_1}$ and $u\_{c\_2}$ do not cancel, giving $\mathrm{Var}(d) = 2\sigma^2$. Here $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = 1$ when each cluster supplies at most one pair; if one cluster supplies $m\_{\mathrm{pair}}$ pairs matched against separate external clusters, sharing one leg creates pair correlation $\rho\_I/2$ and $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = 1 + (m\_{\mathrm{pair}} - 1)\rho\_I/2$ (reaching $\rho\_I$ only if every pair shares $(c\_1, c\_2)$). Kish weighting acts strictly on **pair-level weights** $w\_i$ across uncorrelated pairs under small shared-leg $m\_{\mathrm{pair}}$ (distinct from unit-level cluster-and-weight additivity in [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.3), because unequal leg weights break $u\_c$ cancellation, yielding $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d}\_w) = \sigma\_d^2 \sum w\_i^2/(\sum w\_i)^2$ and $n\_{\mathrm{eff}} = n\_{\mathrm{pairs}}/(\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}}\cdot \mathrm{DEFF}\_w)$ inside $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md) §2.6). Meanwhile, non-random selection ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$, governed by Meng's factor $((1-f)/f)\bar{\rho}^2$ in [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.2.2) enters as a separate systematic location interval $\pm \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})$ on $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$, never inside $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

### 9.5.4 How a Verdict Is Read

Every falsifier reads outcomes through four fixed zones using realized $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \sigma\_d/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}$, and cutoffs $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ from §9.5.2:

- **Exposure admissibility ($F\_2$ bilateral gate):** Before scoring either branch of $F\_2$, check $(1 - 1/T)^n \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ (or at least one realized $T$-period shock). If it fails without a shock, mark the run **non-informative** (*untested*)—never refuting or corroborating the vital safeguard.
- **Refutation:** If $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ ($\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) \ge 1 - \alpha$) and any exposure gate passes, that safeguard fails in the declared regime, obliging revision or withdrawal.
- **Corroboration:** If $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \ge d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ ($\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) \ge 1 - \alpha$) and any exposure gate passes, the safeguard is corroborated at that severity level.
- **Inconclusive zone:** If $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \in (d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}, d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}})$, neither verdict clears post-data severity $1 - \alpha$.

Any decisive verdict must publish its sealed parameters alongside §9.7.3's inversion band ($\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \pm z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$) showing where shifting $\delta\_{\min}$ reverses the call.

Pre-registering $F\_4$ is equally clarifying: if doubled slack successfully buffered barrier-free moderate shocks, non-substitutability would refine into a **regime-conditional boundary law**—strict near absorbing barriers and partially compensable inside shock-free interiors (§9.7.2).

## 9.6 The Synthesis and the Pre-Registered Falsifiers

Our synthesis instrument is the $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial cube in $(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace^3$, audited through the Leontief minimum. It pits the complete triad $(1,1,1)$ against bare point optimization $(0,0,0)$, three single-axis ablations isolating Collapse Propositions 1–3, and three double-axis ablations—$(1,0,0)$, $(0,1,0)$, and $(0,0,1)$—where one lone axis cannot prevent or even hastens ruin.

Following [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), each single-axis loss surfaces a distinct collapse:

- **Dropping $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ (`(0,1,1)`):** induces blind undersizing from biased point averages rather than robust structural floors (Neth & Gigerenzer 2015; Haldane & Madouros 2012).
- **Dropping $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ (`(1,0,1)`):** fails the survival clock race ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$) once Kornai's (1986) bailout illusion is stripped away.
- **Dropping $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ (`(1,1,0)`):** invites cynical capture, $\mathrm{PRI}$ framing manipulation ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), perverse proxy rewards, and cross-perimeter spillover across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$, Kerr 1975; Ostrom 2010; Dietz, Ostrom, & Stern 2003; [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md) §7.7.2; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

Primary conflict-of-interest research demonstrates why losing governance turns scientific uncertainty into manufactured delay. Globally, more than 350,000 chemicals have been registered; in passive-smoking studies, approximately one-third of 100 articles concluded no harm, and 74% of the authors of those non-harm articles were affiliated with the tobacco industry (Schäffer et al. 2023). Similarly, a review of 39 atrazine studies at environmentally relevant doses (11 industry-sponsored, 24 non-industry-sponsored, 4 with no funding disclosures) found that 50.0% (12/24) of non-industry studies concluded atrazine was harmful compared with 18.1% (2/11) of industry-sponsored studies (Bero et al. 2016). Every factorial cell diagnosis names a mechanism—never a composite rank score.

Holding buffer and leverage fixed across runs (aside from $F\_4$'s slack substitution) isolates each axis cleanly. Two negative controls bound inference: an oversized reserve with blind epistemic inputs still fails whenever the barrier is reachable, while a calm reversible world where naive rules survive marks where full triadic safeguards are unnecessary.

## 9.7 Weight, Severity, and the Parameters of the Test

### 9.7.1 Two limits of the deciban ledger

The deciban sum of §9.2.3 assumes conditional independence across clues—an assumption with two sharp failure boundaries.

First, consider two nested operational clues: sustained wind at the current fix clears one speed bar, or clears a stricter bar, each predicting hurricane strength next fix. Because the stricter event implies the wider one, adding both deciban scores double-counts the wider condition inside the narrower one. More observations never fix this error: it is redundant counting of a nested pair.

Second, a closed-form limit exposes simple repetition. Repeating the same clue worth $\mathrm{LR} = 1 + \eta$ ($\eta > 0$) $m$ times adds $10m\log\_{10}(1 + \eta)$ decibans and pushes posterior probability toward $1$ under false independence. Yet evidential weight $V$ remains frozen at $V\_0 = K\_0 / (K\_0 + I\_0)$, because echoing one clue adds zero new knowledge ($\Delta K\_j = 0$). High posterior odds paired with stagnant weight $V$ flags double counting at once—which is why Stochology tracks $(P, V)$ jointly alongside §9.2.6's hoop refutation.

### 9.7.2 The narrowing a conjunctive theory can always buy

A refuted conjunct need not sink the full programme if the rescue frontier is pre-registered rather than improvised. Under Lakatos (1970), narrowing $F\_4$ after $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ ($\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}} \ge 1 - \alpha$) is progressive only if it predicts a risky boundary: doubled slack fails strictly past $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(B\_t) < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ (with $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}} = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ and congestion $\rho\_t$ driving $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$; [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.3.2) or near absorbing cliffs, cushioning moderate shocks only inside barrier-free headroom—keeping the failed $F\_4$ refutation openly on record.

### 9.7.3 The design parameters are not sealed

The four brakes make the framework self-auditable only if outsiders can verify every threshold. One choice strains that assumption: $\delta\_{\min}$ is selected by the framework designer.

The exact sensitivity spans three lines of algebra. At realized effective pairs $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \sigma\_d/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}$. Given positive quantile $z\_{1-\alpha} > 0$, our realized thresholds are $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ and $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$. Solving for $\delta\_{\min}$ locates two exact flip points:

- **Refutation flips** ($\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}} \ge 1 - \alpha$) whenever $\delta\_{\min} \ge \delta\_{\min}^{\mathrm{fals,flip}} := \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ (which is positive when $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} > -z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$; otherwise every $\delta\_{\min} > 0$ refutes).
- **Corroboration flips** ($\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}} \ge 1 - \alpha$) whenever $\delta\_{\min} \le \delta\_{\min}^{\mathrm{corrob,flip}} := \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ (positive only when $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} > z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$; otherwise no $\delta\_{\min} > 0$ corroborates).

Crossing one boundary shifts a decisive verdict into the inconclusive zone; crossing the full **inversion band** of width $2z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ around $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ flips refutation all the way to corroboration. Choosing $\delta\_{\min}$ is therefore a substantive design commitment.

Self-application fails if design cutoffs move after data arrive—and even cryptographic sealing fails if pre-sealed constants bake in framing bias ($\mathrm{PRI}$, [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)). Following [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.1.2, the programme seals every design parameter upfront: $\delta\_{\min}$, $\sigma\_d$, $\alpha$, $1-\beta$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $F\_2$'s $\alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ over $(T, n)$, the pairing rule, and Brake 2's $(\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}, c\_{\mathrm{urgent}})$. It anchors $\delta\_{\min}$ externally to physical harm and affected-party survival thresholds, leaves `T1` open to attested reopening, and prints inversion band $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \pm z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ beside every verdict.

A falsifier paired with its inversion band tells readers whether a verdict reflects physical evidence or a tuned cutoff. The closing thesis—that a programme specifying what refutes it earns authority to guide decisions—holds in a leaner, honest form: it earns that authority only when pre-committed thresholds are externally anchored and sealed before execution.
