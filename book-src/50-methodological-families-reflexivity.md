## 9.1 The Five Methodological Families of Stochology

Stochology replaces no discipline. It works as an integrative syntax across the three-plane mediation circuit linking world ($P\_1$), archive ($P\_2$), and institution ($P\_3$) ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)):

```
               INSTITUTION (P3: Decision, Court, Clinic)
              /  ^                                   /  ^
   T6 Perform/    \T5 Temporal            T1 Frame  /    \T4 Formalize
            v      \                               v      \
   WORLD (P1: Phenomenon)  <=== T3 Observe / T2 Test ===>  ARCHIVE (P2: Data & Model)
               \                                         /
                +--- Integral Closure: T7 (Liability) --+
```

Rather than carving up disconnected islands, the five methodological families represent **distinct inferential regimes** governed together by all three Canonical Operators—Epistemic Margin ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$), Vital Margin ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$), and Institutional Governance ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$)—with explicit limits on what each family can prove:

## 9.2 Family 5 in Detail: Galenic *Sēmeîa*, Process Tracing, and Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$

Historians, investigative reporters, judges, and bedside clinicians rarely estimate a slope over ten thousand spreadsheets. Instead, they reconstruct a causal chain inside a **single unfolding case** by weighing qualitative signs. That lineage runs from *sēmeîa* in Hippocrates's *Prognostic* I (c. 400 BCE, reading signs across past, present, and future) and Galen's *De locis affectis* I.1 (c. 200 CE) through modern *Process Tracing* (Van Evera 1997; Bennett & Checkel 2015).

This qualitative family stands on wide historical ground. Ibn al-Haytham's *iʿtibār* cross-tested optical and physical claims by varying isolated conditions against rival hypotheses (Sabra 1989). Ibn Khaldūn's *Muqaddimah* built structural historiography by testing chroniclers' narratives against demographic, ecological, and institutional realities (Rosenthal 1958). Galen (c. 200 CE) called diagnosis over hidden internal causes *tekhnikòs stokhasmós*—skilled conjecture between exact knowledge and total ignorance—and warned that universal infallible signs rarely exist for every disease; Stochology formalizes that qualitative fallibility by placing real diagnostic clue probabilities inside the open interior probability interval $(0, 1)$.

### 9.2.1 Epistemic Foundations: The Clinical and Archival Clue

Every qualitative clue $E$ (denoting an observed evidentiary sign here, distinct from sequential *e*-process wealth $E\_t$ and expectation $\mathbb{E}$ below) is scored by two conditional probabilities:

1. **Sensitivity under Focal Hypothesis $H$:** $p\_H = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H) \in (0, 1)$.
2. **False-Positive Rate under Rival Explanation $\neg H$:** $p\_{\neg H} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H) \in (0, 1)$.

The pair is the whole evaluation. A clue is never strong or weak in isolation; it is informative only relative to competing accounts, and supporting $H$ when observed requires positive diagnosticity ($p\_H > p\_{\neg H}$; reversing the inequality shifts support toward $\neg H$). Under equifinality—when several rival mechanisms inside $\neg H$ could also produce clue $E$—$p\_{\neg H}$ must track the strongest plausible alternative rather than an easy strawman.

### 9.2.2 Bennett & Checkel Diagnostic Test Taxonomy

The square $(p\_H, p\_{\neg H})$ partitions into Van Evera's four classic qualitative tests:

- **Straw in the wind:** modest necessity and modest sufficiency. The likelihood ratio stays mild, nudging odds slightly without proving or refuting anything.
- **Hoop test:** **high necessity** with non-trivial discrimination against the strongest plausible rival ($\mathrm{LR}^- \le \epsilon\_{\mathrm{hoop}} \ll 1$, barring tautological hoops where $p\_H \approx p\_{\neg H}$). If $H$ happened, clue $E$ *must* appear.
  - Likelihood ratio if observed: $\mathrm{LR}^+ = \frac{p\_H}{p\_{\neg H}} > 1$ (gentle support).
  - Likelihood ratio if **absent**: $\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \ll 1$ (**severe elimination**).
  - *Epistemic principle:* Clearing a hoop does not prove $H$, whereas **failing a pre-registered hoop triggers §9.2.6's non-compensatory refutation rule** (deductive elimination for definitional impossibilities, or a non-compensatory falsification audit under a bounded false-refutation budget for fallible empirical hoops).
- **Smoking gun:** **high sufficiency** (approaching a pathognomonic signature where one exists, tempered by Galen's warning against assuming infallible signs).
  - Likelihood ratio if observed: $\mathrm{LR}^+ = \frac{p\_H}{p\_{\neg H}} \gg 1$ (**strong confirmation**).
  - Likelihood ratio if absent: $\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \approx 1$ when $p\_H, p\_{\neg H} \ll 1$ (absence does not clear $H$ out).
- **Doubly decisive:** **both necessary and sufficient** ($\mathrm{LR}^+ \gg 1$ and $\mathrm{LR}^- \le \epsilon\_{\mathrm{hoop}} \ll 1$).
  - If observed: confirms $H$ strongly ($\mathrm{LR}^+ \gg 1$) within the interior probability bounds modeled in §9.2.6.
  - If absent: refutes or suspends $H$ ($\mathrm{LR}^- \ll 1$, enforced by §9.2.6).

Notice how the four tests partition inquiry duties rather than clues themselves. A case built purely from smoking guns can confirm but never eliminate; a case built purely from hoops can eliminate but never confirm.

### 9.2.3 Turing–Good Deciban Calculus

Following Alan Turing's wartime Bletchley Park weights and I. J. Good (1950, 1979), evidence strength $W(E; H : \neg H)$ (writing $W$ for Turing–Good evidential weight in decibans, distinct from Brake 2's welfare audit $W\_{\mathrm{welfare}}$ in §9.3.2 and pair weights $w\_i$ in §9.5.3) adds logarithmically in **decibans** ($\mathrm{db}$, Good's unit distinct from acoustic decibels $\mathrm{dB}$) from likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H)/\mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)$:
$$W(E; H : \neg H) = 10 \log\_{10} \frac{\mathbb{P}(E \mid H)}{\mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)} = 10 \log\_{10} \mathrm{LR}$$

Given $m$ clues $E\_1, \dots, E\_m$ that are conditionally independent under both $H$ and $\neg H$, their individual likelihood ratios $\mathrm{LR}\_j$ multiply and total weight $W\_{\mathrm{total}}$ adds:
$$W\_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum\_{j=1}^m W(E\_j; H : \neg H) = 10 \log\_{10} \prod\_{j=1}^m \mathrm{LR}\_j$$

Starting from prior odds $\mathrm{Odds}\_0$, Bayes' Rule turns decibans into posterior odds $\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}$ and posterior probability $\mathbb{P}(H \mid E\_{1..m})$:
$$\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}} = \mathrm{Odds}\_0 \times 10^{W\_{\mathrm{total}} / 10}, \qquad \mathbb{P}(H \mid E\_{1..m}) = \frac{\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}{1 + \mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}}}$$

Adding $+10\mkern2mu\mathrm{db}$ multiplies the odds of $H$ tenfold, while $-10\mkern2mu\mathrm{db}$ shrinks them tenfold.

### 9.2.4 John Maynard Keynes's Evidential Weight $V$

In *A Treatise on Probability* (1921), John Maynard Keynes separated probability balance $P$ from **evidential weight** $V$—how much relevant evidence supports the verdict relative to remaining ignorance—without proposing one cardinal formula. Stochology tracks that qualitative depth (distinct from Lebesgue cube volume $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}$ in §9.4) alongside odds using acquired knowledge $K \ge 0$ and initial ignorance $I\_0 > 0$:
$$V = \frac{K}{K + I\_0}$$

Starting from initial knowledge $K\_0$, non-redundancy is enforced by **provenance-graph deduplication**: nested logical implications, repeated echoes of one primary record, or mechanically derived consequences add $\Delta K\_j = 0$, whereas each genuinely independent primary clue $j \in \lbrace 1, \dots, m \rbrace$ contributes knowledge increment $\Delta K\_j > 0$ to posterior weight $V\_{\mathrm{post}}$:
$$V\_{\mathrm{post}} = \frac{K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^m \Delta K\_j}{K\_0 + \sum\_{j=1}^m \Delta K\_j + I\_0}$$

That formula holds $I\_0$ fixed only under a closed-frame idealization; whenever attested `T1` reopening (§9.3.3, §9.7.3) reveals brand-new ignorance or *Not askable* mechanisms ($\Delta I > 0$), $I\_0$ updates upward ($I\_0 \mapsto I\_0 + \Delta I$), pulling $V\_{\mathrm{post}}$ downward until inquiry covers the expanded frame. Probability reports which way evidence leans; weight $V$ reports how deeply the record was checked, separating fragile high-odds hunches from well-tested conclusions.

### 9.2.5 The Same Inference in Symbols

Across three conditionally independent clues—doubly decisive, smoking gun, and hoop ($j = 1, 2, 3$)—each adds deciban score $W\_j = 10\log\_{10}\mathrm{LR}\_j$ and deduplicated depth increment $\Delta K\_j \ge 0$ to prior odds $\mathrm{Odds}\_0 = p\_0 / (1 - p\_0)$ ($p\_0 \in (0, 1)$) and baseline weight $V\_0 = K\_0 / (K\_0 + I\_0)$ ($K\_0 \ge 0, I\_0 > 0$). Multiplying likelihood ratios sums $W\_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum\_j W\_j$ into $\mathrm{Odds}\_{\mathrm{post}} = \mathrm{Odds}\_0 \prod\_j \mathrm{LR}\_j$, while $K\_{\mathrm{post}} = K\_0 + \sum\_j \Delta K\_j$ yields $V\_{\mathrm{post}} = K\_{\mathrm{post}}/(K\_{\mathrm{post}} + I\_0)$: clues commute algebraically under independence, though chronological order still tests causal precedence.

### 9.2.6 Refutability and the Asymmetry of the Hoop Test

Qualitative case reconstruction is epistemically valid only under three rules:

- empirical priors obey Lindley's (1985) **Cromwell's rule** ($p\_0 \in (0, 1)$, reserving $0$ and $1$ solely for logical or definitional entailments), while fallible empirical clues sit in $p\_H, p\_{\neg H} \in (0, 1)$ with positive diagnosticity ($p\_H > p\_{\neg H}$) and pre-registered hoop asymmetry $\mathrm{LR}^- \le \epsilon\_{\mathrm{hoop}} \ll 1$;
- each provenance-deduplicated clue updates both Bayesian odds and evidential weight $V$ (with $I\_0 \mapsto I\_0 + \Delta I$ upon attested reopening);
- **Non-compensatory hoop refutability (Popper 1959; Mayo 2018):** the protocol pre-registers at least one hoop test or doubly decisive clue whose failure cannot be offset by positive decibans elsewhere.

Because modeling fallible empirical signs inside $(0, 1)$ forbids $p\_H = 1$ or $p\_{\neg H} = 0$, together with positive diagnosticity ($p\_H > p\_{\neg H}$), a missing empirical hoop clue leaves a finite likelihood factor $\mathrm{LR}^- \in (0, 1)$ ($W^- > -\infty$):
$$\mathrm{LR}^- = \frac{1 - p\_H}{1 - p\_{\neg H}} \in (0, 1) \qquad (W^- > -\infty),$$

so flattering clues could numerically swamp a missing necessity test under naive Bayes alone. Yet treating a single noisy miss ($p\_H < 1$) as an automatic irreversible elimination would incur uncalibrated Type I false-refutation risk: under conditional independence of the $m$ empirical hoops given $H$, that error is $1 - \prod\_{j=1}^m p\_{H,j}$, and under arbitrary dependence it is bounded above by the Bonferroni sum $\sum\_{j=1}^m (1 - p\_{H,j})$, contradicting Popper's (1959, ch. VIII) and Mayo's (2018) severe-error discipline if left unconstrained. Stochology therefore separates **definitional impossibility hoops** ($p\_H = 1$ logically under the model's hard core, whose failure refutes $H$ deductively outside Cromwell's prior rule) from **fallible empirical hoops** ($p\_H \in (0, 1)$): failing a pre-registered fallible hoop updates odds via $\mathrm{LR}^-$ and fires a **non-compensatory falsification audit and suspension**—blocking positive decibans elsewhere from clearing $H$—subject to a pre-registered family-wise false-refutation budget $1 - \prod\_{j=1}^m p\_{H,j} \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{hoop}}$ under conditional independence given $H$ (or Bonferroni $\sum\_{j=1}^m (1 - p\_{H,j}) \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{hoop}}$ when hoops may be dependent).

## 9.3 Reflexive Self-Application: The Four Internal Goodhart Brakes

Any institution that converts a diagnostic indicator into an executive target destroys the indicator (Goodhart, 1975; Strathern, 1997; Espeland & Stevens, 1998). Reflexivity is incomplete until the analyst's own tools enter the frame: the discipline supplying the question, the career served by the verdict, and the measurement instruments themselves (Bourdieu 2004). Stochology protects its own safeguards from bureaucratic decay through four internal brakes.

### 9.3.1 Brake 1 (Epistemic Margin): Skeptical Inflation vs. CORP Resolution

- *Perversion:* Analysts could score $100\%$ coverage effortlessly by stretching bounds all the way to $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$, or predicting the flat sample mean everywhere. Coverage rises mechanically with width, while pooling heterogeneous strata or fitting isotonic recalibration in-sample inflates apparent resolution without real within-stratum skill; conversely, imposing hard upper caps on interval width creates a pincer that pressures analysts to understate $\bar{\rho}$ or omit *Not askable* strata ($\mathrm{PRI}$ gaming).
- *Mathematical Brake:* Recall Murphy (1973) and Dimitriadis, Gneiting, and Jordan's (2021) CORP Brier decomposition into $\bar{y}(1 - \bar{y}) + \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}$ ([Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md); [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md) §7.1.1). Predicting the constant base rate ($p\_t \equiv \bar{y}$) earns zero calibration error ($\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = 0$) yet **zero resolution** ($\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = 0$), because it cannot distinguish storms from calm days. Epistemic audits therefore require **out-of-fold, within-stratum debiased resolution** ($\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}^{\mathrm{debiased}} > 0$ across contiguous out-of-fold blocks, rejecting forecasts whose cross-validated Brier score fails to beat within-stratum climatology $\mathrm{UNC}\_0$). Rather than hard interval-width ceilings that penalize honest MNAR uncertainty, audits test observed width $U\_t - L\_t$ against `PRI`-verified support bounds (`T1`–`T3`) for unjustified padding and bind Regime II minimax-regret probes $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}}$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)) strictly to sealed, externally audited $[L\_t, U\_t]$ endpoints.

### 9.3.2 Brake 2 (Vital Margin): Regressive Hoarding vs. Covariant Welfare Ceiling

- *Perversion:* Managers could hoard idle reserves ($R \to \infty$) under the banner of extreme prudence, starving present community needs; conversely, a captured evaluator or cost-cutter could manipulate measurement units or urgency weights to weaponize an upper hoarding ceiling as an austerity cap below survival requirements.
- *Mathematical Brake:* Conceptually motivated by Scarf's (1960) upper $(S, s)$ reserve ceilings and Samuelson's (1971) caution against unbounded finite-horizon over-retention drag (neither paper posits this functional), Stochology defines a stylized **ex-post welfare audit** $W\_{\mathrm{welfare}}(R)$ (with physical reserve stock $R$, distinct from the recording indicator in $\rho\_{R,Y}$, and $W\_{\mathrm{welfare}}$ distinct from §9.2's deciban weight $W$) using pre-anchored physical reference scale $R\_{\mathrm{ref}} > 0$, dimensionless precautionary weight $\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}} > 0$, and dimensionless linear urgency cost $c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} > 0$ per reference unit:
  $$W\_{\mathrm{welfare}}(R) = \kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}} \ln\\!\left(1 + \frac{R}{R\_{\mathrm{ref}}}\right) - c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} \frac{R}{R\_{\mathrm{ref}}}$$
  Normalizing $R$ by $R\_{\mathrm{ref}}$ preserves Stevens ratio-scale covariance under unit rescaling ($R \mapsto sR$, $R\_{\mathrm{ref}} \mapsto sR\_{\mathrm{ref}}$). To stop Brake 2 from stripping survival slack, the audit binds $R$ above the physical **survival-and-impairment floor** $R\_{\mathrm{floor}} := \max\bigl(B\_{\mathrm{req}}, B\_{\mathrm{tail}}\bigr) \ge 0$, where $B\_{\mathrm{req}} = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)\mkern2mu\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t}/(1 - c\_{\mathrm{imp}})$ plus any heavy-tail $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ reserve ($\alpha \le 2$) and Kingman utilization headroom ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.3.2, §6.8). Because $W\_{\mathrm{welfare}}$ is strictly concave in $R$, Karush–Kuhn–Tucker optimality over $R \ge R\_{\mathrm{floor}}$ yields the unique covariant ex-post hoarding ceiling:
  $$R^\ast = \max\\!\left(R\_{\mathrm{floor}},\mkern3mu R\_{\mathrm{ref}}\left(\frac{\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}}{c\_{\mathrm{urgent}}} - 1\right)\right)$$
  In plain words, whenever $\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}} \le c\_{\mathrm{urgent}}$ or the unconstrained optimum sits below $R\_{\mathrm{floor}}$, the ceiling locks cleanly at $R\_{\mathrm{floor}}$, never flagging survival-necessary buffers; past $R^\ast$, every extra reference unit hoarded buys less logarithmic security than the linear opportunity harm it inflicts. Strictly, $W\_{\mathrm{welfare}}(R)$ and $R^\ast$ act solely as an **ex-post audit of regressive hoarding above $R\_{\mathrm{floor}}$** ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md))—never as a live rationing cap—with $(\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}, c\_{\mathrm{urgent}}, R\_{\mathrm{ref}}, R\_{\mathrm{floor}})$ pre-anchored outside the unit (§9.7.3).

### 9.3.3 Brake 3 (Institutional Governance): Procedural Theater vs. Bounded *Kairós* Fast-Path

- *Perversion:* Compliance boards can drag reviews out ($\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} > 0$) past a shorter survival window, or executives can maintain lean fragility to trigger chronic emergency exceptions and sunset chattering while silencing alerts under a `10 bits/s` flood.
- *Mathematical Brake:* Define total fast-path recovery time $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} := \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{fast}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}^{\mathrm{fast}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}^{\mathrm{fast}} > 0$ (bypassing committee deliberation $\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} > \tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}$ so $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}^{\mathrm{fast}} = 0$), alongside survival horizon $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} > 0$. Whenever $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$, waiting for full committee review fails physically. Inside the fenced fast-path window
  $$\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}},$$
  Step 1 evaluates survival against $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{fast}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ rather than committee latency, whereas any drop below $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}$ ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{fast}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$) forecloses even defensive release and fires **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** unconditionally ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)). Timing urgency never creates sovereign impunity; extending Sosa's (2015) individual virtue-epistemology standard of meta-aptness by institutional analogy, Stochology binds that fenced fast-path window with four anti-capture locks ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)):
  1. An independent clock monitor—not the asset-holding executive—deterministically verifies $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$, pairing [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) (§8.2.2) **two-track algedonic intake**: pre-authorized physical hazard sentinels trip immediate zero-deliberation holds on validated sensors, while discretionary frontline warnings carry zero status discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) inside pre-registered **per-stream mixture sequential *e*-processes** ($E\_t^{(j)} = \sum\_{k=1}^t w\_k \prod\_{s=k}^t e\_s^{(j)} \ge E\_{\mathrm{trip}}$ with pre-committed weights $w\_k > 0$, $\sum\_{k \ge 1} w\_k \le 1$, and supermartingale null bound $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[e\_s^{(j)} \mid \mathcal{F}\_{s-1}] \le 1$, so Ville's inequality $\mathbb{P}\_{H\_0}(\exists t : E\_t^{(j)} \ge E\_{\mathrm{trip}}) \le 1/E\_{\mathrm{trip}}$ holds exactly without imposing a supermartingale-breaking wealth floor while each new shift enters with undiluted weight $w\_k$, or $\alpha$-spent discrete epoch resets $\sum\_{k \ge 1} \alpha\_k \le \alpha$ at $E\_{\mathrm{trip},k} = 1/\alpha\_k$, rate-capped low-weight noise without altering witness standing, pre-registered objective grading for $e\_s^{(j)}$, and external ombudsman routes shielding whistleblowers against off-ledger scope starvation or contract non-renewal).
  2. Only pre-authorized defensive buffer releases and one-way halt latches ([Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md)) may fire, keeping oversight queues inside human deliberative capacity near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025) and free of polycentric congestion (Black 2008; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.4.1).
  3. The fast-path expires automatically on a non-self-extending sunset changeable only by collegiate ratification (Elster 1979); if the sunset expires while $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$ persists, the system escalates automatically to **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** rather than re-triggering consecutive fast-path episodes.
  4. Sealed bitemporal logs and partial-identification intervals $[L\_t, U\_t]$ govern mandatory ex-post **consolidated parent-level escrowed clawbacks** (preventing subsidiary shell-dumping) and prospective `T1` frame reopening without rewriting history.

### 9.3.4 Brake 4 (Composite Metric): Anti-Checklist Rule & Nested Leontief Box Geometry

- *Perversion:* Turning $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr)$ into an executive KPI—or tying bonuses to per-step `Interior` clearance counts and concave chart recodings $\varphi\_a$—invites box-ticking, denominator gaming, and spillover dumping (Espeland & Stevens, 1998; Hacking, 2002).
- *Institutional & Geometric Brake:*

On trajectories that hit the absorbing ruin barrier ($R\_{t+1} \le 0$), $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ folds solely over pre-absorption steps without fabricating post-absorption numbers (such as zero), while arrival at the absorbing ruin barrier (and its ruin step) is tracked separately by the structural ruin indicator alongside $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$. Likewise, incentives may never reward raw `Interior` step-predicate pass counts near $\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}}$ ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1); audits inspect raw physical slack distributions alongside binary clearance flags and freeze chart normalizations $\varphi\_a$ and native thresholds $\tau\_{a,\mathrm{native}}$ upstream at `T1`.

## 9.4 Leontief Non-Substitutable Aggregation vs. Multiplicative and Arithmetic Collapse

### 9.4.1 Proof of Chart-Conditional 3D Lebesgue Feasible Volume Contraction

Take a declared strictly increasing axis normalization $(e, v, g) = \bigl(\varphi\_{\mathrm{epi}}(s\_{\mathrm{epi}}), \varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}(s\_{\mathrm{vital}}), \varphi\_{\mathrm{gov}}(s\_{\mathrm{gov}})\bigr) \in [0, 1]^3$ and diagnostic threshold $\tau \in (0, 1)$. [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4) compares the Lebesgue volume of the Leontief box $[\tau, 1]^3$, $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3$ (written $V\_{\min}$ there, distinct from Keynes's weight $V$), against the multiplicative product region $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)$, $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau) = 1 - \tau + \tau\ln\tau - \frac{\tau}{2}(\ln\tau)^2$: inside one fixed chart, as $\tau$ rises, $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)$ shrinks much faster than $[\tau, 1]^3$.

Crucially, ordinal box membership differs from cardinal Lebesgue volume (Stevens 1946; Krantz, Luce, Suppes, & Tversky 1971; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Any top-anchored strictly increasing $C^1$ per-axis recoding $\psi\_a : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($\psi\_a(1) = 1$, $\psi\_a' > 0$) preserves coordinate-wise box membership and maps $[\tau, 1]^3$ cleanly onto $\prod\_a [\psi\_a(\tau), 1]$ with pushed-forward Lebesgue volume $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}(\tau; \psi) = \prod\_a \bigl(1 - \psi\_a(\tau)\bigr) = \int\_{[\tau, 1]^3} \lvert\det J\_\psi\rvert\mkern3mu dS$, while distorting the curved boundary $\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ once nonlinear and changing cardinal volume under $dS$. Meanwhile, pushforward Lebesgue volume transforms by constant Jacobian $\lvert\det J\_\psi\rvert = \prod\_a \alpha\_a$ across **any constant-Jacobian affine map** $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + \beta\_a$ ($\alpha\_a > 0$), holding the pushforward ratio $\mathrm{Vol}(\psi([\tau, 1]^3)) / \mathrm{Vol}(\psi(\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)))$ equal to $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}(\tau)/\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)$ (whereas re-evaluating $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}/\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ directly at shifted coordinates $\psi(\tau)$ changes the ratio and $\psi(\Omega\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau))$ ceases to be a product level set once $\alpha\_a < 1$). Specializing to the **restricted affine monoid** $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ ($0 < \alpha\_a \le 1$; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4) preserves top-anchoring $\psi\_a(1) = 1$ inside $[0, 1]^3$ and threshold-ratio scaling $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}(\tau\_1;\psi)/\mathrm{Vol}\_{\min}(\tau\_2;\psi)$, while preserving weakest-axis identity ($\arg\min\_a S\_a$) and single-threshold $\mathrm{OLC}$ (Sen 1985) requires **common** scaling ($\alpha\_a \equiv \alpha$) across axes because distinct $\alpha\_a \ne \alpha\_b$ reverse rankings. Grounded in social-choice theory (Arrow 1950; Sen 1985), non-utility threshold boxes declare threshold calibrations $\varphi\_a(\tau\_{a,\mathrm{native}}) = \tau$ transparently upstream at `T1` while forbidding compensatory exchange rates once calibrated, and confine $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ strictly to ex-post audits, avoiding Harsanyi's (1975) maximin-paralysis critique.

### 9.4.2 False Retrospective Collapse Flags on Healthy Grids vs. False-Safe Clearances on Ruin Boundaries

Two ex-post grid diagnostics make the retrospective failure of alternative aggregators vivid (remembering that live action authorization rests exclusively on the per-step operator's Regimes I–III and [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1, never on retrospective $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$):

1. **A healthy factorial grid**, where every single margin sits comfortably above retrospective cutoff $\tau$:
   - **Pure Leontief ($\min$):** zero false retrospective collapse flags, because the minimum of numbers above $\tau$ stays above $\tau$.
   - **Hybrid governance** (combining a minimum with a product): issues false retrospective collapse flags across a large fraction of healthy states whenever multiplying two moderate factors dips below $\tau$.
   - **Full product:** issues false retrospective collapse flags over almost the entire healthy grid, clearing only the extreme corner where all three scores hug $1$.
2. **A single-axis ruin grid**, where one margin collapses to zero (or an absorbing barrier is crossed, where $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ folds over pre-absorption steps while the structural ruin indicator records absorption separately) while the other two look healthy:
   - **Compensatory arithmetic mean:** averages one zero with two high scores and clears the cutoff—issuing a **false-safe retrospective clearance in every single case**, concealing a lethal structural breach.
   - **Pure Leontief ($\min$):** zero false-safe retrospective clearances. Wherever a missing safeguard hits an absorbing cliff, the Sprengel–Liebig–Leontief minimum (Sprengel 1828; Liebig 1855; Leontief 1941) dominates averages and multiplicative blends.

Notice the strict role of analogy here. Gilboa and Schmeidler's (1986) Maxmin Expected Utility (MEU)—minimizing expected utility across priors—serves purely as a structural analogy across axes. Whether smooth ambiguity preferences (Klibanoff, Marinacci, & Mukerji 2005) allow doubled physical slack to partially buffer a weak axis during mild, barrier-free shocks stays open for pre-registered falsifier $F\_4$ (§9.5.3, §9.7.2).

## 9.5 Severe Testing and the Five Pre-Registered Falsifiers

### 9.5.1 Deborah Mayo's Severity Principle and Burden-of-Proof Inversion

A familiar methodological trick sets the null hypothesis to "zero difference" ($H\_0: \Delta = 0$) and boasts that a rule is "validated" whenever a noisy test fails to reject $H\_0$. Stochology reverses that burden of proof (Mayo 2018) by testing observed harm reduction $\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}}$ against substantive harm threshold $\delta\_{\min}$ under null $H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}$ and falsifying alternative $H\_1^{\mathrm{fals}}$:
$$H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} \ge \delta\_{\min} \quad \text{vs.} \quad H\_1^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} < \delta\_{\min}$$

Refuting a safeguard thus cannot rest on noisy silence: data must demonstrate with post-data severity that omitting it loses less than $\delta\_{\min}$.

### 9.5.2 Pre-Registered Design Formula

Model paired harm reductions under Spanos's (2021) adequacy checks as $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \sim \mathcal{N}(\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}}, \sigma\_d^2 / n\_{\mathrm{eff}})$ (requiring bounded outcomes such as survival indicators, capped losses, or bounded shortfall fractions in $[0, 1]$ so $\sigma\_d < \infty$ holds under heavy tails $\alpha \le 2$ in $F\_2$, or non-parametric anytime-valid rank tests; and replacing normal quantiles $z\_{1-\alpha}$ and $\Phi$ with Student-$t$ quantiles $t\_{\nu, 1-\alpha}$ and CDF $F\_\nu$ at cluster-level degrees of freedom $\nu \le G - 1$, or Satterthwaite df under unequal cluster weights, whenever $\sigma\_d$ is estimated; Mayo 2018), where $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is defined in §9.5.3 and power $1 - \beta$ sits at $\Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} = 0$.

Fix one-sided $\alpha \in (0, 0.5)$ ($z\_{1-\alpha} = \Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha) > 0$) and power $1 - \beta$ with $\beta \in (0, 1 - \alpha)$ ($z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta} > 0$). Given $\delta\_{\min} > 0$ and $\sigma\_d > 0$, four **pre-data planned** quantities seal beforehand:
$$n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}} = \left\lceil \left( \frac{(z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta})\sigma\_d}{\delta\_{\min}} \right)^2 \right\rceil, \qquad \mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}} = \frac{\sigma\_d}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}}},$$
$$d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}.$$

Given observed mean $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ at realized effective count $n\_{\mathrm{eff}} > 0$ and pre-declared systematic selection bound $\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \ge 0$ ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}} = 0$ under randomized or unselected arms; §9.5.3), realized cutoffs follow symmetrically:
$$\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \frac{\sigma\_d}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} - z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}, \qquad d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}} = \delta\_{\min} + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}.$$

Since $\Phi$ increases strictly with $\Phi(z\_{1-\alpha}) = 1 - \alpha$, worst-case selection-adjusted post-data severity evaluates refutation at the upper selection endpoint $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})$ and corroboration at the lower endpoint $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})$:
$$\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}^{\mathrm{worst}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) = \Phi\\!\left(\frac{\delta\_{\min} - \bigl(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})\bigr)}{\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}\right) \ge 1 - \alpha \iff \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}},$$
$$\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{worst}}(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}) = \Phi\\!\left(\frac{\bigl(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})\bigr) - \delta\_{\min}}{\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}}\right) \ge 1 - \alpha \iff \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} - \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \ge d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$$
(substituting $F\_\nu$ and $t\_{\nu, 1-\alpha}$ when $\sigma\_d$ is estimated).

Observations must clear $z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})$ below $\delta\_{\min}$ to refute or above $\delta\_{\min}$ to corroborate, leaving the gap honestly inconclusive and matching planned cutoffs when $\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) = 0$ and $n\_{\mathrm{eff}} = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$.

Before trusting either severity score, the statistical model must pass **statistical adequacy** via rigorous misspecification checks (Spanos 2021). As Spanos proves, violated distributional, independence, or homogeneity assumptions distort nominal Type I error probabilities, turning both cutoffs and severity values into model artifacts rather than genuine evidence against $H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}$.

### 9.5.3 The Five Canonical Falsifiers

Five falsifiers are pre-registered before evaluation, each returning a paired harm-reduction contrast $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ and selection bound $\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \ge 0$ judged under §9.5.2 and §9.5.4:

1. **$F\_1$ ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$, observational transport):** tests whether the epistemic margin cuts coverage shortfall when transporting across real missingness and clustering against externally audited out-of-sample ground-truth targets. It fails if, on that externally benchmarked transport design, selection-adjusted paired shortfall reduction over uncorrected arms satisfies $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.
2. **$F\_2$ ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$, heavy tails and queues):** tests whether the vital margin prevents barrier absorption over bounded survival shortfalls ($\sigma\_d < \infty$) under heavy-tailed demand ($\alpha \le 2$) at queue utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}}$ and return period $T > 1$, once the unilateral shock-exposure gate passes. It fails if selection-adjusted paired survival-shortfall reduction over unbuffered arms at shared endowment obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ (even if unbuffered units survive once tail-rate adequacy confirms genuine exposure).
3. **$F\_3$ ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$, multitask proxy gaming):** tests whether governance curbs proxy cannibalization of the substantive goal. It fails if, with governance active over matched common-endowment control arms, proxy scores rise while selection-adjusted paired substantive-goal gain obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.
4. **$F\_4$ ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$, non-compensability):** tests non-substitutability—our central structural claim—by contrasting a strict-minimum arm against an arm substituting doubled physical slack at matched total endowment or carrying-cost-adjusted net payoff under mild shocks. It fails if the strict minimum's selection-adjusted paired harm-reduction advantage obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.
5. **$F\_5$ ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$, cross-perimeter non-externalization):** tests whether the reception-perimeter fold catches cross-perimeter harm export invisible to local three-axis checks across externally monitored adjacent reception perimeters. It fails if, across coupled perimeters with declared spillover channel and tolerance $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}}$, selection-adjusted paired deficit-detection gain over perimeter-exporting arms obeys $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$.

Why does the second falsifier require an explicit **unilateral** shock-exposure gate? Over $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}} \ge 1$ cluster-adjusted effective independent shock-exposure steps (distinct from pair count $n\_{\mathrm{pairs}}$), a quiet run misses a $T$-period tail shock with discrete geometric probability $(1 - 1/T)^{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}}} < \exp(-n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}}/T)$ ([Chapter 03](03-the-biggest-number-in-the-room.md); using the continuous Poisson bound $\exp(-n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}}/T)$ under continuous-time arrivals). Crucially, conditioning corroboration on observing at least one shock inside a short run that fails the deterministic horizon gate inflates conditional Type I false-corroboration error strictly above $\alpha$ and violates Spanos adequacy (since safeguard gains concentrate in shock periods while calm steps incur carrying costs). Therefore, **corroboration** requires the pre-data horizon gate $(1 - 1/T)^{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}}} \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ (or $\exp(-n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}}/T) \le \alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$) unconditionally, whereas **refutation** admits either a realized verified $T$-period shock or dual-bound tail-rate adequacy confirming that exposure was genuinely probable over $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}}$ under externally verified $T$ (blocking adversaries from faking small $T$ or running short pilots to manufacture false zero-shock refutations).

All planned targets—$\delta\_{\min}$, $\sigma\_d$, $\alpha$, $1 - \beta$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $d\_{\mathrm{corrob}}^{\mathrm{plan}}$, $F\_2$'s $\alpha\_{\mathrm{win}}$ over $(T, n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{shock}})$, and the pairing rule—seal cryptographically beforehand, published beside realized cutoffs and §9.7.3's selection-widened inversion band.

For single-axis ablations ($F\_1$–$F\_3$, $F\_5$, and §9.6's factorial cube), matched pairs with and without that safeguard share common buffer and leverage; $F\_4$ uses its matched-endowment or carrying-cost-adjusted substitution contrast, and arm assignment seals beforehand so pairing cannot shift ex post.

Proper pairing also prevents double-counting cluster variance or confusing selection bias with variance. Write paired arm outcomes as $\mu\_A + u\_c + \varepsilon\_A$ and $\mu\_B + u\_c + \varepsilon\_B$ under homoscedastic errors $\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon\_A) = \mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon\_B) = \sigma\_\varepsilon^2$, zero $(u\_c, \varepsilon\_A, \varepsilon\_B)$ cross-covariances, and zero cluster-by-arm interaction so cluster shock $u\_c$ cancels inside a cluster. Define total variance $\sigma^2 = \sigma\_u^2 + \sigma\_\varepsilon^2$ and intra-cluster correlation $\rho\_I = \sigma\_u^2/\sigma^2$. Then the unit-level clustered design effect is $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I$ for Moulton's effective cluster size $\tilde{m} = \bar{m}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr)$ ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.3; Kish 1965; Moulton 1986). Within one cluster, difference $d\_c = (\mu\_A - \mu\_B) + (\varepsilon\_A - \varepsilon\_B)$ cancels $u\_c$, giving $\mathrm{Var}(d\_c) = 2\sigma\_\varepsilon^2 = 2\sigma^2(1 - \rho\_I)$, so $\rho\_I$ enters $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{req}}$ once inside $\sigma\_d^2$ ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = 1$).

Across distinct clusters ($c\_1 \ne c\_2$), $u\_{c\_1}$ and $u\_{c\_2}$ do not cancel, giving $\mathrm{Var}(d) = 2\sigma^2$. When each cluster supplies at most one pair, pairs are uncorrelated ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = 1$) and Kish pair-level weights $w\_i$ yield $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d}\_w) = \sigma\_d^2 \sum w\_i^2/(\sum w\_i)^2 = \sigma\_d^2 / (n\_{\mathrm{pairs}}/\mathrm{DEFF}\_w)$ with $\mathrm{DEFF}\_w = 1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(w)$. When hub clusters supply multiple pairs ($m\_{\mathrm{pair}, c}$) matched against separate external clusters, Moulton's effective shared-leg count $\tilde{m}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = \bar{m}\_{\mathrm{pair}}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m\_{\mathrm{pair}})\bigr)$ induces pair correlation $\rho\_I/2$, $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} = 1 + (\tilde{m}\_{\mathrm{pair}} - 1)\rho\_I/2$ (reaching $\rho\_I$ only if every pair shares both $(c\_1, c\_2)$), and exact variance follows the block-correlated quadratic form $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d}\_w) = \sigma\_d^2 \frac{\mathbf{w}^\top \mathbf{\Gamma}\_{\mathrm{pair}} \mathbf{w}}{(\mathbf{1}^\top \mathbf{w})^2}$ with pair correlation matrix $\mathbf{\Gamma}\_{\mathrm{pair}}$. For pair weights uncorrelated with cluster membership, hub sizes $m\_{\mathrm{pair}, c}$, and cluster shocks in the large-cluster limit, **the exact composition is additive** ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}, \mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_w - 1$, giving $n\_{\mathrm{eff}} = n\_{\mathrm{pairs}} / (\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_w - 1)$ inside $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.3; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1), exceeding $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_w - 1$ by a non-negative cross-term when multiplied as $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}}\cdot\mathrm{DEFF}\_w$. Under hub-constant weights ($w\_i \equiv w\_{c\_1}$), that product equals exact pair variance only when hub pair counts are equal ($m\_{\mathrm{pair},c} \equiv \bar{m}\_{\mathrm{pair}}$) or when $w\_c$ is uncorrelated with $m\_{\mathrm{pair},c}$ and shocks; under unequal hub counts and positively correlated hub weights ($w\_c$ rising with $m\_{\mathrm{pair},c}$), exact hub-constant variance uses weight-adjusted $\tilde{m}\_{\mathrm{pair},w} = \sum\_c m\_{\mathrm{pair},c}^2 w\_c^2 / \sum\_c m\_{\mathrm{pair},c} w\_c^2$ inside $\mathrm{DEFF}\_w[1 + (\tilde{m}\_{\mathrm{pair},w} - 1)\rho\_I/2]$, whereas under arbitrary unequal hubs and weights $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{pair}}\cdot\mathrm{DEFF}\_w$ can understate true pair variance—bounded universally only by $\mathrm{DEFF}\_w[1 + (m\_{\mathrm{pair},\max} - 1)\rho\_I/2]$ ($m\_{\mathrm{pair},\max} := \max\_c m\_{\mathrm{pair},c}$) or by the exact quadratic form above. Meanwhile, non-random selection ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$, governed by Meng's factor $((1-f)/f)\bar{\rho}^2$ in [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.2.2) enters as the systematic location interval $\pm \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho})$ propagated in §9.5.2 and §9.7.3, never inside $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

## 9.7 Weight, Severity, and the Parameters of the Test

### 9.7.1 Two limits of the deciban ledger

The deciban sum of §9.2.3 assumes conditional independence across clues—an assumption with two sharp failure boundaries.

First, consider two nested operational clues: sustained wind at the current fix clears one speed bar, or clears a stricter bar, each predicting hurricane strength next fix. Because the stricter event implies the wider one, adding both deciban scores double-counts the wider condition inside the narrower one. More observations never fix this error: it is redundant counting of a nested pair.

Second, a closed-form limit exposes simple repetition. Repeating the same clue worth $\mathrm{LR} = 1 + \eta$ ($\eta > 0$) $m$ times adds $10m\log\_{10}(1 + \eta)$ decibans and pushes posterior probability toward $1$ under false independence. Yet evidential weight $V$ experiences zero increment ($\Delta V = 0$ because $\Delta K\_j = 0$ under provenance-graph deduplication—even when baseline weight $V\_0$ already sits near $1$), flagging duplicate counting immediately; meanwhile, under attested `T1` reopening ($\Delta I > 0$), $I\_0 \mapsto I\_0 + \Delta I$ pulls $V$ downward until new inquiries cover the expanded frame. That is why Stochology tracks $(P, V)$ jointly alongside §9.2.6's hoop refutation.

### 9.7.2 The narrowing a conjunctive theory can always buy

A refuted conjunct need not sink the full programme if the rescue frontier is pre-registered rather than improvised. Under Lakatos (1970), narrowing $F\_4$ after decisive refutation ($\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$, $\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}^{\mathrm{worst}} \ge 1 - \alpha$) is progressive **only** if it generates genuinely novel, risky cross-regime excess content beyond Collapse Proposition 2's already-derived $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(B\_t) < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ boundary (such as testable regime-transition thresholds under $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}} = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ and queue congestion $\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}, t}$; [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.3.2) **and** secures empirical corroboration of part of that novel excess content—while keeping the original failed $F\_4$ refutation openly on record.

### 9.7.3 The design parameters are not sealed

The four brakes make the framework self-auditable only if outsiders can verify every threshold. One choice strains that assumption: $\delta\_{\min}$ is selected by the framework designer.

The exact sensitivity spans three lines of algebra. At realized effective pairs $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, $\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}} = \sigma\_d/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}}$ and selection bound $\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \ge 0$ ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}} = 0$ when unbiased). - **Refutation flips** ($\mathrm{SEV}\_{\mathrm{fals}}^{\mathrm{worst}} \ge 1 - \alpha$) whenever $\delta\_{\min} \ge \delta\_{\min}^{\mathrm{fals,flip}} := \bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) + z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ (which is positive when $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) > -z\_{1-\alpha}\mkern3mu\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}$; otherwise every $\delta\_{\min} > 0$ refutes).
