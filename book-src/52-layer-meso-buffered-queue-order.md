## 1. T4Formalization: what counts as the state

Picture an urgent-care clinic running near full capacity on a winter morning. At nine out of ten rooms busy, patients wait only a few minutes; when a tenth patient arrives per hour, the lobby suddenly backs up for hours.

Here, `T4Formalization` protects how arrivals, waiting rules, and service steps are turned into numbers before anyone trusts a reported wait average. A queue builds its buffer into its definition: a server, an arrival stream, and a queue discipline. Upstream [Layer 01, *Micro-Kinetic & Sensorimotor Order*](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) fixes via `T1Framing` what counts as an arrival unit (sessions versus individual requests) and via `T5Temporal` its minimum actuation latency. Then `T4Formalization` maps that declared stream across $P\_2 \to P\_3$ into state variables and delay bounds.

Three modeling choices fix everything that follows. Count arrivals as discrete people and occupancy is an integer; smooth them into a fluid and it becomes a continuous level; pick first-come-first-served (FCFS) instead of priority triage and wait follows arrival clock rather than clinical class. The equations hold only inside the declaration you made.

Traffic intensity makes that definition bite. Write $\lambda > 0$ for the arrival rate and $\mu\_{s} = 1/T\_{s} > 0$ for the service rate at mean service time $T\_{s} > 0$ (written $\tau\_{s}$ in [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2, distinct from §2's timeout $\tau\_{\mathrm{to}}$). Their product defines dimensionless traffic intensity $\rho$:

$$\rho = \lambda T\_{s} = \frac{\lambda}{\mu\_{s}},$$

where stationary stability requires strict spare capacity $\rho \in (0, 1)$ ($1 - \rho > 0$). Read aloud, $\rho$ measures the fraction of time the server is busy—and because $\lambda$ and $T\_{s}$ depend on how units are defined, two clinics reporting the same utilization can face completely different queues when one counts patient visits and the other counts lab orders.

Once the arrival unit is set, connecting occupancy to wait works cleanly forward, not backward. Little's relation $L = \lambda W$ (Little 1961) equates long-run time-average occupancy $L$ to arrival rate $\lambda$ times customer-average system time $W = W\_{q} + S$—summing queue wait $W\_{q}$ and service duration $S$ (mean $\mathbb{E}[S] = T\_{s}$). Establishing limits for $\lambda$ and $W$ yields $L$ directly.

Inferring wait backward from average occupancy is much harder. Glynn and Whitt (1989, Theorem 5 and Remark 6, pp. 636, 641) prove that knowing $0 < \lambda < \infty$ and time-average occupancy $H(t) \to L$ warrants only the lower inequality $\underline{\lim}\_{n\to\infty}\mkern3mu n^{-1}\sum\_{k=1}^{n} W\_{k} \ge \lambda^{-1} L$, where $W\_{k}$ is the $k$-th customer's system time across $n$ arrivals. Exact lower-limit equality requires infinitely many empty-system visits ($Q(t\_{k}) = 0$ for queue occupancy $Q$), and full convergence requires sublinear terminal waits ($n^{-1} W\_{n} \to 0$). Without that tail bound, Glynn and Whitt's (1989, p. 641) Remark 6 gives an example with empty visits and time-average convergence where $\overline{\lim}\_{n\to\infty}\mkern3mu n^{-1}\sum\_{k=1}^{n} W\_{k} = 2 > 1 = \underline{\lim}\_{n\to\infty}\mkern3mu n^{-1}\sum\_{k=1}^{n} W\_{k} = \lambda^{-1} L$ (denoted $\lambda^{-1} H$ in their cost notation). Average occupancy alone can therefore hide exploding individual wait times behind a quiet lobby count.

What governs expected wait before hitting saturation? Consider a stationary single-server FCFS renewal queue ($GI/G/1$, matching any non-preemptive size-blind work-conserving rule) with independent inter-arrival and service times, stable load $\rho \in (0, 1)$, and finite variance ($\alpha > 2$). Write $c\_{a}^{2} \in [0, \infty)$ for the squared coefficient of variation of inter-arrival times and $c\_{s}^{2} \in [0, \infty)$ for service times, measuring relative spread. Two classical formulas of Kingman bound expected **queue wait** $\mathbb{E}[W\_{q}] = \mathbb{E}[W] - T\_{s}$:

$$\mathbb{E}[W\_{q}] \le \frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\mkern3mu\frac{c\_{a}^{2}/\rho^{2}+c\_{s}^{2}}{2}\mkern3mu T\_{s}, \qquad \mathbb{E}[W\_{q}] \approx \frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\mkern3mu\frac{c\_{a}^{2}+c\_{s}^{2}}{2}\mkern3mu T\_{s}.$$

In plain words, expected wait is driven by two multipliers: how close the server sits to full utilization ($\rho/(1 - \rho)$) and how bursty arrivals and service times are ($c\_{a}^{2}$ and $c\_{s}^{2}$).

The two relations play distinct roles. The first is Kingman's (1962) exact all-load $GI/G/1$ upper bound across $\rho \in (0, 1)$, collapsing to $\mathbb{E}[W\_{q}] = 0$ in a clockwork $D/D/1$ queue ($c\_{a}^{2} = c\_{s}^{2} = 0$, while system sojourn stays $\mathbb{E}[W] = T\_{s} > 0$). The second is Kingman's (1961) heavy-traffic asymptote as $\rho \to 1^{-}$, matching exact Pollaczek–Khinchine waits for Poisson arrivals ($M/G/1$, $c\_{a}^{2} = 1$) at every stable load.

Watch the subtle difference at low traffic. Because inter-arrival variance satisfies $\sigma\_{a}^{2} = c\_{a}^{2}/\lambda^{2} = c\_{a}^{2} T\_{s}^{2}/\rho^{2}$, the universal ceiling carries $c\_{a}^{2}/\rho^{2}$ whenever $c\_{a}^{2} > 0$ (vanishing only in $D/G/1$). It diverges as $c\_{a}^{2} T\_{s}/(2\rho) \to +\infty$ when $\rho \to 0^{+}$, where the universal ceiling exceeds both $M/G/1$ and the heavy-traffic curve and clumped renewals can exceed them. As $\rho \to 1^{-}$, $c\_{a}^{2}/\rho^{2} \to c\_{a}^{2}$; whenever $c\_{a}^{2} + c\_{s}^{2} > 0$, ceiling and asymptote converge to ratio $1$ (difference tending to $c\_{a}^{2} T\_{s}$, while both vanish in $D/D/1$). Heavy tails ($\alpha \le 2$) or correlated arrivals break both formulas.

Near saturation ($\rho \to 1^{-}$), $\mathbb{E}[W\_{q}]$ splits cleanly into a congestion multiplier $\phi(\rho) = \rho/(1-\rho)$ and dispersion $(c\_{a}^{2}+c\_{s}^{2})/2$—separating aggregate load from burstiness. Strictly increasing on $\rho \in (0, 1)$ ($\phi'(\rho) = (1-\rho)^{-2} > 0$), $\phi(\rho)$ crosses $1$ at $\rho = 1/2$ (under $0 < c\_{a}^{2}+c\_{s}^{2} < \infty$). Below half load, congestion attenuates dispersion; above half load, $\phi(\rho)$ blows up hyperbolically, and its reciprocal $\alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}} = (1-\rho)/\rho$ sets cascade tolerance in [Layer 03](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md) (sharing both a $\mathcal{G}\_1$ edge and $\lbrace T\_4, T\_6 \rbrace$ in $\mathcal{G}\_2 = J(7, 3)$; [Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)).

Notice how sharply utilization errors amplify near capacity. Kingman's (1962) finite-load bound is U-shaped when $c\_{a}^{2} > 0$, with load elasticity $(1-\rho)^{-1} - 2c\_{a}^{2}/(c\_{a}^{2} + \rho^{2}c\_{s}^{2})$ turning negative at light load, whereas in heavy traffic load elasticity equals $d\ln\phi(\rho)/d\ln\rho = (1-\rho)^{-1} = 1 + \rho/(1-\rho) > 1$ across $\rho \in (0, 1)$, magnifying tiny load errors as $1 - \rho \to 0^{+}$.

## 2. T6Performative: the announcement that enters the arrival

Here, `T6Performative` protects the queue when posting an estimated wait—or chasing it as a target—alters who joins, leaves, or retries. Write $\lambda(W\_{q})$ for the arrival rate induced by queue wait $W\_{q}$, and $W\_{q}(\lambda)$ for the steady-state wait at load $\lambda$. A closed feedback loop seeks a fixed point:

$$W\_{q} = W\_{q}\bigl(\lambda(W\_{q})\bigr).$$

Two feedback regimes behave very differently. Under continuous wait-averse balking ($\lambda'(W\_{q}) \le 0$), strictly increasing wait ($W\_{q}'(\lambda) > 0$ on $\lambda \in [0, 1/T\_{s})$), $\lim\_{W\_{q}\to\infty}\lambda(W\_{q}) < 1/T\_{s}$, and either $\lambda(0) < 1/T\_{s}$ or heavy-traffic blow-up $\lim\_{\lambda \to (1/T\_{s})^-} W\_{q}(\lambda) = +\infty$, the static equilibrium $W\_{q}^\ast$ exists and is **strictly unique**. Yet lagged true-wait updates $W\_{q,t+1} = W\_{q}(\lambda(W\_{q,t}))$ undergo damped alternation for $W\_{q}'(\lambda^\ast)\lambda'(W\_{q}^\ast) \in (-1, 0)$ and lose local stability once $|W\_{q}'(\lambda^\ast)\lambda'(W\_{q}^\ast)| > 1$ (the non-hyperbolic boundary $= 1$ can still contract asymptotically, while large overshoots with $\lambda(W\_{q,t}) \ge 1/T\_{s}$ exit stationarity altogether)—a threshold easily breached near saturation since $W\_{q}'(\lambda) \propto (1-\rho)^{-2}$. True multiple static equilibria require an upward-sloping branch $\lambda'(W\_{q}) > 0$ (Bronson et al. 2021) or distorted wait postings $\hat{W}\_{q} \ne W\_{q}$.

Timeout-driven retry storms emerge even without wall displays as **kinetic metastability**. Writing $u \in \lbrace 0,\dots,K\rbrace$ for §1's discrete queue occupancy $Q$ up to waiting-room limit $K$ (distinct from [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)'s longitudinal utilization $u$) alongside the **retry orbit** $v \in \mathbb{Z}\_{\ge 0}$ of timed-out requests defines a two-dimensional continuous-time Markov chain $(u, v)$ (Alvaro et al. 2025; Habibi et al. 2024). Unlike retry-free $M/M/1$ or $M/M/c$ queues—simply stable ($\lambda < \mu\_{s}$) or unstable ($\lambda > \mu\_{s}$)—retries lock overloaded systems in place.

Trace the retry transitions directly (Alvaro et al. 2025). Set timeout $\tau\_{\mathrm{to}} > 0$ and a mean geometric retry budget of $m\_{\mathrm{ret}} \ge 1$ attempts, giving continuation probability $p\_{\mathrm{ret}} = m\_{\mathrm{ret}}/(m\_{\mathrm{ret}} + 1) \in (0, 1)$ over an unbounded $\mathbb{Z}\_{\ge 0}$ attempt count. Exogenous arrivals $\lambda$ and completions $\mu\_{s}$ ($u \ge 1$) couple through timeout chance $r(u) = \mathbb{P}(\text{sojourn} > \tau\_{\mathrm{to}} \mid u) \in [0, 1]$: admissions at $u < K$ enter $v$ with probability $r(u)$, orbited items retry at rate $p\_{\mathrm{ret}} v/\tau\_{\mathrm{to}}$ (re-orbiting with probability $r(u)$) or drop at $(1 - p\_{\mathrm{ret}})v/\tau\_{\mathrm{to}}$, and $u$-increments cap at $K$.

Notice the resulting timescale trap. Uncapped retries ($p\_{\mathrm{ret}} \to 1$) ignite non-stationary overload once $r(u) \to 1$ (Habibi et al. 2024). Even for finite $m\_{\mathrm{ret}} < \infty$ ($p\_{\mathrm{ret}} < 1$, with finite orbit truncation for stationary bounds), the ergodic chain keeps one **unique** stationary law, yet tiny escape rates between `Low` and `High` attractors produce a tiny nonzero generator eigenvalue $|\mathrm{Re}(\nu\_{2})| \ll 1$ ($\nu\_{2}$ distinct from arrival rate $\lambda$). By $\tau\_{\mathrm{mix}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{TV}}) \ge \ln(1/(2\delta\_{\mathrm{TV}}))/|\mathrm{Re}(\nu\_{2})|$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{TV}} \in (0, 1/2)$), mixing time stretches far past brief spikes (Alvaro et al. 2025).

Empirical service records confirm abandonment reshapes offered load directly: in a bank call center, Brown et al. (2005) found arrival rates shifted by time of day and week while callers hung up mid-wait, so recording only answered calls deletes the queue's defining feedback loop.

Once wait time becomes a target, Goodhart's (1975) law and Strathern's (1997) maxim warn that metric pressure destroys indicator validity, as Espeland & Sauder (2007) documented across ranked institutions. Perdomo et al. (2020) formalize that retraining loop: repeated risk minimization converges to a unique **performatively stable** equilibrium $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$ (distinct from performative optimality $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}}$) under the general **sufficient** condition $|\varepsilon| < \gamma/\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}}$ (for strong convexity $\gamma > 0$ and joint $(z, \theta)$-gradient smoothness $\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}} > 0$; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3), while in the linear-response model contraction holds iff $\kappa\_{\mathrm{RRM}} = |\varepsilon|L\_{y}/\gamma < 1$ (with mixed sample-gradient sensitivity $L\_{y} = |\partial\_{y}\partial\_{\theta}\ell|$)—diverging or oscillating once $|\varepsilon|L\_{y} \ge \gamma$ ([Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md) §7.3.1). In queues, callback queues and automated deflection brighten posted KPIs without fixing real waits.

Consequently, queue records are non-exchangeable across reporting regimes. Kingman's (1962) bound does **not** carry over automatically to queues with active abandonment ($GI/G/1+G$) or state-dependent balking ($Q\_{t}$), which break independent renewals and cap $\mathbb{E}[W\_{q}]$ even at $\rho\_{\mathrm{offered}} \ge 1$. Conditioning at $\lambda^\ast < 1/T\_{s}$ works only when the thinned stream remains a finite-variance $GI/G/1$ renewal process.

## 3. T7Liability: who holds the buffer

Here, `T7Liability` protects who bears congestion or failure risk when spare capacity looks like accounting waste while its payoff is avoided rupture. Queue design separates **service headroom** ($1 - \rho$, cutting delay and storm risk) from **waiting-room storage** ($K$ in $GI/G/1/K$): expanding $K$ without faster service $\mu\_{s}$ or throttling converts immediate drops into *bufferbloat* and locks more retries above $\tau\_{\mathrm{to}}$ ($r(u) \to 1$), requiring non-discriminatory **admission throttling** (or fast-fail shedding backed by `T3` custody of every rejected, redirected, and abandoned arrival so costly cohorts cannot be pruned off-ledger; [Layer 33](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md), sharing a $\mathcal{G}\_1$ edge and $\lbrace T\_6, T\_7 \rbrace$ in $\mathcal{G}\_2 = J(7, 3)$) so CTMC drift clears (Alvaro et al. 2025; Habibi et al. 2024). Queue disciplines also set liability—delay systems bill visitors' time, loss systems reject past $K$, and abandonment sheds callers—each shifting burden across outside tasks and external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7), making `T7Liability` primary (`T7*`).

Hospital emergency telemetry shows how sharp cohort splits hide behind one headline wait average. Over 28 weeks at the emergency department of Troyes (14 October 2018 to 28 April 2019, 34,358 arrivals), Wartelle et al. (2026) recorded a global average occupancy of 32.5 patients and a global average wait of 4 hours 36 min. Disaggregating by multimorbidity cohort revealed sharp stratification: Class 1 patients (targetable unscheduled care, 5,627 arrivals, 16.4%) averaged 3 hours 41 min versus 4 hours 46 min for Class 0 (28,731 arrivals, 83.6%), while triage (25.6 min) and short-circuit waits (2 hours 44 min) sat well below the 7 hours 12 min long circuit—which absorbed 36.9% (10,596) of Class 0 against 9.8% (554) of Class 1 (Wartelle et al. 2026).

One line of calculus captures under-buffering. For buffer $B \ge 0$ (unit marginal capacity cost), convex decreasing rupture probability $P(B)$ ($P'(B) < 0, P''(B) > 0$), loss $L\_{\mathrm{loss}} > 0$ (distinct from §1's occupancy $L$), and interior condition $-P'(0)\mkern3mu L\_{\mathrm{loss}} > 1$, a full-liability actor equates marginal safety gain to marginal cost:

$$-P'(B)\mkern3mu L\_{\mathrm{loss}} = 1.$$

An actor protected by liability cap $A \in [0, L\_{\mathrm{loss}})$ solves against $\min(L\_{\mathrm{loss}}, A)$ and stops earlier (dropping to $B = 0$ if $-P'(0)\min(L\_{\mathrm{loss}}, A) \le 1$) since $-P'(B)$ decreases, ensuring under-buffering without malice (Innes 1990). In *United States v. Carroll Towing Co.* (Hand 1947), $B < PL$ looks symmetric, yet $B$ sits on the operator's ledger while $PL$ lands downstream.

## 4. The signature: epistemic exposure, ancestor cone, and domain boundaries

Locally, `L02`'s `{T4Formalization, T6Performative, T7Liability}` triple carries signature $(0, 1, 2)$ ($k\_1 = 0$, leaving $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ empty locally) and exposes the queue in isolation to **Collapse Proposition 1 (*Blind Undersizing*)** ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)) through censored balking ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$, where Meng's data-defect correlation is distinct from §1's utilization $\rho = \lambda T\_{s}$), arrival clustering ($\mathrm{DEFF}$), and load intervals ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}}$). Across $\mathcal{G}\_1$, `L02` inherits `T1` and `T5` from [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) ([Appendix B.2](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b2-the-multilayer-graph--35-orders-7-strata-5-exposure-regimes)), while **`T2Severity` and `T3Archive` stay absent across $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{02})$**.

Across the queue triad ([Chapter 26](26-regulation-and-homeostasis.md); [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)), `T6Performative` and `T7Liability` block smooth averages from hiding delays pushed across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$):

- **Blind Undersizing ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$):** Missing `T2Severity` and `T3Archive` masks hidden balking and arrival clumping.
- **Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$):** Heavy tails ($\alpha \le 2$), depleted slack ($1-\rho \to 0^+$), or alert floods past human bandwidth ([Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)) push $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ ([Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)).
- **Cynical Capture and Pillaging ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$):** Capped liability ($A < L\_{\mathrm{loss}}$), metric gaming, or unthrottled storage $K$ exports congestion across callers and external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) while ignoring frontline warnings.

Three boundaries fix where `L02` holds and fails:

1. **Formula limits:** Kingman's bound and stationary equilibria break once heavy tails violate $\alpha > 2$, abandonment ($GI/G/1+G$) breaks renewals, finite rooms ($GI/G/1/K$) spill overflows across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$), or retries ($r(u) \to 1$) trap large-$K$ queues below $\rho = 1$.
2. **Reversible exception (Brake 2):** Consented, reversible, subcritical queues ($\rho\_{\mathrm{eff}} < 1$) well below the retry knee—keeping expected sojourn $(u + 1)T\_{s} \ll \tau\_{\mathrm{to}}$ so timeout probability $r(u) = \mathbb{P}(\text{sojourn} > \tau\_{\mathrm{to}} \mid u) \ll 1$—with guaranteed drain, $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, `T3` arrival-and-drop accounting, and zero export across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) avoid ex-post hoarding penalties under Brake 2 (applied strictly *ex post*, never as a live rationing cap; [Chapter 26](26-regulation-and-homeostasis.md); [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)).
3. **Falsification:** `(0, 1, 2)` *Blind Undersizing* fails if queues lacking `T2Severity` and `T3Archive` routinely absorb non-stationary surges, censored arrivals ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$), and dispersion spikes without vital exhaustion or externalized perimeter harm ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).
