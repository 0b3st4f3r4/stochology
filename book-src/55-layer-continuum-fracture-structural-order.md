## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Derive the Griffith critical length from the energy balance of a cracked body, and say why the result is
   a condition on the flaw and not a statement about the load.
2. Show how the fold threshold and the Weibull weakest-link form couple across two scales, and identify
   which exponent carries the imperfection and which carries the population.
3. Say where the liability for a margin settles when the load and the inspection belong to different
   parties, and why the record, not the decision, becomes the evidence after a failure.

## 1. Severity: a crack is a threshold on the flaw before it is a statement about the load

Picture a pane of tempered glass or a steel bridge plate carrying a steady load without bending. Then a tiny edge nick reaches a few millimeters across, and the whole sheet shatters instantly without any extra weight being added.

A solid structure fails at its worst hidden flaw, not at its average stress. Here, $T\_2$ (`T2Severity`) protects structural judgment against inspections that certify average load while leaving unresolved crack tails unmeasured. Carrying $T\_4$ (`T4Formalization`) as its primary site, `L05` joins [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) as Stratum I's second trans-triadic keystone (`(1, 1, 1)`).

Severity turns on energy release rather than local strength alone. Griffith (1921) framed fracture as an energy threshold across a cracked solid. Consider a straight traction-free crack of length $2a > 0$ inside an isothermal, infinite, linear-elastic plate of unit thickness under quasi-static **plane stress** Mode I tension $\sigma > 0$. Write $\Pi\_{0}$ for baseline elastic energy, $E > 0$ for Young's modulus, and $\gamma > 0$ for surface energy per new crack face (assuming ideally brittle cleavage without tip plasticity). Total energy $\Pi(a)$ balances surface creation cost against released elastic energy:

$$\Pi(a) = \Pi\_{0} + 4\gamma a - \frac{\pi\sigma^{2}a^{2}}{E}.$$

Read aloud, growing a crack pays an upfront surface cost ($4\gamma a$) while releasing elastic strain energy scaling with the square of crack length ($\pi\sigma^{2}a^{2}/E$, with $E > 0$).

Setting plane-stress fracture toughness $K\_{c}^{2} = 2\gamma E > 0$ and finding the turning point ($d\Pi/da = 0$ at $\sigma, E > 0$) fixes the critical half-length $a\_{c}$:

$$\frac{d\Pi}{da} = 4\gamma - \frac{2\pi\sigma^{2}a}{E} = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a\_{c} = \frac{2\gamma E}{\pi\sigma^{2}} = \frac{1}{\pi}\left(\frac{K\_{c}}{\sigma}\right)^{2}.$$

In plain words, any scratch shorter than $a\_{c}$ resists growth, while any crack passing $a\_{c}$ releases more energy than it consumes and runs away.

Geometry and real materials modify this ideal limit without erasing the threshold. Thick-section **plane strain** replaces $E$ with $E/(1-\nu^{2})$ via Poisson's ratio $\nu \in (0, 1/2)$ (Griffith 1921), keeping curvature negative ($\Pi''(a\_{c}) = -2\pi\sigma^{2}(1-\nu^{2})/E < 0$) and giving ideal brittle limit $K\_{Ic}^{2} = 2\gamma E/(1-\nu^{2}) > K\_{c}^{2}$ (though thick-section plasticity flips that ordering to $K\_{Ic} < K\_{c}$). Because $\Pi''(a\_{c}) = -2\pi\sigma^{2}/E < 0$ in plane stress, $a\_{c}$ acts as an unstable energy barrier of height $\Pi(a\_{c}) - \Pi(0) = 2\gamma a\_{c} \propto \sigma^{-2}$.

Beyond ideal elastic plates, several physical effects complicate propagation. Plasticity, rising resistance curves ($R$-curves, distinct from member strengths $R\_i$ in §2), and cyclic fatigue grow cracks subcritically toward $a\_{c}$, while thermal gradients interact with local symmetry ($K\_{II} = 0$) to trigger oscillatory crack paths past a supercritical threshold (Corson et al. 2009). Asymmetric voids can even act as **fracture diodes** whose effective toughness $G\_{c}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ blocks cracks in one direction via compliant-to-stiff pinning while easing forward renucleation, an asymmetry that homogenizes away below the $K$-dominant spacing scale and turns defect-sensitive when void spacing widens (Brodnik et al. 2021).

Inverting the energy balance as critical stress $\sigma\_{c}(a) = K\_{c}/\sqrt{\pi a}$ (or $K\_{Ic}/\sqrt{\pi a}$ in plane strain) exposes the inspection trap. Strength is random because flaw size $a$ is random, and the worst crack is the hardest to verify. Collapsing directional fracture arrest onto one scalar $K\_{c}$ performs a non-injective measurement cut ([Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md), sharing $\lbrace T\_2, T\_4 \rbrace$ in $\mathcal{G}\_2 = J(7, 3)$) without imputing strategic agency to passive material voids. Yet if orientation logs stay outside pre-seal custody (`T7Liability`), ex-post actors can invoke scalar $K\_{c}$ as an alibi ([Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)). Worse, multiplying load by factor $\lambda > 1$ shrinks $a\_{c}$ by $\lambda^{2}$: doubling stress cuts critical crack size to one quarter, turning yesterday's safe inspection gate fatal with zero change in the log. That fracture geometry also sets the temporal race inherited from [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md): subcritical growth ($a < a\_{c}$) leaves finite survival time $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ for inspection and unloading to clear $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, whereas crossing $a\_{c}$ drives brittle cleavage at elastic-wave speeds ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \to 0^+$) before any response acts.

Reviewing nominal loads therefore fails `T2Severity`. Checking average stress has zero power against $\Pr[a \ge a\_{c}(\sigma)]$ and omits uncertainty quantification (BIPM, IEC, IFCC, ILAC, ISO, IUPAC, IUPAP, & OIML 2008/2012), while missing `T3Archive` across $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{05})$ leaves sub-resolution flaws below instrument detection limits unrecorded ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$). Roy (1952) turned safety-first planning into a tail chance bound $\Pr[\text{loss} \ge d] \le \alpha$ ($\alpha \in (0, 1)$), though moment inequalities still presume finite variance.

## 2. Formalization: the threshold is a fold, and the population is the same fold counted

Here, `T4Formalization` protects the model against smoothing sudden snap-through collapse and population weakest-link failure into mild average behavior. A threshold crossed irreversibly behaves like a geometric softening fold. Write the subcritical potential energy $V\_{\mathrm{sub}}(x)$ over structural deflection $x$, load headroom $\mu > 0$, and geometric imperfection $h \ge 0$:

$$V\_{\mathrm{sub}}(x) = \frac{\mu}{2}x^{2} - \frac{x^{4}}{4} - h x, \qquad V'\_{\mathrm{sub}}(x) = \mu x - x^{3} - h.$$

Read aloud, $V\_{\mathrm{sub}}(x)$ maps the local intact valley bounded by quartic softening barriers and shows how an initial dent $h$ tilts that valley toward runaway collapse. When perfectly symmetric ($h = 0, \mu > 0$), $x = 0$ forms a local stable minimum ($V''\_{\mathrm{sub}}(0) = \mu > 0$) separated from unstable snap-off by hilltop barriers at $x = \pm\sqrt{\mu}$ of height $\mu^{2}/4$; as headroom $\mu$ shrinks to $0$, both barriers collapse onto $x = 0$ and the stable valley disappears altogether for $\mu \le 0$.

Any real defect $h > 0$ destroys that valley well before zero headroom. Setting $V'\_{\mathrm{sub}} = V''\_{\mathrm{sub}} = 0$ coalesces the intact local minimum and adjacent hilltop at $\mu = 3x^{2}$ and $x\_{\mathrm{fold}} = (h/2)^{1/3} > 0$ (with the remaining unstable hilltop root at $x = -2(h/2)^{1/3}$), yielding critical threshold $\mu\_{\mathrm{crit}} > 0$:

$$\mu\_{\mathrm{crit}} = 3\left(\frac{h}{2}\right)^{2/3}.$$

In plain words, tiny imperfections pack disproportionate punch: shrinking dent $h > 0$ by factor $k > 1$ lowers the collapse threshold only by $k^{2/3}$, leaving $k^{-2/3}$ of the knockdown intact. Below $\mu\_{\mathrm{crit}}$, the intact valley vanishes altogether rather than merely turning unlikely, leaving zero stable equilibrium and driving dynamic snap-through ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \to 0^+$) past any post-onset repair ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$).

Keep this analogy strict: $V\_{\mathrm{sub}}(x)$ models snap-through buckling or cohesive softening via a local quadratic valley bounded by quartic barriers, not an algebraic relabeling of Griffith's quadratic $\Pi(a)$. On $a \ge a\_{0} > 0$ with $da \ge 0$, $\Pi''(a) < 0$ already lacks any local stable valley and scales strength as $\sigma\_{c} \propto a\_{0}^{-1/2}$ rather than $h^{2/3}$, sharing nonlinear defect sensitivity without variable substitution. Under slow continuous drift, Scheffer et al. (2009) observe critical slowing prior to folds, yet noisy variance spikes invite the prosecutor's fallacy (Boettiger & Hastings 2012; [Layer 03](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md)) and vanish completely before abrupt brittle cleavage ($da = 0$ until $a\_{0} = a\_{c}$) or sudden shock loads.

At the scale of an entire structure, the same cliff repeats across every element in series. Consider $N \ge 1$ **non-redundant series** members carrying equal load $r \ge 0$ with i.i.d. strengths $R\_{i}$ and individual member survival probability $S(r) = \Pr[R > r]$ (distinct from Triad axes $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ and Stratum `S1`). System survival equals the chance that the weakest member $R\_{(1)} = \min\_{i} R\_{i}$ holds:

$$\Pr[R\_{(1)} > r] = S(r)^{N}.$$

Read aloud, a chain with $N$ non-redundant links survives only if every single link survives at once.

Under the two-parameter Weibull (1939) law $F(r) = 1 - \exp[-(r/r\_{0})^{m}]$ ($r \ge 0$, scale $r\_{0} > 0$, Weibull shape parameter $m > 0$ distinct from §4's guard margins, zero threshold $r\_{u} = 0$), system survival becomes $\exp[-(r/r\_{1})^{m}]$ with $r\_{1} = r\_{0}N^{-1/m}$, shrinking strength down by $N^{-1/m}$. This scaling fails under parallel load sharing, spatial correlation ($\mathrm{DEFF} > 1$), or a hard strength floor $r\_{u} > 0$ ($R\_{(1)} \ge r\_{u}$). Inside its series domain, it stands as the mechanical ancestor of Stochology's Leontief triad minimum ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)).

Notice how fold and series minimum interlock across scales: a member's valley vanishes at the fold, and a non-redundant bridge fails at its first collapsed member. Both exponents ($2/3$ and $m$) live in the defect distribution rather than average stress. Although mean strength $\mathbb{E}[R] = r\_{0}\Gamma(1 + 1/m)$ scales $r\_{0}$ inside $r\_{1}$, survival depends on the lower tail near $r \to 0^{+}$ (the Weibull minimum attraction domain, distinct from upper-tail extremes; Embrechts, Klüppelberg & Mikosch 1997).

## 3. Liability: the margin is paid by a party who does not choose the load

Here, $T\_7$ (`T7Liability`) protects downstream users and bystanders when the party choosing loads or cost cuts is insulated from the rupture ledger. Sizing against average demand lets load-setters keep upside savings while occupants absorb collapse risk.

As proved in [Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md), limited liability at asset cap $A < L$ truncates Hand's (1947) negligence standard $B < PL$ (where $B$ is precaution cost, $P$ rupture probability, and $L$ expected harm, written $L\_{\mathrm{loss}}$ in [Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md)) to $B < P\min(A, L)$ ([Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)). That convex call-like payoff above debt fuels risk-shifting (Innes 1990; Jensen & Meckling 1976) wherever decision-makers lack skin in the game (Taleb 2018). Pricing damage at mean load also erases Jensen's convexity gap $\Delta\_{J} = \mathbb{E}[\phi(X)] - \phi(\mathbb{E}[X]) \ge 0$ under convex harm $\phi$ ($\mathbb{E}[\lvert X\rvert], \mathbb{E}[\lvert\phi(X)\rvert] < \infty$). Because upstream design cuts stay hidden, Holmström's (1979) informativeness principle requires tying accountability to informative observable signals rather than invisible effort.

The split ledger persists through paperwork asymmetry. Upstream load escalation leaves no signed receipt, whereas field inspection leaves a dated sheet naming an inspector and tolerance. Power (1997) showed how verifiable audit forms displace substantive safety, pinning blame on the last signer with the least power over the load. Because `T3Archive` is absent across $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{05})$ (§4), isolating frontline inspector fault from upstream overload requires importing [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.3.1's blame-transfer split $\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}} = \max(0, b\_{\mathrm{frontline}} - L\_{\mathrm{oper}})$ together with externally sealed, bitemporal `T3` load-and-raise receipts ([Layer 04](54-layer-geophysical-chaos-data-assimilation-order.md); [Layer 29](79-layer-official-statistics-forensic-accounting-order.md)) and bankruptcy-remote consolidated clawbacks ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{clawback}}$).

Upstream-raised mining tailings dams show this split ledger inside continuous soil mechanics. Unlike seismic shaking, **undrained static liquefaction** strikes loose, saturated, contractive tailings under tiny monotonic nudges such as dam raises, toe creep, or piping. Those nudges drive plastic contraction ($\dot{\varepsilon}\_v^{ps} = \sin\psi\_m\mkern3mu\dot{\gamma}^p$, mobilized friction $0 < \phi\_m < \phi\_{\mathrm{cv}}$, positive plastic shear rate $\dot{\gamma}^p > 0$, and dilatancy $\psi\_m < 0 \implies \dot{\varepsilon}\_v^{ps} < 0$ under tension-positive sign) until pore pressure and **contractive strain-softening** collapse the crest just like §2's softening fold (Sottile, Cueto, & Sfriso 2020). Here too the survival clock inherited from [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) splits sharply: slow upstream raises leave finite $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ for monitoring and buttressing inside $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$, whereas once contractive softening triggers, flow liquefaction runs away in seconds ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \to 0^+$) past any committee halt. Traditional limit-equilibrium peak/residual checks miss softening work input; capturing triggering demands state-dependent effective-stress analysis, whereas drained or dense dilative soils sit outside contractive collapse (Sottile, Cueto, & Sfriso 2020), anchoring high-hazard infrastructure safety in [Layer 34](84-layer-infrastructure-nuclear-hydraulic-safety-order.md).

*Grimshaw v. Ford Motor Co.* exposes the same split ledger in tort law. As Dowie (1977) reported, an internal Ford memorandum (*Fatalities Associated with Crash-Induced Fuel Leakage and Fires*) plugged federal death valuations into a ledger so repair cost appeared to exceed harm ($B > PL$). Those prices never reached car buyers or safety specs. Although that specific memo was excluded at trial, admitted records of cost-driven delays upheld punitive damages, affirmed after remittitur by the California Court of Appeal (1981), showing neither secret pricing nor capped awards legitimize unconsented tail transfers ([Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)).

Time erodes vigilance inside organizations as well. Vaughan (1996) traced how safe launches on the outcome tape (*skopós*) made past assumptions look overly cautious, misreading survival luck as command headroom (*télos*) against non-commutation ([Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)) while margins decayed (Perrow 1984; Leveson 2011). Douglas (2000) emphasized that every acceptance cutoff carries a value judgment over whose injury matters.
