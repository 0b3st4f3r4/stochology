## 1. Why the audit must be syntactic

Picture an automated safety interlock standing before a rocket valve or hospital pump. Before releasing a command, the monitor wants a guarantee that the program will never drain a reserve twice or exceed its budget. Yet if the monitor tries to predict everything arbitrary code might do at runtime, the rocket clears the tower—or the patient crashes—while the verifier is still thinking.

Here, $T\_1$ (`T1Framing`) protects that pre-actuation boundary by pinning the audit to explicit, decidable tape syntax before any execution starts. By Rice's theorem (1953)—descending from Turing's (1936) halting problem—every non-trivial semantic property of what an arbitrary Turing-complete program computes is undecidable. Waiting on a general semantic proof inside a finite loop violates physical survival timing.

**Domain boundary:** Rice's obstruction vanishes when programs stay inside finite-state or sub-Turing decidable fragments. Even there, semantic checking still faces combinatorial state blow-up ($g^{7}$ in §3), rare verifier soundness bugs, and unmodeled external premises (§4–§5).

The practical escape separates fast syntax parsing from ordered rule checking ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.5; [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1). First, a linear-time context-free $\mathrm{LL}(1)$ grammar parses each step's skeleton ($\langle\textit{Framing}\rangle\langle\textit{Counting}\rangle\langle\textit{Body}\rangle$), admitting optional clauses (`[deff]`, $[\langle\textit{Brake}\rangle]$, `[insulate | open | reopen]`) and looking ahead one token to separate `halt` from the next `frame`.

Over that parse tree, after a static pre-stage check rejects any cross-axis mismatch between a declared collapse proposition and guard site, the five-stage acceptance predicate runs in strict order:

1. Stage 1 (*Skeleton*) checks an attested control tape, framing mode (routing attested `reopen` notices to `CounterInductiveReopen` quarantine instead of silent edits), non-inverted bounds ($L\_t \le U\_t$), positive capacity, non-negative reserves, positive frontier band width ($\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} > 0$), derivation budget (`BudgetExceeded`), non-finite control-tape or threshold scalars, and write/read scope (enforced at the host capability boundary).
2. Stage 2 (*Totality*) confirms at least one guard reading per triad axis.
3. Stage 3 (*Finiteness*) rejects non-finite or missing guard margins in $\mathbf{m}\_t$ (while out-of-range tape, extent, or scale scalars fail structural sealing upfront).
4. Stage 4 takes the per-step Leontief minimum, and Stage 5 checks the frontier band (requiring declared sites and propositions to attain that fold).

At the same time, static derivation checks enforce multiset resource balance. Crucially, the predicate obeys **contingency invariance** ($\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t; \boldsymbol{\tau}) = \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}'\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t; \boldsymbol{\tau})$)—judging the command tape $\mathbf{u}\_t$ and margins $\mathbf{m}\_t$ (*télos*) independently of the contingency tape $\mathbf{w}\_t$ (*skopós*), so a lucky outcome never clears an unbuffered command—paired with **stage non-commutation** across the five ordered stages: permuting Stage 4 ahead of Stage 1 clears an attested `reopen` transcript as `Interior` whenever $m\_\ast \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}}$ instead of quarantining `CounterInductiveReopen`, while evaluating Stage 4 before Stage 3 lets non-finite margins fail threshold checks silently ([Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)).

Framing places this check on the decidable side of Rice's line, binding pre-data specs and `CounterInductiveReopen` flags ([Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md)) to explicit rules. Asking whether code matches human intent tests semantics; checking whether a derivation respects declared resource counts takes linear pre-write time. It constrains what enters the tape without guaranteeing range bounds, physical units, or finite computer arithmetic (§4–§5).

## 2. Linear logic and affine resources

Here, $T\_4$ (`T4Formalization`) protects physical reserves and cryptographic seals by encoding them in a type grammar where items cannot be quietly cloned or thrown away. Girard's linear logic (1987) and modern affine type systems remove two structural rules that ordinary logic grants freely: *weakening* (dropping an unused premise) and *contraction* (copying a premise to use twice).

**Strict linear logic** bans both rules, forcing every linear resource to be consumed **exactly once** unless tagged with Girard's reusable *of course* modality (`!A`). An **affine** discipline restores weakening while keeping contraction banned, so each affine resource can be spent **at most once**.

The three Stochology axes split naturally across those rules:

- Single-opening pre-outcome seals and raised algedonic alarms obey **strict linear** rules: neither can be duplicated nor quietly dropped by a signature downstream.
- Physical reserves obey **affine** accounting: unused slack may sit idle above Stage 4–5's $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ floor, never quietly zeroed out below it.
- Read-only shared declarations sit under reusable `!A`.

In distributed systems, strict linear typing directly yields **anti-replay capability tokens**. Each authorization carries a unique ID consumed once into a monotonically growing used-set, blocking network retries or state rollbacks from reviving spent authority.

Consider two separated contexts $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ typing programs $t : A$ and $u : B$. The multiplicative tensor rule joins them without letting either context borrow from the other:

$$\frac{\Gamma \vdash t : A \qquad \Delta \vdash u : B}{\Gamma, \Delta \vdash t \otimes u : A \otimes B}$$

In plain words, splitting contexts $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ guarantees no resource name appears on both branches. A checker verifies at compile time off the tree's shape—without running the code—that every affine reserve hits at most one leaf and every strict linear obligation hits exactly one.

Systems languages still expose an explicit escape hatch from affine safety. Formalizing Rust's core calculus ($\lambda\_{\mathrm{Rust}}$), Jung et al. (2017) proved that pure syntactic *progress and preservation* breaks once safe code mixes with `unsafe` blocks, grounding soundness in manual proofs inside library interiors while leaving foreign-function interfaces (`FFI`) outside. Across real hybrid code combining `unsafe` and `FFI` (Xu et al. 2021), affine guarantees hold only if unverified interiors honor their memory and layout contracts—requiring runtime sanitization at the boundary (§3, §7).

### Paraconsistent containment, local consistency, and the linear live boundary

Notice the exact boundary separating live actuation from premise custody. Inside fast pre-actuation loops constrained by Rice's theorem (1953) and $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$, strict linear and affine typing (Girard 1987) together with Stage-One `Unparsable` quarantine remain mandatory: Stochology never paraconsistentizes the live acceptance predicate.

Whereas Girard's linear logic removes *weakening* and *contraction* to guard resource counts, **paraconsistent logic** removes unrestricted explosion (*ex contradictione quodlibet*) over ingested archive claims at `T1Framing`. In Newton da Costa's foundational hierarchy $C\_n$ (da Costa 1993 [1963]; da Costa 1974), $C\_1$ retains positive classical deduction while conditioning *reductio ad absurdum* on an object-language local-consistency operator $A^\circ := \neg(A \land \neg A)$ and an embedded strong classical negation $\neg^\ast A := \neg A \land A^\circ$. Because $C\_1$ admits no finite truth-table matrix, da Costa and Alves (1977) characterize it through non-truth-functional bivaluations $v$ into $\lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$: $v(A) = 0$ forces $v(\neg A) = 1$, whereas $v(A) = 1$ permits both $v(\neg A) = 0$ and $v(\neg A) = 1$ (marking $v(A^\circ) = 0$). **Logics of Formal Inconsistency** (`LFIs`; Carnielli, Coniglio, & Marcos 2007; Carnielli & Coniglio 2016) generalize $A^\circ$ into a primitive consistency connective $\circ A$ governed by the **Principle of Gentle Explosion**:

$$\{A, \neg A\} ⊬\_{\mathrm{LFI}} B, \qquad \{A, \neg A, \circ A\} \vdash\_{\mathrm{LFI}} B.$$

Read aloud, an uncertified contradiction between proposition $A$ and its negation $\neg A$ never licenses deducing an arbitrary formula $B$ under $\vdash\_{\mathrm{LFI}}$, whereas classical *reductio* and Disjunctive Syllogism recover cleanly wherever local consistency $\circ A$ is explicitly attested. Any uncertified conflict therefore enters `Unparsable` or `CounterInductiveReopen` quarantine rather than trivializing unrelated checks—yet quarantine never overrides live **Regime III-B (`Algedonic Halt`)** survival-clock preemption when $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)), falls back under time pressure to the **worst admissible prior sealed vintage** ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)), and throttles adversarial conflict floods through per-stream isolated *e*-processes within human deliberative limits ($\approx 10\text{ bits/s}$; Zheng & Meister 2025; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)). Under §7's `(1, 2, 0)` governance exposure, whoever attests $\circ A$ must remain institutionally separated from whoever writes the tape, sets $U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$, sets premise expiry windows, and signs the log.

## 3. Bounded enumeration and the coverage ledger

Here, $T\_5$ (`T5Temporal`) protects verification from blowing past the physical survival clock during exhaustive state search. Testing $g \ge 1$ discrete levels across $7$ guard sites takes $g^{7} \ge 1$ checks, so an uncapped sweep can stall until the system crashes (Collapse Proposition 2).

Every generative search therefore carries a mandatory coverage ledger with a positive integer step cap $C\_{\mathrm{enum}} \ge 1$ ($C\_{\mathrm{enum}} \in \mathbb{Z}\_{\ge 1}$, distinct from §2's $C\_1$ calculus). Once checks reach $C\_{\mathrm{enum}}$, the search stops inside its time budget and marks the rest unverified. The contract defines **coverage fraction** $c(g) = \min(1, C\_{\mathrm{enum}}/g^{7}) \in (0, 1]$ and **truncation fraction** $1 - c(g) \in [0, 1)$: coverage stays complete ($c(g) = 1$) while $g^{7} \le C\_{\mathrm{enum}}$, then drops as $C\_{\mathrm{enum}}/g^{7}$. An honest report publishes both numbers and downgrades uncovered states from "clean" to an explicit abstention.

That ledger states a set inclusion rather than a sample count. Let $V\_{\mathrm{viol}}$ (distinct from Keynesian evidential weight) denote all grammar violation shapes, $V\_{\mathrm{rej}}$ those caught by the predicate, and $U = U\_{\mathrm{trunc}} \cup U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$ ($U\_{\mathrm{trunc}} \cap U\_{\mathrm{escape}} = \varnothing$, distinct from §1's interval upper bound $U\_t$) the declared blind spot combining budget truncation ($U\_{\mathrm{trunc}}$) and human-declared unverified interiors ($U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$, such as `unsafe` or `FFI` boundaries). Closure requires:

$$V\_{\mathrm{viol}} \subseteq V\_{\mathrm{rej}} \cup U, \qquad U \cap V\_{\mathrm{rej}} = \varnothing.$$

Read aloud, every valid grammar fault must either be caught ($V\_{\mathrm{rej}}$) or sit inside the declared blind spot ($U$), with no shape claiming both verdicts at once. Full grid sweeps ($c(g) = 1$) empty only $U\_{\mathrm{trunc}} = \varnothing$ while leaving $U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$ intact. Under Brake 1 ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)), $U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$ cannot serve as an unbounded dump for unverified code: every `unsafe`/`FFI` escape seam must carry explicit boundary surface caps and conservative margin widening rather than letting $c(g) = 1$ certify unguarded interiors. Sampling shows only observed runs, whereas bounded enumeration checks the grammar's finite shapes—falsified if any accepted write outside $U$ breaks a resource rule, though §4 and §5 show how valid grammar writes still fail when errors sit outside the tape's vocabulary.

## 4. The three collapses, and two declarations that licensed a wrong write

1. **Collapse Proposition 1** ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$, silent domain narrowing): casting values into narrow types without bounding out-of-range inputs. This caused the 1996 Ariane 5 Flight 501 loss, where a 64-bit horizontal velocity overflowed a 16-bit signed integer inherited from Ariane 4's smaller envelope (Lions 1996) (*fails if* unguarded narrowings or unit-stripped interfaces routinely preserve safety across out-of-envelope shifts).
2. **Collapse Proposition 2** ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$, unbounded proof latency): running uncapped verification inside a live loop until committee or solver latency outlasts survival time (*fails if* unbounded semantic solvers or uncapped state sweeps inside live loops consistently satisfy $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ through surges).
3. **Collapse Proposition 3** ($\neg S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$, ambient authority): letting code write outside its contract scope and spend shared reserves without an attested tape or declared starting state.

The Mars Climate Orbiter failure in 1999 exposes the unit-level twin of Collapse 1 (NASA 1999). Navigation expected newton-seconds, yet a ground tool emitted unlabeled pound-force seconds that flight software accepted cleanly. Both programs type-checked, yet stripped unit tags understated every thruster burn. **Parametric physical-dimension types** reject mismatched unit exponents at compile time (`T4Formalization`), yet lose protection wherever untyped `FFI` or raw file parsers drop units back to plain scalars—leaving calibration and scale invariance outside pure syntax ([Chapter 57](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md)).

A subtler flaw hides inside floating-point signatures themselves. Type checkers treat finite-precision IEEE-754 numbers (`FP64`, `FP32`) and compact formats (`BF16`) as surrogates for exact real numbers $\mathbb{R}$ ([Chapter 10](10-numbers-order-bounds.md)). Machine addition breaks associativity: $(a \oplus b) \oplus c \neq a \oplus (b \oplus c)$, hiding schedule dependence under `T1Framing`.

Empirical systems show how execution order breaks reproducibility. Parallel GPU reductions and tensor kernels diverge across thread schedules and can throw runtime errors when strict determinism is demanded (Shanmugavelu et al. 2024). Likewise, database storage layouts, SIMD lanes, and hash aggregations reorder floating-point sums over `float` and `double` columns, violating data independence (Müller, Arteaga, Hoefler, & Alonso 2018). Safe arithmetic demands outward interval bounds ([Chapter 10](10-numbers-order-bounds.md)) or order-invariant accumulators (`RSum` / `repro<ScalarT, L>`; Müller et al. 2018; [Chapter 14](14-linear-algebra.md)), paying extra memory and barrier latency budgeted under `T5Temporal` ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$) instead of stalling live loops.

## 5. What a type can say, and what it cannot

The chapter's core object is a typing judgment $\Gamma \vdash e : A$ over a multiset context $\Gamma$ enforcing exact counts ($= 1$ for seals and alarms), affine caps ($\le 1$ for reserves), or explicit reuse `!A` (Girard 1987). Even with unit types, syntax checks only consumption rules while leaving three realities open:

1. **Consumer identity:** one actor authoring the spec, setting $U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$, and signing the log still type-checks.
2. **Premise truth:** true and false claims inhabit the exact same syntactic type.
3. **Unmodeled physics:** shifted flight envelopes (Lions 1996), unit-stripped interfaces (NASA 1999), and non-associative hardware sums over `FP32`/`FP64`/`BF16` ([Chapter 10](10-numbers-order-bounds.md); [Chapter 14](14-linear-algebra.md)).

Type preservation and progress guarantee internal consistency—conditional inside Rust libraries on `unsafe` proofs (Jung et al. 2017)—while contingency invariance and stage non-commutation (§1) separate *télos* from *skopós* and guard stage order. Neither verifies the physical plant without expiry rules (§6), boundary sanitization across $U\_{\mathrm{escape}}$, and separated governance (§7).

## 6. Expiry: the proof that was valid at issuance

Every certificate speaks about premises at issuance, while the write executes later. Lamport's (1978) *happens-before* relation shows why form alone cannot cross that gap: events are causally ordered only by a message chain, leaving distant writes concurrent with stale declarations unless clocks or signed chains bind them.

Empirical premises also change after initial release, just as Croushore and Stark's (2001) economic vintages revise historical GDP under identical labels. Let each of $n \ge 1$ declared premises carry a non-empty validity window $[a\_i, b\_i]$ ($a\_i \le b\_i$) aligned under causal ordering (Lamport 1978), and write $\ell(t) = \lvert\lbrace i \in \lbrace 1, \dots, n \rbrace : t > b\_i \rbrace\rvert \in \lbrace 0, \dots, n \rbrace$ for the count of expired premises at time $t$. A well-formed write (`wellformed`) is licensed ($\mathrm{licence}(t) = 1$) only while every window holds:

$$\mathrm{licence}(t) = 1 \iff \Bigl( t \in \bigcap\_{i=1}^{n} [a\_i, b\_i] \Bigr) \wedge \mathrm{wellformed}.$$

In plain words, the licence holds only inside the shared overlap of every active premise window. Writing too early ($t < \max\_i a\_i$) fails immediately ($\mathrm{licence}(t) = 0$) even with zero expirations ($\ell(t) = 0$). Once active ($t \ge \max\_i a\_i$), admissibility matches $\ell(t) = 0$ and triggers re-checking whenever $\ell(t) > 0$—requiring at most $m$ linear re-checks across $m \ge 1$ writes, far cheaper than re-running semantic verification. Under Brake 3 ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)), each $[a\_i, b\_i]$ operates as a **non-self-extending sunset** set independently of the tape author: expired windows force automatic **Regime III-B** halt or `CounterInductiveReopen` quarantine rather than unilateral extension or pre-renewed rollover.

Without expiry gates, stale certificates fuel Vaughan's (1996) normalization of deviance in the Challenger disaster: past flights that happened not to blow up were treated as proof of live safety margins. Expiry keeps every certificate tied to present time rather than its birthday.
