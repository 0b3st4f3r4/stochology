This order descends from the coupled-field layer: a form that regenerates is a topology maintained across
parts, and the maintenance is what the parent order describes without a clock. It opens edges the rest of
the stratum takes up — [Layer 13](63-layer-immunological-self-governance-order.md),
[Layer 14](64-layer-taxonomy-systematics-ontology-order.md) and
[Layer 15](65-layer-stratigraphy-paleobiology-deep-time-order.md) each stand on it — because every
later order of the living stratum needs a criterion of sameness before it can say that something has been
preserved, restored or lost.

1. State why a criterion of sameness precedes any measurement of a form, and what a frame's omissions cost
   the record that inherits them.
2. Derive the length scale and the boundary position of a maintained gradient, and show why the escape from

3. Say what a threshold readout enacts and does not observe, and why a positioning error and a boundary

## 1. The frame: which difference counts as a different form

A tissue has no identity apart from a criterion of sameness, and the criterion is not read off the
specimen. Before anyone measures a developing organ, someone has decided which differences are variation
within a form and which are a different form; that decision is the frame of this order, and every later
measurement is an answer given inside it. The decision cannot be avoided by measuring harder, because
measurement returns differences and the frame is what sorts them into the ones that count and the ones that
do not. The order's first site is the question, not the instrument.

Canguilhem (1943/1966) is the precise statement of what that sorting is. The normal is not the frequent:
a norm is carried by the living thing as the way it can go on living, and the pathological is not a
deviation measured against a mean but another way of living judged against a norm. Read into development,
the consequence is sharp. The frequency of a malformation tells you nothing about whether it is one, and a
series of specimens ordered by how often a variant appears is not yet a series ordered by form. What the
embryologist calls the same organ is a judgement about which differences the organism can absorb, and the
judgement is made before the count.

The frame also has a price, and the price is paid by the record. A criterion of sameness that ignores a
difference makes that difference invisible to every later assay, so what the criterion leaves out is not
corrected downstream — it is inherited. The defect this order carries is therefore one level up from the
archival defect of a missing entry: here the recording instrument is a criterion, and a criterion selects.
Three samenesses are in play at once, and they can disagree: the same organ across individuals, the same
organ across time within one individual, and the same organ across a regeneration. Each is answered by a
different criterion. An assay that silently adopts one of the three reports a result about the other two,
which is the ordinary way a developmental claim travels further than its frame.

## 2. The clock: the gradient as maintained memory, and the barrier as the wait

A form that regenerates must remember where its parts are, and the memory is a maintained disequilibrium,
not a stored coordinate. Take a source that produces a substance at a fixed rate, a medium in which
it diffuses with coefficient $D$, and a decay that removes it at rate $1/\tau$. In one dimension the steady
state balances diffusion against removal,

$$D\mkern3mu\frac{d^{2}c}{dx^{2}} = \frac{c}{\tau},$$

whose decaying solution is

$$c(x) = c\_0\mkern3mu e^{-x/\lambda}, \qquad \lambda = \sqrt{D\tau}.$$

The length scale is the geometric mean of a transport coefficient and a lifetime, and that is the whole
content of the order's temporal site in one expression: a gradient reaches as far as the substance can
travel before it is destroyed. Set the source to zero and the pattern erases in a time of order $\tau$, so
a tissue that must remember a position must keep paying for the source. The gradient is not information
that was written and can be read at leisure; it is a flow that stops when the supply stops, and the
persistence of the form is the persistence of the flow.

The readout converts that scale into a boundary. A cell that responds above a threshold $c^{\ast}$ sits on the
side of the position

$$x^{\ast} = \lambda \ln\frac{c\_0}{c^{\ast}},$$

so the boundary of a territory is a logarithm of a ratio of concentrations, and its position moves with the
length scale, the source and the threshold at once. Nothing about that expression is a fixed address. The
boundary is where a reading crosses a level, and the level is supplied by the reader.

The second half of the temporal site is the wait and not the flow, and it belongs to the form's
stability. Waddington (1957) described development as a landscape in which a pathway runs down a valley
whose curvature holds it against perturbation, and the valley is the object the word canalization names. A
valley is a basin with a barrier, and the time to leave a basin is governed by the same thermal tail that
sets a reaction rate. The fraction of a population above a barrier $\Delta V$ at noise level $k\_B T$ is
$e^{-\Delta V/k\_B T}$ (Boltzmann 1877), so the mean time to cross is

$$\tau\_{\mathrm{esc}} = \tau\_0\mkern3mu e^{\Delta V / k\_B T},$$

with $\tau\_0$ the attempt time. Both consequences are about clocks, not about shapes. The transition from
labile to fixed is sharp in the barrier and slow in time: because the exponent is
linear in $\Delta V$, a modest deepening of the valley multiplies the waiting time, so a form becomes
irreversible not when a lock is installed but when the time to leave exceeds the life of the tissue.
The window in which a form is still plastic is finite and computable from the barrier, which means
that the claim "this form is stable" is a claim about a waiting time and not about a wall.

The second consequence has a corollary that concerns the observer rather than the tissue. An experiment
that follows a form for a fixed period will report it as stable whenever the escape time exceeds that
period, and the same tissue followed longer will report a transition at a date the first experiment could
not see. Stability is therefore indexed to a horizon: nothing in the observation distinguishes a barrier
that is high from a barrier that is merely high relative to the window, and a claim that a pathway is fixed
is a claim about the ratio of two times. A report that omits the window has omitted the denominator, and the
form it describes as settled is settled only for the observer who stopped looking.

The chaperone buffer makes the point unavoidable. Rutherford & Lindquist (1998) showed that a chaperone
absorbs the effect of genetic variation so that development stays on its canonical path, and that when the
buffer is exhausted under stress the variation it had been holding appears at once. In the vocabulary of
this order, that is a capacitor, and a capacitor is a delayed release, which is to say a clock. The buffer
was never a guarantee that the form would hold; it was a debt with a maturity date, and the maturity date
is reached when the stress arrives. A description of development that reports only the shape of the valley
and not the depth of the buffer reports the form and omits the time at which it will stop being one.

## 3. The boundary as a readout: the threshold that makes the tissue

Differentiate the boundary position with respect to the threshold and the performative site appears:

$$\delta x^{\ast} = -\mkern3mu\frac{\lambda}{c^{\ast}}\mkern3mu\delta c^{\ast},$$

so a fluctuation in a reader's sensitivity is a displacement of anatomy. This is not a measurement error to
be averaged away. A tissue has no way to distinguish a boundary that moved because the source moved from one
that moved because the reader drifted, since both appear as the same displacement of the same line. What
the readout reports as the location of a boundary is therefore partly a property of the readout, and a
change in the reader changes the object while leaving the gradient untouched. The classification does not
describe a boundary that was already there; it makes the boundary by deciding where a continuous quantity
crosses a level.

The reader also has a ceiling, and the ceiling moves the boundary a second time. A response that saturates,
as any finite array of receptors must, stops resolving above the level at which it is full, so once the
gradient passes that level the boundary is pinned by the reader and no longer follows the source. In the
algebra above the threshold has become a ceiling, and $\delta c^{\ast}$ no longer measures a sensitivity but a
saturation point; the tissue then reads a boundary at the furthest position the reader can still
distinguish, whatever the gradient does beyond it. A source that keeps producing past that point changes the
concentration and not the anatomy, and the reading is indistinguishable from a boundary that has stopped
moving because the form has been fixed. Two very different states, a settled form and a saturated reader,
present as the same observation, and only an instrument that can be attenuated tells them apart.

The loop closes on identity. What passes a regeneration assay is the same organ, so the assay's criterion
becomes the definition for everything downstream, and a structure that fails only in a difference below the
assay's resolution is, for every practical purpose, the same. That is a performative effect in the strict
sense: the description does not report an identity that existed independently of it, it fixes the identity
that later descriptions will report. And the effect is recursive, because the criterion is then used to
decide which specimens are evidence about development at all, so the sample that tests the criterion is
selected by it.

Waddington's landscape is the clearest case, and it is worth stating without deference. A picture of
development as a set of canonical valleys makes the path that leaves a valley a failure, and the failures
are then counted as evidence that the valleys are real. The picture is not thereby wrong; it is
constitutive. Once it is the standard, the norm against which a deviation is a defect has been supplied by
the description, and the question of whether the organism carries that norm has been answered by
construction, not by argument. The frame of §1 has come back as the instrument of §3, and
this order's triple is intact: the clock of §2 times something only because a criterion of sameness says
what is being timed, and the readout of §3 decides when the timing has arrived.
