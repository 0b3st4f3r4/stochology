## By the end of this chapter you can

1. State why the self/non-self decision is a conjunctive test rather than a weighted sum, and derive the bound on its false-positive rate without assuming the two arms are independent.
2. Explain why the reference against which the test runs is a sample of the body, and why an uncovered tissue produces a coverage failure rather than a misclassification.
3. Say why the cost of both errors is paid by a party that did not decide, and why a brake built on the channel it disciplines makes the halt necessarily late.

## 1. A test with two arms that must fire in one encounter

Picture a roaming immune cell bumping into a liver cell in the dark with no outside referee or appeal court available. Attacking a healthy tissue triggers lethal autoimmunity, while letting a fast-dividing virus pass unchallenged costs the host its life, all judged live inside the same living body.

Here, $T\_2$ (`T2Severity`) protects the body against false attacks by forcing two separate molecular keys to turn together in one encounter rather than trading one signal off against the other. Bretscher and Cohn stated this two-signal rule cleanly: antigen recognition alone (`Signal 1`) drives tolerance and inactivation, whereas immune firing requires associative recognition (`Signal 2`, originally modeled as helper carrier recognition and later broadened to APC co-stimulation) in the same meeting (Bretscher & Cohn 1970). Janeway later rooted `Signal 2` in pattern-recognition receptors detecting conserved microbial signatures (Janeway 1989).

Still, a simple self-versus-microbe split leaves a twofold seam: friendly gut bacteria and fetal tissue stay tolerated without injury, while sterile trauma leaks danger signals that supply `Signal 2` without infection (Matzinger 1994). On the specificity arm, `Layer 13` inherits its chemical amplifier from [Layer 11: Biochemical Kinetic & Molecular Order](61-layer-biochemical-kinetic-molecular-order.md). Coupling kinetic proofreading (McKeithan 1995) to digital `ERK-1` amplification and fast `SHP-1` negative feedback lets a T cell scan $1 \times 10^5$–$1 \times 10^6$ self ligands accurately: converting an agonist into a weak-agonist pMHC cuts complex lifetime less than 5-fold (from $10.8$ to $2.3\text{ s}$ at room temperature) yet drives a $2 \times 10^4$-fold drop in potency (Kersh et al. 1998; modeled in Altan-Bonnet & Germain 2005; compare [Chapter 24](24-chemical-rates-equilibrium-buffers.md)).

Represent an encounter by nonnegative specificity strength $s \ge 0$ and danger costimulation $c \ge 0$ compared against fixed thresholds $\theta\_s, \theta\_c > 0$, with single-arm false-alarm probabilities $p\_s = P(s \ge \theta\_s \mid \mathrm{self})$ and $p\_c = P(c \ge \theta\_c \mid \mathrm{self})$. Without assuming independence between arms, the conjunctive rule $s \ge \theta\_s \wedge c \ge \theta\_c$ obeys the exact Fréchet–Hoeffding bounds around the independent benchmark $p\_s p\_c$:

$$\max(0,\mkern5mu p\_s + p\_c - 1) \mkern5mu\le\mkern5mu P\bigl(s \ge \theta\_s \wedge c \ge \theta\_c \mid \text{self}\bigr) \mkern5mu\le\mkern5mu \min(p\_s,\mkern5mu p\_c).$$

Read aloud, false firing can never beat the weaker of the two individual error rates, and collapses straight to zero if either arm locks shut.

Compare that with an additive score $s + \lambda c \ge \theta$ ($\lambda, \theta > 0$). Contrary to careless claims, an additive sum does carry a distribution-free union/Makarov upper ceiling:

$$P(s + \lambda c \ge \theta \mid \text{self}) \mkern5mu\le\mkern5mu \inf\_{a + \lambda b = \theta} \min\bigl\lbrace{}1,\mkern5mu P(s \ge a \mid \text{self}) + P(c \ge b \mid \text{self})\bigr\rbrace.$$

In plain words, what separates conjunction from addition is **null-arm veto**: in quiet sterile tissue ($p\_c = 0$, or symmetrically $s = 0$), conjunction guarantees zero self-attack, whereas an additive rule still fires whenever one loud channel exceeds $\theta$. Yet the upper bound $\min(p\_s, p\_c)$ also marks the gate's fragility: once injury inflames tissue and drives $p\_c \to 1^-$, conjunction reduces to single-arm selectivity $p\_s$. [Chapter 40, *Institution, Incentive, Metric*](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) locks in the same conjunctive check across safeguard terms so high confidence on one signal never overrides a zero on the other, tying `T2Severity` to pre-set thresholds rather than post-hoc rule drift.

## 2. The reference is a sample of the body, and the margin is time

Here, $T\_5$ (`T5Temporal`) protects immune readiness inside two clocks: thymic training only previews a sample of body proteins, and peripheral rescue must mobilize before pathogen growth or chronic inflammation wipes out tissue headroom. Central tolerance tests young T cells against an internal library rather than the whole body. Medullary thymic epithelial cells (`mTECs`) rely on `AIRE`-driven transcription to display peripheral tissue proteins inside the thymus (Anderson et al. 2002).

Thymic `Aire` operates by both deleting self-reactive cells and diverting clones into `Foxp3+` regulatory T cells (`Treg`). Consequently, `Aire-/-` mice overrepresent `246` of `6,422` (`3.8%`) recurrent conventional (`Tconv`) TCRs and lose `12.6%` of `215` `Aire`-dependent `Treg`-biased clonotypes into effector `Tconv` cells (Malchow et al. 2016). `Layer 13` inherits that thymic organ architecture upstream from [Layer 12: Morphogenetic & Developmental Order](62-layer-morphogenetic-developmental-order.md) rather than carrying an internal `T3Archive` guard.

Suppose a target self peptide appears on fraction $q \in [0, 1]$ of mTECs, and a thymocyte samples $k \ge 1$ independent contacts with replacement. Under ideal unit deletion or `Treg`-diversion efficiency per expressing contact ($p\_{\mathrm{del}} = 1$), its probability $P\_{\mathrm{escape}}(k, q)$ of dodging central tolerance equals:

$$P\_{\mathrm{escape}}(k, q) = (1 - q)^k,$$

while under partial per-encounter capture $p\_{\mathrm{del}} \in (0, 1)$ this expression is strictly a best-case lower bound on $(1 - q\mkern2mu p\_{\mathrm{del}})^k$. Read aloud, escape drops exponentially with contacts $k$ when $q$ and $p\_{\mathrm{del}}$ are positive, but stays stuck unconditionally at $1$ when $q = 0$.

Single-cell sequencing reveals both broad reach and sharp structural blind spots in that thymic preview:

- **Collective breadth:** Mature `Aire`-positive mTECs collectively transcribe `87%` (`19,293`) of Ensembl protein-coding genes (`FPKM` $\ge 0.13$). Across `141` `Aire`-positive single TECs (`174` mature mTECs hitting `>3000` genes), `3,980` `Aire`-induced genes (`>2-fold`, `5% FDR`) express stochastically without clustering (Sansom et al. 2014).
- **Sparse instant display:** At any instant, only `1–3%` of mTECs display a given tissue-restricted antigen (`TRA`, $q \in [0.01, 0.03]$) (Matsumoto et al. 2023).
- **Structural omission ($q = 0$):** Mature mTECs cover only `60.1%` of protein-coding transcripts (`57,019` known isoforms) across `3,889` `Aire`-regulated `TRA` genes and omit brain-specific microexons ($\le 30\text{ bp}$, $\mathrm{PSI} < 0.1$) (Jansen et al. 2021), creating blind spots where $(1 - 0)^k = 1$.

When an escaped clone spots an unprojected self peptide in peripheral tissue, it trips **only `Signal 1`** ($s \ge \theta\_s$). In healthy uninjured organs ($c < \theta\_c$), the two-signal lock holds tolerance, until concurrent infection or trauma delivers `Signal 2` ($c \ge \theta\_c$). Even when $q > 0$, peripheral `Tregs` must cover low-avidity or cross-reactive survivors, because tightening `Signal 1` thresholds can never fix an unprojected frame ($q = 0$).

Peripheral battles then run strictly on the biological clock (`T5Temporal`). Write regulatory capacity $C(t) > 0$, inflammatory load $L(t) \ge 0$, net reserve $m(t) = C(t) - L(t) > 0$, and margin drawdown rate $\dot{D} > 0$. Tissue survival demands that remaining headroom $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(m)$ outlast cellular response latency $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{cell}}$ across antigen detection ($\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}$), clonal expansion ($\tau\_{\mathrm{recruit}}$), and effector arrest ($\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$):

$$\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}(m) = \frac{m}{\dot{D}} \mkern5mu\ge\mkern5mu \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{cell}} := \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{recruit}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}.$$

In plain words, cellular backup must arrive before sustained attack drains the tissue's reserve margin $m$ to zero. Here $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{cell}}$ tracks cellular latency, never conscious deliberation (distinct from institutional $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ in [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)).

Chronic activation bends both clocks downward through allostatic wear (Sterling & Eyer 1988; McEwen 1998; generalizing [Chapter 26](26-regulation-and-homeostasis.md)'s cumulative time-integral overload $A(T)$ to load-indexed capacity). Persistent inflammation erodes capacity ($dC/dL < 0$), steepening margin loss ($dm/dL = dC/dL - 1 < -1$). Under linear capacity wear $C(L) = C\_0 - \kappa L$ ($C\_0, \kappa > 0$) inside positive margin $L < C\_0 / (1 + \kappa)$: holding a fixed future margin drawdown rate $\dot{D} \equiv -\dot{m} > 0$ subtracts $\Delta\mathrm{TTS} = \kappa L / \dot{D}$ from remaining survival time relative to unworn capacity $C\_0 - L$, whereas if inflammatory load rises dynamically at rate $\dot{L} > 0$, net margin drains faster at $-\dot{m} = (1 + \kappa)\dot{L}$, shortening $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{wear}} = m/\bigl((1+\kappa)\dot{L}\bigr)$ relative to $\kappa = 0$ by the strictly larger amount $\Delta\mathrm{TTS} = \kappa C\_0 / \bigl((1 + \kappa)\dot{L}\bigr)$.

Host defense also races pathogen doubling ([Layer 17: Infectious Disease Epidemiology, Phylodynamics & Outbreak Surveillance Order](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md)). With pathogen and effector cell counts $P\_0, E\_0 > 0$, growth rates $r\_P, r\_E > 0$, critical load thresholds $P\_{\mathrm{crit}} > P\_0$ and $E\_{\mathrm{crit}} > E\_0$ (distinct from probability $P(\cdot)$ above), and activation delay $\tau\_{\mathrm{lag}} > 0$, adaptive timing $T\_{\mathrm{imm}} = \tau\_{\mathrm{lag}} + r\_E^{-1}\ln(E\_{\mathrm{crit}}/E\_0)$ races pathogen lethality $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{pathogen}} = r\_P^{-1}\ln(P\_{\mathrm{crit}}/P\_0)$. Whenever $T\_{\mathrm{imm}} > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{pathogen}}$ (and unconditionally when $\tau\_{\mathrm{lag}} \ge \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{pathogen}}$), the host fails despite sharp receptor matching, echoing [Chapter 47, *Irreversible Time, Non-Ergodicity, and the Vital Margin*](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) and Canguilhem's dynamic health norms (Canguilhem 1943/1966).

## 3. The cost lands on the party that did not decide

Here, $T\_7$ (`T7Liability`) exposes how local immune dispatch without an out-of-band tissue veto shifts collateral damage onto target organs: first via somatic signal-integration blindness inside the lymph node, and then via institutional split ledgers when clinical therapies strip peripheral brakes. Write whole-body false-positive and false-negative costs as $C\_{\mathrm{FP}}^{\mathrm{org}}, L\_{\mathrm{FN}}^{\mathrm{org}} > 0$ (Pauker & Kassirer 1975). Because lymph-node priming samples only fraction $\alpha\_{\mathrm{int}}^{\mathrm{loc}} \in (0, 1)$ of distal parenchymal reserve $m(t)$ within an acute response window (an architectural feedback asymmetry shaped by rapid-pathogen selection rather than clonal utility pricing), its effective local trigger threshold $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{loc}}$ falls strictly below the organismal optimum $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{org}}$:

$$p^\ast\_{\mathrm{org}} = \frac{C\_{\mathrm{FP}}^{\mathrm{org}}}{C\_{\mathrm{FP}}^{\mathrm{org}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}}^{\mathrm{org}}}, \qquad p^\ast\_{\mathrm{loc}} = \frac{\alpha\_{\mathrm{int}}^{\mathrm{loc}}\mkern3mu C\_{\mathrm{FP}}^{\mathrm{org}}}{\alpha\_{\mathrm{int}}^{\mathrm{loc}}\mkern3mu C\_{\mathrm{FP}}^{\mathrm{org}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}}^{\mathrm{org}}} \mkern5mu<\mkern5mu p^\ast\_{\mathrm{org}}.$$

Read aloud, incomplete local sampling of downstream organ reserve triggers effector dispatch at lower suspicion than the whole body can safely afford, dumping collateral injury onto silent bystander tissues that lack a pre-action halt (`T7Liability`).

Peripheral restraint is still built in upfront rather than purely improvised after damage. Sakaguchi et al. (1995) proved that $\mathrm{CD4}^{+}\mathrm{CD25}^{+}$ regulatory T cells exist **constitutively *ex ante*** in healthy hosts: co-transferring them early prevents autoimmunity, whereas delayed delivery days later fails to halt organ damage. Given net effector damage rate $\gamma\_{\mathrm{eff}} > 0$, brake delay $\tau\_{\mathrm{brake}} > 0$, and critical tissue reserve $m\_{\mathrm{crit}} > 0$, braking fails once $\gamma\_{\mathrm{eff}}\mkern3mu\tau\_{\mathrm{brake}} > m\_{\mathrm{crit}}$.

Why does biological braking lag? Endogenous brakes run **in-band** (paracrine `IL-2` expanding `Tregs`, constitutive `CTLA-4` outcompeting `CD28` for `CD80/CD86`, and `PD-1/PD-L1` checkpoint inhibition) on the exact channels driven upward by necrosis $c$. Unlike Beer's **out-of-band** algedonic alarm $S3^\ast$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$) in [Chapter 40, *Institution, Incentive, Metric*](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) and [Chapter 49, *Moral Luck, Apt Agency, and Institutional Design*](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) (Beer 1972), target tissues transmit only local paracrine distress signals and lack an autonomous out-of-band veto able to abort central dispatch once $c \ge \theta\_c$.

Cancer checkpoint blockade shows what stripping those brakes releases across [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md). Blocking `CTLA-4` lowers `Signal 2` priming bars and weakens `Tregs`, while blocking `PD-1/PD-L1` strips peripheral tissue restraint, awakening antitumor immunity alongside immune-related adverse events (`irAEs`). Checkpoint hepatotoxicity triggers acute immune liver injury distinct from idiopathic autoimmune hepatitis, causing fast drawdown ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{eff}}\mkern3mu\tau\_{\mathrm{brake}} > m\_{\mathrm{crit}}$) (Cunningham, Gupta, & Butler 2024), while checkpoint arthritis sustains activated `CD8+` T-cell and macrophage synovitis driving long-term allostatic wear ($dm/dL < -1$) even after therapy stops (Kim & Bass 2026). Whereas lymph-node priming lowers $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{loc}} < p^\ast\_{\mathrm{org}}$ by discounting collateral $C\_{\mathrm{FP}}^{\mathrm{org}}$, clinical checkpoint prescribing creates an institutional split ledger in [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md): when an institution internalizes only fraction $\alpha\_{\mathrm{int}}^{\mathrm{inst}} \in (0, 1)$ of patient toxicity/false-negative harm $L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$, its intervention cutoff $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{inst}} := C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/(C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + \alpha\_{\mathrm{int}}^{\mathrm{inst}} L\_{\mathrm{FN}})$ rises strictly above the patient's Pauker–Kassirer threshold $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/(C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}})$. Unleashed T-cell assays may meet clinical efficacy targets (**test competence**), yet licensing regimes with $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{inst}} > p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ externalize unconsented `irAE` harm onto patients and bystander organs along external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$), violating **clinical aptness** ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md)).

Nor can immune effectors testify to reasons: clinicians inherit only downstream pathology (titers, biopsies, complement). Unlike [Layer 21: Linguistic, Evidential & Pragmatic Order](71-layer-linguistic-evidential-pragmatic-order.md), where hearers drop out along transmission, effectors never had a spoken addressee. Judging therapy *ex post* by tumor shrinkage or `irAE` scars commits outcome bias, whereas *ex ante* clinical margin auditing ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)) checks baseline reserve $m(0)$, monitoring frequency, and rescue timing ($\tau\_{\mathrm{brake}}$ vs. $m\_{\mathrm{crit}}$) before dosing.

## 4. One site per axis, and no second channel

Signature `(1, 1, 1)` puts one non-substitutable guard on each axis ($k\_1 = k\_2 = k\_3 = 1$: `T2Severity`, `T5Temporal`, `T7Liability`), where tuning one threshold collides with `Treg` restraint and bystander protection.

Removing any axis collapses immune regulation ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)):

- **Blind Undersizing (`T2Severity`):** Inflammatory saturation ($p\_c \to 1^-$) or unprojected peripheral epitopes ($q = 0$) break discrimination (*falsified if* self-attack stays absent without conjunctive checks).
- **Impotent Lucidity (`T5Temporal`):** Allostatic erosion ($dm/dL < -1$), slow brake activation ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{eff}}\mkern3mu\tau\_{\mathrm{brake}} > m\_{\mathrm{crit}}$), or $T\_{\mathrm{imm}} > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{pathogen}}$ drains reserves (*falsified if* overdue responses clear unaided).
- **Cynical Capture (`T7Liability`):** Local architectural blindness ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{loc}} < p^\ast\_{\mathrm{org}}$) lacking an out-of-band veto and clinical split ledgers ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{inst}} > p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$) spill harm onto parenchymal and patient reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) (*falsified if* checkpoint blockade or split ledgers protect organs without pre-dose reserve caps or out-of-band halts).

Descending in $\mathcal{G}\_1$ from [Layer 11](61-layer-biochemical-kinetic-molecular-order.md) and [Layer 12](62-layer-morphogenetic-developmental-order.md) (and exporting solely to [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md)), `L13` closes all seven sites across $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{13})$ despite omitting local `T3Archive`, which enters $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{13})$ strictly through `L11`'s upstream parents (`L04, L08`) rather than `L12` (whose cone lacks `T3`), explaining why thymic `AIRE` sampling suffers $q = 0$ omissions unless peripherally buffered ([Appendix B.2](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b2-the-multilayer-graph--35-orders-7-strata-5-exposure-regimes)). **Failure boundary:** adaptive control breaks when inflammation sends $p\_c \to 1^-$, tissue microexons vanish ($q = 0$) during injury, `AIRE`, `Tregs`, or `CTLA-4`/`PD-1` fail, or $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{pathogen}} < T\_{\mathrm{imm}}$. Passive physical barriers and specialized mucosal or fetal tolerance sit outside this loop, while mild reversible exposures cleared without cytolytic harm need no systemic activation (Brake 2 in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)).
