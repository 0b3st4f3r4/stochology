## 1. The rule of three and the trial that cannot see the harm

Consider a newly licensed drug that clears pre-market trials with three thousand volunteers and zero cases of fatal liver failure. Once approved, two million everyday patients fill prescriptions—and within months, emergency wards begin logging rare acute liver injuries. Pre-market testing checks common benefits in small, curated cohorts; post-market safety must watch for rare harms across an open population.

Across $n \ge 1$ independent subjects with complete follow-up and zero adverse events, [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md) fixes the one-sided $0.05$ binomial upper bound $p\_{\mathrm{upper}}(n) \equiv \theta\_{\mathrm{upper}}(n) = 1 - 0.05^{1/n}$. Its large-$n$ Poisson limit is $-\ln(0.05)/n \approx 3/n$, though both fail under differential censoring, unequal person-time, cluster correlation $\mathrm{DEFF} > 1$ ([Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md)), or heterogeneous susceptibility.

Turn the question around for a rare harm occurring at per-patient probability $p \in (0, 1)$. Expected event count hits one ($\mathbb{E}[X] = np = 1$) at sample size $n = 1/p$, yet detection probability is still only $1 - (1 - p)^{1/p} \approx 1 - e^{-1}$. Because $\ln(1 - p) < -p$, achieving a ninety-five per cent chance of observing **at least one** event ($1 - (1 - p)^n \ge 0.95$) requires $n \ge \ln(0.05)/\ln(1 - p)$, safely met by $n \ge -\ln(0.05)/p \approx 3/p$ ($\mathbb{E}[X] \approx 3$). Licensing screens a few thousand volunteers for common effects, whereas release exposes millions—turning surveillance into a new instrument on a different population.

## 2. Sequential *e*-processes, disproportionality, and unseen ascertainment

### Sequential monitoring with *e*-processes

Guard site `T2Severity` keeps continuous post-market monitoring valid so regulators can inspect incoming safety reports week by week without inflating false alarms. Safety filings arrive cumulatively rather than in neat fixed batches, and $m \ge 1$ repeated fixed-horizon checks at level $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ break Type I error control under optional peeking.

Anytime-valid safety monitoring replaces repeated *p*-values with a non-negative test supermartingale, or ***e*-process** $(E\_t)\_{t \ge 1}$, adapted to filtration $(\mathcal{F}\_t)\_{t \ge 1}$. The process starts at $E\_0 = 1$ with $\mathbb{E}\_P[E\_\tau] \le 1$ at any stopping time $\tau$ across composite null $P \in \mathcal{P}\_0$. Crossing threshold $E\_t \ge 1/\alpha$ caps anytime false-alarm probability at $\alpha$ by Ville's maximal inequality (Ville 1939; Ramdas, Grünwald, Vovk & Shafer 2023; [Chapter 30](30-probability-as-language.md)).

A fixed deterministic per-look ratio cannot exceed one under $\mathcal{P}\_0$. Instead, sequential disproportionality across cumulative $2 \times 2$ tables $(a\_t, b\_t, c\_t, d\_t)$ multiplies sequential *e*-variables $E\_t = \prod\_{s=1}^t e\_s$ ($e\_s \ge 0$, $\mathbb{E}\_P[e\_s \mid \mathcal{F}\_{s-1}] \le 1$) built against the reverse-information-projection null prior under unknown baseline rates. Expected stopping time then scales inversely with Kullback–Leibler divergence rather than a fixed look schedule (Grünwald, de Heide & Koolen 2024).

### Unseen events in spontaneous databases

Sequential validity on `T2Severity` does not certify archival completeness on `T3Archive`. Within a single spontaneous database recording singleton reaction-class count $f\_1 \ge 0$ and doubleton count $f\_2 \ge 0$, Chao's (1984) abundance bound gives $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao1}} = f\_1^2/(2f\_2)$ for $f\_2 > 0$ (and bias-corrected $f\_1(f\_1 - 1)/\bigl(2(f\_2 + 1)\bigr)$ when $f\_2 = 0$; [Layer 23](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md)). That bound estimates unseen **reaction classes** under heterogeneous detection ($M\_h$, $\pi\_i > 0$).

Bounding unseen **adverse-event cases** across $t \ge 2$ linked reporting streams requires multi-list capture–recapture: $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(t) = \frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{f\_1^2}{2f\_2}$ when $f\_2 > 0$. At $t = 2$ this simplifies to $f\_1^2/(4f\_2)$, and when $f\_2 = 0$ to $\frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{f\_1(f\_1 - 1)}{2(f\_2 + 1)}$ (Chao 1987; Chao 1989; Chao & Colwell 2017; [Layer 17](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md)). Duplicate reports inflate $f\_2$ and bias both bounds downward, positive list dependence deflates $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}$, and zero-detection strata ($\pi\_i = 0$ under $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md)) remain invisible to both.

## 3. The signal the archive licensed

Rosiglitazone shows how symmetric efficacy conventions misread a post-market safety signal. Pooling published and unpublished trials designed primarily for glycemic control rather than cardiovascular outcomes, Nissen & Wolski (2007) reported a myocardial infarction odds ratio of 1.43 (95% confidence interval $[1.03, 1.98]$). On the log scale, $[\ln 1.03,\mkern3mu \ln 1.98]$ spans width $\ln(1.98/1.03)$ with lower half-width $\ln(1.43/1.03)$, clearing $\ln 1 = 0$ by only $\ln(1.03)$. Small changes in zero-event trial rules or reporting shift that lower bound across one without refuting harm. Judging safety by whether a two-sided interval excludes unity imports an efficacy proof burden; pharmacovigilance requires asymmetric post-data severity (Mayo 2018; [Chapter 50, *Five Methodological Families, Process Tracing, and Reflexive Self-Application*](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)) conditioned on archive provenance.

Two provenance features limit corroboration. First, spontaneous reporting suffers pervasive under-reporting that shifts across clinical settings, reaction severity, and drug–reaction pairs (Hazell & Shakir 2006), leaving raw counts without exposure denominators. Second, epidemiological corroboration is a coherence judgment rather than a mechanical filter. Austin Bradford Hill (1965) framed his nine considerations—including biological gradient, temporality, consistency, and coherence—as "nine different viewpoints" rather than "hard-and-fast rules" or *sine qua non* criteria. He emphasized that incomplete causal certainty never justifies delaying proportionate protection; surviving coherence review triggers a `T7Liability` veto rather than awaiting exhaustive causal proof (Hill 1965).

## 4. Severity, selection bias, and disproportionality on spontaneous archives

Guard site `T3Archive` checks who filed an adverse-event report and who went unrecorded before raw database counts are read as comparative drug risk. Spontaneous archives sample filing behavior ($R \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$, filing share $f = P(R = 1) \in (0, 1)$) over harm $Y$ rather than counting all exposed patients.

By Meng's (2018) Big Data Paradox identity ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md); [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md); [Chapter 45, *From World to Archive: Evidential Weight, Big Data Paradox, and the Uncounted*](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)), selective reporting ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$) sharpens apparent precision around $E[Y \mid R = 1]$. Yet it never closes location bias $E[Y \mid R = 1] - E[Y]$. That gap forces Manski (2003) partial-identification bounds over unfiled strata ($R = 0$). Coding precedes counting as well: standardized dictionaries absorb atypical severe reactions into coarse mild terms, so changing granularity creates a new variable rather than a larger sample (Bowker & Star 1999). Because dramatic harms draw filings while disease-like events stay silent, reporting rates vary across drugs and contexts (Hazell & Shakir 2006), barring any uniform multiplier.

Disproportionality metrics inherit that conditioning without person-time denominators. Consider a $2 \times 2$ archive table counting reports for the target drug with the reaction ($a$), the target drug with other reactions ($b$), and comparator drugs with ($c$) and without ($d$) the reaction, where $a, b, c, d > 0$. From these four cell counts, define the Proportional Reporting Ratio ($\mathrm{PRR}$) and Reporting Odds Ratio ($\mathrm{ROR}$):

$$\mathrm{PRR} = \frac{a/(a+b)}{c/(c+d)}, \qquad \mathrm{ROR} = \frac{ad}{bc}.$$

In plain words, $\mathrm{PRR}$ compares the reaction's share among all reports for the target drug against its share for other drugs, while $\mathrm{ROR}$ computes the cross-product odds ratio of those four archive cells.

Now let true event counts $(A, B, C, D)$ pass through cell reporting probabilities $(r\_{11}, r\_{10}, r\_{21}, r\_{20}) \in (0, 1]^4$. Then $\mathrm{PRR}$ remains invariant only under uniform reporting **within each drug arm** ($r\_{11} = r\_{10}$ and $r\_{21} = r\_{20}$). If dramatic reactions are reported more often than minor ones across both drug columns ($r\_{11} = r\_{21} \ne r\_{10} = r\_{20}$), $\mathrm{PRR}$ distorts whenever $\mathrm{PRR} \ne 1$, and competitive flooding in cell $b$ masks signals.

While $\mathrm{ROR}$ cancels separable drug-and-reaction multipliers ($r\_{11}r\_{20} = r\_{10}r\_{21}$), raw ratios still spike wildly on sparse counts without shrinkage. Moreover, **both** $\mathrm{PRR}$ and $\mathrm{ROR}$—plus sequential *e*-processes built on them—fail under drug-by-reaction interaction reporting ($r\_{11}r\_{20} \ne r\_{10}r\_{21}$, such as media notoriety bias), because voluntary databases record suspicions rather than exposed person-time. Active denominator-linked cohorts and randomized post-market trials ([Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md); [Layer 17](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md)) recover rates subject to confounding and censoring, yet lack power for ultra-rare harms (§1), keeping spontaneous surveillance vital inside its boundary.

## 5. Vital axial exposure `(2, 0, 1)`, regulatory recall veto, and sibling couplings

Guard site `T7Liability` ensures someone holds clear legal duty and fast-acting veto power to pause or recall a suspect therapy when corporate incentives favor delay.

### The timing race when vital clocks are unguarded

Locally at `(2, 0, 1)`, `L18` holds `{T2Severity, T3Archive, T7Liability}` across $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ and $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ while leaving $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ empty (`T4Formalization` and `T5Temporal` are absent locally). Without a survival clock, recall becomes a temporal race under **Collapse Proposition 2 (*Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin*)** ([Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md); [Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 47, *Irreversible Time, Non-Ergodicity, and the Vital Margin*](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)). Let $f\_{\mathrm{harm}} \in (0, 1]$ be the harmed share per exposure unit and $E(t) > 0$ be cumulative exposure. Waiting accrues irreversible injury scaling as $f\_{\mathrm{harm}}\mkern3mu E(t)$ whenever survival headroom drops below effective response latency ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$) over sealed archive state $\mathcal{I}\_t$.

Withdrawal cost stays bounded at suspension when clinical substitutes exist, or when **Regime II conditional access** preserves essential therapy pending **reversible confirmatory re-entry** ([Chapter 36, *Decision, Loss, Threshold*](36-decision-loss-threshold.md); [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Keeping temporary pauses corrigible defends against notoriety-driven false alarms under $r\_{11}r\_{20} \ne r\_{10}r\_{21}$, whereas without substitutes withholding also grows with unmet need $E\_{\mathrm{need}}(t)$.

In distributed polycentric systems, an exact structural isomorphism governs revocable node reintegration ([Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md)). A quarantined unit re-enters only through bounded canary and post-reintegration pharmacovigilance regimes carrying zero voting or witness weight. Upon recurrence, relapse backoff scales exponentially as $T\_{\mathrm{vigil}}(g) = T\_{\mathrm{base}}\,2^{g-1}$, where $T\_{\mathrm{base}} > 0$ is the baseline probation window and $g \in \mathbb{Z}\_{\ge 1}$ is the exile generation.

### Split risk-bearing, normalized anomaly, and withheld studies

Split risk-bearing entrenches delay. Limited liability caps manufacturer downside (Innes 1990) and hidden caution generates moral hazard (Holmström 1979), while patients bear unrecorded injuries and regulators face immediate withdrawal litigation. Along $\mathcal{G}\_1$, `L18` inherits `T1Framing` from [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md), `T4Formalization` from [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md), and `T5Temporal` from [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md) and [Layer 17](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md). By contrast, **`T6Performative` is absent from `L18` and all three direct parents**, entering only transitively upstream ([Appendix B.2](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b2-the-multilayer-graph--35-orders-7-strata-5-exposure-regimes)).

Consequently, reporting duties couple `T7Liability` back into `T3Archive` and `T2Severity` (Perdomo et al. 2020; Goodhart 1975). Defensive flooding of minor filings inflates $b$, diluting $\mathrm{PRR}$ on `T2` and jamming heavy-traffic review queues ($\rho/(1 - \rho)$, [Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md)) past bounded human deliberative capacity ([Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)) across $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ and $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$. Conversely, minimal filing and testimonial discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$; Fricker 2007) bury warnings unless an autonomous algedonic channel $S3^\ast$ guarantees $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ (Beer 1972).

Anomaly normalization (Vaughan 1996) and regulatory toxicology agnotology compound that blindness (Proctor & Schiebinger 2008). Schäffer et al. (2023) documented 9 of 35 (26%) industry developmental neurotoxicity studies withheld from European regulators, alongside only 1 of 1,932 European chemical dossiers above 1,000 tonnes per year compliant across all endpoints. Similarly, Gupta et al. (2024) traced 15 articles cited across 3,666 Opioid Industry Documents Archive records supporting 5 inaccurate prescription opioid safety and effectiveness claims.

### Pre-committed brakes and triadic falsification
