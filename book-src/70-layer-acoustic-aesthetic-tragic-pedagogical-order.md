## 1. The comma analogy: why a frame-bound residue cannot be abolished

Try tuning a keyboard instrument by stepping upward twelve times in acoustically pure fifths. When you arrive at the note that should match your starting pitch seven octaves higher, the two notes noticeably clash. That leftover gap is not a sloppy tuner's mistake; it is an arithmetic mismatch built into forcing a continuous ratio into a fixed grid.

Guard site `T4Formalization` marks where a tidy formal grid—whether keyboard semitones or classroom grading rubrics—leaves an irreducible mismatch that cannot be eliminated inside the grid. Keeping tuning's commas strictly as mathematical residual analogies separates acoustic, tragic, and educational scales. Plato's *Philebus* (56a) ranks Greek melodic tuning among conjectural arts guided by practiced ear (*melétē*) rather than rigid mechanical gauge (Plato *c.* 360 BCE), operating across melodic intervals without later triadic polyphony.

### Pythagorean and syntonic commas

On a logarithmic pitch scale measured in cents ($1200\log\_2$ per octave), a pure fifth has frequency ratio $3/2$ and a pure octave has ratio $2/1$. Because $3$ and $2$ share no prime factor, twelve pure fifths overshoot seven octaves ($2^7$, giving ratio $3^{12}/2^{19}$) by the **Pythagorean comma**:

$$1200 \cdot 12 \log\_2(3/2) - 1200 \cdot 7 = 1200\log\_2\frac{3^{12}}{2^{19}} \approx 23.46\ \text{cents},$$

In plain words, twelve pure fifths overshoot seven octaves by $3^{12}/2^{19} = 531{,}441/524{,}288$, or roughly a quarter of a semitone ($23.46$ cents), leaving a **narrowed** twelfth wolf fifth $1200\log\_2(2^{18}/3^{11}) \approx 678.49\ \text{cents}$.

Separately in early-modern triadic polyphony, requiring a pure major third ($5/4$) produces the **syntonic comma** after four pure fifths versus two octaves and a third:

$$\frac{(3/2)^{4}}{2^{2}\cdot 5/4} = \frac{81}{80}, \qquad 1200\log\_2\frac{81}{80} \approx 21.51\ \text{cents},$$

Read aloud, four fifths exceed two octaves plus a pure major third by ratio $81/80$ ($21.51$ cents), because the prime factor $5$ never cancels powers of $2$ and $3$.

### Equal temperament, meantone, and the analogy limit

Fixed-grid keyboards must distribute those commas across keys, unlike fretless strings or voices that adjust pitch dynamically by ear (*melétē*). Equal temperament splits the octave into twelve $100\text{-cent}$ semitones using exact ratio $2^{1/12}$, a geometric division first solved by Ming theorist Zhu Zaiyu before European keyboard temperaments (Cho 2003). That rule narrows fifths from $1200\log\_2(3/2) \approx 701.96\ \text{cents}$ to $7 \times 100 = 700\ \text{cents}$ (a $1.96\text{-cent}$ trim) while widening major thirds by $400 - 1200\log\_2(5/4) \approx 13.69\ \text{cents}$.

By contrast, quarter-comma meantone trims fifths by $\frac{1}{4}\cdot 1200\log\_2(81/80) = 300\log\_2(81/80) \approx 5.38\ \text{cents}$ to achieve pure $5/4$ thirds, creating a **widened** wolf fifth $1200\log\_2(2^{7}/5^{11/4}) \approx 737.64\ \text{cents}$, while well-temperament spreads the comma unequally across keys (Werckmeister 1691). **Analogy limit:** temperament redistributes mismatches on a one-dimensional log-frequency circle, whereas Stochology's three axes remain non-compensatory under $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$ and irreversible deadlines ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$).

## 2. Attic tragedy as a circuit

Kant's (1790) determinative judgment subsumes particulars under pre-given rules, whereas reflective judgment stays open to the singular case without erasing Adorno's (1966) Non-Identical. Aristotle (*c.* 335 BCE, *Poetics* 1452a29–33, 1453a7–10) characterizes *hamartía* as tragic error distinct from vice or depravity (*Poetics* 1453a7–10) and defines *anagnórisis* as recognition turning ignorance into knowledge (1452a29–33). Pairing tragedy with Canguilhem's (1943/1966) vital normativity marks when rigid rules require rewriting rather than parameter tuning, without ever equating cynical capture with honest *hamartía*.

Classical drama staged all three structural collapses across distinct plots:

- ***Oedipus Tyrannus* (Collapse 1, `T1Framing`):** Inquiry omits the investigator's own position inside the investigated frame until *hamartía* culminates in *anagnórisis* (*c.* 429 BCE).
- ***Agamemnon* (Collapse 3, inherited `T7Liability` via $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{20})$):** Cassandra delivers exact foresight under prophetic discredit $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1$ (Aeschylus 458 BCE, ll. 1072–1330, esp. ll. 1202–1212).
- ***Antigone* (Collapse 3 precipitating Collapse 2, inherited `T7Liability` and `T5Temporal` via $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{20})$):** Creon dismisses the Guard and Haemon and seals Antigone alive before Tiresias arrives; even after relenting from his initial bribery accusation against Tiresias, delayed rescue leaves $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ (*c.* 441 BCE).

By contrast, in *Philoctetes* (409 BCE; Sophocles *c.* 441 / *c.* 429 / 409 BCE; cf. [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md)), passive abstention leaves coercion intact. Neoptolemus is forced into an indivisible Regime III ethical rupture rather than private *anagnórisis*: returning the bow breaks Odysseus's fraud and forces public contestation of instrumental rules (broken dramatically only by Heracles's *exodos* command).

## 3. The measure that absorbs the effort

Here, `T1Framing` (`T1*`) and `T6Performative` govern what teaching and testing frame upfront before effort allocation begins. Chevallard's (1985) didactic transposition moves scholarly inquiry (*savoir savant*) into teachable classroom forms (*savoir enseigné*), collapsing into narrow frame exclusion (`Not askable`) and mechanical drill when closed off from discovery. Under Messick's (1995) unified framework ([Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md)), educational assessment faces **construct underrepresentation**, **construct-irrelevant variance**, and a **consequential aspect**: test coaching inflates observed scores without lowering [Layer 08](58-layer-metrological-information-geometric-order.md)'s non-vanishing **Type-B systematic construct floor $u\_B$** or `CORP–PAV` rank miscalibration, leading reformers to pair high-stakes tests with low-stakes audits (Campbell 1979; Klein et al. 2000; Koretz 2008).

### Why composite audit scores fail under effort allocation

Triangulated audits still fail whenever they sit inside weighted composite scores $w M\_{\mathrm{high}} + (1 - w) M\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ ($w \in [1/2, 1)$) with effort cost slopes $c\_{\mathrm{high}}, c\_{\mathrm{audit}} > 0$. Normalizing output to unit effort productivity ($M\_i = e\_i \ge 0$, per unit score), under a shared linear effort budget $c\_{\mathrm{high}} e\_{\mathrm{high}} + c\_{\mathrm{audit}} e\_{\mathrm{audit}} \le B$ linear effort targets the audit only when $c\_{\mathrm{audit}}/c\_{\mathrm{high}} < (1 - w)/w$. Under separable quadratic effort cost $C(e\_{\mathrm{high}}, e\_{\mathrm{audit}}) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(c\_{\mathrm{high}} e\_{\mathrm{high}}^2 + c\_{\mathrm{audit}} e\_{\mathrm{audit}}^2\bigr)$—which channels effort trade-offs strictly through the weight simplex $w + (1 - w) = 1$ without Holmström & Milgrom's (1991) cross-effort substitution term $\delta e\_{\mathrm{high}} e\_{\mathrm{audit}}$—maximizing $w e\_{\mathrm{high}} + (1 - w)e\_{\mathrm{audit}} - C(e\_{\mathrm{high}}, e\_{\mathrm{audit}})$ sets efforts $e^{\ast}\_{\mathrm{high}} = w/c\_{\mathrm{high}} > 0$ and $e^{\ast}\_{\mathrm{audit}} = (1 - w)/c\_{\mathrm{audit}}$.

The resulting **composite score contributions** scale as $w e^{\ast}\_{\mathrm{high}} = w^2/c\_{\mathrm{high}}$ versus $(1 - w)e^{\ast}\_{\mathrm{audit}} = (1 - w)^2/c\_{\mathrm{audit}}$, tightening the threshold for the audit's **score share** to dominate (without forcing $e^{\ast}\_{\mathrm{high}} = 0$) to $c\_{\mathrm{audit}}/c\_{\mathrm{high}} < \bigl((1 - w)/w\bigr)^2 \le (1 - w)/w$. Because real-understanding audits are harder to game ($c\_{\mathrm{audit}} > c\_{\mathrm{high}}$) at high stakes ($w \in [1/2, 1)$), $c\_{\mathrm{audit}}/c\_{\mathrm{high}} > 1 \ge (1 - w)/w$ never holds. Only unpredictable, consequence-free sampling kept off ranking tables **and paired with hard caps on mandatory drill time** protects validity against instructional-time crowding (Campbell 1979; Klein et al. 2000; Koretz 2008; [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md)).

### Spiral pacing versus board-exam drill

True formation unfolds in Vygotsky's (1978) Zone of Proximal Development (`ZPD`) $[z\_{\mathrm{unaided}}, z\_{\mathrm{assisted}}]$. It advances along Bruner's (1960) spiral curriculum through **enactive**, **iconic**, and **symbolic** shifts (Bruner 1964; Bruner 1966; Chevallard 1985) and Freire's (1970) dialogical *conscientização*, where learners co-interrogate reality as subjects rather than receiving passive deposits under "banking" pedagogy (compounded here by metric surveillance). Longitudinal pacing unfolds across sessions, whereas live probing and instructional interfaces saturate at [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)'s conscious supervisory throughput ceiling ($\sim 10\text{ bits/s}$; Zheng & Meister 2025).

Compressing spiral returns into timed exam drills induces Messick's (1995) construct underrepresentation and Koretz's (2008) score inflation. Maltagliati et al. (2023) compared three pre-clerkship medical cohorts on a 1–10 Likert scale (reporting parametric means and standard deviations over an ordinal 1–10 scale, distinct from [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md)'s ordinal-invariance rule): historical controls from 2020 ($n = 46$, 36% response rate) and 2021 ($n = 52$, 45% response rate, already compressed from 24 to 18 months) versus an intervention cohort in 2022 ($n = 40$, 32% response rate). The 2022 cohort received 500 ungraded board-style questions across 32 mandatory 1-hour in-class sessions of 10–20 questions at 90 seconds each (Maltagliati et al. 2023).

Spiral-retention approval fell from 8.2 (SD 1.6 in 2020; SD 2.2 in 2021) to neutral 5.0 (SD 2.9 in 2022; $p < 0.000005$) (Maltagliati et al. 2023). Perceived wellness time also dropped from the 24-month cohort (2020: 7.2, SD 2.4) to the 18-month cohorts (2021: 5.3, SD 2.3; 2022: 4.8, SD 2.5; $p < 0.005$). Self-rated retention remained neutral across 2020–2022 (5.0 [SD 2.4], 4.6 [SD 2.0], 4.2 [SD 1.9]; $p = 0.15$). USMLE Step 1 averages showed no significant gain either (224 [SD 18], 229 [SD 21], 227 [SD 20]; $p = 0.21$), confounded by cohort compression while students favored self-directed study (Maltagliati et al. 2023). Because pre-clerkship learners are training for [Layer 16](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md)'s four-coordinate clinical judgment and *kairós* triage, mandatory drill crowding—even when ungraded—degrades spiral synthesis and accumulates trainee allostatic wear ($W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t}$) before bedside care begins.
