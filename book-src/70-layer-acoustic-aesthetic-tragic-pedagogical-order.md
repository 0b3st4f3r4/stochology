The order this chapter carries, with the guard sites its triple fixes and the collapse
that triple yields when the order runs without its ancestors:

- `L20` · `S4` · `{T1Framing, T4Formalization, T6Performative}` · `(1, 1, 1)` · trans-triadic keystone · intact triad

`L20` is declared here as a **composite order** uniting three distinct components under that single guard triple: (i) acoustic and aesthetic temperament, (ii) tragic recognition and withheld action, and (iii) pedagogical transposition and formative probing. What the three components share is structural and negative: each faces an irreducible distribution of residuals under a named reference frame, and each governs intervention through a reversible fractional probe rather than a forced closure. What they do not share is equally explicit: acoustics and aesthetics, Attic tragedy, and pedagogy do not reduce to one another, carry no scale isomorphism across tuning intervals, dramatic catharsis, and didactic transposition, and admit no common normalized score across their domains.

3. Audit educational metrics with Chevallard's didactic transposition and Messick's validity triad, and say where the audit stops binding.

## 1. The comma cannot be abolished

A pure fifth is $3/2$ and a pure octave $2/1$, and by the fundamental theorem of arithmetic no power of three is
ever a power of two. Twelve fifths overshoot seven octaves, and the residue is the Pythagorean comma

$$1200 \cdot 12 \log\_2(3/2) - 1200 \cdot 7 = 1200\log\_2\frac{3^{12}}{2^{19}} \approx 23.46\ \text{cents},$$

which is the same interval read as a ratio, $3^{12}/2^{19}$, and converted at twelve hundred cents to the octave. A tuning that insists on exact fifths across eleven steps does not remove the discrepancy. It
concentrates it into the twelfth, where it is unusable. What temperament buys is distribution, not
accuracy: a slack inaudible in every interval, uniform enough to let the instrument move between keys.

The same conservation holds on a series with nothing to do with tuning. Half-hourly demand has a spread around a
single mean and a smaller spread around a profile indexed by weekday and half-hour, which leaves a share of the
variance untouched. The misfit was moved, not removed. A bin's own mean is the best summary it admits, and a
profile that absorbs the calendar absorbs only what the calendar explains.

## 2. Attic tragedy as a circuit

Kant's determinative judgment holds a fixed rule and subsumes the particular under it. That is the naive
optimiser. Reflective judgment has the singular particular alone and must orient action
without amputating what Adorno called the Non-Identical. Attic tragedy made the failure of determinative
rigidity its object: *hamartía* means missing the target, and the surviving plays dramatise the three collapse
propositions.

In *Oedipus Tyrannus* relentless calculative intelligence is applied to the external target while the reflexive
invariants of the agent's own frame stay unexamined. The investigator turns out to be the omitted variable.
In *Agamemnon* Cassandra has calibrated foresight and no authority to stop the ship. In *Antigone* Creon
yields to Tiresias only after sealing Antigone alive, so the rescue arrives after survival time has expired. In
the earlier episodes of that same play the Guard and Tiresias raise the algedonic signal and Creon accuses them
of bribery. In *Philoctetes* Neoptolemus interrupts Odysseus's fraud and returns the bow, which is the
third regime: the shot is withheld.

## 3. The measure that absorbs the effort

Chevallard's didactic transposition shows living inquiry compressed into closed exercises with a single right
number, which trains students into the determinative habit (Chevallard 1985). Messick's triad names the
gates an assessment must pass — construct underrepresentation, construct-irrelevant variance, consequential
validity — and the recommendation is to triangulate with a low-stakes measure beside the high-stakes one
(Messick 1995).

That recommendation fails on one line of marginal arithmetic. Weight the two measures $w$ and $1-w$ at unit cost
each. Raising the composite by a point costs $1/w$ units of effort through the high-stakes measure and $1/(1-w)$
through the audit, so effort flows to the measure that already carries the consequence, in proportion to the ratio
of those costs. The audit binds only where its cost ratio falls below its weight ratio, and nothing harder to
game than the measure it audits satisfies that. What binds is a consequence-free measure: a sample the school cannot
predict, scored where no ranking is published (Campbell 1979; Koretz 2008). Vygotsky's (1978) zone of proximal
development states the same probe in pedagogical form. A task below what the learner already does unaided
teaches nothing. A task beyond what the assisted learner can reach teaches the wrong lesson. The productive
band is the interval between the two, a fractional commitment sized by a width rather than fixed by a
curriculum.

## 4. When the frame is what the formation makes

The conservation claim holds while the reference frame is fixed. Where the judging subject is itself produced by
the transmission there is no invariant to conserve, because the thing the residual is measured against moves
while it is being measured. An instrument at 415 Hz (Haynes 2002) and a second at 440 Hz under ISO 16 (1975) differ by

$$1200\log\_2\frac{440}{415} \approx 101.3\ \text{cents},$$

a semitone. Each is in tune against its own frame and each is a comma-error against the other. So the procedure
names the frame with every residual, measures drift rather than the residual when the frame may move, and audits
the stability of the standard rather than the size of the error. Because the frame itself belongs to each domain, the
acoustic comma, the tragic boundary, and the pedagogical zone share this procedural discipline of named frames and
reversible fractional probes without collapsing their separate scales into one another.

2. **Tragic recognition after the ruin boundary.** In *Antigone*, map Creon's refusal to hear Haemon and Tiresias onto the algedonic channel and the survival clock, and say why catharsis works as a simulated post-mortem in a sandboxed theatre.
