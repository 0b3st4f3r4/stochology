## 1. The Pythagorean comma and well-temperament as vital slack

When Plato separated the conjectural arts (*stokhastikaì tékhnai*) from the exact measuring arts in
*Philebus* 55e–56c `[Literature]`, his primary example of an art governed by trained conjecture was
music (*mousikē*). Mathematical acoustics explains why no single rigid ratio can close the musical scale.

A pure perfect fifth has frequency ratio $3/2$, and a pure octave has ratio $2/1$. Stacking twelve pure
fifths should close the circle of keys at seven octaves, yet by the fundamental theorem of arithmetic
no power of $3$ is ever a power of $2$:

$$\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{12} = \frac{531{,}441}{4{,}096} \approx 129.746 \;\neq\; 2^{7} = 128 \qquad \text{\texttt{[Assumed]}}.$$

The ratio $531{,}441 / 524{,}288 \approx 1.01364$ ($23.46\text{ cents}$ `[Assumed]`) is the **Pythagorean
comma**. A tuning system that insists on exact $3/2$ fifths across eleven steps forces the entire
$23.46\text{-cent}$ discrepancy into the twelfth interval — the howling **wolf fifth** — which makes
modulation outside a narrow set of home keys impossible (Collapse Proposition 1). **Well-temperament**
(developed by Andreas Werckmeister and celebrated in J. S. Bach's *Well-Tempered Clavier* `[Literature]`)
solves this non-closure not by pretending the comma is zero, but by distributing a small, tolerable slack
across the circle of fifths so the instrument can modulate through all twenty-four keys without breaking
while preserving the distinct character (*Non-Identical*) of each key.

## 2. Kant's reflective judgment and Attic tragedy

In the *Critique of the Power of Judgment* (1790) `[Literature]`, Immanuel Kant distinguished two modes
of judgment: **determinative judgment**, which already possesses a fixed universal rule and mechanically
subsumes the particular under it (`engine.rs::NaiveOptimizer`), and **reflective judgment**, where only
the singular, contingent particular is given and the agent must orient action without amputating what
Theodor Adorno called the **Non-Identical** (`vital.rs::from_heavy_tails_and_asymmetry`).

Five centuries before Cicero recorded Antipater's archer, Attic tragedy made the failure of determinative
rigidity its central dramatic object. The tragic word **ἁμαρτία (*hamartía*)** means literally *missing
the target (stókhos)*, and the surviving plays of Aeschylus and Sophocles dramatise the three collapse
propositions with complete structural precision:

- **Collapse Proposition 1 ($\neg S_{\mathrm{epi}}$) in *Oedipus Tyrannus*:** Oedipus applies relentless
  calculative intelligence to the external target (*skopós*) while remaining completely blind to the
  reflexive invariants of his own frame (*télos*), discovering too late that the investigator was the
  omitted variable.
- **Collapse Proposition 2 ($\neg S_{\mathrm{vital}}$) in *Agamemnon* and late *Antigone*:** Cassandra
  possesses perfectly calibrated foresight ($S_{\mathrm{epi}} = 1$) with zero authority to stop the ship;
  and in *Antigone* (vv. 1095–1276 `[Literature]`), Creon finally yields to Tiresias's warning, but
  because he eliminated all vital margin by sealing Antigone alive inside the rock tomb, his rescue party
  arrives after survival time has expired ($\mathrm{TTS}_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}_{\mathrm{recovery}}$).
- **Collapse Proposition 3 ($\neg S_{\mathrm{gov}}$) in early *Antigone* and Regime III in *Philoctetes*:**
  In Episodes I–IV of *Antigone*, when the Guard and Tiresias raise the algedonic signal, Creon accuses
  them of bribery ($\delta_{\mathrm{cred}} \to 1$) and rejects Haemon's plea that the tree that bends in
  the winter torrent preserves its branches while the rigid trunk is uprooted (*Antigone* vv. 712–718
  `[Literature]`). Conversely, in *Philoctetes*, Neoptolemus interrupts Odysseus's instrumental fraud,
  returns Heracles's bow, and exemplifies the third regime of aim (`WithholdShot`).

## 3. Didactic transposition and Messick's validity triad

When complex knowledge is transmitted through schools and assessments, Yves Chevallard's theory of
**didactic transposition** (1985) `[Literature]` shows that *savoir savant* (living inquiry with its open
uncertainties and bounds) is routinely compressed into *savoir enseigné* (closed exercises with a single
right number), training students to become `engine.rs::NaiveOptimizer`s.

Samuel Messick's unified psychometric framework (1989, 1995) `[Literature]` proves that any educational
or institutional assessment faces three non-substitutable validity gates that mirror the Nuclear Triad:
**construct underrepresentation** (omitting practical judgment $a_2$ in favour of easily scored rote speed
$a_1$, triggering Holmström–Milgrom multitasking cannibalisation in `governance.rs::ContractAndLiabilityAudit`),
**construct-irrelevant variance** ($\rho_{R,Y} \neq 0$ and group disparity in
`governance.rs::EmpiricalChouldechovaAudit`), and **consequential validity** (preventing high-stakes test
rankings from corrupting the classroom via `governance.rs::GoodhartVariant`).
