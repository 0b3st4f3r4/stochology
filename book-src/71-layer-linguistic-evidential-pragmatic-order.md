## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Say why an obligatory evidential morpheme makes an utterance a document, and distinguish that from a speaker's optional hedge by the probability that the source slot is left empty.
2. State the cost of locating the mark inside the speech act rather than beside it — unrepairability without a new act and unindexed multi-hop collapse — using the conditional survival product across reporting hops.
3. Explain why declaring a source does not transfer attribution liability even when a reportative suspends truth commitment to the reported content, and identify where downstream relay strips the mark or outruns the survival clock.

## 1. The slot the grammar does not let the speaker leave empty

Imagine reading two dispatch entries after a flash flood. The first note simply reads, *"The river bridge washed out"*—giving no hint whether the clerk saw the bridge fall, deduced it from floating timber, or repeated a rumor. In another language, however, the verb itself cannot even be finished without adding a suffix stating whether the speaker witnessed it, inferred it, or heard it secondhand.

Guard site `T3Archive` checks whether a communication system records source provenance at creation or leaves an empty slot where guesswork hides behind bare assertion. A hedge like *probably* is optional: the speaker chooses whether to add it, so silence about a source carries clear meaning only where omission is grammatical.

In obligatory evidential systems such as Tuyuca and Tariana—matching the obligatory-marking contrast in [Chapter 28](28-the-machine-and-the-deception.md)—a finite declarative verb requires a morpheme encoding how the speaker came to know: witnessed, deduced, inferred, or heard (Aikhenvald 2004). Even where the direct form is morphologically silent inside a closed paradigm, as in Cheyenne (Murray 2014, p. 2:7), that unmarked verb selects one definite value in an obligatory contrast rather than leaving the slot blank. By contrast, Cuzco Quechua uses optional focus enclitics (*=mi*/*=n*, *=si*/*=s*, *=chá*), so a bare declarative stays grammatical and pragmatically implicates best possible grounds (Faller 2019, pp. 8:5, 8:7, 8:38; Korotkova 2020, p. 4:17).

### Total versus partial source recording

That grammatical split changes what every spoken sentence logs. Where evidential marking is optional, an unmarked utterance ($M = \varnothing$) occurs with positive probability $P(M = \varnothing) > 0$, confounding certainty, implication, haste, and evasion. Where finite declarative syntax forces an evidential slot, omission is excluded ($P(M = \varnothing) = 0$), making the map from utterance to source category total by grammar rather than personal diligence. Even in obligatory systems, however, the direct or visual form can serve as a non-literal default for general knowledge, while non-direct forms overlap with mirativity or epistemic modality, requiring genre context.

Forcing a grammatical evidential category records *what kind* of warrant was claimed without flattening sources onto one numeric reliability score. Comparing Tuyuca's five-term verbal paradigm (visual, nonvisual, apparent, secondhand, assumed) with Cuzco Quechua enclitics, Faller (2001, pp. 38, 40–41, 54–57) shows that evidentials separate two orthogonal axes of directness: intervening speakers for reportatives versus inferential steps for reasoning. Because neither axis dominates everywhere, speakers weigh trustworthiness, evidence strength, and likelihood together rather than applying a single context-free rank (Faller 2001, pp. 55–57).

This is the archival site in its purest form. What turns a claim into a document is not ink on paper, but provenance fixed at creation that cannot be altered without issuing a new record. Trouillot located historical silencing in fact creation and archive assembly (Trouillot 1995), while Stoler showed institutional categories deciding legibility before anyone opens a file (Stoler 2009). Both describe deposits governed outside the sentence on fillable forms; an obligatory verbal slot locks source type inside grammar itself.

The claim is structural and narrower than truthfulness. A grammar forcing speakers to mark hearsay also forces them to tag hearsay as hearsay, removing the bare declarative form that bureaucratic prose uses for **uncertainty absorption**—passing an inference upward while shedding qualifying evidence (March & Simon 1958). Still, obligatory marking blocks only silent omission ($P(M = \varnothing) = 0$), not uncertainty absorption itself: an inferential morpheme still hides sample size, a reportative flattens multi-hop chains into one slot, and an intermediary can upgrade a rumor into a direct claim.

## 2. The mark is inside the act, not a comment on it

Guard site `T6Performative` asks whether a source marker is structurally built into the speech act or acts like a detachable footnote that later relays can edit or strip away. It is tempting to treat an evidential particle as commentary tacked onto an assertion, yet Austin's performative account and Searle's constitutive rules show why that fails: an utterance counts as a definite act through the rules in force (Austin 1962; Searle 1969). Asserting a claim with a visual morpheme versus a hearsay morpheme creates two distinct speech acts, just as verb tense belongs inside a claim rather than acting as a side remark about time.

### Semantics and multi-hop chain attrition

Formal semantics separates how an evidential updates the shared record from the main claim. In Cheyenne, Murray (2014, pp. 2:2, 2:7–9) shows that a direct evidential declarative performs two updates: it proposes the at-issue proposition $q$ for confirmation or denial, while its not-at-issue condition $\mathrm{dir}(i, q)$—that speaker $i$ holds direct evidence—updates common ground directly without entering debate.

Across languages, Korotkova (2020, pp. 4:2–3, 4:7–9, 4:11–18) cautions that standard projection tests do not point to one uniform mechanism. Escaping clause-mate negation reflects high syntactic scope rather than projective independence (Korotkova 2020, p. 4:9), and resisting direct denial (*"That's not true"*) stems from propositional anaphora and privileged self-attribution rather than at-issueness (Korotkova 2020, pp. 4:12–14). Question-under-discussion answerhood stays the sharpest diagnostic—though still reversible under prosodic focus in Dutch—aligning with Faller's (2019) question-based discourse model rather than Murray's assertion-only update (Korotkova 2020, pp. 4:16–18).

Placing the mark inside the act yields three structural consequences. First, the mark cannot be patched without performing a second act: editing a footnote alters commentary, whereas revising an evidential morpheme leaves both speech acts in the record. Second, a single slot cannot index the depth of a relay chain. Cuzco Quechua `=si` spans news reports, folktales, and conversation (Faller 2019, p. 8:6), and Cheyenne `-sėstse` renders both *'I hear'* and *'they say'* (Murray 2014, pp. 2:2–3), leaving hop depth $k \ge 1$ and upstream loss invisible.

Consider a single serial chain of $k \ge 1$ reporting hops without repair or parallel corroboration. Let $E\_i$ denote faithful transmission at hop $i$, and define surviving chain reliability as $w\_k := \mathbb{P}\bigl(\bigcap\_{i=1}^{k} E\_i\bigr)$. Whenever every earlier prefix holds positive probability, define conditional attrition at hop $i$ by $\varepsilon\_i := \mathbb{P}\bigl(E\_i^c \mid \bigcap\_{j=1}^{i-1} E\_j\bigr) \in [0, 1)$. Factoring by the probability chain rule yields:

$$w\_k = \prod\_{i=1}^{k}\bigl(1 - \varepsilon\_i\bigr),$$

In plain words, faithful survival across $k$ relays multiplies the survival probabilities $(1 - \varepsilon\_i)$ of every single hop, so even modest losses compound across a chain.

The product falls strictly below one whenever any $\varepsilon\_i > 0$, and absorbs to $w\_k = 0$ if any prefix fails completely ($\varepsilon\_m = 1$ at hop $m \le k$, avoiding undefined $0/0$ terms). It simplifies to geometric decay $(1 - \varepsilon)^k$ only under uniform loss $\varepsilon\_i \equiv \varepsilon$. Across heterogeneous hops ($\max\_m \varepsilon\_m < 1$), sensitivity $|\partial w\_k / \partial \varepsilon\_j| = w\_k / (1 - \varepsilon\_j)$ is governed by the worst bottleneck hop $\arg\max\_j \varepsilon\_j$.

Where $r \ge 2$ independent witnesses corroborate with $w\_{k,m} \in (0, 1)$, joint reliability $1 - \prod\_{m=1}^{r}(1 - w\_{k,m})$ beats any single chain $\max\_m w\_{k,m}$, whereas circular re-citation of one origin leaves Keynesian evidential weight $V$ frozen ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)). Note also that $w\_k$ tracks silent omission ($w\_k \to 0$), whereas **source upgrading**—an intermediary promoting rumor into a firsthand verb—falsifies the label while keeping apparent $w\_k$ artificially high.

### Stripping at transcription and retrieval boundaries

Third, an utterance-bound mark cannot verify the earlier reports it relays. A minute noting that *the officer reported the prisoner was seen* executes its own act without checking the officer's source, and optional metadata fields are routinely skipped or filled defensively.

Automated transcription and translation worsen this stripping before any search index ([Chapter 75, *Layer 25: Media Ecology, Information Retrieval & Performative Order*](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md)) inspects the text. Across parallel corpora, subword tokenizers create length gaps up to 15 times—and over 4 times even in character and byte encodings—warping speech-recognition outputs and overflowing context windows (Petrov et al. 2023). On obligatory paradigms (Tuyuca, Tariana, Cheyenne; Aikhenvald 2004; Murray 2014) and enclitic clauses (Cuzco Quechua; Faller 2019), inflated token counts sever bound suffixes from verb stems and clip trailing clauses. Outputs then pivot into unmarked declaratives ($P(M = \varnothing) > 0$), erasing provenance into degraded $w\_k$ assertions.

## 3. Who answers when the declared source is false

Guard site `T7Liability` keeps whoever relays a report answerable for faithful transmission and for hearing low-status witnesses, instead of deflecting blame onto a distant source. Suppose a speaker adds a hearsay marker to announce that the bridge is closed, and the bridge is actually open. Even though the claim originated elsewhere, relaying it was the speaker's own act. Declaring a source separates answerability for *whether the bridge is closed* from answerability for *whether that report occurred and was relayed faithfully*.

Faller (2019, pp. 8:31–35) separates Goffman's roles: *animator* (speaker), *author* (composer), and *principal* (committer). Bare declaratives merge all three, whereas Cuzco Quechua `=si` binds the animator solely to reportative evidence, permitting denial of $\phi$ without contradiction (Faller 2019, pp. 8:2–4, 8:36–38). In a question under discussion without dissent, however, the Collaborative Principle attaches a dependent truth commitment placing $\phi$ on the floor (Faller 2019, pp. 8:3–4, 8:29, 8:39–43, fn. 32, 34).

### Status discounting, real-time relay, and custodial consent

Fricker's analysis of testimonial injustice shows why citing an outside authority cannot repair credibility (Fricker 2007). Hearers judge the speaker's standing before weighing the cited source. A witness facing status discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in (0, 1)$ keeps effective credibility weight $1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$, stretching alarm latency by $(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})^{-1}$ ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3). Because the attribution itself is testimony, it inherits that discount (Fricker 2007). (Note that $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$ differs from the truncated-normal variance factor $\delta(\alpha)$ in [Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md) and the yield gap $\delta\_{\mathrm{yield}} = r - \mu$ in [Chapter 76](76-layer-sequential-decision-bandit-real-options-order.md).)

Institutions also sever speaker from sentence at the archive gate. In person, an animator answers for attribution; translated into a ministerial digest whose categories set official legibility (Stoler 2009), the text sheds language-specific evidential morphology and loses its addressee, letting citations circulate across archive assembly long after provenance disappears (Trouillot 1995).

Live multilingual interpretation runs into that same attrition in flight under human throughput limits ([Chapter 49, *Moral Luck, Apt Agency, and Institutional Design*](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)). When translating between languages without shared evidential grammar, interpreters cannot concurrently track lagging audio, audit $k$-hop chains ($w\_k$), and rebuild morphology as lexical paraphrases. Overload drops provenance markers first, flattening marked speech into bare claims and driving verification time $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ past survival headroom ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$). Holding `T3Archive` and `T7Liability` intact demands offline bitemporal custody (Snodgrass 2000)—storing raw audio ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$) and morphological glosses apart from digests ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$)—alongside scheduled interpreter rotation.

Custody begins even earlier, with community sovereignty over external corpora. Under CARE—*Collective Benefit*, *Authority to Control*, *Responsibility* (Indigenous worldview stewardship), and *Ethics*—Carroll et al. (2020) show technical openness (*FAIR*) without governance reproduces epistemicide. Paired with Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (`FPIC`), CARE sets a structural refusal gate rather than an optimizable checklist ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), fourth brake): `FPIC` enforces an ex-ante `T6Performative` gate on `T3Archive`, while CARE anchors custodial liability (`T7Liability`) across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) alongside [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md).

## 4. The three sites are one circuit

Read separately, the guards span verb grammar, speech acts, and speaker liability; in practice they operate as one circuit. Source category secures provenance (`T3Archive`), performative marking locks act identity against silent edits (`T6Performative`), and attribution liability survives citation (`T7Liability`). Repairing one alone unravels the others: optional marking restores omission ambiguity, footnote chains invite regress, and excusing reportatives dissolves accountability.

Signature `(1, 0, 2)` leaves $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ locally unguarded ($k\_2 = 0$), so multilingual relays and $w\_k$ chains fail via timing collapse ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}} > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$) unless [Layer 06](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md) and [Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md) supply `T1Framing`, `T4Formalization`, and `T5Temporal` alongside offline custody ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md); `T2Severity` stays open upstream)—*refuted only if* bitemporal logs and interpreter rotation preserve $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

Ablating `T3Archive` via optional omission ($P(M = \varnothing) > 0$) or tokenizer clipping yields blind undersizing ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$; *refuted only if* bitemporal custody retains morphology). Stripping `T6Performative` or `T7Liability` via bare-assertion flattening, testimonial discounting ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)), or non-consensual extraction violating `FPIC` and CARE (Carroll et al. 2020) exports epistemicide across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Layer 35](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md); *refuted only if* ex-ante `FPIC` vetoes and accountable principals govern custody).

Under the non-compensable minimum ([Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)), neither provenance nor CARE/`FPIC` acts as a live optimization checklist, holding strictly *ex post* within $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$.
