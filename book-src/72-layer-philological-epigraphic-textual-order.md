## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Convert a collation into an effective number of witnesses, and say why eliminating the *codices descripti* is a design correction rather than an editorial preference.
2. Show that the severity scale which makes a stemma possible is reconstructed from the witnesses the stemma orders, and say what the method returns when the fixed point is not unique.
3. State the limit of parsimony as an ordering — the count of admissible trees against the number of discriminating readings — and say what the apparatus prints in place of a decision.

## 1. The witnesses are fewer than the copies

Imagine comparing ten medieval manuscript copies of a classical speech and discovering that nine of them repeat the exact same skipped line and smudged word from page four of the tenth. You do not hold ten independent witnesses confirming the passage—you hold one witness echoed nine times.

Guard site `T3Archive` protects textual scholarship from mistaking copied volume for independent confirmation. A text survives only as copies, and copies are rarely independent observations. Lachmann's common-error stemmatics and Maas's *eliminatio codicum descriptorum* rest on that distinction (Lachmann 1850; Maas 1958). Editors collate surviving manuscripts, discard copies derived solely from another surviving exemplar (or reconstructible without them), and organize the remainder into a branching tree recording independent copying acts—the only evidence for the lost archetype.

### Why eliminating copies is a design correction

That pruning obeys survey clustering arithmetic rather than mere neatness ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md); [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md); Kish 1965; Moulton 1986). Partition $n > 0$ surviving manuscripts into $G \ge 1$ stemmatic families (using $G$ here for family count, distinct from graph $\mathcal{G}\_1$) of sizes $m\_1, \dots, m\_G > 0$, with mean size $\bar{m} = n/G \ge 1$ and size variance ratio $\mathrm{CV}^2(m) \ge 0$. Effective family size becomes $\tilde{m} = \bar{m}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr)$. Under random-effects clustering with intraclass reading correlation $\rho\_I \in [0, 1)$ (distinct from Meng's $\rho\_{R,Y}$) and positive witness weights $w\_i > 0$ (whose exact composition with clustering is additive under unit-level independence), the total design effect is $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = 1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I + \mathrm{CV}^2(w) \ge 1$, shrinking variance-effective witness count to $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = n/\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}$.

Take uniform witness weights ($\mathrm{CV}^2(w) = 0$). A single corrupted family ($G = 1$, $m\_1 = \tilde{m} = n$) sends $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} \to 1$ as $\rho\_I \to 1^-$. Across $G > 1$ families, $\rho\_I \to 1^-$ sends $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} \to n/\tilde{m} = G/\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr) < G$ whenever family sizes differ ($\mathrm{CV}^2(m) > 0$). It converges to $G$ in the *joint* equal-size, identical-copy limit ($\mathrm{CV}^2(m) = 0$ and $\rho\_I \to 1^-$), and equals $G$ after *eliminatio* removes *codices descripti* derived exclusively from a surviving exemplar (Maas 1958 [1927], §4) only when every surviving family is anchored by a surviving exemplar and reduces to size one ($m\_g = 1$).

By contrast, independent copies descending from a lost hyparchetype are not redundant *descripti* and cannot be collapsed to one copy per family without erasing ancestor readings. Variance-effective count $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ also stays distinct from survival bias ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$; Meng 2018) and uncopied lineages ($\pi\_i = 0$): counting redundant *descripti* adds nominal bulk, not testimony, because *ponderanda sunt testimonia, non numeranda*.

### Stemmatic boundaries and bipartite ties

A fitted stemma marks sharp evidential boundaries. It detects branchings attested in at least two surviving lines, yet stays blind to any reading that died in the first copy of the archetype without leaving leaves. Where only two independent lines survive, disagreement produces a **bipartite stemmatic tie** (*recensio aperta*). Mechanical majority voting fails there, shifting judgment to internal *selectio* (*usus scribendi* and *lectio difficilior*); closed recension requires $\ge 3$ independent branches to arbitrate mechanically.

Where only one copy survives (*codex unicus*), *recensio* returns that single text as the archetype, uncheckable against innovation without conjectural *divinatio*, just as unlinked fragments without shared errors permit no stemmatic bridge. **Domain failure boundary:** closed-tree *recensio* and pure *eliminatio* break under horizontal cross-contamination (*contaminatio*, importing readings across branches) and independent parallel slips (scribal polygenesis).

## 2. Severity is graded on a scale the witnesses supply

Guard site `T2Severity` requires weighing copying errors rigorously—and marking physical gaps on stone or parchment explicitly—so circular editorial taste cannot pass off guesses as evidence. A stemma is built not from mere agreement in correct text, nor from trivial slips like common vowel spellings, but from indicative agreement in error (*Leitfehler*): *errores coniunctivi* proving shared descent and *errores separativi* proving independence between lines (Maas 1958). Because an orthographic slip, a line-end skip, a parallel-passage harmonization, and a deliberate interpolation differ sharply in diagnostic force, error severity must be graded before building the tree.

Yet the severity scale is reconstructed from the same collation it orders. Let $\sigma$ denote the severity-weighted error grading across variant loci and $\tau$ denote the stemma tree (local philological notation, distinct from standard deviation and acceptance thresholds). The editorial method iterates two coupled maps $\hat{\tau}(\sigma)$ and $\hat{\sigma}(\tau)$:

$$\tau = \hat{\tau}(\sigma), \qquad \sigma = \hat{\sigma}(\tau),$$

In plain words, tree construction depends on how errors are weighted, and error weights depend on which tree is assumed—so a stable reading only holds when both settle on a unique fixed point.

Whenever two distinct trees remain consistent with the error weights each generates, $(\tau, \sigma)$ is non-identified, leaving substantive disagreement over the text. Calling a variant corrupt merely because it sounds uncharacteristic of an author smuggles conclusion into premise. The method holds only when the critical apparatus prints rejected readings beside the chosen text so readers can re-grade $\sigma$ and test rival trees.

### Physical damage on stone and neural epigraphy

Epigraphy enforces that discipline through physical damage rather than literary intuition. Under the Leiden Convention (van Groningen 1932; [Chapter 35](35-inference-and-evidence.md)), brackets separate physical gaps from conjectures: `[abc]` marks restored letters, `[...]` a gap of countable letter-spaces, `[---]` a gap of unknown span, and `(abc)` expanded abbreviations. Non-alphabetic Andean *khipu* archives show a parallel physical grammar, where spin direction, ply, fiber color, knot orientation, and knot tier encoded structured binary accounting records alongside physical cord gaps (Urton 2003).

On a regular *stoichedon* grid over an alphabet of $A \ge 2$ characters, an exact gap of $k \ge 1$ slots permits $A^k$ strings, while an uncertain span of $1$ through $k$ letters permits $\sum\_{j=1}^k A^j = (A^{k+1}-A)/(A-1)$ non-empty fillings. On irregular breaks, letter-width variance widens $k$ into interval $[k\_{\min}, k\_{\max}]$, spanning $\sum\_{j=k\_{\min}}^{k\_{\max}} A^j = (A^{k\_{\max}+1}-A^{k\_{\min}})/(A-1)$ combinations. Brackets measure physical space, not syntax—bounding letter counts never bounds valid grammar.

Neural restoration models confirm how sensitive guesses remain to frame and training corpus. On the `PHI-ML` epigraphic benchmark exceeding 3.2 million words (34,952 training, 2,826 validation, and 2,949 test inscriptions) with 1–10-character spans masked, Assael, Sommerschield, & Prag (2019) evaluated character language models across genres. `LM Epigraphy` trained strictly on `PHI-ML` reached 52.7% character error rate in Table 2 (p. 6371, correcting §5.2's running-prose misprint of 57.3%; 47.0% Top-20 accuracy). By contrast, `LM Philology` trained on Greek literary corpora (`First1KGreek` and `Perseus`) worsened to 68.1% (26.0% Top-20), and joint `LM Philology & Epigraphy` degraded to 65.0% (28.8% Top-20)—proving literary prose imports alien genre expectations (Assael, Sommerschield, & Prag 2019). For `PYTHIA-BI-WORD`, Top-20 accuracy depended heavily on surviving context around the gap, peaking near 500 characters and collapsing when only 20 remained (Assael, Sommerschield, & Prag 2019).

Later systems expand to geographic attribution, dating, and parallel search while keeping human historians decisive. On Ancient Greek inscriptions, *Ithaca* alone hit 62% restoration accuracy, 71% provenance accuracy, and sub-30-year dating distance, while pairing historians with *Ithaca* lifted human restoration accuracy from 25% to 72% (Assael et al. 2022). On Latin inscriptions with arbitrary-length gaps and multimodal context, *Aeneas* retrieved useful parallels in 90% of historian evaluations and raised task confidence by 44%, beating both humans and AI alone on restoration and attribution while achieving a 13-year dating distance (Assael et al. 2025).

## 3. Recension is an ordering, and the ordering does not choose

Guard site `T4Formalization` ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$) makes tree-building explicit, preserves tied parsimonious stemmata alongside physical witness state, and halts short of irreversible single-text collapse. Formalizing recension—collate witnesses, drop *descripti*, order by shared indicative error, and read the root—turns editorial dispute from taste into auditable steps while safeguarding the vital margin of recoverable textual states before fragile codices or weathered stone degrade.

Yet formal ordering meets the super-exponential ceiling of [Layer 14](64-layer-taxonomy-systematics-ontology-order.md). Writing $r$ for independent surviving leaves after *eliminatio* (distinct from §1's total manuscript count $n$), $r = 1$ yields a trivial path, and for $r \ge 3$ labelled leaves strictly binary rooted stemmata already number $(2r-3)!!$ (against $(2r-5)!!$ unrooted binary trees), while polytomies and reticulated graphs from *contaminatio* exceed $(2r-3)!!$. Because candidate trees explode super-exponentially while discriminating readings grow linearly with text length, parsimony returns the **set** of minimal-change trees rather than a unique answer.

When minimal trees disagree, formal recension freezes the conflict inside a $\sigma$-graded apparatus—preventing forced single-archetype reduction or unlogged *eliminatio* from destroying recoverable ancestor states before physical degradation. That split between ranked candidate sets and a single printed reading shows up in Assael, Sommerschield, & Prag (2019): doctoral historians averaged 50 restorations in 2 hours (57.3% CER), while `PYTHIA-BI-WORD` hit 30.1% single-best error and 73.5% Top-20 inclusion. Moreover, Assael, Sommerschield, & Prag (2019) strip punctuation and Leiden brackets (`[]`, `()`) during tokenization, and the `I.PHI` and *Aeneas* training corpora drop `()` expansions while retaining `[abc]` text as plain tokens to preserve corpus coverage—touching the risk of circular **"history from square brackets"** warned against by Badian (1989). Both *Ithaca* (Assael et al. 2022) and *Aeneas* (Assael et al. 2025) cite Badian (1989), evaluate bracket-removal ablations, and emit ranked hypothesis sets rather than single restorations; when downstream workflows bypass those safeguards under unguarded local governance ($k\_3 = 0$, zero local governance guards), they launder guesses into primary evidence unless Leiden brackets (van Groningen 1932) stay mandatory.

**Domain failure boundary:** formal recension cannot determine a unique archetype where equally parsimonious trees conflict, *contaminatio* breaks tree topology, dependent loci distort unweighted steps, or $\sigma$ stays circularly contested; recension removes redundant copies without constituting the text alone.

## 4. The circuit and Collapse Propositions 2 and 3

`T2Severity`, `T3Archive`, and `T4Formalization` form one circuit: copying without severity ignores $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}$, severity without formalization is uncheckable, and forcing one tree or stripping Leiden brackets destroys recoverable textual states while masquerading as settled truth. Locally, `(2, 1, 0)` and direct parents ([Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md) with $k\_2 = k\_3 = 0$ and [Layer 14](64-layer-taxonomy-systematics-ontology-order.md) with $k\_3 = 0$) omit both $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$'s timing guard `T5Temporal` and all of $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ ($k\_3 = 0$: `T6Performative`, `T7Liability`). Inside `Cone(L22)`, `T5Temporal` closes upstream via `L01`, `L03`, `L04`, `L06`, and `L12` (securing physical conservation deadlines), while $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ (`T6`, `T7`) closes via `L01`, `L02`, `L03`, `L05`, `L08`, and `L12` ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)).

**Collapse Proposition 2 (*Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin*, $\neg S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$):** Treating `T4Formalization` as mere tree counting without physical preservation slack leaves an apparatus lucid about tied trees or lacunae while fragile parchment, *khipu* cords, or weathered stone cross $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ before high-resolution imaging and diplomatic transcription complete—or destroys recoverable witness states irreversibly through premature forced recension and unlogged *eliminatio*. **Falsification:** Collapse Proposition 2 **fails if** conservation imaging, full diplomatic collations, and retained tied-tree apparatuses preserve witness states within $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$.

Empty governance induces **Collapse Proposition 3 (*Cynical Capture and Pillaging*, $\neg S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$)** ([Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). Absent `T6Performative` and `T7Liability`, dropping Leiden tags (van Groningen 1932) or taking unbracketed top model predictions out of ranked context launders editorial conjecture into **"history from square brackets"** (Badian 1989; Assael et al. 2022; Assael et al. 2025), while silent emendations without bitemporal logs (Snodgrass 2000) separating diplomatic observation event time ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$) from restoration-display transaction time ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$) offload errors onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$). **Falsification:** Collapse Proposition 3 **fails if** a `(2, 1, 0)` apparatus retains Leiden brackets, rejected variants, cluster counts, and bitemporal logs without claiming finality or spilling onto external reception perimeters.

Unlike macro-document survival bounds in [Chapter 73](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md) (`Chao1`) and named victim registers in [Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md), collating variants at a single *locus* lacks an independent census of readings lost before branching diverged, requiring explicit frame disclosure over false certainty.
