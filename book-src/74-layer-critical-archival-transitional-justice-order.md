## 1. The harm is framed before the archive is sought

Suppose a post-conflict truth commission opens regional offices using an intake form with checkboxes solely for armed battlefield fatalities. When families arrive to report forced disappearances, starved detentions, or mass expulsions, clerks have no matching code on the sheet—so those wrongs finish with zero rows in the final archive.

Guard site `T1Framing` audits the mandate and category design upfront so narrow definitions never erase whole classes of harm before anyone opens a dossier. A commission does not search an undifferentiated past; it reads a case framed before the first file opens.

Under Fricker's (2007) hermeneutical injustice, lacking shared interpretive terms leaves a harm unavailable even to its victims because the least powerful shape official categories least. Rivera Cusicanqui (2012) tracks an analogous co-optation inside official Bolivian multicultural state discourse, where administrative inclusion of permitted indigenous terms keeps subaltern structures *Not askable*; applied to transitional-justice commissions, narrow intake schemas enact that archival silence directly. Trouillot (1995) likewise locates archival silence across four moments—fact creation, archival assembly, narrative retrieval, and retrospective significance—where the first decides what counts as an event at all.

### Mandate support and four-state null semantics

Let $\mathcal{C}$ denote the set of categories licensed by a mandate, $e$ an event, $\mathbf{1}[\cdot]$ the indicator function, and $E\_{\mathrm{reg}}$ the registered archive entries. Admission obeys a hard logical cap:

$$P(e \in E\_{\mathrm{reg}}) \le \mathbf{1}[e \in \mathcal{C}],$$

In plain words, any event $e$ falling outside the mandate categories $\mathcal{C}$ has zero probability of entering the classified register, no matter how diligently archivists work. Only unattributed entries (`NOD`, unrecorded or undetermined perpetrator), oral memory, or attested counter-inductive reopening (`CounterInductiveReopen`; Feyerabend 1970; [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)) keep excluded events reachable.

Exclusion also runs inside $\mathcal{C}$ through **banal intra-schema reclassification**, logging killings or disappearances under routine administrative labels such as *desertion*, *natural death*, or *accident* (§2). Conversely, events inside $\mathcal{C}$ still drop to zero capture whenever marginalized groups remain *Not askable* ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$; [Chapter 45, *From World to Archive: Evidential Weight, Big Data Paradox, and the Uncounted*](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)). Incident mandates log dated testimony while structural mandates collect standing orders; `T1Framing` thus operates as the reflexive lock ($P\_3 \to P\_2$) anchoring $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ ([Chapter 29, *Aim and the Three Planes*](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)).

At intake, dossiers enforce **uncoerced four-state null semantics** ([Chapter 06](06-who-is-missing-from-the-table.md); [Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md)). Extending Codd's RM/T null $\omega$ (Codd 1979) through *A-marks* versus *I-marks* (Codd 1986) to four-valued tables (Codd 1987), commissions distinguish `NOT_OBSERVED` (*A-mark*, eligible `NOD`), `INAPPLICABLE` (*I-mark*, structurally invalid subfield), `MEASURED_ZERO`, and pre-schema *Not askable* ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$ outside $\mathcal{C}$). Coercing `NULL` to zero or collapsing *A-marks* into *I-marks* converts silenced harm into false exoneration.

## 2. The archive of the wrong

Guard site `T3Archive` detects when institutional record-keepers hide their own culpability, flipping recording correlation negative so the thickest files document bureaucratic cover-ups rather than actual harm. Stoler (2009) shows how colonial categories govern legibility because administrators write files to defend state conduct. Critical archival studies treat archives as active power prior to retrieval, whose appraisal and retention rules shape memory and invisibility before any statistical model runs (Schwartz & Cook 2002). A security apparatus leaves authentic artifacts—detention sheets, passengerless requisitions, closed files relabeling killings under §1's routine headings—producing a well-preserved archive of the wrong variable.

### Negative missingness and opposing collector tilts

Where state exposure climbed alongside harm, registration fell wherever culpability peaked. Write $R$ for registration status and $Y$ for true harm; across the tribunal's range, their correlation $\rho\_{R,Y}$ stays strictly negative:

$$\rho\_{R,Y} < 0,$$

In plain words, the worst state-perpetrated harms are the least likely to appear in state logs—reversing the positive severity tilt of outbreak hospitals in [Layer 17](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md), so standard severity corrections push totals in the wrong direction.

Consider a finite population of size $N > 1$, spread $\sigma\_Y > 0$, and interior recorded share $f = n/N \in (0, 1)$ ($\sigma\_R = \sqrt{f(1-f)} > 0$). Under Meng's (2018) identity $\bar{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = \rho\_{R,Y}\sigma\_Y\sqrt{(1-f)/f}$ ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)), bias scales with $\sqrt{(1-f)/f}$ and takes its sign from collector intent. At a full census ($f = 1$), $\sigma\_R = 0$ leaves $\rho\_{R,Y}$ undefined ($0/0$) and bias vanishes identically. Because opposing collector tilts $\rho\_{R\_k, Y}$ shape each list, oral testimony and state alibi files never sample a single distribution, and file thickness measures preservation strategy rather than prevalence.

Peru's 1980–2000 conflict record (`arXiv:1906.04763v2`) displays those opposing list tilts directly (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019). Merging six 2003 Truth and Reconciliation Commission (`CVR`) lists with 20,468 OCR-recovered entries (619 duplicates excluded) from `MIMDES`'s *Censo por la Paz* yielded 34,962 unique victims across 49,833 records and 60 of 127 non-empty overlap patterns. Notice that `MIMDES`'s owning agency was deactivated in 2006 with loss of its digital archive (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019).

Before adding `MIMDES`, `CVR`'s 17,596-entry list logged 9,034 Shining Path killings (`SLU`, 51.3%) versus 6,056 state killings (`EST`, 34.4%) and supplied 9,034 of 9,336 six-list `SLU` records exclusively (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019). By contrast, `COMISEDH` logged 2,933 `EST` (92.5%) versus 78 `SLU` (2.5%) of 3,172 entries, and the Ombudsman (`DP`) logged 3,620 `EST` versus 198 `SLU`; folding in `MIMDES` added 12,442 `SLU` records (4,090 already present) toward 17,687 total `SLU` records (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019).

Distinct from sampling share $f$, capture–recapture uses single-list counts $f\_1 \ge 0$ and overlap doubletons $f\_2 > 0$ via `Chao2`: $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(2) = f\_1^2/(4 f\_2)$ (regularized when $f\_2 = 0$; Chao 1989; Chao & Colwell 2017; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)). Even so, both symmetric unit-heterogeneity models ($M\_h$, equal propensity across lists) and mixed unit-and-list heterogeneity models ($M\_{th}$, list-varying heterogeneous capture) fail when collector repulsion induces negative between-list correlation (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)), and all estimators miss $\pi\_i \equiv 0$ strata. A thick dossier can still be a thick silence.

## 3. The question transitional justice puts

Guard site `T7Liability` shields archivists and witnesses from reprisal, secures custody across agency closures, and keeps named legal proof distinct from dark-figure planning totals. Under Fricker's (2007) testimonial injustice, status discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$) silences witnesses without refuting them. Blocking silencing without flooding adjudicator bandwidth ([Chapter 49, *Moral Luck, Apt Agency, and Institutional Design*](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)) demands an autonomous out-of-band $S3^\ast$ algedonic audit channel (Beer's channel from operations to meta-oversight, distinct from triad coordinates $S\_a$ and Stratum `S5`, with status discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$; Beer 1972) with corroborated linkage on an append-only bitemporal ledger separating valid event time from transaction availability time ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}} \le t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$; Snodgrass 2000; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)).

### Non-retaliation custody and archive escrow

Custody fails whenever clerks face retaliation or archives vanish when commissions dissolve. `T7Liability` pairs bitemporal logs (Snodgrass 2000) with **universal institutional non-retaliation custody** ([Chapter 20, *Legitimacy, Accountability, and Institutions*](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)). That custody shields archivists, whistleblowers, and intake staff via status-blind $S3^\ast$ channels and **externally escrowed** keyed sealing (`HMAC`-`SHA-256`, holding signing keys and verification tags under independent non-agency custody, without punitive staff monitoring; Krawczyk, Bellare, & Canetti 1997; Near & Miceli 1996; Alford 2001; [Chapter 93, *Fracturing the Frame Before the Seal*](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md)). Because cryptographic seals prove only post-seal integrity and cannot detect prior premise rigging (`PRI`), intake also requires pre-seal `T1 <-> T3` mandate cross-checks ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)).

Alongside non-retaliation, `T7Liability` mandates **continuous archive escrow across agency dissolution or mandate closure** under independent `HMAC` witness verification ([Chapter 93, *Fracturing the Frame Before the Seal*](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md)). Dossiers, intake metadata, and `NOT_OBSERVED` provenance must transfer into standing external custody rather than losing digital archives as when `MIMDES`'s owning agency was deactivated in 2006 (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019).

### Named registers versus dark-figure budgets

Individual legal remedies and macro-level planning rely on different evidential objects. Reparations and criminal charges require named victims with case-specific proof ([Layer 31](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md); [Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)), whereas public budgeting and pattern liability rely on partial-identification dark-figure intervals ([Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)). You cannot pay a claimant from a statistical residual, yet budgeting or denying systemic harm from the 34,962 named register erases the uncounted interval (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019).

Analysts separated roles under split blinding: two modeled while a third built 26 contiguous sub-strata over the $31{,}240 + 3{,}173 = 34{,}413$ geo-temporally stratifiable records (31,240 attributed and 3,173 `NOD` records, leaving the remaining $34{,}962 - 34{,}413 = 549$ unique victims outside the 26-stratum imputation subset, assuming conditional MAR across lists with at least four observations per perpetrator-stratum) (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019). Joint imputation yielded 58,234 total victims (95% credible interval 56,741–61,289) under a Conservative prior (`SLU` = 33,736, 95% credible interval 32,879–34,707 per Table 3(a); `EST` = 22,331; `OTR` = 2,099). Under a Diffuse prior, the total climbed to 65,958 (95% credible interval 61,462–75,387; `SLU` = 34,267; `EST` = 29,391; `OTR` = 2,232), concentrating prior sensitivity in state killings (`EST`) while still omitting $\pi\_i \equiv 0$ strata (Manrique-Vallier, Ball, & Sulmont 2019).

### Confidentiality mirrors versus primary custody

Confidentiality requires two-layer custody ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)). Aggregate mirrors publishing stratum counts and missingness rates may use pure $\varepsilon$-differential privacy (Dwork, McSherry, Nissim, & Smith 2006b) or approximate $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differential privacy ($\delta \ll 1/n$, where privacy tail slack $\delta$ is distinct from status discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$; Dwork, Kenthapadi, McSherry, Mironov, & Naor 2006a), yet neither mirror replaces sealed primary custody (`T7Liability`). Input noise $\mathcal{K}$ fails to commute with nonlinear estimators ($\hat{\theta}(\mathcal{K}(X)) \neq \mathcal{K}(\hat{\theta}(X))$; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md); [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md)), wipes out `Chao2` doubletons ($f\_2$) needed for $\hat{n}\_{00}$, distorts higher moments, and attenuates $\rho\_{R,Y}$. Liability here means institutional answerability: signing what the record can and cannot carry without retroactive erasure or mandate-end loss.

## 4. The circuit, and the margin it does not carry

These guards form one circuit: broad mandates over alibi archives miscount events, narrow intake erases harm, and named lists cannot budget unseen totals. Because `(2, 0, 1)` leaves $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ locally unguarded ($k\_2 = 0$, zero local vital guards), survival clocks and formal bounds inherit through [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md)'s ancestor lineage (`L01–L08`: `T4Formalization` from `L02–L08`, `T5Temporal` from `L01`, `L03`, `L04`, `L06`) before `L24` feeds [Layer 31](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md) ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)). Within this `(2, 0, 1)` exposure ([Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)):

1. **Collapse Proposition 1 (*Blind Undersizing*, $\neg S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$):** Ablating `T1Framing` or `T3Archive` ($e \notin \mathcal{C}$, $\rho\_{R,Y} < 0$, $\pi\_i \equiv 0$, `NULL`-to-zero coercion, or swapping privacy mirrors for primary linkage) sizes remedies to the visible floor. It *fails if* records cover a closed population with $\rho\_{R,Y} = 0$ and $\pi\_i > 0$.
2. **Collapse Proposition 2 (*Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin*, $\neg S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$):** Bounding dark figures inside `(2, 0, 1)` without survival clocks leaves $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ so inquiries outlive survivors—*failing if* interim relief holds $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$.
3. **Collapse Proposition 3 (*Cynical Capture and Pillaging*, $\neg S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$):** Ablating `T7Liability` leaves alibis unchecked, discounts warnings, and abandons files at mandate close, exporting harm onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$)—*failing if* bitemporal escrow, non-retaliation shields, and status-blind $S3^\ast$ vetoes internalize perimeter spillover.

Closed universes still condition dark-figure estimates (ongoing violence estimates rates), demanding explicit cut-offs and person-time denominators rather than turning diagnostics into primary custody.
