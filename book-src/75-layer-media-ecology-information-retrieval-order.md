## 1. The frame is decided before the first measurement

A medium is not a pipe of neutral bore. Innis observed that a civilisation's dominant medium carries some kinds of relation easily and others at cost, so the bias acts on what can be communicated before any particular message is chosen (Innis 1951). That survives the change of substrate. A retrieval index is a medium in exactly this sense, and it answers three questions before it measures anything: what counts as a document, what counts as a term, what counts as a match. The three answers are the frame. Everything downstream (score, cut, reranker, judge) operates inside a support the frame has already fixed.

The consequence is arithmetic, not rhetorical. Write $I$ for the event that a record enters the index at all, and $Rk$ for the event that it survives the ranking. For a record relevant to the query,

$$P(\text{retrieved} \mid \text{relevant}) = P(I \mid \text{relevant}) \cdot P(Rk \mid I, \text{relevant}).$$

The first factor is not produced by the ranker and cannot be improved by it. A class of records the schema cannot represent has $P(I) = 0$, and the product is zero however good the second factor becomes. Recall is bounded above by coverage. A system can improve indefinitely on its own benchmark while remaining blind to a class the benchmark never admitted. The frame does not bias the measurement; it decides which measurements exist.

The unit is where the frame does its quietest work. A pipeline that splits a text into passages fixes what can never be retrieved together, and a conditional whose antecedent and consequent land on different sides of a boundary is not ranked low. It is not a candidate. Score fusion trades one proxy for another. Reranking is where an unstated definition of relevance spends its force. A judge model spends compute on a proxy whose discrepancy with the intended quality nobody measured (Lewis et al. 2020). None of that is a defect of the ranker. Each is the frame reappearing as a measurement.

The framing site is not a preface to the analysis. It is the part of the analysis no later stage can revisit. A question that arrives already framed and a document that arrives already chunked are the same event seen from two ends (Chapter 32).

## 2. What the record retains, and who is outside it

Trouillot located the silences of history at four moments — the creation of facts, the assembly of archives, the retrieval into narrative, the retrospective assignment of significance (Trouillot 1995). The first two are decisions about deposit, the third is the frame of §1, the fourth belongs to the institution that will later cite the result as its own evidence. Stoler's reading of colonial archives adds the grain: bureaucratic categories decide what is legible, so the record's shape is the administrator's question made durable (Stoler 2009).

The bias is one quantity, not a suspicion. Let $R = 1$ mark that a unit of the world is registered and $Y$ be the quantity of interest. Then

$$\mathbb{E}[Y \mid R = 1] - \mathbb{E}[Y] = \frac{\rho\_{R,Y}\,\sigma\_{R}\,\sigma\_{Y}}{P(R = 1)},$$

which follows from $\mathrm{Cov}(R, Y) = \rho\_{R,Y}\sigma\_{R}\sigma\_{Y} = P(R=1)\bigl(\mathbb{E}[Y \mid R=1] - \mathbb{E}[Y]\bigr)$. Neither fact is reachable by a correction applied afterwards. The bias is divided by the registration share, so it grows without bound as the registered fraction shrinks. Its sign is carried by $\rho\_{R,Y}$, a property of the deposit, not of the analyst.

The retrieval index then adds a second selection on top of the first, and the two compose rather than add:

$$P(\text{retrieved}) = P(\text{deposited}) \cdot P(\text{indexed} \mid \text{deposited}) \cdot P(\text{retrieved} \mid \text{indexed}).$$

A zero in any factor is absorbing. So an archive can be scrupulously honest about its own contents and systematically wrong about the world: internal consistency is a statement about the third factor, the bias term about the first. The same arithmetic explains why a searchable corpus invites a category error. Because the returned set is coherent it reads as the population, and a share computed over it is silently promoted from a corpus share to a population share (Chapter 73).

## 3. The ranking writes the corpus it ranks

Searches are not observations of a fixed object. A ranking is published, the ranked observe it, and their response enters the corpus from which the next ranking is computed. Espeland and Sauder named the mechanism reactivity: a measure feeds back into the behaviour of those measured, so the distribution after publication is not the distribution that was measured (Espeland & Sauder 2007). Perdomo and coauthors gave it decision-theoretic form, distinguishing the fixed point at which a model is stable under its own deployment from the optimum obtained by acting on the response (Perdomo et al. 2020). The leakage pathways of Chapter 37 are the same object seen from the model's side. What this chapter adds is that the corpus, and not only the model, is what the loop rewrites.

The simplest case repays display, because it shows the effect is not a metaphor. Fix a capacity $k$ and retain the top slice of a score $s \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^{2})$, so the cut $\tau$ is the value above which exactly $k$ observations lie and $\alpha = (\tau - \mu)/\sigma$. With $\lambda(\alpha) = \varphi(\alpha)/\bigl(1 - \Phi(\alpha)\bigr)$ and $\delta(\alpha) = \lambda(\alpha)\bigl(\lambda(\alpha) - \alpha\bigr)$,

$$\mathbb{E}[s \mid s \ge \tau] = \mu + \sigma\lambda(\alpha), \qquad \mathrm{Var}(s \mid s \ge \tau) = \sigma^{2}\bigl(1 - \delta(\alpha)\bigr).$$

A report that keeps the top slice records a higher mean and a narrower spread than the population it was drawn from, and the next round of the same report is computed on a corpus already shaped by the previous cut. Iterating the operation moves the report toward a level set of its own score: the spread contracts, the mean rises, nothing has changed in the world. The metric is an intervention in that exact sense. The fixed point exists only when the response map has one; where it does not, the loop has no stable report at all (Chapter 48). Neff and Nagy's account of Tay is the empirical form of the same statement. A model refitted on the distribution its own deployment produced converges onto a point that is a capture by the parties whose loss was not the platform's (Neff & Nagy 2016).

Two conditions bound the claim, and both are checkable. Where reactivity is negligible the loop is a measurement, and ordinary inference applies without modification. Where the objects ranked never observe the ranking (a corpus of fossils, a set of sealed records), the feedback term is absent by construction. So the performative site is not a claim that all measurement is circular. It is the claim that a measurement whose output re-enters its own input has a different object than one whose output does not, and that the difference belongs to the instrument, not to the analyst's honesty.

## 4. The three sites are one circuit

Read separately, the three passages above are three literatures. They are one circuit, and the guard combination is the statement of that fact.

The frame decides the support: which records can be retrieved at all. The deposit decides the bias: which part of the world the support contains, and with what correlation to what it omits. The ranking decides the next support, because the record of a selection becomes the material of the following selection: the third stage rewrites the first. Repair at any single site is undone at another. A perfect ranker over a frame that cannot represent a class retrieves nothing from it. A perfect deposit under a ranking that selects on its own score converges on its own selection. An honest bias correction applied to a corpus whose composition the previous ranking produced estimates a quantity that no longer exists.

The signature `(2, 0, 1)` says what the combination does not contain. Two epistemic sites and one site of governance, the vital margin absent: nothing in this circuit carries a stop-work trigger. A media ecology can be lucid about its framing, exact about its silences, sophisticated about its reactivity, and still publish while the allocation it never measured does the damage. That is the second collapse. It is also why this order sits in Stratum V rather than in the methodological part of the book: the defect it names is not a gap in the analysis but the absence of a margin that would stop the analysis from being acted on.

## By the end of this chapter you can

1. Say what an index decides before it measures — unit, term and match — and show why recall is a ceiling set by coverage rather than a property of the ranking.
2. State an archive's bias term as a ratio over the registration share, and say which of the composed selection factors a given corpus is silent about.
3. Read a published ranking as an intervention on the corpus it ranks, using the retained-slice identities, and name the conditions under which the loop is a measurement instead.
