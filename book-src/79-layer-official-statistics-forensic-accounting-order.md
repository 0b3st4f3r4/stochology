## 1. The digit law, and the support it presupposes

Picture an auditor running a first-digit check on corporate invoices or weather-station pressure logs. When middle digits barely appear, an automated screen flags fraud immediately. Yet a tight physical scale eliminates those digits automatically, whereas a clever bookkeeper can fake natural digits on paper while hiding real debt off the books.

### Guard T3 (Archive): Checking measurement range and missing entities before testing digits

In forensic accounting and official statistics, `T3Archive` ($T\_3$) protects analysts from misreading narrow numeric bounds or omitted off-ledger entities.

Newcomb (1881) and Benford (1938) observed that the first significant digit $d \in \lbrace 1, \dots, 9 \rbrace$ of positive scale-spanning numbers ($X > 0$) follows $P(d) = \log\_{10}(1 + 1/d)$. That yields $P(1) \approx 0.3010$, $P(2) \approx 0.1761$, $P(3) \approx 0.1249$, and $P(9) \approx 0.0458$. Delivered by a log-uniform mantissa, Benford's law is the unique leading-digit measure unchanged when every number is multiplied by a positive constant (Pinkham 1961; Hill 1995).

A deviation from Benford's curve can reflect narrow numeric support rather than misconduct. Take $X > 0$ log-uniform on just half a decade $[1, \sqrt{10})$ ($\sqrt{10} \approx 3.1623$, normalizing $\log\_{10}$-length $1/2$). Digit one spans $[1, 2)$ ($\log\_{10} 2 \approx 0.3010$), digit two spans $[2, 3)$ ($\log\_{10}(3/2) \approx 0.1761$), and digit three spans $[3, \sqrt{10})$ ($1/2 - \log\_{10} 3 \approx 0.0229$). Rescaling by two gives $P(1) = 2\log\_{10} 2 \approx 0.6021$, $P(2) = 2\log\_{10}(3/2) \approx 0.3522$, and $P(3) = 1 - 2\log\_{10} 3 \approx 0.0458$ (summing to one before rounding). All remaining digits vanish ($P(4) = \dots = P(9) = 0$) solely because tighter bounds truncate support mechanically.

Consider a stylized barometric range where sea-level pressure in hectopascals hovers around $10^3\text{ hPa}$, inside $[900, 1100)\text{ hPa} \subset [900, 2000)\text{ hPa}$ (only extreme cyclones below $900\text{ hPa}$ admit digit eight). Under that bounded scale, every reading begins with nine or one, leaving digits two through eight at zero.

Write $p\_d \ge 0$ for observed digit shares and $b\_d \ge 0$ for Benford benchmarks, where $\sum\_{d=1}^{9} p\_d = \sum\_{d=1}^{9} b\_d = 1$. Nigrini's Mean Absolute Deviation $\mathrm{MAD} = \frac{1}{9}\sum\_{d=1}^{9}\lvert p\_d - b\_d\rvert$ absorbs $1 - b\_1 - b\_9 \approx 1 - 0.3010 - 0.0458 = 0.6532$ from the seven missing digits alone. By the triangle inequality, $\lvert p\_1 - b\_1\rvert + \lvert p\_9 - b\_9\rvert \ge \lvert(p\_1 + p\_9) - (b\_1 + b\_9)\rvert = 1 - b\_1 - b\_9$. Digits one and nine must therefore add at least that same gap again:

$$\mathrm{MAD} \ge \frac{2 \times 0.6532}{9} \approx 0.145$$

Read aloud, an honest barometer confined between nine hundred and eleven hundred hectopascals shows a mean digit gap of at least $0.145$. That is nearly ten times Nigrini's $0.015$ cutoff for nonconformity (Nigrini 2012; Slepkov, Ironside, & DiBattista 2015).

Conversely, Deckert, Myagkov and Ordeshook (2011) showed in election counts that matching Benford's law is neither necessary nor sufficient for honest tallying. Multi-decade numeric span is therefore necessary rather than sufficient: fixed price tiers or physical bounds exceed $\mathrm{MAD} = 0.015$ innocently, while fabricated accounts can match Benford performatively (`T6Performative`). Test numeric scale first, test digits second, and treat digit flags strictly as leads for perimeter audits rather than verdicts.

## 2. Conservation, the dark archive, and the revision record

Pacioli's (1494) double-entry bookkeeping matches every debit with a credit, enforcing nominal monetary conservation $A - L - E = 0$ across assets $A$, liabilities $L$, and equity $E$. That principle extended older paired-account audit practices such as Inka dual-strand *khipu* cords reconciling physical storehouse flows long before print manuals (Urton 2003). Yet clever structuring pushes debt off the balance sheet, as in Enron's unconsolidated partnerships (Powers et al. 2002) and Lehman's Repo 105 transfers (Valukas 2010).

Across $t \ge 2$ deduplicated binary audit lists (symmetric binomial list incidence, Model $M\_h$, inclusion probability $\pi\_i > 0$), unobserved discrepancy incidence obeys $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}] \ge \frac{t-1}{t}\frac{(\mathbb{E}[f\_1])^2}{2\mathbb{E}[f\_2]}$. Analysts estimate it via `Chao2` as $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(t) = \frac{t-1}{t}\frac{f\_1^2}{2f\_2}$ (reducing to $f\_1^2/(4f\_2)$ at $t = 2$, for singleton count $f\_1 \ge 0$ and doubleton count $f\_2 > 0$; Chao 1989; Chao & Colwell 2017; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1). That differs from single-stream `Chao1` $f\_1^2/(2f\_2)$ (Chao 1984). Discrepancy counts neither measure dollar size nor detect mixed unit-and-list heterogeneity (Model $M\_{th}$, $p\_{i1} \ne p\_{i2}$), negative inter-list dependence, suppressed list singletons ($f\_1 = 0$), or totally omitted entities ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)), demanding independent external custodian lists alongside append-only bitemporal seals, personal executive certification, and clawback.

Nominal monetary conservation closes only priced claims, exporting unbooked ecological, water, carbon, energy, and stranded-asset draws onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) and physical catchment basins. Bridging `L29` to [Layer 35](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md), non-substitutable satellite accounts track native-unit conservation of physical stock $S\_k$ (distinct from Sen's index $P\_S$, Stratum `S6`, and Beer's $S3^\ast$ below), $\Delta S\_k = I\_k - O\_k - D\_k$, across inflows $I\_k$, disclosed outflows $O\_k$, and unbooked draw $D\_k$ without dollar netting. As [Chapter 22](22-thermodynamics-and-entropy.md) establishes, AI and data-center efficiency has not decoupled total power and cooling withdrawals from physical basin limits (Masanet et al. 2020; Li et al. 2025). Because physical telemetry is dual-use, undisclosed draws ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$), scope shifting, paper offsets, and un-impaired stranded capital invite greenwashing capture unless independent custody audits native fluxes without perimeter spillover.

Official statistics form bitemporal vintages pairing each reference period $t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$ with its publication date $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Layer 04](54-layer-geophysical-chaos-data-assimilation-order.md); [Layer 08](58-layer-metrological-information-geometric-order.md); [Layer 23](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md)). A real-time archive logs initial releases alongside later benchmark values (Croushore & Stark 2001). Revisions to United States macroeconomic series have nonzero means, are large relative to the underlying series, and can be predicted from information available at first release, leaving initial releases inefficient forecasts (Aruoba 2008). Write initial release as $g\_0 \ne 0$ and benchmark as $g\_0 + r$ ($r \ne 0$). The headline sign flips iff $\mathrm{sgn}(r) = -\mathrm{sgn}(g\_0)$ and $\lvert g\_0\rvert < \lvert r\rvert$, with nonzero mean revisions tilting that flip asymmetrically so policy reacts to predictable bias rather than white noise. Contracts and fiscal rules tied to first releases therefore require published historical Aruoba revision intervals around $g\_0$ paired with adjustment or clawback hooks once benchmark vintages post at $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}' > t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$.

Every census rests on classification ([Layer 14](64-layer-taxonomy-systematics-ontology-order.md)). Official categories turn messy realities into countable administrative units (Desrosières 1993; Scott 1998). To expose collector drift directly inside official numbers, Mahalanobis (1946) built India's national surveys around interpenetrating sub-samples. Now suppose an agency updates its classification rule from $C\_1$ in period one (count $N\_1$) to $C\_2$ in period two (count $N\_2$). Let $N\_2'$ denote the period-two population re-coded under the old rule $C\_1$. Over a common population, the observed headline change $N\_2 - N\_1$ splits cleanly:

$$N\_2 - N\_1 = \underbrace{\bigl(N\_2 - N\_2'\bigr)}\_{\text{reclassification}} + \underbrace{\bigl(N\_2' - N\_1\bigr)}\_{\text{change in the world}}.$$

Read aloud, the shift between two reported counts equals the difference caused purely by changing definitions ($N\_2 - N\_2'$) plus the real change measured under the original rule ($N\_2' - N\_1$).

To block baseline laundering and hidden first-release bias (Croushore & Stark 2001; Aruoba 2008), the **dual-coded non-retroactive vintage bridge rule** locks $(t\_{\mathrm{ev}}, t\_{\mathrm{disp}}, C\_1)$ vintages append-only. It mandates independent raw-intake overlap dual-coding $(N\_2, N\_2')$ across seasonal and cyclical horizons to isolate $N\_2 - N\_2'$ and stamps $C\_2$ back-casts with that bridge at $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}' > t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$. That rule fails whenever destroyed archives erase $N\_2'$, altered administrative intake turns $N\_2'$ into a synthetic model imputation rather than independent dual coding, or actors game $C\_2$ reactively (`T6Performative`). Separating honest definitional updates from restatement laundering additionally locks `CounterInductiveReopen` (`T1Framing`; Canguilhem 1943/1966; [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)) under all four invariants of [Chapter 93](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md) (§3): append-only `HMAC` lineage, strict expansion ($\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$), custodian-borne escrow ($\tau\_{\mathrm{reopen}} > 0$), and independent statistical-board veto ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

### Guard T4 (Formalization): Ensuring welfare indices preserve deprivation without threshold cliffs

In welfare and poverty measurement, `T4Formalization` ($T\_4$) protects the poorest members of a population from disappearing behind blunt headcount cutoffs.

Extending his entitlement findings—where aggregate food supply stayed stable while collapsed purchasing entitlements produced famine (Sen 1981)—Sen (1976) proved that a simple poverty headcount ratio $H \in [0, 1]$ violates both monotonicity and transfer axioms. Consider $q > 0$ poor people ($H > 0$) with non-negative incomes $y\_i \ge 0$, mean income-gap ratio $I \in [0, 1)$, and poor-group Gini inequality $G\_{\mathrm{poor}} \in [0, 1]$. Set $P\_S := 0$ at $H = 0$ and $P\_S := H$ at $I = 1$. Sen's exact index then reads $P\_S = H\bigl[I + (1 - I)G\_{\mathrm{poor}}\frac{q}{q+1}\bigr] \in [0, 1]$, approaching $H[I + (1 - I)G\_{\mathrm{poor}}]$ as $q \to \infty$.

At fixed $q \ge 2$ strictly below poverty line $z$ ([Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md) §5.1, dialectical penumbra), rank-linear weights make gains to the poorest reduce $P\_S$ more than equal gains to higher-ranked poor recipients near $z$. Yet because $P\_S$ couples headcount $H = q/n$ with rank normalization $2/\bigl((q + 1)nz\bigr)$, crossing $z$ via small lifts or regressive transfers drops $H$ discontinuously by $1/n$ and makes **$P\_S$ itself discontinuous at $z$**, violating Sen's Weak Transfer Axiom whenever a regressive transfer from the poorest pushes a near-threshold recipient above $z$; rank weighting also lacks subgroup decomposability while missing uncounted deprivation across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$). Under `T4Formalization`, welfare audits therefore pair $P\_S$ with continuous, subgroup-decomposable, transfer-consistent squared poverty-gap indices $\frac{1}{n}\sum\_{y\_i < z}\bigl((z - y\_i)/z\bigr)^2$ and an uncompensable bottom-quantile floor.

## 3. The keystone, and where the thesis stops

### Guard T7 (Liability): Auditing beyond self-reported ledgers via adverse counterparties

Under `T7Liability` ($T\_7$), creditors and public bodies require verification beyond self-reported books when off-sheet deals hide real leverage. As a `(1, 1, 1)` accounting keystone (`T3*`) of Stratum `S6`, `L29` closes $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{29})$ by inheriting `T1Framing`, `T2Severity`, `T5Temporal`, and `T6Performative` via [Layer 08](58-layer-metrological-information-geometric-order.md), [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md), [Layer 23](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md), and [Layer 28](78-layer-macro-finance-econometrics-actuarial-order.md) ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)). Missing sites trigger ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)):

- **Without `T3Archive` (*Blind Undersizing*):** Reserves are sized on incomplete registries ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$ off-sheet vehicles, unbridged breaks, $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$)—*fails if* full-perimeter registers hold.
- **Without `T4Formalization` (*Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin*):** Scalar headcounts mask deep deprivation, or raw ledger dumps exceed review capacity ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md))—*fails if* non-convex buffers preserve $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$.
- **Without `T7Liability` (*Cynical Capture and Pillaging*):** Restatements and Goodhart arbitrage (Goodhart 1975) shift hidden insolvency onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$)—*fails if* clawbacks and status-blind sporadic audits (Beer's $S3^\ast$, distinct from $S\_k$, $P\_S$, and Stratum `S6`) protect reserves.

Conservation identities, digit tests, and revision margins expire when vehicles sit outside the reporting perimeter or rules are gamed performatively (`T6Performative`; Deckert, Myagkov & Ordeshook 2011): internally consistent books ratify management's chosen boundary.
