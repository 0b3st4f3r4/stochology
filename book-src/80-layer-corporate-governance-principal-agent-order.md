## 1. Multitask contracts and the incentive that cannibalises itself

Picture a lending desk, factory floor, or hospital ward where managers tie bonuses strictly to how many files a worker closes each week. Measured throughput climbs immediately. Yet workers hit that number by quietly skipping unmeasured maintenance, safety checks, and careful handoffs until an unnoticed defect causes a failure.

### Guard T1 (Framing): Protecting unmeasured duties when pay rewards a narrow metric

In principal–agent contracting, `T1Framing` ($T\_1$, `L30`'s primary site `T1*`) protects vital unmeasured work when bonuses attach solely to easy-to-count metrics.

Connecting institutional judgment across the three planes ([Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md); [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)), Holmström and Milgrom (1991) show why high-powered piece rates crowd out unmeasured duties (`T1Framing`, `T6Performative`). Consider a risk-neutral agent (risk aversion $r = 0$, distinct from hurdle rate $r\_{\mathrm{rf}}$) splitting effort $(a\_1, a\_2)$ between noisy proxy $y\_1 = a\_1 + \varepsilon\_1$ and an unmeasured safety task ($\bar{a}\_2 > 0$). That safety task gets zero piece rate ($\beta\_2 = 0$) because its variance is effectively infinite (Holmström 2016). Suppose effort carries strictly convex substitution cost $C(a\_1, a\_2) = \frac{1}{2}c\_1 a\_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}c\_2\bigl(a\_2 - (\bar{a}\_2 - \gamma a\_1)\bigr)^2$. Here $c\_1, c\_2 > 0$ are cost weights and $\gamma \ge 0$ is the substitution slope, giving positive Hessian determinant $\det\nabla^2 C = c\_1 c\_2 > 0$.

First-order conditions $\partial C/\partial a\_2 = 0$ and $\partial C/\partial a\_1 = \beta\_1$ solve directly to $a\_1^\ast(\beta\_1) = \beta\_1/c\_1$ and $a\_2^\ast(\beta\_1) = \bar{a}\_2 - (\gamma/c\_1)\beta\_1$. That holds on the interior $a\_2^\ast \ge 0$ ($\beta\_1 \le c\_1\bar{a}\_2/\gamma$ when $\gamma > 0$), clamping at $a\_2^\ast = 0$ above. Raising piece rate $\beta\_1$ drains unmeasured safety linearly—favoring muted incentives, fixed salaries, and explicit rules.

Setting $c\_1 = c\_2 = 1$ around target $a^\ast = (1, 1)$ ($\bar{a}\_2 = 1$) simplifies effort to $a\_1 = \beta\_1$ and $a\_2 = 1 - \gamma\beta\_1$ ($\gamma \ge 0$). Minimizing quadratic loss $L(\beta\_1) = (\beta\_1 - 1)^2 + (\gamma\beta\_1)^2$ yields optimal pay slope $\beta\_1^\ast = 1/(1 + \gamma^2) \in (0, 1]$ and minimum loss $L^\ast = \gamma^2/(1 + \gamma^2)$. Because $1 + \gamma^2 \ge 1 > 0$, safety effort stays bounded away from zero at $a\_2^\ast(\beta\_1^\ast) = 1 - \gamma/(1 + \gamma^2) \ge 1/2 > 0$. At zero substitution ($\gamma = 0$), first-best pay holds ($\beta\_1^\ast = 1, L^\ast = 0$). As substitution grows ($\gamma \to \infty$), optimal pay collapses toward a flat salary ($\beta\_1^\ast \to 0$) while loss approaches $L^\ast \to 1$.

Everywhere on $\gamma > 0$, $\beta\_1^\ast + L^\ast = 1$ with opposing sensitivities $\partial L^\ast/\partial \gamma = -\partial \beta\_1^\ast/\partial \gamma = 2\gamma/(1 + \gamma^2)^2 > 0$ (equal at $\gamma = 1$). Doubling substitution $\gamma \mapsto 2\gamma$ cuts optimal incentive weight by $(1 + \gamma^2)/(1 + 4\gamma^2) < 1$ and raises loss by $4(1 + \gamma^2)/(1 + 4\gamma^2) > 1$. Across three symmetric tasks ($c\_k = 1$, $\bar{a}\_k = 1$, $\gamma > 0$), $L\_3(\beta\_1) = (\beta\_1 - 1)^2 + 2(\gamma\beta\_1)^2$ shrinks pay to $\beta\_1^\ast = 1/(1 + 2\gamma^2)$ with loss $L\_3^\ast = 2\gamma^2/(1 + 2\gamma^2)$. Only complementarity ($\gamma < 0$), splitting tasks across distinct roles, or granting residual asset ownership reverses the distortion (Holmström & Milgrom 1991; Holmström 2016).

When opaque algorithmic proxies replace human supervision ($\beta\_2=0$), pay attenuation sharpens. Consider $m\ge 1$ unmeasured substitution margins $\gamma(P)\in[\underline{\gamma},\overline{\gamma}]$ ($\underline{\gamma}\ge 0$, bounded $\overline{\gamma} < \infty$) across $P\in\mathcal{U}$ (or $\overline{\gamma}:=\sup\_{P\in\mathcal{U}}\lvert\gamma(P)\rvert \in [0, \infty)$ when complementarity $\gamma<0$ with interior $a\_{2,k}^\ast\ge 1$ is admitted) and carry-over $\psi\ge 0$, setting effective substitution $\tilde{\gamma}:=(1+\psi)\overline{\gamma} < \infty$. Because $L\_{1+m}(\beta\_1;\gamma)=(\beta\_1-1)^2+m(1+\psi)^2\gamma^2\beta\_1^2$ rises in $\lvert\gamma\rvert$ for $\beta\_1>0$, minimax contracting chooses $\beta\_1^{\mathrm{DRO}}=1/(1+m\tilde{\gamma}^2)\le 1/(1+\gamma(P)^2)$ across $P\in\mathcal{U}$ with worst-case loss $m\tilde{\gamma}^2/(1+m\tilde{\gamma}^2)<1$, preserving $a\_{2,k}^\ast\ge 1-\tilde{\gamma}\beta\_1^{\mathrm{DRO}}\ge 1/2>0$. By contrast, opaque dynamic rules $\beta\_{1,t}=1$ hiding external shocks (Holmström 1979) clamp unmeasured effort to zero ($a\_{2,k}^\ast=0$) on the **substitution branch** whenever realized $(1+\psi)\gamma(P)\ge 1$—whereas complementarity ($\gamma(P) < 0$) raises $a\_{2,k}^\ast = 1 - (1+\psi)\gamma(P) > 1$ instead of clamping ([Layer 27](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md); [Chapter 87](87-aiming-against-counterfactual-winds.md); [Chapter 91](91-when-the-target-reads-the-archer.md)). Moreover, muting linear slopes $\beta\_1^{\mathrm{DRO}}$ protects safety only when contracts also forbid **implicit binary throughput or non-renewal cliffs** ($\mathbf{1}\lbrace y\_1 \ge y\_{\min}\rbrace$), which otherwise coerce precarious staff to strip $a\_2 \to 0$ even at $\beta\_1 = 0$.

## 2. Limited liability, risk shifting, and the case that refutes the remedy

### Guard T7 (Liability): Stopping downside caps and lean staffing from shifting ruin onto outsiders

Under corporate charters, `T7Liability` ($T\_7$) protects outsiders when limited liability rewards risky gambles whose losses spill elsewhere. Separating ownership from control (Berle & Means 1932) lets rent extraction pose as performance pay (Bebchuk & Fried 2004). Under limited liability for ex-ante effort (`T7Liability`), Innes (1990) proves two contract theorems: unrestricted return shapes yield a discontinuous *live-or-die* contract paying all output $y$ to the manager above a threshold and zero below, while bilateral principal monotonicity ($0 \le R'(y) \le 1$) gives investors **standard debt** $\min(y, D)$ and managers a **levered call** $\max(y - D, 0)$ above $D \ge 0$ (Holmström 2016; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

Although linear above $D$, the hinge at $D$ on $y \ge 0$ makes that payoff globally convex, rewarding mean-preserving-spread risk-shifting (Jensen & Meckling 1976; Taleb 2018) that privatizes gains while offloading losses. Deferral and clawback escrow $\gamma\_{\mathrm{clawback}}$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md), distinct from §1's substitution slope $\gamma$) restore downside exposure **only when vested in an uncaptured external trustee or court outside board waiver or option-repricing authority, spanning physical hazard latencies ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{hazard}}$) with bans on derivative collars and hedging alongside parent-enterprise veil-piercing across undercapitalized hazardous subsidiaries ($q \ge c$)**. Raising interest rates symmetrically induces adverse selection and risk-shifting, rationing credit as lender expected returns bend down (Stiglitz & Weiss 1981).

Long-Term Capital Management tested linear equity without leverage floors: in 1998 partners held stakes junior to creditors while running roughly 125 billion dollars in assets on 4.7 billion in equity (Lowenstein 2000). Since equity cushion $E/A$ ($0 < E < A$) equals the reciprocal of leverage $A/E$, an asset drop matching $E/A$ wipes out $E$ and spills losses onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$). Neither debt nor equity prevents ruin once survival headroom $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ drops below recovery latency $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}} = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)). Where exogenous swings drive output, informative external signals prevent unfair scapegoating under Holmström's (1979) Informativeness Principle ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

Accounting illusions and thin physical slack follow the exact same pattern (`T3Archive` vs. `T5Temporal`). Sloan (1996) documented the accrual anomaly: accounting accruals persist less than cash flows, leaving earnings-fixated investors with lower future returns. Mirroring accrual exhaustion, lean queues strip idle buffer $(1 - \rho\_q)$ as queue utilization $\rho\_q \in (0, 1)$ (distinct from §4's data-defect correlation $\rho\_{R,Y}$) rises ([Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md)): in stationary $GI/G/1$ queues with mean service time $T\_s > 0$, Pareto tail index $\alpha\_{\mathrm{Pareto}} > 2$ ([Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md)), and finite variance ($0 < c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 < \infty$), Kingman's (1961) asymptote $\mathbb{E}[W\_q] \approx \frac{\rho\_q}{1 - \rho\_q}\frac{c\_a^2 + c\_s^2}{2}T\_s$ diverges as $\rho\_q \to 1^{-}$ (unlike deterministic $D/D/1$, where $c\_a^2 = c\_s^2 = 0$ keeps $\mathbb{E}[W\_q] = 0$).

## 3. The incentive constraint is a constraint on reports

### Guard T6 (Performative): Keeping self-reported metrics truthful and frontline warnings safe

In corporate reporting, `T6Performative` ($T\_6$) protects truth-telling when executives self-report pay inputs and precarious workers risk firing.

Hurwicz (1972) formalized the general constraint on self-reported information (`T6Performative`). Suppose participants report private types $(\theta\_i, \theta\_{-i}) \in \Theta\_i \times \Theta\_{-i}$ into an outcome rule $g$, yielding utility $u\_i$ for agent $i$. The mechanism is dominant-strategy incentive compatible when truthful reporting $\theta\_i$ always beats any false report $\theta\_i' \in \Theta\_i$, regardless of others' reports $\theta\_{-i}$:

$$u\_i\bigl(g(\theta\_i, \theta\_{-i}), \theta\_i\bigr) \ge u\_i\bigl(g(\theta\_i', \theta\_{-i}), \theta\_i\bigr).$$

Read aloud, a mechanism makes honesty a dominant strategy only if reporting your true type $\theta\_i$ yields at least as much utility as submitting any false claim $\theta\_i'$, no matter what everyone else reports.

In exchange economies, Hurwicz (1972) proved no dominant-strategy incentive-compatible mechanism always satisfies Pareto efficiency and individual rationality together. Suppose executives self-report the score entering $g$ without misreporting penalties, and utility $u\_i(g, \theta\_i)$ rises strictly in pay $g$. Then any strictly increasing schedule $g(\theta\_i')$ destroys truthfulness at every non-maximal type $\theta\_i < \sup \Theta\_i$ with $\theta\_i' > \theta\_i$—not merely at bonus cliffs—just as any strictly decreasing schedule induces under-reporting ($\theta\_i' < \theta\_i$), leaving constant flat pay ($\beta\_1 = 0$) alone dominant-strategy truthful across all types. Adding expected misreporting cost $\kappa(\theta\_i', \theta\_i) \ge 0$ (audit probability times clawback) sets truth condition $u\_i\bigl(g(\theta\_i', \theta\_{-i}), \theta\_i\bigr) - u\_i\bigl(g(\theta\_i, \theta\_{-i}), \theta\_i\bigr) \le \kappa(\theta\_i', \theta\_i)$. Under bounded attachable wealth ($\kappa \le p\_{\mathrm{audit}} W\_{\mathrm{escrow}}$) or gambling-for-resurrection convexity near insolvency, private gains $\Delta u\_i > \kappa$ still defeat truth-telling unless full escrow parity and mandatory insolvency substitution hold. Muting piece rates (§1) curbs effort substitution, while pricing misreports above private gain (§2) secures honest disclosure.

Statutory protections reach at most permanent or tenured employees (who still suffer isolation; Alford 2001), inviting legal `T7Liability` arbitrage (Near & Miceli 1996; [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)). Fixed-term staff, contractors, and fellows—closest to physical hazards and external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$)—face swift dismissal, passive contract non-renewal, hostile shift reassignment, or off-ledger scope starvation, with lasting career, income, and health harm even among shielded whistleblowers ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)). Safe disclosure therefore requires pre-dismissal external multi-party bitemporal custody ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}}\le t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$, `HMAC`-`SHA-256`; Snodgrass 2000; Krawczyk et al. 1997; free of punitive surveillance and small-team de-anonymization), an **automatic rebuttable presumption of retaliation** on any subsequent non-renewal or adverse scope change paired with an **escrow-funded interim wage-continuation stay**, and collective external sporadic-audit channels (Beer's $S3^\ast$, distinct from Triad axes $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$; $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}=0$; Beer 1972; Fricker 2007)—independent inspectorates, ombudsmen, and trade or professional bodies—reviewed asynchronously outside live operational queues within human attention capacity and contractor survival headroom ($\tau\_{\mathrm{external}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{worker}}$; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

## 4. The collapses, and where the thesis stops
