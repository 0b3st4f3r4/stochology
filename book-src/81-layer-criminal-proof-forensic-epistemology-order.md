## 1. Loss ratios, three standards, and the odds of a suspect

Picture a courtroom facing the very same set of facts under two different legal rules: a civil contract dispute over money versus a criminal trial over someone's liberty. Society demands far stronger proof before locking someone in prison because a false conviction causes deep, largely irreversible harm compared with a mistaken acquittal. Every standard of criminal proof begins from that asymmetry of error costs, long before any forensic test enters the record.

### Guard T1 (Framing): Fixing the error-cost ratio and rejecting naked base rates

In criminal procedure, `T1Framing` ($T\_1$) protects the moral error-cost ratio from collapsing into administrative convenience or mistaking naked group prevalence for individualized guilt.

Across the three mediation planes ([Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md); [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)), a legal standard of proof encodes an error-cost ratio rather than an intrinsic property of evidence ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)). Under expected regret (Pauker & Kassirer 1975), threshold probability $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/(C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}}) \in (0, 1)$ balances wrongful-conviction cost $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = L\_{\mathrm{FP}} > 0$ against wrongful-acquittal cost $L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$ ($L\_{\mathrm{FP}} = 0$ is only a degenerate limiting boundary). Contrast that with the cost–loss form $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = \min\bigl(1,\mkern3mu C/(\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}})\bigr)$ ($C, L\_{\mathrm{loss}} > 0$, $\eta \in (0, 1]$, writing $L\_{\mathrm{loss}}$ for gross preventable loss distinct from error costs $L\_{\mathrm{FP}}, L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1). Both thresholds coincide ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$) **if and only if** $\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}} > C$ and $C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/L\_{\mathrm{FN}} = C/(\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}} - C)$, and never when $\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}} \le C$ (where $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = 1 > p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$). Legal burdens therefore read $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ directly.

Writing $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = k\mkern3mu L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ ($k > 0$) yields $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = k/(k + 1)$ and orders the three classical standards cleanly. Preponderance of the evidence sets parity ($k = 1$, $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 1/2$, where exact ties defeat the party bearing the persuasion burden). Clear and convincing proof raises the ratio to $1 < k < 10$. Proof beyond a reasonable doubt encodes Blackstone's (1765–1769) ten-to-one rule ($k = 10$, $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 10/11$): better ten guilty people escape than one innocent suffer.

Inspecting both extremes clarifies how that ratio works. As wrongful-conviction cost vanishes ($L\_{\mathrm{FP}} \to 0^+$), the threshold drops to $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} \to 0$. Conversely, as wrongful-acquittal cost vanishes ($L\_{\mathrm{FN}} \to 0^+$ with $L\_{\mathrm{FP}} > 0$), $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} \to 1$. The odds cutoff $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}/(1 - p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}) = L\_{\mathrm{FP}}/L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ then diverges, undefined at $L\_{\mathrm{FN}} = 0$ and unreachable for finite likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR}$ whenever $\mathbb{P}(E \mid H\_d) > 0$.

Under Cromwell's interior prior odds $O\_0 \in (0, \infty)$ and likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR} \in (0, \infty)$, posterior odds $O\_0 \cdot \mathrm{LR}$ must exceed $L\_{\mathrm{FP}}/L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$. In a uniform closed suspect pool of size $N \ge 2$, prior odds sit at $O\_0 = 1/(N - 1)$ (undefined at $N = 1$), forcing required $\mathrm{LR}$ to scale with $N$. Because claiming an exhaustive closed pool ($\sum\_{i=1}^N P(H\_i) = 1$) violates Cromwell's floor outside the suspect list and allows an unlogged trawl over a database of size $M \ge N$ (`T3` omission) to inflate posterior odds via post-hoc pool shrinkage, valid `T1Framing` must carry an explicit outside-pool hypothesis ($P(H\_{\mathrm{out}}) \ge \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}} > 0$) and pre-register total database comparisons $M$ (`T3Archive`, [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md); $e$-BH multiplicity control, Wang & Ramdas 2022) before computing $O\_0$ or a cold-hit $\mathrm{LR}$. Even then, clearing a source-level cutoff never proves offence-level guilt. Bare class frequency without individualized evidential weight ($V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}}) = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}}/(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} + n\_0) = 0$ at $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} = 0$, paired with qualitative clue depth $K/(K + I\_0)$ and error-stake anchor $n\_0 > 0$; Cohen 1977; Keynes 1921; [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1; credal scale in [Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md)) fails criminal proof just as it bars civil liability ([Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)).

## 2. The prosecutor's fallacy and the archive a conviction rested on

### Guard T2 (Severity): Testing forensic weight through likelihood ratios and blind trials

In forensic science, `T2Severity` ($T\_2$) protects verdicts from transposed conditional odds, untested feature-matching claims, and corrupted custody archives.

Let $E$ denote forensic evidence, $I \equiv H\_d$ the defence hypothesis of innocence, and $H\_p$ the prosecution hypothesis. The prosecutor's fallacy confuses $\mathbb{P}(E \mid I)$, the chance of seeing the evidence if innocent, with $\mathbb{P}(I \mid E)$, the chance of innocence given the evidence, omitting both prior odds $O\_0$ and relative likelihood under $H\_p$.

In Sally Clark's 1999 conviction, a paediatrician cited a $1$-in-$8{,}543$ rate for an unexplained infant death in an affluent non-smoking home and squared it to $1$ in $73$ million, wrongly assuming independent sibling deaths while ignoring prior odds (R v Clark [2003] EWCA Crim 1020; Royal Statistical Society 2001). Calling that product baseless, the Royal Statistical Society (2001) stressed relative likelihood across hypotheses. In 2003, the appeal court quashed the conviction after undisclosed post-mortem microbiology revealed *Staphylococcus aureus* across multiple sites including cerebrospinal fluid, adding that the statistical error alone would likely have warranted reversal (R v Clark [2003] EWCA Crim 1020).

A valid likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H\_p)/\mathbb{P}(E \mid H\_d)$ ($\mathbb{P}(E \mid H\_d) > 0$) needs empirical error rates from blind black-box studies outside examiner discretion (PCAST 2016) to separate method error from random-match probabilities and adjust cold-hit matches for logged database-search multiplicity $M$. In Ulery, Hicklin, Buscaglia and Roberts (2011), $169$ fingerprint examiners judged roughly $100$ pairs each from a $744$-pair pool: among $4{,}083$ nonmated comparisons judged of value for individualization, they made six false positives ($0.1$ percent overall false-positive rate), and $85$ percent made at least one false negative ($7.5$ percent overall false-negative rate), though independent re-examination caught every false positive (Ulery, Hicklin, Buscaglia & Roberts 2011).

Reviewing feature comparison broadly, PCAST (2016) concluded that bitemark analysis lacks foundational validity. It bounded two latent-print black-box false-positive rates up to $1$ in $306$ ($1$ in $604$ best estimate) and $1$ in $18$ ($1$ in $24$ best estimate), warning that open tests and biasing context understate casework error. It also cited a firearms black-box study at $1$ in $66$, with a confidence bound of $1$ in $46$ (PCAST 2016).

When an entire forensic laboratory compromises custody, case-by-case patchwork fails. Between 2003 and 2012, a chemist at Massachusetts's William A. Hinton State Laboratory tainted and misreported drug samples (Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court 2014). The state's highest court initially granted affected defendants a conclusive presumption of egregious government misconduct while still requiring individualized proof of plea impact (Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court 2014). Once individual motions proved unworkable, in January 2017 the court rejected global vacatur and ordered three-letter prosecutorial triage (Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court 2017). That order yielded an April 2017 declaratory judgment (amended June 2017) and a Special Master report recording over $21{,}500$ cases moved for dismissal out of slightly fewer than $22{,}000$ affected (Massachusetts Supreme Judicial Court 2017).

Statistically, unrecorded tampering destroys a certificate's diagnostic weight: when clean and tainted files yield indistinguishable certificates ($\mathbb{P}(E \mid H\_{\mathrm{clean}}) = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H\_{\mathrm{tainted}})$), certificate weight collapses to $\mathrm{LR}\_{\mathrm{cert}} = 1$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), dropping cases without independent proof below $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ whenever prior guilt odds sit below $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ ($p\_0 < p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$). Any binary triage screen with contamination prevalence $\pi \in (0, 1)$, sensitivity $\mathrm{Se}$, and false-positive rate $\mathrm{FPR}$ obeys $\mathrm{PPV} = \pi\mathrm{Se}/(\pi\mathrm{Se} + (1 - \pi)\mathrm{FPR})$. For an informative screen ($\mathrm{Se} > \mathrm{FPR}$), degrading toward indistinguishable flagging ($\mathrm{Se} \approx \mathrm{FPR} \to 1$) pulls positive predictive value $\mathrm{PPV}$ down to $\pi < 1$, whereas an incomplete screen ($\mathrm{Se} < 1$) at high prevalence collapses negative predictive value $\mathrm{NPV} = (1 - \pi)(1 - \mathrm{FPR})/\bigl((1 - \pi)(1 - \mathrm{FPR}) + \pi(1 - \mathrm{Se})\bigr)$. Proof thresholds judge one defendant; a broken custody archive invalidates every dossier built on it.

## 3. Fairness, pretrial delay, and where the thesis stops

For interior group base rates $p\_A, p\_B \in (0, 1)$, non-trivial predictions ($\mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1) > 0$ across groups), imperfect precision ($0 < \mathrm{PPV}\_A = \mathrm{PPV}\_B < 1$), non-zero sensitivity ($\mathrm{FNR}\_A = \mathrm{FNR}\_B < 1$), and $\mathrm{FPR}\_B > 0$ when $p\_A \ne p\_B$, Chouldechova's (2017) impossibility identity ([Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.3.2; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3) proves that maintaining predictive parity ($\mathrm{PPV}\_A = \mathrm{PPV}\_B$) alongside equal false-negative rates forces false-positive ratios to track base-rate odds. Broward County risk scores display that split. Among two-year non-reoffenders, $44.85$ percent of Black defendants versus $23.45$ percent of white defendants were labelled higher risk (Larson, Mattu, Kirchner & Angwin 2016). Among reoffenders, however, $47.72$ percent of white versus $27.99$ percent of Black defendants were labelled lower risk (Larson, Mattu, Kirchner & Angwin 2016).

Because base rate $p$ measures re-arrest inside a selectively policed archive ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$; [Layer 09](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md); [Layer 24](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md)) further censored by pretrial detention itself (detained defendants cannot rearrest, leaving outcome labels unobserved), stratifying by employment or neighbourhood shifts cell base rates without preserving conditional parity ($\mathrm{PPV}\_{A,s} = \mathrm{PPV}\_{B,s}$). Error incidence depends jointly on parity metric, partition, cutoff, and label sign ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.6.2). Preventing parity, partition, or sign shopping under trade-secret opacity therefore requires `T3Archive` selective-label Manski bounds, open pre-registered calibration audits, and lexicographic **Scanlonian non-aggregative side-constraints** (Scanlon 1998) over full affected-party harm ($L\_{\mathrm{inst}} \ll L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$) ahead of uniform social-cost minimization (Corbett-Davies et al. 2017), tying risk scores to credal scales ([Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md)) and strategic gaming ([Layer 27](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md)).

Pretrial detention turns courtroom backlog into plea coercion. Dobbie, Goldin and Yang (2016) estimate local average treatment effects at the bail-leniency margin across $328{,}492$ cases in Philadelphia and $97{,}538$ in Miami-Dade. Pre-trial release lowers conviction (`Any Guilty Offense`) by $15.6$ percentage points ($27.3$ percent from the detained mean) and guilty pleas by $12.0$ percentage points ($27.5$ percent). Three to four years later, it also raises formal employment by $10.2$ percentage points ($26.9$ percent) and tax filing by $4.3$ percentage points ($14.0$ percent). Yet marginal release raises failure to appear by $15.0$ percentage points in Philadelphia and pre-disposition rearrest by $7.6$ percentage points (Dobbie, Goldin, & Yang 2016).

Overloaded defender dockets push queue latency $\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{queue}})$ ([Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)) beyond human deliberative capacity, while unpreserved biological traces and witness memory undergo taphonomic decay ($S(T) = e^{-\lambda T}$, decay rate $\lambda$ distinct from corrupted share below; [Layer 23](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md)) over pretrial windows $T$, jointly driving $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)). Even under full pretrial release and fast queues, prosecutorial charge stacking ($L\_{\mathrm{trial}} \gg L\_{\mathrm{plea}}$) paired with plea waivers of *Brady* and forensic discovery coerces risk-averse defendants below $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 10/11$. Coerced pleas then act as an irreversible stopping hazard ([Layer 26](76-layer-sequential-decision-bandit-real-options-order.md)) exporting ruin onto defendants, households, and external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$), requiring mandatory pre-plea *Brady* and calibration disclosure, bans on discovery or reopening plea waivers, and an independent probable-cause and admissible-$\mathrm{LR}$ gate (`T1`/`T2`) on charged counts prior to bargaining.

Posterior updating stops, across distinct regimes rather than one algebraic state, when blind error rates are missing (leaving the forensic $\mathrm{LR}$ unidentifiable), when clean and tainted certificates are observationally indistinguishable ($\mathrm{LR}\_{\mathrm{cert}} = 1$), when individualized weight vanishes ($V = 0$), or when endogenous corruption drives lab failure toward totality (corruption fraction $\lambda \to 1^-$; Manski 2003, 2026; [Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)), continuously widening identification intervals $[(1 - \lambda)P\_{\mathrm{obs}},\, (1 - \lambda)P\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \lambda]$ to the vacuous $[0, 1]$ while the institutional burden threshold $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ stays fixed by error losses.

Pre-committed loss asymmetry therefore requires courts to exclude uncalibrated artifacts and judge strictly on independent evidence against $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$, backed by **adverse Manski inferences or dismissal/vacatur sanctions** against state custody spoliation rather than mere exclusion (which otherwise rewards losing exculpatory samples pre-seal).
