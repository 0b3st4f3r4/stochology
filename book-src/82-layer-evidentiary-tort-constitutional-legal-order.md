1. Derive the three standards of proof from the expected-regret threshold, separate that form from the cost-loss form, and resolve the Gatecrasher Paradox with Keynes's weight.

## 1. Two thresholds, three standards, and the class that does not aggregate

Imagine a packed rodeo stadium where fewer than half the spectators bought tickets at the gate. Pick anyone at random and odds say they entered unpaid, yet no civil judge will order a ticket refund from gate math alone without individualized proof. Civil proof, tort liability, and constitutional rules all wrestle with that divide between group averages, tail risks, and individual accountability.

### Guard T1 (Framing): Requiring individualized case weight before thresholds trigger liability

In civil adjudication, `T1Framing` ($T\_1$) protects individual parties from being found liable on bare group averages when case-specific evidential weight is absent.

Two decision rules govern judging before outcomes settle ([Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md)) across the three mediation planes ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1). Cost–loss sets $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = \min\bigl(1,\mkern3mu C/(\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}})\bigr)$ ($C, L\_{\mathrm{loss}} > 0$, $\eta \in (0, 1]$, writing $L\_{\mathrm{loss}}$ for gross preventable loss distinct from wrongful-acquittal cost $L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ and tort harm $L$), whereas expected regret sets $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/(C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}}) \in (0, 1)$ ($C\_{\mathrm{FP}}, L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$); both coincide if and only if $\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}} > C$ and $C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/L\_{\mathrm{FN}} = C/(\eta L\_{\mathrm{loss}} - C)$. Legal proof follows the second rule. Preponderance demands $p > 1/2$ at parity $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ (so an exact tie $p = 1/2$ fails the persuasion burden). Clear and convincing proof sets $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = k\_{\mathrm{ratio}} L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ ($1 < k\_{\mathrm{ratio}} < 10$, $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = k\_{\mathrm{ratio}}/(k\_{\mathrm{ratio}} + 1)$), while proof beyond reasonable doubt uses Blackstone's ten-to-one ratio ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 10/11$; Blackstone 1765–1769).

Yet clearing $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ on naked class prevalence still fails individual proof. In L. Jonathan Cohen's Gatecrasher Paradox (Cohen 1977), $1{,}000$ spectators fill a rodeo where $499$ tickets sold, meaning $501$ entered unpaid ($p = 0.501 > 1/2$), yet courts refuse recovery against any picked attendee. Keynes (1921) separated probability from evidential weight qualitatively as knowledge against ignorance. Stochology indexes case-specific operational weight via $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}}) = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} / (n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} + n\_0)$ alongside qualitative clue depth $K/(K + I\_0)$ ($n\_0, I\_0 > 0$ anchored to affected civil stakes; [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1; credal measurement in [Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md)): bare base rates leave $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} = 0$ and $V = 0$.

Group liability also runs into Chouldechova's (2017) disparity bound on interior group base rates $p\_A, p\_B \in (0, 1)$ with non-trivial predictions ($\mathbb{P}(\hat{Y} = 1) > 0$), imperfect precision ($0 < \mathrm{PPV} < 1$), $\mathrm{FNR} < 1$, and $\mathrm{FPR}\_B > 0$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.3.2; [Layer 31](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md)). It breaks individualized civil proof in three mathematical ways. First, under fixed gatecrasher totals ($\sum\_i G\_i = 501$), individual indicators correlate negatively ($\mathrm{Cov}(G\_i, G\_j) < 0$), so applying the cutoff per person condemns all $1{,}000$ spectators. Second, across multiple legal elements, product rule $P(E\_1 \cap E\_2) = P(E\_1)P(E\_2)$ breaks under correlation. Third, group averages ([Layer 14](64-layer-taxonomy-systematics-ontology-order.md)) never replace proof of individual causation, meaning that non-signature mass toxic harms whose attributable fraction falls below individual preponderance require a separate **bounded collective/proportional liability circuit** (§2) capped strictly at aggregate epidemiological excess harm rather than letting $V = 0$ summary dismissals immunize polluters.

## 2. Tort liability and the two circuits

### Guard T4 (Formalization): Pricing convex harm and solvency caps instead of mean damage

In tort law, `T4Formalization` ($T\_4$) protects potential victims when mean-value negligence rules and thin equity caps hide catastrophic tail harm.

Learned Hand's negligence formula in *United States v. Carroll Towing Co.* (Hand 1947) states $B < PL$, the cost–loss rule at $\eta = 1$, $C = B$, comparing care burden $B > 0$ against expected harm $PL$ ($P \in [0, 1]$, $L > 0$). Negligence regulates how much care an actor takes, yet once due care is met it leaves total activity volume unpriced; strict liability prices both residual risk and activity scale.

Hand's inequality breaks in two directions under real losses. First, evaluating harm at average hazard $\phi(\mathbb{E}[X])$ rather than expected harm $\mathbb{E}[\phi(X)]$ for convex loss $\phi$ ($\mathbb{E}[|X|], \mathbb{E}[|\phi(X)|] < \infty$) misses Jensen's gap $\Delta\_J = \mathbb{E}[\phi(X)] - \phi(\mathbb{E}[X]) \ge 0$ and heavy tails ([Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md); [Layer 05](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md)). Second, capping collectible damages at firm equity $W\_{\mathrm{eq}} < L$ leaves recoverable loss concave ($\min(W\_{\mathrm{eq}}, L) = W\_{\mathrm{eq}}$), cutting the firm's private threshold to $B < PW\_{\mathrm{eq}}$. Symmetrically, limited-liability equity holds a convex payoff $\max(A - X, 0)$ in assets $A$ against loss $X$, encouraging risky asset substitution toward higher variance (Jensen & Meckling 1976). Under ex-ante effort, Innes (1990) likewise identifies the discontinuous live-or-die contract ([Layer 30](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md)).

The institutional remedy splits into two distinct circuits matching Sosa's (2015) *télos*–*skopós* ledger ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)). On the compensation circuit (strict liability), injured third parties recover immediately from an ex-ante capitalized reserve even when an operator's act was careful and unlucky; for non-signature mass toxic harms where individual case weight sits at $V = 0$, a parallel **epidemiological excess-risk or market-share escrow** allocates proportional contributions strictly capped at aggregate excess harm without permitting over-recovery. On the accountability circuit, punitive sanctions, bonus clawbacks, and license revocations target reckless or inapt conduct, even when lucky, preventing firms from scapegoating frontline operators for under-capitalized corporate buffers. Closing judgment-proofing arbitrage further requires tort-victim priority above secured affiliate debt, parent and beneficial-owner veil-piercing across [Layer 30](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md) control chains, statutory bans on pre-dispute forced arbitration, class waivers, and hazard nondisclosure agreements (which blind `T3` registries), and treating statutory or agency standards strictly as **non-preemptive minimum floors** rather than liability ceilings.

## 3. Constitution and statute as circuit breakers

### Guard T7 (Liability): Locking pre-commitments, witness shields, and supermajority vetoes

In constitutional governance, `T7Liability` ($T\_7$) protects citizens and whistleblowers from retroactive decrees, silenced warnings, and concentrated majorities exporting harm onto outsiders.

A constitution acts as Elster's (1979) pre-commitment device. Bans on *ex post facto* laws and bills of attainder enforce syntactic sealing and validity windows prior to outcomes ([Layer 06](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md)), blocking retroactive rule rewrites, while bicameral and judicial vetoes add conjunctive anti-rigging gates. Whistleblower shields and unsafe-work refusal set status discounting at zero ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) along Beer's (1972) uncompromised algedonic channel $S3^\ast$ ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)). To stop high-frequency alert floods (`Compliance DoS`) from saturating review ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ pairs automated telemetry streams with corroborated sequential likelihood-ratio triage and non-self-extending sunsets inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Layer 33](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md)), while keeping a distinct, low-threshold intake path for **sworn lone-insider disclosures** (such as suppressed corporate memos) routed directly to external $S3^\ast$ inspectorates or ombudsmen with pre-termination vault custody ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.2.2) so corroboration requirements never lock out isolated whistleblowers. Automated-decision rights protections likewise bind declared operational envelopes and reversible human review inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ without reducing fundamental rights to risk-neutral cost–loss trades.

Limits on constitutional rights invoke structured proportionality: any measure restricting constitutional principle $\Pi\_i$ to advance competing principle $\Pi\_j$ (distinct from Three Planes $P\_1$–$P\_3$) must be legitimate, suitable, necessary, and proportionate *stricto sensu*. Alexy (2002) formalizes that balance through weight formula $W\_{i,j}^{\mathrm{Alexy}}$ (distinct from firm equity $W\_{\mathrm{eq}}$), where $I\_i, W\_i^{\mathrm{abs}}, R\_i^{\mathrm{prem}}$ and $I\_j, W\_j^{\mathrm{abs}}, R\_j^{\mathrm{prem}}$ measure interference or satisfaction importance, abstract weight, and empirical premise reliability (distinct from Refusals $R\_1$–$R\_3$ and reserves $R\_t$):

$$W\_{i,j}^{\mathrm{Alexy}} = \frac{I\_i \cdot W\_i^{\mathrm{abs}} \cdot R\_i^{\mathrm{prem}}}{I\_j \cdot W\_j^{\mathrm{abs}} \cdot R\_j^{\mathrm{prem}}}.$$

Read aloud, $W\_{i,j}^{\mathrm{Alexy}}$ compares the interference–weight–reliability product for $\Pi\_i$ against $\Pi\_j$, favoring $\Pi\_i$ above $1$, $\Pi\_j$ below $1$, and deadlocking at $1$.

On Alexy's geometric triadic scale ($\lbrace 2^0, 2^1, 2^2 \rbrace$ for $I, W^{\mathrm{abs}}$; $\lbrace 2^0, 2^{-1}, 2^{-2} \rbrace$ for $R^{\mathrm{prem}}$), every factor stays positive ($I\_i, W\_i^{\mathrm{abs}}, R\_i^{\mathrm{prem}}, I\_j, W\_j^{\mathrm{abs}}, R\_j^{\mathrm{prem}} > 0$). That positivity preserves reciprocity $W\_{j,i}^{\mathrm{Alexy}} = 1/W\_{i,j}^{\mathrm{Alexy}}$ and penalizes weak factual premises via lower $R\_j^{\mathrm{prem}}$. Whereas [Layer 06](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md) audits syntactic gates, scale invariance sits in [Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md). Under Stevens's (1946) ordinal limits, geometric triadic labels remain ordinal grades whose verdicts flip under monotone relabeling unless an explicit external ratio chart holds. Crucially, multiplying $(I \cdot W^{\mathrm{abs}} \cdot R^{\mathrm{prem}})$ is **compensatory** on both sides of the ratio: high $I\_j W\_j^{\mathrm{abs}}$ can offset weak $R\_j^{\mathrm{prem}}$, and high $I\_i W\_i^{\mathrm{abs}}$ can offset weak $R\_i^{\mathrm{prem}}$. Proportionality therefore complies with Stochology's non-compensatory triad discipline only when **symmetric** epistemic floors ($\min(R\_i^{\mathrm{prem}}, R\_j^{\mathrm{prem}}) \ge R\_{\min} > 0$, calibrated at $\mathrm{PRI} = 0$ independent empirical grounding above the weakest discrete grade) and vital/core-rights floors clear first, confining $W\_{i,j}^{\mathrm{Alexy}}$ strictly to **post-floor tie-breaking** on verified ratio scales.

Real legal orders also generate **normative antinomies** where statutory duties conflict directly, commanding both $O(p)$ and $O(\neg p)$, as when statutory confidentiality collides with an imminent-harm warning duty. Classical deontic logic suffers **deontic explosion** ($\lbrace O(p), O(\neg p) \rbrace \vdash O(q)$ for arbitrary $q$), trivializing the legal code or inviting arbitrary executive override. **Paraconsistent deontic logic** (da Costa & Carnielli 1986) blocks deontic explosion to isolate conflicting obligations locally, allowing courts to apply Alexy's (2002) post-floor proportionality strictly above non-compensatory epistemic and vital floors, or escalate indivisible binary binds ($a \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$) to attested constitutional reopening. Historically, closed axiomatic legal codes are themselves contingent rather than natural law: Mesopotamian collections such as Hammurabi's stele preserved retrospective, casuistic *dīnāt mīšarim* ("just verdicts") rather than general deductive statutes applied by courts (Bottéro 1992).

Constitutional entrenchment extends that gate across time. Assume single-peaked preferences in one dimension ($d = 1$) with continuous strictly increasing ideal-point distribution $F$, and suppose defeating status quo $x$ requires a supermajority exceeding fraction $q\_{\mathrm{sup}} \in (1/2, 1)$ (distinct from internalized liability $q$). Then $x$ resists both rightward coalitions ($1 - F(x) \le q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$) and leftward coalitions ($F(x) \le q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$) across the closed core between quantiles $F^{-1}(1 - q\_{\mathrm{sup}})$ and $F^{-1}(q\_{\mathrm{sup}})$:

$$F^{-1}(1 - q\_{\mathrm{sup}}) \le x \le F^{-1}(q\_{\mathrm{sup}}).$$

Read aloud, any status quo $x$ inside $[F^{-1}(1 - q\_{\mathrm{sup}}), F^{-1}(q\_{\mathrm{sup}})]$ resists both leftward and rightward supermajority coalitions of size $q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$.

Strict coalition resistance ($1 - F(x) < q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$ and $F(x) < q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$) holds across the open interior $(F^{-1}(1 - q\_{\mathrm{sup}}), F^{-1}(q\_{\mathrm{sup}}))$. As $q\_{\mathrm{sup}} \to (1/2)^+$, the core narrows to the median $\lbrace F^{-1}(1/2) \rbrace$; raising $q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$ buys deliberate inertia, cutting external harm on outvoted minorities while raising decision costs (Buchanan & Tullock 1962). Yet that stable core needs one dimension: in $d \ge 2$ dimensions, moderate supermajorities leave an empty core, requiring $q\_{\mathrm{sup}} > d/(d + 1)$ by Helly's theorem to prevent cycles, while legislative *logrolling* still builds minimal winning coalitions. Worse, because $F$ counts only enfranchised voters, high $q\_{\mathrm{sup}}$ can lock in harm exported to unrepresented outsiders across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$). Preventing dead-hand immunity without inviting litigant compliance-DoS therefore requires **justiciable transboundary and representative *locus standi*** for affected non-voters and future cohorts, mandatory sunsets, and attested counter-inductive `T1Framing` reopening adjudicated by an uncaptured external forum under custodian- or state-posted escrow.

## 4. The form at both ends, and the case where it is applied correctly
