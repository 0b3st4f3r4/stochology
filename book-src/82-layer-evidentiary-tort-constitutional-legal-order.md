## 1. Two thresholds, three standards, and the class that does not aggregate

Imagine a packed rodeo stadium where fewer than half the spectators bought tickets at the gate. Pick anyone at random and odds say they entered unpaid, yet no civil judge will order a ticket refund from gate math alone without individualized proof. Civil proof, tort liability, and constitutional rules all wrestle with that divide between group averages, tail risks, and individual accountability.

### Guard T1 (Framing): Requiring individualized case weight before thresholds trigger liability

In civil adjudication, `T1Framing` ($T\_1$) protects individual parties from being found liable on bare group averages when case-specific evidential weight is absent.

Two decision rules govern judging before outcomes settle ([Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md)) across the three mediation planes ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md); [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)). Cost–loss sets $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = C/(\eta L)$ ($C, L > 0$, $\eta \in (0, 1]$), whereas expected regret sets $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = C\_{\mathrm{FP}}/(C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}})$ ($C\_{\mathrm{FP}}, L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$). Legal proof follows the second rule. Preponderance demands $p > 1/2$ at parity $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ (so an exact tie $p = 1/2$ fails the persuasion burden). Clear and convincing proof sets $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = k L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$ ($1 < k < 10$, $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = k/(k + 1)$), while proof beyond reasonable doubt uses Blackstone's ten-to-one ratio ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 10/11$; Blackstone 1765–1769).

Yet clearing $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$ on naked class prevalence still fails. In L. Jonathan Cohen's Gatecrasher Paradox (Cohen 1977), $1{,}000$ spectators fill a rodeo where $499$ tickets sold, meaning $501$ entered unpaid ($p = 0.501 > 1/2$), yet courts refuse recovery against any picked attendee. Keynes (1921) separated probability from evidential weight qualitatively as knowledge against ignorance. Stochology indexes case-specific weight quantitatively via $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}}) = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} / (n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} + n\_0)$ ($n\_0 > 0$; [Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md)): bare base rates leave $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}} = 0$ and $V = 0$.

Group liability also runs into Chouldechova's (2017) disparity bound across unequal base rates ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Layer 31](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md)) and breaks in three mathematical ways. First, under fixed gatecrasher totals ($\sum\_i G\_i = 501$), individual indicators correlate negatively ($\mathrm{Cov}(G\_i, G\_j) < 0$), so applying the cutoff per person condemns all $1{,}000$ spectators. Second, across multiple legal elements, product rule $P(E\_1 \cap E\_2) = P(E\_1)P(E\_2)$ breaks under correlation. Third, group averages ([Layer 14](64-layer-taxonomy-systematics-ontology-order.md)) never replace proof of individual causation.

## 2. Tort liability and the two circuits

### Guard T4 (Formalization): Pricing convex harm and solvency caps instead of mean damage

In tort law, `T4Formalization` ($T\_4$) protects potential victims when mean-value negligence rules and thin equity caps hide catastrophic tail harm.

Learned Hand's negligence formula in *United States v. Carroll Towing Co.* (Hand 1947) states $B < PL$—the cost–loss rule at $\eta = 1$, $C = B$, comparing care burden $B > 0$ against expected harm $PL$ ($P \in [0, 1]$, $L > 0$). Negligence regulates how much care an actor takes, yet once due care is met it leaves total activity volume unpriced; strict liability prices both residual risk and activity scale.

Hand's inequality breaks in two directions under real losses. First, evaluating harm at average hazard $\phi(\mathbb{E}[X])$ rather than expected harm $\mathbb{E}[\phi(X)]$ for convex loss $\phi$ ($\mathbb{E}[|X|], \mathbb{E}[|\phi(X)|] < \infty$) misses Jensen's gap $\Delta\_J = \mathbb{E}[\phi(X)] - \phi(\mathbb{E}[X]) \ge 0$ and heavy tails ([Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md); [Layer 05](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md)). Second, capping collectible damages at firm equity $W\_{\mathrm{eq}} < L$ leaves recoverable loss concave ($\min(W\_{\mathrm{eq}}, L) = W\_{\mathrm{eq}}$), cutting the firm's private threshold to $B < PW\_{\mathrm{eq}}$. Symmetrically, limited-liability equity holds a convex payoff $\max(A - X, 0)$ in assets $A$ against loss $X$, encouraging risky asset substitution toward higher variance (Jensen & Meckling 1976). Under ex-ante effort, Innes (1990) likewise identifies the discontinuous live-or-die contract ([Layer 30](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md)).

The institutional remedy splits into two distinct circuits matching Sosa's (2015) *télos*–*skopós* ledger ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md); [Layer 01](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)). On the compensation circuit (strict liability), injured third parties recover immediately from an ex-ante capitalized reserve even when an operator's act was careful and unlucky. On the accountability circuit, punitive sanctions, bonus clawbacks, and license revocations target reckless or inapt conduct—even when lucky—preventing firms from scapegoating frontline operators for under-capitalized corporate buffers.

## 3. Constitution and statute as circuit breakers

### Guard T7 (Liability): Locking pre-commitments, witness shields, and supermajority vetoes

In constitutional governance, `T7Liability` ($T\_7$) protects citizens and whistleblowers from retroactive decrees, silenced warnings, and concentrated majorities exporting harm onto outsiders.

A constitution acts as Elster's (1979) pre-commitment device. Bans on *ex post facto* laws and bills of attainder enforce syntactic sealing and validity windows prior to outcomes ([Layer 06](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md)), blocking retroactive rule rewrites, while bicameral and judicial vetoes add conjunctive anti-rigging gates. Whistleblower shields and unsafe-work refusal set status discounting at zero ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) along an uncompromised algedonic channel ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)). To stop alert floods (`Compliance DoS`) from saturating review ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ pairs with corroborated sequential likelihood-ratio triage and non-self-extending sunsets inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Layer 33](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md)). Automated-decision laws work identically, binding declared envelopes and reversible human review inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ under Hand's error-cost margin.

Limits on constitutional rights invoke structured proportionality: any measure restricting right $P\_i$ to advance competing principle $P\_j$ must be legitimate, suitable, necessary, and proportionate *stricto sensu*. Alexy (2002) formalizes that balance through weight formula $W\_{i,j}$, where $I\_i, W\_i, R\_i$ and $I\_j, W\_j, R\_j$ measure interference or satisfaction importance, abstract weight, and empirical premise reliability:

$$W\_{i,j} = \frac{I\_i \cdot W\_i \cdot R\_i}{I\_j \cdot W\_j \cdot R\_j}.$$

Read aloud, $W\_{i,j}$ compares the interference–weight–reliability product for $P\_i$ against $P\_j$, favoring $P\_i$ above $1$, $P\_j$ below $1$, and deadlocking at $1$.

On Alexy's geometric triadic scale ($\lbrace 2^0, 2^1, 2^2 \rbrace$ for $I, W$; $\lbrace 2^0, 2^{-1}, 2^{-2} \rbrace$ for $R$), every factor stays positive ($I\_i, W\_i, R\_i, I\_j, W\_j, R\_j > 0$). That positivity preserves reciprocity $W\_{j,i} = 1/W\_{i,j}$ and penalizes weak factual premises via lower $R\_j$. Whereas [Layer 06](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md) audits syntactic gates, scale invariance sits in [Layer 07](57-layer-probability-credence-measurement-order.md). Under Stevens's (1946) ordinal limits, multiplying purely ordinal grades flips verdicts under monotone relabeling unless an explicit ratio chart holds. Crucially, $W\_{i,j}$ is **compensatory** because high $I\_j W\_j$ offsets weak $R\_j$. Balancing therefore satisfies non-compensatory triad rules only after external epistemic ($R\_j \ge R\_{\min} > 0$) and vital floors hold.

Constitutional entrenchment extends that gate across time. Assume single-peaked preferences in one dimension ($d = 1$) with continuous strictly increasing ideal-point distribution $F$, and suppose defeating status quo $x$ requires a supermajority exceeding fraction $q \in (1/2, 1)$. Then $x$ resists both rightward coalitions ($1 - F(x) \le q$) and leftward coalitions ($F(x) \le q$) across the closed core between quantiles $F^{-1}(1 - q)$ and $F^{-1}(q)$:

$$F^{-1}(1 - q) \le x \le F^{-1}(q).$$

Read aloud, any status quo $x$ inside $[F^{-1}(1 - q), F^{-1}(q)]$ resists both leftward and rightward supermajority coalitions of size $q$.

Strict coalition resistance ($1 - F(x) < q$ and $F(x) < q$) holds across the open interior $(F^{-1}(1 - q), F^{-1}(q))$. As $q \to (1/2)^+$, the core narrows to the median $\lbrace F^{-1}(1/2) \rbrace$; raising $q$ buys deliberate inertia, cutting external harm on outvoted minorities while raising decision costs (Buchanan & Tullock 1962). Yet that stable core needs one dimension: in $d \ge 2$ dimensions, moderate supermajorities leave an empty core, requiring $q > d/(d + 1)$ by Helly's theorem to prevent cycles, while legislative *logrolling* still builds minimal winning coalitions. Worse, because $F$ counts only enfranchised voters, high $q$ can lock in harm exported to unrepresented outsiders across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$), requiring attested counter-inductive `T1Framing` reopening and sunsets to prevent dead-hand immunity.

## 4. The form at both ends, and the case where it is applied correctly

At the upper boundary, $C \ge \eta L$ pushes $C/(\eta L) \ge 1$, clamping $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = 1$: precaution looks cost-dominated inside that ledger, whereas incommensurable harms lie outside cost–loss pricing ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)). Inside $\eta L > C > 0$ with $L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$, both rules match uniquely at $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = \frac{C}{\eta L - C}L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$. For fixed $n\_0 > 0$, case weight $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{case}})$ climbs steeply on initial case-specific evidence before leveling off near one.

*Grimshaw v. Ford Motor Co.* stress-tests Hand's loss input. Dowie (1977) documents a 1972 federal agency calculation in *Fatalities Associated with Crash-Induced Fuel Leakage and Fires*. That table priced life at $\unicode{x24}200{,}725$—rounded to $\unicode{x24}200{,}000$ alongside $\unicode{x24}67{,}000$ per serious burn injury—against an $\unicode{x24}11$-per-car modification estimated to avert $180$ burn deaths and $180$ burn injuries annually (Dowie 1977). Because $B > PL$ under that table, Hand's test appeared to clear inaction.

At trial, although the internal "Grush-Saunby Report" was excluded, other cost-benefit evidence entered and the jury awarded $\unicode{x24}125$ million in punitive damages, remitted to $\unicode{x24}3.5$ million and affirmed on appeal (California Court of Appeal 1981). What failed was not arithmetic, nor mere tragic hindsight (*anagnórisis*; [Layer 20](70-layer-acoustic-aesthetic-tragic-pedagogical-order.md)). Hand's rule is an **ex-ante** bound, yet pulling $L$ from a manufacturer's or regulator's unilateral price table systematically undervalues non-reciprocal tail risk shifted onto unconsenting bearers ([Layer 05](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md)). Substituting ex-post collectible payouts fails equally: statutory caps, equity limits ($W\_{\mathrm{eq}} < L$), and remittitur export uncompensated harm onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

## 5. The collapse, and where the thesis stops

As Stratum VII's `T7*` `(1, 1, 1)` keystone—one of two `(1, 1, 1)` keystones in Stratum VII alongside [Layer 35](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md) (`T6*`)—Layer 32 unites `T1Framing`, `T4Formalization`, and `T7Liability` ([Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). It inherits structural and vital deficits from [Layer 05](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md), [Layer 30](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md) (`(1, 0, 2)`), and [Layer 31](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md) (`(2, 0, 1)`), while inheriting `T2Severity`, `T3Archive`, `T5Temporal`, and `T6Performative` upstream ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md)). Each missing axis drives a distinct collapse:

- **Without $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ (*Blind Undersizing*):** Bare prevalence at $V = 0$ over biased archives underprices danger.
- **Without $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ (*Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin*):** Docket congestion ([Layer 02](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md)) misses physical collapse deadlines ([Layer 34](84-layer-infrastructure-nuclear-hydraulic-safety-order.md)).
- **Without $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ (*Cynical Capture and Pillaging*):** Limited-liability shells conceal off-ledger transfers ([Layer 29](79-layer-official-statistics-forensic-accounting-order.md)) while silenced witnesses ([Layer 24](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md)) shift losses onto the public ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)).

This collapse diagnosis fails if courts impose liability at $V = 0$ without aggregate over-recovery, maintain convex-tail precaution under $W\_{\mathrm{eq}} < L$ without capitalized reserves, or protect external reception perimeters using fixed one-dimensional supermajorities amidst multidimensional conflict.

The legal threshold model presupposes an exogenous loss ratio and breaks whenever one conflicted actor supplies both cost figures and evidence—a defendant's expert, captive regulator, or self-auditing insurer. That is **endogenous capture**, not *"one noisy source"*: Manski's (2003, 2026) identification bounds require an external corruption cap $\lambda \in [0, 1)$, widening to the vacuous interval $[0, 1]$ as $\lambda \to 1^-$. Conflicted artifacts therefore require exclusion or independent valuation.

When bounded intervals $p \in [p\_L, p\_U]$ and $p^\ast \in [p^\ast\_L, p^\ast\_U]$ stay disjoint ($p\_L > p^\ast\_U$ or $p\_U < p^\ast\_L$), the verdict stays invariant. When they overlap ($p\_L \le p^\ast\_U$, $p\_U \ge p^\ast\_L$), divisible reversible steps take expected-loss bounds $[L\_t, U\_t] := [p\_L \eta L,\mkern3mu p\_U \eta L]$. Those steps follow Regime II minimax regret $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}} = (U\_t - C)/(U\_t - L\_t)$ ($L\_t < C < U\_t$, $U\_t - L\_t > 0$). By contrast, irreversible stakes force Regime III refusal, exclusion, or escalation ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)).
