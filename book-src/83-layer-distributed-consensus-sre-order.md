## 1. What can be agreed before an action is taken

Three results bound a distributed system. Fischer, Lynch and Paterson (1985) show that no deterministic protocol in an asynchronous network with an unannounced failure guarantees safety and termination together, since a slow node is indistinguishable from a dead one. An unbounded consensus round therefore violates reflexive brake 3 ($\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$) while the plant crosses the ruin barrier. Actuation can require a conjunctive gate, and emergency braking must be asymmetric: any single validated sentinel trips the breaker inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$. Lamport's happens-before relation (Lamport 1978) gives causal order without a shared clock, which is the foundation of a bitemporal read: an audit reconstructs what a node knew at transaction time and never contaminates it with later messages. Merkle chaining of each step's control tape makes any alteration of a past premise invalidate every later seal.

## 2. Tail amplification, quorums and budgets in series

A request that fans out across $m$ nodes finishes when the slowest node returns, so with an independent tail spike of probability $\varepsilon$ per node the fanned-out request meets one with probability $1 - (1 - \varepsilon)^m$, which rises toward certainty as the fan-out grows (Dean & Barroso 2013). The fan-out is a tail amplifier: a rare event at one server becomes the common case of the request, and the amplification is exponential rather than proportional. Past high utilisation, Kingman's factor $\rho/(1-\rho)$ inflates the tail past client timeouts, clients retry, the arrival rate multiplies while efficiency falls, and the cluster locks into a metastable state that survives the return of normal traffic (Bronson et al. 2021).

Both forms are cheap to solve at their ends. Byzantine safety needs $2f + 1$ matching attestations out of $3f + 1$ replicas, so a single unit of tolerated fault costs three units of hardware, and the tolerated fraction tends to one third while the required reachability tends to two thirds — a ratio no engineering choice moves. The budget composes as a series: a target availability multiplied across services that each meet it falls geometrically, so a chain of many services delivers an availability far below the target every one of them promises, and the error budget in minutes is divided by the same factor. Availability does not compose. It is spent.

A measured arrival series makes the second form concrete. When the upper tail of the hourly count sits well above the mean, a server dimensioned to run at a chosen utilisation of the mean runs near saturation during that tail hour, and its queueing factor moves from the reciprocal of the free capacity to a much larger value, because the same utilisation target and the same service time produce that much more waiting. The design decision is therefore a decision about which quantile of the arrival process the utilisation target is stated against, and a target stated against the mean is a target stated against a quantity the busy hour never sees.

## 3. Circuit breakers, budgets, and what operations does with them

The three-state breaker is the triadic regime table in production. Closed is buffered action while $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$; open is the brake, failing fast to shed retry load so the downstream can drain inside $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$; half-open is the reversible fractional probe, admitting a bounded canary fraction to test whether the recovery basin has re-formed (Nygard 2007). An error budget turns the same idea into arithmetic: an availability objective reserves its complement as downtime, deployments freeze when the reserve is spent, and a blameless post-mortem evaluates the incident through a telic grade, so that an engineer who followed a sound protocol during an unprecedented surge is not made the scapegoat for structural under-provisioning (Allspaw 2012).

The same discipline governs deployed models. Drift is a two-tape phenomenon: data drift and concept drift need different instruments and have different remedies, and confusing them lets a monitor report health while the function decays. Fallback is the half-open breaker of the model layer, because a system that can only serve the champion model has an infinite recovery time and fails the survival inequality the moment the champion degrades. A model card is the command tape written down, and a model deployed without one is a decision with no attributable author.

On 1 August 2012 a deployment reached seven of eight servers at Knight Capital and the eighth kept running an obsolete code path that had been repurposed rather than removed, and the firm lost more than $\unicode{x24}460$ million within about forty-five minutes (SEC 2013). The form applied and its recommendation appeared to be followed, since a partial deployment looks like the half-open state. It is nonetheless wrong, because it conflates two fractions: a probe is a fraction of traffic running the whole artifact, and what the firm had was a fraction of the artifact receiving its share of traffic, which is a partition. No member of that fleet could answer the question the probe was meant to ask. The corrected rule is one line: the canary must be identical in build and isolated in the request path, and no member of the serving set may run a build no other member has been tested against. That exclusion is invisible to error-budget arithmetic, which was healthy throughout.

## 4. The collapse, and where the thesis stops

The collapses follow the signature, which carries no site on the epistemic axis. Without $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ the dashboard reports mean latency while the tail and the shared power and network dependencies across zones go unread: Blind Undersizing. Without $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ a configuration change is pushed globally at full scale with no fractional canary, and a retry storm locks the fleet into metastable ruin: Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin. Without $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ the frontline operator is prosecuted on the word of an uncalibrated software ledger — the pathology of the British Post Office *Horizon* scandal (Christie 2020) — Cynical Capture and Pillaging.

The thesis is that consensus makes actuation safe and asymmetric braking fast, and it assumes the brake is a decision some local agent may take. It fails when the halt path itself is routed through the consensus layer, because then the impossibility result migrates from liveness onto safety and the prescription cannot be implemented as written. Make the brake a one-way latch: any single validated sentinel trips it without a vote, the latch persists until an operator clears it, and only the release requires the quorum. What remains is a declared quantity, the number of independent latches and the drill record showing each one tripped in production under load.
