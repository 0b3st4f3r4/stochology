## 1. Persistence, freeboard, and two absorbing boundaries

The reserve environment this book reasons with has a hydraulic ancestor. For independent draws the rescaled range of cumulative storage deficits grows as $n^{0.5}$, and Hurst (1951) found that river discharges, rainfall and temperatures grow as $n^{H}$ with $H$ between $0.70$ and $0.80$. Persistence is therefore a temporal cluster design effect $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{temp}} \sim n^{2H-1}$, and a reservoir sized on independent variance empties during a multi-year drought.

A dam operator sits between two absorbing boundaries. Keeping storage low leaves agriculture and drinking water dry; filling the reservoir to maximise hydroelectric revenue spends the flood control freeboard, which is the empty headroom $1 - \rho\_t$, so the first extreme surge overtops the crest and erodes the wall. Freeboard is a ratio of exponents rather than a distance. At $H = 0.5$ the design effect is one at every horizon; above it the effect compounds with the horizon, so a century of record carries the information of far fewer independent years and the range grows as $n^{H}$ against $n^{0.5}$. The two statements are one statement, since $\sqrt{n^{2H-1}} = n^{H-1/2}$, and a reservoir sized on the independent-draws law is short by exactly that factor.

## 2. Defence in depth where the causes are shared

Probabilistic risk assessment multiplies redundant barriers: with $k$ independent layers failing at probability $p$ the combined figure is $p^k$. At Fukushima Daiichi a single tsunami inundated the diesel generators and switchboards that sat in the same low-lying basements, the intra-barrier correlation went to one, and $k$ nominal layers became an effective count near one.

Redundant barriers act in parallel rather than in series, and common-cause coupling imposes a hard ceiling on what adding layers can buy. With single-layer failure probability $p = 1 - r$, $k$ independent barriers fail together at probability $p^k$ for a system reliability $R\_{\mathrm{ind}}(k) = 1 - (1 - r)^k$ approaching certainty as $k$ grows; under a shared-cause failure fraction $\rho \in [0, 1]$, joint failure probability becomes $P\_{\mathrm{fail}}(k) = (1 - \rho)p^k + \rho p$, so parallel system reliability is $R\_{\mathrm{par}}(k) = 1 - (1 - \rho)(1 - r)^k - \rho(1 - r) \le 1 - \rho p$. Once $(1 - \rho)p^k \ll \rho p$, adding another nominal layer reduces $\partial P\_{\mathrm{fail}}/\partial k = (1 - \rho)p^k \ln p$ toward zero and leaves $R\_{\mathrm{par}}(k)$ pinned at $1 - \rho(1 - r)$, whereas components placed in series multiply reliabilities as $r^k$ and degrade monotonically with $k$ ($\partial(r^k)/\partial k = r^k \ln r < 0$). Defence in depth is an exponent only where the failure modes are independent, so the engineering question is not how many layers to stack but what the layers share; in the tail, the effective barrier count shrinks to $k/\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{tail}}$ and the rupture probability is governed by that deflated count.

## 3. Diagnosis, re-derivation, and three disasters read as one

Perrow (1984) named the two axes that make a plant unsteerable once a barrier fails, and both land on the same inequality. Interactive complexity inflates the time to diagnose the plant state and deploy a valid recovery plan; tight coupling compresses the time before core uncovery or runaway pressure crosses an irreversible barrier. The survivor condition $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}}$ then fails and the accident becomes normal, because the physical clock outruns the diagnostic clock. Leveson's STAMP/STPA audit (2011) replaces the myth of operator error with a control-loop check, and the three canonical cases read as one failure. At Three Mile Island the indicator wired the solenoid command rather than the valve stem while over a hundred alarms saturated the operator's channel capacity. At Bhopal refrigeration, scrubber and flare were shut off while operator warnings were discounted. At Chernobyl a test ran with emergency cooling disabled in a reactor whose void coefficient was positive. The recovery clock decomposes, and the middle term is the one institutions forget: $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}} = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{diagnosis}} + \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{re-derivation}} + \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{execution}}$. Diagnosis is instrumented and execution is rehearsed; re-derivation, rebuilding a valid model of the current plant, is usually neither. Apollo 13 is the reference case: fifteen simulators reconfigured in parallel from live telemetry to re-derive procedures for a vehicle outside its design envelope, and what made it possible was inventory, not courage — models that could be re-parameterised faster than the consumables ran out. Where the middle term is absent the inequality can be met only by widening the left side, which means freeboard and capital instead of procedure. Three consequences are testable before an accident. Every safety model must declare its envelope in telemetry units. The re-derivation path must be exercised outside the critical path. The authority to act on a re-derived procedure must be pre-delegated, because where every deviation needs an escalation the effective recovery time is the bureaucratic latency.

The contemporary form of that asset is the digital twin, a replica of a specific asset driven by its own telemetry. The tempting inference is that the unassimilated parts are also represented; they are not, they are the prior of the model at the resolution of its author. So the twin must publish which variables it assimilates and which it merely renders, in telemetry units, because that list is its domain of validity. It must also be re-derivable, since a replica that cannot be re-parameterised from the measured state of a damaged asset is a museum piece with a live dashboard. Surface interaction on the Moon is the boundary case, a domain where the predictive model is explicitly missing, and shipping the twin without that declaration converts an honest gap into a silent one.

## 4. The storm arithmetic, the collapse, and where the thesis stops

The measured spread of the hazard shows how far the design event sits from the centre of the record. Across a long storm archive the extreme wind is a multiple of the median and the largest event is a further multiple of the extreme, and for a surge load scaling with the square of the wind the credible extreme load exceeds the median by the square of that ratio, so freeboard sized against a typical storm is short by an order of magnitude against the credible one.

Brumadinho is the case where the form was followed and the recommendation was wrong for a reason the local triple cannot resolve on its own. On 25 January 2019 Dam I of the Córrego do Feijão mine failed and killed 270 people, and the expert panel attributed the failure to static liquefaction of the tailings rather than to overtopping or a foundation deficiency (Robertson et al. 2019). The structure had its stage monitored, its freeboard reported and its factor of safety recomputed on schedule, so both local formal and temporal margins (`T4Formalization` and `T5Temporal`) alongside engineering accountability (`T7Liability`) were active in the `(0, 2, 1)` triple, and the boundary crossed was a constitutive one outside that local triple: the material was a contractive saturated deposit whose strength falls when sheared, so the margin was a distance in state space rather than in metres. Worse, the checks meant to be independent were not. Limit-equilibrium stability, piezometric monitoring and design review shared one constitutive assumption, so three nominally distinct safeguards collapsed under $\rho \to 1$ to a single common-cause check rather than an independent barrier product $p^k$. Because `{T4Formalization, T5Temporal, T7Liability}` leaves the epistemic axis locally unguarded ($k\_1 = 0$), operating the order in isolation from its upstream epistemic ancestors ([Layer 04](54-layer-geophysical-chaos-data-assimilation-order.md) and [Layer 32](82-layer-evidentiary-tort-constitutional-legal-order.md)) leaves no local check against an un-identified constitutive regime or a common-cause correlation $\rho \to 1$.

The collapses follow the signature. Multiplying barrier probabilities without a common-cause design effect, or reading the command signal as the physical valve state, is epistemic axial exposure under isolated operation: Blind Undersizing. Running a dam without freeboard or a tightly coupled reactor where survival time is shorter than recovery time is the vital axis gone: Impotent Lucidity Before Ruin. Decommissioning scrubbers and refrigeration to cut cost while ignoring frontline warnings is the governance axis gone: Cynical Capture and Pillaging.

The thesis is that an institution buys survival by widening the recovery side, in steel, in procedure and in delegated authority, and it assumes the hazard has an onset that some loop can be inside. It is invalidated when stored energy can be released faster than any loop: a vapour-cloud detonation, a seismic rupture beneath a dam, an exothermic runaway. Then survival time has a physical floor below any achievable recovery time, the inequality is unsatisfiable in principle, and no margin on the recovery side repairs it. The procedure is to state that floor for each stored energy on the site in the plant's own units, compare it with the minimum credible diagnosis time, and where it is smaller declare the plant operationally unsteerable, install only passive devices whose action time is zero, and budget the residual risk as separation distance and exclusion.
