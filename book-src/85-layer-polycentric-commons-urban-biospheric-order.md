## 1. The 1992 cod collapse: three axes at once

For five centuries the northern cod off Newfoundland was one of the richest marine commons on Earth. In July 1992 the moratorium closed a fishery whose spawning biomass had fallen by more than $98$ percent since the early 1960s, taking the economy out from under hundreds of coastal communities (Hutchings & Myers 1994; Task Force on Incomes and Adjustment in the Atlantic Fishery 1993).

Quantitative science was present throughout. What failed was all three axes at once.

The epistemic axis failed through Meng's big-data paradox. Assessors relied on commercial trawler catch per unit effort, a vast stream of hauls taken from dense spawning aggregations that the trawlers targeted with sonar, so the selection correlation was strongly positive and the intra-haul clustering large. Commercial effort stayed high to the eve of commercial extinction.

The vital axis failed through fixed-quota management at maximum sustainable yield. Under logistic growth a quota below $H\_{\mathrm{MSY}} = rK/4$ admits a stable stock and an unstable threshold with a basin $D(H) = K\sqrt{1 - 4H/(rK)}$, and at the maximum the two equilibria merge at $K/2$, so the basin is exactly zero and the slightest overestimate pushes the stock into depensation.

The governance axis failed through testimonial discounting. Inshore fixed-gear fishers, whose traps sampled fixed locations without sonar hyperstability, reported failing catches of younger and smaller fish for years. Their local *mētis* was filed as anecdote, and residual model uncertainty became the alibi for delay.

## 2. Monoculture, semi-lattice, and Ostrom's principles

Scott (1998) traced the same pathology through high-modernist planning. Prussian fiscal foresters replaced mixed-age forest with rectilinear spruce to maximise one cadastral metric; the first rotation set a record by spending down soil fungi, predators and deadwood moisture, and the second produced forest death. Alexander (1965) and Jacobs (1961) give the topological reason a living city and a living ecosystem share. Both are semi-lattices of overlapping sets where a master plan is a tree, and stripping the cross-links raises nominal efficiency in calm weather while removing every alternative path when the primary node saturates.

Ostrom (1990) distilled eight principles from commons that survived centuries, and they map onto the full agent in the reserve environment. Clearly defined boundaries are the declared frame and population coverage; congruence with local conditions is the Canguilhem–Holling basin and the survival inequality; collective choice by those affected is an anti-premise-rigging openness with a conjunctive gate; monitoring by accountable observers is calibrated ex-ante verification with a bitemporal log; graduated sanctions are telic grading that separates honest bad luck from predatory buffer stripping; low-cost conflict resolution is deliberation held inside the survival clock; recognition of the right to organise is an algedonic channel with zero status-based testimonial discount; and nested enterprises are the homomorphism itself, where each higher scale inherits the non-compensable minimum of every layer beneath it:

$$M\_{\mathrm{cumulative}}^{(1..35)} = \min\_{1 \le \ell \le 35}\mkern5mu\min\mkern-3mu\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}^{(\ell)},\mkern3muS\_{\mathrm{vital}}^{(\ell)},\mkern3muS\_{\mathrm{gov}}^{(\ell)}\bigr).$$

Polycentric commons are not sovereign concessions granted from above by a transcendent authority but immanent organizational forms through which the constituent power of a multitude coordinates common preservation (Spinoza 1670). By uniting Ostrom's nested self-governance with non-compensatory triadic margins, the thirty-fifth order shows that collective survival requires neither market commodification nor centralized command (Kropotkin 1902), but horizontally federated commons anchored in physical basins and non-discounted frontline accountability. Rockström et al. (2009) fix the outer boundary of that architecture: the ceiling is a set of biophysical limits rather than a budget, so a commons that governs its own extraction while crossing one of them has governed the wrong variable.

Read as a condition rather than a recommendation, the mapping states what has to be true for the closure to hold. Each principle is a term in the payoff of the unit it binds, and the nested closure holds only where the rule at the higher scale cannot be revoked by the unit it binds: a principle the competing centre can repeal is a move in the same game, and whenever $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} > 1/T$ the replicator differential of [Evolution, Variation, and Selection](25-evolution-variation-selection.md) then selects the centre that repeals it. §4 names the other failure, a stock coupled across boundaries defeating congruence even where every centre keeps its own rules. The constitutional level is necessary and not sufficient, and the closure is a condition on the level of the rule as much as on its content.

## 3. Closing thirty-five layers

Across all thirty-five layers, from the phosphorylation cascade of a receptor and the Rayleigh wave of a Griffith crack through the philologist's stemma and the epigrapher's bracketed stone to the constitutional court, the distributed cluster and the planetary fishery, the law of aim is invariant: ignorance of the future wind excuses no ignorance of one's own lens, vessel or pact. Where the three safeguards hold together action in the dark is buffered and accountable, and where any one is traded away the weakest link names the ruin before it arrives.

The graph does not end in a further specialisation; it ends in liability. Because $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ exhausts every three-site combination of the seven guard sites, a thirty-sixth layer would break the bijection between the two graphs. The declared anchor of stratum `S7` is `T7Liability`, the one guard site whose transit is the integral three-plane closure `World <-> Archive <- Institution` rather than a single directed transit or reflexive lock. Every earlier order therefore terminates here: a claim about the world, a record in the archive and an institution that must answer for both are bound into one circuit, and the commons the final layer names is exactly the object no single plane can own alone. The Leontief floor, at that point, is an institutional fact and not an arithmetic one.

Read through the $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} > 1/T$ inequality of [Evolution, Variation, and Selection](25-evolution-variation-selection.md), the third collapse proposition is not a fourth failure mode but the same condition with its terms administered. Limited liability sets the divesting unit's tail cost to zero ($q = 0$, leaving $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} = c$ uncompensated), the agnotological alibi lengthens the effective interval $T$ between shocks, and status-based testimonial discounting removes the signal that would have corrected it ([The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). The governance axis is therefore not one reading among three—it does not enter the minimum as one reading among three, because it sets the terms on which the other two are read. The architectural variable is the level of the floor, not its wording.

## 4. The closure is a minimum, and the basin is a square root

The closure is a number. For axis scores whose minimum falls well below their arithmetic mean, the gap between the two measures how far the weakest axis sits below the level an aggregate would report. An institution that must publish a number publishes the smallest unhedged exposure measured in the units of its own ruin barrier, and labels it a threshold device rather than a score ([The Triad and the Minimum](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). The same shape holds one level up. With one order far below the rest and the other thirty-four near the ceiling, the minimum is that one order's margin while the mean is near the ceiling, and as sound orders are added to a commons with one broken centre the mean rises toward the ceiling while the minimum does not move. That widening gap is the formal reason a commons cannot compensate a failed centre with healthy ones. It is also why the polycentric claim does not apply to a hierarchy with a single governor, where the minimum collapses to that governor's margin.

The vital form is the width of the basin under a constant quota, $D(H) = K\sqrt{1 - 4H/(rK)}$, and both ends are exact: at no harvest the width is the whole carrying capacity, and at the maximum sustainable yield it is zero rather than small. Between the ends the approach is a square root, so the width falls steeply for the last increments of quota. Approaching the maximum removes most of what remained while the quota moves a fraction of the way, and $dD/dH$ diverges at the maximum, so the marginal price of quota there is infinite.

In 2011 the Chao Phraya basin flooded, and the World Bank's estimate of the damage was THB $1.425$ trillion, about $\$46.5$ billion (World Bank 2012). The setting was polycentric in Ostrom's sense and locally congruent in exactly the way the principles recommend: provinces, municipalities and industrial estates each holding and releasing water against their own gauges inside their own boundary. That arrangement produced the loss. A flood wave is a coupled stock, not a set of separable ones, so a centre that holds its own water behaves correctly and imposes the cost on its neighbour — the semi-lattice read as a hazard. The recommendation that would have been right violates congruence, since it needs a basin-level authority with unilateral control over the coupled storage while the flood is in progress, with compensation paid to the sub-basins flooded to protect the others.

## 5. Where the thesis stops

The thesis is that a commons governed by many centres has a margin equal to the minimum over those centres, and that graduated sanctions and local monitoring hold each one inside its basin. It assumes the resource regenerates on a timescale longer than the institutional cycle, so monitoring and sanction have time to act on a flow. It is invalidated when the stock is non-renewable or recruits on a decadal timescale — an aquifer, an ore body, a fish stock whose recruitment lag exceeds a minister's term — because then the minimum is a countdown rather than a margin, and monitoring appropriation is useless when the stock is the budget. The procedure is to convert rate rights into a cumulative extraction ceiling with tradable shares of a declared stock, audited against an independent reserve estimate, and to attach the sanction to the depletion trajectory rather than to the harvest level, so the institution answers for the integral it is actually spending.

Luhmann's systems theory would stop the argument one step earlier. On his account there is no society as a whole over which a minimum could be taken, only functionally differentiated systems that observe one another, and negation is an operation of the observer rather than a property of the object (Luhmann 1984). The reply is that the minimum is not asserted of a society; it is asserted of a circuit whose boundary can be drawn and whose three axes can be read off records. Where the boundary cannot be drawn, the invalidation condition above already says the thesis does not apply.
