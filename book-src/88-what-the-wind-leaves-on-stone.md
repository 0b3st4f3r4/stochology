## 6.3.1 Epistemic Fallacy, Silences, and Missing Survivors

Roy Bhaskar (1975) named the **epistemic fallacy**: mistaking what gets recorded for what actually exists. Michel-Rolph Trouillot (1995) localized that collapse in documentary power across four moments: source creation, archive assembly, narrative retrieval, and retrospective significance. Reading incident tables without modeling absent strata mistakes silence inside files for safety outside them.

Abraham Wald's (1943) survival inversion ([Chapter 06, *Who Is Missing From the Table*](06-who-is-missing-from-the-table.md), inferring hidden losses from survivors) shows how missing strata invert counts. Obermeyer et al.'s (2019) health-risk audit ([Chapter 33, *Measuring and Erring*](33-measuring-and-erring.md), where cost labels hid sicker patients) reveals the same danger in proxy target labels. Unmodeled selection, dependence, and label distortion trigger **Collapse Proposition 1 (*Blind Undersizing*)** ([Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), sizing buffers to false certainty): reserves tuned to point estimates fail on the first unrecorded excursion.

## 6.3.2 Meng's Identity, Additive Design Effects, and Keynesian Weight

Before `T3` passes counts onward to `T2` or `T4`, raw sample volume breaks along three coupled dimensions. Those dimensions are derived in [Chapter 34, *Sample, Population, Archive*](34-sample-population-archive.md) (sampling bias and effective counts) and [Chapter 45, *From World to Archive: Evidential Weight, Big Data Paradox, and the Uncounted*](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) (harmonic collapse and design effects).

**Selection correlation ($\rho\_{R,Y}$) and breakdown correlation ($\rho\_{R,Y}^\star$).** Meng's (2018) non-asymptotic identity decomposes finite-population error via the correlation $\rho\_{R,Y}$ between recording and outcome and the sampling fraction $f = n/N \in (0, 1)$. Unlike design-unbiased Horvitz–Thompson (1952) estimation under known inclusion probabilities, under the self-normalized **Hájek** ratio estimator unequal weights shrink that effective sampling fraction down to $f\_w = f/\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} \le f$ ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} \ge 1$), widening the scarcity multiplier and capping effective sample size near $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\bar{\rho})$ ($\bar{\rho} > 0$) regardless of nominal database size (Squire 1988; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1).

**Additive cluster and weight design effects.** Suppose intra-cluster correlation obeys $\rho\_I \in [0, 1]$ under the random-effects cluster model and unit weights are independent within clusters and uncorrelated with cluster membership, cluster size, and cluster effects in the large-cluster limit (Kish 1965; Moulton 1986). Under those conditions, **the exact composition is additive**: $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - 1 \ge 1$ ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md), effective sample size under clustering; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md), design-effect composition; permitting negative-$\rho\_I$ fixed-cluster models only inside the positive-semidefiniteness ceiling without gating $\ge 1$). That yields the variance-effective sample size $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = n / \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} > 0$ (whereas weights constant inside clusters yield a multiplicative upper bound).

**Keynesian evidential weight ($V$).** Guarded by **Reflexive Brake 1** ([Chapter 50, *Five Methodological Families*](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), preventing skeptical inflation from masquerading as caution), `T3` maps bias-adjusted $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$ against decision-calibrated $n\_0$ (Keynes 1921) onto $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})) \in [0, 1)$. That keeps random noise distinct from selection bias.

## 6.3.3 Unseen Populations, Bitemporal Vintages, and Judge-Panel Blindness

To bound **Not observed** eligible units ($\pi\_i > 0$), `T3` imports $t$-list `Chao2` ($\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}$) and Lincoln–Petersen floors ([Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md), capture–recapture estimators; [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md), bounding uncounted survivors), drawing on Chao (1984, 1987, 1989), Chao and Colwell (2017), Lum, Price, and Banks (2013), and Bareinboim and Pearl (2016). Crucially, `T3` separates those eligible omissions from **Not askable** strata ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$) barred from every list, which displace uncounted harm onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)).

Following Snodgrass (2000), Croushore and Stark (2001), and Aruoba (2008), `T3` enforces **bitemporal vintage separation** ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}} \le t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$): urgent decisions lock to the worst admissible prior sealed vintage ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}} \le t\_{\mathrm{cut}}$ at decision cutoff time $t\_{\mathrm{cut}}$, distinct from quantile level $\tau\_q$), while full reconciliation waits asynchronously outside live operational queues under bounded deliberate review ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) §4.5, event versus availability time; [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), protecting urgent clocks; [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), keeping live queues near 10 bits/s, Zheng & Meister 2025). Shifted bounds threatening survival escalate to **Regime III** rather than acting on stale numbers.

That archive gate closes the loop with [Chapter 86](86-posture-before-bowstring-draws.md) (§6.1.3, why panel averaging preserves door bias $b\_{\mathrm{door}}$). Kleinberg et al. ## 6.3.4 Sharp MNAR Quantile and $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ Bounds

Mean design-effect corrections cannot protect tail promises ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md), why effective sample size fails upper quantiles). Guarding against the worst 10 percent of storms ($p = 0.90$) when 20 percent go unrecorded ($1 - f = 0.20$) allows every disaster to hide in the unseen slice, pinning the worst-case tail bound at ceiling $y\_{\max}$ regardless of sample count.

Four recent results chart this non-random missingness (MNAR) frontier. Molinari (2020) characterizes sharp identified sets via random-closed-set capacities, and Fan and Park (2010) build uniform confidence sets for interval-identified targets including Fréchet–Hoeffding quantile bounds. Dorn and Guo (2023) prove under odds-ratio sensitivity $\Lambda \ge 1$ that quantile levels $\tau\_q = \Lambda/(\Lambda + 1)$ collapse infinitely many propensity constraints. Khan, Komarova, and Nekipelov (2026) introduce Random Set Quantile extraction ($\tau\_q \in (1/2, 1)$) under discrete covariates.

To derive exact distribution-free bounds, work under the **loss convention** where positive $Y$ means harm ([Chapter 13](13-integrals-and-accumulation.md) §4, tail risk integrals) on **finite bounded support** $Y \in [y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ ($-\infty < y\_{\min} \le y\_{\max} < \infty$). Let $f = \mathbb{P}(R = 1) \in (0, 1]$ be the observed fraction and $F\_Y$ the full law of $Y$. Let $F\_{\mathrm{obs}}(y) = \mathbb{P}(Y \le y \mid R = 1)$ have left-continuous inverse $F\_{\mathrm{obs}}^{-1}(v)$, and let $F\_{\mathrm{mis}}$ be the missing-stratum law on $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$. Inverting the Manski (1990; 2003; 2026) envelope at level $p \in (0, 1)$ fixes the sharp **MNAR quantile bounds** $q\_L(p)$ and $q\_U(p)$ ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md), worst-case missing mass placement): $q\_L(p) = y\_{\min}$ for $p \le 1 - f$ and $q\_L(p) = F\_{\mathrm{obs}}^{-1}\bigl(\frac{p - (1 - f)}{f}\bigr)$ for $p > 1 - f$, while $q\_U(p) = F\_{\mathrm{obs}}^{-1}(p/f)$ for $p \le f$ and $q\_U(p) = y\_{\max}$ for $p > f$.

Now pass from quantiles $\mathrm{VaR}\_u(Y) := \inf\lbrace y \in \mathbb{R} : F\_Y(y) \ge u\rbrace$ ($u \in (0, 1)$) to coherent Expected Shortfall $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p(Y) = \frac{1}{1 - p}\int\_p^1 \mathrm{VaR}\_u(Y)\mkern3mu du$ ($p \in (0, 1)$; Artzner et al. 1999; Acerbi & Tasche 2002; [Chapter 13](13-integrals-and-accumulation.md) §4, tail-average risk; [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md), coherent tail measures). Finite support guarantees $\mathbb{E}[|Y|] < \infty$, keeping Acerbi and Tasche's (2002) integral coherent even at discrete boundary atoms. Placing extremal missing point masses $F\_{\mathrm{mis}} = \delta\_{y\_{\min}}$ and $\delta\_{y\_{\max}}$ attains $q\_L(u) \le \mathrm{VaR}\_u(Y) \le q\_U(u)$ **simultaneously for every** $u \in (0, 1)$. That yields **sharp MNAR $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ bounds** $[\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, L},\mkern5mu \mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U}]$, recovering Manski's mean bounds as $p \to 0^+$:

$$\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, L} = \frac{1}{1 - p}\int\_p^1 q\_L(u)\mkern3mu du, \qquad \mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U} = \frac{1}{1 - p}\int\_p^1 q\_U(u)\mkern3mu du.$$

In plain words, the sharpest lower and upper Expected Shortfall bounds simply average the worst-case missing-data quantile curves above level $p$.

This proves a **Tail Saturation Theorem**. Whenever the missing share exceeds the tail share ($1 - f > 1 - p$, meaning $p > f$), every $u \in [p, 1]$ satisfies $u > f$. That locks **both** $q\_U(p) = y\_{\max}$ and $\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U} = y\_{\max}$ regardless of sample size $n \to \infty$ ($\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U} = y\_{\max}$ already saturates at $p = f$, since $\lbrace f \rbrace$ has measure zero). Under unbounded upper support ($y\_{\max} = +\infty$) with $f < 1$, $q\_U(u) = +\infty$ on $(f, 1]$ forces $\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U} = +\infty$ for **every** $p \in (0, 1)$. Unbounded tails then pass directly to Filter II ([Chapter 13](13-integrals-and-accumulation.md) §5, unbounded tail integrals; [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md), heavy-tail regimes).

Check this concretely on [Chapter 87](87-aiming-against-counterfactual-winds.md)'s $[0, 100]$ example (§6.2.4, where $\mathrm{DRO}$ failed under missing data), with $Y \mid (R = 1) \sim \mathrm{Uniform}(0, 20)$ ($F\_{\mathrm{obs}}^{-1}(v) = 20v$) and observed fraction $f = 0.80$. At the median $p = 0.50 \le f$ ($p > 1 - f = 0.20$), we obtain $q\_L(0.50) = 7.5$, $q\_U(0.50) = 12.5$, and:

$$\mathrm{CVaR}\_{0.50, L} = 2\int\_{0.50}^{1} 20\frac{u - 0.20}{0.80}\mkern3mu du = 13.75, \qquad \mathrm{CVaR}\_{0.50, U} = 2\\!\left(\int\_{0.50}^{0.80} 20\frac{u}{0.80}\mkern3mu du + \int\_{0.80}^{1} 100\mkern3mu du\right) = 49.75.$$

At $p = 0.90 > f = 0.80$, however, the unobserved mass $0.20$ exceeds the upper tail probability $0.10$. The lower bounds remain $q\_L(0.90) = 17.5$ and $\mathrm{CVaR}\_{0.90, L} = 18.75$, yet **both upper bounds hit the ceiling**: $q\_U(0.90) = \mathrm{CVaR}\_{0.90, U} = 100$. In plain words, whereas empirical $\mathrm{DRO}$ missed the true mean $28$ completely, MNAR $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ proves before impact that once $1 - f > 1 - p$, archives hold zero upper-tail power below $y\_{\max}$.

## 6.3.5 Laminar Boundary of `T3`

Exact point recovery at `T3` holds only for strictly descriptive summaries of exhaustively enumerated closed frames ($f = 1$, uniform weights, zero unrecorded revisions or label distortion). Even $f = 1$ with uniform weights leaves $\rho\_{R,Y}$ undefined (whereas non-uniform weights keep Meng's weighted bias $\rho\_{w,Y}\mkern2mu\mathrm{CV}(w)\mkern2mu\sigma\_Y$, [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)) without curing label substitution (Obermeyer et al. 2019) or superpopulation transport. Statutory rules declaring living strata *Not askable* also clash with vital normativity (Canguilhem 1943/1966) and displace uncounted harm onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), tracking externalized damage ex post).

Elsewhere, `T3` exports $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(|\rho\_{R,Y}|)$, Keynesian weight $V$ under Brake 1, *Not observed* floor $\hat{n}\_{00}$ separated from *Not askable* strata, worst sealed vintage ($t\_{\mathrm{disp}} \le t\_{\mathrm{cut}}$), and sharp $[q\_L(p), q\_U(p)]$ and $[\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, L}, \mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U}]$. Yet this laminar framing assumes reconciliation ends offline. Under concurrent seven-site collision ([Chapter 95, *Collision at Ten Bits per Second*](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), where live attention bottlenecks), unpruned `T3` audits trigger `Compliance DoS`, just as scalarizing valid intervals exposes the vital margin at `T4`.
