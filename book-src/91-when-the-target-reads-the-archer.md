## 1. From Passive Camera to Reactive Target

Classical forecasting treats a ledger as a static photograph. Extending Karl Marx's (1867) analysis of commodity fetishism, Georg Lukács (1923) diagnosed **reification** (*Verdinglichung*) when human-made institutional scores confront people as alien laws of nature ([Chapter 07](07-when-the-measure-becomes-the-goal.md), when measures turn into targets; [Appendix E](appendix-e-lexicon.md), glossary of classical and technical terms). Jean-Paul Sartre (1960) similarly named the *practico-inert*: past choices hardened into institutions that now box in their makers. In the *Theses on Feuerbach*, Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels (1845) observed that truth is proved in living practice rather than detached contemplation (Thesis II), and that the educator must himself be educated (Thesis III).

Historical epistemology explains how those alien scoreboards acquire real social force. Alfred Sohn-Rethel (1978) showed that commodity exchange generates **real abstraction** (*Realabstraktion*) inside social practice before pure concepts codify it, converging with Jean-Pierre Vernant's (1982) account of public law in the Greek *pólis* and Richard Seaford's (2004) linkage of coinage to impersonal metaphysical substance, while Erwin Panofsky (1951) traced how institutionalized mental habits reshape physical architecture itself. Under Goodhart's law, an operational metric detaches as just such an autonomous formal sphere that materially reorganizes workflows while masking harm displaced across external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

Across statistics, econometrics, and sociology, reactive worlds break four naive assumptions.

**Policy breakdown and looping.** Goodhart (1975, 1984), Lucas (1976), Campbell (1979), Strathern (1997), and Hacking (1995, 2002), alongside Espeland and Sauder (2007), trace how high-stakes targets corrupt indicators. High-stakes scores reshape people around the metric itself ([Chapter 40, *Institution, Incentive, Metric*](40-institution-incentive-metric.md), how incentives distort proxies; [Chapter 48, *Reactive Worlds: Causal Invariance, Performativity, and Goodhart's Law*](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md), performativity and Goodhart channels).

**Performativity versus vital normativity.** Donald MacKenzie (2006) contrasted the passive **camera** baseline ($\varepsilon = 0$) with *generic*, *effective*, *Barnesian*, and *counterperformative* models that alter the markets they describe. By contrast, Georges Canguilhem's (1943/1966) **vital normativity** distinguishes living norm-creation under *infidélité du milieu* from strategic evasion.

**Strategic gaming and social burden.** Building on Manheim and Garrabrant's (2018) four Goodhart channels and Hardt et al.'s (2016) strategic classification, Kleinberg and Raghavan (2020) separate genuine effort from proxy gaming. In Nairobi ($N = 1{,}557$), Björkegren, Blumenstock, and Knight (2020) found that disclosing standard scoring rules cut predictive accuracy by 23 percent on average, pushing naive LASSO pooled RMSE from $\unicode{x24}4.00$ to $\unicode{x24}4.93$, while strategy-robust estimation outperformed standard estimators under transparency by 13 percent on average ($\unicode{x24}4.31$ pooled RMSE). Yet Milli et al. (2019) prove that strategic classification still shifts social burden $B^+(f)$ disproportionately onto disadvantaged groups.

**Pre-deployment language manipulation.** Adversarial performativity also rewrites public framing beforehand. Across 1,448 ExxonMobil and Mobil advertisements (1924–2013) and internal records, Geoffrey Supran and Naomi Oreskes (2020) found that among post-2004 "Acknowledge" advertorials (93%, or 14/15, implicit), 87% (13/15) pivoted to "risk(s)" counseling delay. Across five sectors, Rebecca F. Goldberg and Laura N. Vandenberg (2021) coded 28 doubt tactics, including abusing data access (#27) and invoking overregulation (#10).

## 2. Ex-Ante Gate 1: CORP-PAV, Causal Invariance, and Discrepancy

Offline Gate 1 ($g\_{\text{ex-ante}, t}$) screens out uncalibrated or spurious rules through three filters derived in Parts III–IV ([Chapter 37, *Model, Fit, and Error*](37-model-fit-and-error.md), out-of-sample calibration and variance; [Chapter 39, *Cause, Intervention, Counterfactual*](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md), invariant causes versus correlations; [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md), Gate 1 causal and calibration screens; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4, ordinal normalization).

First, building on Dimitriadis, Gneiting, and Jordan's (2021) exact Brier Score decomposition $\mathrm{BS}(p) = \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} + \mathrm{UNC}\_0$ from tie-pooled `CORP-PAV`, Gate 1 computes contiguous chronological or cluster-block debiased calibration $\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}^{\mathrm{debiased}} = \max(0, \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \hat{b}\_{\mathrm{PAV}}^{\mathrm{CV}})$ as an empirical finite-sample optimism adjustment rather than an exact identity, paired with cross-validated optimism-adjusted discriminative resolution $\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}^{\mathrm{debiased}}$ against external non-performative `T3` benchmarks rather than Barnesian proxy labels. Under **Reflexive Brake 1** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), blocking flat or overfitted rules), Gate 1 rejects certification whenever out-of-fold loss fails climatology ($\mathrm{BS}\_{\mathrm{CV}}(p) \ge \mathrm{UNC}\_0$) or $\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}^{\mathrm{debiased}} \le 0$.

Second, Gate 1 separates true causal invariance from passive feature importance (`SHAP`, Lundberg & Lee 2017; `LIME`, Ribeiro, Singh, & Guestrin 2016; Pearl 2009) via **Invariant Causal Prediction (ICP)** (Peters, Bühlmann, & Meinshausen 2016). ICP requires two core preconditions: no hidden confounding between candidate predictors and $Y$, and no environment intervening directly on $Y$, meaning performative shifts $\mathcal{D}(\theta)$ altering $Y$'s conditional distribution across rules falsify ICP invariance directly ($\mathcal{A} = \emptyset$, distinct from underpowered non-rejection $\emptyset \in \mathcal{A}$ collapsing $\hat{S}^\ast = \emptyset$). Both falsified ($\mathcal{A} = \emptyset$) and empty ($\hat{S}^\ast = \emptyset$) outputs route to **Regime II** or **Regime III** ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), cautious probing or refusal under the triad minimum).

Third, Kennedy and O'Hagan's (2001) discrepancy Gaussian process widens posterior uncertainty toward the prior ceiling $\sqrt{s^2 + \sigma\_{\mathrm{noise}}^2}$ under **covariate extrapolation** (anchored by pre-registered block validation and geometric distance caps), while failing alone under in-support conditional shifts $P\_\theta(Y \mid X = x)$ where $|x - x\_{\mathrm{train}}| \lesssim \ell$.

## 3. Performative Shift, Non-Contraction, and Offline DRO Failure

Deployed rules alter populations via a decision-dependent distribution $\mathcal{D}(\theta)$ (Perdomo et al. 2020), decoupling training accuracy from deployment risk ([Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md); [Chapter 77](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md)). Pair linear Gaussian response $Y \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta) = \mathcal{N}(\mu\_0 + \varepsilon\theta, \sigma^2)$ ($\sigma > 0$, sensitivity $\varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$) with regularized quadratic loss $\ell(y;\theta) = \frac{1}{2}(y - \theta)^2 + \frac{\lambda}{2}\theta^2$ (externally pre-registered $\lambda > 0$, strong convexity $\gamma = 1 + \lambda$).

Under the $\ell\_1$-product metric $|\theta - \theta'| + |y - y'|$, joint gradient smoothness $\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}} = \max(1 + \lambda, 1) = 1 + \lambda$ enforces Perdomo et al.'s (2020) sufficient threshold $|\varepsilon| < \gamma/\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}} = 1$, whereas isolating cross-sensitivity $L\_y = |\partial\_y\partial\_\theta \ell| = 1$ yields exact contraction $\kappa\_{\mathrm{RRM}} = |\varepsilon|L\_y/\gamma = |\varepsilon|/(1 + \lambda)$. Whenever $|\varepsilon| < 1 + \lambda$ ($1 + \lambda - \varepsilon \ne 0$), repeated risk minimization converges to **performative stability** $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \mu\_0/(1 + \lambda - \varepsilon)$, differing from **Stackelberg optimality** $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}} = (1 - \varepsilon)\mu\_0 / ((1 - \varepsilon)^2 + \lambda)$, which still presumes unavailable local knowledge (Hayek 1945; Scott 1998) while externalizing harm.

### Theorem 1 (Offline DRO Ambiguity Failure Under Performative Shift $\mathcal{D}(\theta)$)

Distributionally robust optimization ($\mathrm{DRO}$) draws an uncertainty ball around training data $\hat{P}\_n$ or baseline $\mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)$ and optimizes against its worst member. Theorem 1 proves that this offline fence bursts once a new rule $\theta \ne \theta\_0$ pushes the population outside discrete support or the fixed ball radius.

Draw $n$ i.i.d. baseline samples $Y\_i \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0) = \mathcal{N}(\mu\_0 + \varepsilon\theta\_0, \sigma^2)$ ($\sigma > 0$) to form discrete empirical law $\hat{P}\_n = \frac{1}{n}\sum\_{i=1}^n \delta\_{Y\_i}$, evaluating a $1$-Lipschitz or bounded loss inside a Wasserstein-1 ball $\lbrace Q : W\_1(Q, \hat{P}\_n) \le \varepsilon\_n \rbrace$ (radius $\varepsilon\_n > 0$ distinct from sensitivity $\varepsilon$; unbounded quadratic loss $\ell(y;\theta)$ on $\mathbb{R}$ explodes to $+\infty$ inside any positive $W\_1$ ball and requires $W\_2$ or bounded support, while the closed forms above govern the quadratic model) or $\phi$-divergence balls $\lbrace Q : D\_\phi(Q \parallel \hat{P}\_n) \le \rho\_{\mathrm{div}} \rbrace$ and $\lbrace Q : D\_\phi(Q \parallel \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)) \le \rho\_{\mathrm{div}} \rbrace$ ($\rho\_{\mathrm{div}} \ge 0$, distinct from queue utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}}$ and cognitive load $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}$; Mohajerin Esfahani & Kuhn 2018; Duchi & Namkoong 2021; Rahimian & Mehrotra 2019). Under homoscedastic normal location shift:

$$W\_1\bigl(\mathcal{D}(\theta), \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)\bigr) = |\varepsilon(\theta - \theta\_0)|.$$

By the triangle inequality, $W\_1(\mathcal{D}(\theta), \hat{P}\_n) \ge |\varepsilon(\theta - \theta\_0)| - W\_1(\hat{P}\_n, \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0))$. As $n \to \infty$ ($W\_1(\hat{P}\_n, \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)) \to 0$, $\varepsilon\_n \to 0$), any shift $\varepsilon(\theta - \theta\_0) \ne 0$ escapes the Wasserstein ball around $\hat{P}\_n$, and escapes the ball around $\mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)$ whenever $|\varepsilon(\theta - \theta\_0)| > \varepsilon\_n$. Around **discrete** $\hat{P}\_n$, absolute continuity $Q \ll \hat{P}\_n$ under superlinear divergence growth $\lim\_{t \to \infty}\phi(t)/t = +\infty$ (or bounded divergence radius $\rho\_{\mathrm{div}} < 1$ under Total Variation) excludes continuous $\mathcal{D}(\theta)$ on $\mathbb{R}$ altogether (Mohajerin Esfahani & Kuhn 2018), just as bounded-support shifts break $Q \ll \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)$ ([Chapter 87](87-aiming-against-counterfactual-winds.md)).

Around $\mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)$, $\mathcal{D}(\theta) \ll \mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)$ holds yet exits once $D\_\phi(\mathcal{D}(\theta)\parallel\mathcal{D}(\theta\_0)) > \rho\_{\mathrm{div}}$ (for $\mathrm{KL}$, whenever $\frac{\varepsilon^2(\theta - \theta\_0)^2}{2\sigma^2} > \rho\_{\mathrm{div}}$). Strictly **ex post**, **Reflexive Brake 2** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.2) audits regressive hoarding via externally pre-anchored covariant ceiling $R^\ast = \max\bigl(R\_{\mathrm{floor}},\mkern3mu R\_{\mathrm{ref}}(\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}/c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} - 1)\bigr)$ (pre-anchored physical scale $R\_{\mathrm{ref}} > 0$, survival-and-impairment floor $R\_{\mathrm{floor}} \ge 0$, weights $\kappa\_{\mathrm{prec}}, c\_{\mathrm{urgent}} > 0$; $R^\ast$ distinct from Ethical Refusals $R\_1, R\_3$), never acting as an ex-ante rationing cap on live reserves and remaining subordinate to $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

In short, offline $\mathrm{DRO}$ fences yesterday's world, so any rule moving people past that fence escapes the safety guarantee.

### Proposition 2 (Non-Contractive Oscillation and Divergence)

Now ask what happens if an organization notices its model drifting under human reaction and simply retrains faster and faster to catch up. Proposition 2 proves that whenever public reaction sensitivity $|\varepsilon|$ meets or exceeds the rule's smoothing floor $1 + \lambda$, repeated retraining never settles down. Iterates march off linearly, flip in a two-step seesaw, or blow up, so faster retraining accelerates collapse.

For $|\varepsilon| \ge 1 + \lambda$, the contraction condition $\kappa\_{\mathrm{RRM}} = |\varepsilon|/(1 + \lambda) < 1$ fails across three cases:

1. **Linear drift:** at $\varepsilon = 1 + \lambda$ ($\mu\_0 \ne 0$), no fixed point exists and iterates march off linearly as $\theta\_t = \theta\_0 + t\mu\_0/(1+\lambda)$.
2. **Period-2 seesaw:** at $\varepsilon = -(1 + \lambda)$, algebraic fixed point $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \mu\_0/(2(1+\lambda))$ exists, yet repeated risk minimization enters a non-convergent **period-2 cycle** $\theta\_{t+1} - \theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = -(\theta\_t - \theta\_{\mathrm{PS}})$ for any $\theta\_0 \ne \theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$ ([Chapter 07](07-when-the-measure-becomes-the-goal.md), Goodhart cycling under target shifts).
3. **Exponential blow-up:** for $|\varepsilon| > 1 + \lambda$ ($\theta\_0 \ne \theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$), $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$ repels iterates monotonically ($\varepsilon > 1+\lambda$) or with alternating blow-up ($\varepsilon < -(1+\lambda)$). Faster retraining worsens instability.

Halting divergence requires one of two strict institutional remedies.

**Remedy 1: subordinated rule freeze.** A rule freeze over a pre-registered window is valid **only** when physical survival time clears recovery ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md), crosswind vessel survival margin) and live ex-ante/operational receiver protections hold: Scanlonian refusal against irreversible harms, $B\_{\mathrm{solvency}}$ or live discharge ceilings for compensable risk, and externally attested per-step non-export standing via `T3`/`T7` affected-party algedonic stop-triggers (while $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}$ and $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ remain strictly ex post under Brake 4). Expected detection-adjusted clawback liability must hold ($q := p\_{\mathrm{audit}} q\_{\mathrm{claw}} \ge c$ for audit detection $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} \in (0, 1]$ and compliance cost $c > 0$, backed by insolvency-proof escrow), a non-self-extending **sunset** must bind (**Reflexive Brake 3**, [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)), consecutive freezes without independent external ratification and mandatory cooldown are barred, and $T\_1$ counter-inductive `reopen` must stay open. Because freezing $\theta$ halts RRM oscillation only while offering strategic adversaries a stationary target to game, freezes must pair with randomized or low-powered probes.

**Remedy 2: low-powered pay and sealed probe draws.** Alternatively, to curb **internal** multitask staff gaming, organizations can decouple low-powered pay (Holmström & Milgrom 1991; noting that external population reactivity $|\varepsilon| \ge 1 + \lambda$ still requires step dampening, exogenous randomization, or Remedy 1 freezes) from independent low-stakes audits, keeping scoring rules, cutoffs, and sampling protocols public and contestable while sealing only specific sampled probe draws until ex-post review alongside non-disclosed `T3` multi-list capture–recapture ([Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md), curbing performativity and high-powered proxy gaming). NAEP audits exposing TAAS test-drilling (Klein et al. 2000) and UK money targets inducing bank balance-sheet reclassification (Goodhart 1975, 1984) document this divergence empirically.

In plain words, retraining faster on a strongly reactive population turns feedback into a pendulum or explosion unless institutions freeze rule updates under strict sunsets or lower metric stakes.

## 4. Dialogic and Perimeter Reception

## 5. The Laminar Boundary of T6

Calibrated, invariant rules break once disclosed thresholds become targets, defeating any illusion of an ungameable proxy. Laminar $T\_6$ assumes two idealizations that Part VII drops. First, it assumes rule freezes obey Brake 3 sunsets rather than mutating into permanent decrees under queue utilization saturation ($\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}} = \lambda/\mu\_s \to 1^-$, arrival/service rates distinct from §3's $(\mu\_0, \lambda)$) and compliance overload ([Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md), $T\_5 \oplus T\_1 \oplus T\_6 \oplus T\_7$ hijacked exception capture). Second, it assumes targets adapt around one archer's $\mathcal{D}(\theta)$ instead of drawing a second autonomous bow across shared boundaries ([Chapter 98](98-when-the-target-draws-its-bow.md), $[T\_1 \dots T\_7]\_A \otimes [T\_1 \dots T\_7]\_B$ when targets draw bows, and fifth falsifier $F\_5$). Reactive restraint therefore requires pre-outcome cryptographic locks on $\mathbf{u}\_t$ and undiscounted alarms ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) at $T\_7$.
