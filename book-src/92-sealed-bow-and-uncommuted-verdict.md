## 1. Closure of the Circuit ($T\_7$): Replicator Selection, Moral Hazard, and Agnotology

As established in [Chapter 25, *Evolution, Variation, and Selection*](25-evolution-variation-selection.md) and [Chapter 40, *Institution, Incentive, Metric*](40-institution-incentive-metric.md), compliant units (cost $c > 0$) and non-compliant units (expected penalty $q = p\_{\mathrm{audit}} q\_{\mathrm{claw}} \ge 0$ for audit detection $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} \in (0, 1]$ and clawback $q\_{\mathrm{claw}} \ge 0$) evolve in replicator log-odds at differential $-\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} = q - c$. Any positive compliance-cost gap $c > q \ge 0$ erodes compliance monotonically before the first shock; under limited liability ($q = 0$; Innes 1990) and exponential waiting times $\tau\_{\mathrm{shock}} \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1/\bar{T}\_{\mathrm{shock}})$ with mean shock interval $\bar{T}\_{\mathrm{shock}} > 0$ (distinct from trajectory horizon $T$), $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} = c > 1/\bar{T}\_{\mathrm{shock}}$ drives expected pre-shock log-odds decay past one nat and accelerates calm-window extinction ([Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); multi-shock persistence under $0 \le q < c$ remains an open conjecture). Enforcing $q \ge c$ requires bankruptcy-remote clawback escrow $q\_{\mathrm{claw}} > c / p\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ rather than nominal shell penalties. Hence **institutional governance is not one reading among three** on a scorecard: $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ fixes the sign of $q - c$ across the circuit.

## 3. The Five-Stage Acceptance Predicate and Laminar Corollary

Both gates feed the deterministic five-stage predicate $\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t; \boldsymbol{\tau})$ fixed in [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) (§B.1, the grammar and ordered predicate; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.5, formal acceptance syntax). Prior to Stage (i), a static pre-stage check refuses any cross-axis `(proposition, site)` type mismatch (emitted without a verdict). On compatible inputs with thresholds $\boldsymbol{\tau} = (\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}, \delta\_{\mathrm{band}})$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} > 0$ distinct from status discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$), its five fixed stages evaluate (i) $\mathrm{LL}(1)$ skeleton and threshold invariants ($L \le U$, $C\_{\mathrm{cap}} > 0$, $R\_{\mathrm{res}} \ge 0$, $\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} > 0$, returning `Skeleton`; derivation overruns `BudgetExceeded`; and attested `reopen` quarantine `CounterInductiveReopen`), (ii) $3/2/2$ axial coverage (`AxialCoverage`), and (iii) margin finiteness (`MarginMissing`). Stages (iv) and (v) then compute step Leontief fold $m\_\ast = \min\_a s\_a$ (`Collapse` when $m\_\ast < \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}$, or `Skeleton` if declared `(proposition, site, step)` metadata is missing or names a site strictly above $m\_\ast$; distinct from ex-post trajectory $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$) and the frontier band ($\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} \le m\_\ast < \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}} \implies \mathrm{Boundary}$, or `Skeleton` if declared site is missing or misses $m\_\ast$; else `Interior`). Across an $N$-step trajectory $\mathcal{S} = (\sigma\_1, \dots, \sigma\_N)$ ($N \ge 1$; empty sentences quarantine as `Skeleton`), Pass one runs Stages (i)–(iii) across all steps so structural, coverage, finiteness, or reopening faults anywhere in $\mathcal{S}$ preempt `Collapse`, and Pass two runs Stages (iv)–(v) step by step from each step's own sealed state.

### Corollary 1 (Laminar Contingency Invariance and Stage Non-Commutation)

Before checking the corollary, notice the two protections it locks in. First, judging an archer fairly means grading what was prepared and measured before impact, never letting a lucky gust rewrite a reckless release. Second, the five audit stages act like airlock doors in one strict sequence: checking thresholds before checking whether numbers are missing lets broken traces pass as healthy.

Along a single laminar trajectory, [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) (§B.1, stage order and tape sealing) implies both step invariants directly. Stages (i)–(v) read only sealed $\mathbf{u}\_t$, margins $\mathbf{m}\_t$, and thresholds $\boldsymbol{\tau} = (\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}, \delta\_{\mathrm{band}})$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} > 0$) while leaving $\mathbf{w}\_t$ unread in every branch. Consequently, $\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t; \boldsymbol{\tau}) = \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}'\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t; \boldsymbol{\tau})$ holds for every contingency tape $\mathbf{w}'\_t$, formalizing Antipater's *télos*–*skopós* split (granting moral non-culpability under exogenous wind only when upstream $\mathrm{PRI} = 0$ and identifiable criteria $\mathrm{LR} \ne 1$ hold). Moreover, permuting stages (i)–(v) breaks quarantine and soundness: moving **Stages (iv) and (v)** ahead of Stage (i) clears attested `reopen` probes as `Interior` whenever provisional margins satisfy $m\_\ast \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}}$ (and swapping `(iv, v)` into `(v, iv)` clears `Collapse` as `Interior`), moving Stage (iv) ahead of Stage (iii) lets $\mathrm{NaN}$ fail threshold checks silently, and moving Stage (iii) ahead of Stage (ii) treats empty axes ($\min \emptyset$) as numerical missingness rather than `AxialCoverage` (Feyerabend 1970).

In plain words, luck never alters the step verdict inside step $t$, and scrambling the five audit stages destroys safety quarantine.

**Survival crisis with governance corruption.** When both strike together, **survival pre-emption** ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ verified against independent external clock telemetry; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md), finite-clock crosswind vessel race) fires `Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)` ahead of missing-slot quarantine (`Regime III-A`). Logging channel corruption alongside that halt defeats compliance denial-of-service.

No unilateral probe can certify itself: attested reopening (Feyerabend 1970) stays in `CounterInductiveReopen` quarantine and escalates to standpoint-threshold governance ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), non-neutral omission under the minimum; [Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md), unsealing binary lethal-omission forks). ## 4. Concave Minimum Bias, Simultaneous Bands, and Exact Intersection-Union Testing

Evaluating a minimum from noisy finite samples systematically tilts downward because $\min(x\_1, x\_2) = \frac{1}{2}(x\_1 + x\_2) - \frac{1}{2}|x\_1 - x\_2|$ is concave across tied axes.

### Theorem 2 (Downward Finite-Sample Plug-In Bias of the Minimum)

Before reading the bias formula, notice why taking the minimum of estimated safety scores behaves differently from taking their average. If three independent measurements have the same true safety level, random error pushes one estimate slightly low and another slightly high. Taking the minimum always picks up the lowest noisy dip, never the high bounce, so naive plug-in estimates systematically understate the true weakest margin unless sample size grows large.

Let $\hat{\theta} = (\hat{\theta}\_1, \dots, \hat{\theta}\_k)$ be integrable unbiased estimators ($\mathbb{E}[|\hat{\theta}\_m|] < \infty$, $\mathbb{E}[\hat{\theta}\_m] = \theta\_m$) on native unbounded margin coordinates $\theta\_m \in \mathbb{R}$ across $k \ge 2$ axes (distinct from trajectory step fold $m\_\ast$ and margin vector $\mathbf{m}\_t$) with minimizer $m^\ast \in \mathrm{argmin}\_m \theta\_m$ and positive part $u\_+ = \max(u, 0)$. Plug-in bias $b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) = \mathbb{E}[\min\_m \hat{\theta}\_m] - \min\_m \theta\_m$ obeys:

$$b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) = -\mathbb{E}\Bigl[\max\_{m \ne m^\ast} (\hat{\theta}\_{m^\ast} - \hat{\theta}\_m)\_+\Bigr] \le 0.$$

In a symmetric tie ($\theta\_1 = \theta\_2 = \theta$, independent $\hat{\theta}\_1 \sim \mathcal{N}(\theta, \sigma\_1^2)$, $\hat{\theta}\_2 \sim \mathcal{N}(\theta, \sigma\_2^2)$ with $\sigma\_1^2 + \sigma\_2^2 > 0$), $\mathbb{E}[|\hat{\theta}\_1 - \hat{\theta}\_2|] = \sqrt{2(\sigma\_1^2 + \sigma\_2^2)/\pi}$ yields strict downward bias:

$$b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) = -\sqrt{\frac{\sigma\_1^2 + \sigma\_2^2}{2\pi}} < 0.$$

Across $k \ge 2$ i.i.d. Gaussian tied axes $\hat{\theta}\_m \sim \mathcal{N}(\theta, \sigma^2 / n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}})$ ($\sigma > 0$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} > 0$), Gaussian maximal bounds give $|b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta})| \le \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}}\sqrt{2\ln k}$, shrinking only as $1 / \sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}$ when axes tie closely. Because $b\_{\min} \approx 0$ on untied single-axis breaches, operators are strictly barred from adding back the symmetric tie offset $+\sqrt{(\sigma\_1^2 + \sigma\_2^2)/(2\pi)}$ to plug-in point estimates to clear safety cutoffs; safety assessment must use conservative simultaneous lower bounds $\min\_m \hat{\theta}\_{m, L}$ or calibrated testing below.

To separate genuine overlap from depletion **offline and ex post** (never steering live step actuation), a **two-sided simultaneous confidence band** maps a $(1 - \alpha)$ rectangle $\prod\_{m=1}^3 [\hat{\theta}\_{m, L}, \hat{\theta}\_{m, U}]$ into $[\min\_m \hat{\theta}\_{m, L},\mkern5mu \min\_m \hat{\theta}\_{m, U}]$ ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.6, simultaneous uncertainty bands), requiring multiplicity allocation. Conversely, the one-sided **Intersection-Union Test (IUT)** checks whether all three axes clear viability threshold $\tau$ (distinct from predicate $\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}$ and latencies) simultaneously, whose worst-case false-positive risk on $\mathbb{R}^3$ occurs with two axes infinitely safe and one on the fence, needing no $\alpha/3$ Bonferroni discount (Schoenen et al. 2024; Hothorn 2021; Kidd & Lin 2021).

Formally, the one-sided safety hypotheses test whether at least one axis fails ($H\_0$) against all three clearing $\tau$ ($H\_1$):

$$H\_0: \bigcup\_{m=1}^{3} \Theta\_{0,m} = \bigcup\_{m=1}^{3} \lbrace \theta\_m \le \tau \rbrace \qquad \text{versus} \qquad H\_1: \bigcap\_{m=1}^{3} \lbrace \theta\_m > \tau \rbrace.$$

Define normalized IUT statistics $Z\_m = (\hat{\theta}\_m - \tau)/\mathrm{se}\_m$ ($\mathrm{se}\_m > 0$, distinct from Guard Sites $T\_1$–$T\_7$) and marginal level-$\alpha$ rejection regions $R\_m = \lbrace Z\_m > c\_{\alpha, m} \rbrace$ (critical cutoffs $c\_{\alpha, m}, c\_\alpha$ distinct from compliance cost $c$; $\sup\_{\theta \in \Theta\_{0,m}} \mathbb{P}\_\theta(R\_m) \le \alpha$, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$). All-or-none rejection $R = \bigcap\_{m=1}^3 R\_m$ ($\min\_m Z\_m > c\_\alpha$ under common cutoff $c\_\alpha$) holds **marginal level-$\alpha$ validity** without Bonferroni $\alpha / 3$ adjustment under arbitrary dependence:

$$\sup\_{\theta \in \bigcup\_{m=1}^3 \Theta\_{0,m}} \mathbb{P}\_\theta\\!\left(\bigcap\_{j=1}^3 R\_j\right) = \max\_{1 \le m \le 3} \sup\_{\theta \in \Theta\_{0,m}} \mathbb{P}\_\theta\\!\left(\bigcap\_{j=1}^3 R\_j\right) \le \max\_{1 \le m \le 3} \sup\_{\theta \in \Theta\_{0,m}} \mathbb{P}\_\theta(R\_m) \le \alpha.$$

Unadjusted IUT holds level $\le \alpha$ everywhere, avoiding both naive plug-in $\min\_m \hat{\theta}\_m > \tau$ and power-penalizing $\alpha/3$, while reaching $\alpha$ on unbounded boundary rays ($\theta\_m = \tau$, $\theta\_{j \ne m} \to +\infty$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$; on normalized bounded coordinates $[0, 1]^3$, finite-sample Type I error stays strictly below $\alpha$). Under **mutually independent** exact level-$\alpha$ marginals, rejection at $\theta\_1 = \theta\_2 = \theta\_3 = \tau$ shrinks to $\alpha^3$; positive correlation lifts it toward $\alpha$, while one borderline axis depresses power (Hothorn 2021; Kidd & Lin 2021). Under allocation-bias drift $\eta = 0.1 \cdot E\_{32}$ (where $E\_{32}$ is the length-32 all-ones vector, distinct from *e*-process wealth and efficacy) across $r = 100{,}000$ lists ($2$ co-primary endpoints, $N = 32$), Schoenen et al. (2024) found mean Type I error reaching 0.0790 under `PBR(4)` ($P\_{\mathrm{RP}}(\mathrm{T1E} \le 0.05) = 0.00$), showing why IUT marginal validity presupposes upstream `T1`–`T3` randomization, blinding, and selection-bias guards alongside Gate 2.
