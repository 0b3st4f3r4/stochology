## 1. Pre-Seal Premise Rigging Undecidability

Under Stochology's **Premise Rigging Index** ($\mathrm{PRI} = \max(c\_{\mathrm{epi}}, c\_{\mathrm{spill}}) \in [0, 1]$; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md) §2.3, inquiry openness and omitted risk; [Appendix E](appendix-e-lexicon.md) §E.2, technical glossary), two hidden shortfalls track what was trimmed off the ledger.

Epistemic shortfall $c\_{\mathrm{epi}} = 1 - w\_{\mathrm{disp}}/w\_{\mathrm{true}}$ compares disclosed width $w\_{\mathrm{disp}} \in [0, w\_{\mathrm{true}}]$ against true width $w\_{\mathrm{true}} > 0$. Spillover shortfall $c\_{\mathrm{spill}}$ compares disclosed harm $D\_{\mathrm{disp}} \in [0, D\_{\mathrm{true}}]$ against latent harm $D\_{\mathrm{true}} \ge 0$ displaced onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)), equaling $0$ if $D\_{\mathrm{true}} = 0$ and $1 - D\_{\mathrm{disp}}/D\_{\mathrm{true}}$ otherwise. As $\mathrm{PRI} \to 1$, width or harm vanishes pre-seal, so a valid $T\_7$ seal never certifies an honest $T\_1$ frame.

### Theorem 1 (Observational Equivalence of Pre-Seal Framing Under Cryptographic Custody)

A cryptographic seal works like wax on a folded letter: it proves nobody rewrote the text after hardening, while hiding whatever was left outside beforehand—allowing an honest dossier and an amputated one to yield identical tamper-proof vaults.

Let disclosed dossier $\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ carry width $w\_{\mathrm{disp}} > 0$, disclosed external harm $D\_{\mathrm{disp}} \ge 0$, and externally custodied `SHA-256`/RFC 2104 HMAC tag $\mathcal{M}\_{\mathrm{HMAC}}(\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{disp}})$ locked at release $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ (Krawczyk, Bellare, & Canetti 1997). Fix rigging fraction $\alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}} \in [0, 1)$ ($1 - \alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}} > 0$), setting $w\_{\mathrm{true}} = w\_{\mathrm{disp}} / (1 - \alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}}) > 0$. If $D\_{\mathrm{disp}} > 0$, setting $D\_{\mathrm{true}} = D\_{\mathrm{disp}} / (1 - \alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}})$ gives $\mathrm{PRI} = \alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}}$.

If $D\_{\mathrm{disp}} = 0$, $D\_{\mathrm{true}} = 0$ preserves $\mathrm{PRI} = \alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}}$, whereas any uncounted harm $D\_{\mathrm{true}} > 0$ drives $c\_{\mathrm{spill}} = 1 \implies \mathrm{PRI} = 1$; likewise, $w\_{\mathrm{disp}} = 0 < w\_{\mathrm{true}}$ drives $\mathrm{PRI} = 1$. Because cryptographic custody inspects only $\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ without observing omitted territory $\Omega \setminus \Omega\_{\mathrm{disp}}$, both $\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ and $\mathcal{M}\_{\mathrm{HMAC}}(\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{disp}})$ match across every $\alpha\_{\mathrm{PRI}} \in [0, 1)$, rendering honest and amputated frames observationally equivalent inside the vault.
