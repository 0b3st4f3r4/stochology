## 1. Syntactic Invariance Versus Trajectory Pushforward

Treating Antipater's *télos*–*skopós* split as ahistorical invites Carneades's warning against severing good aim from real target risk (Plutarch c. 100 CE; Stobaeus 1884, *Eclogae* II.76.11–15, 77.1–5). Stochology's grammar therefore separates **single-step syntactic invariance** from **multi-step material pushforward**.

By stage non-commutation ([Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1; [Chapter 92](92-sealed-bow-and-uncommuted-verdict.md) §3), step $t$ evaluates $\mathcal{A}$ solely on sealed control tape $\mathbf{u}\_t$, thresholds $\boldsymbol{\tau} = (\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}, \delta\_{\mathrm{band}})$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} > 0$), and margins $\mathbf{m}\_t$, remaining invariant across all contingency tapes $\mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{w}'\_t$.

Judging step $t - 1$ solely on pre-impact commitments prevents hindsight bias, yet realized contingency $\mathbf{w}\_{t-1}$ propagates physically across World, Archive, queues, caregivers, and external reception before step $t$ seals ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §4). For $t > 1$, let $R\_{t-1}$ be prior reserve (net of any carried bailout or credit obligations), $\Delta R(\mathbf{u}\_{t-1}, \mathbf{w}\_{t-1})$ net reserve change, $O\_{t-1}$ prior archive, $\Phi\_{\mathrm{archive}}$ the archive update operator, $Q\_{t-1} \ge 0$ carried deliberation/repair backlog, and $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t} \ge 0$ cumulative caregiver allostatic load ([Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)). Let $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}} > 0$ (distinct from acceptance threshold $\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}$) and $\omega > 0$ denote external reception-perimeter tolerance and spillover weight, and let $d\_t, s\_t \ge 0$ measure baseline strain and spillover ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7, reception-perimeter diagnostic fold). Contingency $\mathbf{w}\_{t-1}$ pushes forward across all three planes:

$$R\_t = R\_{t-1} + \Delta R(\mathbf{u}\_{t-1}, \mathbf{w}\_{t-1}), \quad O\_t = \Phi\_{\mathrm{archive}}(O\_{t-1}, \mathbf{u}\_{t-1}, \mathbf{w}\_{t-1}), \quad Q\_t = \Phi\_{\mathrm{queue}}(Q\_{t-1}, \mathbf{w}\_{t-1}), \quad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t} = \mathrm{clamp}\\!\left(\frac{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}} - (d\_t + \omega s\_t)}{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}}},\mkern3mu 0,\mkern3mu 1\right).$$

Luck never alters yesterday's verdict, yet yesterday's weather sets today's starting reserve, archive, backlog, caregiver endurance, and external perimeter slack before release.

Strictly increasing chart $\varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4, ordinal unit-cube normalization) folds reserve $R\_t \ge 0$, clock headroom $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} / \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(Q\_{t-1})$ at $T\_5$ (carrying residual backlog $Q\_{t-1}$), and caregiver endurance $R\_0^{\mathrm{care}} - W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t}$ into vital margin $m\_{\mathrm{vital}, t} = \min\bigl(\varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}(R\_t),\mkern2mu \varphi\_{\mathrm{clock}}(\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} - \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}),\mkern2mu \varphi\_{\mathrm{care}}(R\_0^{\mathrm{care}} - W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t})\bigr)$ so rolled liquidity never hides exhausted queues or staff. Meanwhile, $\Phi\_{\mathrm{archive}}$ updates $O\_t \mapsto m\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}$ at $T\_3/T\_2$ before $\mathbf{u}\_t$ seals.

## 2. The Phantom *Skopós* Inside Step $t = 1$

Even at the very first step $t = 1$, control tape $\mathbf{u}\_1$ already embeds an anticipated *skopós* across three parameters spanning $T\_1$, $T\_2$, and $T\_4$.

First, assume outlay $C > 0$, efficacy $\eta \in (0, 1]$, and loss $L > 0$. The clamped cost–loss bar $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}(L) \in (0, 1]$ decreases strictly in $L$ on the interior $0 < C < \eta L$ and clamps at $1$ when $C \ge \eta L$ ([Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md) §3, knowledge thresholds in action; [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md), decision loss thresholds; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md), standpoint openness and threshold gaps). Because affected residents face larger losses than the institution ($L\_{\mathrm{affected}} > L\_{\mathrm{inst}}$), $0 < C < \eta L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$ yields $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}(L\_{\mathrm{affected}}) < p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}(L\_{\mathrm{inst}})$. That gap quietly displaces $L\_{\mathrm{affected}} - L\_{\mathrm{inst}}$ onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) before any observation begins, so step-1 control tape $\mathbf{u}\_1$ must bind its denominator to $L \ge L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$ with `FPIC` standing whenever external perimeters are exposed.

Second, on bounded outcome support $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ with observed share $p\_{\mathrm{obs}} \in [0, 1]$, Manski's (2003, 2026) partial identification width $\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}}$ scales proportionally with missing share $1 - p\_{\mathrm{obs}}$ ([Chapter 35](35-inference-and-evidence.md), inference under missing evidence; [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md), sharp partial identification bounds; [Chapter 87](87-aiming-against-counterfactual-winds.md) §6.2.3, finite-sample counterfactual interval coverage). When sample bounds invert ($\hat{\theta}\_U < \hat{\theta}\_L$), Jörg Stoye's (2021) $\mathrm{CI}^{\mathrm{MA}}$ stays non-empty: it covers every true parameter $\theta\_0 \in \Theta\_I$ pointwise at level $1 - \alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}$ ($\ne$ Pareto $\alpha$) when well-specified (whole-set coverage of $\Theta\_I$ requires distinct critical values) and concentrates around pseudotrue $\theta^\ast = (\sigma\_U\theta\_L + \sigma\_L\theta\_U)/(\sigma\_L + \sigma\_U)$ ($\sigma\_L + \sigma\_U > 0$) when $\theta\_L > \theta\_U$, without recovering parameters excised by truncation. Inverted sample bounds ($\hat{\theta}\_U < \hat{\theta}\_L$) therefore trigger misspecification quarantine (`CounterInductiveReopen`) rather than deploying pseudotrue $\theta^\ast$ as compliant coverage.

Third, at $T\_4$, curved harm induces Jensen's (1906) variance gap $\Delta\_J \ge 0$ separating expectation from nonlinear loss, forcing $\mathbf{u}\_1$ to price dispersion (failing for quadratic loss at $\alpha \le 2$; [Chapter 02](02-the-average-is-not-the-story.md), why averages mislead on curved losses; [Chapter 12](12-derivatives-and-convexity.md), convexity and Jensen's gap; [Chapter 31](31-distribution-and-moment.md), distribution moments and infinite variance). Mistaking that mirror for reality repeats [Aim Among Mirrors](00-preface.md), erasing the Non-Identical remainder (Adorno 1966).

## 3. The Adversarial *Anti-Gettier* Support-Censoring Trap

In Edmund Gettier's (1963) problem and its institutional parallels ([Chapter 04](04-it-worked-so-i-was-right.md), mistaking lucky outcomes for competence; [Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md), knowledge versus environmental luck), false premises yield true beliefs by luck ($G(p, c) = c/(p+c)$), hiding fragility across calm steps $t > 1$ as reserves $R\_t$ erode. Superposition $T\_2 \oplus T\_3 \oplus T\_4 \oplus T\_5$ creates its mirror-image inverse ([Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md) §2, knowledge reliability versus deceptive worlds): the **adversarial anti-Gettier trap**.

Here an agent passes Ville's (1939) supermartingales and demonstrates first-order adroitness and severity **conditional solely on $Y \le y\_{\mathrm{cens}}$**. Yet it fails Spanos's (2021) statistical adequacy, Mayo's (2018) unconditional severity against $T\_3$ omission ([Chapter 87](87-aiming-against-counterfactual-winds.md) §6.2.2), and Sosa's (2007, 2015) **full aptness**, which demands checking deceptive facades, Goodhart (1975)/Strathern (1997) gaming, and Trouillot (1995)/Bowker & Star (1999) archival silences before trusting calm numbers ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.1).

When an unrecorded surge strikes, over-leverage ruins both syndicate and external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$) while sealed logs read clean ($\mathrm{LR} = 1$). Empirical climate and opioid archives ([Chapter 93](93-fracturing-the-frame-before-the-seal.md) §1; Supran & Oreskes 2020; Goldberg & Vandenberg 2021, tactic #27; Gupta et al. 2024) confirm this pre-seal censorship.

Dropping flood surges above sensor ceiling $y\_{\mathrm{cens}}$ produces a double illusion: chopping extreme losses lifts apparent average return while compressing apparent volatility, tricking risk formulas past the true Kelly ruin boundary.

To compute both biases in closed form under **right-truncation** at $T\_3$ (dropping exceedances $> y\_{\mathrm{cens}}$, distinct from recorded censoring counts) against the unbounded Pareto baseline ($y\_{\max} \to \infty$, closely approached on $[y\_0, y\_{\max}]$ when $y\_{\max}/y\_0 \gg 1$), let gross return $g\_{\mathrm{gross}}$ exceed safe yield $r$ ($g\_{\mathrm{gross}} > r \ge 0$). Let losses follow unbounded $Y \sim \mathrm{Pareto}(y\_0, \alpha)$ with minimum loss $y\_0 > 0$, tail exponent $\alpha > 2$, and survival function $\mathbb{P}(Y > y) = (y\_0/y)^\alpha$ on $y \ge y\_0$. Net return $X = g\_{\mathrm{gross}} - Y$ then carries baseline mean $\mu\_{\mathrm{true}} > r$ and variance $\sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2$:

$$\mu\_{\mathrm{true}} = g\_{\mathrm{gross}} - \frac{\alpha y\_0}{\alpha - 1} > r, \qquad \sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2 = \frac{\alpha y\_0^2}{(\alpha - 1)^2(\alpha - 2)}.$$

Right-truncating above $y\_{\mathrm{cens}} > y\_0$ leaves visible fraction $p\_{\mathrm{vis}} = 1 - (y\_0/y\_{\mathrm{cens}})^\alpha \in (0, 1)$ on $[y\_0, y\_{\mathrm{cens}}]$ and conditional density $f\_{\mathrm{cens}}(y) = \alpha y\_0^\alpha y^{-\alpha-1}/p\_{\mathrm{vis}}$ (distinct from Kelly leverage fraction $f$). Integrating yields strict upward drift distortion and downward variance distortion relative to the unbounded baseline for every $\alpha > 2$ and $y\_{\mathrm{cens}} > y\_0$ (and likewise relative to $[y\_0, y\_{\max}]$-bounded Pareto moments when $y\_{\mathrm{cens}} < y\_{\max}$):

$$\mu\_{\mathrm{cens}} = g\_{\mathrm{gross}} - \frac{\alpha y\_0}{\alpha - 1}\mkern3mu\frac{1 - (y\_0 / y\_{\mathrm{cens}})^{\alpha - 1}}{p\_{\mathrm{vis}}} > \mu\_{\mathrm{true}}, \qquad \sigma\_{\mathrm{cens}}^2 = \frac{\alpha y\_0^2\bigl(1 - (y\_0 / y\_{\mathrm{cens}})^{\alpha - 2}\bigr)}{(\alpha - 2)\mkern3mu p\_{\mathrm{vis}}} - (g\_{\mathrm{gross}} - \mu\_{\mathrm{cens}})^2 < \sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2.$$

Dropping unobserved extreme surges thus simultaneously inflates net yield ($\mu\_{\mathrm{cens}} > \mu\_{\mathrm{true}}$) and deflates apparent risk ($\sigma\_{\mathrm{cens}}^2 < \sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2$).

Three errors then lock together across $T\_2 \oplus T\_3 \oplus T\_4 \oplus T\_5$.

**1. Manski ceiling compression.** Under bounded physical support $[y\_0, y\_{\max}]$ ($y\_{\max} > y\_{\mathrm{cens}}$), infinite $y\_{\max}$ sends Manski's upper bound and $\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p,U}$ to $+\infty$ ([Chapter 88](88-what-the-wind-leaves-on-stone.md) §6.3.4, Tail Saturation when storm logs go missing). Replacing $y\_{\max}$ by $y\_{\mathrm{cens}}$ and treating visible rows as exhaustive depresses Manski's (2003, 2026) upper bound:

$$U\_t^{\mathrm{cens}} = \mathbb{E}\_{\mathrm{cens}}[Y] < p\_{\mathrm{vis}}\mathbb{E}\_{\mathrm{cens}}[Y] + (1 - p\_{\mathrm{vis}})y\_{\mathrm{cens}} < p\_{\mathrm{vis}}\mathbb{E}\_{\mathrm{cens}}[Y] + (1 - p\_{\mathrm{vis}})y\_{\max} = U\_t^{\mathrm{true}}.$$

**2. Upward drift illusion.** Truncation inflates net drift ($\mu\_{\mathrm{cens}} > \mu\_{\mathrm{true}}$).

**3. Variance collapse at $T\_4$ and over-leverage at $T\_5$.** Define true Kelly fraction $f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}} = (\mu\_{\mathrm{true}} - r)/\sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2 > 0$. Stochology's shrinkage multiplier $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) = 1/(1 + 1/n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) \in (0, 1)$ ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} > 0$; motivated by Baker & McHale 2013; [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), finite-sample irreversible leverage shrinkage) recommends $f^\ast\_{\mathrm{epistemic}} = k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}})(\mu\_{\mathrm{cens}} - r)/\sigma\_{\mathrm{cens}}^2$. In continuous diffusion growth $g\_{\mathrm{time}}(f) = r + f(\mu\_{\mathrm{true}} - r) - \frac{1}{2}f^2\sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2$ ([Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md) §2.1, Itô–Kelly crosswind growth; [Chapter 78](78-layer-macro-finance-econometrics-actuarial-order.md), actuarial ruin order), doubling Kelly $2f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}}$ hits baseline parity $g\_{\mathrm{time}}(2f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}}) = r$. Zero logarithmic growth sits at $f\_0 = f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}} + \sqrt{(f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}})^2 + 2r/\sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2} \ge 2f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}}$, equaling $2f^\ast\_{\mathrm{true}}$ only at $r = 0$.

In discrete single-period wealth $1 + r + f(g\_{\mathrm{gross}} - r - Y)$, unbounded Pareto support gives positive ruin probability for every $f > 0$. Bounded support $[y\_0, y\_{\max}]$ with $y\_{\max} > g\_{\mathrm{gross}} - r$ instead imposes discrete solvency $f < f\_{\mathrm{solv}} := (1 + r)/(y\_{\max} - g\_{\mathrm{gross}} + r)$ (alongside slightly tighter bounded true moments than the unbounded $(\mu\_{\mathrm{true}}, \sigma\_{\mathrm{true}}^2)$ baseline above).
