## 1. The $10\text{ bits/s}$ Cognitive Queue and Compliance Denial-of-Service

Zheng and Meister (2025) showed that against $\sim 10^9\text{ bits/s}$ of peripheral sensory input, deliberate human behavioral and sensorimotor throughput saturates near $C\_{\mathrm{human}} \approx 10\text{ bits/s}$ reliably within roughly a factor of two ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.4.1, three-regime agency cognitive queue limits; [Appendix D](appendix-d-claim-ledger.md)).

In $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.2.2; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2), status-discounted detection $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}})$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$) and physical actuation $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > 0$ leave **deliberation headroom** $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}} := \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} - \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}) - \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > 0$ (kept distinct from [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.6.1 and [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3 #19's **detection headroom** $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$). If $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$ drives $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}} \le 0$, Collapse Proposition 2 strikes immediately.

When all seven guards compete for that deliberate channel during crises, random alert bursts drive waiting queues upward so paperwork forces recovery past survival well before full utilization.

Under unpruned $\bigoplus\_{j=1}^7 T\_j$, each incident carries audit load $B\_{\mathrm{audit}} = \sum\_{j=1}^7 B\_j > 0$ bits across $T\_1$–$T\_7$ and service time $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}}/C\_{\mathrm{human}} > 0$. Even in an isolated crisis ($\lambda \to 0^+$; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.4.1), $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} > T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ breaks survival.

For recurring arrivals ($\lambda > 0$), modeling the non-preemptive human reviewer as a stationary $GI/G/1$ queue with finite second moments ($0 \le c\_a^2, c\_s^2 < \infty$, $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$, tail index $\alpha > 2$) and cognitive queue utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} = \lambda \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} \in (0, 1)$ makes expected sojourn $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} + \mathbb{E}[W\_q]$ follow Kingman's (1961, 1962) heavy-traffic asymptote in $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}})$ ([Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.3; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md) §2.2).

Since $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$, $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \to 1^-$ drives $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) \to +\infty$, while heavy tails ($\alpha \le 2$) or bursts break finite moments earlier. Yet survival already fails at **sub-unit utilization**. On the Markovian benchmark ($c\_a^2 = c\_s^2 = 1$, exact $M/M/1$ identity $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}})$), enforcing the **expected-sojourn baseline** $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ ($T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}} > 0$) fixes only a necessary average ceiling $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{\max}(\lambda, T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) := C\_{\mathrm{human}}\mkern3mu T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/(1 + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}})$ and $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{\max} := \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/(1 + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) < 1$. Because stationary $M/M/1$ sojourn $S\_{\mathrm{delib}}$ is exponentially distributed around $\mathbb{E}[S\_{\mathrm{delib}}]$, pinning $B\_{\mathrm{audit}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{\max}$ still misses the hard survival deadline with probability $\mathbb{P}(S\_{\mathrm{delib}} > T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) = e^{-1} \approx 36.79\%$ (`Collapse Proposition 2`). Holding per-incident deadline exceedance below $\varepsilon \in (0, e^{-1})$ ($\mathbb{P}(S\_{\mathrm{delib}} \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) \ge 1 - \varepsilon$) tightens the operational ceiling via $\ln(1/\varepsilon) > 1$ alongside token-bucket peak arrival bounds ($\lambda\_{\mathrm{peak}}$) and preemptive emergency priority:

$$B\_{\mathrm{audit}} \le B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}(\lambda, T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) := \frac{C\_{\mathrm{human}}\mkern3mu T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}}{\ln(1/\varepsilon) + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}}, \qquad \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \le \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{(\varepsilon)} := \frac{\lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}}{\ln(1/\varepsilon) + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}} < 1$$

(with still tighter quantile margins whenever $c\_s^2 > 1$). Similarly, even a compact 1-byte (`8 bits/cue`) Level-1 synchronous operator cue stays strictly within the $C\_{\mathrm{human}} \approx 10\text{ bits/s}$ deliberate ceiling only at cue rates $\le 1\text{ cue/s}$ ($2\text{ cues/s} = 16\text{ bits/s} > 10\text{ bits/s}$), so static packet packing without arrival-rate throttling fails immediately under burst arrivals.

As alerts arrive more frequently ($\lambda$ rises), safe packet size $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}$ shrinks rapidly, triggering collapse well below unit utilization.

Only at $\lambda \to 0^+$ does allowable load approach $C\_{\mathrm{human}}T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/\ln(1/\varepsilon)$ (or $C\_{\mathrm{human}}T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ on single-packet service), opening **Compliance Denial-of-Service**: adversaries or competing overseers (Black 2008) spike $\lambda$ with low-PPV false alarms (Wong et al. 2021), launch synchronized $m$-packet bursts ($c\_a^2 \gg 1$) behind compliant long-run mean $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} < 1/2$, or bloat $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ past $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}$, forcing $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ (**Collapse Proposition 2**). Frontline teams absorb double binds, scapegoating ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}}$; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.3.1; [Prologue](00-preface.md)), and harm exported onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

## 2. The Chamfered Cognitive Frontier: Budget Constraint Versus Weighted Sum

Calm offline review can inspect Epistemic, Vital, and Governance checks across the whole $[\tau, 1]^3$ cube, whereas live cognitive bit budgets slice a corner off where all three axes demand simultaneous full scrutiny, without trading extra buffers for captured governance.

With positive headroom $T\_{\mathrm{rem}, t}^{\mathrm{delib}} > 0$, quantile reliability $\varepsilon \in (0, e^{-1})$, and indexed arrival rate $\lambda\_t > 0$, human capacity fixes a per-incident cognitive bit budget $B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t} := C\_{\mathrm{human}} T\_{\mathrm{rem}, t}^{\mathrm{delib}} / (\ln(1/\varepsilon) + \lambda\_t T\_{\mathrm{rem}, t}^{\mathrm{delib}}) > 0$ (replacing $\ln(1/\varepsilon)$ by $1$ recovers only the $36.79\%$-breach expected-sojourn baseline). Define $\mathbf{r}\_t = (r\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}, r\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}, r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t}) \in [0, 1]^3$ as **live verification resolution** over the Epistemic ($T\_1$–$T\_3$), Vital ($T\_4$–$T\_5$), and Governance ($T\_6$–$T\_7$) guard clusters on linear chart $\varphi\_a$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §2; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4), distinct from ex-post infima $(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}})$.

Minimal resolution $\tau \in (0, 1)$ defines cube $[\tau, 1]^3$ of volume $(1 - \tau)^3$. Pricing bits per unit depth at externally audited parameters $\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}, \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}, \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} > 0$ (protecting $r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t}$ lexicographically so epistemic complexity inflation never starves $T\_6$–$T\_7$ oversight toward $\tau$), live verification obeys the linear bit budget:

$$\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}} r\_{\mathrm{epi}, t} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}} r\_{\mathrm{vital}, t} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t} \le B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t}.$$

In reflected shortfall coordinates $y\_a = 1 - r\_{a, t} \in [0, 1 - \tau]$, define clipping deficit $\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} := \kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} - B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t}$ so the constraint becomes $\sum\_a \kappa\_a y\_a \ge \Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}}$. If $\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} \le 0$, the budget never binds. In the **single-corner regime** $0 \le \Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} \le \min\_a \kappa\_a(1 - \tau)$, the budget hyperplane clips a right tetrahedron of volume $\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}}^3/(6\mkern3mu\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}\kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}})$ off corner $(1, 1, 1)$ without touching faces $y\_a = 1 - \tau$. That leaves **chart-conditional chamfered volume** $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{chamfer}}(\tau)$:

$$\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{chamfer}}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3 - \frac{(\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}})\_+^3}{6\mkern3mu\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}\kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}} \qquad \bigl(\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} \le \min\_a \kappa\_a\mkern3mu(1 - \tau)\bigr),$$

where $(x)\_+ = \max(x, 0)$ (slicing a pyramid off the top corner while keeping the lower floor on all three clusters).

Two guardrails bound this formula. First, for $\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} > \min\_a \kappa\_a(1 - \tau)$, simple subtraction overflows $[\tau, 1]^3$ and requires inclusion–exclusion over breached faces $y\_a = 1 - \tau$, hitting zero once $B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t} \le \tau\sum\_a \kappa\_a$, at which point the verification box collapses and the operator enters **`Regime III-B` (Algedonic Halt)** or **`Regime I` (Safe Abstention)** rather than silently compressing resolution below $\tau$. Second, $(1 - \tau)^3$ and $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{chamfer}}(\tau)$ are Lebesgue measures conditional on linear chart $\varphi\_a$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §2; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4); applying strictly increasing top-anchored $C^1$ coordinate diffeomorphisms $\psi\_a : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($\psi\_a(1) = 1$, $\psi\_a' > 0$) transforms the threshold floor forward to $\tilde{\tau}\_a = \psi\_a(\tau)$, curving the hyperplane via $\psi^{-1}$ while rescaling the Lebesgue volume density by $|\det J\_{\psi^{-1}}|$.

Constitutionally, $\sum\_a \kappa\_a r\_{a, t} \le B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t}$ prices **live bit expenditure** (protecting $r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t}$ lexicographically; it is **not one reading among three**), never a compensatory trade-off or decision rule. Live authorization belongs solely to the canonical operator (`Regimes I–III`; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §5; [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1). Strictly **ex post**, **Reflexive Brake 4** folds $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ (**The minimum is not a weighted sum**), adding no fourth axis and issuing no live command ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.4; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7): extra stock never compensates captured governance, nor do thorough audits redeem an expired survival clock.

## 3. Frugal Crisis Pruning, Decisive Link, and Survival Preemption

When shocks compress $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ toward seconds, institutions separate **offline compilation** (sealing multiverse ($T\_1$), multi-list ($T\_3$), and counterfactual checks onto $\mathbf{u}\_t$ outside live shifts; [Chapter 94](94-yesterdays-crosswind-in-the-string.md)) from online **frugal crisis pruning**.

Under small samples and high variance, pruning weak cues beats heavily parameterized models (Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012; Neth & Gigerenzer 2015). Yet fast-and-frugal heuristics fail when recognition cue validity nears chance (falling from 89% at $\alpha\_{\mathrm{recog}} = 0.86$ to 54% at $\alpha\_{\mathrm{recog}} = 0.51$; Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012, subscripted distinct from Pareto exponent $\alpha$), under strategic gaming ($T\_6$), or under uncalibrated human scatter (Kleinberg et al. 2017/2018). Live triage therefore formats offline-validated cues as integer **natural frequency trees** (Gigerenzer & Hoffrage 1995, lifting Bayesian accuracy from 16% to 46% over conditional probabilities) with rotated/randomized live cue subsets so adversaries cannot game static pruned omissions.

Cutting packet size $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ alone does **not** hold $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} < 1/2$ ($\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}) < 1$ on $[0, 1)$) if false alarms spike $\lambda$. In stationary $GI/G/1$ queues, finite expected waiting time requires finite **service-time** second moment $c\_s^2 < \infty$ (Kiefer & Wolfowitz 1956, with $c\_a^2 < \infty$ additionally required for Kingman's upper delay bound). Keeping deliberation within quantile deadline headroom ($\mathbb{P}(S\_{\mathrm{delib}} \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) \ge 1 - \varepsilon$) requires single-packet headroom $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/\ln(1/\varepsilon)$, bounded post-filter arrival rate $\lambda\_{\mathrm{post}} \le \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{-1} - \ln(1/\varepsilon)/T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ ($\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \le \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{(\varepsilon)}$, reducing to $\lambda\_{\mathrm{post}} \le \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{-1} - 1/T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ on the expected-sojourn benchmark), and [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.2.2's two-track filtering: pre-authorized physical hazard sentinels trip immediate holds outside *e*-process lag, while discretionary alerts enter at $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ inside per-stream **mixture $e$-processes** ($E\_t^{(j)} = \sum\_{k=1}^t w\_k \prod\_{s=k}^t e\_s^{(j)} \ge E\_{\mathrm{trip}}$, $w\_k > 0$, $\sum\_{k \ge 1} w\_k \le 1$, $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[e\_s^{(j)} \mid \mathcal{F}\_{s-1}] \le 1$, preserving Ville's inequality $\mathbb{P}\_{H\_0}(\exists t : E\_t^{(j)} \ge E\_{\mathrm{trip}}) \le 1/E\_{\mathrm{trip}}$ while giving every incoming epoch undiluted weight $w\_k$, or $\alpha$-spent discrete epoch resets $\sum\_{k \ge 1} \alpha\_k \le \alpha$ at $E\_{\mathrm{trip},k} = 1/\alpha\_k$; Ville 1939; Ramdas et al. 2023) screened for shared-provenance independence. Answering Harsanyi's (1975) maximin-paralysis critique, bounded triage grasps the [Prologue](00-preface.md)'s **decisive link** (*"the particular link in the chain"*; Lenin 1975) before it snaps.
