## 1. The $10\text{ bits/s}$ Cognitive Queue and Compliance Denial-of-Service

Zheng and Meister (2025) showed that against $\sim 10^9\text{ bits/s}$ of peripheral sensory input, deliberate human behavioral and sensorimotor throughput saturates near $C\_{\mathrm{human}} \approx 10\text{ bits/s}$ reliably within roughly a factor of two ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.4.1, three-regime agency cognitive queue limits; [Appendix D](appendix-d-claim-ledger.md)).

In $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.2.2; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2), $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}, a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t})$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$, $a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t} \in [0, 1]$ finite on $(0, 1]$, cadence $\Delta t\_{\mathrm{shift}} > 0$) and $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > 0$ leave $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}} := \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} - \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}, a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t}) - \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}} > 0$ ($\ne$ [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.6.1's $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{detect}}$); $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$ or $a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t} \to 0^+$ drives $T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}} \le 0$ (**Collapse Proposition 2**).

When all seven guards compete for that deliberate channel during crises, random alert bursts drive waiting queues upward so paperwork forces recovery past survival well before full utilization.

Under unpruned $\bigoplus\_{j=1}^7 T\_j$, each incident carries audit load $B\_{\mathrm{audit}} = \sum\_{j=1}^7 B\_j > 0$ bits across $T\_1$–$T\_7$ and service time $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}}/C\_{\mathrm{human}} > 0$. Even in an isolated crisis ($\lambda \to 0^+$; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.4.1), $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} > T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ breaks survival.

For recurring arrivals ($\lambda > 0$), modeling the non-preemptive human reviewer as a stationary $GI/G/1$ queue with finite second moments ($0 \le c\_a^2, c\_s^2 < \infty$, $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$, tail index $\alpha > 2$) and cognitive queue utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} = \lambda \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} \in (0, 1)$ makes expected sojourn $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} + \mathbb{E}[W\_q]$ follow Kingman's (1961, 1962) heavy-traffic asymptote in $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}})$ ([Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md); [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.3; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md) §2.2).

Since $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$, $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \to 1^-$ drives $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) \to +\infty$, while heavy tails ($\alpha \le 2$) or bursts break finite moments earlier. Yet survival already fails at **sub-unit utilization**. On the Markovian benchmark ($c\_a^2 = c\_s^2 = 1$, exact $M/M/1$ identity $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{cog}}/(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}})$), enforcing the **expected-sojourn baseline** $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ ($T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}} > 0$) fixes only a necessary average ceiling $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{\max}(\lambda, T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) := C\_{\mathrm{human}}\mkern3mu T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/(1 + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}})$ and $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{\max} := \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/(1 + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) < 1$. Because stationary $M/M/1$ sojourn $S\_{\mathrm{delib}}$ is exponentially distributed around $\mathbb{E}[S\_{\mathrm{delib}}]$, pinning $B\_{\mathrm{audit}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{\max}$ still misses the hard survival deadline with probability $\mathbb{P}(S\_{\mathrm{delib}} > T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) = e^{-1} \approx 36.79\%$ (`Collapse Proposition 2`). Holding per-incident deadline exceedance below $\varepsilon \in (0, e^{-1})$ ($\mathbb{P}(S\_{\mathrm{delib}} \le T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) \ge 1 - \varepsilon$) tightens the operational ceiling via $\ln(1/\varepsilon) > 1$ alongside token-bucket peak arrival bounds ($\lambda\_{\mathrm{peak}}$) and preemptive emergency priority:

$$B\_{\mathrm{audit}} \le B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}(\lambda, T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}) := \frac{C\_{\mathrm{human}}\mkern3mu T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}}{\ln(1/\varepsilon) + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}}, \qquad \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} \le \rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}^{(\varepsilon)} := \frac{\lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}}{\ln(1/\varepsilon) + \lambda T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}} < 1$$

(tighter for $c\_s^2 > 1$ or heavy tails). Synchronous cues ($B\_{\mathrm{cue}} > 0$) cap $\lambda\_{\mathrm{cue}} \le C\_{\mathrm{human}}/B\_{\mathrm{cue}}$ and $\le \lfloor \Delta t\_{\mathrm{win}}\mkern2mu C\_{\mathrm{human}}/B\_{\mathrm{cue}}\rfloor$ per $\Delta t\_{\mathrm{win}} \ge B\_{\mathrm{cue}}/C\_{\mathrm{human}}$ ($\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} < 1/2$ at $\lambda\_{\mathrm{cue}} < C\_{\mathrm{human}} / (2 B\_{\mathrm{cue}})$).

As alerts arrive more frequently ($\lambda$ rises), safe packet size $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}$ shrinks rapidly, triggering collapse well below unit utilization.

Only at $\lambda \to 0^+$ does allowable load approach $C\_{\mathrm{human}}T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/\ln(1/\varepsilon)$ (or $C\_{\mathrm{human}}T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}$ on single-packet service), opening **Compliance Denial-of-Service**: adversaries or competing overseers (Black 2008) spike $\lambda$ with low-PPV false alarms (Wong et al. 2021), launch synchronized $m$-packet bursts ($c\_a^2 \gg 1$) behind compliant long-run mean $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} < 1/2$, or bloat $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ past $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}$, forcing $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) > \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ (**Collapse Proposition 2**). Frontline teams absorb double binds, scapegoating ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}}$; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.3.1; [Prologue](00-preface.md)), and harm exported onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

**Fluent wrongness latency.** For $Q\_s\ll P\_0$ ($0<D\_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q\_s\parallel P\_0)<\infty$; [Chapter 28](28-the-machine-and-the-deception.md) §2), **i.i.d.** $(X\_i)\_{i\ge 1}$ ($\tau\_{\mathrm{cue}}=B\_{\mathrm{cue}}/C\_{\mathrm{human}}>0$, $C\_{\mathrm{human}}T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}\ge B\_{\mathrm{cue}}$) caps $m\le\lfloor T\_{\mathrm{rem}}^{\mathrm{delib}}/\tau\_{\mathrm{cue}}\rfloor$; $\mathbb{P}\_{P\_0}(\text{reject})\le\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}$ and $(N,\psi\_N)$ ($\mathbb{P}\_{P\_0}(\psi\_N=1)\le\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}$, $\mathbb{P}\_{Q\_s}(\psi\_N=1)\ge 1-\beta\_{\mathrm{test}}$, $0<\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}<1-\beta\_{\mathrm{test}}<1$, $\ne\alpha$; $d\_{\mathrm{KL}}(1-\beta\_{\mathrm{test}}\parallel\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}})>0$; Wald 1947; Kullback & Leibler 1951; Kaufmann, Cappé, & Garivier 2016) yield $\mathrm{Power}\_{\mathrm{surf}}(m)\le\min(1,\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}+\sqrt{m D\_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q\_s\parallel P\_0)/2})$, $\mathbb{E}\_{Q\_s}[N]\ge d\_{\mathrm{KL}}(1-\beta\_{\mathrm{test}}\parallel\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}})/D\_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q\_s\parallel P\_0)$, $\mathbb{E}\_{Q\_s}[\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}^{\mathrm{surf}}]=\tau\_{\mathrm{cue}}\mkern2mu\mathbb{E}\_{Q\_s}[N]$. As $D\_{\mathrm{KL}}\to 0^+$, power $\to\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}$, cutting severity (Mayo 2018), raising complacency (Parasuraman & Manzey 2010) and overtrust (Perry et al. 2023), or pushing $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}^{\mathrm{surf}}>\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ (**Collapse Proposition 2**) absent external oracles. Symmetrically, **opaque correctness** ([Chapter 28](28-the-machine-and-the-deception.md) §2) denotes formally valid automated derivations whose raw size exceeds $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}^{(\varepsilon)}$: routing them directly into human review induces Compliance DoS and erodes $a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t}$ unless gated by isolated orthogonal checkers plus human-scale structural summaries.

## 2. The Chamfered Cognitive Frontier: Budget Constraint Versus Weighted Sum

Calm offline review can inspect Epistemic, Vital, and Governance checks across the whole $[\tau, 1]^3$ cube, whereas live cognitive bit budgets slice a corner off where all three axes demand simultaneous full scrutiny, without trading extra buffers for captured governance.

With positive headroom $T\_{\mathrm{rem}, t}^{\mathrm{delib}} > 0$, quantile reliability $\varepsilon \in (0, e^{-1})$, and indexed arrival rate $\lambda\_t > 0$, human capacity fixes a per-incident cognitive bit budget $B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t} := C\_{\mathrm{human}} T\_{\mathrm{rem}, t}^{\mathrm{delib}} / (\ln(1/\varepsilon) + \lambda\_t T\_{\mathrm{rem}, t}^{\mathrm{delib}}) > 0$ (replacing $\ln(1/\varepsilon)$ by $1$ recovers only the $36.79\%$-breach expected-sojourn baseline). Define $\mathbf{r}\_t = (r\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}, r\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}, r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t}) \in [0, 1]^3$ as **live verification resolution** over the Epistemic ($T\_1$–$T\_3$), Vital ($T\_4$–$T\_5$), and Governance ($T\_6$–$T\_7$) guard clusters on linear chart $\varphi\_a$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §2; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4), distinct from ex-post infima $(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}})$.

Minimal resolution $\tau \in (0, 1)$ sets $[\tau, 1]^3$. With weights $\kappa\_a > 0$ and reserve $B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min} \in [\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}\tau, \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}})$ upfront ($r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t} \ge B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}/\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} \ge \tau$ against $T\_6$–$T\_7$ bloat), live checks obey:

$$\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}} r\_{\mathrm{epi}, t} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}} r\_{\mathrm{vital}, t} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t} \le B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t} \qquad \bigl(\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t} \ge B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min} \ge \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}\tau\bigr).$$

In shortfalls $y\_a = 1 - r\_{a, t}$ ($y\_{\mathrm{epi}}, y\_{\mathrm{vital}} \in [0, 1 - \tau]$, $y\_{\mathrm{gov}} \in [0, 1 - B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}/\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}]$), set $\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} := \sum\_a \kappa\_a - B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t}$ ($\sum\_a \kappa\_a y\_a \ge \Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}}$). For $0 \le \Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} \le \min\bigl(\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}(1 - \tau),\mkern3mu \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}(1 - \tau),\mkern3mu \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} - B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}\bigr)$, the plane cuts one right tetrahedron from $(1, 1, 1)$, giving **chart-conditional chamfered volume** $\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{chamfer}}(\tau)$:

$$\mathrm{Vol}\_{\mathrm{chamfer}}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^2 \left(1 - \frac{B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}}{\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}}\right) - \frac{(\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}})\_+^3}{6\mkern3mu\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}\kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}} \qquad \bigl(\Delta\_{\mathrm{cut}} \le \min\bigl(\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}(1 - \tau),\mkern3mu \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}(1 - \tau),\mkern3mu \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} - B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}\bigr)\bigr),$$

with $(x)\_+ = \max(x, 0)$ (equaling $(1 - \tau)^3$ only at $B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min} = \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}\tau$).

Past $\min\bigl(\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}}(1 - \tau),\mkern3mu \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}}(1 - \tau),\mkern3mu \kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}} - B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}\bigr)$, inclusion–exclusion hits infeasibility at $B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t} < B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min} + \tau(\kappa\_{\mathrm{epi}} + \kappa\_{\mathrm{vital}})$, firing **`Regime III-B`** in Step 1 ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §5; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md) §3) ahead of Step 3; $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}} = 0$ needs Step-1 clearance ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$, i.e., $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}$ under Brake 3) via clocks, Brake 3, locks, $W\_{\mathrm{escrow}}$, $S3^\ast$, and reopening ([Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md)). Box volumes sit on $\varphi\_a$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §2; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.4); top-anchored $C^1$ $\psi\_a : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($\psi\_a(1) = 1, \psi\_a' > 0$) send $\tau \mapsto \tilde{\tau}\_a = \psi\_a(\tau)$ and $\Omega\_{\mathrm{ch}} \subseteq [\tau, 1]^3$ ($\ne \Omega\_{\mathrm{unspec}}$) to $\int\_{\Omega\_{\mathrm{ch}}} |\det J\_\psi(r)|\,dr$ (never $|\det J\_{\psi^{-1}}|$), curving cuts unless affine $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ scales by $\prod\_a \alpha\_a$ ($\alpha\_a \in (0, 1], \ne \alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}$).

Constitutionally, $\sum\_a \kappa\_a r\_{a, t} \le B\_{\mathrm{cog}, t}$ prices **live bit expenditure** (protecting $r\_{\mathrm{gov}, t} \ge B\_{\mathrm{gov}, \min}/\kappa\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ lexicographically; it is **not one reading among three**), never a compensatory trade-off or decision rule. Live authorization belongs solely to the canonical operator (`Regimes I–III`; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §5; [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1). Strictly **ex post**, **Reflexive Brake 4** folds $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ (**The minimum is not a weighted sum**), adding no fourth axis and issuing no live command ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.4; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7): extra stock never compensates captured governance, nor do thorough audits redeem an expired survival clock.

## 3. Frugal Crisis Pruning, Decisive Link, and Survival Preemption

When shocks compress $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ toward seconds, institutions separate **offline compilation** (sealing multiverse ($T\_1$), multi-list ($T\_3$), and counterfactual checks onto $\mathbf{u}\_t$ outside live shifts; [Chapter 94](94-yesterdays-crosswind-in-the-string.md)) from online **frugal crisis pruning**.

Under small samples and high variance, pruning weak cues beats heavily parameterized models (Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012; Neth & Gigerenzer 2015). Yet fast-and-frugal heuristics fail when recognition cue validity nears chance (participant reliance falling from 89% at $\alpha\_{\mathrm{recog}} = 0.86$ to 54% at $\alpha\_{\mathrm{recog}} = 0.51$; Brighton & Gigerenzer 2012, subscripted distinct from Pareto exponent $\alpha$), under strategic gaming ($T\_6$), or under uncalibrated human scatter (Kleinberg et al. 2017/2018). ## 4. Anti-"Audit Society" Limit and Complex-Systems Hysteresis

Together, Haldane & Madouros's (2012) bank-rulebook comparison and Toumi et al.'s (2026) anatomy of EU Joint Clinical Assessment proliferation confirm Power's (1997) **"Audit Society"** warning, converging with Tainter's (1988) diminishing returns on how verification rituals displace real judgment.

Complex-systems simulations explain why institutions cannot shed bureaucratic bloat *after* overshoot. In an exploratory four-stock ODE simulation, Bardi, Falsini, and Perissi (2018) showed stock-flow lags generating Seneca-curve hysteresis under **non-renewable** resources (where cutting bloat fails to restore earlier productivity) versus recurrent attractors under renewables. In a stochastic ABM and mean-field ODE ($N = 400$ agents) motivated by U.S. federal regulation growing roughly 2,500 pages per year (1950–2000), Schunck et al. (2024) formalized Return on Complexity $\mathrm{ROC}(t)$, showing expansion past $\mathrm{ROC}(t) < 0$ triggers collapse **under a low status-exploration / exit ratchet ($p\_e < 1/N$)** while $p\_e \ge 1/N$ destabilizes that fixed point.

Multiverse runs ($T\_1$), multi-list capture–recapture ($T\_3$), and forensic vault audits ($T\_7$) must therefore execute asynchronously via dedicated non-frontline off-shift staffing outside live queues so triage holds $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} < 1/2$. As the [Prologue](00-preface.md) warns, endless audit ritual drains strength before reserves expire, while manufactured emergencies invite exception capture ([Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md)).

Even under sub-critical triage, sustained vigilance near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng & Meister 2025) erodes **vital normativity**, Canguilhem's (1943/1966) power to absorb *infidélité du milieu* and institute new norms. That wear accumulates allostatic load $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t}$ (in native caregiver-strain units distinct from deliberate channel capacity), eroding usable capacity $C\_{\mathrm{human}}(W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t})$ and depleting Axis-2 endurance $S\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}^{\mathrm{care}}$ unless checked by mandatory shift rotation and rest-unwinding floors ($\Delta W\_{\mathrm{rest}, t} > 0$; [Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md) §1.1.1; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.4.1; [Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)), while spillover onto external reception perimeters folds into ex-post $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$.
