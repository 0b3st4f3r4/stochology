## 1. From Acyclic Ancestor Cones to Recurrent Coupled Maps

Cumulative cone evaluation $\mathcal{H}(L\_i) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_j)$ ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.6) presupposes acyclic $\mathcal{G}\_1$: strict upward dependencies extinguish basal shocks in finitely many steps ($M\_{\mathrm{DAG}}^m = 0$), whereas a single downward loop closes a cycle where shocks echo indefinitely.

Ordering $m \ge 2$ strata upward with strictly sub-diagonal upward couplings $c\_1, \dots, c\_{m-1} > 0$ forms nilpotent matrix $M\_{\mathrm{DAG}}$ ($\det(\lambda I - M\_{\mathrm{DAG}}) = \lambda^m$, $\rho(M\_{\mathrm{DAG}}) = 0$). Adding one downward edge $g\_{\mathrm{down}} > 0$ from upper stratum $j$ to lower stratum $i$ ($1 \le i < j \le m$, loop length $\ell = j - i + 1 \ge 2$) produces cyclic matrix $M\_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ with:

$$\det(\lambda I - M\_{\mathrm{cyc}}) = \lambda^{m - \ell}\\!\left(\lambda^\ell - g\_{\mathrm{down}} \prod\_{k=i}^{j-1} c\_k\right), \qquad \rho(M\_{\mathrm{cyc}}) = \left(g\_{\mathrm{down}} \prod\_{k=i}^{j-1} c\_k\right)^{1/\ell} > 0,$$

specializing to $\lambda^m - g\_{\mathrm{down}} \prod\_{k=1}^{m-1} c\_k$ when $(i, j) = (1, m)$.

Positive $\rho(M\_{\mathrm{cyc}}) > 0$ breaks nilpotency without automatically blowing up linear deficits while $\rho(M\_{\mathrm{cyc}}) < 1$. Collapse ensues only when cyclic feedback meets curved losses and random shocks inside a **stochastic recurrent coupled map** on stratum health vector $H\_t \in [0, 1]^m$, losing headroom to coupled deficits and noisy daily loads.

Set baseline health $H\_{i,0}^{\mathrm{base}} \in (0, 1]$, non-negative couplings $A\_{ij} \ge 0$ (combining upward $c\_k$ and downward edges such as $g\_{\mathrm{down}}$), convexity $\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} \ge 0$, and total step load $s\_{i,t} = \bar{s}\_i + \eta\_{i,t}$ ($\bar{s}\_i \ge 0$, $\mathbb{E}[\eta\_{i,t}] = 0$). With $\mathrm{clamp}(x, 0, 1) = \min(1, \max(0, x))$, one step updates:

$$H\_{t+1} = \Phi(H\_t, \eta\_t), \qquad H\_{i, t+1} = \mathrm{clamp}\\!\left(H\_{i, 0}^{\mathrm{base}} - \sum\_j A\_{ij}\Bigl((1 - H\_{j,t}) + \gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}(1 - H\_{j,t})^2\Bigr) - s\_{i,t},\mkern3mu 0,\mkern3mu 1\right).$$

Here stratum deficit $d\_{j,t} := 1 - H\_{j,t} \in [0, 1]$ drives $T\_4$ convexity ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} d\_{j,t}^2$), $T\_3$ omissions ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$) hide links $A\_{ij} > 0$, and $T\_6$ performativity induces metric edges $E\_{\mathrm{metric}}$ ([Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md)). Live authorization stays strictly with the per-step operator (`Regimes I–III`; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) §B.1), while completed trajectories fold strictly ex post into $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ under Reflexive Brake 4 ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)).

## 2. Convexity Variance Transfer and Phase-Locked Collapse

Four established mechanisms converge inside $\Phi(H\_t, \eta\_t)$.

**1. Cascade overload across topologies.** Adilson E. Motter and Ying-Cheng Lai (2002) studied shortest-path load redistribution when high-load **nodes** drop out ([Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.6 #26), triggering global cascades below critical tolerance $\alpha\_c$, contrasted with Kirchhoff flows and metric edges $E\_{\mathrm{metric}}$.

**2. Endogenous risk spirals and contractual tail export.** Jón Danielsson and Hyun Song Shin (2003) ([Chapter 78](78-layer-macro-finance-econometrics-actuarial-order.md)) showed uniform risk rules synchronize forced sales during downturns, driving physical strain into $S\_1$. Simultaneously, **contractual liability caps $X \wedge K$** ($K$ distinct from hop/threshold $k$; [Part VI — Conclusion](part-vi-conclusion.md)) truncate payer balance sheets while exporting uncapped physical damage $(X - K)\_+$ downward onto lower strata and external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$).

**3. Institutional hysteresis, complexity drag, and normativity.** Ugo Bardi, Sara Falsini, and Ilaria Perissi (2018) and Florian Schunck et al. (2024) establish irreversible non-renewable collapse and complexity-drag ratchets in [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md). C. S. Holling (1973) separated equilibrium return speed (*stability*) from shock absorption across basins (*resilience*; [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)), while Georges Canguilhem (1943/1966) showed *infidélité du milieu* breaks rigid operating norms.

**4. Support and ball-radius limits of robust optimization.** Local $f$-divergence balls confine worst-case laws to empirical support (Rahimian & Mehrotra 2019; Duchi & Namkoong 2021), while Wasserstein balls fail once cross-stratum feedback exits their finite ball radius (Mohajerin Esfahani & Kuhn 2018).

Even when linear couplings appear safe on average ($\rho(A) < 1$), curved losses convert random volatility into extra *mean* strain via Jensen's gap, dragging the healthy operating point to a synchronized cliff.

Two exact steps prove how collapse occurs under linearly stable coupling ($\rho(A) < 1$). First, on quadratic damage function $\phi(d) = d + \gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} d^2$ ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} > 0$), fluctuations around mean deficit $\bar{d}\_{j,t} = \mathbb{E}[d\_{j,t}]$ with finite variance $\sigma\_{j,t}^2 = \mathrm{Var}(H\_{j,t}) < \infty$ ($\alpha > 2$ pre-clamp) yield the exact **Jensen convexity gap**:

$$\Delta\_J(\phi, d\_{j,t}) = \mathbb{E}\bigl[\phi(d\_{j,t})\bigr] - \phi(\bar{d}\_{j,t}) = \gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}\mkern3mu\sigma\_{j,t}^2 \ge 0.$$

Every coupled link $A\_{ij} > 0$ thus feeds volatility $\sigma\_{j,t}^2$ into downward **mean deficit** $\sum\_j A\_{ij}\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}\sigma\_{j,t}^2$; post-clamp variance stays bounded by $1/4$ only by clipping excursions onto $H = 0$, whereas heavy-tailed shocks ($\alpha \le 2$; [Chapter 89](89-curvature-of-the-flight-path.md)) drive pre-clamp $\Delta\_J \to +\infty$.

Second, in the unclipped interior, expected deficits evolve with Jacobian $J\_D(\bar{d}) = A\mkern3mu\operatorname{diag}\bigl(1 + 2\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}\bar{d}\_j\bigr)$, so viability $1 - \bar{d}\_i^\ast \ge \tau$ requires $\rho(J\_D(\bar{d}^\ast)) < 1$, not just $\rho(A) = 0$. In a symmetric profile with baseline health $b \in (0, 1]$, mean load $s \ge 0$, stationary interior variance $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, row sum $\sum\_j A\_{ij} = a \in (0, 1)$ ($\rho(A) = a < 1$), and effective load $L\_{\mathrm{eff}} := 1 - b + s + a\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}\sigma^2 \ge 0$ ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} > 0$), equilibrium obeys $a\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}\bar{d}^2 - (1 - a)\bar{d} + L\_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0$, with:

$$\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}} = (1 - a)^2 - 4a\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} L\_{\mathrm{eff}}, \qquad \bar{d}\_{\pm} = \frac{(1 - a) \pm \sqrt{\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}}}}{2a\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}}, \qquad J(\bar{d}\_{\pm}) = a\bigl(1 + 2\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}}\bar{d}\_{\pm}\bigr) = 1 \pm \sqrt{\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}}}.$$

While $\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}} > 0$ and $\bar{d}\_- \le 1 - \tau$, branch $\bar{d}\_-$ remains stable ($J(\bar{d}\_-) < 1$) inside separatrix $\bar{d}\_+$; pushing $4a\gamma\_{\mathrm{conv}} L\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ past $(1 - a)^2$ sends $\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}}$ across zero at $J(\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{fold}}) = 1$, extinguishing every stratum onto $H = 0$ in a fold bifurcation despite $\rho(A) = a < 1$.

## 3. Sacrificial Decoupling Breakers: Controlled Islanding

Once downward coupling drives $\rho(J\_D(\bar{d})) \ge 1$ or $\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}} < 0$, layer-wise review stalls against human deliberate capacity ([Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), collision at ten bits per second). Committee deliberation pushes recovery time $\tau\_r = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ past hop propagation $k\tau\_p$ ([Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md), multi-hop cascade speed). Routing emergency halts through slow consensus similarly imports asynchronous blocking onto safety ([Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md), distributed consensus bottlenecks). Containment therefore requires ***ex ante* structural decoupling** (Perrow 1984) or **autonomous out-of-band sacrificial breakers** via Beer's (1972) $S3^\ast$ channel ([Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md), circuit breakers in distributed systems). Those breakers act inside local window $\tau\_{\mathrm{local}} < \tau\_p$ while severing metric edges $E\_{\mathrm{metric}}$ (Goodhart 1975).

Decoupling separates two algebraic regimes.

**1. Full nilpotent islanding.** Severing all downward and self-loop edges ($A\_{ij} = 0$ for all $i \le j$) restores strict lower-triangularity ($\rho(A) = 0$) and guarantees finite-step termination.

**2. Subcritical contractive damping.** Throttling downward links while preserving a loop ($A\_{ij} > 0$, $c\_k > 0$) keeps $\rho(A) > 0$ (non-nilpotent) yet stabilizes interior states under a contractive fixed point. That equilibrium requires $\rho(J\_D(\bar{d}^\ast)) < 1$ and $H\_i^\ast \ge \tau$ across strata—reducing to $\Delta\_{\mathrm{fold}} > 0$ and $\bar{d}\_- \le 1 - \tau$ in §2's symmetric profile—with fluctuations confined inside the separatrix basin.

When inter-stratum couplings incur time-varying delays $0 \le \tau\_e(t) \le \bar{\tau}$ ($\bar{\tau} > 0$) and jitter $-\mu\_- \le \dot{\tau}\_e(t) \le \mu\_+ < 1$ ($0 \le \mu\_+ < 1$, $\mu\_- \ge 0$) inside osmotic sector $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}} \in (0, 1]$, subcritical contractive damping requires regulating $\bar{\tau}$ below the Wirtinger–RCC closed-form bound $\bar{\tau}^{\mathrm{NL\text{-}Q}}\_{\max}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) = H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}})/(\bar{\eta}\,\Gamma\,\lambda\_{\max}(L\_{\mathcal{G}}))$ under Anderson & Morley's (1985) degree ceiling ([Chapter 26](26-regulation-and-homeostasis.md) §1; [Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md) §2; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2 `(15a)`). That $\sqrt{2}$-baseline numerator lies strictly below the scalar Hopf limit ($\sqrt{2} < \pi/2$) and takes $(\bar{\eta}, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}}) = (1, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}})$ under pure telemetry versus **$\bigl(1 + \mu\_-,\mkern2mu \frac{1 - \mu\_+}{1 + \mu\_-}\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}\bigr)$ under Doppler distortion**, pairing $(\bar{\eta}\_{ij}, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff},ij})$ inside $B\_{ij}$ so unadjusted $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$ never inflates one-hop budgets. Checks must run locally so delayed feedback never amplifies fold synchronization.

Either decoupling regime books an explicit sacrificial write-down ($\Delta \bar{s}\_u > 0$) on institutional stratum $u$—maintaining $H\_u^\ast(\bar{s}\_u + \Delta\bar{s}\_u) \ge \tau$ or wiping equity and creditors—rather than draining basal stocks. Reopening a tripped three-state breaker ([Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md), half-open canary recovery) faces hysteresis (Ulrich 2017; Bardi, Falsini, & Perissi 2018): because shock narrows vital normativity (Canguilhem 1943/1966), returning directly to full load re-locks failure unless a bounded canary probe confirms headroom ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$).

Unilateral islanding must never buy local balance ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}^{(A)} > \tau$) by dumping unserved demand onto external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md); [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)). Trajectories fold **strictly ex post** into $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ under Reflexive Brake 4 without adding a fourth axis or driving any live step ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7).

**Failure conditions.** Decoupling fails if (i) common-cause shocks strike simultaneously ($k = 0$) or $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{local}}$, (ii) limited liability shifts losses onto lower strata or third parties (external reception-perimeter failure $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} = 0$, Collapse Proposition 3), or (iii) premature reopening ignores hysteresis or $T\_3$ blind spots ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$).

## 4. Anti-Pythagorean Scope Limits and Trans-Stratified Domains

Downward collapse tempts readers to treat Part V's thirty-five-order matrix as a closed Pythagorean cosmology—or to invent a thirty-sixth stratum whenever crises cross layers. Both moves fail.

Across three planes ($P = 3$: World $P\_1$, Archive $P\_2$, Institution $P\_3$), the two premises in [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) (§B.2, combinatorial layer mapping) fix $n = P(P - 1) + 1 = 7$ guard sites. Setting five orders per stratum via $b(n) = \binom{n}{3}/n = (n - 1)(n - 2)/6 = 5$ yields $\binom{n}{3} = 5n$, whose unique positive integer root is $n = 7$—fixing $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ combinations ([Chapter 09](09-sets-relations-functions.md) §4; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)). That count is a theorem of the three-plane artifact, not a law of nature.

[Chapter 70](70-layer-acoustic-aesthetic-tragic-pedagogical-order.md) (acoustic tuning and tragic limits) grounds this modesty in music. Since $(3/2)^{12} \neq 2^7$, the **Pythagorean comma** $1200\log\_2(3^{12}/2^{19}) \approx 23.46\text{ cents}$ forces any tuning temperament to distribute slack or absorb a "wolf fifth." Part V obeys the exact same rule.

**1. Strata anchors are recorded designations.** Headings ($S\_1$–$S\_7$) across Part V are a **designation recorded rather than derived**, organizing comparison rather than deducing natural law. Downward feedback $S\_7 \to S\_1\text{–}S\_6$ acts inside coupled map $\Phi$.
