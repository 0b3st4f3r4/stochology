## Arm 1 — Perishable Algedonic Sabotage Across $T\_5$ and $T\_1$

Consider an initial protective reserve $R\_0 > 0$ supporting an exposed physical base $B > 0$ that suffers an unlogged fractional bleed rate $h > 0$ per shift, removing $h B > 0$ units each shift before visible rupture. Writing smooth horizon $\tau\_{\mathrm{cont}}(h) = R\_0 / (h B)$ separates discrete shifts-to-ruin $\tau\_{\mathrm{ruin}}$ from its differentiable envelope:

$$\tau\_{\mathrm{ruin}} = \left\lceil \frac{R\_0}{h B} \right\rceil, \qquad \tau\_{\mathrm{cont}}(h) = \frac{R\_0}{h B}, \qquad \frac{\partial \tau\_{\mathrm{cont}}}{\partial h} = -\frac{R\_0}{h^2 B} < 0.$$

Doubling the unlogged bleed rate $h$ or exposed base $B$ halves the remaining survival window hyperbolically ($\partial \tau\_{\mathrm{cont}} / \partial h < 0$).

Now couple silent bleed to $T\_1 \oplus T\_5$ alarm saturation inside $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}) + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ ([Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md)): status discounting $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$ dilates detection latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}})$ hyperbolically as $1/(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})$ ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

Meanwhile, under the cognitive collision limit ([Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)), noisy alert streams push queue utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}} = \lambda\_{\mathrm{cog}}\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} \in [0, 1)$ upward ($\lambda\_{\mathrm{cog}} > 0$, false-alarm share $1 - \mathrm{PPV}$, spurious rate $\alpha \in (0, 1)$), exploding $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ via Kingman's (1961) asymptote past $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ while silent bleed drains $R\_0$.

Empirical audits confirm how sponsor-skewed filings and industry doubt manufacture this Arm 1 blindness ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md); [Chapter 68](68-layer-pharmacovigilance-regulatory-toxicology-order.md); [Chapter 87](87-aiming-against-counterfactual-winds.md); [Chapter 91](91-when-the-target-reads-the-archer.md); Schäffer et al. 2023; Bero et al. 2016; Supran & Oreskes 2020).

Captured submissions mask hazards while noise crowds attention, so Stochology locks $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ ([Prologue — Aim Among Mirrors](00-preface.md); [Legitimacy, Accountability, and Institutions](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)). Because requiring unanimous agreement across $m \ge 2$ channels indiscriminately lets captured sponsors silence a lone whistleblower, $T\_5$ enforces the bifurcation of [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) and [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md).

**Pre-authorized physical hazard sentinels** trip defensive holds on one validated reading, whereas **discretionary alerts** enter at $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ inside **per-stream lower-floored** sequential likelihood ratios ($\prod\_{s \le t} e\_s \ge E\_{\mathrm{trip}}$) audited independently by Beer's (1972) $S3^\ast$ alongside an isolated unqueued track for protected unsafe-work refusal and pre-termination bitemporal notice custody plus external union/inspectorate routes shielding subcontracted and temporary staff from off-ledger non-renewal ([Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) §3; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.2.2). When $m \ge 2$ **conditionally independent** streams passing provenance-graph independence checks (excluding shared sponsors, methodologies, or common sensors) agree, spurious noise collapses to $\alpha^m$—failing immediately under shared sponsor capture.

## Arm 2 — Self-Authored Scarcity and Schmittian Exception Capture ($T\_5 \oplus T\_6 \oplus T\_7$)

Where Arm 1 dulls alarms until bleed exhausts $R\_0$, **Arm 2** exploits high utilization to shrink slack until survival time falls inside ordinary committee review, manufacturing a permanent pretext to suspend oversight.

Let buffer utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}} \in (0, 1)$ (distinct from queue traffic utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}} = \lambda/\mu$ and cognitive utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{cog}}$) approach maximum buffer $B\_{\max} > 0$, shrinking effective slack to $B\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}}) = B\_{\max}(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{buf}})$. Under crisis drawdown rate $d > 0$ and committee latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} > 0$ satisfying interior regime $0 < d\mkern3mu\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} < B\_{\max}$, survival time and critical utilization obey:

$$\mathrm{TTS}(\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}}) = \frac{B\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}})}{d} = \frac{B\_{\max}(1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{buf}})}{d}, \qquad \rho\_{\mathrm{buf,crit}} = 1 - \frac{d\mkern3mu\tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}}{B\_{\max}} \in (0, 1),$$

so operating above $\rho\_{\mathrm{buf,crit}}$ guarantees $\mathrm{TTS}(\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}}) < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$.

Pushing $\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}} > \rho\_{\mathrm{buf,crit}}$ turns [Chapter 91](91-when-the-target-reads-the-archer.md)'s subordinated rule freeze into permanent override, accelerated when shrinking slack degrades effective service capacity $\mu(\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}})$ and drives queue congestion $\rho\_{\mathrm{queue}} / (1 - \rho\_{\mathrm{queue}})$ inside $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ([Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md); [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)). As Karl Marx (1852, chs. IV–VII) observed in *The Eighteenth Brumaire*, executive power entrenches rule by turning fear of disorder into an unending state of siege—whereas inventing crises to stampede attention is usurpation ([Prologue](00-preface.md)).

Sovereign-exception decisionism—often labeled Schmittian—treats crisis as juridical norm-suspension (or permanent normalized exception) above law. Yet Thomas Hobbes (1651, ch. 21) grounded obedience in protection whose failure dissolves subjects' obligation, and Baruch Spinoza (2007 [1670], ch. 17)—developed as constituent *potentia* against constituted *potestas* by Antonio Negri (1981) and Enrique Dussel (2008 [2006])—showed power never alienates without remainder ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)).

Nor do safety vetoes entail the maximin paralysis critiqued by John C. Harsanyi (1975) or passive "wait-and-learn" deferral rejected even under bilateral irreversibility (Sunstein 2003, 2010; Hahn & Sunstein 2005): Philip Pettit's (1997) non-domination pairs contestability with **Reflexive Brake 3** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)), while avoiding the discursive dilemma proved by Christian List and Philip Pettit (2002) demands a conjunctive safety veto. Stripping slack triggers **Collapse Proposition 2**, manufacturing the time famine used to suspend Governance (**Collapse Proposition 3**; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).

Display dashboards cannot repair Arm 2 capture. Without enforceable consequence, polycentric proliferation merely creates accountability disorder ([Chapter 92](92-sealed-bow-and-uncommuted-verdict.md), accountable forums versus theatre; [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), multiple accountability disorder; Bovens 2007; Black 2008). Real governance demands the non-substitutable conjunction $\mathrm{Observability} \times \mathrm{Authority} \times \mathrm{Consequence}$ alongside anti-closure separation ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md), institutional accountability with teeth; [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md), preventing metric capture). It also binds Stochology's three ethical refusals $R\_1$–$R\_3$ (distinct from reserve $R\_0$; [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md), foundational ethical refusals of judging): no metric surveillance over reception ($R\_1$), no pact betrayal, double binds (Bateson 1972/2000), or scapegoating (Girard 1986 [1982]; $R\_2$), and no covert premise-rigging ($R\_3$).

When exception capture disables consequence, physical and moral deficits spill onto care workers and public social reproduction, silently degrading external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 98](98-when-the-target-draws-its-bow.md), cross-boundary externalization when targets draw bows).

**Moral injury versus burnout.** Under chronic austerity and betrayal, moral injury diverges structurally from burnout. In a systematic review of 15 studies (total $N \approx 21{,}700$, 2019–2025, from 1,525 records / 891 screened), Kakouris et al. ## Constitutional Precommitment (Elster 1979) and Survival–Corruption Co-Reporting

How can **Reflexive Brake 3** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.3.3, bounded *kairós* reflexivity under strict rules) permit rapid protection without licensing self-authored exception? Distinguish fast-path recovery latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} := \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{fast}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}^{\mathrm{fast}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}^{\mathrm{fast}} > 0$ (zero committee delay, $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}^{\mathrm{fast}} = 0$) from ordinary committee-bound recovery latency $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{ord}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}} > \tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}$, with positive survival horizon $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} > 0$. Survival below $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}}$ (or below $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{fast}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$) immediately triggers **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), lexicographic survival halt; [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md), finite-clock preemption). Only the non-empty interior window $\tau\_{\mathrm{fast}} \le \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$ licenses a pre-committed emergency fast-path bound by Jon Elster's (1979) constitutional precommitment under four invariants.

**1. Telemetry custody separation and mass-balance audits.** Buffer utilization $\rho\_{\mathrm{buf}}$, drawdown $d$, and $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ must be metered outside executive command and coupled to periodic physical mass-balance and $T\_3$ inventory audits detecting unlogged bleed $hB$. Extending Jon Elster's (1979) precommitment analysis, if a coercive monopoly captures the clock monitor itself, precommitment collapses and residual refusal returns ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md), legitimate institutional checks; [Prologue](00-preface.md), refusal of captured metrics).

**2. Bounded defensive release only.** Fast-path activation releases only pre-escrowed defensive buffers $B\_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and halt latches. It carries zero power to relax $T\_3$ archive selection bounds, $T\_4$ diagnostic thresholds, $T\_6$ performative constraints, attested $T\_1$ counter-inductive reopening (`CounterInductiveReopen`), or $T\_7$ dissent logs.

**3. Automatic non-self-extending sunset and replenishment lock.** Every bypass expires on an immutable clock or once $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$ (restoring ordinary committee latency $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{ord}}$); once activated, subsequent non-halt buffer releases remain locked until buffer replenishment completes and an uncaptured Bovensian forum under $T\_7$ conjunctive review explicitly ratifies renewal (otherwise escalating automatically to `Regime III-B` if the clock expires while $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$ persists).

**4. Mandatory reopening and bankruptcy-remote clawback escrow.** Write the replicator differential $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} = c - p\_{\mathrm{audit}} q\_{\mathrm{claw}}$ with compliance cost $c > 0$, exception rent $\Delta\pi\_{\mathrm{exc}} \ge 0$, and protected minimum audit floor $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} \ge p\_{\min} > 0$ funded independently of executive budgets ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md); [Chapter 92](92-sealed-bow-and-uncommuted-verdict.md)). Impunity ($q\_{\mathrm{claw}} = 0$) erodes compliance before the first shock, while multi-shock persistence remains an open heuristic conjecture ([Chapter 25](25-evolution-variation-selection.md)). Only a constitutional floor placed *above* competing units—**not one reading among three**—setting $q\_{\mathrm{claw}} > \max(c, c + \Delta\pi\_{\mathrm{exc}}) / p\_{\mathrm{audit}}$ onto bankruptcy-remote, parent-consolidated deferred executive compensation flips $\gamma\_{\mathrm{select}} < 0$; as $p\_{\mathrm{audit}} \to 0^+$ under captured oversight, required escrow diverges ($c / p\_{\mathrm{audit}} \to +\infty$) into collision with Brake 2's hoarding ceiling $R^\ast$, proving why nominal fines fail and independent audit floors are mandatory.

When survival breaches ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$) coincide with governance corruption, **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** preempts missing-slot quarantine (`Regime III-A`) to defeat compliance denial-of-service ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md)), binding the corruption flag to the $T\_7$ docket and routing unmodeled ruptures to attested $T\_1$ `CounterInductiveReopen` ([Chapter 92](92-sealed-bow-and-uncommuted-verdict.md)).
