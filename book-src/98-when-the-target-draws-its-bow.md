## Why Relational Alterity Must Not Become a Fourth Plane $P\_4$

When downstream harm is unmistakable, a tempting shortcut suggests adding a fourth plane $P\_4$ (Alterity) beside $\mathsf{World}$, $\mathsf{Archive}$, and $\mathsf{Institution}$, or inserting $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ inside $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$. Stochology rejects both shortcuts.

Under [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md) (§B.2), [Appendix E](appendix-e-lexicon.md), [Chapter 09](09-sets-relations-functions.md), [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) (§9.6), and [Chapter 96](96-arrows-that-bend-the-strata.md), ordered crossings fix $n(P) = P(P - 1) + 1$ guard sites. At $P = 3$, $n(3) = 7$ sites ($T\_1$–$T\_7$) solve $b(n) = \binom{n}{3}/n = (n - 1)(n - 2)/6 = 5$ uniquely and yield Vandermonde partition $\sum\_{k\_1+k\_2+k\_3=3} \binom{3}{k\_1}\binom{2}{k\_2}\binom{2}{k\_3} = 12 + 9 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 35$ triadic combinations across seven five-order strata. Stratum anchors remain a **designation recorded rather than derived**. Appending $P\_4$ inflates the count to $n(4) = 13$ and $\binom{13}{3} = 286$ without adding any mediation plane: external spillovers still travel through world consequences, archive omissions, and institutional rules.

Nor can $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ enter $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$: **The minimum is not a weighted sum**, and merging internal collapse with exported harm blurs self-failure with predation. Replying to Luhmann (1984), Stochology audits a bounded three-plane circuit ([Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)) strictly **ex post** under **Reflexive Brake 4** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.7) inside $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$, never as a live control law.

## The Two-Archer Externalization Theorem and Unilateral Pricing Failure

When upstream town $A$ raises floodwalls while downstream community $B$ absorbs deflected surges, $A$'s internal safeguards can stay above $\tau$ at the exact moment exported load drives $B$'s reception tolerance to zero.

Couple upstream Archer $A$ ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}^{(A)}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}^{(A)}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}^{(A)} > \tau \in (0, 1)$, so $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}^{(A)} > \tau$) with peer Archer $B$ over finite horizon $t \in \lbrace 1, \dots, T\rbrace$ (keeping Archer index $B$ distinct from posture/buffer quantities $B\_{A,t}, B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}}, B\_{B,t}$, solvency reserve $B\_{\mathrm{solvency}}$, and social burden $B^+(f)$, and keeping $\delta\_{\min}$ distinct from $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$). Suppose posture $B\_{A,t} \ge 0$ exports spillover $s\_{B,t} = \kappa B\_{A,t}$ ($\kappa > 0$) onto $B$, whose living normativity (Canguilhem 1943/1966) carries static exogenous baseline load $d\_B \ge 0$, spillover weight $\omega\_B > 0$, and tolerance $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B} > 0$. With $\mathrm{clamp}(u, 0, 1) := \min(\max(u, 0), 1)$, downstream reception-perimeter slack at step $t$ and across the trajectory is:

$$\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}^{(B)} = \mathrm{clamp}\\!\left(\frac{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B} - (d\_B + \omega\_B s\_{B,t})}{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}},\mkern3mu 0,\mkern3mu 1\right), \qquad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{(B)} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}^{(B)}.$$

**Two-Archer Inter-Triadic Externalization Theorem.** *Under bilateral static exogenous baseline headroom $d\_B < \tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$, set $B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}} := (\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B} - d\_B)/(\omega\_B \kappa) > 0$. Whenever at any step $t$ either $B\_{A,t} \ge B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}}$ or $d\_B \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$ (in which case $[0, B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}})$ is empty and mandates remedial decompression before any new discharge), cumulative load hits $d\_B + \omega\_B \kappa B\_{A,t} \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$. That load collapses $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}^{(B)} = 0$ and drives $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{(B)} = 0$ while $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}^{(A)} > \tau$. Holding $B\_{A,t} \in [0, B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}})$ across all finite steps $t \in \lbrace 1, \dots, T\rbrace$ under static $d\_B < \tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$ keeps $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{(B)} > 0$.*

Because $A$'s ledger omits $B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}}$ and $B$ also draws $[T\_1 \dots T\_7]\_B$, defensive counter-discharges $B\_{B,t}$, caregiver attrition, or defendant flight raise dynamic load $d\_{B,t} > d\_{B,0}$ (shrinking $B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}}$) and turn unilateral fortification into bilateral escalation.

One might try levying a unilateral solvency reserve on $A$. Under Scanlonian contractualism (Scanlon 1998; Steuwer 2021; [Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md); [Chapter 15](15-probability-as-a-measure.md)), **no liquid reserve buys irreversible physical ruin or breached rights**, demanding ex-ante refusal and **free, prior, and informed consent (`FPIC`)** ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md); [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)), or bounded emergency barriers. Burden of proving reversibility and non-export sits asymmetrically on exporter $A$ (defaulting to irreversible Scanlonian refusal under ambiguity), and economic duress or wage premiums never license `FPIC` waivers breaching non-waivable vital floors $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$. Only for **verified compensable, reversible** exposures with monetizable harm $E\_{\mathrm{harm}} > 0$ does that convention stipulate $B\_{\mathrm{solvency}} = E\_{\mathrm{harm}}\mkern3mu\gamma\_{\mathrm{consent}}\mkern3mu\gamma\_{\mathrm{voice}} \ge E\_{\mathrm{harm}}$ ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{consent}}, \gamma\_{\mathrm{voice}} \ge 1$).

Unilateral solvency pricing fails twice. First, for non-compensable collapse ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{(B)} = 0$), large multipliers $\gamma\_{\mathrm{consent}}, \gamma\_{\mathrm{voice}} \gg 1$ cannot turn money into survival. Second, for compensable harm, $A$ prices $E\_{\mathrm{harm}}$ inside its own archive ($T\_{3,A}$) under $L\_{\mathrm{inst}} \ll L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$: at equal $\gamma \ge 1$, $B\_{\mathrm{solvency}}^{(A)} / B\_{\mathrm{solvency}}^{(B)} = E\_{\mathrm{harm}}^{(A)} / E\_{\mathrm{harm}}^{(B)} \ll 1$ reproduces $T\_1$'s cost–loss asymmetry ($p\_{\mathrm{inst}}^\ast / p\_{\mathrm{affected}}^\ast = L\_{\mathrm{affected}} / L\_{\mathrm{inst}}$ under $0 < C < \eta L\_{\mathrm{inst}} < \eta L\_{\mathrm{affected}}$; [Chapter 86](86-posture-before-bowstring-draws.md); [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)), compounded at $T\_6$ by social burden $B^+(f)$ (Milli et al. 2019; [Chapter 91](91-when-the-target-reads-the-archer.md)).

## Care Externalization, Social Reproduction, and Judicial Bow-Asymmetry

Two-Archer externalization governs everyday care work and courtroom bail whenever institutions balance internal ledgers on unpriced human endurance.

**Care deficits and democratic deficits.** In democratic theory, Parra Jounou and Tronto (2024) emphasize that "the care deficit and the democratic deficit are two sides of the same coin": care cannot be commodified into neglect, demanding mandatory "care impact" evaluation rather than "carewashing". That refutes the myth that $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}^{(A)}$ flourishes independently while paired civic reception perimeters drain away (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{(B)}$).

**Social reproduction and racialized care strain.** Farris (2026) connects the political ethics of care to social reproduction feminism, showing how market paradigms degrade life-sustaining labor into allegedly "unproductive" classed, gendered, and racialized work amid accelerating inequality. Pires and Mundim (2026), reading nursing labor through Nancy Fraser's critique, trace exploitation and sentimental "domestication", citing empirical disparities where Black women in nursing work 26 hours and 8 minutes more than white men and 21 hours and 20 minutes more than white women for identical monthly pay.

Hospital ledger $A$ looks balanced because racialized caregivers in external reception perimeter $B$ absorb uncompensated strain ($s\_{B,t}$) until attrition breaches $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$'s vital normativity (Canguilhem 1943/1966). Distinguishing dimensionless causal variance shares $(v\_{\mathrm{struct}}, v\_{\mathrm{op}}, v\_{\mathrm{exog}})$ behind blame-inversion ratio $b\_{\mathrm{fl}}/v\_{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md) §4) from absolute loss units $(L\_{\mathrm{oper}}, L\_{\mathrm{exog}})$ ([Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) §8.3) prevents shifting structural offloading onto frontline blame.

**Judicial pretrial detention asymmetry.** Asymmetric courtroom targeting exhibits the same two-archer structure ([Chapter 81](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md); [Chapter 86](86-posture-before-bowstring-draws.md); Dobbie, Goldin, & Yang 2016; Kleinberg et al. 2017/2018). Pretrial detention shifts employment, tax, and conviction costs onto defendant community perimeter $B$. Meanwhile, algorithms optimizing court $A$'s score face **selective labels** ($T\_3$ MNAR in jailed outcomes), **omitted payoffs** in perimeter $B$, and Chouldechova's (2017) trilemma ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)). As Valdrighi, Valera, and Raimundo (2026) prove ([Chapter 77](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md); Perdomo et al. 2020, $\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}}/L\_y$ contraction in [Chapter 91](91-when-the-target-reads-the-archer.md)), naive retraining diverges under selective labels; in [Chapter 77](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md)'s selective-label contraction notation (where $\kappa\_{\mathrm{sel}}$ bounds label-selection sensitivity and $L\_G < 1$ is the retraining contraction modulus), even `Robust RRM` holds asymptotic iterates ($\limsup\_{t\to\infty}$) within $\kappa\_{\mathrm{sel}}/(1 - L\_G)$ only while label sensitivity $(L\_x, L\_\theta)$ stays unidentifiable absent worst-case domain bounds or naturally occurring quasi-random institutional assignment ($P(O = 1 \mid X) > 0$, such as random judge assignment in Dobbie, Goldin, & Yang 2016). Active algorithmic randomization over human pretrial liberty is forbidden, and defense or public-defender standing must audit $T\_{3,A}$ selective-label bounds across $T\_{1,A} \leftrightarrow T\_{1,B}$ without immunity shields.

## The Fifth Falsifier ($F\_5$) and Polycentric Governance

The externalization theorem proves logically that $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}^{(A)} > \tau$ can coexist with $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{(B)} = 0$, while empirical refutability rests on the **fifth canonical falsifier ($F\_5$)** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.5.2–9.5.3). Testing $H\_0^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} \ge \delta\_{\min}$ against $H\_1^{\mathrm{fals}}: \Delta\_{\mathrm{harm}} < \delta\_{\min}$ ($\delta\_{\min} > 0$) across coupled multi-unit perimeters requires a declared spillover channel, $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}}$, and $\delta\_{\min}$ sealed under **$B$-side or external co-custody**, paired with a mandatory **bilateral stress-exposure admissibility gate** (requiring high-probability approach to $B\_A^{\mathrm{crit}}$ or realized boundary exceedance so calm-window pilots remain non-informative): $F\_5$ **refutes** Stochology's cross-perimeter claim if perimeter-accounted arms fail to beat exporting arms jointly in paired deficit-detection gain and realized physical spillover reduction across *Not askable*-checked registries ($\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} + \Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) \le d\_{\mathrm{crit}}^{\mathrm{obs}}$ at severity $\ge 1 - \alpha$, reported with selection-widened inversion band $\bar{d}\_{\mathrm{obs}} \pm \bigl(\Delta\_{\mathrm{sel}}(\bar{\rho}) + z\_{1-\alpha}\mathrm{SE}\_{\mathrm{obs}}\bigr)$; [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.7.3).

Preventing cross-perimeter collapse demands **polycentric coupling** across $[T\_1 \dots T\_7]\_A \otimes [T\_1 \dots T\_7]\_B$ (Ostrom 1990; Ostrom 2010), bounded where top-down imposition crowds out cooperation or spatial displacement blinds dispersed monitors (Dietz, Ostrom, & Stern 2003).

**1. Shared boundary archives and framing ($T\_{3,A} \leftrightarrow T\_{3,B}$, $T\_{1,A} \leftrightarrow T\_{1,B}$).** Co-custody of $T\_3$ baseline and selective-label registries beside $T\_1$ denominators stops $A$ from shrinking $E\_{\mathrm{harm}}$ to internal compliance fees; whenever $B$ exercises [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)'s `CARE`/`FPIC` archival sovereignty or Ethical Refusal $R\_1$, non-export is audited asymmetrically from $A$'s own outbound physical discharge meters without coercing community data extraction.

**2. Contractualist refusal and aggregate basin caps ($T\_{6,A} \leftrightarrow T\_{6,B}$).** Irreversible threats trigger Scanlonian ex-ante refusal (Scanlon 1998), while admissible compensable risks carry multipliers $\gamma\_{\mathrm{consent}}, \gamma\_{\mathrm{voice}} \ge 1$ (barring desperation-coerced buyouts below $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$). Across split corporate entities or shared-basin neighbors $A\_1, \dots, A\_m$, independent physical metering of dynamic baseline $d\_{B,t}$ and $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$ bounds aggregate basin load $d\_{B,t} + \sum\_{k=1}^m \omega\_B \kappa\_k B\_{A\_k, t} < \tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$ (with mandatory decompression whenever $d\_{B,0} \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{tol}, B}$). That ceiling preserves $B$'s vital normativity (Canguilhem 1943/1966) without inviting baseline padding or Hansson's (2004) individual-veto *"stalemates"* ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md), equitable risk imposition without paralysis).

**3. Bandwidth-bounded accountability ($T\_{7,A} \leftrightarrow T\_{7,B}$) and Brake 4.** Pairing Bovens's (2007) empowered forum with $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$ algedonic triage inside the cognitive ceiling ([Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), collision at ten bits per second) prevents the `"multiple accountability disorder"` analyzed by Black (2008) and compliance denial-of-service. Sealed logs fold strictly **ex post** into $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ under **Reflexive Brake 4** ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), methodological reflexivity without steering live steps), never driving live steps.
