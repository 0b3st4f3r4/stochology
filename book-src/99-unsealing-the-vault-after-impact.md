## Binary Lethal-Omission Impasses vs. Irreversible-Probe Refusal

Under ordinary partial identification (Manski 2026; Montiel Olea, Qiu, & Stoye 2025), consider bounds $L\_t < C < U\_t$ straddling cost $C > 0$ for an **irreversible** act. Declining the act ($a = 0$) secures **unilateral irreversible-probe refusal** whenever abstention preserves survival headroom ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$) and spares external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$). That rule presupposes waiting itself stays non-lethal ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md); [Chapter 90](90-crosswind-clock-and-vessel.md)).

Under superposed stress, a binary bifurcation opens. Suppose an institution faces a **strictly binary choice** $a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$ with $L\_t < C < U\_t$, where inaction ($a = 0$) drives either the vessel or affected recipients across an absorbing barrier within the step horizon. Calling inaction "neutral caution" then executes Collapse Proposition 2 by omission.

**Safe irreversible-probe refusal.** When bounds straddle $C$, $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ holds, and $a = 0$ preserves internal viability and third-party external reception perimeters (audited ex post via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ inside $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ and $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$), refusing an uncorroborated act protects Epistemic and Vital Margins together.

**Binary lethal-omission impasse.** Suppose instead that both options carry unidentifiable ambiguity *and* $a = 0$ breaches survival or exports lethal harm. Passive abstention is then non-neutral. Escalation passes to Stochology's **standpoint cost–loss weighting** ([Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md), standpoint decision loss thresholds; [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), non-neutral omission under Regime III; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md), inquiry openness to affected-party stakes), logging the forced trade-off rather than feigning caution. Deliberative escalation may still outrun survival time ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} < \tau\_{\mathrm{bureaucratic}}$; [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md), bounding emergency reflexivity fast-paths) or saturate deliberate oversight ([Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), collision at ten bits per second). Whenever it does, pre-committed **Regime III-B (Algedonic Halt)** separates halting unsafe affirmative acts from pre-authorized emergency protective actuation ($a = 1$ default whenever inaction $a = 0$ is verified lethal and deliberative time expires, bounded by [Chapter 97](97-the-hijacked-kairos-and-exception.md)'s Brake 3 sunsets) ahead of incomplete forms.

## Retrospective Interference Matrix $\Lambda\_{ab}$ Inside $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ (Reflexive Brake 4)

Define the first-order **diagnostic interference matrix** $\Lambda\_{ab} := -\partial S\_a / \partial \xi\_b = \partial S\_a / \partial S\_b$ ($\Lambda\_{aa} = 0$) and boundary sensitivities $\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}} := -\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} / \partial \xi\_a = \partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} / \partial S\_a$, evaluated under pre-registered or externally attested sensitivity bounds independent of the audited institution. $$S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max\\!\left(0,\mkern3mu S\_a - \sum\_{b \ne a}\bigl([\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\mkern3mu\Delta \xi\_b + [-\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\mkern3mu(\Delta S\_b)\_+\bigr)\right), \qquad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max\\!\left(0,\mkern3mu \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} - \sum\_a\bigl([\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+\mkern3mu\Delta \xi\_a + [-\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+\mkern3mu(\Delta S\_a)\_+\bigr)\right),$$

yielding the two-component effective co-diagnostic pair without a fourth axis:

$$\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \left(\min\_{a \in \lbrace \mathrm{epi}, \mathrm{vital}, \mathrm{gov}\rbrace} S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}},\mkern5mu \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}}\right).$$

Inspect both symbolic limits. Setting $(\Delta S\_a)\_+ = (\Delta S\_b)\_+ = 0$ recovers the pure co-degrading branch ($[\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\Delta\xi\_b$, $[\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\,+\Delta\xi\_a$); further, in the **laminar Part VI limit**—zero interference ($\Lambda\_{ab} = 0$ for $a \ne b$ and $\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}} = 0$) or vanishing increments ($\Delta \xi\_a = \Delta \xi\_b = (\Delta S\_a)\_+ = (\Delta S\_b)\_+ = 0$)—we get $S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}} = S\_a$ and $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$, recovering $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = (M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}) = \mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ identically.
