## A.1 Filter I (Axis 1 — $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$): Canonical Operator I — Partial-Identification Set & Effective Sample Contraction

$$\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{epi}}(\mathcal{D}\_t; \alpha) = \bigl[\hat{\theta}\_L - c\_{\mathrm{IM}}(\alpha)\hat{\sigma}\_L / \sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}},\ \hat{\theta}\_U + c\_{\mathrm{IM}}(\alpha)\hat{\sigma}\_U / \sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}\bigr], \qquad \frac{1}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})} = \frac{1}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}} + \left(\frac{1 - f}{f}\right)\bar{\rho}^2$$

With $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ defined in item 10, this holds for sample size $n \ge 1$, random-effects design effect $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 1$ ($\rho\_I \in [0, 1]$; see item 10 for the negative-ICC fixed-cluster scope), sampling fraction $f = n/N \in (0, 1]$ (extending $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ continuously at $f = 1$ or $\bar{\rho} = 0$; Meng's $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ requires $f \in (0, 1)$ and $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$), pre-declared data-defect bound $\bar{\rho} \in [0, 1]$, miscoverage level $\alpha \in (0, 1/2)$, and estimated endpoint standard deviations $\hat{\sigma}\_L, \hat{\sigma}\_U > 0$.

1. **Cromwell's Interior Probability Floor (Binary Pair vs. Finite Partition of $m \ge 2$ Atoms):**
   $$p \in [\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}, 1 - \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}] \quad \Bigl(0 < \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}} < \frac{1}{2} \text{ for } (H, \neg H)\Bigr), \qquad p\_j \in \bigl[\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}, 1 - (m - 1)\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}\bigr] \quad \Bigl(0 < \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}} < \frac{1}{m},\ m \ge 2\Bigr)$$

   Domain: prior/event probability $p$, partition size $m \ge 2$ (distinct from item 10's cluster sizes $m\_c$).

2. **Economic Cost–Loss Threshold, Pauker–Kassirer Expected-Regret Threshold & EVPI:**
   $$p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = \min\\!\left(1,\mkern3mu \frac{C}{\eta L}\right), \qquad p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = \frac{C\_{\mathrm{FP}}}{C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}}} = \frac{1}{1 + B\_{\mathrm{net}} / H\_{\mathrm{net}}}, \qquad \mathrm{EVPI}(\pi\_0) = \min\bigl(C, \pi\_0 \eta L\bigr) - \pi\_0 \min\bigl(C, \eta L\bigr) \ge 0$$

   Domain: intervention cost $C > 0$, gross preventable loss $L > 0$, false-positive cost $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} > 0$, false-negative loss $L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$, mitigation effectiveness $\eta \in (0, 1]$, net harm $H\_{\mathrm{net}} = C\_{\mathrm{FP}}$, net benefit $B\_{\mathrm{net}} = L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$, prior probability $\pi\_0 \in [0, 1]$.
   These thresholds are non-interchangeable: **cost–loss** $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$ (clamping at $1$ when $\eta L \le C$) pairs with $\mathrm{EVPI}(\pi\_0)$, whereas **expected-regret** $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 1/(1 + B\_{\mathrm{net}}/H\_{\mathrm{net}})$ (Pauker & Kassirer 1975) compares two error costs and equals $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$ **if and only if** $\eta L > C$ and $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} / L\_{\mathrm{FN}} = C / (\eta L - C)$ ([Chapter 81](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md)). Under the **interface disclosure rule**, every risk display reports both its consequence ratio ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$ vs. $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$) and its commitment regime (reversible $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}} \in (0, 1)$ vs. binary $a \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$).

3. **$e\text{-BH}$ False Discovery Rate Control Under Arbitrary Dependence (Wang & Ramdas 2022):**
   $$k^\ast = \max\left\lbrace k \in \lbrace 1, \dots, K\rbrace : \frac{k E\_{(k)}}{K} \ge \frac{1}{\alpha}\right\rbrace$$

   Domain: hypothesis count $K \ge 1$ (distinct from item 12's payout cap $K$ and item 15's carrying capacity), FDR level $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ (distinct from item 12's Pareto tail exponent), and non-negative *e*-values $E\_1, \dots, E\_K \ge 0$ ordered as $E\_{(1)} \ge \dots \ge E\_{(K)} \ge 0$ ($k^\ast = 0$ if the set is empty) satisfying null validity $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_{0,k}}[E\_k] \le 1$ for every true null $k \in \mathcal{H}\_0$ (or more generally $\sum\_{k \in \mathcal{H}\_0} \mathbb{E}[E\_k] \le K$, guaranteeing $\mathrm{FDR} \le \alpha$ under arbitrary dependence; Wang & Ramdas 2022).

4. **Ville's Test Martingale Inequality (Ville 1939):**
   $$E\_t = \prod\_{i=1}^t L\_i, \qquad \mathbb{P}\_{H\_0}\left(\sup\_{t \ge 1} E\_t \ge \frac{1}{\alpha}\right) \le \alpha$$

   Domain: error level $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, initial wealth $E\_0 = 1$, and adapted non-negative likelihood-ratio / *e*-value increments $L\_i \ge 0$ (distinct from loss $L$ in item 2, Manski lower bounds $L\_d^{\mathrm{M}}$ in item 5, and node loads $L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0)$ in item 26) satisfying $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[L\_i \mid \mathcal{F}\_{i-1}] \le 1$. Under pre-registered $t = 0$ hypothesis-adjustment budgets $K\_{\max} \in \mathbb{N}\_0$, uniform union-bound deflation over $K\_{\max} + 1$ pre-funded variants scales null-valid wealth as $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}(E\_T, K\_{\max}) = E\_T / (K\_{\max} + 1)$ (distinct from excess-content auditing, fresh out-of-sample *e*-processes, and attested counter-inductive reopening $\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$ quarantined under `§A.5`; [Chapter 18](18-philosophy-of-science.md), [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)).

5. **Two-Arm Manski Non-Parametric ATE Bounds & Adaptive Imbens–Manski Confidence Interval:**
   $$L\_d^{\mathrm{M}} = p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d}\mkern3mu\bar{Y}\_{\mathrm{obs}, d} + (1 - p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d})\mkern2mu y\_{\min}, \qquad U\_d^{\mathrm{M}} = p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d}\mkern3mu\bar{Y}\_{\mathrm{obs}, d} + (1 - p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d})\mkern2mu y\_{\max}, \qquad \mathrm{ATE}\_L^{\mathrm{M}} = L\_1^{\mathrm{M}} - U\_0^{\mathrm{M}}, \qquad \mathrm{ATE}\_U^{\mathrm{M}} = U\_1^{\mathrm{M}} - L\_0^{\mathrm{M}}, \qquad \Phi\\!\left(c\_{\mathrm{IM}} + \frac{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}\mkern2mu\hat{\Delta}}{\hat{\sigma}\_{\max}}\right) - \Phi(-c\_{\mathrm{IM}}) = 1 - \alpha$$

   Domain: bounded potential outcomes $Y(1), Y(0) \in [y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ ($y\_{\min} \le y\_{\max}$) under marginal randomized or unconfounded treatment assignment $D \perp (Y(1), Y(0))$ with overlap $0 < \mathbb{P}(D = 1) < 1$ (conditional assignment $D \perp (Y(1), Y(0)) \mid X$ instead integrates over covariate strata under positivity $0 < \mathbb{P}(D = 1 \mid X) < 1$), observation indicator $R \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$ (distinct from $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}$ and reserve $R\_t$), and arm observation rates $p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d} = \mathbb{P}(R = 1 \mid D = d) \in [0, 1]$ for $d \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$ (with $p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d}\bar{Y}\_{\mathrm{obs}, d} := \mathbb{E}[RY \mid D = d] = 0$ at $p\_{\mathrm{obs}, d} = 0$). For the Imbens–Manski interval, estimated identification width is $\hat{\Delta} = \hat{\theta}\_U - \hat{\theta}\_L \ge 0$, $\hat{\sigma}\_L, \hat{\sigma}\_U > 0$ are **estimated endpoint standard deviations** (not standard errors), $\hat{\sigma}\_{\max} = \max(\hat{\sigma}\_L, \hat{\sigma}\_U)$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} > 0$, and $\alpha \in (0, 1/2)$. Adaptive under uniform asymptotic normality and superefficiency regularity conditions for pointwise coverage of $\theta\_0 \in [\theta\_L, \theta\_U]$ (Manski 1990, 2003; Imbens & Manski 2004; pre-tested refinement in Stoye 2009 and never-empty interval $\mathrm{CI}^{\mathrm{MA}}$ in Stoye 2021; [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)), shifting $c\_{\mathrm{IM}}$ with $\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}\mkern2mu\hat{\Delta}/\hat{\sigma}\_{\max}$ (whole-set coverage uses $z\_{1-\alpha/2}$).

6. **Lee (2009) Monotone Attrition Trimming Fraction:**
   $$q = \frac{p\_T - p\_C}{p\_T} \quad (p\_T > p\_C > 0)$$

   Domain: randomized or unconfounded treatment assignment $D \perp \bigl(Y(1), Y(0), R(1), R(0)\bigr)$ with overlap $0 < \mathbb{P}(D = 1) < 1$, potential retention indicators $R(d) \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$ (distinct from $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}$ and reserve $R\_t$) satisfying monotone retention $R(1) \ge R(0)$ almost surely, continuous treated outcome distribution $Y(1)$, and arm observation rates $p\_T := \mathbb{P}(R = 1 \mid D = 1)$ and $p\_C := \mathbb{P}(R = 1 \mid D = 0)$ (distinct from item 18's base rates $p\_A, p\_B$). Under $0 < p\_C < p\_T \le 1$, $q \in (0, 1)$ (reducing to $q = 0$ at $p\_T = p\_C > 0$; distinct from item 26a's Byzantine quorum $q$), yielding sharp bounds on the *always-observed* stratum $\lbrace R(1) = R(0) = 1 \rbrace$.

7. **Peterson (1976) Competing-Risks Fréchet Bounds & Worst-Case Censoring Bounds:**
   $$\max\bigl(0, S\_1(t) + S\_2(t) - 1\bigr) \le S(t) \le \min\bigl(S\_1(t), S\_2(t)\bigr), \qquad 1 - \frac{d + c}{n} \le S\_{\mathrm{cens}}(t) \le 1 - \frac{d}{n}$$

   Domain: marginal survival probabilities $S\_1(t), S\_2(t) \in [0, 1]$ ($t \ge 0$, distinct from Triad coordinates $S\_a$), bounding net survival $S(t)$ and overall survival $S\_{\mathrm{cens}}(t)$ for cohort size $n \ge 1$, observed events $d \ge 0$ (distinct from treatment arm index $d$), and censored count $c \ge 0$ ($d + c \le n$).

8. **Keynes's (1921) Evidential Weight & Cost–Loss Reference Scale:**
   $$V\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})\bigr) = \frac{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) + n\_0} \in [0, 1), \qquad n\_0 := n\_{\mathrm{req}}(\alpha, \beta, p^\ast) = \left(\frac{z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta}}{\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}}}\right)^2 \frac{1 - p^\ast}{p^\ast} > 0, \qquad p^\ast = \frac{C}{\eta L} \in (0, 1)$$
   evaluated at pre-declared data-defect bound $\bar{\rho} \in [0, 1]$ ($\bar{\rho} \ge |\rho\_{R,Y}|$), $f \in (0, 1]$, Type I/II tolerances $\alpha, \beta \in (0, 1)$ ($\alpha + \beta < 1$ so $z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta} > 0$), and relative resolution $\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}} > 0$ satisfying $p^\ast(1 + \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}}) \le 1$, where $p^\ast = C/(\eta L) \in (0, 1)$ is anchored to non-capped affected-party stakes so $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}))$ reports threshold-relative adequacy rather than shifting under liability caps (distinct from stake-free evidential depth in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) §9.2.4, and $V \to 1$ remains blind to *Not askable* strata where $\pi\_i \equiv 0$).

9. **Meng's (2018) Exact Big Data Paradox Identity:**
   $$\bar{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = \rho\_{R,Y} \times \sqrt{\frac{1 - f}{f}} \times \sigma\_Y, \qquad n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\rho\_{R,Y}) = \frac{f}{1 - f}\frac{1}{\rho\_{R,Y}^2}$$

   Domain: finite population size $N \ge 2$, sample count $1 \le n \le N$, sampling fraction $f = n/N \in (0, 1]$ (distinct from item 26a's Byzantine fault count), population standard deviation $\sigma\_Y \ge 0$ (dividing by $N$), and finite-population correlation $\rho\_{R,Y} = \mathrm{Corr}(R, Y) \in [-1, 1]$ between recording indicator $R \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$ (distinct from $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}$ and reserve $R\_t$) and outcome $Y$; $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\rho\_{R,Y})$ requires $f \in (0, 1)$ and $\rho\_{R,Y} \in [-1, 1]\setminus\lbrace 0 \rbrace$ (at $\rho\_{R,Y} = 0$ or census $f = 1$, sampling is unbiased and $\bar{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = 0$).

10. **Kish–Moulton Design Effects (Equal vs. Unequal Clusters & Weighting):**
    $$\tilde{m} = \bar{m}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I, \qquad \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} = 1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(w), \qquad \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - 1, \qquad n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = \frac{n}{\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}}$$

    Domain: $n = \sum\_{c=1}^C m\_c \ge 1$ units in $C \ge 1$ clusters ($\ne$ cost $C$, $C\_i$, $C\_{\mathrm{human}}$) of sizes $m\_1, \dots, m\_C \ge 1$ ($m\_{\max} := \max\_{1 \le c \le C} m\_c$), **population** $\mathrm{CV}^2(m) = \sigma\_m^2 / \bar{m}^2 \ge 0$ ($1/C$ divisor, $\bar{m} = n/C \ge 1$, $\tilde{m} = \sum\_{c=1}^C m\_c^2 / n \ge \bar{m} \ge 1$; $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (m - 1)\rho\_I$ if $m\_c \equiv m$) and $\mathrm{CV}^2(w) = \sigma\_w^2 / \bar{w}^2 \ge 0$ ($1/n$ divisor, $w\_i > 0$, $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} \ge 1$). Under **random-effects clustering**, $\rho\_I \in [0, 1]$ ($\ne \rho\_{R,Y}, \rho, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$) keeps $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} \ge 1$ and $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 1$ ([Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)). Under negative-$\rho\_I$ equicorrelation (PSD $1 + (m\_{\max} - 1)\rho\_I > 0$, $\rho\_I \in [-1, 1]$), $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} \in (0, 1)$ strictly for $m\_{\max} \ge 2$ ($1$ at $m\_{\max} = 1$) and additive $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} \in (0, 1)$ iff $\mathrm{CV}^2(w) < (\tilde{m} - 1)(-\rho\_I)$ (else $\ge 1$). Under weights independent within clusters and of membership, $m\_c$, and random effects $(u\_c, e\_{cj})$ in the large-$C$ limit (or finite cross-product balance $n\sum\_{c=1}^C\sum\_{j \ne k} w\_{cj}w\_{ck}/(\sum\_{c,j}w\_{cj})^2 = \tilde{m} - 1$), exact composition is additive ($1 + (\tilde{m}-1)\rho\_I + \mathrm{CV}^2(w)$), while $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}}\cdot\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$ exceeds it by a non-negative cross-term for $\rho\_I \ge 0$ (reversing for $\rho\_I < 0$). Under shock-independent cluster weights ($w\_{cj} = w\_c$), that product matches exact variance if $m\_c \equiv \bar{m}$, $w\_c$ is large-$C$ independent of $m\_c$, or $\sum\_c m\_c(m\_c - \tilde{m})w\_c^2 = 0$; otherwise exact variance uses $\tilde{m}\_w = \sum\_c m\_c^2 w\_c^2 / \sum\_c m\_c w\_c^2$ in $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}[1 + (\tilde{m}\_w - 1)\rho\_I]$, whereas that product can understate variance, bounded for $\rho\_I \ge 0$ by $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}[1 + (m\_{\max} - 1)\rho\_I]$ or $n\mkern2mu \mathbf{w}^\top \mathbf{\Gamma}\mathbf{w}/(\mathbf{1}^\top \mathbf{w})^2$, and shock-dependent weights require conditional shock-weighted variance.

11. **Chao's (1984, 1987, 1989) & Chao–Colwell (2017) Lower Bounds on the Uncounted Population (Abundance vs. $t$-List Incidence):**
    $$\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao1}} = \frac{f\_1^2}{2 f\_2}, \qquad \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(t) = \frac{t - 1}{t}\mkern3mu\frac{f\_1^2}{2 f\_2}, \qquad \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(2) = \frac{f\_1^2}{4 f\_2} = \frac{(n\_{10} + n\_{01})^2}{4 n\_{11}} \ge \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{LP}}$$

    Domain: in single-stream abundance sampling under repeated detections with individual heterogeneity (Model $M\_h$), $f\_1 \ge 0$ counts singletons (classes detected once) and $f\_2 \ge 0$ counts doubletons (classes detected twice); in $t$-list deduplicated binary incidence ($t \ge 2$), $f\_1 \ge 0$ counts units on exactly one list ($n\_{10} + n\_{01}$ at $t = 2$) and $f\_2 \ge 0$ counts units on exactly two lists ($n\_{11}$ at $t = 2$; both distinct from sampling fraction $f = n/N$ and fault count $f$). The unadjusted ratio formulas require $f\_2 > 0$ ($n\_{11} > 0$, $n\_{10}, n\_{01} \ge 0$); at $f\_2 = 0$, Chao & Colwell's (2017) bias-corrected floors $f\_1(f\_1 - 1)/\bigl(2(f\_2 + 1)\bigr)$ (`Chao1`) and $\frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{f\_1(f\_1 - 1)}{2(f\_2 + 1)}$ (`Chao2`, reducing to $f\_1(f\_1 - 1)/\bigl(4(n\_{11} + 1)\bigr)$ at $t = 2, n\_{11} = 0$) preserve finiteness. Across $t \ge 2$ deduplicated lists under **symmetric binomial list incidence** ($p\_{ij} = p\_i$, Model $M\_h$), $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}] \ge \frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{(\mathbb{E}[f\_1])^2}{2\mathbb{E}[f\_2]}$ (Chao 1989); under homogeneous list-varying coverage ($p\_{ij} = p\_j$, Model $M\_t$), the ratio of expectations $\mathbb{E}[n\_{10}]\mathbb{E}[n\_{01}]/\mathbb{E}[n\_{11}]$ (the population analogue of Lincoln–Petersen $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{LP}}$) matches $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}]$ while AM–GM keeps $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(2) \ge \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{LP}}$, and under $M\_{th}$ ($p\_{i1} \ne p\_{i2}$) or negative dependence neither guarantees a floor. Multi-list linkage bounds only *Not observed* eligible units ($\pi\_i > 0$ on some registry); **Not askable** strata ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$ outside every frame) never enter $f\_1$ or $f\_2$, leaving $\hat{n}\_{00}$ blind while uncounted harm drains onto $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$.

## A.2 Filter II (Axis 2 — $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$): Canonical Operator II — Non-Ergodic Reserve & Survival-Time Barrier

$$dR\_t = \bigl(u\_t - \dot{D}\_t\bigr)dt, \qquad d\ln W\_t = \bigl(r + f\_t(\mu - r) - \frac{1}{2}f\_t^2\sigma^2\bigr)dt + f\_t\sigma\mkern3mu dB\_t^{\mathrm{BM}}$$

Domain: reserve replenishment $u\_t$ and unit drawdown $\dot{D}\_t$ on physical reserve $R\_t$ (distinct from §A.1's selection indicator $R$ and $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}$), wealth $W\_t > 0$ ($\ne W\_q, W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t}$ in item 15, §A.3 $W$), Brownian motion $B\_t^{\mathrm{BM}}$ ($\ne B\_t \ge 0$ in item 15, $B\_{\mathrm{audit}}$), multiplicative drift $\mu$, hurdle rate $r$, finite-variance leverage fraction $f\_t$ (distinct from §A.1's sampling fraction $f$ and §A.6's fault count $f$), and volatility $\sigma > 0$ ($\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}} > 2$, item 14); queuing waiting-time and survival-time barriers follow item 15.

12. **Convexity Gap (Jensen 1906), Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS) & Coherent Expected Shortfall ($\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$):**
    $$\Delta\_J = \mathbb{E}[f(X)] - f(\mathbb{E}[X]), \qquad \mathrm{CRPS}(F, y) = \int\_{-\infty}^{\infty}\bigl(F(t) - \mathbf{1}\lbrace y \le t\rbrace\bigr)^2 dt \stackrel{\mathcal{P}\_1}{=} \mathbb{E}\_F|X - y| - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}\_F|X - X^\prime|, \qquad \mathrm{CVaR}\_p(X) = \frac{1}{1 - p}\int\_p^1 \mathrm{VaR}\_u(X)\mkern3mudu$$

    Domain: convex function $f$ (distinct from observed share $f$, leverage $f\_t$, and fault count) with $\mathbb{E}[|X|], \mathbb{E}[|f(X)|] < \infty$ ($\Delta\_J \ge 0$); in $\mathrm{CRPS}$ (Hersbach 2000; Gneiting & Raftery 2007), the integral form $\int\_{-\infty}^{\infty}(F(t) - \mathbf{1}\lbrace y \le t\rbrace)^2 dt$ converges pointwise for every $y \in \mathbb{R}$ when Pareto tail exponent $\alpha > 1/2$ (diverging identically to $+\infty$ for $\alpha \le 1/2$), whereas for $\alpha \in (1/2, 1]$ we have $\mathrm{CRPS}(F, y) \sim y$ as $y \to \infty$ so the expected score $\mathbb{E}\_{Y \sim F}[\mathrm{CRPS}(F, Y)] = +\infty$ diverges (leaving propriety over $[0, \infty]$ vacuous as $\infty \le \infty$). Finite expected-score comparisons, the kernel difference $\mathbb{E}\_F|X - y| - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}\_F|X - X^\prime|$ ($X, X^\prime \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} F$), and non-vacuous **strict propriety** therefore hold strictly on $\mathcal{P}\_1$ ($\mathbb{E}\_F|X| < \infty$, Pareto $\alpha > 1$, with the kernel difference collapsing to $\infty - \infty$ for $\alpha \le 1$). Under loss convention (quantile $p \in (0, 1)$, $\mathbb{E}[X^+] < \infty$, $\alpha > 1$, even where variance diverges for $1 < \alpha \le 2$), $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ is coherent across continuous and discrete laws (Artzner et al. 1999; Acerbi & Tasche 2002), whereas $\mathrm{VaR}\_p$ violates subadditivity (and under MNAR missingness with observed share $f < 1$ on $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$, sharp upper $\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U} = y\_{\max}$ once $p \ge f$). Contractual payout limits or deductibles $X \wedge K$ (payout cap $K > 0$, distinct from hypothesis count $K$, budget $K\_{\max}$, carrying capacity $K$ in item 15, and Hill order count $k$) truncate recorded payer severity while keeping physical tail intensity intact and exporting unpaid overflow $(X - K)\_+$ onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md), [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md), [Chapter 96](96-arrows-that-bend-the-strata.md)).

13. **Hill's (1975) Tail Index Estimator:**
    $$\hat{\alpha}\_{k,n} = \left(\frac{1}{k}\sum\_{i=1}^k \ln\frac{X\_{(n - i + 1)}}{X\_{(n - k)}}\right)^{-1}$$

    Domain: sample size $n \ge 2$, Hill upper-order tail count $1 \le k < n$ (distinct from shrinkage $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}})$ and coordinate count $k$ in §A.6), $X\_{(n)} > X\_{(n-k)} > 0$, effective tail count $k\_{\mathrm{eff}} = k / \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{tail}} > 2$ ($\mathbb{E}[\hat{\alpha}\_{k,n}] = \frac{k}{k-1}\alpha$ for $k > 1$ under pure Pareto tails with exponent $\alpha > 0$, written $\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}}$ in items 14–15; Hill 1975).

14. **Itô (1951) Time-Average Growth, Kelly (1956) Allocation & Epistemic Shrinkage (motivated by Baker & McHale 2013):**
    $$g\_{\mathrm{time}}(f) = r + f(\mu - r) - \frac{1}{2}f^2\sigma^2, \qquad k\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}\bigr) = \frac{1}{1 + 1 / n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}} \in (0, 1), \qquad f^\ast\_{\mathrm{epistemic}} = k\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}\bigr)\frac{\hat{\mu} - r}{\sigma^2}$$

    Domain: known unit-period return variance $\sigma^2 > 0$ ($\sigma > 0$), $\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}} > 2$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} > 0$, drift $\mu, \hat{\mu} > r$ (hurdle rate $r = r\_{\mathrm{rf}}$, dimensionless epistemic shrinkage $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) \in (0, 1)$ distinct from Hill tail cutoff $k$, and leverage fraction $f^\ast\_{\mathrm{epistemic}}$). Here $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) = (1 + 1/n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}})^{-1}$ relies on the **unit-period estimation-variance hypothesis** $\sigma\_{\hat{\mu}}^2 := \mathrm{Var}(\hat{\mu}) = \sigma^2 / n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$, giving predictive variance $\sigma\_{\mathrm{pred}}^2 = \sigma^2 + \sigma\_{\hat{\mu}}^2 = \sigma^2(1 + 1/n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}})$ (which fails under fixed-span continuous-time sampling where $\mathrm{Var}(\hat{\mu}) = \sigma^2 / T$, and differs from Baker & McHale's 2013 SNR-dependent frequentist shrinkage $\bigl(1 + \frac{\sigma^2}{n(\mu - r)^2}\bigr)^{-1}$); estimating unknown $\hat{\sigma}^2$ requires stronger scale-invariant shrinkage ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.1.2).

15. **Kingman's (1961, 1962) Queue Bound, Simchi-Levi Survival Condition & Schaefer (1954)–Holling (1973) Constant-Harvest Basin:**
    $$\mathbb{E}[W\_q] \le \left(\frac{c\_a^2/\rho^2 + c\_s^2}{2}\right)\frac{\rho}{1 - \rho}\tau\_s, \qquad \mathbb{E}[W\_q] \approx \left(\frac{c\_a^2 + c\_s^2}{2}\right)\frac{\rho}{1 - \rho}\tau\_s, \qquad \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}} := \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}, a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t}) + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}, \qquad \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} = \frac{(1 - c\_{\mathrm{imp}}) B\_t}{\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t), \qquad D(H) = K\sqrt{1 - \frac{4H}{rK}}$$

    Domain: stationary $GI/G/1$ renewal queue with **i.i.d. interarrival times** (mean $1/\lambda > 0$, squared coefficient of variation $0 \le c\_a^2 < \infty$) and **i.i.d. service times** (mean $\tau\_s > 0$, squared coefficient of variation $0 \le c\_s^2 < \infty$) mutually independent, yielding traffic utilization $\rho := \lambda \tau\_s \in (0, 1)$ ($\ne \rho\_{R,Y}, \rho\_I, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}, \rho\_i$), $\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}} > 2$, and $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$ for $\rho \to 1^-$ divergence; operating buffer $B\_t \ge 0$ ($>$ safe interior; $=$ boundary), crisis deficit rate $\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t} \equiv \dot{D}\_{\mathrm{deficit}} > 0$, impairment $c\_{\mathrm{imp}} \in [0, 1)$, testimonial discount $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$ ($\ne \delta\_{\mathrm{band}}, \delta(\mathcal{G})$), and adroitness $a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t} \in [0, 1]$ (finite on $(0, 1]$). Schaefer's (1954) logistic harvest $\dot{X} = rX(1 - X/K) - H = 0$ (growth $r > 0 \ne r\_{\mathrm{rf}}$; capacity $K > 0 \ne$ cap $K$; harvest $0 \le H \le rK/4$) gives basin width $D(H)$ (Holling 1973) with $dD/dH \to -\infty$ as $H \to (rK/4)^-$ ([Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md) §1). Here $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ realizes $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}} = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{diagnosis}} + \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{re-derivation}} + \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{execution}}$ via sequential detection latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}, a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t})$ ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{diagnosis}}$, pre-ceiling quotient scales by $((1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t})^{-1}$; bypass needs $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$, $a\_{\mathrm{skill}, t} \ge a^\star\_{\mathrm{skill}}(w\_{\min})$), institutional and cognitive deliberation $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{re-derivation}}$ on sealed $\mathcal{I}\_t$, including cognitive queueing $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}}/C\_{\mathrm{human}}$), and post-authorization execution $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{execution}}$, scaling with $\mathbb{E}[W\_q]$). Cross-perimeter $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}$ is audited strictly *ex post* in §A.7 rather than inside Operator II. Across zero-rest shifts, cumulative allostatic care wear $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t} = \sum\_{s=0}^{t-1}\psi\_s$ ($t \ge 1$, $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, 0} = 0$, in native strain units, $\ne C\_{\mathrm{human}} \approx 10\text{ bits/s}$; [Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md), [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), [Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)) erodes Axis-2 endurance silently.

15a. **Wirtinger–RCC Delay–Jitter Ceiling and Anderson–Morley One-Hop Spectral Bound:**
    $$\bar{\tau} < \bar{\tau}^{\mathrm{NL\text{-}Q}}\_{\max}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) := \frac{H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}})}{\bar{\eta}\,\Gamma\,\lambda\_{\max}(L\_{\mathcal{G}})}, \qquad H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) := \sqrt{1 - \frac{\mu\_+}{2} + \sqrt{\frac{\mu\_+^2}{4} + (1 - \mu\_+)\frac{2\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}}}{1 + \rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}}}}}, \qquad \lambda\_{\max}(L\_{\mathcal{G}}) \le \max\_{\lbrace i, j\rbrace \in E}(d\_i + d\_j).$$
    Domain: simple undirected coupled graph $\mathcal{G} = (V, E)$ ($E \ne \emptyset$) with Laplacian eigenvalue $\lambda\_{\max}(L\_{\mathcal{G}}) > 0$ and vertex degrees $d\_i, d\_j \ge 0$; uniform global worst-case delay ceiling $0 \le \tau\_e(t) \le \bar{\tau}$ across all edges $e \in E$ ($\bar{\tau} > 0$, distinct from queue service time $\tau\_s$ and recovery latencies); uniform jitter bounds $-\mu\_- \le \dot{\tau}\_e(t) \le \mu\_+ < 1$ ($0 \le \mu\_+ < 1$, $\mu\_- \ge 0$); osmotic conductance sector $[\gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}}, \Gamma]$ ($\Gamma \ge \gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}} > 0$, raw ratio $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}} := \gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}}/\Gamma \in (0, 1]$, distinct from queue utilization $\rho$); and $\sqrt{2}$-baseline numerator $H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) > 0$ defined over **raw** $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$, internally substituting $(\bar{\eta}, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}}) = (1, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}})$ under pure telemetry and $\bigl(1 + \mu\_-,\mkern2mu \frac{1 - \mu\_+}{1 + \mu\_-}\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}\bigr)$ under Doppler distortion (Seuret & Gouaisbaut 2013; Park, Ko, & Jeong 2011). This ceiling lies strictly below the scalar Hopf and augmented Wirtinger limits. For Anderson & Morley's (1985) one-hop edge budget $B\_{ij} := \theta\_{\mathrm{safe}}\, H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\hat{\mu}\_{+,ij}, \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) / (\bar{\eta}\_{ij}\,\Gamma\,\hat{\bar{\tau}}\_{ij}) > 0$ ($\theta\_{\mathrm{safe}} \in (0, 1)$, $\hat{\bar{\tau}}\_{ij} > 0$, $0 \le \hat{\mu}\_{+,ij} < 1$, $\hat{\mu}\_{-,ij} \ge 0$, $\bar{\eta}\_{ij} \in \lbrace 1, 1 + \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}\rbrace$), $H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}$ takes **raw** $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$ (never already-scaled $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff},ij}$; [Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md)) and substitutes $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff},ij} = \frac{1 - \hat{\mu}\_{+,ij}}{1 + \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}}\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$ internally whenever $\bar{\eta}\_{ij} = 1 + \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}$. Crucially, testing $u\_{ij} = (d\_i + d\_j)/B\_{ij} \le 1$ with merely **edge-local** $(\hat{\bar{\tau}}\_{ij}, \hat{\mu}\_{+,ij}, \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij})$ fails the global eigenbound on asymmetric graphs (such as a star-plus-leaf graph placing a long delay on a low-degree leaf edge while hub edges carry short delays); soundness requires evaluating $u\_{ij} = (d\_i + d\_j)/B \le 1$ against a uniform global worst-case budget $B := \theta\_{\mathrm{safe}}\, H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) / (\bar{\eta}\,\Gamma\,\bar{\tau})$ built from uniform global worst-case $(\bar{\tau}, \mu\_+, \mu\_-)$ across $E$ (or equivalently $\max\_{e \in E}(d\_i + d\_j) \le \min\_{e \in E} B\_e$ where $B\_e$ uses global worst-case parameters, ensuring $\lambda\_{\max}(L\_{\mathcal{G}}) \le \max\_e(d\_i + d\_j) \le B$). Under dead-band isolation ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}} \to 0^+$), $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}} \to 0$, and heavy-tailed jumps ($0 < \alpha < 2$ within $\alpha \le 2$) can still breach the basin in one step.

## A.3 Filter III (Axis 3 — $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$): Canonical Operator III — Bitemporal Accountability & Performative Integrity Gate

$$\mathcal{O}\_{\mathrm{gov}}(\pi\_t, \mathcal{I}\_t) = \min\bigl(g\_{\text{ex-ante}, t}(\text{CORP}, \text{ICP}, \text{Perdomo}),\mkern5mu g\_{\mathrm{kairos}, t}(\text{List–Pettit}, \text{Fricker}, \text{Bateson}),\mkern5mu g\_{\text{ex-post}, t}(\text{Sosa}, \text{Holmström}, \text{Vault})\bigr)$$

16. **Isotonic CORP-PAV Brier Score Decomposition (Dimitriadis, Gneiting & Jordan 2021):**
    $$\mathrm{BS}(p) = \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} + \mathrm{UNC}\_0$$

    Domain: $p\_1, \dots, p\_n \in [0, 1]$, outcomes $y\_1, \dots, y\_n \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$ ($n \ge 1$), base rate $\bar{y} = \frac{1}{n}\sum\_{i=1}^n y\_i$, and $\mathrm{BS}(p) = \frac{1}{n}\sum\_{i=1}^n (p\_i - y\_i)^2$. Tie-pooled `PAV` gives unique monotone $\hat{\pi}\_i = \hat{\pi}(p\_i) \in [0, 1]$ with $y - \hat{\pi}$ orthogonal to $\hat{\pi} - \bar{y}\mathbf{1}$, so $\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = \mathrm{BS}(p) - \mathrm{BS}(\hat{\pi}) = \frac{1}{n}\sum\_{i=1}^n (p\_i - \hat{\pi}\_i)^2 + \frac{2}{n}\sum\_{i=1}^n (p\_i - \hat{\pi}\_i)(\hat{\pi}\_i - y\_i) \ge 0$ (cross-term $> 0$ when $\hat{\pi}$ merges violating blocks), $\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum\_{i=1}^n (\hat{\pi}\_i - \bar{y})^2 \ge 0$, and $\mathrm{UNC}\_0 = \bar{y}(1 - \bar{y}) \ge 0$ ([Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md)). Audits subtract clamped block-CV optimism $\hat{b}\_{\mathrm{PAV}}^{\mathrm{CV}} := \max\bigl(0,\mkern2mu \mathrm{BS}\_{\mathrm{OOF}}(\hat{\pi}\_{\mathrm{train}}) - \overline{\mathrm{BS}}\_{\mathrm{in}}(\hat{\pi}\_{\mathrm{train}})\bigr)$ for **cross-validated optimism-adjusted resolution** via $\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}^{\mathrm{debiased}} = \max\bigl(0,\mkern3mu \mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \hat{b}\_{\mathrm{PAV}}^{\mathrm{CV}}\bigr)$, $\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}^{\mathrm{debiased}} := \mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} - \hat{b}\_{\mathrm{PAV}}^{\mathrm{CV}}$, and $\mathrm{BS}\_{\mathrm{CV}}(p) < \mathrm{UNC}\_0$ ([Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md)).

17. **Perdomo et al. (2020) Performative Contraction & Linear-Response Equilibria ($L\_2$ Regularization $\lambda > 0$, $\gamma = 1 + \lambda$, $\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}} = 1 + \lambda$, $L\_y = 1$):**
    $$\kappa\_{\mathrm{RRM}} = \frac{|\varepsilon| L\_y}{\gamma} = \frac{|\varepsilon|}{1 + \lambda} < 1, \qquad \theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \frac{\mu\_0}{1 + \lambda - \varepsilon}, \qquad \theta\_{\mathrm{PO}} = \frac{(1 - \varepsilon)\mu\_0}{(1 - \varepsilon)^2 + \lambda}$$

    Domain: loss $\ell(y; \theta) = \frac{1}{2}(y - \theta)^2 + \frac{\lambda}{2}\theta^2$ with regularization weight $\lambda > 0$ (distinct from queue arrival rate $\lambda$ and §A.7's boundary sensitivity $\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}$), performative conditional mean $\mathbb{E}\_{Y \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)}[Y] = \mu\_0 + \varepsilon\theta$ (baseline mean $\mu\_0 \in \mathbb{R}$, sensitivity $\varepsilon \ne 1 + \lambda$, distinct from §A.1's $\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}, \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}}$), and **$\theta$-invariant conditional variance** $\mathrm{Var}\_{Y \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)}(Y) = \sigma^2 < \infty$ ($\partial\_\theta \mathrm{Var}(Y \mid \theta) = 0$; [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md) specializes to Gaussian $Y \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu\_0 + \varepsilon\theta, \sigma^2)$). Strong convexity is $\gamma = 1 + \lambda$ and Perdomo et al.'s (2020, Assumption A1) joint $\ell\_1$-product gradient Lipschitz constant is $\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}} = \max(1 + \lambda, 1) = 1 + \lambda$ (sufficient bound $|\varepsilon| < \gamma / \beta\_{\mathrm{joint}} = 1$). Tracking mixed sample-gradient sensitivity $L\_y = |\partial\_y \partial\_\theta \ell| = 1$ yields the exact linear RRM modulus $\kappa\_{\mathrm{RRM}} = |\varepsilon| L\_y / \gamma = |\varepsilon|/(1 + \lambda) < 1$ (convergent iff $|\varepsilon| < 1 + \lambda$).

18. **Chouldechova's (2017) Algebraic Fairness Trilemma:**

    Domain: group base rates $p\_A, p\_B \in (0, 1)$ (distinct from item 6's retention rates and item 12's shortfall quantile $p$).

## A.4 Leontief Structural Complementarity of the Nuclear Triad

21. **Leontief Structural Complementarity:**
    $$M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \in [0, 1]$$
    where on a sequential trajectory of horizon $T$ with pre-absorption step set $\mathcal{T}\_{\mathrm{pre}} := \lbrace 1 \le t \le T : \neg\mathrm{absorbed}\_t \rbrace$:
    $$S\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} S\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}\bigl(\mathrm{Cov}\_T, V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}), \Delta\_{\mathrm{id}, t}\bigr), \quad S\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \inf\_{t \in \mathcal{T}\_{\mathrm{pre}}} \min\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{tail}, t}, S\_{\mathrm{time}, t}, S\_{\mathrm{surv}, t}\bigr), \quad S\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \min\bigl(g\_{\text{ex-ante}, t}, g\_{\mathrm{kairos}, t}, g\_{\text{ex-post}, t}\bigr)$$
    When a trajectory enters the absorbing ruin barrier ($\mathrm{absorbed}\_t$ holds at some step $t \le T$), $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ folds strictly over pre-absorption steps $\mathcal{T}\_{\mathrm{pre}}$ (whenever $\mathcal{T}\_{\mathrm{pre}} \ne \emptyset$), while crossing into the absorbing ruin barrier itself is captured by the separate **structural ruin indicator** (reporting absorption status, earliest ruin step, and terminal reserve). Consequently, $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$'s binding-axis attribution never fabricates a vital reading from post-absorption absorbed states (comparing the non-absorbed coordinates $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ and $S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ after absorption, and folding all three coordinates over $\mathcal{T}\_{\mathrm{pre}} = \lbrace 1, \dots, T \rbrace$ when unabsorbed). The minimum is not a weighted sum and carries no exchange rate: an intact margin cannot purchase an exhausted one, and the trajectory fold is an infimum rather than an average because an exhausted step is unredeemed by later surplus. A site margin $m\_j$ and threshold $\tau\_j$ share one ordinal scale per site (Stevens 1946; Krantz, Luce, Suppes & Tversky 1971), leaving axis folds $s\_a = \min\_{j \in a} m\_j$ incommensurable across axes until calibrated to $S\_a = \varphi\_a(s\_a) \in [0, 1]$ on one shared scale under **ordinal level comparability ($\mathrm{OLC}$)** (Sen 1985). Only common strictly increasing maps preserve cross-axis ordering; independently recoding each axis via top-anchored $C^1$ strictly increasing $\psi\_a : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($\psi\_a' > 0$, $\psi\_a(0) \ge 0$, $\psi\_a(1) = 1$) maps $\lbrace{}S : S\_a \ge \tau\ \forall a\rbrace$ to $\lbrace{}S' : S'\_a \ge \psi\_a(\tau)\rbrace$ with diagonal Jacobian ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 89](89-curvature-of-the-flight-path.md)):

$$J\_\psi = \operatorname{diag}\bigl(\psi\_{\mathrm{epi}}'(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}),\mkern5mu \psi\_{\mathrm{vital}}'(S\_{\mathrm{vital}}),\mkern5mu \psi\_{\mathrm{gov}}'(S\_{\mathrm{gov}})\bigr), \qquad \det J\_\psi = \prod\_{a} \psi\_a'(S\_a) > 0, \qquad \mathrm{Vol}(\tau; \psi) = \prod\_a \bigl(1 - \psi\_a(\tau)\bigr) = \int\_{[\tau, 1]^3} \lvert \det J\_\psi \rvert \mkern3mu dS.$$

On the top-anchored **restricted affine monoid** $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ ($0 < \alpha\_a \le 1$, identity $\alpha\_a = 1$, non-group since inverses exit $[0, 1]$ at $0$ when $\alpha\_a < 1$; distinct from significance level $\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}}$ and Pareto tail exponent), the constant Jacobian $\det J\_\psi = \prod\_a \alpha\_a$ scales pushforward box volume while preserving pushforward box-volume ratios $\mathrm{Vol}(\tau\_1; \psi)/\mathrm{Vol}(\tau\_2; \psi) = \bigl((1 - \tau\_1)/(1 - \tau\_2)\bigr)^3$ for **arbitrary distinct** $(\alpha\_{\mathrm{epi}}, \alpha\_{\mathrm{vital}}, \alpha\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \in (0, 1]^3$ (whereas new-chart ratios $V\_{\min}(\psi(\tau))/V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\psi(\tau))$ vary under $\psi$). By contrast, preserving cross-axis $\arg\min\_a S\_a$ under Sen's (1985) $\mathrm{OLC}$ requires a **common** slope $\alpha\_a \equiv \bar{\alpha}\_{\mathrm{aff}}$, and matching the unscaled cube volume $V\_{\min}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3$ forces $\alpha\_a \equiv 1$, confirming Lebesgue box volume as a cardinal chart-conditional measure rather than a Stevens (1946) ordinal invariant.

## A.5 The Grammar of Aim — Syntax Layer and Acceptance Predicate

$$\mathcal{S} = (\sigma\_1, \dots, \sigma\_N), \quad \sigma\_t = (\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t), \quad \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{S}; \boldsymbol{\tau}) \in \lbrace\mathrm{Interior}, \mathrm{Boundary}, \mathrm{Collapse}, \mathrm{Unparsable}\rbrace$$

22. **Acceptance Predicate & Fixed Five-Stage Order:**
    Over well-typed sentences $\mathcal{S} = (\sigma\_1, \dots, \sigma\_N)$ ($N \ge 1$; empty sentences quarantine as `Unparsable` (`Skeleton`)) of step tuples $\sigma\_t = (\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t)$ partitioned $3/2/2$ across $\lbrace{}T\_1, T\_2, T\_3\rbrace$, $\lbrace{}T\_4, T\_5\rbrace$, $\lbrace{}T\_6, T\_7\rbrace$ with declared threshold pair $\boldsymbol{\tau} = (\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}, \delta\_{\mathrm{band}})$ ($\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} > 0$, frontier band width distinct from §A.3's $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$ and §A.6's $\delta(\mathcal{G})$), any static cross-axis `(proposition, site)` type mismatch (where a declared collapse proposition and site belong to different triad axes) is refused pre-stage prior to predicate evaluation. On every well-typed step $\sigma\_t$, the single-step predicate $\mathcal{A}(\sigma\_t; \boldsymbol{\tau})$ preserves the four-verdict codomain $\lbrace\mathrm{Interior}, \mathrm{Boundary}, \mathrm{Collapse}, \mathrm{Unparsable}\rbrace$ and runs sequentially through the five non-commuting stages specified in [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate): (i) $\mathrm{LL}(1)$ skeleton validity (with missing tape, inverted bounds $L > U$, $C\_{\mathrm{cap}} \le 0$, $R\_{\mathrm{res}} < 0$, or non-positive band width $\delta\_{\mathrm{band}} \le 0$ quarantined as `Skeleton`, derivation overruns as `BudgetExceeded`, and attested $\texttt{reopen}$ quarantined as `CounterInductiveReopen`), (ii) full $3/2/2$ axial coverage (`AxialCoverage`), (iii) margin finiteness (`MarginMissing`), (iv) axis fold $s\_a = \min\_{j \in a} m\_j$ ($a \in \lbrace\mathrm{epi}, \mathrm{vital}, \mathrm{gov}\rbrace$) and Leontief step fold $m\_\ast = \min\_a \min\_{j \in a} m\_j$ (distinct from retrospective trajectory $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$: $m\_\ast < \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} \implies \mathrm{Collapse}$, or `Unparsable` (`Skeleton`) if declared `(proposition, site, step)` metadata is missing or names a site strictly above $m\_\ast$), and (v) frontier band ($\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} \le m\_\ast < \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}} \implies \mathrm{Boundary}$, or `Unparsable` (`Skeleton`) if the declared site is missing or misses $m\_\ast$; else $\mathrm{Interior}$). Across $N$-step trajectories $\mathcal{S} = (\sigma\_1, \dots, \sigma\_N)$ without reading $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$, sentence acceptance executes two passes matching [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate): Pass one checks Stages (i)–(iii) across all steps $\sigma\_1, \dots, \sigma\_N$, returning the earliest `Unparsable` ahead of any Stage (iv) `Collapse`; Pass two then applies Stages (iv)–(v) step by step from each step's own sealed state, stopping at the earliest Stage (iv)/(v) `Unparsable` (`Skeleton`) or `Collapse`, else returning `Boundary` with the step-minimal near-critical margin $\min\_{1 \le t \le N} m\_{\ast,t} \in [\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}, \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}})$ and the attaining site of the earliest minimizing step if at least one step enters the band, or `Interior` only when every step satisfies $m\_{\ast,t} \ge \tau\_{\mathrm{acc}} + \delta\_{\mathrm{band}}$. Upstream of step execution, the *ex-ante* reflexive self-audit vector $\mathcal{R}\_t = (\mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}, \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}, \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{gov}, t})$ ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) §5) grounds the declared tape without turning $\mathcal{A}$ into a post-trajectory diagnosis.

23. **Non-Commutation Property of Aim:**
    $$\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{m}; \boldsymbol{\tau}) = \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{w}^\prime, \mathbf{m}; \boldsymbol{\tau}) \quad \forall \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{w}^\prime.$$
    The acceptance predicate is invariant to the contingency tape $\mathbf{w}\_t$ (*skopós*), and its five-stage order `(i)–(v)` does not commute under stage permutation ([Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)). No accidental external success can redeem depleted guard margins $\mathbf{m}\_t$ or an uncalibrated control tape $\mathbf{u}\_t$ (*télos*), realizing Antipater's separation of aim from external outcome.

## A.6 The Multilayer Knowledge Graph ($\mathcal{G}\_1 \leftrightarrow \mathcal{G}\_2$)

$$\binom{7}{3} = 12 + 9 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 35, \qquad \mathcal{H}(L\_i) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_j)$$

24. **Vandermonde Partition of Triadic Exposure Regimes:**

    - **Trans-Triadic Keystone ($k\_1=1, k\_2=1, k\_3=1$):** $\binom{3}{1}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{1} = 3 \times 2 \times 2 = 12$ orders;
    - **Governance Axial Exposure ($k\_3=0, k\_1 \ge 1, k\_2 \ge 1$):** $\binom{3}{2}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{0} + \binom{3}{1}\binom{2}{2}\binom{2}{0} = 6 + 3 = 9$ orders;
    - **Vital Axial Exposure ($k\_2=0, k\_1 \ge 1, k\_3 \ge 1$):** $\binom{3}{2}\binom{2}{0}\binom{2}{1} + \binom{3}{1}\binom{2}{0}\binom{2}{2} = 6 + 3 = 9$ orders;
    - **Epistemic Axial Exposure ($k\_1=0$):** $\binom{3}{0}\binom{2}{2}\binom{2}{1} + \binom{3}{0}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{2} = 2 + 2 = 4$ orders;
    - **Pure Epistemic Concentration ($k\_1=3$):** $\binom{3}{3}\binom{2}{0}\binom{2}{0} = 1$ order.

25. **DAG Ancestor Cone Leontief Homomorphism:**
    $$\mathcal{H}(L\_i) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_j) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} \min\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}(L\_j), S\_{\mathrm{vital}}(L\_j), S\_{\mathrm{gov}}(L\_j)\bigr).$$
    **Homomorphism Monotonicity:** If any basal order in the dependency cone collapses ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_{\mathrm{basal}}) = 0$), then $\mathcal{H}(L\_i) = 0$ for every downstream dependent discipline. This transfer is a model reading of the dependency graph rather than an observed cumulative margin: higher-order social and legal institutions cannot stand when foundational physical or epidemiological margins fail.

    Because $\min$ is concave, estimating $k \ge 2$ coordinate margins via integrable unbiased estimators $\hat{\theta}\_m$ ($\mathbb{E}[|\hat{\theta}\_m|] < \infty$, $\mathbb{E}[\hat{\theta}\_m] = \theta\_m$; distinct from Hill tail cutoff $k$) induces downward estimation bias $b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) \le 0$ under sampling overlap around $m^\ast \in \arg\min\_m \theta\_m$:

    $$b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) := \mathbb{E}\\!\left[\min\_{1 \le m \le k} \hat{\theta}\_m\right] - \min\_{1 \le m \le k} \theta\_m = -\mkern3mu\mathbb{E}\\!\left[\max\_{m \ne m^\ast} \bigl(\hat{\theta}\_{m^\ast} - \hat{\theta}\_m\bigr)\_+\right] \le 0.$$

    Separating estimation uncertainty from physical breach uses either a joint $(1 - \alpha)$ confidence rectangle $\prod\_{m=1}^k [\hat{\theta}\_{m,L}, \hat{\theta}\_{m,U}]$ inducing a simultaneous minimum band, or an $\mathrm{IUT}$ over marginal level-$\alpha$ rejection regions $\lbrace T\_m > c\_{\alpha, m} \rbrace$ satisfying $\sup\_{\theta \in \Theta\_{0,m}} \mathbb{P}\_\theta(T\_m > c\_{\alpha, m}) \le \alpha$ for $\Theta\_{0,m} = \lbrace \theta : \theta\_m \le \tau \rbrace$, testing $H\_0: \bigcup\_m \lbrace \theta\_m \le \tau \rbrace$ vs. $H\_1: \bigcap\_m \lbrace \theta\_m > \tau \rbrace$ at unadjusted marginal level $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ under arbitrary dependence (with viability threshold $\tau$, distinct from latencies and $\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}$; [Chapter 92](92-sealed-bow-and-uncommuted-verdict.md)):

    $$\min\_{1 \le m \le k} \hat{\theta}\_{m,L} \le \min\_{1 \le m \le k} \theta\_m \le \min\_{1 \le m \le k} \hat{\theta}\_{m,U}, \qquad \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{IUT}} = \bigcap\_{m=1}^k \lbrace T\_m > c\_{\alpha, m}\rbrace.$$
26. **Motter–Lai (2002) Cascade Breakdown Condition:**
    $$C\_i = (1 + \alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}, i}) L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0), \qquad \alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}, i} = \frac{1 - \rho\_i}{\rho\_i} \ge 0, \qquad \mathcal{F}(t + 1) = \mathcal{F}(t) \cup \bigl\lbrace{}j \notin \mathcal{F}(t) : L\_j\bigl(\mathcal{G}\_0 \setminus \mathcal{F}(t)\bigr) > C\_j\bigr\rbrace.$$
    Domain: initial node load / shortest-path betweenness $L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0) > 0$ (distinct from loss $L$, likelihood factors $L\_i$, and Manski bounds $L\_d^{\mathrm{M}}$), node capacity $C\_i > 0$ (distinct from cost $C$ and $C\_{\mathrm{human}}$), node load factor $\rho\_i = L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0)/C\_i \in (0, 1]$ (distinct from queue utilization $\rho$, $\rho\_{R,Y}$, $\rho\_I$, and $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$), and load tolerance $\alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}, i} \ge 0$ (distinct from test level $\alpha$, Pareto exponent, and affine slope $\alpha\_a$; reducing to Motter & Lai's uniform $\alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}} = (1 - \rho)/\rho \ge 0$ when $\rho\_i \equiv \rho$). Here $L\_j(\mathcal{G}\_0 \setminus \mathcal{F}(t))$ recomputes **global shortest-path betweenness** after removing failed **nodes** $\mathcal{F}(t)$ (Motter & Lai 2002), distinct from local equal split across $z \ge 1$ neighbours ($L\_0 + L\_0/z > C$ once uniform $\rho = L\_0 / C \in (0, 1)$ exceeds $z/(z + 1)$, or $\alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}} < 1/z$). Below critical $\alpha\_c$, high-load node removal cascades.

26a. **One-Hop Byzantine Admissibility Band and Hybrid RFDE–CRDT Tracking Bound:**
    $$q\_{\mathrm{fraud}} \in \bigl[f + 1,\; \delta(\mathcal{G}) - f - 1\bigr], \qquad q\_{\mathrm{rehab}} \in \bigl[q\_{\mathrm{fraud}} + 1,\; \delta(\mathcal{G}) - f\bigr] \quad \bigl(\delta(\mathcal{G}) \ge 2f + 2,\; \kappa\_v(\mathcal{G}) \ge f + 1,\; n \ge 2f + 3\bigr), \qquad |\widehat{M}\_i(t) - M\_{\mathrm{Common}}(t)| \le D\_{\max}\sup\_{\xi \in [t - D\_{\max}, t]} \|\dot{s}(\xi)\|\_\infty \quad (t \ge T\_0 + D\_{\max}).$$
    Domain: up to $f \ge 1$ Byzantine units (fault count $f$, distinct from §A.1's sampling fraction and §A.2's leverage) on neighborhood graph $\mathcal{G}$ of $n \ge 2f + 3$ vertices (node count $n$, distinct from sample size $n$) with minimum vertex degree $\delta(\mathcal{G}) \ge 2f + 2$ (distinct from $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}$ and $\delta\_{\mathrm{band}}$) and Menger vertex connectivity $\kappa\_v(\mathcal{G}) \ge f + 1$ (sparse extension via $f$-local/$r$-robustness and peer rewiring in LeBlanc et al. 2013 and Leitão, Pereira, & Rodrigues 2007). Here **witness quorum $q$** (distinct from Lee's trimming fraction $q$) denotes the threshold count of distinct active non-self neighbor attestations: for fraud quarantine, an actually Byzantine node occupies one of the $f$ fault slots and retains at least $\delta(\mathcal{G}) - f + 1$ honest neighbors, yielding standalone quarantine ceiling $q\_{\mathrm{fraud}} \in [f + 1,\; \delta(\mathcal{G}) - f + 1]$ (unexiled closed-neighborhood agreement allows $\delta(\mathcal{G}) \ge 2f + 1$, $n \ge 2f + 2$), tightened to $q\_{\mathrm{fraud}} \in [f + 1,\; \delta(\mathcal{G}) - f - 1]$ when active non-self rehabilitation runs; for rehabilitation across generations $g \in \mathbb{Z}\_{\ge 1}$ with clock-free exponential backoff $T\_{\mathrm{vigil}}(g) = T\_{\mathrm{base}}\,2^{g-1}$ ($T\_{\mathrm{base}} > 0$, checked against verifiable elapsed ticks) and conductance caps $0 < \theta\_{\mathrm{canary}} < \theta\_{\mathrm{vigil}} < 1$, an exiled honest node has **zero probationary self-voting and zero probationary witness weight** and may face $f$ Byzantine neighbors, leaving at least $\delta(\mathcal{G}) - f$ active honest neighbors and requiring $\delta(\mathcal{G}) \ge 2f + 2$, $n \ge 2f + 3$ so asymmetric quorum $q\_{\mathrm{rehab}} \in [q\_{\mathrm{fraud}} + 1,\; \delta(\mathcal{G}) - f]$ never deadlocks, while restricting votes to active non-self peers and re-evaluating quorums upon join-semilattice merges (Shapiro et al. 2011; [Chapter 83](83-layer-distributed-consensus-sre-order.md)). For the tracking bound over propagation horizon $D\_{\max} > 0$ and post-partition stabilization origin $T\_0 \ge 0$, assume the reserve trajectory $s(\xi)$ is continuous and piecewise differentiable on $[T\_0, t]$ without unlogged discontinuous resets, the common aggregate $M\_{\mathrm{Common}}(t) = M(s(t))$ is **$1$-Lipschitz with respect to $\|\cdot\|\_\infty$** ($|M(x) - M(y)| \le \|x - y\|\_\infty$, e.g. coordinate minimum), and unit $i$'s local estimate $\widehat{M}\_i(t) = M(\hat{s}\_i(t))$ evaluates delayed component readings $\hat{s}\_{i,k}(t) = s\_k(t - d\_{ik}(t))$ subject to bounded inter-node delays $0 \le d\_{ik}(t) \le D\_{\max}$.

## A.7 Reception-Side Margin — The Co-Diagnostic Pair, Not a Fourth Axis

$$\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}},\mkern5mu \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr), \qquad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}, \qquad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t} = \mathrm{clamp}\Bigl(\frac{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}} - (d\_t + \omega s\_t)}{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}}}, 0, 1\Bigr)$$

$$\xi\_b := 1 - S\_b \in [0, 1], \qquad \Lambda\_{ab} := -\frac{\partial S\_a}{\partial \xi\_b} = \frac{\partial S\_a}{\partial S\_b}\ (\Lambda\_{aa} = 0), \qquad \lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}} := -\frac{\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}}{\partial \xi\_a} = \frac{\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}}{\partial S\_a}$$

$$S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max\Bigl(0,\, S\_a - \sum\_{b \ne a}\bigl([\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\mkern3mu\Delta\xi\_b + [-\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\mkern3mu(\Delta S\_b)\_+\bigr)\Bigr), \quad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max\Bigl(0,\, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} - \sum\_a\bigl([\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+\mkern3mu\Delta\xi\_a + [-\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+\mkern3mu(\Delta S\_a)\_+\bigr)\Bigr), \quad \mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \bigl(\min\_a S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}},\, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}}\bigr)$$

Domain: normalized tolerances of **external reception perimeters** (distinct from internal Canguilhem–Holling survival basins) define the *ex-post* boundary fold $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T}\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}$, governed by reception tolerance $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}} > 0$ (distinct from $\tau\_{\mathrm{acc}}$ and latencies), perimeter drift $d\_t \ge 0$ (distinct from event count $d$ and vertex degree $d\_i$), shock intensity $s\_t \ge 0$ (distinct from §A.4's axis fold $s\_a$ and §A.6's reserve trajectory $s(\xi)$), and shock weight $\omega > 0$ (neither margin is a probability, nor a fourth triadic axis, preserving $P = 3$ planes, seven sites, and $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ orders). Here attested *ex-post* co-degrading deficit increments satisfy $\Delta \xi\_a, \Delta \xi\_b \ge 0$ (with $[u]\_+ := \max(0, u)$) and antagonistic over-pressing increments satisfy $(\Delta S\_a)\_+, (\Delta S\_b)\_+ \ge 0$: setting $(\Delta S\_a)\_+ = (\Delta S\_b)\_+ = 0$ recovers the pure co-degrading effective fold of [Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md) ($[\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\Delta\xi\_b$ and $[\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+\Delta\xi\_a$), whereas antagonistic over-pressing ($+\Delta S\_b > 0$) activates the duals $[-\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+ = [-\partial S\_a/\partial S\_b]\_+ > 0$ and $[-\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+ = [-\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}/\partial S\_a]\_+ > 0$ (recovering $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ identically when $\Lambda\_{ab} = \lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}} = 0$ or all increments vanish). As a first-order tangent, this expansion fails at $\min$ corners, under heavy-tailed jumps ($0 < \alpha < 2$ within Pareto $\alpha \le 2$), and under superadditive cycles ([Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)). The minimum is not a weighted sum, and **Reflexive Brake 4** keeps both $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ and $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ strictly *ex post*, driving no live step.
