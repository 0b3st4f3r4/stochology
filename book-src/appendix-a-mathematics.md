## A.1 Filter I (Axis 1 — $S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$): Canonical Operator I — Partial-Identification Set & Effective Sample Contraction

$$\mathcal{I}\_{\mathrm{epi}}(\mathcal{D}\_t; \alpha) = \bigl[\hat{\theta}\_L - c\_{\mathrm{IM}}(\alpha)\hat{\sigma}\_L / \sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}},\ \hat{\theta}\_U + c\_{\mathrm{IM}}(\alpha)\hat{\sigma}\_U / \sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}\bigr], \qquad \frac{1}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})} = \frac{1}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}} + \left(\frac{1 - f}{f}\right)\bar{\rho}^2$$

With $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ defined in item 10, this holds for $n \ge 1$, $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 1$, $f=n/N \in (0,1]$ (extending $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) = n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$ continuously at $f = 1$ or $\bar{\rho} = 0$; Meng's $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ requires $f \in (0,1)$ and $\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$), $\bar{\rho} \in [0,1]$, $\alpha \in (0, 1/2)$, and $\hat{\sigma}\_L,\hat{\sigma}\_U > 0$.

1. **Cromwell's Interior Probability Floor (Binary Pair vs. Finite Partition of $m \ge 2$ Atoms):**
   $$p \in [\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}, 1 - \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}] \quad \Bigl(0 < \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}} < \frac{1}{2} \text{ for } (H, \neg H)\Bigr), \qquad p\_j \in \bigl[\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}, 1 - (m - 1)\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}}\bigr] \quad \Bigl(0 < \varepsilon\_{\mathrm{floor}} < \frac{1}{m},\ m \ge 2\Bigr)$$

2. **Economic Cost–Loss Threshold, Pauker–Kassirer Expected-Regret Threshold & EVPI:**
   $$p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}} = \min\\!\left(1,\mkern3mu \frac{C}{\eta L}\right), \qquad p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = \frac{C\_{\mathrm{FP}}}{C\_{\mathrm{FP}} + L\_{\mathrm{FN}}} = \frac{1}{1 + B\_{\mathrm{net}} / H\_{\mathrm{net}}}, \qquad \mathrm{EVPI}(\pi\_0) = \min\bigl(C, \pi\_0 \eta L\bigr) - \pi\_0 \min\bigl(C, \eta L\bigr) \ge 0$$

   Domain: $C,L,C\_{\mathrm{FP}},L\_{\mathrm{FN}} > 0$, $\eta \in (0,1]$, $H\_{\mathrm{net}} = C\_{\mathrm{FP}}$, $B\_{\mathrm{net}} = L\_{\mathrm{FN}}$, $\pi\_0 \in [0,1]$.
   These thresholds are non-interchangeable: **cost–loss** $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$ (clamping at $1$ when $\eta L \le C$) pairs with $\mathrm{EVPI}(\pi\_0)$, whereas **expected-regret** $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}} = 1/(1 + B\_{\mathrm{net}}/H\_{\mathrm{net}})$ (Pauker & Kassirer 1975) compares two error costs and equals $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$ only when $C\_{\mathrm{FP}} = C$ and $L\_{\mathrm{FN}} = \eta L - C > 0$. Under the **interface disclosure rule**, every risk display reports both its consequence ratio ($p^\ast\_{\mathrm{CL}}$ vs. $p^\ast\_{\mathrm{PK}}$) and its commitment regime (reversible $a^\ast\_{\mathrm{MMR}} \in (0,1)$ vs. binary $a \in \lbrace 0, 1\rbrace$).

3. **$e\text{-BH}$ False Discovery Rate Control Under Arbitrary Dependence (Wang & Ramdas 2022):**
   $$k^\ast = \max\left\lbrace k \in \lbrace 1, \dots, K\rbrace : \frac{k E\_{(k)}}{K} \ge \frac{1}{\alpha}\right\rbrace$$

   Domain: $K \ge 1$, $\alpha \in (0,1)$, $E\_{(1)} \ge \dots \ge E\_{(K)} \ge 0$ ($k^\ast=0$ if empty).

4. **Ville's Test Martingale Inequality (Ville 1939):**
   $$E\_t = \prod\_{i=1}^t L\_i, \qquad \mathbb{P}\_{H\_0}\left(\sup\_{t \ge 1} E\_t \ge \frac{1}{\alpha}\right) \le \alpha$$

   Domain: $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, $E\_0 = 1$, and adapted $L\_i \ge 0$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[L\_i \mid \mathcal{F}\_{i-1}] \le 1$. Under pre-registered operational hypothesis-adjustment budgets $K\_{\max} \in \mathbb{N}\_0$, *ad hoc* Lakatosian deflation scales null-valid wealth as $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}(E\_T, K\_{\max}) = E\_T / (K\_{\max} + 1)$ (distinct from attested counter-inductive reopening $\mathcal{F}\_t \subsetneq \mathcal{F}\_{t+1}^\ast$ quarantined under `§A.5`; [Chapter 18](18-philosophy-of-science.md), [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)).

5. **Two-Arm Manski Non-Parametric ATE Bounds & Adaptive Imbens–Manski Confidence Interval:**
   $$\mathrm{ATE}\_L^{\mathrm{M}} = L\_1^{\mathrm{M}} - U\_0^{\mathrm{M}}, \qquad \mathrm{ATE}\_U^{\mathrm{M}} = U\_1^{\mathrm{M}} - L\_0^{\mathrm{M}}, \qquad \Phi\\!\left(c\_{\mathrm{IM}} + \frac{\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}\mkern2mu\hat{\Delta}}{\hat{\sigma}\_{\max}}\right) - \Phi(-c\_{\mathrm{IM}}) = 1 - \alpha$$

   Domain: $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$ ($y\_{\min} \le y\_{\max}$), width $\hat{\Delta} = \hat{\theta}\_U - \hat{\theta}\_L \ge 0$, $\hat{\sigma}\_L, \hat{\sigma}\_U > 0$, $\hat{\sigma}\_{\max} = \max(\hat{\sigma}\_L, \hat{\sigma}\_U)$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} > 0$, $\alpha \in (0, 1/2)$. Adaptive for pointwise coverage of $\theta\_0 \in [\theta\_L, \theta\_U]$ (Manski 1990, 2003; Imbens & Manski 2004; pre-tested refinement in Stoye 2009 and never-empty interval $\mathrm{CI}^{\mathrm{MA}}$ in Stoye 2021; [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)), shifting $c\_{\mathrm{IM}}$ with $\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}\mkern2mu\hat{\Delta}/\hat{\sigma}\_{\max}$ (whole-set coverage uses $z\_{1-\alpha/2}$).

6. **Lee (2009) Monotone Attrition Trimming Fraction:**
   $$q = \frac{p\_T - p\_C}{p\_T} \quad (p\_T > p\_C > 0)$$

   Domain: under monotone retention ($R(1) \ge R(0)$) and $0 < p\_C < p\_T \le 1$, $q \in (0, 1)$ (reducing to $q = 0$ at $p\_T = p\_C > 0$).

7. **Peterson (1976) Competing-Risks Fréchet Bounds & Worst-Case Censoring Bounds:**
   $$\max\bigl(0, S\_1(t) + S\_2(t) - 1\bigr) \le S(t) \le \min\bigl(S\_1(t), S\_2(t)\bigr), \qquad 1 - \frac{d + c}{n} \le S\_{\mathrm{cens}}(t) \le 1 - \frac{d}{n}$$

   Domain: marginal survival $S\_1(t), S\_2(t) \in [0, 1]$ ($t \ge 0$), bounding $S(t)$ and $S\_{\mathrm{cens}}(t)$ for cohort $n \ge 1$, events $d \ge 0$, censored $c \ge 0$ ($d + c \le n$).

8. **Keynes's (1921) Evidential Weight & Cost–Loss Reference Scale:**
   $$V\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})\bigr) = \frac{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})}{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho}) + n\_0} \in [0, 1), \qquad n\_0 := n\_{\mathrm{req}}(\alpha, \beta, p^\ast) = \left(\frac{z\_{1-\alpha} + z\_{1-\beta}}{\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}}}\right)^2 \frac{1 - p^\ast}{p^\ast} > 0, \qquad p^\ast = \frac{C}{\eta L} \in (0, 1)$$
   evaluated at bound $\bar{\rho} \in [0, 1]$ ($\bar{\rho} \ge |\rho\_{R,Y}|$), $f \in (0, 1]$, $\alpha, \beta \in (0, 1)$ ($\alpha + \beta < 1$), and $\varepsilon\_{\mathrm{rel}} > 0$ ($V \to 1$ remains blind to *Not askable* strata where $\pi\_i \equiv 0$).

9. **Meng's (2018) Exact Big Data Paradox Identity:**
   $$\bar{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = \rho\_{R,Y} \times \sqrt{\frac{1 - f}{f}} \times \sigma\_Y, \qquad n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\rho\_{R,Y}) = \frac{f}{1 - f}\frac{1}{\rho\_{R,Y}^2}$$

   Domain: $f=n/N \in (0,1)$, $\sigma\_Y \ge 0$, $\rho\_{R,Y} \in [-1,1]\setminus\lbrace 0\rbrace$ in $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}$ ($\rho\_{R,Y}=0$ marks unbiased sampling).

10. **Kish–Moulton Design Effects (Equal vs. Unequal Clusters & Weighting):**
    $$\tilde{m} = \bar{m}\bigl(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m)\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I, \qquad \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} = 1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(w), \qquad \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - 1, \qquad n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = \frac{n}{\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}}}$$

    Domain: $n \ge 1$, clusters $m \ge 1$ (mean $\bar{m} \ge 1$, $\mathrm{CV}^2(m) \ge 0$, $\tilde{m} \ge 1$; equal-cluster $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + (m - 1)\rho\_I$ when $\mathrm{CV}^2(m) = 0$), $w > 0$, $\mathrm{CV}^2(w) \ge 0$, and $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}}, \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} > 0$. With uncorrelated weights and cluster membership, the exact composition is additive ($\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{total}} = 1 + (\tilde{m}-1)\rho\_I + \mathrm{CV}^2(w)$), whereas multiplying $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}}$ and $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$ acts only as an upper bound.

11. **Chao's (1984, 1987, 1989) & Chao–Colwell (2017) Lower Bounds on the Uncounted Population (Abundance vs. $t$-List Incidence):**
    $$\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao1}} = \frac{f\_1^2}{2 f\_2}, \qquad \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(t) = \frac{t - 1}{t}\mkern3mu\frac{f\_1^2}{2 f\_2}, \qquad \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(2) = \frac{f\_1^2}{4 f\_2} = \frac{(n\_{10} + n\_{01})^2}{4 n\_{11}} \ge \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{LP}}$$

    Domain: $f\_2 > 0$ ($n\_{11} > 0$), $n\_{10}, n\_{01} \ge 0$, $t \ge 2$; at $f\_2 = 0$, $f\_1(f\_1 - 1)/\bigl(2(f\_2 + 1)\bigr)$ (`Chao1`) and $\frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{f\_1(f\_1 - 1)}{2(f\_2 + 1)}$ (`Chao2`, reducing to $f\_1(f\_1 - 1)/\bigl(4(n\_{11} + 1)\bigr)$ at $t = 2, n\_{11} = 0$) preserve finiteness (Chao & Colwell 2017). Across $t \ge 2$ deduplicated lists under **symmetric binomial list incidence** ($p\_{ij} = p\_i$, Model $M\_h$), $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}] \ge \frac{t - 1}{t}\frac{(\mathbb{E}[f\_1])^2}{2\mathbb{E}[f\_2]}$ (Chao 1989); under homogeneous list-varying coverage ($p\_{ij} = p\_j$, Model $M\_t$), Lincoln–Petersen matches $\mathbb{E}[n\_{00}]$ while AM–GM keeps $\hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{Chao2}}(2) \ge \hat{n}\_{00,\mathrm{LP}}$, and under $M\_{th}$ ($p\_{i1} \ne p\_{i2}$) or negative dependence neither guarantees a floor. Multi-list linkage bounds only *Not observed* eligible units ($\pi\_i > 0$ on some registry); **Not askable** strata ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$ outside every frame) never enter $f\_1$ or $f\_2$, leaving $\hat{n}\_{00}$ blind while uncounted harm drains onto $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$.

## A.2 Filter II (Axis 2 — $S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$): Canonical Operator II — Non-Ergodic Reserve & Survival-Time Barrier

$$dR\_t = \bigl(u\_t - \dot{D}\_t\bigr)dt, \qquad d\ln W\_t = \bigl(r + f\_t(\mu - r) - \frac{1}{2}f\_t^2\sigma^2\bigr)dt + f\_t\sigma\mkern3mu dB\_t$$

Domain: reserve replenishment $u\_t$ and unit drawdown $\dot{D}\_t$ on $R\_t$, multiplicative drift $\mu$, hurdle $r$, finite-variance leverage $f\_t$, and volatility $\sigma > 0$ ($\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}} > 2$, item 14); queuing waiting-time and survival-time barriers follow item 15.

12. **Convexity Gap (Jensen 1906), Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS) & Coherent Expected Shortfall ($\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$):**
    $$\Delta\_J = \mathbb{E}[f(X)] - f(\mathbb{E}[X]), \qquad \mathrm{CRPS}(F, y) = \int\_{-\infty}^{\infty}\bigl(F(t) - \mathbf{1}\lbrace y \le t\rbrace\bigr)^2 dt \stackrel{\mathcal{P}\_1}{=} \mathbb{E}\_F|X - y| - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}\_F|X - X^\prime|, \qquad \mathrm{CVaR}\_p(X) = \frac{1}{1 - p}\int\_p^1 \mathrm{VaR}\_u(X)\mkern3mudu$$

    Domain: convex $f$ with $\mathbb{E}[|X|], \mathbb{E}[|f(X)|] < \infty$ ($\Delta\_J \ge 0$); in $\mathrm{CRPS}$ (Hersbach 2000; Gneiting & Raftery 2007), the integral form $\int\_{-\infty}^{\infty}(F(t) - \mathbf{1}\lbrace y \le t\rbrace)^2 dt$ converges for Pareto $\alpha > 1/2$ and is **proper** on all Borel laws $\mathcal{P}$, whereas the kernel difference $\mathbb{E}\_F|X - y| - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}\_F|X - X^\prime|$ ($X, X^\prime \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} F$) holds and is **strictly proper only on $\mathcal{P}\_1$** ($\mathbb{E}\_F|X| < \infty$, $\alpha > 1$, collapsing to $\infty - \infty$ for $\alpha \le 1$); under loss convention ($p \in (0, 1)$, $\mathbb{E}[X^+] < \infty$, $\alpha > 1$, even where variance diverges for $1 < \alpha \le 2$), $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ is coherent across continuous and discrete laws (Artzner et al. 1999; Acerbi & Tasche 2002), whereas $\mathrm{VaR}\_p$ violates subadditivity (and under MNAR missingness $f < 1$ on $[y\_{\min}, y\_{\max}]$, sharp upper $\mathrm{CVaR}\_{p, U} = y\_{\max}$ once $p \ge f$). Contractual payout limits or deductibles $X \wedge K$ ($K > 0$, distinct from Hill order count $k$) truncate recorded payer severity while keeping physical tail intensity intact and exporting unpaid overflow $(X - K)\_+$ onto external reception perimeters ($\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$; [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md), [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md), [Chapter 96](96-arrows-that-bend-the-strata.md)).

13. **Hill's (1975) Tail Index Estimator:**
    $$\hat{\alpha}\_{k,n} = \left(\frac{1}{k}\sum\_{i=1}^k \ln\frac{X\_{(n - i + 1)}}{X\_{(n - k)}}\right)^{-1}$$

    Domain: $1 \le k < n$, $X\_{(n)} > X\_{(n-k)} > 0$, effective tail count $k\_{\mathrm{eff}} = k / \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{tail}} > 2$ ($\mathbb{E}[\hat{\alpha}\_{k,n}] = \frac{k}{k-1}\alpha$ for $k > 1$ under pure Pareto tails; Hill 1975).

14. **Itô (1951) Time-Average Growth, Kelly (1956) Allocation & Epistemic Shrinkage (motivated by Baker & McHale 2013):**
    $$g\_{\mathrm{time}}(f) = r + f(\mu - r) - \frac{1}{2}f^2\sigma^2, \qquad k\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}\bigr) = \frac{1}{1 + 1 / n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}} \in (0, 1), \qquad f^\ast\_{\mathrm{epistemic}} = k\bigl(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}\bigr)\frac{\hat{\mu} - r}{\sigma^2}$$

    Domain: known variance $\sigma^2 > 0$ ($\sigma > 0$), $\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}} > 2$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} > 0$, $\mu, \hat{\mu} > r$ (baseline $r = r\_{\mathrm{rf}}$, dimensionless multiplier $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) \in (0, 1)$); estimating unknown $\hat{\sigma}^2$ requires stronger scale-invariant shrinkage ([Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) §6.1.2).

15. **Kingman's (1961, 1962) Queue Bound, Simchi-Levi Survival Condition & Schaefer (1954)–Holling (1973) Constant-Harvest Basin:**
    $$\mathbb{E}[W\_q] \le \left(\frac{c\_a^2/\rho^2 + c\_s^2}{2}\right)\frac{\rho}{1 - \rho}\tau\_s, \qquad \mathbb{E}[W\_q] \approx \left(\frac{c\_a^2 + c\_s^2}{2}\right)\frac{\rho}{1 - \rho}\tau\_s, \qquad \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}} := \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}) + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}, \qquad \mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} = \frac{(1 - c\_{\mathrm{imp}}) B\_t}{\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t), \qquad D(H) = K\sqrt{1 - \frac{4H}{rK}}$$

    Domain: $\rho \in (0,1)$, $\tau\_s,\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t} > 0$ ($\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t} \equiv \dot{D}\_{\mathrm{deficit}}$), $0 \le c\_a^2, c\_s^2 < \infty$ ($\alpha\_{\mathrm{Hill}} > 2$; $c\_a^2 + c\_s^2 > 0$ for $\rho \to 1^-$ divergence), $B\_t \ge 0$ ($>$ safe interior; $=$ boundary), impairment $c\_{\mathrm{imp}} \in [0, 1)$, and $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \in [0, 1)$; Schaefer's (1954) logistic harvest $\dot{X} = rX(1-X/K)-H=0$ ($r,K>0$, $0 \le H \le rK/4$) gives basin width $D(H)$ (Holling 1973) with $dD/dH \to -\infty$ as $H \to (rK/4)^-$ ([Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md) §1). Here $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ realizes $\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{recovery}} = \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{diagnosis}} + \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{re-derivation}} + \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{execution}}$ via three non-overlapping phases: sequential detection latency $\tau\_{\mathrm{detect}}(\delta\_{\mathrm{status}})$ ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{diagnosis}}$, dilated by $(1 - \delta\_{\mathrm{status}})^{-1}$, requiring pre-authorized bypass $\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$), institutional and cognitive deliberation $\tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{re-derivation}}$ on sealed $\mathcal{I}\_t$, including cognitive queueing $\tau\_{\mathrm{cog}} = B\_{\mathrm{audit}}/C\_{\mathrm{human}}$), and post-authorization execution $\tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ ($\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{execution}}$, scaling with $\mathbb{E}[W\_q]$). Cross-perimeter $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}$ is audited strictly *ex post* in §A.7 rather than inside Operator II. Across zero-rest shifts, cumulative allostatic care wear $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t} = \sum\_{s=0}^{t-1}\psi\_s$ ($t \ge 1$, $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, 0} = 0$, expressed in native strain units distinct from conscious channel capacity $C\_{\mathrm{human}} \approx 10\text{ bits/s}$; [Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md), [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), [Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)) erodes Axis-2 endurance silently.

15a. **Wirtinger–RCC Delay–Jitter Ceiling and Anderson–Morley One-Hop Spectral Bound:**

    Domain: simple undirected coupled graph $\mathcal{G} = (V, E)$ ($E \ne \emptyset$) with Laplacian eigenvalue $\lambda\_{\max}(L\_{\mathcal{G}}) > 0$ and vertex degrees $d\_i, d\_j \ge 0$; delays $0 \le \tau\_e(t) \le \bar{\tau}$ ($\bar{\tau} > 0$); jitter $-\mu\_- \le \dot{\tau}\_e(t) \le \mu\_+ < 1$ ($0 \le \mu\_+ < 1$, $\mu\_- \ge 0$); osmotic conductance sector $[\gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}}, \Gamma]$ ($\Gamma \ge \gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}} > 0$, $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}} := \gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}}/\Gamma \in (0, 1]$); and numerator $H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\mu\_+, \mu\_-, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}) > 0$ taking $(\bar{\eta}, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}}) = (1, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}})$ under pure telemetry and $\bigl(1 + \mu\_-, \frac{1 - \mu\_+}{1 + \mu\_-}\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}\bigr)$ under Doppler distortion (Seuret & Gouaisbaut 2013; Park, Ko, & Jeong 2011). This $\sqrt{2}$-baseline ceiling lies below the scalar Hopf and augmented Wirtinger limits; defining edge-coupled sector $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff},ij} = \rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$ under pure telemetry ($\bar{\eta}\_{ij} = 1$) and $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff},ij} = \frac{1 - \hat{\mu}\_{+,ij}}{1 + \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}}\rho\_{\mathrm{sec}}$ under Doppler distortion ($\bar{\eta}\_{ij} = 1 + \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}$), Anderson & Morley's (1985) bound gives one-hop edge utilization $u\_{ij} = (d\_i + d\_j)/B\_{ij} \le 1$ against budget $B\_{ij} := \theta\_{\mathrm{safe}}\, H\_{\mathrm{Thm3}}(\hat{\mu}\_{+,ij}, \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}, \rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff},ij}) / (\bar{\eta}\_{ij}\,\Gamma\,\hat{\bar{\tau}}\_{ij}) > 0$ ($\theta\_{\mathrm{safe}} \in (0, 1)$, $\hat{\bar{\tau}}\_{ij} > 0$, $0 \le \hat{\mu}\_{+,ij} < 1$, $\hat{\mu}\_{-,ij} \ge 0$, $\bar{\eta}\_{ij} \in \lbrace 1, 1 + \hat{\mu}\_{-,ij}\rbrace$). Under dead-band isolation ($\gamma\_{\mathrm{osm}} \to 0^+$), $\rho\_{\mathrm{sec,eff}} \to 0$, and heavy-tailed jumps ($0 < \alpha < 2$ within $\alpha \le 2$) can still breach the basin in one step.

## A.4 Leontief Structural Complementarity of the Nuclear Triad

21. **Leontief Structural Complementarity:**
    $$M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \in [0, 1]$$
    where on a sequential trajectory of horizon $T$:
    $$S\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} S\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}\bigl(\mathrm{Cov}\_T, V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}), \Delta\_{\mathrm{id}, t}\bigr), \quad S\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \min\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{tail}, t}, S\_{\mathrm{time}, t}, S\_{\mathrm{surv}, t}\bigr), \quad S\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \min\bigl(g\_{\text{ex-ante}, t}, g\_{\mathrm{kairos}, t}, g\_{\text{ex-post}, t}\bigr)$$
    The minimum is not a weighted sum and carries no exchange rate: an intact margin cannot purchase an exhausted one, and the trajectory fold is an infimum rather than an average because an exhausted step is unredeemed by later surplus. A site margin $m\_j$ and threshold $\tau\_j$ share one ordinal scale per site (Stevens 1946; Krantz, Luce, Suppes & Tversky 1971), leaving axis folds $s\_a = \min\_{j \in a} m\_j$ incommensurable across axes until calibrated to $S\_a = \varphi\_a(s\_a) \in [0, 1]$ on one shared scale under **ordinal level comparability ($\mathrm{OLC}$)** (Sen 1985). Only common strictly increasing maps preserve cross-axis ordering; independently recoding each axis via $C^1$ strictly increasing $\psi\_a : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ ($\psi\_a' > 0$, $\psi\_a(0) \ge 0$, $\psi\_a(1) = 1$) maps $\lbrace{}S : S\_a \ge \tau\ \forall a\rbrace$ to $\lbrace{}S' : S'\_a \ge \psi\_a(\tau)\rbrace$ with diagonal Jacobian ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Chapter 89](89-curvature-of-the-flight-path.md)):

$$J\_\psi = \operatorname{diag}\bigl(\psi\_{\mathrm{epi}}'(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}),\mkern5mu \psi\_{\mathrm{vital}}'(S\_{\mathrm{vital}}),\mkern5mu \psi\_{\mathrm{gov}}'(S\_{\mathrm{gov}})\bigr), \qquad \det J\_\psi = \prod\_{a} \psi\_a'(S\_a) > 0, \qquad \mathrm{Vol}(\tau; \psi) = \prod\_a \bigl(1 - \psi\_a(\tau)\bigr) = \int\_{[\tau, 1]^3} \lvert \det J\_\psi \rvert \mkern3mu dS.$$

On the **restricted affine monoid** $\psi\_a(x) = \alpha\_a x + (1 - \alpha\_a)$ ($0 < \alpha\_a \le 1$, identity $\alpha\_a = 1$, non-group since inverses exit $[0, 1]$ at $0$ when $\alpha\_a < 1$), $\det J\_\psi = \prod\_a \alpha\_a$ scales box volume while preserving common-$\alpha\_a$ volume ratios, confirming $V\_{\min}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3$ as a chart-dependent measure rather than an institutional invariant.

## A.5 The Grammar of Aim — Syntax Layer and Acceptance Predicate

$$\mathcal{S} = (\sigma\_1, \dots, \sigma\_N), \quad \sigma\_t = (\mathbf{u}\_t, \mathbf{w}\_t, \mathbf{m}\_t), \quad \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{S}; \boldsymbol{\tau}) \in \lbrace\mathrm{Interior}, \mathrm{Boundary}, \mathrm{Collapse}, \mathrm{Unparsable}\rbrace$$

    $$\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{m}; \boldsymbol{\tau}) = \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{w}^\prime, \mathbf{m}; \boldsymbol{\tau}) \quad \forall \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{w}^\prime.$$
    The acceptance predicate is invariant to the contingency tape $\mathbf{w}\_t$ (*skopós*), and its five-stage order `(i)–(v)` does not commute under stage permutation ([Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)). No accidental external success can redeem depleted guard margins $\mathbf{m}\_t$ or an uncalibrated control tape $\mathbf{u}\_t$ (*télos*), realizing Antipater's separation of aim from external outcome.

## A.6 The Multilayer Knowledge Graph ($\mathcal{G}\_1 \leftrightarrow \mathcal{G}\_2$)

$$\binom{7}{3} = 12 + 9 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 35, \qquad \mathcal{H}(L\_i) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_j)$$

24. **Vandermonde Partition of Triadic Exposure Regimes:**
    Partitioning $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ unique guard triples across the Nuclear Triad with partition sizes $(3, 2, 2)$ for $(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}})$ decomposes into five mutually exclusive regimes:
    $$\binom{7}{3} = \sum\_{k\_1+k\_2+k\_3=3} \binom{3}{k\_1}\binom{2}{k\_2}\binom{2}{k\_3} = 12 + 9 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 35.$$
    - **Trans-Triadic Keystone ($k\_1=1, k\_2=1, k\_3=1$):** $\binom{3}{1}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{1} = 3 \times 2 \times 2 = 12$ orders;
    - **Governance Axial Exposure ($k\_3=0, k\_1 \ge 1, k\_2 \ge 1$):** $\binom{3}{2}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{0} + \binom{3}{1}\binom{2}{2}\binom{2}{0} = 6 + 3 = 9$ orders;
    - **Vital Axial Exposure ($k\_2=0, k\_1 \ge 1, k\_3 \ge 1$):** $\binom{3}{2}\binom{2}{0}\binom{2}{1} + \binom{3}{1}\binom{2}{0}\binom{2}{2} = 6 + 3 = 9$ orders;
    - **Epistemic Axial Exposure ($k\_1=0$):** $\binom{3}{0}\binom{2}{2}\binom{2}{1} + \binom{3}{0}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{2} = 2 + 2 = 4$ orders;
    - **Pure Epistemic Concentration ($k\_1=3$):** $\binom{3}{3}\binom{2}{0}\binom{2}{0} = 1$ order.

25. **DAG Ancestor Cone Leontief Homomorphism:**
    $$\mathcal{H}(L\_i) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_j) = \min\_{L\_j \in \mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)} \min\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}(L\_j), S\_{\mathrm{vital}}(L\_j), S\_{\mathrm{gov}}(L\_j)\bigr).$$
    **Homomorphism Monotonicity:** If any basal order in the dependency cone collapses ($M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}(L\_{\mathrm{basal}}) = 0$), then $\mathcal{H}(L\_i) = 0$ for every downstream dependent discipline. This transfer is a model reading of the dependency graph rather than an observed cumulative margin: higher-order social and legal institutions cannot stand when foundational physical or epidemiological margins fail.

    Because $\min$ is concave, estimating coordinate margins $\hat{\theta}\_m$ ($\mathbb{E}[\hat{\theta}\_m] = \theta\_m$, $k \ge 2$) induces downward estimation bias $b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) \le 0$ under sampling overlap around $m^\ast \in \arg\min\_m \theta\_m$:

    $$b\_{\min}(\hat{\theta}) := \mathbb{E}\\!\left[\min\_{1 \le m \le k} \hat{\theta}\_m\right] - \min\_{1 \le m \le k} \theta\_m = -\mkern3mu\mathbb{E}\\!\left[\max\_{m \ne m^\ast} \bigl(\hat{\theta}\_{m^\ast} - \hat{\theta}\_m\bigr)\_+\right] \le 0.$$

    Separating estimation uncertainty from physical breach uses either a joint $(1 - \alpha)$ confidence rectangle $\prod\_{m=1}^k [\hat{\theta}\_{m,L}, \hat{\theta}\_{m,U}]$ inducing a simultaneous minimum band, or an $\mathrm{IUT}$ rejecting $H\_0: \bigcup\_m \lbrace \theta\_m \le \tau \rbrace$ vs. $H\_1: \bigcap\_m \lbrace \theta\_m > \tau \rbrace$ at unadjusted marginal level $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ ([Chapter 92](92-sealed-bow-and-uncommuted-verdict.md)):

    $$\min\_{1 \le m \le k} \hat{\theta}\_{m,L} \le \min\_{1 \le m \le k} \theta\_m \le \min\_{1 \le m \le k} \hat{\theta}\_{m,U}, \qquad \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{IUT}} = \bigcap\_{m=1}^k \lbrace T\_m > c\_{\alpha, m}\rbrace.$$
26. **Motter–Lai (2002) Cascade Breakdown Condition:**
    $$C\_i = (1 + \alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}, i}) L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0), \qquad \alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}, i} = \frac{1 - \rho\_i}{\rho\_i} \ge 0, \qquad \mathcal{F}(t + 1) = \mathcal{F}(t) \cup \bigl\lbrace{}j \notin \mathcal{F}(t) : L\_j\bigl(\mathcal{G}\_0 \setminus \mathcal{F}(t)\bigr) > C\_j\bigr\rbrace.$$
    Domain: initial node load $L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0) > 0$, load factor $\rho\_i = L\_i(\mathcal{G}\_0)/C\_i \in (0, 1]$ (reducing to Motter & Lai's uniform $\alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}} = (1 - \rho)/\rho \ge 0$ when $\rho\_i \equiv \rho$). Here $L\_j(\mathcal{G}\_0 \setminus \mathcal{F}(t))$ recomputes **global shortest-path betweenness** after removing failed **nodes** $\mathcal{F}(t)$ (Motter & Lai 2002)—distinct from local equal split across $z \ge 1$ neighbours ($L\_0 + L\_0/z > C$ once uniform $\rho = L\_0 / C \in (0, 1)$ exceeds $z/(z + 1)$, or $\alpha\_{\mathrm{tol}} < 1/z$). Below critical $\alpha\_c$, high-load node removal cascades.

26a. **One-Hop Byzantine Admissibility Band and Hybrid RFDE–CRDT Tracking Bound:**
    $$q \in \bigl[f + 1,\; \delta(\mathcal{G}) - f + 1\bigr] \quad \bigl(\delta(\mathcal{G}) \ge 2f + 1,\; \kappa\_v(\mathcal{G}) \ge f + 1,\; n \ge 2f + 2\bigr), \qquad |\widehat{M}\_i(t) - M\_{\mathrm{Common}}(t)| \le D\_{\max}\sup\_{\xi \in [t - D\_{\max}, t]} \|\dot{s}(\xi)\|\_\infty \quad (t \ge T\_0 + D\_{\max}).$$
    Domain: up to $f \ge 1$ Byzantine units on neighborhood graph $\mathcal{G}$ (sparse extension via $f$-local/$r$-robustness and peer rewiring in LeBlanc et al. 2013 and Leitão, Pereira, & Rodrigues 2007); propagation horizon $D\_{\max} > 0$; post-partition origin $T\_0 \ge 0$; continuous reserve rate $\dot{s}(\xi)$ separating local estimate $\widehat{M}\_i(t)$ from common state $M\_{\mathrm{Common}}(t)$ on a join-semilattice (Shapiro et al. 2011); and clock-free rehabilitation over generations $g \in \mathbb{Z}\_{\ge 1}$ with $T\_{\mathrm{vigil}}(g) = T\_{\mathrm{base}}\,2^{g-1}$ ($T\_{\mathrm{base}} > 0$), caps $0 < \theta\_{\mathrm{canary}} < \theta\_{\mathrm{vigil}} < 1$, and quorums $q\_{\mathrm{fraud}} < q\_{\mathrm{rehab}}$.

## A.7 Reception-Side Margin — The Co-Diagnostic Pair, Not a Fourth Axis

$$\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}},\mkern5mu \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr), \qquad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}, \qquad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t} = \mathrm{clamp}\Bigl(\frac{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}} - (d\_t + \omega s\_t)}{\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}}}, 0, 1\Bigr)$$

$$\xi\_b := 1 - S\_b \in [0, 1], \qquad \Lambda\_{ab} := -\frac{\partial S\_a}{\partial \xi\_b} = \frac{\partial S\_a}{\partial S\_b}\ (\Lambda\_{aa} = 0), \qquad \lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}} := -\frac{\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}}{\partial \xi\_a} = \frac{\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}}{\partial S\_a}$$

$$S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max\Bigl(0,\, S\_a - \sum\_{b \ne a}[\Lambda\_{ab}]\_+\mkern3mu\Delta\xi\_b\Bigr), \quad \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max\Bigl(0,\, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} - \sum\_a[\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}]\_+\mkern3mu\Delta\xi\_a\Bigr), \quad \mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}} = \bigl(\min\_a S\_a^{\mathrm{eff}},\, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}^{\mathrm{eff}}\bigr)$$

Domain: normalized tolerances of **external reception perimeters** (distinct from internal Canguilhem–Holling survival basins) define the *ex-post* boundary fold $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T}\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}, t}$, governed by reception tolerance $\tau\_{\mathrm{tol}} > 0$, drift $d\_t \ge 0$, shock intensity $s\_t \ge 0$, and shock weight $\omega > 0$ (neither margin is a probability, nor a fourth triadic axis, preserving $P = 3$ planes, seven sites, and $\binom{7}{3} = 35$ orders). Under co-degrading deficits ($\Delta \xi\_b \ge 0$), cross-axis sensitivity $\Lambda\_{ab}$ and boundary sensitivity $\lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}}$ yield $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ (recovering $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ when $\Lambda\_{ab} = \lambda\_{a \to \mathrm{basin}} = 0$); antagonistic over-pressing ($+\Delta S\_b > 0$) activates the duals $-\partial S\_a/\partial S\_b > 0$ and $-\partial \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}/\partial S\_a > 0$, while first-order bounds fail at $\min$ corners, heavy-tailed jumps ($0 < \alpha < 2$ within $\alpha \le 2$), and superadditive cycles ([Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)). The minimum is not a weighted sum, and **Reflexive Brake 4** keeps both $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ and $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ strictly *ex post*, driving no live step.
