## C.3 How to Read This List

An entry asserts three things only: that the work exists, that it appeared in the stated year, and that it warrants the specific claim attached to it in the text. Where a page or section is omitted, the bibliography warrants the claim as formulated in the book; where a source and the book diverge, the book is corrected, and no entry validates a formal derivation, which stands or falls on its stated proof.

Bibliographic entries are never silently altered or hedged. An erroneous year, venue, or pagination is corrected visibly in the revision record, and an unverifiable source is removed while its dependent claim is re-argued or withdrawn.
