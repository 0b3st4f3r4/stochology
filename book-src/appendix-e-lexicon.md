# Appendix E — Lexicon and the Architecture Map

Three vocabularies carry this treatise and none of them is self-explanatory to a reader arriving from a single discipline. The first is classical: the Greek and Latin terms in which the conjectural arts named their own practice, and which the framework adopts rather than translates because the translation loses the distinction the term was coined to keep. The second is technical: the operators, indices and inequalities the chapters use, each defined once here with the source that warrants it, so that a symbol met in a chapter can be resolved without re-reading the argument that introduced it. The third is political: the vocabulary of constituent and constituted power in which the institutional plane is argued.

A lexicon is not a summary. Each entry states what the term names and stops; the argument for the term is the chapter that uses it, and where the chapter and this appendix could be read as disagreeing, the chapter governs. As a boundary of scope, the marginal theorems referenced across entries belong to their named literature sources, while the treatise's own contribution consists in the coupled invariants, breakdown thresholds and non-compensatory bounds linking those entries across planes — without which the work would remain only a pedagogical anthology.

The appendix closes with the architecture map: one table laying the three planes, the three mediating filters, the seven constitutive tensions and the three safeguards onto one another. It is a reading aid for the framework half of the book — the filter a tension belongs to, the promise each rationalization keeps, the inversion by which it fails, and the safeguard that answers the inversion.

## E.1 Classical Terms

- **Ἀκρίβεια (*akríbeia*).** Exactness or precision grounded in fixed mechanical measurement, number and weighing (*métron*, *arithmós*, *stathmós*), which Plato contrasts in the *Philebus* (55e–56c) with the conjectural rigor of *stokhasmós*. The contrast is the origin of the framework's refusal to treat measurement and judgment as the same act.
- **Εὐστοχία (*eustokhía*), εὐστοχεῖν (*eustokheîn*).** Good aim, or skill in hitting the mark across an uncertain medium; the lexical bridge between the root **στόχος (*stókhos*)** and the figure of the archer (**τοξότης, *toxótēs***).
- **Καιρός (*kairós*).** The fleeting critical instant of crisis or opportunity, in clinical medicine (*Aphorismi* I.1: *ho dè kairòs oxýs*), navigation and strategy, when decisive action cannot be postponed beyond the physical survival window. It is the temporal form of the vital margin.
- **Καθῆκον (*kathēkon*).** In Stoic ethics, an appropriate action or incumbency suited to one's roles (*personae*) and circumstances, developed especially by Panaetius of Rhodes and transmitted in Cicero, *De Officiis* I.107–121.
- **Μελέτη (*melétē*).** Disciplined practice, study or exercise, by whose conjectural aim (*melétēs stokhasmōi*, Plato, *Philebus* 56a) the conjectural arts attune judgment without a mechanical gauge.
- **Μῆτις (*mētis*).** Plastic, vigilant and adaptive practical intelligence capable of navigating shifting and ambiguous terrain (Detienne and Vernant 1974).
- **Dialectic (determinate negation, *Aufhebung*).** Hegel's (1807; 1812–1816) name for the movement in which a determination is negated and the negation carries a content, so that what a shape of thought loses is replaced by the specific distortion the loss produces rather than by nothing; *aufheben* holds both senses at once, to preserve and to end. The collapse propositions use the first moment — each names the pathology a missing axis produces in the axes that remain — and the prohibition on optimizing the triad uses the second: the verdict is preserved as a reading and ended as a rule.
- **Non-Identical (*das Nichtidentische*) and Reification (*Verdinglichung*).** In Lukács (1923) and Adorno (1966), reification treats historically produced abstractions as natural things, whereas the Non-Identical names the singular, material excess of the object that resists exhaustion by any concept or statistical summary. It leaves operational traces in the width of a partial-identification interval, in a model discrepancy term, and in the care that no column records.
- **Προηγμένα (*proēgmena*).** In Stoic ethics, the preferred indifferents (rendered *producta* or *praeposita* in Cicero, *De Finibus* III.20–22), external outcomes such as health or striking the target that a rational agent selects without equating them with virtue.
- **Σημεῖα (*sēmeîa*).** Observable clinical symptoms, clues and diagnostic traces (Latin *signa*), theorized in the Hippocratic *Prognostic* and *On the Art* and in Galenic medicine alongside conclusive signs (*tekmēria*). The diagnostic clue of Part V is the same object under a different name.
- **Σκοπός (*skopós*).** The watched external target or mark, connected to *skopeîn*, "to look at," and rendered in Latin as *scopus*, *meta* or *propositum*. Antipater of Tarsus separates it from the internal end.
- **Στόχος (*stókhos*), στοχάζεσθαι (*stokházesthai*), στοχαστικὴ τέχνη (*stokhastikē tékhnē*).** In its literal primary sense attested in Liddell, Scott and Jones (1940, *LSJ* s.v. στόχος), *stókhos* denotes a pillar or post set up as a mark for archers, and by extension an aim, shot or conjecture (Latin *coniectura*). From it derive the verb *stokházesthai*, the noun *stokhastēs* and the category *stokhastikē tékhnē*, the conjectural art theorized by Alexander of Aphrodisias and classified among the conjectural arts in the medical introductory tradition transmitted under Galen's name, which Jacob Bernoulli (1713) revived as *ars conjectandi sive stochastice*.
- **Τέλος (*télos*).** In Antipater's archer analogy (Stobaeus, *Eclogae* II.76.9–15), the internal consummate end of doing everything within one's power continuously and undeviatingly to obtain the primary natural things, as distinct from the external outcome of striking the *skopós*.
- **Τόξον (*tóxon*), τοξότης (*toxótēs*).** The bow and the archer, an etymological family distinct from *stókhos* that supplies the classical figure of aiming under crosswinds.

## E.2 Technical Terms

- **Cognitive influence versus cognitive manipulation (premise rigging).** In Simon's (1947) bounded rationality and Habermas's (1984) theory of communicative action, legitimate cognitive influence structures attention by sharing partial-identification intervals and spillover losses transparently, whereas manipulation rigs the decision labyrinth at the top by concealing epistemic width or collateral harm while preserving the formal appearance of frontline autonomy.
- **CORP and CRPS decomposition.** The consistent, optimally binned, reproducible pool-adjacent-violators isotonic decomposition of the Brier score into miscalibration and discriminative resolution, and the analogous decomposition of the continuous ranked probability score (Gneiting and Raftery 2007; Dimitriadis, Gneiting and Jordan 2021).
- **Design effect and effective sample size.** Variance inflation from intra-cluster correlation, $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} = 1 + [\bar{m}(1 + \mathrm{CV}\_m^2) - 1]\rho\_{\mathrm{intra}}$ (Kish 1965; Moulton 1986), composed with Kish's weight-concentration factor $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} = n\_{\mathrm{f}}/n\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$ over $n\_{\mathrm{Kish}} = (\sum w\_i)^2/\sum w\_i^2$: in the two-component model with weights independent of clusters the exact composition is additive, $\mathrm{DEFF} = \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}} + \mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}} - 1$, so the product $\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{cluster}}\cdot\mathrm{DEFF}\_{\mathrm{Kish}}$ is a non-negative upper bound that exceeds it by $[\bar{m}(1 + \mathrm{CV}\_m^2) - 1]\rho\_{\mathrm{intra}}\mkern3mu\mathrm{CV}^2(w)$ and coincides with it when either factor is one. The sampling-variance effective sample size is then $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} = n/\mathrm{DEFF}$, floored at the number of clusters. It is strictly separated from Meng's (2018) selection-bias sample size $n\_{\mathrm{Meng}}(\bar{\rho}) = \frac{f}{1-f}\bar{\rho}^{-2}$, evaluated at a declared bound $\bar{\rho} \ge |\rho\_{R,Y}|$ rather than at a measured correlation: $\rho\_{R,Y}$ is defined against the unobserved population mean, and $|\rho\_{R,Y}| \le 1$ is the only bound the identity supplies, so the entry is a map and not an estimate. The first inflates variance, the second moves the location, and only the first belongs in a standard-error denominator.
- **Double-bind trap.** In Bateson's (1972) pragmatics of communication, the organizational trap $\mathrm{DB}\_t = \beta\_{\mathrm{proxy}, t}\cdot\delta\_{\mathrm{status}, t}\cdot(1 - \mathbf{1}\{\text{brake}\_t\}) \in [0, 1]$ inflicted when coercive single-metric pressure, status-based testimonial silencing of alarms and a disabled constitutional veto simultaneously close every legitimate path of action for frontline operators.
- **e-value and e-process.** A single-stage *e*-value is a nonnegative random variable $E \ge 0$ with $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[E] \le 1$; a sequential *e*-process $(E\_t)\_{t \ge 1}$ is a nonnegative supermartingale satisfying $\mathbb{E}\_{H\_0}[E\_\tau] \le 1$ at an arbitrary stopping time $\tau$ (Ville 1939; Shafer 2021; Grünwald, de Heide and Koolen 2024; Ramdas et al. 2023).
- **$E$-value of sensitivity (confounding).** In VanderWeele and Ding's (2017) sense, the minimum strength of association, on the risk-ratio scale, that an unmeasured confounder would need with both the exposure and the outcome for the observed association to be explained away, $\mathrm{RR} + \sqrt{\mathrm{RR}\mkern3mu(\mathrm{RR} - 1)}$ for a risk ratio above one, and, when both sides are pinned to the same coefficient, the root above one of $\mathrm{RR} = \rho^2/(2\rho - 1)$. It is a threshold on a bound, not an estimate and not a test statistic, and it is not the *e*-value of the preceding entry: the two share a name and nothing else, one being a confounder strength in risk-ratio units and the other a nonnegative random variable with null expectation bounded by one.
- **Epistemic margin index.** The dimensionless chart coordinate $S\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \varphi\_{\mathrm{epi}}(s\_{\mathrm{epi}}) \in [0, 1]$ of the first filter, obtained by a fixed strictly increasing chart $\varphi\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ over the native-unit epistemic axis fold $s\_{\mathrm{epi}}$ across partial-identification coverage ($\mathrm{Cov}\_T$), Keynesian evidential weight $V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}, t})$, and identification-width / CORP resolution sharpness ($\Delta\_{\mathrm{id}, t}$) under the first reflexive brake, and aggregated across a finished trajectory by the temporal infimum $S\_{\mathrm{epi}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} S\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}\bigl(\mathrm{Cov}\_T, V(n\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}), \Delta\_{\mathrm{id}, t}\bigr)$ rather than by a shift average or a compensatory weighted mixture. It is strictly diagnostic of a completed trajectory and never serves as a per-step control rule in place of Canonical Operator I.
- **Imbens–Manski confidence interval.** A two-sided interval $[\hat{\theta}\_L - c\_\alpha\hat{\sigma}\_L/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}},\mkern3mu \hat{\theta}\_U + c\_\alpha\hat{\sigma}\_U/\sqrt{n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}}]$ for a partially identified parameter $\theta \in [\theta\_L, \theta\_U]$, where $c\_\alpha \in [z\_{1-\alpha}, z\_{1-\alpha/2}]$ adapts to the ratio of identification width to sampling error (Imbens and Manski 2004).
- **Institutional governance index.** The dimensionless chart coordinate $S\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \varphi\_{\mathrm{gov}}(s\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \in [0, 1]$ of the third filter, obtained by a fixed strictly increasing chart $\varphi\_{\mathrm{gov}}$ over the native-unit governance axis fold across the ex-ante gate ($g\_{\text{ex-ante}, t}$), the *kairós* real-time gate ($g\_{\mathrm{kairos}, t}$), and the ex-post accountability gate ($g\_{\text{ex-post}, t}$), and aggregated across a finished trajectory by the temporal infimum $S\_{\mathrm{gov}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \min\bigl(g\_{\text{ex-ante}, t}, g\_{\mathrm{kairos}, t}, g\_{\text{ex-post}, t}\bigr)$ rather than by a shift mean or performative-weighted average. Bitemporal pre-registration is not a separate term of that minimum: a confirmatory seal admits the ex-post liability share into $g\_{\text{ex-post}, t}$, whereas an unconfirmed seal zeroes that gate; the coordinate is strictly diagnostic of a completed trajectory and never replaces Canonical Operator III.
- **Kelly fraction and epistemic shrinkage.** The exposure fraction $f\_{\text{plug-in}}^\ast = (\mu - r)/\sigma^2$ maximizing excess logarithmic growth (Kelly 1956), shrunk by $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}/(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}} + 1)$ under finite-sample parameter variance (Baker and McHale 2013).
- **Liebig–Leontief minimum.** The bottleneck aggregation rule $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}}) \in [0, 1]$, the limit as $\sigma \to 0^+$ of a constant-elasticity-of-substitution aggregator, which captures the serial non-compensability of the three safeguards at their critical survival threshold.
- **Post-data severity.** In Mayo's (2018) error statistics, the probability $\mathrm{SEV}(\theta > \theta\_{\mathrm{crit}}; x\_0) = \mathbb{P}(T(X) \le T(x\_0) \mid \theta = \theta\_{\mathrm{crit}})$ evaluated after observing $x\_0$, distinguished from the pre-registered design parameters $(\alpha, 1 - \beta)$.
- **Reflexive state vector.** The triple $\mathcal{R}\_t = (\mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{epi}, t}, \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{vital}, t}, \mathcal{R}\_{\mathrm{gov}, t})$ gathering, at each step and before the outcome is known, what the agent can know about its own archive, its own vessel and its own constitutional pact. Every coordinate is observable inside the agent's own apparatus, which is why ignorance of the world is no excuse for ignorance of the instrument.
- **Scapegoat shift.** Combining Girard's (1986) anthropology of sacrificial substitution with Simon's (1947) premise rigging and Holmström's (1979) informativeness principle, the excess blame $\Delta\_{\mathrm{scapegoat}} = \max(0, b\_{\mathrm{frontline}} - L\_{\mathrm{oper}})$ shifted onto frontline operators when leadership breaks its bitemporal commitment and disguises a structural loss as an operational failure.
- **Time-to-survive and time-to-recover.** The breathing-time inequality $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}, t} = B\_t / \dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}, t}(\mathcal{I}\_t) = \tau\_{\mathrm{detect}} + \tau\_{\mathrm{delib}}(\mathcal{I}\_t) + \tau\_{\mathrm{actuate}}$ (Simchi-Levi, Schmidt and Wei 2014), where $B\_t$ is the physical buffer, $\dot{D}\_{\mathrm{crisis}, t} > 0$ is the crisis drawdown rate, and the effective three-latency recovery time couples through deliberation and actuation delays to queue congestion and to the critical slowing down of an approaching fold (Scheffer et al. 2009).
- **Vital margin index.** The dimensionless chart coordinate $S\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}(s\_{\mathrm{vital}}) \in [0, 1]$ of the second filter, obtained by a fixed strictly increasing chart $\varphi\_{\mathrm{vital}}$ over the native-unit vital axis fold across tail and convexity safety ($S\_{\mathrm{tail}, t}$), non-ergodic time-average / Kelly leverage headroom ($S\_{\mathrm{time}, t}$), and survival-horizon vs. recovery-latency and basin-width clearance ($S\_{\mathrm{surv}, t}$), and aggregated across a finished trajectory by the temporal infimum $S\_{\mathrm{vital}} = \inf\_{1 \le t \le T} \min\bigl(S\_{\mathrm{tail}, t}, S\_{\mathrm{time}, t}, S\_{\mathrm{surv}, t}\bigr)$ rather than by an arithmetic mean over shifts. It is strictly diagnostic of a completed trajectory and never replaces Canonical Operator II.

## E.3 Political Terms

- **Agnotological alibi.** The deliberate production of scientific uncertainty by state or corporate actors to rationalize regulatory paralysis while extraction continues toward the ruin barrier (Proctor and Schiebinger 2008).
- **Algedonic entitlement.** An irrevocable communication right held by frontline operators and affected third parties carrying zero status or credential discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$), allowing distress signals to bypass the administrative hierarchy and reach overall governance directly under corroborated likelihood-ratio sequential filtering respectful of the human throughput ceiling near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Beer 1972; Zheng & Meister 2025).
- **Canguilhem–Holling basin.** The dynamic domain of attraction $D(H) = K\sqrt{1 - 4H/(rK)}$ bounding the capacity of an ecological or social commons to absorb shocks under harvesting rate $H$ (Holling 1973; Canguilhem 1966).
- **Communisme des esprits.** The intellectual communism of the multitude articulated in Spinoza's *Ethics* IV and in Matheron (1969), in which knowledge is non-rivalrous and superadditive, so that sharing it enlarges collective *potentia*.
- **Conatus.** The striving of every entity to persevere in its being (*Ethics* III, propositions 6–7), the ontological ground of the vital margin and of self-governance.
- **Discursive dilemma.** The result, stated by Pettit (2001), that majority voting on interconnected premises can yield a set of conclusions the same majority rejects when voting on conclusions directly.
- **Double bind.** Bateson's (1972) pathological communication structure in which a victim faces contradictory demands across meta-communicative levels and is forbidden to escape or to comment on the contradiction.
- **Immanent institutions.** Governance frameworks, rules and reserve vaults rooted in the horizontal practices, material buffers and monitoring protocols of commoners, without reliance on a transcendent sovereign.
- **Leontief non-compensability.** The structural principle that surplus on one axis cannot offset failure on another, formalized as the bottleneck minimum over the three margins.
- **Multitudo.** The plural multitude in Spinoza's political philosophy: the collective body whose combined constituent power forms the only legitimate foundation of political authority.
- **Polycentric commons.** Ostrom's (1990) architecture of self-governance, characterized by multiple overlapping and autonomous decision centres operating under shared, nested constitutional principles.
- **Potentia.** Constituent power: the immanent, horizontal capacity of individuals and communities to act, to produce and to persevere in being.
- **Potestas.** Constituted power: centralized, vertical and alienated authority that commands obedience through decree and coercive enforcement.
- **Premise rigging.** The administrative manipulation of the decision agenda so that the admissible options narrow to extractive ones, leaving the formal procedure intact while the conclusion is fixed in advance (Simon 1947; Habermas 1984).

## E.4 The Architecture Map

Seven constitutive tensions, one per guard site, arranged over the six ordered crossings of distinct planes and the integral closure that together make the seven transits of the mediation circuit. Each row states what the rationalization promises when it works, what it becomes when it is severed from its limits, and the safeguard that answers the inversion. The promise column is not a claim that the practice is benign; it is the claim any institution must be able to make before the practice is licensed at all.

| Guard site | Transit and filter | Tension | Promise → inversion | Safeguard and methodological requirement |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `T1` | Institution → inquiry framing · Filter I | Situational interest versus intellectual rectitude | Orienting inquiry toward socially relevant problems → structural agnotology, selective reporting, and the Type III framing error | Antidogmatic reserve, bitemporal pre-registration, specification curve, and strong objectivity |
| `T2` | Hypothesis → empirical test · Filter I | Accommodating confirmation versus fruitful refutation | Articulating theory against evidence → *ad hoc* patching of anomalies and opportunistic stopping | Progressive problemshift, anytime-valid *e*-processes, and severe testing on two-arm bounds |
| `T3` | Population → documentary record · Filter I | Sample volume versus evidential weight and omission | Overcoming uncertainty by accumulating a massive record → the big-data paradox, cluster echo, and erasure of the uncounted | Evidential weight, design effect, unseen-count bound, bitemporal vintages, and a lexicographic safety floor |
| `T4` | Phenomenon → formal summary · Filter II | Domesticating formalization versus reflexive formalization | Achieving public clarity in a formal language → arithmomorphic rigidity and the erasure of convex tail harm behind a mean | Dialectical penumbra, ordinal scale invariance, Jensen's gap, CRPS, and the tail index |
| `T5` | Ensemble → historical trajectory · Filter II | Ensemble average versus trajectory in irreversible time | Optimizing policy by expected ensemble outcome → multiplicative volatility drain, queue strangulation, and absorption at the ruin barrier | Entropic time, epistemic shrinkage, the basin of attraction, queue slack, and the survival inequality |
| `T6` | Policy rule → social field · Filter III | Passive observation versus performative intervention | Guiding action by historical regularities → target collapse, self-fulfilling and self-defeating prophecy | Calibration of the performative map, invariant causal prediction, discrepancy terms, and performative stability |
| `T7` | Integral mediation circuit · Filter III and triadic synthesis | Process (*télos*) and responsibility versus outcome (*skopós*) and asymmetry | Evaluating agency by rational competence → outcome bias, technocratic impunity, risk-shifting upward, and testimonial deafness | Meta-aptness, the ethics of imposed risk, a conjunctive veto, the algedonic channel, clawbacks, and disclosure |

*Table E.1: The seven guard sites over the three planes, with the promise, the inversion and the safeguard of each tension.*

The map is a reading aid and carries no verdict. A trajectory is judged by the decision procedure of Appendix B.1, which reads declared margins rather than rows of this table, and a table that could itself return a verdict would be the checklist the fourth reflexive brake prohibits.
