Three vocabularies anchor the treatise: classical, dialectical, and ancestral terms across Mediterranean, Nilotic, African, Asian, Abya Yala, Oceanian, and modern critical traditions (§E.1); an index of technical constructs resolving each term to its equations in [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) and short definition in [Appendix G](appendix-g-glossary.md) (§E.2); and political terms of constituent and constituted power (§E.3).

A lexicon is not a summary and carries no normative authority: **no section of this lexicon (`§E.1–§E.4`) establishes an independent standard or returns a verdict** (under Reflexive Brake 4, chapters `00`–`99` govern wherever any appendix and chapter disagree). While cited sources establish the marginal theorems, the treatise's contribution lies in the coupled invariants, breakdown thresholds, and non-compensatory bounds linking planes; the closing map (`§E.4`, `Table E.1`) aligns planes, filters, tensions, and guard sites as a strictly non-normative reading aid.

## E.1 Classical Terms

- **Ἀκρίβεια (*akríbeia*).** Exactness or precision grounded in counting, measuring, and weighing (*arithmētikē*, *metrētikē*, *statikē*, alongside *métron*, *státhmē*, and carpenter's straightedge), which Plato contrasts in the *Philebus* (55e–56c; Plato *c.* 360 BCE) with the conjectural rigor of *stokhasmós*. The contrast is the origin of the framework's refusal to treat measurement and judgment as the same act.
- **Ἀναγνώρισις (*anagnórisis*) and ἁμαρτία (*hamartía*).** In Aristotle's *Poetics* (1452a22–33, 1453a8–10; Aristotle *c.* 335 BCE, trans. Fyfe), *hamartía* (*hamartánein*) names missing the mark or fatal error, the counterpart of *stokházesthai* ("to aim at"), while *anagnórisis* strictly names "a change from ignorance to knowledge" (1452a29–32), attaining its finest form when coinciding with reversal (*peripéteia*, 1452a32–33); Stochology extends this pair (as in Sophocles's *Philoctetes*) to the moment an unexpected impact forces an agent or institution to confront what its sealed frame excluded.
- **Εὐστοχία (*eustokhía*), εὐστοχεῖν (*eustokheîn*).** Good aim, or skill in hitting the mark across an uncertain medium; the lexical bridge between the root **στόχος (*stókhos*)** and the figure of the archer (**τοξότης, *toxótēs***).
- **Καθῆκον (*kathēkon*).** In Stoic ethics, an appropriate action or duty (*officium*) suited to one's roles (*personae*) and circumstances, formulated in Cicero's *De Officiis* (I.7–8 defining *officium*/*kathēkon*, and I.107–121 on the four *personae*; Cicero 44 BCE).
- **Καιρός (*kairós*).** The fleeting critical instant of crisis or opportunity, in clinical medicine (*Aphorismi* I.1: *ho dè kairòs oxýs*), navigation and strategy, when decisive action cannot be postponed beyond the physical survival window. It is the temporal form of the vital margin.
- **Μελέτη (*melétē*).** Disciplined practice, study or exercise, by whose conjectural aim (*melétēs stokhasmōi*, Plato, *Philebus* 56a) the conjectural arts attune judgment without a mechanical gauge.
- **Μῆτις (*mētis*).** Plastic, vigilant and adaptive practical intelligence capable of navigating shifting and ambiguous terrain (Detienne and Vernant 1974).
- **Προηγμένα (*proēgmena*).** In Stoic ethics, the preferred indifferents (rendered *producta*, *praeposita*, or *promota* in Cicero, *De Finibus* III.51–52, alongside *selectio* and the spear/arrow analogy at III.20–22; Cicero 45 BCE), external outcomes such as health or striking the target that a rational agent selects without equating them with virtue.
- **Σημεῖα (*sēmeîa*).** Observable clinical symptoms, clues and diagnostic traces (Latin *signa*), theorized in the Hippocratic *Prognostic* and *On the Art* and in Galenic medicine alongside conclusive signs (*tekmēria*). The diagnostic clue of Part V is the same object under a different name.
- **Σκοπός (*skopós*).** The watched external target or mark, connected to *skopeîn*, "to look at," and rendered in Latin as *scopus*, *meta* or *propositum*. Antipater of Tarsus separates it from the internal end.
- **Στόχος (*stókhos*), στοχάζεσθαι (*stokházesthai*), στοχαστικὴ τέχνη (*stokhastikē tékhnē*).** In its literal primary sense attested in Liddell, Scott and Jones (1940, *LSJ* s.v. στόχος), *stókhos* denotes a pillar or post set up as a mark for archers, and by extension an aim, shot or conjecture (Latin *coniectura*). From it derive the verb *stokházesthai*, the noun *stokhastēs* and the category *stokhastikē tékhnē*, the conjectural art theorized by Alexander of Aphrodisias and classified among the conjectural arts in the medical introductory tradition transmitted under Galen's name, which Jacob Bernoulli (1713) revived as *ars conjectandi sive stochastice*.
- **Τέλος (*télos*).** In Antipater's Stoic formula (Stobaeus 1884, *Eclogae* II.76.11–15), the internal consummate end of doing everything within one's power continuously and undeviatingly to obtain the primary natural things, paired with Stoic *skopós*/*télos* distinctions (*Ecl.* II.76.16–23, II.77.1–5, 16–27; distinct from the earlier Peripatetic foil at *Ecl.* II.47.7–11) and separated from the external target (*skopós*) in the archer simile transmitted by Cicero (*De Finibus* III.22; Cicero 45 BCE) and Plutarch (*De communibus notitiis* 1070f–1071e; Plutarch *c.* 100 CE).
- **Τόξον (*tóxon*), τοξότης (*toxótēs*).** The bow and the archer, an etymological family distinct from *stókhos* that supplies the classical figure of aiming under crosswinds.

### Modern Dialectical and Methodological Terms

- **Counter-induction (*Principle of Proliferation*).** Feyerabend's (1970) methodological rule requiring the deliberate invention and elaboration of hypotheses inconsistent with accepted theories or established facts, so that refuting evidence invisible from inside an established paradigm can surface without relaxing severity.
- **Dialectic (determinate negation, *Aufhebung*).** Hegel's (1807; 1812–1816) name for the movement in which a determination is negated and the negation carries a content, so that what a shape of thought loses is replaced by the specific distortion the loss produces rather than by nothing; *aufheben* holds both senses at once, to preserve and to end. The collapse propositions use the first moment (each names the pathology a missing axis produces in the axes that remain), and the prohibition on optimizing the triad uses the second: the verdict is preserved as a reading and ended as a rule.
- **Non-Identical (*das Nichtidentische*) and Reification (*Verdinglichung*).** In Lukács (1923) and Adorno (1966), reification treats historically produced abstractions as natural things, whereas the Non-Identical names the singular, material excess of the object that resists exhaustion by any concept or statistical summary. It leaves operational traces in the width of a partial-identification interval, in a model discrepancy term, and in the care that no column records.

### Ancestral and Planetary Lineage Terms

- **Bùkěshèng (*不可勝*) and kěshèng (*可勝*).** Master Sun's strategic asymmetry between holding one's own position undefeated through disciplined internal preparation (*bùkěshèng*) and waiting passively for an opponent's or environment's exposure (*kěshèng*; Griffith 1963), prefiguring the separation between controllable safeguards and exogenous shocks.
- **Huehuetlahtolli and *tlalticpac*.** The Nahuatl elders' civic discourses on comportment across *tlalticpac*, the narrow, slippery earthly ridge where careless steps produce irreversible ruin, arguing continuous calibration and restraint rather than once-for-all certainty (Anderson & Dibble 1969; Burkhart 1989).
- **Iʿtibār (*اعتبار*).** Ibn al-Haytham's structured variational experimentation across optical and astronomical instruments, subjecting conjecture to repeatable empirical alteration instead of trusting ocular habit or textual authority (Sabra 1989).
- **Khipu and *qollqa*.** Paired Andean governance safeguards joining dual-strand audit cords for cross-checked demographic and tribute accounting (*khipu*; Urton 2003) with reciprocally stocked storehouses across ecological tiers engineered against multi-year harvest failures and protracted drought tails (*qollqa*; Murra 1975).
- **Ptahhotep and Edwin Smith surgical triage.** Nilotic Old Kingdom refusal of closed-craft perfection, recognizing sound counsel even among maids at the grindstones (Lichtheim 1973), paired with the three-way surgical triage sorting injuries into treatable cases, conditions contended under uncertainty, and hopeless cases withheld from futile intervention (Breasted 1930; [Aim Among Mirrors](00-preface.md), [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md), [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md)).
- **Qhipnayra.** The Aymara spatio-temporal frame (*nayra*, attested past before the eyes; *qhipa*, unobserved future behind one's back) grounding trajectory evaluation in inspected precedent rather than unexamined projection (Núñez & Sweetser 2006; Rivera Cusicanqui 2012; [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md), [Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md), [Chapter 86](86-posture-before-bowstring-draws.md)).
- **Sānbiǎo (*三表*, Three Standards).** Mozi's joint validity criterion requiring every institutional maxim or doctrine to withstand simultaneous examination against historical root, common sensory witness, and public consequence (Johnston 2010).
- **Sankofa (*Sɛ wo werɛ fi na wosankɔfa a, yɛnkyiri*).** Akan epistemic norm authorizing structured return to recover neglected or silenced precedent without punitive disgrace (Twi oral tradition; Gyekye 1996; Wiredu 1996; [Aim Among Mirrors](00-preface.md), [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)).
- **Syādvāda (*स्याद्वाद*).** Jaina doctrine of sevenfold modal predication (*anekāntavāda*), indexing every factual claim to an explicit standpoint and boundary condition rather than flattening partial truths into unconditional assertion (Matilal 1981).
- **Ubuntu and Akan consensual ethics.** Southern and West African relational accountability principles in which epistemic standing and collective legitimacy depend on mutual recognition and uncoerced minority voice rather than numerical override alone (Wiredu 1996; Metz 2007).
- **Whakataukī (*Kia whakatōmuri te haere whakamua*).** Māori proverbial precept, advancing backwards into the future with eyes held on the past, anchoring present decisions in inherited ruptures and ecological continuity (Rameka 2016).

## E.3 Political Terms

This section pairs the treatise's operational institutional constructs with political-philosophical lineage vocabulary anchored in [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) and [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md); historical philosophical terms that serve strictly as conceptual background (*communisme des esprits*, *conatus*, *immanent institutions*, *multitudo*) do not appear as standalone technical headwords in [Appendix G](appendix-g-glossary.md).

- **Agnotological alibi.** The deliberate production of scientific uncertainty by state or corporate actors to rationalize regulatory paralysis while extraction continues toward the ruin barrier (Proctor and Schiebinger 2008).
- **Algedonic entitlement.** An irrevocable communication right held by frontline operators and affected third parties carrying zero status discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$), allowing distress signals to bypass hierarchy under corroborated likelihood-ratio filtering with $e\_t > 1$ and $E\_{\mathrm{trip}} = 1/\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}} > 1$ ($\alpha\_{\mathrm{test}} \in (0, 1)$). Beside preventing status silencing ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} \to 1^-$), symmetric false-alarm calibration blocks spoofed alert flooding (*compliance denial of service*) from saturating oversight above the human throughput ceiling near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Beer 1972; Zheng and Meister 2025).
- **Canguilhem–Holling basin.** The constant-harvest attraction basin $D(H) = K\sqrt{1 - 4H/(rK)}$ from Schaefer's (1954) logistic harvest model ($\dot{X} = rX(1 - X/K) - H$, $r, K > 0$, $0 \le H \le rK/4$), whose boundary derivative diverges ($dD/dH \to -\infty$ as $H \to (rK/4)^-$) precipitously near maximum yield, paired with Holling's (1973) resilience basin width and Canguilhem's (1943/1966) vital normativity.
- **Communisme des esprits.** Alexandre Matheron's (1969) phrase for the non-rivalrous intellectual communism of the multitude grounded in Spinoza's immanent *potentia* (Matheron 1969; Negri 1981; Spinoza 2007 [1670]), in which shared knowledge enlarges collective agency.
- **Conatus.** In Spinoza's *Ethics* (Part III, propositions 6–7; Spinoza 1985 [1677]), the striving of every entity to persevere in its being, read in Stochology's political-philosophical background alongside Spinoza's *Theological-Political Treatise* rather than closed *more geometrico* substance monism (Spinoza 2007 [1670]; Negri 1981; Dussel 2008; [Legitimacy, Accountability, and Institutions](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md), [Layer 35](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)).
- **Cumulative allostatic care wear.** Uncompensated zero-rest strain accumulation $W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, t} = \sum\_{s=0}^{t-1}\psi\_s$ ($W\_{\mathrm{allostatic}, 0} = 0$, measured in native strain units distinct from conscious channel capacity $C\_{\mathrm{human}} \approx 10\text{ bits/s}$) across caregiver shifts that depletes vital endurance invisibly while inanimate equipment buffers appear intact, linking care and democratic deficits across social reproduction and public buffers (Zheng and Meister 2025; Parra Jounou and Tronto 2024; Pires and Mundim 2026; Lightman and Kevins 2025/2026; [Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md), [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md), [Chapter 99](99-unsealing-the-vault-after-impact.md)).
- **Discursive dilemma.** The result, stated by Pettit (2001), that majority voting on interconnected premises can yield a set of conclusions the same majority rejects when voting on conclusions directly.
- **Double bind.** Bateson's (1972) pathological communication structure in which a victim faces contradictory demands across meta-communicative levels and is forbidden to escape or to comment on the contradiction.
- **Immanent institutions.** Governance frameworks, rules and reserve vaults rooted in the horizontal practices, material buffers and monitoring protocols of commoners, without reliance on a transcendent sovereign (Ostrom 1990; 2010; [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md), [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)).
- **Leontief non-compensability.** The structural principle that surplus on one axis cannot offset failure on another, formalized as the bottleneck minimum over the three margins.
- **Multiple accountabilities disorder and procedural theatre.** Institutional pathology in which multiplying overlapping, forum-shopped reporting obligations without enforceable sanction fragments responsibility (Bovens 2007; Black 2008) while documentary inflation becomes self-referential audit ritual divorced from decision relevance (Toumi et al. 2026), saturating human deliberation capacity near $10\text{ bits/s}$ (Zheng and Meister 2025).
- **Multitudo.** The plural multitude in Spinoza's political philosophy: the collective body whose combined constituent power forms the only legitimate foundation of political authority (Spinoza 2007 [1670]; Negri 1981).
- **Polycentric commons.** Ostrom's (1990; 2010) architecture of self-governance, characterized by multiple overlapping and autonomous decision centres operating under shared, nested constitutional principles, bounded in knowledge stewardship by CARE community governance (Carroll et al. 2020) and `FPIC` non-extraction against compulsory $P\_2$ archival enclosure (see [Appendix G](appendix-g-glossary.md)).
- **Potentia.** Constituent power: the immanent, horizontal, and obediential capacity of plural individuals and communities to act, to produce, and to persevere in being (*Theological-Political Treatise*, rejecting any closed *more geometrico* deductive absorption into unitary substance; Spinoza 2007 [1670]; Negri 1981; Dussel 2008).
- **Potestas.** Constituted power: centralized, vertical and alienated authority that commands obedience through decree and coercive enforcement.
- **Premise rigging.** The administrative manipulation of the decision agenda so that the admissible options narrow to extractive ones, leaving the formal procedure intact while the conclusion is fixed in advance (Simon 1947; Habermas 1984, crediting Simon for decision premises without attributing Stochology's algebraic index; cf. [Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md); [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3 (20a)).
- **Real abstraction and tyranny of structurelessness.** Material abstractions generated in commodity exchange, coinage, and institutional scoreboards before conceptual reflection (*Realabstraktion*; Sohn-Rethel 1978; Seaford 2004), paired with the symmetric pitfall in which abolishing explicit, auditable procedures hands coordination to unaccountable informal cliques rather than desacralizing provisional rules (Freeman 1972; [Legitimacy, Accountability, and Institutions](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md); [Institution, Incentive, Metric](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)).

## E.4 The Architecture Map

Seven constitutive tensions, one per guard site, arranged over the six ordered crossings of distinct planes and the integral closure that together make the seven transits of the mediation circuit. Each row states what the rationalization promises when it works, what it becomes when it is severed from its limits, and the safeguard that answers the inversion. The promise column is not a claim that the practice is benign; it is the claim any institution must be able to make before the practice is licensed at all.

| Guard site | Transit and filter | Tension | Promise → inversion | Safeguard and methodological requirement |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `T1` | Institution → inquiry framing · Filter I | Situational interest versus intellectual rectitude | Orienting inquiry toward socially relevant problems → structural agnotology, selective reporting, and the Type III framing error | Antidogmatic reserve, bitemporal pre-registration, specification curve, and strong objectivity |
| `T2` | Hypothesis → empirical test · Filter I | Accommodating confirmation versus fruitful refutation | Articulating theory against evidence → *ad hoc* patching of anomalies and opportunistic stopping | Progressive problemshift, anytime-valid *e*-processes, and severe testing on two-arm bounds |
| `T3` | Population → documentary record · Filter I | Sample volume versus evidential weight and omission | Overcoming uncertainty by accumulating a massive record → the big-data paradox, cluster echo, and erasure of the uncounted | Evidential weight, design effect, unseen-count bound, bitemporal vintages, and a lexicographic safety floor |
| `T4` | Phenomenon → formal summary · Filter II | Domesticating formalization versus reflexive formalization | Achieving public clarity in a formal language → arithmomorphic rigidity and the erasure of convex tail harm behind a mean | Dialectical penumbra, ordinal scale invariance, Jensen's gap, CRPS, and the tail index |
| `T5` | Ensemble → historical trajectory · Filter II | Ensemble average versus trajectory in irreversible time | Optimizing policy by expected ensemble outcome → multiplicative volatility drain, queue strangulation, and absorption at the ruin barrier | Entropic time, epistemic shrinkage, the basin of attraction, queue slack, and the survival inequality |
| `T6` | Policy rule → social field · Filter III | Passive observation versus performative intervention | Guiding action by historical regularities → target collapse, self-fulfilling and self-defeating prophecy | Calibration of the performative map, invariant causal prediction, discrepancy terms, and performative stability |
| `T7` | Integral mediation circuit · Filter III and triadic synthesis | Process (*télos*) and responsibility versus outcome (*skopós*) and asymmetry | Evaluating agency by rational competence → outcome bias, technocratic impunity, risk-shifting upward, and testimonial deafness | Meta-aptness, the ethics of imposed risk, a conjunctive veto, the algedonic channel, clawbacks, and disclosure |

*Table E.1: The seven guard sites over the three planes, with the promise, the inversion and the safeguard of each tension.*

The map is a reading aid and carries no verdict. A trajectory is judged by the decision procedure of [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate), which reads declared margins rather than rows of this table, and a table that could itself return a verdict would be the checklist the fourth reflexive brake prohibits.
