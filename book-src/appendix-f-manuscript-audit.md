A treatise that argues across one hundred numbered chapters, seven part introductions, seven part conclusions, and seven stratum openings can lose its own joints if no single register records what each file establishes, which plane or guard triple it carries, and where its claim deliberately stops. This appendix provides that editorial audit map across every non-appendix spine section in reading order. Each section's structured table pairs a file's structural position with its core object or operator, its central thesis, and its declared boundary or failure mode.

Like [Appendix E](appendix-e-lexicon.md), this map is a reading and audit aid and carries no authority and no verdict. Authority runs strictly from chapter to appendix: where any table cell here and a chapter could be read as diverging on a definition, an identity, a literature source, or a scope condition, the chapter governs and this appendix is repaired, because the chapter is where the claim, its source, and its derivation sit side by side. Nor do these tables form a compliance checklist or an evaluation rubric, which the fourth reflexive brake forbids; they record the architectural division of labor so that a reader or auditor can inspect continuity, non-substitutable axis coverage, and declared falsification boundaries across the whole manuscript without mistaking the index for the argument.

Nine sections organize this audit: eight sections (`F.0`–`F.7`) mirror the table of contents, while Section `F.8` provides a strictly non-normative reading aid for disciplinary entry doors, spiral depth progression, and scope boundaries under the fourth reflexive brake. Section F.0 records the Prologue. Sections F.1 through F.4 record Parts I through IV, each framed by its part introduction and part conclusion. Section F.5 records Part V from its part introduction across the seven strata, each preceded by its stratum opening and followed by its five layer chapters, to its part conclusion. Section F.6 records Part VI across its introduction, seven single-shot laminar trajectory chapters, and its part conclusion. Section F.7 records Part VII across its introduction, seven superposed counter-inductive trajectory chapters, and the closing conclusion of the treatise.
