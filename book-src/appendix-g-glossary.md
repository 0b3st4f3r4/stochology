## G.1 Alphabetical Glossary

### A

- **A-marks and I-marks (four-state intake null semantics).** Four mutually exclusive intake states—`NOT_OBSERVED`, `INAPPLICABLE`, `MEASURED_ZERO`, and pre-schema *Not askable*—barring silent null-to-zero coercion (Codd 1979; 1986; 1987).
- **Absorbing barrier.** Boundary state $R\_{t+1} \le 0$ from which physical reserve trajectories cannot re-enter positive liquidity or survival without external reconstitution.
- **Accountability forum.** Binding institutional triad of actor disclosure obligation, interrogative authority, and enforceable sanction separating real governance from ceremonial disclosure (Bovens 2007).
- **Ad hoc deflation.** Pre-registered fixed divisor $R\_{\mathrm{Lakatos}}(E\_T, K\_{\max}) = E\_T / (K\_{\max} + 1)$ ($K\_{\max} \in \mathbb{N}\_0$) diluting *e*-process wealth across budgeted hypothesis adjustments while quarantining un-budgeted narrowings (`Unparsable`; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1, [Chapter 18](18-philosophy-of-science.md), [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)).
- **Agnotology.** The deliberate institutional manufacture, amplification, or strategic maintenance of scientific doubt and missing data to defer precautionary action while extraction continues (Proctor & Schiebinger 2008; Supran & Oreskes 2020).
- **Algedonic channel.** A non-hierarchical pain-or-emergency signal path carrying zero status discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) from frontline operations directly to constitutional override oversight when ordinary reporting queues saturate or fail.
- **Allostatic load and care wear.** Zero-rest caregiver strain accumulation depleting vital endurance invisibly while inanimate physical buffers appear intact (McEwen 1998; Morley et al. 2019; [Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md), [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), [Chapter 95](95-collision-at-ten-bits-per-second.md)).
- **Anagnórisis.** Recognition-with-reversal (*peripéteia*) when unexpected failure exposes an excluded frame boundary; defined in [Appendix E §E.1](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e1-classical-terms).
- **Anderson–Morley one-hop spectral budget.** Neighborhood stability bound converting Anderson and Morley's (1985) Laplacian eigenvalue ceiling into per-edge capacity limits without centralized network computation.
- **Anti-Gettier censoring trap.** An adversarial or quiescent archive regime that truncates tail observations above a ceiling $y\_{\mathrm{cens}}$, causing calibrated over-confidence and excessive leverage precisely before an out-of-support excursion strikes.
- **Anytime-valid *e*-process.** Nonnegative adapted process with null expectation at most one at any stopping time, generalizing supermartingales without alpha-spending inflation (Ville 1939; Ramdas et al. 2023; Grünwald, de Heide, & Koolen 2024; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1).
- **Attenuation bias.** Systematic shrinkage of an estimated regression slope or association magnitude toward zero caused by classical measurement error in predictors or explanatory variables.
- **Attested counter-inductive reopening.** Append-only filtration expansion downstream of an immutable cryptographic vault under custodian escrow ($\tau\_{\mathrm{reopen}} > 0$), contrasted with illicit opportunistic contraction (Feyerabend 1970; [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)).

### B

- **Baker–McHale shrinkage.** Finite-sample epistemic factor shrinking plug-in Kelly leverage under uncertain drift and known variance ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2; motivated by Baker & McHale 2013).
- **Bayes factor and deciban weight.** Conditional likelihood ratio $\mathrm{LR} = \mathbb{P}(E \mid H) / \mathbb{P}(E \mid \neg H)$ updating prior odds, expressed additively in logarithmic decibans (Good 1950).
- **Berkson's paradox.** Spurious negative correlation induced between two mutually independent causes when sampling, clinical admission, or administrative logging conditions on their shared downstream collider.
- **Binary lethal-omission impasse.** Discrete boundary under partial identification where abstaining crosses an absorbing barrier as surely as action, forbidding fractional probing (Manski 2026; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1).
- **Bitemporal vintage archive.** A documentary ledger separating valid world-event time $t\_{\mathrm{ev}}$ from archival availability time $t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$ ($t\_{\mathrm{ev}} \le t\_{\mathrm{disp}}$) so retrospective estimates never leak future revisions into ex-ante calibration (Croushore & Stark 2001).
- **Bruner's spiral curriculum.** Enactive-iconic-symbolic progression across Vygotsky's (1978) zone of proximal development, separated from the $10\text{ bits/s}$ conscious ceiling binding acute checks (Bruner 1960; Zheng & Meister 2025; [Appendix F §F.8](appendix-f-manuscript-audit.md#f8-disciplinary-entry-doors-spiral-depth-and-scope-boundaries)).
- **Bùkěshèng and kěshèng.** Strategic split between internal reserve readiness (*bùkěshèng*) and passive opportunism (*kěshèng*); defined in [Appendix E](appendix-e-lexicon.md#ancestral-and-planetary-lineage-terms) (Griffith 1963).
- **Byzantine quorum.** A fault-tolerant consensus protocol capable of reaching consistent distributed agreement across $n \ge 3f + 1$ replicas despite up to $f$ arbitrarily malicious or equivocating nodes (Lamport 1978; Castro & Liskov 1999).

### C

- **Calibration.** Agreement between issued probability forecasts and empirical conditional outcome frequencies across bins, isolated from discriminative sharpness by isotonic CORP-PAV decomposition (Dimitriadis, Gneiting, & Jordan 2021).
- **Canguilhem–Holling basin.** Constant-harvest attraction basin joined to Holling resilience width and Canguilhem's vital normativity; defined in [Appendix E §E.3](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e3-political-terms).
- **Canonical operators.** The three per-step decision rules governing real-time inference ($\mathcal{O}\_{\mathrm{epi}}$), physical survival ($\mathcal{O}\_{\mathrm{vital}}$), and institutional accountability ($\mathcal{O}\_{\mathrm{gov}}$), separated strictly from ex-post trajectory diagnosis.
- **Capture–recapture lower bound.** Nonparametric unseen-class estimators ($\mathrm{Chao1}$, $\mathrm{Chao2}$; Chao 1984; 1989) failing under heterogeneity, list dependence, or *Not askable* classes ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$).
- **CARE Principles and FPIC non-extraction boundary.** Indigenous community data sovereignty (*Collective Benefit*, *Authority to Control*, *Responsibility*, *Ethics*) paired with Free, Prior, and Informed Consent barring unconsented $P\_2$ archival seizure at `T6Performative` (Carroll et al. 2020; [Appendix F §F.8](appendix-f-manuscript-audit.md#f8-disciplinary-entry-doors-spiral-depth-and-scope-boundaries)).
- **Causal-conservative join-semilattice (`RFDE–CRDT`).** Replicated state-merge law (Shapiro et al. 2011) selecting the newer causal epoch, breaking concurrent ties conservatively by component-wise minimum, and growing spent linear authorization identifiers monotonically.
- **Central limit breakdown.** Failure of sample averages to stabilize rapidly around Gaussian distributions whenever population variance diverges ($\alpha \le 2$), serial persistence is long-range ($H > 0.5$), or intra-cluster correlation collapses $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}$.
- **Chamfered cognitive frontier.** Clipping of the upper corner of the viable triadic box $[\tau, 1]^3$ when finite human bandwidth ($C\_{\mathrm{human}}$ near $10\text{ bits/s}$) limits simultaneous real-time safeguard verification without altering the non-compensatory bottleneck metric (Zheng & Meister 2025).
- **Chouldechova trilemma.** The algebraic impossibility of simultaneously equalizing positive predictive value, false-positive rate, and false-negative rate across subpopulations whenever underlying base rates differ.
- **Circuit breaker.** An architectural switch that trips open to shed load, island coupled dependencies, or halt non-essential actuation before queue congestion or cascade variance destroys shared reserve capacity.
- **Clawback escrow.** Ex-post asset or bond forfeiture ($q > c$) reversing a replicator's calm-window divestment advantage whenever deliberate under-provisioning externalizes catastrophic losses.
- **Co-diagnostic pair ($\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$).** The retrospective tuple $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = (M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}})$ pairing the three-axis bottleneck minimum with minimum cross-perimeter reception tolerance, evaluated strictly ex post and never used as a per-step control law.
- **Collider bias.** Spurious conditional dependence created between unrelated exposures when an archive filters or stratifies observations by a common descendant event.
- **Common-cause failure ($\beta$-factor).** Correlated simultaneous breakdown of nominally redundant defense-in-depth layers driven by a shared physical, power, software, or constitutional dependency, bounding joint failure probability well above naive independent products.
- **Comparative proportionality.** Precautionary requirement that corrective intervention must neither omit countervailing substitute risks nor inflict expected harm exceeding the hazard it replaces (Sunstein 2003; 2005).
- **Competing risks.** Mutually exclusive failure or survival events whose joint dependence leaves marginal failure probabilities partially unidentified from observed time-to-first-event logs alone.
- **Compliance Denial of Service (*Compliance DoS*).** Adversarial injection of formally valid archival discrepancies or procedural permutations exceeding an institution's cognitive throughput channel ($\sim 10\text{ bits/s}$), triggering physical ruin through delayed verification.
- **Concave plug-in bias ($b\_{\min} \le 0$).** Downward finite-sample bias induced by Jensen's inequality when noisy coordinate estimates enter the concave minimum fold ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.6).
- **Conditional Value-at-Risk ($\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$ / Expected Shortfall).** Coherent subadditive upper-tail expectation above quantile $p \in (0, 1)$ for finite-mean loss laws ($\alpha > 1$; Artzner et al. 1999; Acerbi & Tasche 2002; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2).
- **Conjectural art (*stokhastikē tékhnē*).** Disciplined conjectural craft without guaranteed external outcome; see *stókhos* in [Appendix E §E.1](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e1-classical-terms).
- **Constituent vs. constituted power (*potentia* vs. *potestas*).** Immanent horizontal survival capacity vs. vertical command by decree; defined in [Appendix E §E.3](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e3-political-terms).
- **Contractual payout cap ($X \wedge K$) and unpaid overflow ($(X - K)\_+$).** Retention or liability truncation ($K > 0$, distinct from Hill cutoff $k$) that bounds recorded payer severity while keeping physical tail intensity intact and exporting unpaid overflow onto external reception perimeters ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.2; [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md), [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md), [Chapter 96](96-arrows-that-bend-the-strata.md)).
- **Controlled islanding.** Planned sacrificial throttling or severing of downward cross-stratum coupling links to restore acyclicity and break phase-locked cascade synchronization across dependent layers.
- **CORP-PAV decomposition.** Unique isotonic regression splitting binary score risk into miscalibration ($\mathrm{REL}$), resolution ($\mathrm{RES}$), and uncertainty (Dimitriadis, Gneiting, & Jordan 2021; [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3).
- **Counterfactual (*potential outcome*).** Unobserved state a unit would occupy under an alternative intervention assignment, forming the fundamental missing-data problem at the root of partial identification (Manski 1990).
- **Counter-induction.** Formulating hypotheses incompatible with entrenched theories so suppressed anomalies surface; defined in [Appendix E §E.1](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e1-classical-terms) (Feyerabend 1970).
- **Cramér–Rao bound and Rao–Blackwell conditioning.** Fisher-information variance floor and variance-non-increasing conditioning on sufficient statistics, foundational to Kolkata's statistical vanguard (Rao 1945).
- **Critical slowing down.** Divergence of recovery time and rising variance or lag-one autocorrelation as a smooth dynamical system approaches a saddle-node tipping point, failing when discontinuous Lévy jumps bypass smooth precursors.
- **Cromwell's rule.** Bayesian regularity discipline bounding prior and posterior probabilities inside the open simplex away from $0$ and $1$ so no finite update turns un-revisable (Lindley 1985).
- **Cyclic downward causation.** Feedback whereby high-stratum financial, legal, or bureaucratic incentives ($S\_6$–$S\_7$) erode physical buffers and documentary fidelity in lower strata ($S\_1$–$S\_3$), overturning static acyclic DAG evaluations.

### D

- **Decisive link.** The single structural bottleneck or leverage transition whose stabilization at the fleeting *kairós* prevents systemic fracture across a whole causal chain without attempting exhaustive simultaneous surveillance.
- **Defense in depth.** Layered architectural barrier stacking, effective only against independent hazards and defeated whenever common-cause coupling or systemic risk compression erodes every layer together.
- **Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN).** Store-carry-forward custody retaining disconnected telemetry, out-of-band $S3^\ast$ alerts, and `HMAC-SHA256` trails inside $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}$ while honoring `FPIC` consent (Fall 2003).
- **Design effect ($\mathrm{DEFF}$).** Variance inflation from intra-cluster correlation and unequal weights; under independence, the exact composition is additive ([Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.1; Kish 1965).
- **Diagnostic interference matrix ($\Lambda\_{ab}$).** Strictly *ex post* cross-plane and boundary sensitivity array forming $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ under Reflexive Brake 4 ([Appendix A §A.7](appendix-a-mathematics.md#a7-reception-side-margin--the-co-diagnostic-pair-not-a-fourth-axis)).
- **Differential privacy ($\varepsilon$-DP and $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-DP).** Randomized release bounds isolating supplementary public analytical mirrors from immutable primary custody needed for record linkage and $\rho\_{R,Y}$ audits (Dwork et al. 2006a; Dwork et al. 2006b).
- **Discursive dilemma.** Majority voting on interconnected premises yielding conclusions rejected by direct vote on conclusions; defined in [Appendix E §E.3](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e3-political-terms) (Pettit 2001).
- **Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO).** Worst-case optimization over a Wasserstein or $\phi$-divergence ambiguity ball centered at the observed empirical distribution $\hat{P}\_n$, failing when `T3` selection truncates support or `T6` performativity shifts $D(\theta)$ outside the ball (Mohajerin Esfahani & Kuhn 2018; Duchi & Namkoong 2021).
- **Do-calculus ($\mathrm{do}(x)$).** Structural causal intervention operator replacing an upstream mechanism by a fixed policy assignment, separating causal transport from passive observational conditioning.
- **Door bias ($b\_{\mathrm{door}}$).** Systematic error embedded inside an institutional archive's recording rule, unaffected by increasing panel size ($J \to \infty$) or aggregating more noisy human judges.
- **Double bind ($\mathrm{DB}\_t$).** Coercive proxy pressure, testimonial silencing, and disabled safety veto closing all compliant paths; defined in [Appendix E §E.3](appendix-e-lexicon.md#e3-political-terms) and [Appendix A](appendix-a-mathematics.md) §A.3.
- **Duhem–Quine underdetermination.** Impossibility of testing a focal substantive hypothesis in complete isolation from auxiliary measurement, boundary, and background assumptions (Duhem 1906; Quine 1951).

### E

- **Ecological fallacy.** Invalid inference of individual-level relationships or causal effects from aggregated cluster or regional summary statistics.
- **Eliminatio codicum descriptorum.** Textual-criticism rule assigning zero independent evidential weight to any manuscript witness wholly copied from an extant or independently reconstructible exemplar before stemmatic collation begins (Maas 1958).
- **Ephemeral threshold governance (`FROST` and Feldman VSS).** Two-round threshold Schnorr signing paired with verifiable secret sharing using durable single-use nonces and peer fault verification (Feldman 1987; Komlo & Goldberg 2021).
- **Epistemic injustice.** Wrong done to an agent specifically in their capacity as a knower through status-based **testimonial discounting** or **hermeneutical gaps** in shared interpretive categories (Fricker 2007).
