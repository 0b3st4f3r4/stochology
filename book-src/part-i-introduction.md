*From the archer's interval opened in [the Prologue](00-preface.md), Part I moves to everyday choices where action must come before certainty—stating once what these eight chapters try to show, and in what order. It argues nothing the chapters do not argue; it simply points out where to look.*

## The case the part takes seriously

The part begins with a choice that cannot wait. Acting under uncertainty is not a puzzle where all the odds sit neatly in a textbook and only arithmetic remains. It is the situation where you have to decide *before* the facts finish arriving—and where the choice you make alters the world you will later observe. Everything the rest of the book builds follows from taking that sentence seriously.

[Chapter 01](01-deciding-without-knowing.md) sets the loop in motion: a real **World** where storms and hazards unfold, an **Archive** that records only part of them, an **Institution** that decides across split incentives, and an outcome that reshapes tomorrow's records. That closed loop cannot be judged one isolated step at a time without losing what makes it a loop.

## Three comforts the part removes

The next three chapters take away three comforting habits that make decisions look easier than they are.

[Chapter 02](02-the-average-is-not-the-story.md) separates the average from the story it is asked to tell. An average hides spread and extreme hits, while real harm often curves sharply upward when things go badly. [Chapter 03](03-the-biggest-number-in-the-room.md) separates the biggest flood or fire in your logbook from what physical survival demands: a short, quiet record proves almost nothing about rare shocks, and survival depends on holding reserves that outlast recovery. [Chapter 04](04-it-worked-so-i-was-right.md) separates how carefully someone aimed from whether things worked out. Calm weather and missing failures can make reckless luck look like genius, while rare bad luck can punish sound preparation.

Each of these three chapters tests what a record of past results can really prove. Together they show that a ledger of outcomes is not yet a record of good judgment.

## From belief to table

The following three chapters move from personal beliefs to the tables and scorecards institutions rely on.

[Chapter 05](05-what-a-number-doesnt-say.md) asks what a bare number hides: uncalibrated dials behind crisp decimal places, rare-condition false alarms hidden inside alarming percentages, and the margin of uncertainty missing from point claims. [Chapter 06](06-who-is-missing-from-the-table.md) asks who never made it onto the spreadsheet at all—separating unmeasured blank cells from true zeros and from realities the form never even had a column for—and maps the closed three-arena loop across World, Archive, and Institution. [Chapter 07](07-when-the-measure-becomes-the-goal.md) asks what happens once people know they are graded by a number: targets warp behavior, break proxies, and reshape tomorrow's records.

Three chapters, one lesson: every number is a claim about a population, filtered through a record, inside a world that reacts to being measured.

## The claim the part establishes

[Chapter 08](08-three-things-you-cant-trade.md) gathers the whole opening argument into its simplest form. A sound decision under uncertainty needs three things—honest evidence, physical survival slack, and accountable authority—and none of them can buy the others. Perfect forecasts cannot rescue a plant with an empty emergency tank; huge stockpiles cannot fix a biased count; fair votes cannot replace clean water. Because those three safeguards do not trade, the whole is set by its weakest link—the **minimum** rather than a weighted average—and that minimum serves strictly as an after-the-fact audit of what failed, never a live scorecard to chase.

That is all Part I asks you to carry, using concrete stories, whole-number examples, and elementary algebra—comparing action costs against avoided losses, checking skewed spreads, timing reserves against repair delays, counting base rates with natural frequencies, spotting missing rows, and watching targets backfire. Readers looking for formal derivations are pointed forward on purpose: Part II supplies the mathematical, philosophical, and natural-science bases, Part III builds the formal foundations, and Part IV runs the sequential framework.

## What the part defers

Three things are left open by design.

First, the three safeguards in Part I are drawn on simple illustrative scales rather than calibrated across planes. Calibrating cross-plane measures, running the full seven-guard-site circuit across World, Archive, and Institution ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) and [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md)), and binding authority to those who bear downstream harm—opened in [the Prologue](00-preface.md)—belong to Parts III and IV.

Second, formal axioms, measure-theoretic probability spaces, and coupled system equations stay deferred to Parts II–IV. Holding back the full formal machinery is deliberate: handing out formulas before the reader sees the circuit turns tools into thoughtless recipes, and turning a number into a rule of thumb is the very trap [Chapter 07](07-when-the-measure-becomes-the-goal.md) warns against.

Third, no final policy rule is settled here. Part I clears away false assumptions and marks the minimum safeguards to protect; building full instruments comes after grounding what records, living bodies, and institutions can actually bear.
