# Part II — Conclusion

*This page states what the eighteen chapters supply, and what the part deliberately does not conclude. It adds no claim of its own.*

## What the part has supplied

The part supplies foundational tools without assembling a decision rule. Each chapter remains inside its single-domain scope, leaving cross-plane synthesis to Part III.

From the formal group ([Chapter 09](09-sets-relations-functions.md)–[Chapter 15](15-probability-as-a-measure.md)): set algebras, partial orders, finite-precision outward bounds, non-ergodic path limits, curvature and convexity gaps, coherent Expected Shortfall and bounded-support expectation brackets, linear operators, null kernels, and orthonormal or spectral invariants, and measure-theoretic conditioning under reactivity.

From epistemology, ethics, and institutions ([Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md)–[Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)): warranted knowledge beyond Gettier luck, paraconsistent containment and inductive weight beyond past runs, scientific severity under fallibilism, contractualist limits on non-reciprocal risk, and contestable, non-externalizing legitimacy.

From the natural sciences ([Chapter 21](21-mechanics-determinism-chaos.md)–[Chapter 26](26-regulation-and-homeostasis.md)): finite chaotic horizons, dissipative ceilings, non-ergodic trajectories and ecological attraction basins, finite-band buffers, evolutionary selection differentials with requisite variety, and delay-bound homeostasis, allostatic wear, and queueing survival clocks.

## Why the objects do not combine by themselves

The temptation at the end of a part like this is to combine. Bounds, curvature, measure, warrant, precaution, entropy, buffers, selection, and feedback sit on the table, and the next move is to weight them.

That move is not available. A bound answers "how far can this be from the truth"; a buffer answers "how long can this run before it stops"; a legitimacy rule answers "who may decide".

No exchange rate exists because no two share a kind. A weighted sum needs a common unit, and the only unit available, a preference, is what each was built to constrain.

Part III therefore joins the non-substitutable coordinates by a minimum rather than an exchange rate. The minimum is not a weighted sum, nor a concession to caution or an online control rule. As [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) proves, unlike a product of coordinates that contracts the balanced threshold box while still allowing surplus on two axes to subsidize weakness on the third, the minimum preserves each threshold independently without trading one axis's collapse for another's surplus, leaving bounded interior slack substitution open to pre-registered testing in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md).

## What the part does not conclude

It does not conclude that the framework holds. Part II tests nothing of the triad; it shows only that its materials exist and hold in their domains.

It does not conclude that three is the right number: that count belongs to the circuit in Part III, not to mathematics or biology.

Nor does it conclude that epistemic or physical margins suffice. [Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md) and [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) show that a careful, buffered arrangement stays illegitimate when it imposes irreversible harm bearers could reasonably reject, denies contestable, rank-blind voice free of status discounting, or exports deficits onto unrepresented external reception perimeters, even where literal unanimous veto is unavailable.

That failure is not an isolated defect of one coordinate among peers. The governance coordinate is not one reading among three: as [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) and [Chapter 25](25-evolution-variation-selection.md) show, it fixes the sign of the competitive selection differential pricing the other margins, so its collapse hollows out the other margins in calm years before shocks arrive.
