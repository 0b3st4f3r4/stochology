# Part II — Conclusion

*This page states what the eighteen chapters supply, and what the part deliberately does not conclude. It adds no claim of its own.*

## What the part has supplied

The part supplies objects, one per chapter. That is the discipline it imposes on itself. A base chapter that
supplied two objects would leave the reader unable to say which one the framework later borrowed.

From the formal group: the distinction between relation and function ([Chapter 09](09-sets-relations-functions.md)); order and bounds ([Chapter 10](10-numbers-order-bounds.md)); limits ([Chapter 11](11-sequences-limits-continuity.md)); convexity and the asymmetry it produces ([Chapter 12](12-derivatives-and-convexity.md)); accumulation ([Chapter 13](13-integrals-and-accumulation.md)); linear maps and what a change of basis preserves ([Chapter 14](14-linear-algebra.md)); and measure ([Chapter 15](15-probability-as-a-measure.md)).

From the second group: the conditions under which a true belief is knowledge ([Chapter 16](16-what-knowledge-is.md)); the structure of inductive support and its limits ([Chapter 17](17-justification-and-induction.md)); the way a scientific claim is exposed to refutation and revised ([Chapter 18](18-philosophy-of-science.md)); the ethics of imposing a risk on a party who did not choose it ([Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)); and the difference between an institution's legitimacy and its performance ([Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md)).

From the third group: finite horizons under sensitive dependence ([Chapter 21](21-mechanics-determinism-chaos.md)); entropy ([Chapter 22](22-thermodynamics-and-entropy.md)); the statistical arrow of time ([Chapter 23](23-statistical-physics-and-time.md)); rates, equilibrium and buffers ([Chapter 24](24-chemical-rates-equilibrium-buffers.md)); variation and selection ([Chapter 25](25-evolution-variation-selection.md)); and feedback with homeostasis ([Chapter 26](26-regulation-and-homeostasis.md)).

## Why the objects do not combine by themselves

The temptation at the end of a part like this is to combine. Bounds, convexity, measure, warrant, consent,
entropy, buffers, selection and feedback are all on the table, and the obvious next move is to weight them.

The part concludes that the move is not available. Each object was built for a different question. A bound
answers "how far can this be from the truth". A buffer answers "how long can this run before it stops". A
legitimacy rule answers "who may decide".

No exchange rate exists between them, because no two are quantities of the same kind. A weighted sum would
need a common unit, and the only unit available, a preference, is what each of the three was introduced to
constrain.

The book's combination is therefore a minimum and not a sum. The minimum is not a concession to caution. It is
the only operation the objects admit.

## What the part does not conclude

It does not conclude that the framework is correct. Nothing in Part II tests the triad. The part establishes
that the materials for it exist, and that each is sound in its own domain.

It does not conclude that three is the right number. The count is not a fact about mathematics or about
biology. It is a fact about the circuit, and the circuit is stated in Part III.

Nor does it conclude that a well-built margin is sufficient. [Chapter 19](19-ethics-and-the-imposition-of-risk.md)
and [Chapter 20](20-legitimacy-and-institutions.md) both show the opposite: an arrangement can be epistemically
careful and physically robust, and still illegitimate, because the party who bears the risk never agreed to it.

That possibility is not a defect of the framework. It is one of the three axes, and it is why the minimum can
sit at zero while two coordinates are high.

## What the next part does

Part III assembles. It begins with what judging is and is not ([Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md)), sets the
machine against it ([Chapter 28](28-the-machine-and-the-deception.md)), and then states the circuit and its
guard sites ([Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md)).

Everything the bases supplied is spent there. The bounds become the epistemic margin, the buffers and horizons
become the vital margin, the ethics and the institutional analysis become the governance coordinate. The
minimum is stated at the end of that part, in [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md), and it is stated
as a diagnostic, not a control law.
