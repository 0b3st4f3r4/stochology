# Part III — Conclusion

*This page states what Part III has established, and what it deliberately leaves as a limit. It adds no claim of its own.*

## What the fifteen chapters established

The circuit closes across three non-substitutable safeguards. Grounded in classical *stókhos*, [Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md) through [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) separate internal aim (*télos*) from external hit (*skopós*), deny independent standing to machine compressions, and fix $n = P(P - 1) + 1 = 3 \cdot 2 + 1 = 7$ guard sites across the $P = 3$ planes of World, Archive, and Institution. On that circuit, [Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md) through [Chapter 35](35-inference-and-evidence.md) (built on [Chapter 30](30-probability-as-language.md)'s probability language) assemble the **epistemic axis**, combining pre-data openness ($\mathrm{PRI}$), non-statistical Type B floors, additive design-effect discounting, anytime-valid *e*-processes, and Manski partial-identification bands.

The **vital axis** ([Chapter 31](31-distribution-and-moment.md) on heavy-tail regimes, $\mathrm{CRPS}$, and $\Delta\_J$, joined with [Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md)) tracks non-ergodic ruin through the survival inequality $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}$, while [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md) and [Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md) supply cross-axis decision-threshold, calibration, and structural model-discrepancy bridges. [Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md) and [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) link world and institution via causal invariance ($\mathrm{ICP}$), zero testimonial discounting ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$), sunsetted algedonic channels, and the **governance axis**, whose constitutional floor fixes the sign of the selection differential and is **not one reading among three**.

These three safeguards combine without compensation. The minimum is not a weighted sum. [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) pairs $M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S\_{\mathrm{epi}}, S\_{\mathrm{vital}}, S\_{\mathrm{gov}})$ with $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ in the co-diagnostic pair $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = \bigl(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}\bigr)$. It separates chart-conditional viable volumes $V\_{\min}(\tau) = (1 - \tau)^3$ and $V\_{\mathrm{prod}}(\tau)$, requires ordinal level comparability ($\mathrm{OLC}$) for cross-axis ranking, isolates the three collapse propositions, and grounds the four reflexive brakes before handing falsifiers $F\_1$–$F\_5$ to [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md).

$\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ stays strictly *ex post* and diagnostic, never a control law. Both components fold from a finished trajectory, leaving live step action solely to the three-regime operator over pre-action margins.

## The result the part is most careful about

The part's deepest distinction is not the minimum itself. It is separating pre-outcome step acceptance and action from the retrospective fold that diagnoses afterward.

A five-stage syntactic predicate classifies each step (`Interior`, `Boundary`, `Collapse`, or `Unparsable` quarantine) over pre-action sealed margins, invariant to the contingency tape and distinct from both the three-regime live operator (including Safe Abstention) and the *ex-post* trajectory fold $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$. Conflating syntactic acceptance, per-step operation, and retrospective diagnosis breaks contingency invariance, five-stage non-commutation, or the constitutional separation between live operation and *ex-post* diagnosis.

Contingency-tape invariance and five-stage non-commutation are proved in [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md), while [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) pairs that proof with the prefix, contingency, and seal transcript tests (logging attested forward declarations strictly inside the seal test).

## What the part leaves as a limit

Part III does not prove that the circuit's transits exhaust every feature of reality. Given three distinct planes, six ordered crossings, and integral closure in [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md), the count of seven guard sites and thirty-five triads follows conditionally from those premises rather than an empirical census.

The part also leaves open the scale of application: a single act, a trajectory, and an institutional history read the three axes differently, and Part IV studies the middle scale, a sequential trajectory. Across all scales, indivisible binary impasses ($a \in \lbrace{}0, 1\rbrace$) and uncompensated strain tracked through $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$ still demand explicit institutional settlement rather than continuous probing.
