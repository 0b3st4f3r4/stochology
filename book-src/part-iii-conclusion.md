# Part III — Conclusion

*This page states what Part III has established, and what it deliberately leaves as a limit. It adds no claim of its own.*

## What the fifteen chapters established

The circuit is closed. [Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) states three planes with ordered crossings and a closure, and derives the guard sites from the transits. The consequence is that no plane can be analysed in isolation: an archive is an archive of a world, selected by an institution, and the institution reads the archive it produced.

The three axes are distinct quantities. The epistemic axis is built in [Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md) through [Chapter 35](35-inference-and-evidence.md) from framing and identification, not from confidence, with [Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md) and [Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md) pricing decision thresholds and structural discrepancy. The vital axis is built in [Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md) from path dependence, absorbing barriers, and irreversible time, not from the width of a distribution, with [Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md) separating passive observation from intervention and counterfactual invariance. The governance axis is built in [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) from the sign of the differential, not from a third score.

The combination is a minimum, and the minimum is not an aggregate. [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) states this as an identity: the aggregate that trades one axis against another is not a weaker version of the minimum, it is a different object, and the difference is what the three collapse propositions describe.

The verdict is diagnostic, not a control law. The triad is folded from a finished trajectory; acting is done by the per-step operator. Any text that turns the diagnosis into a decision rule has broken the clause, and the contract now checks for it.

The reflexive brakes are stated. [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) names the four reflexive brakes and the prefix, contingency and seal tests that expose the diagnostic separation on the circuit's own records, and hands the four pre-registered empirical falsifiers and their severity bar to [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md).

## The result the part is most careful about

The part's most careful result is not the minimum. It is the separation between two folds that share a name.

There is a fold that accepts or rejects a single step, computed over margins that are sealed before the step is taken. There is a fold that diagnoses a trajectory, computed after the trajectory has ended. The first is invariant to the contingency tape; the second is a function of realized outcomes and cannot be. Identifying them would contradict the non-commutation property, and it would also make the diagnostic into a control law by the back door.

The separation is stated in [Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) and in Appendix B, and the contract checks that the two are not conflated.

## What the part leaves as a limit

The part does not show that the circuit's inventory of transits is complete in the world. It shows that, given three planes and ordered crossings between distinct planes plus the closure, the count is seven and the triples are thirty-five. The premise is stated where the circuit is stated, and the check in the contract reads the artifact rather than the world.

The part also does not settle the level at which the framework is applied. A single decision, a trajectory of decisions and an institution's history are three objects, and the axes are read differently at each level. The next part takes the middle one.

## What the next part does

[Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md) states the loop and the naive optimizer that ignores it; the three filters then operationalize the three axes in sequence — the epistemic filter from world to archive in [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md) through [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md), the vital filter in [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md) and [Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md), the governance filter in [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md) and [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md), and the methodological synthesis and reflexive audit in [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md).

What Part IV adds is not a new axis. It adds the case, and with it the demonstration that the three filters compose into one operator that acts per step.
