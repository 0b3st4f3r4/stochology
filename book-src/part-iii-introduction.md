# Part III — Introduction

*This page states once what the fifteen chapters of Part III assemble, and in what order. It argues nothing the chapters do not argue.*

## The part where the framework is built

Parts I and II left two things standing: a thesis that three things must be carried and do not trade, and a stock of objects — bounds, convexity, measure, warrant, consent, entropy, buffers, selection, feedback. Part III spends both. It states the circuit, derives the three axes from it, fixes the guard sites that watch it, and closes on the minimum.

Fifteen chapters carry the transition from the stock of objects in Part II to the operational loop of Part IV.

## Judging, and its contrast

[Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md) says what judging is. Not the calculation of an optimum from a known distribution: the act of committing to a claim before the outcome is available, under conditions in which the commitment can be wrong. [Chapter 28](28-the-machine-and-the-deception.md) sets the machine against it — a system that predicts well and is deceived anyway, because its fit is to a record its own actions have shaped.

The pair fixes the terms everything else is stated in: a judgment is an act, an act has an author, and an author can be wrong in ways the record cannot show.

## The circuit and the guard sites

[Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) states the circuit. Three planes — world, archive, institution — with ordered crossings between them, and a closure that returns the whole to itself. The guard sites come out of the transits: every transit is a place the circuit can be cut, and the closure is itself a guarded object.

[Chapter 30](30-probability-as-language.md) supplies the language the archive speaks in, and [Chapter 31](31-distribution-and-moment.md) the objects it speaks about. Both are about grammar, not about truth. A probability statement is a sentence with a subject; a distribution is a claim about a population the sentence has already selected.

## From the world to the archive

[Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md) puts the question before the data, because a question asked after the data is a question the data has already answered. [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md) separates the error a measurement can estimate from the error it cannot. [Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md) states the relation between a sample, its population and the archive that holds it, and [Chapter 35](35-inference-and-evidence.md) turns that relation into an account of what evidence supports.

Four chapters, and the epistemic axis in construction. What they produce is not a confidence interval. It is a statement about what the archive can and cannot identify.

## Decision, model, time, cause

[Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md) states the decision problem with its loss, its thresholds, and minimax regret. [Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md) separates sample fit from structural model discrepancy. [Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md) introduces dependence, absorbing barriers, and irreversible paths — where the vital axis takes shape once the record is a path rather than a sample. [Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md) establishes intervention, counterfactuals, and the causal invariance required before entering the institutional plane.

With the vital path in view alongside the evidential limits, the question is no longer only what is true or how fit a model looks, but how long the arrangement can continue under irreversible dependence — and what mechanism intervention changes.

## The institution, and the minimum

[Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) states the institutional plane: incentives, metrics, limited liability, and the way a metric is read by the party it measures. Its load-bearing claim is that the governance coordinate is not one reading among three; it fixes the sign of the differential on which the other two are priced.

[Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) then states the minimum, the three collapse propositions, the four reflexive brakes, and the three regimes of action (leaving the four pre-registered falsifiers to [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)). It also marks the boundary the rest of the book respects: the triad is a diagnostic folded from a finished trajectory, and acting is done by the per-step operator, never by the aggregate.
