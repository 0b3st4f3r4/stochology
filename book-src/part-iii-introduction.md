*This page states once what the fifteen chapters of Part III assemble, and in what order. It argues nothing the chapters do not argue.*

## The part where the framework is built

Parts I and II left two things standing. They established a thesis, that three distinct safeguards must hold without trading off, and supplied a toolkit of mathematical, philosophical, and scientific concepts: bounds, convexity, measure, warrant, consent, entropy, buffers, selection, and feedback.

Part III puts both to work. Over fifteen chapters, it builds the mediation circuit, assembles the three safeguards, fixes the seven guard sites, and closes on a retrospective diagnostic pair that judges completed trajectories without steering live moves.

## Judging, and its contrast

[Chapter 27](27-what-judging-is.md) recovers *stókhos*: reasoned aim before an outcome arrives. Drawing on Plato, Hippocratic medicine, Galen, Antipater, Sophocles, and parallel traditions across continents, it separates human judgment from routine measurement or hindsight optimization.

[Chapter 28](28-the-machine-and-the-deception.md) sets predictive machines against that standard. A statistical or simulation model compresses a historical archive or closed ruleset. Without world-grounding or personal liability, it becomes vulnerable to fluent wrongness, metric capture, authority laundering, and confident extrapolation.

Together, these two chapters set the terms of Part III: accountable judgment belongs to an author who answers for consequences, never to a machine.

## The circuit and the guard sites

[Chapter 29](29-aim-and-the-three-planes.md) unveils the full mediation circuit across three planes: World, Archive, and Institution. Six ordered crossings between distinct planes plus one integral closure fix $n = P(P - 1) + 1 = 7$ guard sites, partitioned three, two, and two across the safeguards.

[Chapter 30](30-probability-as-language.md) builds the archive's plural language of probability, measurement scales, and anytime-valid evidence accumulation (*e*-processes). [Chapter 31](31-distribution-and-moment.md) maps heavy-tailed risk across four moment-existence regimes, explaining tail estimators, proper scoring, Jensen's convexity gap, and competing-risk bounds.

## From the world to the archive

[Chapter 32](32-the-question-before-the-data.md) examines how questions are framed before data arrive, guarding against Type III framing errors and post-hoc cherry-picking. [Chapter 33](33-measuring-and-erring.md) separates statistical scatter from unshrinkable non-statistical uncertainty floors, protects scale invariance, and distinguishes true zero from missingness.

[Chapter 34](34-sample-population-archive.md) traces how selection bias, clustering, and uneven weights deflate large datasets into small effective samples, while bounding unseen records via capture–recapture. [Chapter 35](35-inference-and-evidence.md) turns incomplete archives into honest partial-identification intervals, sensitivity bounds, and individualized evidential weight.

Together, these four chapters construct the **epistemic axis**. Rather than forcing fake point certainty, they publish bounded intervals showing exactly what the archive can and cannot identify.

## Decision, model, time, cause

[Chapter 36](36-decision-loss-threshold.md) connects intervals to action through asymmetric loss thresholds, live cognitive alert limits, and reversible minimax-regret probing. [Chapter 37](37-model-fit-and-error.md) separates estimation noise, data leakage, performative drift, and structural model discrepancy from apparent in-sample fit.

[Chapter 38](38-time-and-dependence.md) turns to irreversible time, building the **vital axis** around non-ergodicity, absorbing ruin barriers, queueing congestion, and the survival-versus-recovery clock. [Chapter 39](39-cause-intervention-counterfactual.md) separates correlation from causal intervention and counterfactual responsibility, while banning status discounting of frontline warnings before entering institutional design.

With time horizons beside evidential limits, inquiry asks not just what fits past rows, but what survives a shock and whose warning is heard.

## The institution, and the minimum

[Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md) builds the institutional safeguard: aligning liability with power, protecting metric integrity, keeping frontline distress channels open, and sealing commitments before outcomes unfold. Its load-bearing claim is that the governance coordinate is not one reading among three; it fixes the sign of the competitive differential on which the other two are priced.

[Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) brings all fifteen chapters together in a co-diagnostic pair, uniting the non-compensatory Leontief minimum with the reception-basin fold for external spillover. It states the three collapse propositions, defends ordinal comparability across axes, sets four reflexive brakes against gaming, and partitions per-step action into three ordered regimes, leaving the five empirical falsifiers to [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md). Throughout, the diagnostic verdict remains strictly retrospective; per-step action belongs solely to the canonical operator.
