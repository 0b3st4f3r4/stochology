# Part IV — Conclusion

*This page states what Part IV has shown, and what it hands to Part V. It adds no claim of its own.*

## What the nine chapters showed

The framework can be run. That is the part's result, and the only result a part of this kind can claim. Not that the framework is true. That it is operational: each axis becomes a step-level operator with explicit inputs, outputs, and failure limits.

Across *The Community and the Reserve* ([Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md)), Part IV turned those three margins into working per-step safeguards:

- **Filter I ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$; [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)–[Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md)):** returns a non-collapsing interval and evidential weight $V$ under open framing, non-parametric partial-identification bounds, selection correlation $\rho\_{R,Y}$ (recording indicator $R$, distinct from physical reserve $R\_{t+1} \le 0$ below), additive design effects, and bitemporal vintages.
- **Filter II ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$; [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md)–[Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md)):** returns a shrunk leverage multiplier $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}})\in(0,1)$ inside the rational corridor $(0,f^\ast]$ and survival margin $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}}\ge\mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$ above the absorbing ruin barrier ($R\_{t+1}\le 0$).
- **Filter III ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$; [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md)–[Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)):** enforces calibration, causal invariance, performative stability, undiscounted frontline alarms ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}}=0$), and pre-sealed custody vaults.
- **Reflexive Synthesis ([Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md)):** closes the loop across five methodological families, four Reflexive Brakes, and five pre-registered falsifiers ($F\_1$–$F\_5$).

## The composition, stated once

The three Canonical Operators act together at every step across lexicographic regimes. **Governance fixes the institutional terms (the sign of the differential $q - c$, internalized regulatory charge minus reserve upkeep cost; [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md)) on which the epistemic and vital margins are audited, and is not one reading among three.** Their outputs (bounded identification, vital survival time, and gated authorization) are incommensurable and never traded off in a weighted sum.

The strictly retrospective **co-diagnostic pair** $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}=(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}},\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}})$ folds from a finished trajectory to identify which axis failed or whether deficits spilled across external reception perimeters via $\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}}$. It is never a control law and never drives live action.

## What remains unresolved

Two boundaries stay open at the close of Part IV.

The first is institutional distinguishability and cross-scale coupling. Single-episode vaults cannot diagnose conduct when pre-seal protocols are underspecified (as at Macondo; [Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md)) or formally non-discriminating ($\mathrm{LR}=1$), nor when coupled units shift losses across mandates and perimeters.

The second is empirical self-application and cone validation. Falsifier $F\_4$ leaves open whether doubled slack partly buffers moderate barrier-free shocks, pre-committed cutoffs need external anchoring and `T1` reopening, and multi-layer dependency cones await domain-by-domain testing.

It closes in [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md), whose ancestor cone covers twenty-eight of the thirty-five orders and bounds from above the cumulative retrospective minimum across all five terminal cones, recovering $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}=(M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}},\Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}})$ alongside the square-root harvest margin derived from Schaefer's (1954) logistic model.
