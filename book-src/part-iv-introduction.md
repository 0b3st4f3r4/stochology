*This page states once what the nine chapters of Part IV do, and why the part is organized as a loop, three operators, and a synthesis. It argues nothing the chapters do not argue.*

## From the framework to a case

Part III built the three-plane circuit, the seven guard sites, and the Nuclear Triad. A framework that stops at static blueprints stays untested; Part IV puts the machinery to work step by step.

Throughout these nine chapters, we follow one running institutional case, *The Community and the Reserve*, alongside real-world empirical archives. The case is not a closing decoration. It is the living trajectory on which every operator acts.

## How the architecture runs across the part

[Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md) supplies the unfolding trajectory that the remaining chapters govern: a world with heavy-tailed demand above an absorbing reserve barrier ($R\_{t+1} \le 0$, physical reserve stock distinct from recording indicator $R$ in $\rho\_{R,Y}$ and ex-post hoarding ceiling $R^\ast$), filtered by a biased archive and steered by an institution under proxy pressure. Only strictly reversible choices under hard budgets and zero external spillover can safely skip idle slack.

Across **Filter I ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$; [Chapters 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md)–[45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md))**, **Canonical Operator I** keeps framing open against premise rigging ($\mathrm{PRI}$) and $e\text{-BH}$ multiplicity, replaces invented points with anytime-valid *e*-processes and sharp non-parametric bounds, separates variance-adjusted ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$) from bias-penalized counts ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$) and evidential weight $V$, and tracks *Not observed* (`Chao2`) separately from *Not askable* ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$) exclusions across bitemporal vintages.

Across **Filter II ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$; [Chapters 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md)–[47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md))**, **Canonical Operator II** enforces Stevens scale invariance, convex shortfall gaps ($\Delta\_J$), proper $\mathrm{CRPS}$ scoring, and coherent $\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$, then pairs Itô variance drain and finite-sample leverage shrinkage $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) \in (0, 1)$ with Kingman's utilization wall and global Wald Maximin across Filter I's admissible set so survival runway clears recovery latency ($\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$).

Across **Filter III ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$; [Chapters 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md)–[49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md))**, **Canonical Operator III** closes three sequential gates: out-of-fold CORP-PAV calibration, causal invariance, Kennedy–O'Hagan discrepancy, and Perdomo performative contractivity (sensitivity $|\varepsilon| < \gamma/L\_y = 1 + \lambda$ in the linear-quadratic model with strong convexity $\gamma$, mixed gradient sensitivity $L\_y = 1$, and penalty $\lambda$, distinct from Perdomo et al.'s general sufficient bound $\gamma/\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}}$ and proxy pressure $\beta\_{\mathrm{proxy}}$) in Gate 1; List–Pettit conjunctive vetoes and zero-discount frontline alarms ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) sized for bounded human attention in Gate 2; and Chouldechova parity audits paired with bitemporal cryptographic vaults (exposing $\mathrm{LR} = 1$ or Macondo-style unspecified protocols) in Gate 3.

Finally, [Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) unites quantitative models with qualitative process tracing across five methodological families, applies four Reflexive Brakes (including the ex-post hoarding ceiling $R^\ast$, distinct from physical reserve $R\_t$) and Leontief box geometry, and pre-registers five empirical falsifiers ($F\_1$–$F\_5$).

## How the filters compose

The three filters operate jointly on every shift rather than as a one-pass assembly line. Epistemic, vital, and governance operators constrain each step together under lexicographic priority, while governance fixes the institutional terms ($q - c$, internalized regulatory charge minus reserve upkeep cost, [Chapter 40](40-institution-incentive-metric.md); distinct from Lee's trimming fraction $q$ in [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)) on which margins are audited.

Notice that the running case looks worse before it stabilizes. Filter I widens narrow answers into intervals, Filter II proves that knowing the interval does not buy survival time, and Filter III shows that bought time can still be captured or spilled onto neighbors. Live action stays strictly with the per-step operators, while the **co-diagnostic pair** $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = (M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}})$ folds ex post to identify which margin or external reception perimeter failed.
