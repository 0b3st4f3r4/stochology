*This page states once what the nine chapters of Part IV do, and why the part is organised as a loop, three operators, and a synthesis. It argues nothing the chapters do not argue.*

## From the framework to a case

Part III built the three-plane circuit, the seven guard sites, and the Nuclear Triad. A framework that stops at static blueprints stays untested; Part IV puts the machinery to work step by step.

Throughout these nine chapters, we follow one running institutional case, *The Community and the Reserve*, alongside real-world empirical archives. The case is not a closing decoration. It is the living trajectory on which every operator acts.

## The loop

[Chapter 42](42-sequential-loop-naive-agent.md) sets the stage by contrasting a textbook Markov decision process with a physical community reserve. A naive point optimizer treats a biased, clustered archive as representative, ignores convex surge losses and gaming incentives, and runs with no safety buffer above an absorbing ruin barrier ($R\_{t+1} \le 0$). Only strictly reversible choices under hard budgets—with zero external spillover—can safely skip idle slack.

That opening chapter supplies the object the rest of Part IV acts on: an unfolding **trajectory**, not an isolated one-shot bet.

## Filter I: from world to archive ($S\_\{\mathrm{epi}}$)

Across three chapters, the epistemic axis becomes **Canonical Operator I**. First, [Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md) guards how questions are framed before data is read ($T\_1$). It enforces Cromwell's non-zero probability floor, contrasts institutional versus affected-community cost–loss thresholds, separates severe tests from underpowered safety claims, tracks premise rigging ($\mathrm{PRI}$), and controls multiplicity across specification curves via $e$-BH.

Next, [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md) faces structural underdetermination at $T\_2$ (*Test for severity*). It pairs anytime-valid *e*-processes with sharp non-parametric bounds (Manski, Lee, and Peterson) and Imbens–Manski coverage intervals. Using the LaLonde benchmark, it shows why non-refutable identification assumptions cannot buy credible point estimates.

Finally, [Chapter 45](45-world-to-archive-big-data-paradox.md) closes Filter I at $T\_3$ (*Observe and transduce*). When recording correlates with need ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$) or observations arrive in clusters, raw volume $n$ misleads. The chapter separates variance-adjusted counts ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}$) from selection-adjusted counts ($n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\bar{\rho})$) and Keynes's evidential weight $V$, uses `Chao2` bounds for *Not observed* units without masking *Not askable* populations ($\pi\_i \equiv 0$), and enforces bitemporal vintages to separate look-ahead leakage from reporting lag.

Taken together, Filter I outputs a bounded evidential region, an honest evidential weight, and an explicit map of what the archive cannot see.

## Filter II: asymmetry and irreversible time ($S\_\{\mathrm{vital}}$)

Across two chapters, the vital axis becomes **Canonical Operator II**. [Chapter 46](46-non-identical-asymmetry-heavy-tails.md) guards how measurements and losses are formalized ($T\_4$). It replaces arbitrary binary cutoffs with dialectical penumbra tracking, blocks invalid averages of ordinal scores via Stevens's scale invariance, pairs convex shortfall gaps ($\Delta\_J$) with proper $\mathrm{CRPS}$ scoring, and classifies tail regimes using Hill's index, Generalized Pareto thresholds, and coherent Expected Shortfall ($\mathrm{CVaR}\_p$).

[Chapter 47](47-irreversible-time-vital-margin.md) adds the physical clock at $T\_5$ (*Temporal*). It introduces Itô variance drain, shrinks leverage inside $(0, f^\ast]$ using the finite-sample multiplier $k(n\_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{var}}) \in (0, 1)$, and traces Canguilhem–Holling resilience basins alongside Kingman's utilization wall. Operational viability hinges on the survival race $\mathrm{TTS}\_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}\_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathcal{I}\_t)$—survival buffer time outlasting effective recovery time—bounded globally by Wald Maximin over Filter I's admissible set rather than vulnerable local Info-Gap radii.

Filter II therefore delivers a physical survival horizon and a buffer floor above ruin, never a gamble on a favorable average.

## Filter III: reactive worlds and institutions ($S\_\{\mathrm{gov}}$)

Across two chapters, the governance axis becomes **Canonical Operator III** through three sequential gates. [Chapter 48](48-reactive-worlds-performativity-goodhart.md) constructs Gate 1 ($T\_6$, *Perform*) for environments where publishing a rule alters human behavior. It combines out-of-fold CORP-PAV calibration, invariant causal prediction, Kennedy–O'Hagan discrepancy models, and Tian–Pearl counterfactual bounds with Goodhart's variants and performative retraining limits ($|\varepsilon| < \gamma/L\_y = 1 + \lambda$ in the linear-quadratic model at $L\_y = 1$, distinct from Perdomo et al.'s general sufficient bound $\gamma/\beta\_{\mathrm{joint}}$).

[Chapter 49](49-moral-luck-apt-agency-institutions.md) constructs Gates 2 and 3 ($T\_7$, *Liability*). Gate 2 pairs List–Pettit conjunctive vetoes with an undiscounted frontline alarm channel ($\delta\_{\mathrm{status}} = 0$) sized for bounded human attention. Gate 3 uses Chouldechova's fairness trilemma and bitemporal cryptographic vaults to separate genuine skill from moral luck, while exposing underspecified pre-seal protocols such as Macondo or non-discriminating records ($\mathrm{LR} = 1$).

## Synthesis and reflexivity

[Chapter 50](50-methodological-families-reflexivity.md) tests whether the framework holds together across five methodological families, including qualitative and historical process tracing with a non-compensatory hoop veto. It then turns the triad onto itself through four Reflexive Brakes (including the ex-post hoarding ceiling $R^\ast$), Leontief box geometry, and five pre-registered falsifiers ($F\_1$–$F\_5$).

## How the filters compose

The three filters operate jointly on every shift rather than as a one-pass assembly line. Epistemic, vital, and governance operators constrain each step together under lexicographic priority, while governance fixes the terms ($q - c$) on which margins are audited.

Notice that the running case looks worse before it stabilizes. Filter I widens narrow answers into intervals, Filter II proves that knowing the interval does not buy survival time, and Filter III shows that bought time can still be captured or spilled onto neighbors. Live action stays strictly with the per-step operators, while the **co-diagnostic pair** $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}} = (M\_{\mathrm{Leontief}}, \Theta\_{\mathrm{basin}})$ folds ex post to identify which margin or external reception perimeter failed.
