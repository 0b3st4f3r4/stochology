*This page gives a plain-English reader's guide to Part V before [Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) begins. It explains how the thirty-five chapters are organized, how to decode every chapter's identity card, and what the two architecture graphs actually test.*

## A plain-English guide to Part V

Part IV tested the Stochology circuit on one running town case. Part V stress-tests the exact same circuit across thirty-five real-world domains—from a hand catching a falling cup to climate forecasts, clinical trials, archival silences, financial ruin, and constitutional law.

To navigate [Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) through [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md) without getting lost in symbols, you only need to decode five building blocks printed at the top of every chapter.

### 1. What an Order (`L01`–`L35`) is

An **Order** is not an academic department or a textbook subject. It is a recurring real-world situation where someone must decide and act before all the facts are in, using an incomplete record and a finite physical buffer.

Turbulence, immunology, and museum taxonomy look unrelated in a university catalog. In Stochology they are simply different orders (`L04`, `L13`, and `L14`) where an institution frames a question, builds a partial archive, and pays real time or survival margin for mistakes. Each order gets one dedicated chapter in monotone sequence: order `Lnn` is argued in Chapter `50 + nn` (so `L01` sits in [Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) and `L35` closes in [Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)).

### 2. Why there are thirty-five orders: $\binom{7}{3} = 35$

The mediation circuit links three planes—World ($P\_1$), Archive ($P\_2$), and Institution ($P\_3$)—creating six directed crossings between distinct planes plus one integral closure of the entire loop ($3 \cdot 2 + 1 = 7$; see [Chapter 41, *The Triad and the Minimum*](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md) and [Appendix E](appendix-e-lexicon.md)). Those seven pressure points are the **seven guard sites** ($T\_1$ through $T\_7$).

In ordinary words, each guard site watches one failure mode:

- **Epistemic axis ($S\_{\mathrm{epi}}$):** $T\_1$ (*Framing*) asks what counts as the question before reading data; $T\_2$ (*Severity*) tests how strongly evidence separates competing claims; $T\_3$ (*Archive*) checks who or what went uncounted in the record.
- **Vital axis ($S\_{\mathrm{vital}}$):** $T\_4$ (*Formalization*) prevents averages and rigid labels from hiding tail extremes; $T\_5$ (*Temporal*) checks whether survival headroom outlasts physical recovery time.
- **Governance axis ($S\_{\mathrm{gov}}$):** $T\_6$ (*Performative*) watches whether publishing a metric makes people game it; $T\_7$ (*Liability*) ensures frontline alarms survive and the risk-creator cannot dump harm onto outsiders.

Every Part V chapter focuses on one **Unique Guard Combination** $\lbrace T\_i, T\_j, T\_k \rbrace$: a three-site spotlight drawn from those seven guards. Counting every distinct three-site combination from seven choices gives exact combinatorial arithmetic:

$$\binom{7}{3} = \frac{7 \times 6 \times 5}{3 \times 2 \times 1} = 35.$$

Read aloud, there are thirty-five distinct triples you can form from seven guards, and Part V assigns one triple to each chapter. The number thirty-five is simply that combinatorial count, not a claim about how many disciplines exist.

### 3. What a Signature `(E, V, G)` tells you

The seven guard sites split three, two, and two across Epistemic ($E$), Vital ($V$), and Governance ($G$). A chapter's **Signature** $(E, V, G)$ simply counts how many of its three local guards sit on each axis ($E + V + G = 3$):

- **Trans-triadic keystones `(1, 1, 1)` (twelve orders):** One guard sits on every axis locally. Chapters like [Chapter 51 (`L01`)](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md) or [Chapter 55 (`L05`)](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md) show all three sides of the Nuclear Triad at once inside one domain.
- **Binary triads and pure concentrations (twenty-three orders):** At least one coordinate is `0`—such as `(2, 1, 0)`, `(0, 1, 2)`, `(1, 0, 2)`, or the unique `(3, 0, 0)` of [Chapter 59 (`L09`)](59-layer-experimental-sampling-causal-order.md). As [Appendix B.2](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b2-the-multilayer-graph--35-orders-7-strata-5-exposure-regimes) specifies, a `0` entry records only a zero local guard count ($k\_a = 0$ in that order's three-site triple), never setting a normalized trajectory axis score $S\_a = 0$ and never making the architectural order itself `Unparsable`, because upstream ancestors in $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)$ supply coverage across the missing axis. By contrast, if a practitioner treats that domain as self-contained and seals a hypothetical isolated trajectory trace carrying only its local guards and zero upstream readings, that unclosed trace fails the grammar's Stage two axial totality check as `Unparsable` ([Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate))—vividly exposing which collapse proposition fires when upstream dependencies are ignored.

### 4. What the dependency graphs $\mathcal{G}\_1$ and $\mathcal{G}\_2$ mean

Two graphs knit the thirty-five chapters together so no domain stays isolated:

- **$\mathcal{G}\_1$ (the vertical dependency map):** Think of $\mathcal{G}\_1$ as a one-way prerequisite network—a directed acyclic graph (DAG, "what rests on what"). Starting from the root at [Layer 01 (Chapter 51)](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md), every order $L\_i$ inherits the constraints of its upstream **ancestor cone**, written $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)$. When a chapter has a local `0` in its signature, its $\mathcal{G}\_1$ ancestors show where the missing safeguard comes from—and how failures upstream poison conclusions downstream.
- **$\mathcal{G}\_2$ (the horizontal cousin map):** Written $J(7, 3)$, $\mathcal{G}\_2$ links any two orders whose guard triples share two out of three sites. Each chapter therefore has $3 \times (7 - 3) = 12$ close structural cousins in unexpected fields. A ribosome proofreading aminoacyl-tRNA selection during protein synthesis ([`L11`](61-layer-biochemical-kinetic-molecular-order.md), `S3`) and an epidemic tracing team ([`L17`](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md), `S4`), for example, share two guards (`{T2, T5}`)—facing the exact same trade between severe accuracy (`T2`) and biological time (`T5`) even though one lives in a cell and the other in a city (here `L11` also lies in $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{17})$, whereas pairs like `L11` and [`L26`](76-layer-sequential-decision-bandit-real-options-order.md) in `S6` are strictly non-ancestral lateral cousins).

### 5. What a Stratum (`S1`–`S7`) is

Part V groups the thirty-five chapters into seven thematic **Strata** (`S1`–`S7`) of five chapters each, rising from physical matter to human institutions. Each stratum is anchored to one primary guard site as a **designation recorded rather than derived** in [Appendix B](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b2-the-multilayer-graph--35-orders-7-strata-5-exposure-regimes). The resulting grid—five orders per stratum, with each guard site serving as primary in five strata—is a deliberate property of the mapping table rather than a theorem about nature:

- **Stratum `S1` ([*The Substrate That Charges for Time*](stratum-i-opening.md); [Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)–[Chapter 55](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_5$:** Physical substrates—reflexes, queues, networks, chaotic atmospheres, and fracturing solids—where irreversible time runs out before estimates finish.
- **Stratum `S2` ([*The Instrument and Its Commitment*](stratum-ii-opening.md); [Chapter 56](56-layer-formal-computable-type-order.md)–[Chapter 60](60-layer-cryptographic-zero-knowledge-adversarial-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_1$:** Formal instruments—linear types, credences, metrology, experimental design, and cryptography—where choices made before data decide what can be seen.
- **Stratum `S3` ([*The Living Substrate*](stratum-iii-opening.md); [Chapter 61](61-layer-biochemical-kinetic-molecular-order.md)–[Chapter 65](65-layer-stratigraphy-paleobiology-deep-time-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_4$:** Living substrates—molecular kinetics, embryos, immune systems, taxonomy, and fossil strata—where biology trades energy and time for form.
- **Stratum `S4` ([*The Interval Between the Decision and the Evidence*](stratum-iv-opening.md); [Chapter 66](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md)–[Chapter 70](70-layer-acoustic-aesthetic-tragic-pedagogical-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_2$:** Clinical and evaluative orders—pharmacology, epidemics, drug safety, metascience, and pedagogy—where action precedes slow confirmation.
- **Stratum `S5` ([*The Record and Its Author*](stratum-v-opening.md); [Chapter 71](71-layer-linguistic-evidential-pragmatic-order.md)–[Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_3$:** Archival and linguistic orders—grammatical evidentials, philology, historical taphonomy, justice archives, and media feeds—where recording rules shape memory.
- **Stratum `S6` ([*The Adversary Inside the Measurement*](stratum-vi-opening.md); [Chapter 76](76-layer-sequential-decision-bandit-real-options-order.md)–[Chapter 80](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_6$:** Strategic and economic orders—bandits, games, macro-finance, national accounting, and corporate governance—where targets bend human behavior.
- **Stratum `S7` ([*The Irreversible Decision*](stratum-vii-opening.md); [Chapter 81](81-layer-criminal-proof-forensic-epistemology-order.md)–[Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)) $\leftrightarrow T\_7$:** Legal and high-hazard orders—criminal proof, tort law, distributed computing, nuclear and dam safety, and shared commons—where integral accountability locks all three planes together.

Each stratum opens with a two-minute primer stating the shared challenge uniting its five chapters.

## How to read a chapter

Every Part V chapter follows the same four-part rhythm:

1. **The identity card:** A short opening blockquote and badge naming the order (`Lnn`), stratum, guard triple, signature `(E, V, G)`, and $\mathcal{G}\_1$ parents (or basal root status $\mathrm{Anc}^\ast(L\_{01}) = \lbrace{}L\_{01}\rbrace$ in [Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)).
2. **Three outcomes:** Clear goals telling you what the chapter equips you to explain.
3. **Domain story to formal rule:** Each chapter starts with a concrete situation in its domain and walks through its three guard sites one by one. It then reads its signature—showing which collapse proposition fires when an axis drops to zero, or how a `(1, 1, 1)` keystone operates inside $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_i)$ ([Appendix B.2](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b2-the-multilayer-graph--35-orders-7-strata-5-exposure-regimes)) while the co-diagnostic pair $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$ audits finished runs *ex post* ([Chapter 41](41-the-triad-and-the-minimum.md); [Appendix A.7](appendix-a-mathematics.md#a7-reception-side-margin--the-co-diagnostic-pair-not-a-fourth-axis); [Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)).
4. **The bridge:** A handoff pointing forward along the graph to the next chapter.

## What the part tests

Part V is the book's own reality check. If the mediation circuit truly captures judgment under uncertainty, it must speak cleanly wherever an archive is filtered, a buffer is finite, and someone authorizes an act. Testing that claim thirty-five times in a row reveals exactly where the circuit holds—and where each domain's own physics or politics forces a hard boundary.
