# Stratum I — The Substrate That Charges for Time

*An opening page for Stratum I, stated once at the level its five chapters share. It names where each
instance is argued and does not restate them.*

---

Stratum I is argued in five numbered chapters of its own — [Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md), [Chapter 52](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md), [Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md), [Chapter 54](54-layer-geophysical-chaos-data-assimilation-order.md) and [Chapter 55](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md), one order each. The
stratum's own argument — that a physical substrate is read only by acting on it, that the action costs
time, and that the cost is charged in three different currencies before an estimate can be formed — is
stated once, at the level the five orders share, on this page.

These are the orders closest to matter and furthest from intention. A surface is felt before its next
state is known; a queue is filled before its service time is known; a network cascades before the
response is chosen; a fluid diverges before the horizon closes; a flaw propagates before the inspection
that would have found it. Read separately they are five specialities of engineering. Read together they
are one problem stated five times: **the substrate is a device for converting time into information,
and the conversion is priced whether or not anyone agreed to pay.**

## The mechanism and the two clocks

The first order is the cleanest case, and it sets the pattern the other four repeat. A sensorimotor
surface does not observe a state and then act; it acts, and the state it then holds is the state its own
action produced. The consequence is that the two clocks cannot be compared by improving the estimate: one is the
interval the mechanism needs to resolve a difference, the other the interval the environment gives it
before the state changes. The comparison is an inequality between durations, and the inequality does not
contain the estimator ([Chapter 51](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md)).

A margin that belongs to the surface cannot be carried
by the model. A model inside the loop buys a better prediction of a state, never a longer interval
before the state arrives, and the two are not interchangeable. The order is a keystone because framing, vital margin and liability are all read at the surface, and
none of them can be recovered from the other two.

## The queue, the network, and the announcement

Three of the five orders are about delay that has a shape. A buffer is a formal object before it is a
cost, and its behaviour near saturation is not an intuition but a bound: heavy traffic fixes the order of
magnitude of the wait, and the bound is a property of the arrival process and not of the service
discipline (Kingman 1961; Kingman 1962). The second thing a queue teaches is that the buffer is
announced before it is spent: an expected-delay claim enters the arrival process, so the measurement is
part of the system it measures ([Chapter 52](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md)).

A cascade moves the same argument up one level. Propagation is a property of the graph, not of the node:
whether a shock stays local is settled by the topology, and the tolerance a node needs is a function of
the load it carries against its capacity, so a heavily loaded network needs almost no amplification to
cross into a cascade ([Chapter 53](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md)). The third lesson of the
stratum is already visible there: the edge that carries the measurement is often created by the
measurement, and a topology read only from the instrument is a topology shaped by it.

## The horizon, and the flaw

The fourth order is the one that removes the option of waiting. Deterministic chaos does not make a
prediction wrong; it makes the horizon at which the prediction is wrong a computable quantity, and that
horizon grows only logarithmically in the precision of the initial condition (Lorenz 1963; Richardson
1922). The practical consequence is severe and often missed: improving the initial condition from a
coarse to a fine measurement buys a handful of days, not a season. What the archive can constrain is the
distribution, and constraining a distribution is a different act from constraining a trajectory — which
is why assimilation is a discipline of its own and not a larger computation (Evensen 1994).

The fifth order closes the stratum with the smallest object and the sharpest threshold. A crack is a
threshold on the flaw before it is a statement about the load: below the critical length the crack does
not grow, above it every increment of stress grows it, and the transition is a fold and not a
gradual loss (Griffith, in the form [Chapter 55](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md)
derives). The population of components is the same fold counted, so the weakest link and the basin of
attraction are one argument at two scales — and the margin that keeps the fold from being crossed is
paid by a party who does not choose the load.

## What this stratum does not settle

It does not settle the exchange rate between its five currencies. A millisecond of latency, a packet
lost in a cascade, a day of forecast horizon and a micrometre of crack are not convertible, and nothing
here converts them. The common structure is a shape, a duration compared against a duration, and
not a unit.

It also does not replace the five chapters that carry its instances. The kinetics of a surface are
argued where the loop needs them, the queue bounds where the buffer is priced, the cascade where the
network is drawn, the horizon where the archive is read, and the flaw where liability is assigned. What
this page adds is the sentence those five cannot say from inside themselves: that the substrate charges
for time before it reports, and that no amount of measurement refunds the charge.
