Stratum I covers five basal physical orders: [Layer 01: Micro-Kinetic & Sensorimotor Order](51-layer-micro-kinetic-sensorimotor-order.md), [Layer 02: Meso-Buffered Queue Order](52-layer-meso-buffered-queue-order.md), [Layer 03: Macro-Coupled Topology Order](53-layer-macro-coupled-topology-order.md), [Layer 04: Geophysical Fluid Dynamics, Lorenz Chaos & Data Assimilation Order](54-layer-geophysical-chaos-data-assimilation-order.md), and [Layer 05: Continuum Fracture and Structural Order](55-layer-continuum-fracture-structural-order.md).

Together, these five chapters state one shared rule at the physical scale: **a physical substrate can only be read by acting on it, and every action burns irreversible time before a report comes back.**

## What the five orders share

These are the orders closest to raw matter and furthest from human paperwork. Yet they all obey one race between two clocks: how long a physical buffer holds versus how quickly an observer detects trouble, decides, and acts.

Across Stratum I, the physical setup scales up step by step from a single contact point to an entire continuous solid:

1. **One fast contact (`L01`):** A body or vehicle brakes on a surface faster than conscious thought can plan. Survival hangs on reaction delay and friction headroom.
2. **One shared waiting room (`L02`):** Arrivals pile up at a single server or clinic desk. Near full utilization, wait times explode—and posted wait numbers feed back to change who joins the line.
3. **A web of coupled nodes (`L03`):** Queues wire together into power grids and server meshes. A local fault cascades across edges, including hidden links created simply by sharing a target dashboard.
4. **A chaotic fluid field (`L04`):** Winds and oceans mix continuously across scales. Tiny measurement gaps grow exponentially until the forecast horizon shuts.
5. **A stressed solid (`L05`):** Bridges, plates, and earthen dams fail at their worst unseen flaw. Here, static load hides brittle fracture until the weakest link gives way.

In every step, physical failure arrives on the clock before spreadsheet averages notice. Anchored to `T5Temporal` as its designated stratum site, Stratum I proves that waiting for cleaner estimates never refunds lost physical time.

## What this stratum does not settle

Stratum I does not convert its five physical currencies into one common price. A millisecond of braking latency, a dropped network packet, a forecast day, and a micrometer of steel crack have no shared exchange rate.

What unites them is the same geometric shape: a remaining survival duration compared against an effective response duration. Better data analysis after the clock runs out cannot buy back a single second of lost margin.
