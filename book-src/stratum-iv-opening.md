# Stratum IV — The Interval Between the Decision and the Evidence

*An opening page for Stratum IV, stated once at the level its five chapters share. It names where each instance is argued and does not restate them.*

---

Stratum IV is argued in five numbered chapters of its own: [Chapter 66](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md), [Chapter 67](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md), [Chapter 68](68-layer-pharmacovigilance-regulatory-toxicology-order.md), [Chapter 69](69-layer-metascience-scientometrics-replication-order.md) and [Chapter 70](70-layer-acoustic-aesthetic-tragic-pedagogical-order.md), one order each. Its argument belongs to the five together rather than to any one of them, so it is stated once, here: in practice the decision is taken before conclusive evidence exists, and the interval between the two is the object being governed.

Across clinical care, epidemics, drug regulation, scientific publishing, and formative education, action precedes conclusive confirmation while interim records remain incomplete or skewed: dose precedes response, case definition precedes counts, approval precedes rare harm, publication precedes replication, and narrow grids or tragic blindness precede late recognition (*anagnórisis*) and long-term mastery. Five orders confront one temporal predicament: **decisive evidence arrives late, under incumbent authorship of clinical, surveillance, regulatory, publication, or high-stakes testing archives (`L16`–`L19` and `L20` §3), or across frame-bound arithmetic residues and tragic *hamartía* (`L20` §§1–2).**

## What the five orders share

Across bedside dosing, epidemic surveillance, post-market safety, selective publication, and formative assessment, action cannot wait for completed proof. Stratum IV is anchored by recorded designation (`S4 ↔ T2`) at guard site `T2Severity`, which demands tests strong enough to rule out harm rather than mistaking noisy null studies for safety (`L16`–`L19` carry `T2Severity` directly, while `L20` inherits it upstream through $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{20})$ via `L16` and `L07`–`L08`).

Left unchecked, early commitments either let the acting party shape the clinical, surveillance, regulatory, publication, or high-stakes testing record in its own favor (through under-powered trials, shifting case definitions, voluntary under-reporting, significance filters, and test-score inflation) or trap learners and agents inside rigid grids, time-crowding drills, and unexamined frames until tragic reversal arrives.

To keep early choices corrigible, live action cannot lean on the retrospective co-diagnostic pair $\mathcal{D}\_{\mathrm{diag}}$, which evaluates finished trajectories strictly *ex post*. Instead, commitments are structured *ex ante*, before outcomes arrive, using pre-sealed thresholds open to attested `CounterInductiveReopen` ([Appendix B.1](appendix-b-architecture-verification.md#b1-the-grammar-of-aim--syntax-layer-and-acceptance-predicate)), reversible fractional probes strictly confined to divisible, linear, non-ruinous regimes ([Chapter 43](43-inquiry-openness-multiverse.md); [Chapter 44](44-underdetermination-partial-identification.md)), and unannounced low-stakes audits kept off ranking tables and bounded against mandatory time-crowding.

## How the five orders build step by step

The five chapters walk outward from one patient at the bedside to an entire scientific field and tragic or formative judgment:

1. **Bedside dosing ([Chapter 66](66-layer-pharmacological-clinical-diagnostic-order.md), `L16`):** Starts with one patient and one drug, where clearance lags and split hospital-versus-patient error costs make fixed doses unsafe.
2. **Outbreak surveillance ([Chapter 67](67-layer-epidemiology-outbreak-surveillance-order.md), `L17`):** Scales that timing lag to a population, where contagion doubles faster than reporting lines and shifting case definitions hide spread.
3. **Post-market safety ([Chapter 68](68-layer-pharmacovigilance-regulatory-toxicology-order.md), `L18`):** Follows approved therapies into millions of prescriptions, where rare injuries surface inside biased voluntary filings and delayed recalls.
4. **Scientific literature ([Chapter 69](69-layer-metascience-scientometrics-replication-order.md), `L19`):** Steps back to research communities, where file-drawer omissions and career metrics distort what gets published and replicated.
5. **Tragic and formative order ([Chapter 70](70-layer-acoustic-aesthetic-tragic-pedagogical-order.md), `L20`):** Completes the stratum as a balanced keystone, tracing fixed-grid mismatches from acoustic tuning and Attic tragedy to classroom assessment.

That one-to-five walk is pedagogical rather than a linear dependency edge: in $\mathcal{G}\_1$, `L16` feeds `{L17, L18, L20}`, `L17 -> L18 -> L19` chains forward alongside upward exports `L17 -> L35` ([Chapter 85](85-layer-polycentric-commons-urban-biospheric-order.md)) and `L19 -> L25` ([Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md)), and `L20` closes as a global sink (`out-degree = 0`) with no `L19 -> L20` edge.

## What this stratum does not settle

It does not price the interval. An organ clearance lag (`L16`), a day of epidemic reporting delay (`L17`), a rare post-market adverse event (`L18`), a selected publication effect size (`L19`), and a framing mismatch or formative breakthrough (`L20`) are not convertible, and nothing here converts them. The common structure is a lag between decision and evidence, not a shared unit.

Nor does every delayed verdict fail: the interval is structural. This opening poses the question no single chapter settles alone: when late evidence arrives, can the sealed record still overturn the early decision?
