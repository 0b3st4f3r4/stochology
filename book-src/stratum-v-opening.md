# Stratum V — The Record and Its Author

*An opening page for Stratum V, stated once at the level its five chapters share. It names where each
instance is argued and does not restate them.*

---

Stratum V is argued in five numbered chapters of its own — [Chapter 71](71-layer-linguistic-evidential-pragmatic-order.md), [Chapter 72](72-layer-philological-epigraphic-textual-order.md), [Chapter 73](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md), [Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md) and [Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md), one order each. Its argument belongs to the five rather than to any one of them: where the record is the only access, the record's author, its witnesses and its retrieval order are part of the evidence. That is stated once, here.

These five orders are the ones in which nothing can be observed, only read. A grammar decides what a speaker must disclose about how she knows. A manuscript tradition decides how many witnesses a text really has. A fossil bed decides which organisms a period can be said to contain. An archive decides whose harm is documented, and a ranking decides what the corpus will contain after it has been ranked. Read separately they are five humanistic disciplines. Read together they are one problem stated five times: **the record is not a window on the past but a residue of it, and the residue was produced by someone with an interest in what it retains.**

## What the grammar obliges the speaker to disclose

The first order shows the problem in its smallest form. Evidentiality is not a comment on a statement. In the languages that grammaticalise it the mark sits inside the assertion, and a speaker who cannot supply it cannot make the claim ([Chapter 71](71-layer-linguistic-evidential-pragmatic-order.md); Aikhenvald 2004). Austin's point runs the same way from the other side: an utterance is an act, and the act's felicity conditions are part of its content, not decorations around it (Austin 1962).

The epistemic consequence is the one the stratum builds on. Testimonial knowledge depends on the hearer's ability to discount the speaker's interest, and the discount is not symmetric between speakers (Fricker 2007): a deficit in the hearer's willingness to believe is a different failure from a deficit in the speaker's reliability, and the two produce the same silence. The order carries a vital axial exposure because, although its two governance sites fix provenance and speaker answerability at the moment of utterance, nothing in the linguistic circuit supplies a temporal or physical stop-work buffer that halts downstream re-citation once the evidential mark is stripped across hops.

## Witnesses, and the ordering that does not choose

The second order is philology, the stratum's formal core. The number of copies is not the number of witnesses: copies descended from one another carry one testimony, and a text transmitted through a single line has one witness however many volumes survive (Lachmann 1850, in the form [Chapter 72](72-layer-philological-epigraphic-textual-order.md) states). Recension is then an ordering of the witnesses, and the ordering does not choose among readings. It establishes which differences are evidence and which are the scribe's.

The order's discipline is a severity judgement on a scale the witnesses supply, and it is why the stratum cannot be done by counting. A reading attested twice by copies of one exemplar is attested once, so the authority of a variant is a function of the genealogy rather than of the tally. The governance absence here is the one the stratum names: a recension is auditable only if the criterion, the date and the right to contest it are recorded with the text.

## The residue, and the rate that belongs to the window

The third order states the stratum's hardest fact, and it is a fact about sampling rather than about history. A fossil assemblage is a residue of a living population, filtered by preservation, and the filter is not random with respect to the traits that make a body preservable (Efremov 1940). So a rate measured over a window belongs to that window: a background extinction rate and a mass extinction rate are not one quantity observed at two magnitudes, because the window is what the rate is defined against.

The chapter's institutional reading makes this order a keystone. Reading survival as representativeness is the error that turns an archive into a census: what persisted is treated as what existed, and the inference from residue to population is made without the filter that produced the residue. It is the same error the epidemiological order names from the other side, and it is why the two strata share a guard site rather than a subject.

## The archive of the wrong, and the ranking that writes its corpus

The fourth order is where the stratum becomes explicitly political. An archive is not a neutral store. What is deposited, by whom, and under what description are decisions that fix what can later be asked (Stoler 2009; Trouillot 1995). Transitional justice is the limiting case, because the harm is framed before the archive is sought: a commission that begins from a category of victim has already decided which testimony will be intelligible.

The fifth order closes the stratum with the mechanism that makes the problem self-reinforcing. A retrieval system does not describe a corpus; it selects it, and the selection changes what the next round will contain, so the ranking writes the corpus it ranks ([Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md)). The bias is measurable: the expectation of a recorded value differs from the expectation over the population by the correlation between selection and outcome times the standard deviations of selection and outcome, divided by the fraction recorded — equivalently $\rho\_{R,Y}\sigma\_{Y}\sqrt{(1-f)/f}$ at recorded fraction $f$ — so the distortion grows without bound as the archive thins (the derivation is in the chapter). Innis's point about the medium biasing the civilisation is the same claim at another scale (Innis 1951), and the reactivity literature supplies the missing dynamic: a measure that is acted upon changes the behaviour it measures (Espeland & Sauder 2007; Perdomo et al. 2020).

## What this stratum does not settle

It does not give an exchange rate between a grammatical evidential, a variant reading, a fossil gap, a deposition and a ranking weight. The common structure is that each is a residue produced under a filter, not that each converts into the others.

Nor does it claim that every archive is a distortion. The stratum's claim is conditional, and therefore checkable: where the filter is correlated with the outcome, the record's expectation is biased by that correlation; where the filter is independent, the record is unbiased and the argument does not bite. What the page adds to the five chapters is the sentence none of them can state alone: the record is evidence about its own production before it is evidence about the world.
