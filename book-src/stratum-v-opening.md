# Stratum V — The Record and Its Author

*An opening page for Stratum V, stated once at the level its five chapters share. It names where each instance is argued and does not restate them.*

---

Stratum V is argued in five numbered chapters of its own — [Chapter 71](71-layer-linguistic-evidential-pragmatic-order.md), [Chapter 72](72-layer-philological-epigraphic-textual-order.md), [Chapter 73](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md), [Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md) and [Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md), one order each. Its argument belongs to the five rather than to any one of them: where the record is the only access, the record's author, its witnesses, and its retrieval order are part of the evidence. That is stated once, here.

These five orders are the ones in which nothing can be observed directly, only read. A grammar decides what a speaker must disclose about how she knows. A manuscript tradition decides how many independent witnesses a text really has. A fossil bed decides which organisms a period seems to contain. An archive decides whose harm is documented, and a ranking decides what a corpus will contain after it has been ranked. Read separately, they are five humanistic and media disciplines. Read together, they state one problem five times: **the record is not a window on the past but a residue of it, produced or filtered by grammatical, scribal, physical, institutional, or ranking rules that decide what it retains.**

## What the five orders share

Stratum V is anchored by designation to `T3Archive`—governing how events enter, survive, and get weighted in an archive. Whenever preservation correlates with the outcome ($\rho\_{R,Y} \ne 0$, registration–value correlation), larger corpora sharpen precision around a biased mirror.

Every corpus consumer stands downstream of an unchosen filter, so custody rules precede world inference. In $\mathcal{G}\_1$, `L21`, `L22`, and `L25` end as parallel sinks, `L24` feeds criminal proof (`L31`), and `L23` feeds forensic ledgers (`L29`) and `L31`.

## How the five orders build step by step

The five chapters trace archival distortion from inside a single spoken verb out to automated digital feeds:

1. **Spoken grammar ([Chapter 71](71-layer-linguistic-evidential-pragmatic-order.md), `L21`):** Begins inside a single utterance, where obligatory evidential markers enforce source disclosure at creation while multi-hop relays strip provenance away.
2. **Copied manuscripts and carved stone ([Chapter 72](72-layer-philological-epigraphic-textual-order.md), `L22`):** Moves to scribal traditions, where shared copying mistakes reveal clustered witnesses and physical bracket conventions mark broken stone.
3. **Deep time and material rot ([Chapter 73](73-layer-taphonomic-historiographical-order.md), `L23`):** Widens to fossil beds and historical fragments, where uneven decay over long time windows makes durable materials look artificially common.
4. **Power and archival silence ([Chapter 74](74-layer-critical-archival-transitional-justice-order.md), `L24`):** Confronts deliberate institutional curation, where narrow mandates make harms unaskable and culpable keepers reverse reporting bias.
5. **Search rankings and synthetic loops ([Chapter 75](75-layer-media-ecology-information-retrieval-order.md), `L25`):** Closes the stratum in digital retrieval, where ranking rules and recursive AI training rewrite the future corpus they index.

## What this stratum does not settle

It does not give an exchange rate between a grammatical evidential, a variant reading, a fossil gap, a deposition, and a ranking weight. Each is a residue produced under a filter, not convertible into the others.

Nor does it claim that every archive is a distortion. The stratum's claim is conditional, and therefore checkable: where the filter correlates with the outcome, the record's average is displaced by that correlation; where the filter is independent, the record is unbiased and the distortion disappears. What this page adds to the five chapters is the sentence none can state alone: the record is evidence about its own production before it is evidence about the world.
