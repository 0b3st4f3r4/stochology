# Stratum VI — The Adversary Inside the Measurement

*An opening page for Stratum VI, stated once at the level its five chapters share. It names where each instance is argued and does not restate them.*

---

Strata I through V already met wait-induced queue feedback, certificate shopping, scientometric effort drift, high-stakes exam drills, alibi archives, and search-ranking gaming across the ten earlier orders carrying `T6Performative`. Stratum VI consolidates `T6Performative` as its designated stratum anchor (`S6 ↔ T6`), crossing into markets, balance sheets, national ledgers, and formal contracts where pay, credit, solvency, or legal liability hangs directly on the published number.

Five linked orders trace the passage from adaptive sampling and timing into explicit economic, accounting, and contractual reactivity:

- **Sequential choices and real options (`L26`, [Chapter 76](76-layer-sequential-decision-bandit-real-options-order.md)):** A stopping rule or irreversible investment hurdle races non-stationary drift and adaptive sampling bias while leaving institutional governance (`T6Performative`, `T7Liability`) locally unguarded ($k\_3 = 0$), relying on $\mathrm{Cone}(L\_{26})$ until handed forward to `L27` and `L28`.
- **Strategic games and performativity (`L27`, [Chapter 77](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md)):** Applicants and platforms observe a published classifier, shift cheap features to clear the threshold, and alter the population distribution.
- **Macro-finance and actuarial ruin (`L28`, [Chapter 78](78-layer-macro-finance-econometrics-actuarial-order.md)):** Insurers and leveraged funds share the same thin-tailed risk models, triggering synchronized fire-sales and procyclical margin spirals once calm buffers evaporate.
- **Official statistics and forensic accounting (`L29`, [Chapter 79](79-layer-official-statistics-forensic-accounting-order.md)):** Self-reported balance sheets look balanced inside the ledger while concealing debt, ecological draws, or shifting definitions outside the chosen corporate or statistical perimeter.
- **Corporate governance and contracts (`L30`, [Chapter 80](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md)):** High-powered pay tied to narrow proxies drains unmeasured safety duties, while limited liability turns upside bets into someone else's loss.

## What the five orders share

Read separately, these look like distant corners of economics, finance, and law. Read together, Stratum VI is anchored to `T6Performative` by designation recorded rather than derived around one structural rule: **when the estimate enters the payoffs it estimates, strategic response belongs inside the stratum's model (or inherited ancestor cone) rather than outside it.**

Across Stratum VI, historical calibration breaks whenever participants observe published rules and reorganize around whatever goes uncounted, homogeneous risk limits synchronize forced liquidations, or adaptive stopping and narrow corporate boundaries truncate the ledger. Statistical tuning alone cannot hold a safety margin unless institutional design binds adaptive sampling, strategic gaming, limited liability, and omitted external risk together.

## What this stratum does not settle

This stratum neither prices reputation nor treats every principal-agent contract as a trap: whenever payoff tracks a narrow proxy more closely than the true goal, adaptive stopping biases logged samples, shared risk models amplify leverage cycles, or accounting perimeters omit off-sheet claims, unguarded estimates fail across Stratum VI's mechanisms. Nor does any formula remove strategic conflict outright; clean-looking numbers require external counterparty checks, bitemporal custody, and status-blind frontline witnesses.
