# Stratum VI — The Adversary Inside the Measurement

*An opening page for Stratum VI, stated once at the level its five chapters share. It names where each instance is argued and does not restate them.*

---

Through Strata I to V, measured systems did not read the scorecard to beat it. Stratum VI crosses into reactive markets and contracts where pay, credit, survival, or liability hangs on the published number.

Five orders confront an adversary inside the measurement:

- **Sequential choices and real options (`L26`, [Chapter 76](76-layer-sequential-decision-bandit-real-options-order.md)):** A stopping rule faces an environment that moves while you wait, and adaptive testing bends the very archive it logs.
- **Strategic games and performativity (`L27`, [Chapter 77](77-layer-game-theory-strategic-adversarial-order.md)):** Applicants and platforms observe a published classifier, shift cheap features to clear the threshold, and alter the population distribution.
- **Macro-finance and actuarial ruin (`L28`, [Chapter 78](78-layer-macro-finance-econometrics-actuarial-order.md)):** Insurers and leveraged funds share the same thin-tailed risk models, triggering synchronized fire-sales once calm margins evaporate.
- **Official statistics and forensic accounting (`L29`, [Chapter 79](79-layer-official-statistics-forensic-accounting-order.md)):** Self-reported balance sheets look balanced inside the ledger while concealing debt, ecological draws, or shifting definitions outside the perimeter.
- **Corporate governance and contracts (`L30`, [Chapter 80](80-layer-corporate-governance-principal-agent-order.md)):** High-powered pay tied to narrow proxies drains unmeasured safety duties, while limited liability turns upside bets into someone else's loss.

Read separately, these look like distant corners of economics and finance. Read together, they anchor Stratum VI by recorded designation at `T6Performative` around one structural rule: **when the estimate enters the payoffs it estimates, strategic response belongs inside the model rather than outside it.**

Across all five strategic orders, historical calibration breaks once participants observe the rule and reorganize around whatever goes uncounted. Statistical tuning alone cannot hold a safety margin unless institutional design binds strategic adaptation, limited liability, and omitted external risk together.

## What this stratum does not settle

This stratum neither prices reputation nor treats every principal-agent contract as a trap: whenever payoff tracks a narrow proxy more closely than the true goal, the proxy displaces the goal through these five mechanisms. Nor does any formula remove strategic conflict outright; clean-looking numbers require external counterparty checks, bitemporal custody, and status-blind frontline witnesses.
