# `stochology`

Pure-Rust (`std` only, zero external dependencies) library implementing **Stochology** (from *stókhos*, aim or conjecture, and *lógos*, reasoned account) — an integrative framework and transversal research program for the theory and ethics of aim in judgment and decision-making under structural uncertainty when action must precede the outcome.

## Architecture: The Full Mediation Circuit and the Nuclear Triad

- [`kernel`](src/kernel.rs) — Deterministic `SplitMix64` simulation (`DEFAULT_SEED = 2026`), FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 and RFC 2104 keyed HMAC-SHA256 sealing, Welford online moments, Pool-Adjacent-Violators (PAV) isotonic regression, and Cholesky SPD solver.
- [`engine`](src/engine.rs) — The sequential Agent–World–Institution loop (`CommunityReserveEnv`), `NaiveOptimizer`, `StochologicalAgent`, and the $2\times 2\times 2$ factorial ablations + 100-seed Monte Carlo evaluation.
- [`epistemic`](src/epistemic.rs) (**Filter I · Axis 1 [$S_{\mathrm{epi}}$]: Chapters 2–4**) — `EpistemicMarginState` (Canonical Operator I): antidogmatic openness (`cromwell_clamp`), severe testing vs. agnotology, Kimball/Mitroff Type III framing, *e*-BH multiverse discovery control, Ville *e*-processes, Manski & Lee partial-identification bounds, and the unified harmonic effective sample size law ($n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ uniting Meng's $\rho_{R,Y}$, Campbell–Moulton $\mathrm{DEFF}$, Kish weighting, Chao $\hat{n}_{00}$, and bitemporal vintages).
- [`vital`](src/vital.rs) (**Filter II · Axis 2 [$S_{\mathrm{vital}}$]: Chapters 5–6**) — `VitalMarginState` (Canonical Operator II): ordinal scale invariance, Jensen's convexity gap $\Delta_J$, empirical CRPS, Hill tail index $\hat{\alpha}$ with cluster-corrected `HollingTailRuptureAudit`, Itô variance drain, `KellySizingRegime` (Baker–McHale epistemic Kelly vs. tail-robust WDRO-CVaR Kelly), Kingman heavy-traffic queues, $\mathrm{TTS}_{\mathrm{survival}} \ge \mathrm{TTR}_{\mathrm{recovery}}$, Canguilhem–Holling resilience basins, Wald survivorship inversion, and Sniedovich No-Man's-Land safeguards.
- [`governance`](src/governance.rs) (**Filter III · Axis 3 [$S_{\mathrm{gov}}$]: Chapters 7–8**) — `GovernanceState` (Canonical Operator III): Gate 1 ex-ante CORP-PAV calibration with Sparre Andersen harmonic degrees-of-freedom finite-sample debiasing $\hat{b}_{\mathrm{PAV}}(n_{\mathrm{eff}}) = H_{n_{\mathrm{eff}}}/(4 n_{\mathrm{eff}})$, `IcpIdentificationStatus` causal invariance, Kennedy–O'Hagan GP discrepancy, Tian–Pearl bounds, Perdomo/MacKenzie performativity, and Goodhart's Law; Gate 2 real-time Beer $S3^*$ algedonic bypass ($\delta_{\mathrm{cred}} = 0$), List–Pettit conjunctive veto, and `KairosSynchronousPanel` / `ZhengMeisterQueueAudit` two-tier cognitive queue ($\le 10\text{ bits/s}$); Gate 3 ex-post `TemporalVault`, Sosa's `TelicGrade`, Holmström–Innes liability vs. Simon–Girard scapegoating, and Chouldechova's fairness trilemma.
- [`synthesis`](src/synthesis.rs) (**Triadic Synthesis [$M_{\mathrm{Leontief}}$]: Chapter 9**) — `TriadicIntegrity` ($M_{\mathrm{Leontief}} = \min(S_{\mathrm{epi}}, S_{\mathrm{vital}}, S_{\mathrm{gov}})$), `CollapseProposition`, `ProcessTracingInference`, `ReflexiveBrakeAudit`, `LeontiefVsMultiplicativeAudit`, and `PreregisteredFalsifier`.
