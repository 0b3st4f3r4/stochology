# Stochology — The CAPIBA Protocol: Methodological Architecture for Judgment and Decision-Making under Uncertainty

This document specifies the **CAPIBA Protocol** (*Calibrated, Anytime-valid, Performative-aware, Invariant, Bitemporal Auditing Protocol*), the scientific and operational auditing protocol of **Stochology** for data architecture, stochastic modeling, causal inference, and structured inter-agent communication. It pairs operational decision-making with algorithmic gates for performative prediction, decision theory under severe uncertainty, strong information contraction, tail design effects in unequal clusters, and production drift adaptation.

---

> **Reading guide.** The protocol is organized in four tiers: **Tier 0 — Normative Kernel** (§1), **Tier 1 — Lenses and Uncertainty Typing** (§2), **Tier 2 — The Six Ordered Pairs** (§3), and **Tier 3 — Syntheses** (§5, full specifications in [`docs/syntheses/`](../docs/syntheses/)). §4 covers operation and §6 the architectural mapping. Tier 0 binds as **principles and mechanisms**; the numbers and vocabulary those principles are checked with are canonical defaults that each application recalibrates in a pre-registered **Protocol Profile** (§1.6) — the kernel is fixed, the rest is clay shaped to the application. The course syllabus ([`docs/SYLLABUS.md`](../docs/SYLLABUS.md)) is an authorial pedagogical frame over this specification, not part of it.

## 1. Normative Kernel (Tier 0): Contract, Prohibitions, and Boundary Invariants

### 1.1. The Four Prohibitions
- **Prohibition 1:** Ban on unstructured free prose between modules/agents (defeating multi-agent "adjective laundering" and masking of surface-correlational "Stochastic Parrots" whose passive error $\mathrm{MSE}_{e_0} = 0.012$ jumps to $\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{do}(X)} = 19.13$ under intervention); all claims transit exclusively via typed `EpistemologicalCard` (`cards/*.yaml`) validated by `EpistemologicalCard::audit()`.
- **Prohibition 2:** Ban on the lone mean under nonlinearity or heavy tails; mandatory reporting of $f(\mathbb{E}[X]), \mathbb{E}[f(X)], \Delta\_J, \text{median}, q\_{05}, q\_{95}, \mathrm{CRPS}$.
- **Prohibition 3:** Ban on confusing predictive fit with causal/performative validity; mandatory audit of $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$ vs. $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}}$ and $\frac{\varepsilon\beta}{\gamma} < 1$.
- **Prohibition 4:** Ban on dogmatic extremes ($0\%$ or $100\%$, Cromwell's Rule $p \in [\varepsilon\_{\text{floor}}, 1 - \varepsilon\_{\text{floor}}]$) and omitted attempts ($K \ge 1$, *e-process* $E\_t$, and CORP-Murphy decomposition).

### 1.2. The Five Boundary Invariants ($\Phi\_1$ to $\Phi\_5$)
- **$\Phi\_1$ (Simulability):** Fixed seed declared before any fitting (canonical `seed = 2026`; a `ProtocolProfile` pre-registers its own, §1.6), `SplitMix64` with lock-free `.split(stream_id)` and `.split_fork_join_pair(branch_tag)` substream derivation via Steele, Lea & Flood 2014 / Steele & Vigna 2021 LXM `MonteCarloSplitTreeAudit`, uniform, normal, exponential, Marsaglia-Tsang Gamma, Beta, Student-$t$, and Poisson samplers, Welford-Terriberry 4th-order `OnlineMoments`, and block bootstrap `block_bootstrap_ci`) and executable generative null model.
- **$\Phi\_2$ (Independent Unit & Exceedance Design Effect):** Moulton-Campbell unequal-cluster correction $\tilde{m} = \bar{m}(1 + \mathrm{CV}^2(m\_j))$, threshold exceedance ICC $\rho\_I(p)$, $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(p) = \frac{n}{1 + (\tilde{m} - 1)\rho\_I(p)}$, and multi-quantile exceedance profiles (`ExceedanceDesignEffect::profile_across_quantiles`).
- **$\Phi\_3$ (Severe Test & Missingness):** Rubin classification (`MCAR`, `MAR`, `MNAR`), SHA-256 and RFC 2104 `HMAC-SHA256` pre-registration seal, and Pearl's Ladder rung.
- **$\Phi\_4$ (Scope, Fairness & Exit):** Chouldechova's binary algebraic impossibility identity $\mathrm{FPR}\_g = \frac{p\_g}{1-p\_g}\frac{1-\mathrm{PPV}\_g}{\mathrm{PPV}\_g}(1-\mathrm{FNR}\_g)$ (`disparate_impact_fpr_ratio`, `minmax_penalty_disparity`), Kleinberg-Mullainathan-Raghavan (KMR 2017) continuous score impossibility gap $\mathrm{Var}\_g(S) = p\_g(1 - p\_g)(m\_{g,+} - m\_{g,-})$ (`ChouldechovaAudit::kmr_continuous_impossibility_gap`), scope statement, and numeric exit clause.
- **$\Phi\_5$ (Question Resolution):** Ban on answering daily/shift operational decisions with coarse annual/monthly aggregates (canonical English vocabulary in `verify_resolution_phi5`; a profile declares the vocabulary of its own language and domain, §1.6).

### 1.4. Epistemological Card Schema (`EpistemologicalCard`)
Every claim is serialized in YAML (`cards/*.yaml`) and validated by `EpistemologicalCard::audit()` (or `EpistemologicalCard::audit_with(&profile)` under a shaped application, §1.6), carrying top-level scalars (`id`, `layer`, `uncertainty`), five structured sections, and an optional sixth, `protocol_profile`:

### 1.5. Claim Provenance Discipline

Every quantitative claim that leaves the code carries exactly one provenance class stated next to the number: **Measured** (computed on a named `data/*.csv`), **Simulated** (seeded generative output; discarded claims are said to be discarded), **Assumed** (a hand-set input or design target), or **Literature (informative)** (a transcribed constant with its source). The binding rules are in [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) §II.C.

### 1.6. Kernel and Clay: Protocol Profiles (`ProtocolProfile`)

The kernel is not a list of numbers. It is the set of **principles** (the four Prohibitions and $\Phi\_1$–$\Phi\_5$) and the **mechanisms** that make each one auditable: a seed declared before fitting, a manifest-sealed dataset, the SHA-256 pre-registration seal, the attempt counter $K \ge 1$, findings conservation, Cromwell's interval, the CORP-Murphy identity, cross-authorship, a scope with an explicit non-applicability boundary, and a numeric exit clause. These never change. What changes from one application to another is the **clay**: the thresholds, tolerances, vocabularies, data location, and replication counts with which the principles are checked. An application states them in the `protocol_profile` section of its card, parsed into `ProtocolProfile` (`crates/stochology-core/src/card/profile.rs`) and enforced by `EpistemologicalCard::audit_with`.

Rules of the clay:

1. **The profile is pre-registered.** For any non-canonical profile, `preregistration_payload_for` appends the SHA-256 of `ProtocolProfile::fingerprint` to the card's payload, so recalibrating a number after seeing the data breaks `PHI3_PREREGISTRATION_HASH`. The canonical profile leaves the payload untouched, which is why the four canonical cards keep their seals.
2. **Unknown fields are rejected; omitted fields inherit the canonical value.** `name` and `rationale` are mandatory, so every shaped application states why it departs from the defaults.
3. **A profile never waives a principle.** Only the instruments in `WAIVABLE_INSTRUMENTS` can be waived, each under its admissibility condition and with a written reason; anything else is `PROFILE_KERNEL_WAIVER`, and an empty reason is `PROFILE_WAIVER_REASON`. A waived instrument is reported, not hidden: `AuditReport` lists every waiver with the findings it suppressed, and `gates -- --audit-card` prints them.
4. **Every profile parameter is *Assumed*** (§1.5): it is a design choice of the application, never a result.
5. **The order of the 4 Filters and of the 8 SIGA Steps is kernel.** The profile sets the Funnel's numbers (decision volume, modeling cost, $n\_{\min}$, $\chi^2\_{\text{crit}}$), not its sequence (`run_full_siga_roadmap_empirical_with_profile`).
6. **A new dataset needs no Rust.** With `dataset_columns`, `ColumnMappedCsvStrategy` reads the application's CSV (cluster, forecast, binary outcome, and a value column under a `square`, `exp`, or `hinge` loss) in place of a hand-written extractor.
7. **`G1`–`G8` and the 27 negative proofs audit the canonical profile.** A shaped application is audited with `gates -- --audit-card <card.yaml>`.

**Worked example (simulated, seed 31).** The worked example in `examples/clay/` (`card_clay_night_shift_overflow.yaml`) shapes the protocol for a thought experiment about night-shift ward overflow, asked in Portuguese. Its profile uses seed 31, a stricter e-process ($1/\alpha \ge 40$), a Portuguese $\Phi\_5$ vocabulary (`plantão`, `anual`, `média`, …), 500 permutations, and a dataset under its own manifest. It also waives `PHI4_CHOULDECHOVA` with a stated reason. On its 2,410 simulated ward-nights over 48 wards, `gates -- --audit-card` approves it end to end: $n\_{\mathrm{eff}}(0.90) = 206$, $\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} = 0.0026$, $E\_{30} = 319.3 \ge 40$, null $p = 0.0020$. The same card fails under the canonical profile (`PHI1_SEED`, `PHI3_DATASET_PROVENANCE`, `PHI3_PREREGISTRATION_HASH`, `PHI4_CHOULDECHOVA`). The simulated data support no claim about any real hospital.

**Not yet clay.** The following are still fixed and change only through a re-seal under human review:
- the seed and $\Phi\_5$ vocabulary inside the sealed sources (`rng.rs`, `invariants.rs`);
- the English keyword instruments of the Pearl ladder, missingness bounds, and scope statement;
- the `G8` multi-seed acceptance of the syntheses.

## 2. Structural Diagnosis, Uncertainty Typing, and the Five Transversal Lenses (Tier 1)

Before fitting any parameter, selecting predictive architectures, or estimating probability distributions, every technical claim, scientific hypothesis, or operational model must answer three structural questions:
1. In which ontological layer can the system fail (**World $L\_1$**, **Model $L\_2$**, or **Agent $L\_3$**)?
2. Which class of uncertainty threatens inference validity (**Aleatory**, **Parametric/Structural Epistemic**, or **Knightian**)?
3. Which **severe test**, with genuine empirical or logical refutation power, supports the confidence placed in the claim?

### 2.1. Uncertainty Typing: Kennedy-O'Hagan Discrepancy and Info-Gap + Sniedovich Safeguard

1. **Aleatory Uncertainty (`aleatory`, $L\_1$):** Irreducible variability; demands safety margins and exposure control.
2. **Parametric Epistemic Uncertainty (`epistemic_parameter`, $L\_2$):** Reducible ignorance about finite parameters $\theta$; uses Laplace's rule of succession $\hat{p} = \frac{s+1}{n+2}$ in small samples.
3. **Structural Epistemic Uncertainty (`epistemic_specification`, $L\_2$):** Formulated via **Kennedy-O'Hagan Structural Discrepancy**:
   $$y(x) = \eta(x, \theta) + \delta(x) + \varepsilon, \qquad \delta(x) \sim \mathcal{GP}(m(x), k(x, x'))$$
   (the implementation uses a zero prior mean, $m \equiv 0$) with anchoring constraints to prevent parameter pseudo-convergence and extrapolation overconfidence, solved via exact Cholesky decomposition $K\_y = LL^\top$ and calibrated via Empirical Bayes Type-II maximum marginal likelihood $\log p(y \mid X, \ell, \sigma\_\delta)$ (`KennedyOHaganDiscrepancy::calibrate_empirical_bayes`).
4. **Knightian Uncertainty (`knightian`, $L\_3$):** Formulated via **Info-Gap Decision Theory** (Ben-Haim) coupled with **Sniedovich's Global Maximin Safeguard** (Wald):
   $$\hat{\alpha}(q, r\_c) = \max\left\lbrace\alpha \ge 0 : \min\_{u \in \mathcal{U}(\alpha, \tilde{u})} R(q, u) \ge r\_c\right\rbrace, \qquad \hat{\beta}(q, r\_w) = \min\left\lbrace\alpha \ge 0 : \max\_{u \in \mathcal{U}(\alpha, \tilde{u})} R(q, u) \ge r\_w\right\rbrace$$
   Enforces the *zero-ing* property ($\hat{\alpha}(q, R(q, \tilde{u})) = 0$) and requires a global Wald Maximin floor outside $\mathcal{U}(\hat{\alpha}, \tilde{u})$ to prevent catastrophic collapse in "No-Man's Land".


1. **Lens 1 — Information (`stochology_lenses::information`):**
   - Data Processing Inequality ($I(W; Z) \le I(W; Y) \le I(W; X)$ across a 3-step Markov chain $W \to X \to Y \to Z$ via `DpiMarkovChain`), exact **Blahut-Arimoto channel capacity** $C(W) = \max\_{p(x)} I(X; Y)$ (`blahut_arimoto_capacity`), empirical transition matrix estimation with Laplace smoothing (`estimate_empirical_channel`), and **Strong Data Processing Inequality (SDPI)** contraction hierarchy:
     $$\eta\_{\chi^2}(W) = \rho\_m^2(X; Y) \le \eta\_{\mathrm{KL}}(W) \le \eta\_{\mathrm{TV}}(W)$$
   - Fano's Error Floor: $H(P\_e) + P\_e \ln(|\mathcal{W}| - 1) \ge H(W) - I(W; Z)$.
   - Meng's Fundamental Identity: $\bar{Y}\_n - \bar{Y}\_N = \rho\_{R,Y} \times \sqrt{\frac{N-n}{n}} \times \sigma\_Y$.
2. **Lens 2 — Time (`stochology_lenses::time`):**
   - Itô Variance Drain: $g\_{\text{time}} = \mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 < \mu = g\_{\text{ensemble}}$.
   - Optimal Asymmetric Kelly Fraction $f^{\ast} = \frac{p}{a} - \frac{1 - p}{b}$ and continuous-return **Empirical Kelly Optimal Leverage** $f^{\ast}\_{\mathrm{emp}} = \arg\max\_f \frac{1}{T}\sum\_{t=1}^T \ln(1 + f R\_t)$ via damped Newton-Raphson (`empirical_kelly_optimal_fraction`), quantifying skewness/kurtosis over-betting penalty relative to the Gaussian Merton ratio $f\_{\mathrm{Merton}} = \hat{\mu}/\hat{\sigma}^2$.
   - Heavy-Tailed Mixing Time under Log-Sobolev Inequality ($\alpha\_{\mathrm{LSI}} = -\frac{1}{2}\ln \eta\_{\mathrm{KL}}$), tail mass $H\_R$, and **Robust State-Space Kalman Filtering & Closed-Loop Control (`RobustKalmanControlAudit`)** contrasting a Gaussian $L\_2$ scalar Kalman filter with a Huber innovation-weighted Kalman filter (Joseph-form update) under heavy-tailed sensor glitches, plus two-sensor precision fusion and the Joseph-form covariance identity, and comparing a certainty-equivalent clearance with a fixed safety-buffer clearance (labelled Tube-MPC) against simulated disturbances.
3. **Lens 3 — Scale and Tail (`stochology_lenses::tail`):**
   - Hill Estimator $\hat{\alpha}\_{k,n} = \left(\frac{1}{k}\sum\_{i=1}^k \ln \frac{X\_{(n-i+1)}}{X\_{(n-k)}}\right)^{-1}$, moment regimes ($\alpha \le 1$, $1 < \alpha \le 2$, $\alpha > 2$), **Dekkers-Einmahl-de Haan (1989) Moment Tail Index** $\hat{\xi}\_{\mathrm{DEdH}} = M\_{k,n}^{(1)} + 1 - \frac{1}{2}\bigl(1 - (M\_{k,n}^{(1)})^2 / M\_{k,n}^{(2)}\bigr)^{-1} \in \mathbb{R}$ (`dedh_moment_tail_index`), **Pickands-Balkema-de Haan Peaks-Over-Threshold GPD** via Hosking-Wallis probability-weighted moments (`gpd_pot_fit`), and **Expected Shortfall ($\mathrm{ES}\_{0.99} / \mathrm{CVaR}\_{0.99}$)** (`expected_shortfall_99`).
4. **Lens 4 — Structure and Invariance (`stochology_lenses::invariance`):**

On **Bridge $L\_3 \to L\_1$**, when a model parameterized by $\theta \in \Theta$ supports consequential decisions, deploying $\theta$ causally alters the environment and induces $Z = (X, Y) \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)$. Performance is governed by Performative Risk $\mathrm{PR}(\theta)$ and Decoupled Performative Risk $\mathrm{DPR}(\theta, \phi)$ (Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dünner & Hardt):

$$\mathrm{PR}(\theta) \triangleq \mathbb{E}\_{Z \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)}[\ell(Z; \theta)], \qquad \mathrm{DPR}(\theta, \phi) \triangleq \mathbb{E}\_{Z \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)}[\ell(Z; \phi)]$$

- **Performative Stability ($\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$):** $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \arg\min\_{\phi \in \Theta} \mathrm{DPR}(\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}, \phi)$ — fixed point of Repeated Risk Minimization ($\mathrm{RRM}$).
- **Performative Optimality ($\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}}$):** $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}} = \arg\min\_{\theta \in \Theta} \mathrm{PR}(\theta)$ — Stackelberg equilibrium anticipating the strategic reaction of the population, attained via **Performative Gradient Descent (`PerfGD`, Izzo, Ying & Zou 2021)** along the total performative gradient $\nabla\_\theta \mathrm{PR}(\theta) = \nabla\_1 \mathrm{DPR}(\theta,\theta) + \nabla\_2 \mathrm{DPR}(\theta,\theta)$ (`PerformativeSystem::perfgd_trajectory`).
- **Canonical Linear Benchmark:** Under $Y(\theta) = \mu + \varepsilon \theta + \xi$ and loss $\ell(z; \phi) = \frac{\gamma}{2}(y - \phi)^2 - \alpha \phi$, passive learning yields $\theta\_{\mathrm{SL}} = \mu + \frac{\alpha}{\gamma}$, retraining converges to $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \frac{\mu + \alpha / \gamma}{1 - \varepsilon}$ (reducing to $\frac{\mu}{1 - \varepsilon}$ when $\alpha = 0$), whereas the true Stackelberg optimum minimizing $\mathrm{PR}(\theta) = \frac{\gamma}{2}[(1 - \varepsilon)\theta - \mu]^2 + \frac{\gamma}{2}\sigma^2 - \alpha\theta$ is $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}} = \frac{\mu}{1 - \varepsilon} + \frac{\alpha}{\gamma(1 - \varepsilon)^2}$. Under Wasserstein-1 sensitivity $W\_1(\mathcal{D}(\theta), \mathcal{D}(\theta')) \le \varepsilon \lVert\theta - \theta'\rVert\_2$, $\gamma$-strong convexity, and $\beta$-smoothness, $\mathrm{RRM}$ contracts linearly if and only if $\frac{\varepsilon \beta}{\gamma} < 1$.
- **Four Variants of Goodhart's Law (`GoodhartTaxonomyAudit`, Manheim & Garrabrant 2018):** Decomposes proxy-target decoupling into: (1) **Regressional Goodhart** ($\mathbb{E}[Y \mid M = m] = \mu\_Y + \rho\_{M,Y}\frac{\sigma\_Y}{\sigma\_M}(m - \mu\_M) < m$ for noisy proxies $\sigma\_\xi > 0$); (2) **Extremal Goodhart** (tail divergence between proxy $\alpha\_M$ and true goal $\alpha\_Y$ in the extreme upper quantile $q\_{99.9}$); (3) **Causal Goodhart** ($\mathrm{do}(M = m)$ on a downstream symptom leaves structural outcome $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathrm{do}(M = m)] = \mu\_Y$ unchanged when $M \notin \hat{S}^{\ast}$ of `run_nonlinear_icp_nile`); and (4) **Adversarial Goodhart** (strategic gaming along the performative distribution gradient $\nabla\_1 \mathrm{DPR}(\theta, \theta)$ in `PerformativeSystem::dpr_gradient_components`).
