# Stochology — The CAPIBA Protocol: Methodological Architecture for Judgment and Decision-Making under Uncertainty

This document specifies the **CAPIBA Protocol** (*Calibrated, Anytime-valid, Performative-aware, Invariant, Bitemporal Auditing Protocol*), the scientific and operational auditing protocol of **Stochology** for data architecture, stochastic modeling, causal inference, and structured inter-agent communication. It pairs operational decision-making with algorithmic gates for performative prediction, decision theory under severe uncertainty, strong information contraction, tail design effects in unequal clusters, and production drift adaptation.

## 1. Deep Structural Diagnosis: Bridge Mechanics, Severe Uncertainty, and the Five Transversal Lenses

Before fitting any parameter, selecting predictive architectures, or estimating probability distributions, every technical claim, scientific hypothesis, or operational model must answer three structural questions:
1. In which ontological layer can the system fail (**World $L\_1$**, **Model $L\_2$**, or **Agent $L\_3$**)?
2. Which class of uncertainty threatens inference validity (**Aleatory**, **Parametric/Structural Epistemic**, or **Knightian**)?
3. Which **severe test**, with genuine empirical or logical refutation power, supports the confidence placed in the claim?

### 1.1. Dynamics of the Three Ontological Layers ($L\_1 \to L\_2 \to L\_3 \to L\_1$)

On **Bridge $L\_3 \to L\_1$**, when a model parameterized by $\theta \in \Theta$ supports consequential decisions, deploying $\theta$ causally alters the environment and induces $Z = (X, Y) \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)$. Performance is governed by Performative Risk $\mathrm{PR}(\theta)$ and Decoupled Performative Risk $\mathrm{DPR}(\theta, \phi)$ (Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dünner & Hardt):

$$\mathrm{PR}(\theta) \triangleq \mathbb{E}\_{Z \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)}[\ell(Z; \theta)], \qquad \mathrm{DPR}(\theta, \phi) \triangleq \mathbb{E}\_{Z \sim \mathcal{D}(\theta)}[\ell(Z; \phi)]$$

- **Performative Stability ($\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}$):** $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \arg\min\_{\phi \in \Theta} \mathrm{DPR}(\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}}, \phi)$ — fixed point of Repeated Risk Minimization ($\mathrm{RRM}$).
- **Performative Optimality ($\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}}$):** $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}} = \arg\min\_{\theta \in \Theta} \mathrm{PR}(\theta)$ — Stackelberg equilibrium anticipating the strategic reaction of the population, attained via **Performative Gradient Descent (`PerfGD`, Izzo, Ying & Zou 2021)** along the total performative gradient $\nabla\_\theta \mathrm{PR}(\theta) = \nabla\_1 \mathrm{DPR}(\theta,\theta) + \nabla\_2 \mathrm{DPR}(\theta,\theta)$ (`PerformativeSystem::perfgd_trajectory`).
- **Canonical Linear Benchmark:** Under $Y(\theta) = \mu + \varepsilon \theta + \xi$ and loss $\ell(z; \phi) = \frac{\gamma}{2}(y - \phi)^2 - \alpha \phi$, passive learning yields $\theta\_{\mathrm{SL}} = \mu + \frac{\alpha}{\gamma}$, retraining converges to $\theta\_{\mathrm{PS}} = \frac{\mu + \alpha / \gamma}{1 - \varepsilon}$ (reducing to $\frac{\mu}{1 - \varepsilon}$ when $\alpha = 0$), whereas the true Stackelberg optimum minimizing $\mathrm{PR}(\theta) = \frac{\gamma}{2}[(1 - \varepsilon)\theta - \mu]^2 + \frac{\gamma}{2}\sigma^2 - \alpha\theta$ is $\theta\_{\mathrm{PO}} = \frac{\mu}{1 - \varepsilon} + \frac{\alpha}{\gamma(1 - \varepsilon)^2}$. Under Wasserstein-1 sensitivity $W\_1(\mathcal{D}(\theta), \mathcal{D}(\theta')) \le \varepsilon \lVert\theta - \theta'\rVert\_2$, $\gamma$-strong convexity, and $\beta$-smoothness, $\mathrm{RRM}$ contracts linearly if and only if $\frac{\varepsilon \beta}{\gamma} < 1$.
- **Four Variants of Goodhart's Law (`GoodhartTaxonomyAudit`, Manheim & Garrabrant 2018):** Decomposes proxy-target decoupling into: (1) **Regressional Goodhart** ($\mathbb{E}[Y \mid M = m] = \mu\_Y + \rho\_{M,Y}\frac{\sigma\_Y}{\sigma\_M}(m - \mu\_M) < m$ for noisy proxies $\sigma\_\xi > 0$); (2) **Extremal Goodhart** (tail divergence between proxy $\alpha\_M$ and true goal $\alpha\_Y$ in the extreme upper quantile $q\_{99.9}$); (3) **Causal Goodhart** ($\mathrm{do}(M = m)$ on a downstream symptom leaves structural outcome $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathrm{do}(M = m)] = \mu\_Y$ unchanged when $M \notin \hat{S}^{\ast}$ of `run_nonlinear_icp_nile`); and (4) **Adversarial Goodhart** (strategic gaming along the performative distribution gradient $\nabla\_1 \mathrm{DPR}(\theta, \theta)$ in `PerformativeSystem::dpr_gradient_components`).

### 1.2. Uncertainty Typing: Kennedy-O'Hagan Discrepancy and Info-Gap + Sniedovich Safeguard

1. **Aleatory Uncertainty (`aleatory`, $L\_1$):** Irreducible variability; demands safety margins and exposure control.
2. **Parametric Epistemic Uncertainty (`epistemic_parameter`, $L\_2$):** Reducible ignorance about finite parameters $\theta$; uses Laplace's rule of succession $\hat{p} = \frac{s+1}{n+2}$ in small samples.
3. **Structural Epistemic Uncertainty (`epistemic_specification`, $L\_2$):** Formulated via **Kennedy-O'Hagan Structural Discrepancy**:
   $$y(x) = \eta(x, \theta) + \delta(x) + \varepsilon, \qquad \delta(x) \sim \mathcal{GP}(m(x), k(x, x'))$$
   (the implementation uses a zero prior mean, $m \equiv 0$) with anchoring constraints to prevent parameter pseudo-convergence and extrapolation overconfidence, solved via exact Cholesky decomposition $K\_y = LL^\top$ and calibrated via Empirical Bayes Type-II maximum marginal likelihood $\log p(y \mid X, \ell, \sigma\_\delta)$ (`KennedyOHaganDiscrepancy::calibrate_empirical_bayes`).
4. **Knightian Uncertainty (`knightian`, $L\_3$):** Formulated via **Info-Gap Decision Theory** (Ben-Haim) coupled with **Sniedovich's Global Maximin Safeguard** (Wald):
   $$\hat{\alpha}(q, r\_c) = \max\left\lbrace\alpha \ge 0 : \min\_{u \in \mathcal{U}(\alpha, \tilde{u})} R(q, u) \ge r\_c\right\rbrace, \qquad \hat{\beta}(q, r\_w) = \min\left\lbrace\alpha \ge 0 : \max\_{u \in \mathcal{U}(\alpha, \tilde{u})} R(q, u) \ge r\_w\right\rbrace$$
   Enforces the *zero-ing* property ($\hat{\alpha}(q, R(q, \tilde{u})) = 0$) and requires a global Wald Maximin floor outside $\mathcal{U}(\hat{\alpha}, \tilde{u})$ to prevent catastrophic collapse in "No-Man's Land".

## 4. Bitemporal Production Governance, Vault, and Drift Adaptation

### 4.1. Bitemporal Clock, Synthetic Canary, and Single-Opening Vault (`stochology_governance::{clock, vault}`)
- **Bitemporal Asymmetry ($t\_{\text{disp}} \ge t\_{\text{ev}}$):** Filters feature records strictly by system availability timestamp $t\_{\text{disp}} \le \tau$ rather than event timestamp $t\_{\text{ev}}$, and injects a high-cardinality synthetic canary column ($I(\text{Canary}; Y) = 0$) whose non-zero importance triggers an immediate look-ahead intrusion alarm.
- **Single-Opening Temporal Vault (`TemporalVault`):** Enforces a cryptographic SHA-256 pre-registration seal (re-verified at opening time with `VaultError::SealTampered` on payload mutation) and an immutable single-opening lock (`opened_count: 0 -> 1`). Any non-positive confirmation outcome automatically downgrades the study to `ExploratoryHypothesisGenerating`, and any second opening attempt is blocked.

### 4.2. Murphy-CORP Circuit Breaker, Covariate Drift (ExTRA-WCP-T), Online ACI, and Label Shift (`stochology_governance::drift`)
- **Murphy-CORP Circuit Breaker:** Separates pure Covariate/Target Shift ($\Delta\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} > \tau\_{\mathrm{REL}}, \Delta\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} \approx 0$) from Concept Drift ($\Delta\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}} < -\tau\_{\mathrm{RES}}$), halting automated predictions under concept breakdown.
- **Covariate Drift Adaptation (`ExTRA-WCP-T`) & Online Adaptive Conformal Inference (`AdaptiveConformalInference`):** Fits exponential tilt density ratios $\hat{w}(x) = \exp(\hat{\theta}^\top \phi(x) - \hat{\psi})$ for weighted conformal intervals with Lei & Candès coverage lower bound $1 - \alpha - d\_{\mathrm{TV}}(P\_X, Q\_X)$, and adapts threshold quantiles online under non-exchangeable time series via Gibbs & Candès (2021) $\alpha\_{t+1} = \alpha\_t + \gamma(\alpha - \mathrm{err}\_t)$.
- **Label Shift Adaptation (4 Canonical Estimators):** Simultaneously estimates target class priors $q\_Y$ via **BBSE** ($q = C^{-1}\mu$), **MLLS/SLD** (EM fixed point), **DFM** (characteristic-function kernel matching with condition number $\kappa(M)$), and **OTTER** (Sinkhorn-Knopp optimal transport scaling with golden-section sub-grid refinement).
- **Predetermined Change Control Plan (`PredeterminedChangeControlPlan`), Profit & Loss Attribution Test (`ProfitLossAttributionTest`), and `EvaluatedCompetenceCriterion`:** Enforces a pre-registered modification boundary (`PCCP` with SHA-256 protocol hash, maximum allowed $\mathrm{REL}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}$, and minimum $\mathrm{RES}\_{\mathrm{CORP}}$ retention), evaluates Basel III FRTB MAR33 dual-metric attribution (**Spearman rank correlation** $r\_s$ + two-sample **Kolmogorov-Smirnov** $D\_{\mathrm{KS}}$ with `Green` / `Amber` / `Red` traffic-light zones), and requires out-of-domain **Evaluated Competence** before autonomous policy updates, firing the VSM Algedonic Channel (*"not measured, not executed"*) on any violation. Gate `G7` feeds the plan a real GRAPA *e-process* on the H1 vault model's calibration residuals $Y\_i - p\_i$ as a paired severe test: the update is approved under the executable null $Y\_i \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(p\_i)$ (`seed = 2026`) and must be rolled back on the real 2023 outcomes.
