## Specification

**Physical, Analog, and Wave-Interference Computing (`stochology_lenses::analog`):**
- Formalizes the 5-axis physical-computational synthesis (`AnalogWaveComputingSynthesis`, plus empirical real-data wave/RRAM audit `EmpiricalWaveAnalogAudit`):
 - **Axis 1 (von Neumann Bottleneck & Recursive Model Collapse — `VonNeumannModelCollapseAudit` & `GerstgrasserAccumulateAudit`):** Quantifies Mark Horowitz's (ISSCC 2014, 45nm CMOS) energy hierarchy ($0.1\text{ pJ}$ Int32 add, $0.9\text{ pJ}$ FP32 add, $3.7\text{ pJ}$ FP32 mul, $5.0\text{ pJ}$ 8KB SRAM read vs. $640.0\text{ pJ}$ off-chip DRAM read $\implies 711.1\times$ DRAM-to-FP32-add asymmetry and $6400\times$ vs. Int32 add), IEA global data-center electricity demand trajectory ($269.1 \to 485.1 \to 945.0 \to 1200.0\text{ TWh}$ across 2020–2035), $G=8$ generations of recursive synthetic training ("AI contaminating AI" under Epoch AI's ~300T human token ceiling), proving that unanchored synthetic recursion collapses tail variance ($\sigma_G^2 / \sigma_0^2 < 0.01,\ D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_0 \parallel P_G) > 0.60\text{ nats}$) whereas neurosymbolic symmetry/moment constraints + anchored physical observations preserve $\sigma_G^2 / \sigma_0^2 = 0.95$ ($D_{\mathrm{KL}} < 0.02\text{ nats}$), and **Gerstgrasser et al.'s (ICML 2024) Accumulate-vs.-Replace Theorem (`GerstgrasserAccumulateAudit`, `--defect replace_collapse`)**, proving that replacing human data diverges linearly ($E_n^{\mathrm{Replace}} = \frac{\sigma^2 d}{T-d-1}n$) whereas accumulating preserves a universal Basel-bounded asymptote ($E_n^{\mathrm{Accum}} = \frac{\sigma^2 d}{T-d-1}\sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{i^2} \le \frac{\sigma^2 d}{T-d-1}\frac{\pi^2}{6}$).
 - **Axis 2 (Native Differential Equation Isomorphism & Liquid ODEs — `NativeOdeIsomorphismAudit`):** Integrates the mechanical Mass-Spring-Damper ODE ($m\ddot{x} + c\dot{x} + kx = F(t)$) and the isomorphic Analog RLC Circuit ODE ($L\ddot{q} + R\dot{q} + \frac{1}{C}q = V(t)$ with $L=m, R=c, 1/C=k$) via 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4), verifying machine-precision isomorphism ($\max_t |x_{\text{mech}}(t) - q_{\text{RLC}}(t)| = 0.0 < 10^{-14}$), RK4 accuracy vs. exact analytical underdamped solution ($< 10^{-7}$ vs. coarse digital Euler error $> 0.05$), and adaptive relaxation in Liquid Time-Constant (LTC) / Neural ODE cells ($\tau_{\text{eff}}(u) = \frac{\tau}{1 + \tau f(u)}$).
 - **Axis 3 (AIMC Crossbar, The "Analog Wall" Threshold Cliff vs. Noise-Aware Training, Peking University 24-Bit `HP-INV` + `BlockAMC`, and `ABNAT / HaLoRA` — `AimcAnalogWallHpInvAudit`):** Simulates Analog In-Memory Computing (AIMC) crossbar arrays executing $I_j = \sum_{i=1}^N V_i G_{ij}$ in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ physical time via Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws, PCM power-law resistance drift $G(t) = G_0(t/t_0)^{-\nu}$ with Analog Error-Correcting Codes (A-ECC recovery $\ge 95\%$), Prohibition 2 `NonlinearSummary` over stochastic crossbar loss ($\Delta_J > 0.15$), Ferran Alia's **"Analog Wall" threshold cliff** across $\sigma \in [0.0, 0.8]$ (standard digital accuracy collapsing to $35.0\%$ at $\sigma = 0.60$ vs. **Noise-Aware Training (NAT)** preserving $63.5\%$), **Sun Zhong et al.'s (Peking University, *Nature Electronics* Oct 2025) `HP-INV`** solver combining closed-loop 3-bit $\mathrm{TaO}_x$ RRAM Low-Precision Inversion (`LP-INV`, $C_{\mathrm{LP}} = \tilde{A}_{\text{3bit}}^{-1}$) with bit-sliced 24-bit `HP-MVM` iterative refinement ($r^{(k)} = b - A x^{(k)}, \Delta x^{(k)} = C_{\mathrm{LP}} r^{(k)}$) to solve a $16\times 16$ Massive MIMO matrix equation to $< 10^{-7}$ relative error ($8.70 \times 10^{-12}$ in $7 \le 10$ iterations, 24-bit / FP32 equivalence; informative: a reported $7\text{ W}$ vs. $700\text{ W}$ H100 system power, i.e. $100\times$ lower power, not an energy-efficiency measurement), **Recursive $4\times 4$ Block Matrix Partitioning (`BlockAMC`, `solve_block_amc_hp_inv_16x16`)** solving the $16\times 16$ system via Schur-complement $4\times 4$ RRAM tiles in $16$ block operations (`pku_block_amc_relative_error` $< 10^{-7}$), and **Attention-Based Noise-Aware Training / Hardware-Aware LoRA (`ABNAT / HaLoRA`, `evaluate_abnat_halora_attention_stability`)** reducing softmax attention Frobenius drift by $\ge 85.77\%$ and recovering $\ge 11.28\%$ accuracy on hybrid RRAM/SRAM crossbars.
 - **Axis 4 (Wave-Interference Learning: Gibram Raul's Phase-Amplitude Encoding, `SIREN`, and Stanford Wave-Physics Analog RNN — `WaveInterferenceLearningAudit`):** Formalizes Gibram Raul's wave-superposition paradigm (encoding signals via **phase $\phi$, amplitude $A$, and frequency $\omega$** instead of pixel-by-pixel grids) alongside Vincent Sitzmann et al.'s (NeurIPS 2020) `SIREN` ($\phi_i(x_i) = \sin(\omega_0 W_i x_i + b_i)$), achieving $40\times$ compression over dense pixel grids and machine-precision 2nd-order Helmholtz PDE Laplacian fidelity ($\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{SIREN}}(\nabla^2 u) = 1.3 \times 10^{-27}$ vs. piecewise-linear ReLU failure $\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{ReLU}}(\nabla^2 u) = 2580.3$ where $\mathrm{ReLU}''(x) = 0$ a.e.); and simulates **Tyler Hughes, Ian Williamson, Momchil Minkov & Shanhui Fan's (Stanford, *Science Advances* 2019) Wave-Physics Analog RNN** ($\partial_t^2 u + \gamma \partial_t u = c^2(x, u)\partial_x^2 u + f(x, t)$ with recurrent state $h_t = [u(x,t), u(x,t-\Delta t)]$ and optical/acoustic Kerr nonlinearity $c(x,u) = c_0(x)/\sqrt{1 + \chi u^2}$), routing wave energy by constructive interference to passive detector probes ($99.9\%$ probe selectivity).
 - **Axis 5 (The 20-Watt Brain Sensory Funnel via DPI, Turing Deciban Triage & Post-Digital Ecosystem — `SensoryDpiOrganoidHybridAudit`):** Audits biological sensory compression ($1.6\times 10^9\text{ bit/s}$ retinal cones $X \to 1.0\times 10^7\text{ bit/s}$ retinal ganglion cells $Y \to 10\text{ bit/s}$ conscious motor-decision stream $Z$) via Lens 1 `DpiMarkovChain` ($I(W; Z) \le I(W; Y) \le I(W; X)$ across a $1.6\times 10^8 : 1$ bandwidth reduction while retaining $56.4\%$ of survival-critical mutual information at $20\text{ W}$), couples Zheng & Meister's $10\text{ bit/s}$ conscious ceiling to Turing-Good additive deciban weights ($W = 10\log_{10}\mathrm{LR} \ge +12.61\text{ dB}$, contrasting the published Epic Sepsis $\mathrm{PPV}=0.12$ (Wong et al. 2021, literature) against the assumed Stochological design operating point's $\mathrm{PPV}=0.563$), and contrasts commercial organoid wetware (Cortical Labs CL1 / FinalSpark, requiring $850\text{–}1000\text{ W}$ rack life-support overhead and $\le 200\text{ days}$ longevity) against hybrid analog-digital and diffractive photonic coprocessors (PKU 40nm RRAM $7\text{ W}$, EnCharge `EN100` $24.2\text{ TOPS/W}$ at $8.25\text{ W}$, Mythic $120\text{ TOPS/W}$, Tsinghua `Taichi` $160\text{ TOPS/W}$ & `ACCEL` $74\text{,}800\text{ TOPS/W}$), enforced in Gate `G8` and `--defect analog_wall`.
