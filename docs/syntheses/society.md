## Specification

**Coupled Public-Private Stock-Flow Consistent Society Synthesis (`stochology_lenses::society`):**
- Pre-registered in `data/preregistration/coupled_society_preregistration.yaml` (`preregistration_hash = 82e6cf8431de...`, registry attempt counter `K = 6`) before code and evaluated once on the virgin confirmation seed block `9_000_200..=9_000_399` (`CoupledPublicPrivateSocietySynthesis`, `EmpiricalCoupledSocietyAudit`). `shielded = false` runs the naive baseline, `all_identities_closed` closes on every seed in both modes, and `G8` gates 4 named claims (3 seed-dependent, 1 `.det.`), each passing on $\ge 95\%$ of the 20 pre-registered development seeds and of `9_000_200..=9_000_399` (lowest one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson bound on a confirmation pass rate: $0.935$); one further pre-registered claim is refuted on confirmation and moved to `discarded_claims` without changing any parameter. Unless a dataset is named, numbers below are **simulated** under **assumed** pre-registered structural inputs. Enforced in Gate `G8` and `--defect coupled_society`:
  - **Closed-Economy SFC Transaction-Flow Accounting (`SfcFlowMatrix`):** 4-sector (Households, Production Firms, Commercial Banks, Public Regulator/Treasury + Capital Account; 7 rows $\times$ 5 columns) transaction-flow matrix (`Godley & Lavoie 2007`; `Kalecki 1954`) under an **assumed** closed-economy setting ($NX \equiv 0$, public debt held as commercial bank reserves $\Delta B\_t \equiv \Delta M\_{B,t}$). Identities close to $< 10^{-12}$ (`sfc_row_column_max_error` $= 1.8 \times 10^{-14}$, Kalecki-Levy profit identity $\Pi\_t = I\_t + (G\_t - T\_t) - S\_{H,t}$, and Godley Three-Balances $(S\_{H,t} - I\_t) + \Pi\_{B,t} \equiv G\_t - T\_t$ error $1.8 \times 10^{-14}$), and `SplitMix64::split` per `(domain_tag, epoch, agent_id)` guarantees exact agent-order permutation invariance (`agent_order_permutation_linf_error` $= 0.0$ across `0..N` vs. `(0..N).rev()`).
  - **`H1a` ($2 \times 2$ Closed- vs. Open-Loop Regulator Coupling DiD — `H1aRegulatorCouplingAblation`):** Holds Carroll (1997) buffer-stock households ($N\_H = 120$, **assumed** $\gamma = 2.0$, target liquid wealth $m^\ast = 1.50$), Basel III `CCyB`, and Bester (1985) credit constant across all 4 cells (`policy_shielded` $\in \lbrace\text{false}, \text{true}\rbrace \times$ `closed_loop` $\in \lbrace\text{false}, \text{true}\rbrace$) under a heavy-tailed supply-chain productivity shock at $t\_0 = 10$ (**assumed** Pareto exponent $\alpha = 2.25$, scale $x\_m = 0.18$, shocked fraction $f\_{\text{shock}} = 0.25$), isolating **only** the public regulator under bitemporal availability lag $\Delta t\_{\text{disp}} = 2$ and administrative `MNAR` selection ($\rho\_{R,Y} = -0.15, f\_{\text{obs}} = 0.75$): `shielded = true` (bitemporal filter + Manski 2004 cost-loss weighted Minimax-Regret point $\theta\_{\text{MMR}} = (1 - p^\ast) U\_M + p^\ast L\_M$ at $p^\ast = 0.12$ + Info-Gap/Sniedovich floor + oracle-free two-point finite-difference `PerfGD`, Izzo et al. 2021) vs. `shielded = false` (event-time `MNAR` point estimate + damped `RRM`). Under the pre-registered baseline run, closed-loop survival is $1.000$ vs. $0.533$ (`open_loop`: $0.733$ vs. $0.500$, Coupling DiD $+0.233$) and the continuous CRRA household welfare Coupling DiD is $+0.4549$ (`society.h1a.closed_loop_coupling_did_crra_welfare_above_0.04`, $100.0\%$ on confirmation seeds). **Discarded claim** `society.h1a.closed_loop_coupling_did_firm_survival_above_0.06`: passes on $95.0\%$ ($19/20$) of the pre-registered development seeds but is refuted at $94.5\%$ ($189/200 < 0.95$) on the virgin confirmation block `9_000_200..=9_000_399` due to discrete $1/N\_F = 1/30$ firm-count quantization across the 4-cell DiD; recorded as discarded without modifying any simulation parameter.
  - **`H1b` (Single-Factor Credit Screening Ablation — `H1bCreditScreeningAblation`):** Isolates Bester (1985) collateral screening (with closed-form existence threshold $\lambda^\ast\_{\text{Bester}} = 0.7534 \in (0.55, 0.85)$ and automatic switch to low-rate collateralized pooling at $\lambda\_{\text{good}} = 0.88 > \lambda^\ast\_{\text{Bester}}$) against Stiglitz-Weiss (1981) uncollateralized pooling. At $\lambda\_{\text{good}} = 0.50$, Bester screening lifts firm survival by $+0.175$ (`society.h1b.bester_collateral_screening_beats_stiglitz_weiss_pooling_by_0.08`, $96.5\%$ on confirmation seeds); at $\lambda\_{\text{good}} = 0.88 > \lambda^\ast\_{\text{Bester}}$, the regime switch avoids repossession deadweight loss ($+0.025$).
  - **`H2` (Single-Factor VCG Surplus Routing & Green-Laffont $O(1/M)$ Leakage — `H2VcgGreenLaffontCommonsAblation`):** Isolates Clarke (1971) pivotal tax surplus routing across cohort sizes $M \in \lbrace 4, 8, 16, 32\rbrace$ under an identical `aGRAPA` betting martingale ($E\_{60} = 2753.9$): (i) proportional rebate ($\omega\_i = \hat{v}\_i / \sum\_k \hat{v}\_k$), (ii) same-cohort equal-share buffer ($\omega\_i = 1/M$), where Green & Laffont's (1979) indirect tax channel re-opens a non-zero profitable misreport $\bar{\varepsilon}\_{\text{same}}(4) = 0.4286 > 10^{-3}$ that decays strictly as $O(1/M)$ to $\bar{\varepsilon}\_{\text{same}}(32) = 0.0363$ (`society.h2.det.same_cohort_buffer_leakage_decays_as_inv_m`, $100.0\%$), and (iii) disjoint-cohort routing ($\omega\_i = 0$ for reporting cohort $A$, funding downstream cohort $B$'s Ostrom 1990 renewable commons buffer), which preserves exact DSIC ($\bar{\varepsilon}\_{\text{disjoint}}(M) = 0.0 < 10^{-12}$) and retains commons stock $S\_T / K = 0.730$ vs. $0.050$ under rebate (`society.h2.disjoint_cohort_vcg_commons_stock_beats_rebate_by_0.10`, $100.0\%$ on confirmation seeds).
  - **Real-data coupler (`EmpiricalCoupledSocietyAudit`):** **Measured** on `cps_observational.csv` ($n = 15{,}992$, Moulton-Campbell $\tilde{m} = 95.65$, $n\_{\text{eff}}(q\_{90}) = 1{,}583.5$, Meng $n\_{\text{eff,Meng}} = 4.5$, Manski width $\$6{,}391.21$, CRRA $\Delta\_J = -0.0975$), `bank_calls.csv`, and `danish_fire.csv`, plus `EpistemologicalCard::audit()` and SHA-256 pre-registration seal check (`card_audit_and_sha256_verified = true`).
  - **Negative proof (`gates -- --defect coupled_society`, exit `1`):** Blocks on all 20 development seeds through seed-dependent claims with identities closed on every seed; in the baseline run the naive arm prints closed-loop firm survival $0.533$ (Coupling DiD $+0.000$, CRRA welfare DiD $+0.0000$), Stiglitz-Weiss pooling survival $0.558$ (Bester gain $+0.000$), and proportional-rebate VCG commons stock $S\_T / K = 0.050$ (disjoint-cohort gain $+0.000$).
