## Specification

**Triadic Mathematical & Physical Synthesis ($\mathbb{C} \times \text{Fractals} \times \mathbb{T}^d$), Technium / Technosphere Evolutionary Ontology, and Spring-and-Horizon Multidimensional Spiral (`stochology_lenses::triad`):**
- Formalizes the 6-bridge synthesis (`ComplexFractalToroidalTechniumSynthesis` and 5-dataset real coupler `EmpiricalTriadicTechniumAudit`) under a three-way verification contract: **claim checks** compare the deployed method with simulated ground truth (in `shielded = false` mode the naive method is actually run, and `G8` requires each named claim to pass on 95% of the development and the sealed confirmation seeds, with `.det.` claims passing everywhere), **identity checks** (`identities_closed`, `all_identities_closed`) must close in both modes, and **informative constants** transcribed from the literature never enter a verification boolean. Claims that miss their pre-registered tolerance are reported as `discarded_findings` (Prohibition 4) and are not approved by `G8`.
  - **Axis 1 ($\mathbb{C}$ — `ComplexWaveGaborHelmholtzAudit`):** Discrete $(\nabla\_h^2 + k^2)\Phi = q\_h$ at $k = 12$: a complex Gabor atom (`WIRE`) with frequency learned by gradient ascent leaves relative residual $1.0 \times 10^{-11}$ vs. $0.609$ for a real `tanh` dictionary; the target lies in the Gabor class, so this checks frequency learning. Identities: `Improved SIREN` $\{\sin, \cos\}$ phase completeness (worst residual $1.6 \times 10^{-15}$; single sine $1.0$, informative) and the Hughes-Minkov-Shi-Fan adjoint gradient vs. finite differences ($9.21 \times 10^{-10}$). `MIRE` PSNR gain informative.
  - **Axis 2 ($\mathbb{C} \leftrightarrow \text{Fractals}$ — `ComplexRealIsomorphismHMatrixAudit`):** Identity: $\mathbb{C}^{4\times 4} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}^{8\times 8}$ inversion closes to $1.12 \times 10^{-16}$. Two-stage `BlockAMC` + 10 `HP-INV` refinements: $1.53 \times 10^{-10}$ vs. $5.7 \times 10^{-2}$ one-shot. $\eta$-admissible rank-3 hierarchical compression of $1/(y - x)$: $9.6 \times 10^{-6}$ vs. $62.0$ when every block is compressed. Tile count, area/energy gains, and closed-form $(2p+1)n$ storage informative.
  - **Axis 3 ($\text{Fractals} \leftrightarrow \mathbb{R}^n$ — `FractionalCtrwAnalogEccAudit`):** Identity: $G\_0 = 2e^2/h = 77.48\,\mu\text{S}$. State-aware PCM drift compensation from one early read: MSE $0.0019$ vs. $0.359$.   - **Axis 4 ($\text{Fractals} \leftrightarrow \text{Scaling Laws}$ — `MultifractalPdhteRagCollapseAudit`):** The `PDHTE` refusal gate blocks thin-tailed data; Welsch-IRLS ATE RMSE $0.048$ vs. OLS $0.200$ under Pareto noise.   - **Axis 5 ($\mathbb{T}^d \leftrightarrow \mathbb{C}$ — `WeylTorusHermiteMehlerDfmAudit`):** Identities: preface cycle $x\_{t+1} = (5x\_t + 3)\bmod 16$ from $x\_0 = 10$ and Mercer mass $1 - \rho^{65}$.
